# Primary sources and reproducibility scope

1. **Maxence Jauberty and Pierrick Méaux, *On the Cardinality of the Walsh Support of a Boolean Function*.** [IACR ePrint 2025/1651](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1651), [archived version of 28 April 2026](https://opencryptology.org/2025/1651/versions/20260428T172000Z.pdf) (CC BY). All statement numbers in this package refer to this version. The [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-026-00925-8) appeared in *Cryptography and Communications* 18 (2026), 2181–2234; its bibliographic record and abstract are available, but we could not verify the complete journal text. The first paragraph under Open Questions in preprint §8, p. 49, asks whether 18 or 21 is the largest support cardinality with a unique shape. The following are the precise earlier ingredients used:
   - §2 Property 6, sizes 4 and 8, gives affine hull dimensions 2 and 3, respectively, and the existence of a Boolean function supported on any affine 2-flat. It credits D. Pei and Z. Qin, “On the Walsh spectra of Boolean functions,” FSE 2000, Theorem 1.
   - §3 Theorem 1 gives the size-of-join identity; §3 Lemma 1 gives the two Parseval exclusions. The form needed is reproved in this package.
   - §4 Definition 8 is the affine equivalence of supports (using their own hulls), and §4 Theorem 2 gives the reduction property. The reduction in the form needed is also reproved in this package.
   - §5 Table 1 reports one EA class at each of sizes 18 and 21 in six variables, not uniqueness at size 21 in all dimensions. We check these specific rows by recomputing the Walsh transforms from the full independent class catalogue.
   - §6 Lemma 4 / Theorem 3 give affine dimension 4 for size 10. Lemma 5 / Theorem 4 give dimension 5 and the union-of-two-cubes description and exact absolute magnitudes for size 13. Proposition 15 gives dimension 4 for the nonplateaued size-16 case. Corollary 8 rules out precisely \(2,3,5,6,7,9,11,12,14,15,17,19\) in all dimensions. Appendix A.4 gives dimension 5 and uniqueness of the size-18 shape in all dimensions; we do not claim either result as new.
   - §7 Lemma 7 gives a nonflat shape of size \(2^j\) for \(j\ge4\) in sufficiently many variables, and §7 Theorem 5 gives existence of all admissible sizes in \(n\ge7\), not inequivalence of shapes. The singleton and affine-plane joins in that section are also prior construction motifs; here the relative placement is used to obtain two different affine dimensions at the same size.

2. **James A. Maiorana, *A Classification of the Cosets of the Reed–Muller Code R(1,6)*.** *Mathematics of Computation* **57** (1991), 403–414, [DOI 10.2307/2938682](https://doi.org/10.2307/2938682). Provides the complete finite classification and the count 150,357. **Philippe Langevin, *Classification of Boolean functions under the affine group***, [university catalogue and methodology](https://langevin.univ-tln.fr/project/agl/agl.html), [class-2-6.txt](https://langevin.univ-tln.fr/project/agl/class-2-6.txt), provides an independent reconstruction with all representatives. The pinned input is 13,676,176 bytes, SHA-256 **8174e08ac8e974e25a662f482be9e6e7969910d83bc3ce22e57eaf9f1d75367d**, also recorded in [upstream.json](upstream.json). The verifier counts records starting at 1 in file order: records **38** and **52** give sizes 18 and 21, respectively. The checksum and two record locators are included; the original catalogue file and its generators are not redistributed. The replay output gives unnormalized magnitudes for six variables, to be divided by 64. It recomputes transforms for all records and checks that the stabilizer-derived orbit sizes sum to \(2^{57}\), the number of cosets, as an extra consistency check. It relies on the cited classification for completeness and disjointness; the sum is not itself a proof of either condition.

3. **Pierrick Méaux, *Extended Resilience and Walsh-Support Constructions of Boolean Functions***, [author preprint and abstract](https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/68891), 2026. The abstract describes related constructions from local truth-table changes and an EA-invariant notion of resilience. This paper is not an input to any proof here.

### Prior-art check

The literature check used the cited primary preprint, the journal bibliographic record and abstract, and searches through 27 November 2026, targeting the period after the 28 April revision. Queries included **“Jauberty” “Méaux” “Walsh support” “cardinality 21”**, **“Walsh supports” “unique shape” “21” Boolean**, **“On the Cardinality of the Walsh Support of a Boolean Function” “21”** (including arXiv, ePrint and ORBilu domain filters), and **“Walsh support” “largest cardinalities” affine**, as well as name, title, and DOI lookups. No matching all-dimensional uniqueness plus universal nonuniqueness above 21 result was located. Because the journal full text was not accessible from this check, the exact scope claim is that this package proves the question as explicitly posed in the linked April 2026 version; it does not claim priority over any inaccessible text. The construction and finite certificate do not address the separate question in JM §8 about signs of spectra, or extensions to vectorial or nonbinary functions.
