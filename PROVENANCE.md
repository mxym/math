# Provenance, comparison and credit

* The [first fixed eight-point proof and author checker](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/0706c1cce4601412e8c9e9c2ffb4f4066a11cde8/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/KissingSharpEvenEightPointEnergy.md) were developed in the repository’s AI-assisted geometric research. The [clarified proof](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/652b474f5b2ae5b65bbb2d81d2013867145d9755/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/KissingSharpEvenEightPointEnergy.md) explicitly spells out the full-support induction; this package uses those bytes, with the unchanged original numerical discovery/checking code.
* A separate AI-assisted Factor reviewer, not the one developing the geometric proof, reviewed the analytic proof, performed a different complete graph enumeration and wrote the independent exact box verifier. The adapter in this package only exports the decisions of the original search. It is **not** a second independent discovery of the witness trees and is not represented as journal peer review.
* The size and form of the triangle-free Ramsey graph census are classical; see [Brendan McKay’s Ramsey graph catalogue](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/ramsey.html). This package does not claim discovery of those graph types.
* Probabilistic energies on spheres and fourth frame moments are standard. Compare [Ehler–Okoudjou on probabilistic frame potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0140) and [weighted spherical-code energy bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07457). Here a specified sign-changing combination of the fourth and second powers is minimized over the weights **before** maximizing over all cardinality-eight codes of minimum angle 60°. The cited general frameworks and the pre-existing graph census are not themselves the claimed eight-point max–min computation.

The packed UTF-8 witness transcript is 235,094 bytes after decompression, SHA-256 `5652e646f8cddc56e010cc04844a0bf4e27b62b7821f736992c26a280f3ef136`. It is newline-delimited: each `AUD` line carries a JSON event (case start, closed split or integer leaf). Its coordinate ordering and full-cover tests are independently reconstructed by `independent-even-eight-boxes.py`. Only this finite certificate and the proof of the small extremal problem are included, not any larger open-case search.
