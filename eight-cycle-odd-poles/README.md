# Exact eight-cycle quantum max-flow in dimensions 3 modulo 4

**Yongxian Zhang.** AI-assisted proof development and internal checks; no external peer review claimed.

Let q=2r+1 be odd. Write F8(q) for the alternating maximum rank of the eight-cycle in the single-shared-tensor periodic model of [Gesmundo–Landsberg–Walter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09106), with both dimensions equal to q over C. The result is F8(q)=q^4-1 for q=3 mod 4, and F8(q)≥q^4-2 for q=1 mod 4 (the last two dimensions in the latter class are not settled here). q=1 trivial, and for r≤2 the respective lower bounds already follow from the old r bound. No even q claim.

## Model, skew coordinates and base

Use the [second-jet spoke deformation](../eight-cycle-second-jet/README.md) and add to A0 the term t(h_k E_{k,k}+h_{-k}E_{-k,-k}), for a single marked nonself class k={k,-k}. Call this a marked perturbation; old perturbations are the A0 return tilt and class-preserving nonzero leaf loops. The un-relabelled M(a,b)=tr(A_{a0}A_{b0}...A_{a3}A_{b3}) has M(b,a)=M(a,ρb), with ρ rotating word positions, and it commutes with ρ. On the -1 eigenspace the induced bilinear form is skew. Use orbit basis sum_s(-1)^s e_{ρ^s w} for each even orbit represented by w; bilinear entries are orbit length times the representative evaluation on the row side. Unweighted representative evaluation is thus equivalent by a fixed invertible row diagonal. The computational sign-relabeling b→-b is a further invertible column permutation with signs depending on choice of orbit representatives, and is undone conceptually when using Pfaffians.

At t=0, in sign-relabelled columns matrix entries preserve every signed word multiset, by the base hub dimers and zero monomers. In un-relabelled convention a multiset pairs with its negative; choose an invertible skew principal complementary block H0 using all full-rank pieces, and an invertible rank-2 minor in each two-zero E_i block of type {0,0,i,-i}. Each such block has 3 antirotation coordinates with positive sort representative (0,0,i,-i), (0,0,-i,i), (0,i,0,-i) in a local ordering i<-i; its exact left/right kernel vector can be taken (-c_{-i}^2,c_i^2,0). This follows directly from the base, or from c8_cubic_symbolic_first.py. Take a principal complement by omitting the first coordinate; the coefficient on that omitted coordinate in the displayed kernel vector is nonzero for generic c. Its sign-relabelled right complement omits the negative representative. This is the modular script's pivot selection in the two-zero blocks; changing omitted-row normalization multiplies residual row/col by c-dependent nonzero factors only. All other base null coordinates in the odd -1 sector are the balanced zero-free multisets K_{i,j} (i≠j, six antirotation coordinates) or K_{i,i} (two). These base rank statements are the old signed-multiset certificates, and E_i rank exactly 2 additionally follows by its universal symbolic computation. Thus the initial Schur matrix S(t) formed after H0 has rows/columns on K0 and E. If unused E coordinates are omitted, entries on kept coordinates are unchanged because the inverse of H0 is unchanged.

## Matrix support through degree three

For a word w let n(w) count its nonzero letters by absolute inverse class. Every nonzero trace walk of degree at most 3 visits the hub, because a walk wholly at a leaf needs eight perturbations. Hub monomers are zero. A hub excursion uses one fixed inverse class a, with two endpoint spoke positions and some intervening leaf loops. All its nonzero labels have class a. Its alternating position sum is zero for even length and has magnitude 1 for odd length. Removing zero labels inside it costs at most one per marked leaf zero loop and one per tilted zero return. This bounds the difference in class a between row and column in magnitude by (#old perturbations of class a)+2(#marked perturbations of class a). An ordinary dimer contributes zero. Also number of nonzero letters plus number of old perturbations is even in a closed walk: every ordinary nonzero spoke toggles hub/leaf parity, each old perturbation reverses this prescription and the marked zero leaf loop does not. These budgets survive H0 elimination: formal inverse expansion gives chains of positive-degree transition matrices separated by H0^-1, which preserves absolute counts (even though un-relabelled signed multisets negate); chain counts telescope and sum the budgets. The two endpoint parities also telescope.

The residual S(0)=0 and S1=0. Potential order-one old terms between K0/E have odd number of old perturbations and even endpoint counts, hence vanish. The marked loop cannot connect distinct K0 blocks, as their counts differ outside at least two classes, and has no direct effect between two zero-free words. For E_i to E_j with i≠j the class budget excludes one loop, and E_i to itself is scalar skew. The remaining marked couplings E_i to K_{i,k}, including i=k, vanish exactly: c8_cubic_symbolic_first.py verifies both universal orbit cases over Q with independent coefficients. The off-block Schur subtraction has no order-one term on true base kernel coordinates. The symbolic scripts use un-relabelled columns; although the repeated two-letter orbit may have size 2, testing a zero coupling is unaffected by the row factor.

Write H2=S2[K0,K0]. It is block diagonal by K_{i,j}. For both endpoints even, degree two admits either two old perturbations or two marked loops. Two old cannot span the count distance ≥4 between distinct balanced multisets, and two marked loops affect only one class, whereas distinct zero-free multisets with equal total count differ in at least two classes. Diagonal block H2_{i,j} depends only on i,j: for two old perturbations an extra virtual class a would require one to enter and one to leave from an all-nonzero endpoint, increasing its positive count without decreasing other counts and thus exceeding length 4. The same argument for two marked loops applies when k is outside i,j. Thus H2_{i,j} for unmarked pair is unchanged by the marker and homogeneous of total degree 2 in its own old perturbation parameters. Repeated-block statement is identical with a single class (the same zero-free count argument). Each block is generically invertible: set h=0 then use prior second-jet certificates. A zero-free K0 block and E_i can at second order interact only if block contains i. Indeed two old perturbations can span only distance ≤2, and two marked loops can change only class k and therefore cannot remove i unless i=k, in which case any nonzero count outside k would be impossible. Their coupling depends only on the block's classes: two old perturbations have their entire budget consumed by adding/deleting two of one class, while two marked loops can only change an absolute class already belonging to the block. (The unmarked block special case reduces immediately to the old coupling.) E_i/E_j distinct have no S2 coupling directly, by the same class budget. Restrict finally to unmarked E coordinates i≠k.

Now eliminate K0 using the invertible Laurent-series block t²(H2+O(t)). The resulting skew matrix W(t) has coefficient at t² precisely W2=S2[E,E]-S2[E,K0]H2^-1S2[K0,E]. For distinct unmarked i,j only the shared block K_{i,j} can contribute, and it is unchanged by the marker. Verify W2_ij=0 by the old universal block identity: c8_cubic_symbolic_second.py calculates over the field Q(c_±i,c_±j,u_±i,u_±j,d_±i,d_±j,e_±i,e_±j), enumerating all direct order-two traces and all common one-zero first-order intermediates (delete one letter from a zero-free K_{i,j} word and add zero). It evaluates exactly zero the Pfaffian of the augmented 8×8 skew matrix on the six K_{i,j} coordinates and two E kernels, where the E×E block is zero by class budget. The six-dimensional block is generically invertible by prior work, so the Pfaffian Schur identity proves the claimed W2 zero. Diagonal vanishes by skew. The code treats all relevant antirotation orbits of size four so representative evaluation alone yields a skew matrix. The special certificate uses no modular inference to prove a polynomial identity.

## Cubic locality

The cubic W3 entry for i,j equals the pairing in S3 of dressed residual row/column coordinates from E_i,E_j, with added K0 parts -H2^-1 S2[K0,E] and its dual. In this expression K0 blocks on i's side have form K_{i,a}, and on j's side K_{j,b}. The dressings are class-local as just shown. Assume i≠j and both unmarked. Degree three permits either (a) one marked loop plus two old perturbations, or (b) three marked loops. For a transition among endpoint supports E_i/K_{i,a} and E_j/K_{j,b}, an a outside {i,j,k} requires ≥4 total absolute-count difference outside k (even when b=a or b=i), which is impossible with only two old perturbations; likewise for b. Thus all endpoints relevant to W3 use only {i,j,k}. Could a virtual class l outside {i,j,k} occur within an S3 Schur chain? Introducing and deleting l would consume both old perturbations. Then outside k all original endpoint counts agree. Inspecting E_i/K_{i,a} versus E_j/K_{j,b} shows the only possibility is both equal K_{i,j}, which has four nonzero letters and no k. A single marked loop cannot introduce and later remove k in the chain, so every intermediate has no k if a virtual l is present. But the marked loop cannot occur without some class k label: any visiting excursion must start with an ordinary outgoing k spoke (there are no outgoing zero tilts). This excludes l, also for a direct closed walk in which no intermediate word is used. For case (b) all endpoints and steps agree outside k, again forcing K_{i,j} at both boundaries; from four letters already outside k no intermediate can acquire k. Thus case (b) contributes zero. Therefore W3_ij depends only on parameters of {i,j,k}; the only possible poles are base constants and leading block inverses for these classes (repeated or mixed). No dependence on the numerical q label arithmetic remains: every walk rule references only zero, a sign, and adjacency order. Row/col scalar normalizations can be picked consistently within the class basis and do not affect poles/rank.

## Exact local pole, characteristic-zero transfer

Take q=7 with unmarked i=1,j=2 and marker k=3. The scripts c8_cubic_jet.py and c8_cubic_pole.py give a reproducible modular calculation at p=65521: first eliminate the base complementary minor, then use the cubic expression with the H2 inverse, all calculated by exact formal arithmetic. Fix the integer arrays in c8_cubic_jet.py and scale all old perturbations of class i by x. The scalar W3_ij (in script coordinates, differing from skew normalization by a nonzero c-only scalar and a possible sign) is
(43196 x^6 + 59992 x^4 + 62561 x² + 44472)/(43012 x^4 + 20173 x² + 13139).
It has a simple pole at x0=12833: numerator evaluates to 26517, denominator has simple zero with reduced denominator 57464. Only H2_{i,j} is singular there, with determinant order 2; all other blocks and base complements are nonsingular. Factor a fixed row diagonal and signed permutation to make the block skew; its Pfaffian P therefore has a simple root. Those diagonals/permutations and the base minor do not depend on x.

For precise transfer, do the same rational construction with the specified integer array over Q_p. The base complements have unit denominators. Hensel lifts the simple root of P to x_* in Q_p. The other block inverses have unit denominators at x_*. A skew matrix inverse has only a first-order Pfaffian denominator (entries are signed complementary Pfaffians divided by its Pfaffian). The cubic pairing uses at most two H2_{i,j} inverses, so P² W3_ij is analytic and p-integral at x_*. Its reduction has a simple zero, because W3_ij has a simple pole and P a simple zero. Hence its derivative at x_* is a unit, and W3_ij really has a pole in characteristic zero (order at least one). All these conditions persist in sufficiently small p-adic neighborhoods on the root locus by the implicit function theorem, or by the same Hensel computation modulo p with varying integer parameter arrays. Divide old variables into nonzero scale factors and arbitrary other class variables locally. Homogeneity of H2_{i,j}, unchanged by any external marker, gives (D_i+D_j)P=6P for the Euler scale derivatives, so D_iP≠0 at the root implies D_jP≠0. Thus the local hypersurface P=0 projects onto a p-adic open neighborhood in each individual full class parameter space (solve implicitly in the other class's scale). Moreover the c coordinates in both classes are independently free locally, since the implicit correction uses an old variable and no c.

## Independent poles give rank

Select any even-sized set U of unmarked classes and pair its labels in a perfect matching. For each matching edge independently choose its two class parameter arrays on the local pole locus above, with their roles assigned as i,j and the same marked class k parameters as in the modular certificate. Use the c variables generic enough for all base complement conditions, possible because c variables are independently free jointly. All other mixed H2 blocks can be chosen invertible at these simultaneous poles. For a pair crossing matching edges, the projections of the two independent local pole choices onto the selected endpoint parameter spaces are open, so a nonzero polynomial condition cannot vanish identically; use the generic nonzero mixed determinant furnished by prior second-jet certs. Blocks incident to k (and both repeated blocks) are already units in the local pole experiment and remain such; excluded unmarked class data can be chosen generically. Only finitely many such nonvanishing conditions are needed. Each selected matching edge keeps its genuine pole; no nonmatching W3 entry with endpoints in U can use a selected pole, because of triple locality and exclusion of k. Deform independently transverse to all selected pair hypersurfaces by changing the first endpoint's old scale in each pair. In the Pfaffian on U, the matching term alone has the product of the leading negative powers of all selected transverse coordinates. Every other matching term misses at least one and is regular in that coordinate. The coefficient cannot cancel. This shows the selected W3 Pfaffian is a nonzero rational function over Q_p, hence also over C (all formulas rational). It follows W(t) on U reaches full rank generically for some t, because W= t³ W3+O(t⁴). The old all-other-sector minor rank is simultaneously attainable by polynomial nonvanishing (take h=0 to verify those minors, then intersect the open conditions). 

The previous r residual directions lose all but at most 1 if r odd (take all r-1 unmarked), and at most 2 if r even (take r-2). If q=3 mod 4, the dimension of the -1 rotation sector is (q^4+q²-2q)/4, odd by reducing the numerator mod 8. It is skew for every tensor, so defect ≥1. Combined, this gives the exact q^4-1 (including q=3 from the earlier bound). For q=1 mod 4 parity yields no positive lower bound and at most two remain. For even q this note makes no improvement on the previous bounds. The small cases q=3,7,11 were already verified separately; the theorem fixes the complete odd congruence class, not every dimension.


## Reproduction

Run `python3 c8_cubic_symbolic_first.py` and `python3 c8_cubic_symbolic_second.py` for the identities over rational function fields. Then run `for x in 0 1 2 3; do OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 c8_cubic_jet.py "$x"; done` followed by `python3 c8_cubic_pole.py`. These two latter scripts use exact arithmetic modulo 65521. The recorded cubic coefficients are polynomials of degree at most two in the old perturbation scale before the second elimination, so the finite interpolation in the script is exact. The Hensel and Pfaffian arguments above, rather than repeated numerical tests in different dimensions, prove the uniform result. Run the four scripts in a scratch directory. The symbolic scripts require SymPy; the modular scripts also require NumPy.
