# The two odd-parity states in Fateev–Zamolodchikov Conjecture 4.8

[Hagendorf, §4.1.6 and Conjecture 4.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.0357#page=35) considers odd \(N\) at the crossing \(\pi/4\) and original twist \(\pi\). We prove the two additional odd magnetic-parity zero states for every real \(\zeta\in[-1,1]\) in the local algebraic parametrization of \(q^+\). This is exactly two in original translation eigenvalue \(-1\), not an assertion about other odd-parity momenta. Where the elliptic parameter is taken in the physical parametrization of the source, the same assertion follows by its stated staggering equivalence. At \(\zeta=0\) the two states have magnetizations \(+1,-1\).

Write \(p=(|0\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2\), \(m=(|0\rangle-|2\rangle)/\sqrt2\), \(h=|1\rangle\), and \(c=1/\sqrt2\), \(a=\sqrt{1-\zeta}\), \(b=\sqrt{1+\zeta}\). Magnetic parity negates \(p,m\) and fixes \(h\). The source's coefficients of \(q^+\) with \(b_2=b_5=0\) give in the dual basis the projected products \(hp=cp,\ hm=-cm,\ p^2=ah,\ m^2=bh,\ pm=h^2=0\). For example \(b_1+b_4=a,\ b_1-b_4=b\) by the displayed parameters in that section. Extend to a commutative associative algebra \(A=\mathbb C1\oplus\langle h,p,m\rangle\) by
\[
h^2=c^2,\qquad p^2=a(h+c),\qquad m^2=b(h-c)
\]
and keeping the other products above. Associativity is manifest: \((1\pm h/c)/2\) split two two-dimensional algebras generated by \(p\) or \(m\), respectively, and their cross products vanish. Each is a separable quadratic algebra if its square coefficient is nonzero, a dual-number algebra otherwise.

Apply the [cyclic normalization argument](CYCLIC-NORMALIZATION.md) at zero twist in the odd parity summand, where the all-scalar subcomplex is absent. Cyclic homology adds over a finite product, see [Loday, Chapter 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-11389-9_2). A separable quadratic summand is \(\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C\), with one odd parity cyclic class in each even degree, because negating its generator exchanges the two idempotents. It has none in odd degrees. When \(a\) or \(b\) vanishes, that summand is \(\mathbb C[z]/z^2\), with \(z\) odd; the untwisted [truncated algebra calculation](TRIGONOMETRIC.md#hochschild-calculation-and-cyclic-reduction) with \(d=2\) gives one positive-weight class of weight \(2j+1\) in degree \(2j\) and no others. The scalar class is even. Hence also at either endpoint there are two odd classes in each even degree and none in odd degrees.

As in §4.1.6 let \(G\) be total magnetic parity and \(U=\prod_j e^{i\pi j s_j^3}\). At odd \(N\) it sends the original \(\pi\)-twisted local Hamiltonian to the periodic one of \(q^+\). For right shift, applying the phases to individual letters gives \(T_0U=UG^{-1}T_\pi\): for a letter shifted from \(j<N\), the extra phase from \(j\to j+1\) is canceled by \(G^{-1}\), and for the wrapped letter the seam supplies the phase at odd \(N\). Thus periodic \(T_0=+1\) and parity \(-1\) correspond exactly to original \(T_\pi=-1\). In that sector the kernel equals the Hodge kernel, proving the asserted two-dimensional result. The physical density agrees with the source by its staggered conjugation. If \(N=1\) is read as a length in the complex, the count is the cohomology count; the local two-site Hamiltonian description concerns rings with at least two sites.

At \(\zeta=0\), \(q^+\) is proportional to the sum of the spin-one trigonometric supercharge and its spin reverse: after normalizing \(q|1\rangle=|00\rangle\), their coefficients on the extreme letter are \(1/\sqrt2\). Hagendorf's relation (12) makes their mixed anticommutators zero and their two Hamiltonians equal. The magnitudes in the [trigonometric count](TRIGONOMETRIC.md) therefore identify the odd classes as \(M=\pm1\), unchanged by the diagonal staggering.
