## No quadratic sharp scalar Bellman germ; forced second-order oscillation

The [new rigorous 005 note](../../notes/projection-bellman-quadratic-oscillation/README.md) proves an **explicit lower oscillation bound** for any normalized homogeneous scalar Bellman potential that would certify the binary `T5` rate by **separate join/product closure**: `liminf |psi(t)−alpha*t²|/t²=0` but `limsup >1/4000`, with exact `alpha=8/81*(c*+log(27/(4*pi))/2)`. Therefore **not even C² regularity is possible at zero**—a stronger structural restriction than polynomial, analytic or smooth-germ tests. The [full analytic proof](../../notes/projection-bellman-quadratic-oscillation/paper.md) uses two actual infinite construction families, and [independent exact rational arithmetic](../../notes/projection-bellman-quadratic-oscillation/code/check.py). The same paper demonstrates that a particularly natural continuous nonanalytic Gamma-interpolation that matches the **entire T5 orbit** still fails product closure on an actual eight-dimensional body, strictly by `>1/20000`, with independent rational and Arb replay. This rules out a **proof architecture and a candidate potential**, not the possibility that the binary T5 orbit itself is the true optimum of the full point-generated class.
