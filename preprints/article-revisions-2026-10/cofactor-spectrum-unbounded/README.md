# Unbounded normalized cofactor spectra for rank-two Gram matrices

Yongxian Zhang (张永贤), South China University of Technology.

[PDF](paper.pdf) · [Self-contained TeX ZIP](paper-source.zip) · [Original proof and certificates](../../../notes/cofactor-spectrum-unbounded/README.md)

The theorem excludes dimension-independent cofactor-ratio bounds. It does not assert a growth rate or solve Lieb's permanental-dominance conjecture. Positive-definite examples need not remain rank two. No Lean formalization is claimed.
