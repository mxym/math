# Unpublished manuscript revision

This directory is a working revision. It is not a new submission, Release, DOI,
or replacement for the frozen October manuscript and its verification artifacts.
The baseline eight TeX sources were copied without modifying the originals.

The revision replaces the universal construction with intrinsic matrix-orbit
projections and a geometric Bell-packing grid, and replaces the support-moment
argument with literal Haar minor moments and an operator lower bound. Finite
round and closure LOCC semantics, genuine physical capacity suprema, fixed-error
integer targets, Berry--Esseen bounds, shrinking-variance conditions, and the
actual original-register circuit are connected in separate Lean extensions.

All new extensions currently have ordinary build evidence only. The independent
completion audit is PRELIMINARY and is being performed on an explicitly named
mathematical snapshot. No final full-paper machine-verification claim is made.

Outstanding integration includes the physical general-rank minimax endpoints,
the explicit APPT example and asymptotics, theorem-to-source correspondence,
final independent replay/controls/CI, bibliography and visual PDF verification.
Classical finite-precision bit complexity remains a separate unproved endpoint;
finite-library word existence and gate-count bounds must not be presented as an
effective classical synthesis algorithm.
