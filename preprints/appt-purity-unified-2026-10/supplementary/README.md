# Higher-dimensional proof sources

The current theorem statements and complete typeset proofs are in `../main.tex`
and `../higher-dimensional-proofs.tex`. These notes preserve the research
development from which those appendices were assembled. Statements that a
manuscript was not being prepared, or that a leading constant remained open,
describe the checkpoint at which the individual note was written.

The final sources are `PROOF.md` (counterexample), `SHARP_ASYMPTOTIC.md`
(unrestricted all-regime equivalent), and `EXACT_RECTANGULAR.md` (exact finite
maximum and equality cases in the stated rectangular range). The other notes
record intermediate constructions, bounds, and the graph argument used in the
final proof. Later phase-rigidity and optimal-fixed-hierarchy research is not
part of this manuscript version.

The five `check*.py` programs use the Python standard library and exact
arithmetic. Their scope is stated in each report: finite algebra, supporting
identities and negative controls. They do not replace the analytic proofs of
all-unitary APPT or asymptotic limits. No higher-dimensional Lean certificate
is claimed.
