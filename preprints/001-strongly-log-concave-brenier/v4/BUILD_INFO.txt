Build information for manuscript 001 version 4

Typesetting: pdfTeX 3.141592653-2.6-1.40.26, TeX Live 2025/dev/Debian;
LaTeX, BibTeX with plain bibliography style, 11-point Latin Modern, US Letter.
Visual inspection: Poppler pdftoppm page renders at 120 dpi.

Run sh build.sh in the artifact directory. The LaTeX build needs no network
resources, external figures or downloaded assets. manuscript.bbl is included
alongside its editable references.bib input.

The public v3 reference is a mathematical proof dependency, not a network build
dependency. Its exact hypotheses are restated in Theorem 2.1 and its immutable
source/PDF links are in the paper and README.

SHA256SUMS identify the exact distributed snapshot. Rebuilding with different
TeX/font/package versions or PDF timestamps can change binary hashes without
changing mathematical content. A separate-directory rebuild is checked for
matching page count and extracted text before final delivery.
