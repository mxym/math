Version 2 mathematical changelog
7 October 2026

1. Extended centered-potential stability from compactly supported targets to all
   probability measures with finite second moment, with the same constant
   sqrt(2/kappa). Theorem 1.1 now states this full scope.

2. Added an elementary local convex compactness lemma and a complete target-
   truncation proof. The limiting potential is identified using integrable
   Fenchel duality; its representative on the source interior may extend as
   +infinity outside that interior. No global Lipschitz claim is made for
   unbounded targets.

3. Added absolute-continuous Wasserstein-curve lifting into centered L2(rho),
   including the derivative bound and the corresponding quadratic-action bound.

4. Strengthened the midpoint consequence to an exponential transport inequality
   for entire conditional Laguerre-cell laws. Derived its Hellinger-affinity
   logarithmic bound, its quadratic mass-difference bound, and the normalized-
   barycenter differential estimate. Proved the finite-distance inequalities for
   unbounded sources by conditional-law W2 convergence under convex truncation.

5. Separated bounded-target quantitative map stability into Theorem 1.2. The
   one-third map exponent, explicit constant, fixed-Gaussian three-atom
   sharpness, and compact log-concave companion theorem are unchanged. No map
   modulus uniform over all finite-second-moment targets is claimed.

6. Clarified generic endpoints in cell-graph connectivity and made the compact
   companion's coupling notation self-contained after restructuring Section 4.

7. Retained all attribution and the blank author field. Updated the editorial
   status: the HAL full-text comparison remains unfinished, without making it
   a prerequisite of the mathematical statements. No priority claim, formal-
   verification claim, or external-peer-review claim is made.

Proof status
The new completion, conditional-law, and curve-lifting proofs were independently
checked algebraically and analytically during revision. No gap was found in that
check. This is ordinary mathematical review and cannot guarantee absence of all
errors. It is not formal verification or external peer review.
