Mathematical changelog from manuscript 001 v4 to v5
7 October 2026

1. Unified the article around source regularity and target-moment stability.
   Retained complete proofs of v4's sharp Gaussian logarithmic exponent,
   full-support bounded-Hessian extension, Gaussian endpoint obstruction,
   three-atom sharpness, and critical finite-q constant order.

2. Extended the minimum-density lemma to arbitrary nonnegative probability
   densities and proper convex potentials finite almost everywhere under the
   source, with explicit legitimate-endpoint and zero-density conventions.

3. Proved the optimal coefficient 7 in the universal translation-mean bound,
   and recovered BV finite-q, logarithmic and stretched-exponential upper
   bounds. These upper conclusions are explicitly credited to manuscript 007;
   the synthesis is not represented as a separate discovery or a uniformly
   stronger tail bound.

4. Identified the exact L1 limit r W_j,h / h = 6|partial_j r| +
   2(-partial_j r)_+ for W1,1 densities. Derived uniform little-o refinements
   of the fixed-source finite-q and stretched-exponential rates. The finite-q
   statement excludes q=infinity, with an explicit interpolation obstruction.

5. Included a full proof of the generalized-Fisher condition r^(1/p) in W1,p,
   p=q/(q-2), for pure one-third interpolation and conditional transport, also
   established in manuscript 008 v1. This shared criterion is counted once.
   Compared it rigorously with manuscript 007's raw translation-ratio
   criterion and proved strict separation using exp(-exp(x^2)).

6. Constructed one smooth full-support 1-strongly log-concave source using
   doubly exponential slope jumps. It simultaneously gives all finite-q
   obstructions, exact logarithmic-second-moment sequence scale, sharp
   stretched-exponential logarithmic power, and the q=2 no-modulus endpoint.
   Delaying the first layer makes the source arbitrarily close to Gaussian in
   total variation and forward relative entropy. The finite-q and stretched-
   exponential endpoint ratios tend to zero; the logarithmic-second-moment
   sequence alone has a strictly positive endpoint limit.

7. Reconciled the overlap with public manuscript 007 v2. Its smooth finite-q
   and q=2 result is not counted again. Logarithmic-second-moment sharpness and
   Gaussian proximity also adapt its geometry; the stronger iterated-logarithm
   scale separation adds the stretched-exponential obstruction. Its positive
   superquadratic and translation-ratio results are credited. All needed new
   proofs are supplied in this manuscript rather than imported from 007.
   Cross-credited manuscript 008 for bounded-overlap/root interpolation and
   the same Fisher criterion. Its boundary phase diagram is not a proof input.
   Fixed the 007 bibliography to distinguish the first extension source from
   the later complete source/PDF snapshot.

8. Retained the exact public-v3 all-P2 potential theorem as the explicit
   quantitative input. No potential theorem is inferred for arbitrary BV or
   Sobolev sources. Blank authorship and non-peer-reviewed/nonformal status
   remain explicit. Historical public versions are unchanged.
