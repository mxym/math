Version 3 mathematical changelog
7 October 2026

Updated title: Sharp Brenier stability under target moment bounds.
Versions 1 and 2 retain their original titles and files byte-for-byte.

1. Extended the compact log-concave companion's centered-potential estimate to
   every P2 target with A=(1+sqrt(162))R, and extended its Hilbert curve-lifting
   derivative and action bounds.

2. Proved a coordinate-clipped derivative interpolation inequality without a
   global Lipschitz assumption. The clipped field is never assumed optimal or
   a convex gradient. Derived explicit coordinate and radial target-tail bounds.

3. Obtained an explicit modulus for uniformly integrable second-moment classes
   and the exponent (q-2)/(3q-2) for each q>2, with constants, zero cases, and
   common-center versions. Qualitative uniform continuity from compactness is
   acknowledged as a classical consequence, without a novelty claim.

4. Added matching finite-q sharpness for a fixed cube-truncated Gaussian in d>=2,
   using exactly two atoms and unit q-th moment. Included strip-mass matching,
   c_a=a+O(a^2), exact coupling/map identities, and their positive asymptotics.

5. Added the fixed full-Gaussian q=2 obstruction, with two target atoms, second
   moments exactly one, W2 tending to zero, and an explicit positive lower bound
   on the map distance. Added the exact uniform-cube endpoint identities and its
   finite-q rare-atom rescaling.

6. Proved the full-standard-Gaussian improvement: sharp exponent one-third on
   every fixed q>2 moment class, with constant sqrt(8d(3+C_q)). The proof controls
   min(r,r_shift), proves absolute integrability before signed translation,
   handles the remaining weight, and uses the existing bounded three-atom
   family for sharpness in d>=2.

7. Extended the positive one-third theorem to full-support kappa-strongly convex
   C1,1 potentials with globally Lambda-Lipschitz gradient, with an explicit
   constant uniform for fixed d,kappa,Lambda,q. Its expectation explicitly uses
   E(H^p). Gaussian inclusion proves class-level sharpness; no claim is made for
   every individual non-Gaussian source. Hard boundaries are excluded here.

8. Preserved bounded-target one-third stability, exponential conditional-cell
   inequalities, all-P2 completion, Gaussian bounded-target sharpness, and the
   full inherited proof framework with attribution.

9. Expanded focused attribution to Delalande-Merigot's bounded-gradient
   interpolation and finite-moment targets, and Letrouit-Merigot's explicit
   bounded-target scope. Retained the blank author field, unfinished HAL
   comparison, and nonformal/non-peer-reviewed status.
