artifacts. Ordinary author builds are distinguished from independent replay and
downloaded-artifact checks. The historical manuscripts and audit scopes retain
their own unchanged sources and evidence.

Uniform classical compiler bit running time remains outside the stated theorem.
Other exclusions include the exact APPT entropy optimizer or maximum-capacity
leading coefficient, growing-D sharp inverse-Haar asymptotics, and unstated
reliability or Gaussian boundary cases. Internal AI/kernel review is distinct
from external human peer review and novelty assessment.
