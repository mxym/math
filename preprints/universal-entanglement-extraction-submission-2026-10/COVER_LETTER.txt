Editors, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory

Please consider the manuscript “The finite-block cost of universality in
entanglement extraction” by Yongxian Zhang as a research article.

The paper studies a global unitary on an existing many-copy register followed
by deterministic local, LOCC, or completely PPT channels. The global unitary
is an explicit entangling resource; the task is distinguished from ordinary
fixed-state LOCC distillation.

Its central contribution is a finite-dimensional support-versus-average method
that determines the sharp minimax fidelity profile of unknown rank-bounded iid
inputs, relatively and uniformly over every target dimension. A common-support
code matches a Haar-average converse because the support dimension and the
reciprocal purity of that average have the same leading asymptotic. This gives
an explicit logarithmic universality loss and a transition at the local cap,
which equality of first-order capacities cannot describe.

The manuscript also derives one actual all-state universal protocol with exact
fidelity and strict-direct reliability exponents, actual capacities and integer
target asymptotics, finite Gaussian estimates with visible dimension dependence,
and an original-register pure-input circuit. For the latter, an actual finite-
label compiler has proved word-length and uniform output-fidelity guarantees;
we do not claim a complete classical bit-time bound.

The main mathematical completion has a separate exact-source Lean audit with
518 theorem endpoints, 175 concrete model definitions, a complete 72,820-
declaration empty-kernel replay and 84 strict proof-forgery controls. The new
effective compiler has its own scope and concrete model checks; the accompanying
evidence records the final verification state. Exact named types and hypotheses
are distinguished from automatic formalization of prose or a novelty guarantee.

This integrated manuscript consolidates two cited repository extraction
preprints, which are preserved. The introduction explains the differences from
previous compression, concentration, coding and distillation work. The package
includes full proofs, source correspondence, pinned formal sources and reproducible
verification records. Substantive AI assistance is disclosed by system, sections
and tasks; internal AI review is not presented as external human peer review.

The sole author received no external funding.
