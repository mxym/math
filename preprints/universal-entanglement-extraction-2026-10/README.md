# The finite-block cost of universality in entanglement extraction

Yongxian Zhang · October 2026 · complete integrated submission manuscript.

[Read the 21-page manuscript](paper.pdf). Sources: `paper.tex` and seven included
section files. The intended venue is **IEEE Transactions on Information Theory**;
this is a submission package, not a claim of acceptance or an external submission.

The paper joins the state-aware spectral method and state-independent Schur
protocol with a sharp unknown-support minimax theorem. Its common-support and
Haar-average comparison determines the entire fidelity profile, uniformly over
every target dimension, with leading constant one. It identifies the explicit
logarithmic target-size loss and gives an optimal integer Bell-pair application
with an effective circuit for unknown pure two-qubit inputs.

The full paper has ordinary mathematical proofs and an independent **internal AI**
written-proof review. The actual finite-dimensional matrix/channel core is Lean
verified in two separately scoped packages. Schur--Weyl and Haar identities,
asymptotic probability, minimax limit synthesis and circuit complexity remain
ordinary mathematics. No end-to-end Lean claim is made.
