# Sources and scope

- Alexandr Valyuzhenich, [*Optimal functions with spectral constraints in hypercubes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10995), [arXiv:2303.10995v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.10995v1), 20 March 2023; *Discrete Mathematics* 347(3), 113816 (2024), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2023.113816). The question anchor is the publicly checked preprint §6, Problem 5 (following Corollary 2), not an assertion about the publisher's pagination. It asks for the minimum support of real functions with exact spectrum {0,3}, for each n≥3. Its own Corollary 2 identifies possible spectra of interval-bound extremizers and concludes only that the support exceeds 2^(n−3) for {0,3}. Its Corollary 1(2) explicitly gives the equality form for one level at or below n/2; Theorem 2(2) at lower interval endpoint 0 characterizes all minimum-support real functions of bounded degree. Both are prior results: our Section 2 gives a short derivation in the needed range, not a new theorem submission on them. Original notation U_i(n) and Spec denote adjacency levels and coincide with the Walsh level numbers.
- Alexandr Valyuzhenich, [*Eigenfunctions and minimum 1-perfect bitrades in the Hamming graph*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2020.112228), *Discrete Mathematics* 344(3), 112228 (2021). This is the support-bound antecedent cited as reference 18 of the source; interval bounds alone do not give the exact separated opposite-parity minimum.
- Alexandr Valyuzhenich and Konstantin Vorob'ev, [*Minimum supports of functions on the Hamming graphs with spectral constraints*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09139), *Discrete Mathematics* 342(5), 1351–1360 (2019). A related interval-eigenspace problem, not the exact two-level problem, as distinguished in the source paper.

The two-level result is an elementary use of antipodal symmetry and existing one-level rigidity, with the tensor argument identifying all minimizers. We do not claim a solution for every prescribed spectrum. We searched the original title and identifier with “Problem 5”, “{0,3}”, “opposite parity”, and “two levels” and read the cited primary paper as well as its relevant references (bounded search 2026-11-27). We did not find the exact opposite-parity two-level statement with all extremizers in those works; this is not a priority claim.
