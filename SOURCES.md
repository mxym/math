# Primary sources and scope

1. **Emanuele Bellini, *Testing CCZ-equivalence to permutations via projections of Walsh zero spaces, with applications to APN functions*.** [arXiv:2611.10269v1 (6 November 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10269v1), [full author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10269v1). The target is Conjecture 14, §7.1. Its \(3\nmid m\) assertion is that there is one spread line regardless of the cube status of \(a\), and whether the line equals \(a\mathbb F_{2^m}\) distinguishes the two cases. This interpretation also follows from the noncube rows of Table 2 at \(m=5,7,11\).
   - §6.1 Proposition 7 gives the projection-dimension condition for a pair of complementary Walsh-zero spaces. §6.2 Theorem 8(3) gives the bilinear fiber criterion applied in §4 of this proof.
   - §7 Lemma 10 supplies parameter equivalences, not needed in the proof for arbitrary \(a\). §7 Proposition 11 already proves that \(a\mathbb F_{2^m}\) is a non-bent component space for \(a\in C\). §7.1 Table 2 and Corollary 12 settle low dimensions with computations or earlier results. Lemma 13 calculates the cubic residue count in the orthogonal of a hyperplane in a spread line. None of the computations, catalogue files or algorithms from this paper are needed as inputs here.
   - The further proof that all maximal component subspaces are spread lines, the complete classification of those lines, and the uniform no-lift argument are the contributions of this package.

2. **Lilya Budaghyan, Ivana Ivkovic and Nikolay Kaleyski, *Triplicate functions*.** *Cryptography and Communications* 15 (2023), 35–83, [DOI and open-access article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-022-00587-2). Theorem 1(2) gives APN for quadratic 3-to-1 canonical triplicates. Theorem 2 and Corollary 12, equation (8), give the signed Gold-like spectrum at Fourier coordinate zero, with sign depending on \(m\). Lemma 5 gives character orthogonality, and the proof of Theorem 7, equations (9)–(10), uses the same starting double sum for even \(m\). We use those inputs, and take the extremal odd-\(m\) upper bound and equality condition \(B^\perp\subseteq\operatorname{Im}F_a\) in §3. The earlier result excluding permutations for all quadratic 3-to-1 functions in doubly-even dimensions is their Theorem 7; it is not claimed here.

3. **Lilya Budaghyan, Claude Carlet and Gregor Leander, *Constructing new APN functions from known ones*.** *Finite Fields and Their Applications* 15 (2009), 150–159, [DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2008.10.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2008.10.001). The switched-cube family is prior art. **Furkan Göloğlu and Ondřej Pavlů, *On CCZ-inequivalence of some families of almost perfect nonlinear functions to permutations*.** *Cryptography and Communications* 13 (2021), 377–391, [DOI 10.1007/s12095-021-00476-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-021-00476-0). Theorem 3 settles this family when \(m\) is even. Bellini's Conjecture 14 only leaves the odd-\(m\) dimensions in its stated range.

4. **Related methods, not imported theorems.** [Sziklai, *On subsets of GF(q2) with dth power differences*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X99000965) and the [maximum-clique treatment of Asgarli and Yip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01522) connect rigidity of powers to maximum cliques. The proof here gives an elementary exterior-power argument over characteristic two, including half-size binary subspaces of size \(2^{m-1}\); it does not assume a clique stability theorem. [Beierle and Carlet, *Gold functions and switched cube functions are not 0-extendable in dimension n > 5*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-022-01111-6), especially §3 Lemma 3, is related to the switched-cube ortho-derivative, but addresses extension, not CCZ permutation lifting. Our main no-lift proof is by minors of an alternating form; an explicit inverse kernel formula is used only for a numerical cross-check.

Primary-source searches through 27 November 2026 included Bellini's exact title and Conjecture 14, “switched cube CCZ permutation”, “non-bent components spread lines”, “linear subspaces cubic residues dimension m−1”, relevant generalized cubic Paley maximum-clique work, and “ortho-derivative switched cube”. The available literature located the exact older inputs above, not a proof covering the whole statement of Conjecture 14. The new finite tests are independent computations with explicitly given field arithmetic, and rerun no author's code.
