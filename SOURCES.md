# Source and scope

- Mark M. Wilde, [Second-order asymptotics for the quantum and private capacities of the erasure channel](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32395v1), arXiv:2611.32395v1, 24 November 2026. The [version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32395) listed only v1 when checked on 28 November 2026. Appendix D.5 specifically leaves the O(log n) converse remainder and the necessity of maximal-message expurgation unresolved, as distinct issues. The model is §§2.3–2.4 (including the squared-fidelity convention), Eqs. (2.12), (2.17), (2.21)–(2.25). Theorems 3.1–3.2 and Corollary 6.2 give the O((ln n)²) upper remainders and the precise Gaussian quantiles. The lower bounds are unchanged: §6.1 and Appendix C, using the stated maximal-message loss. No constant-order converse, third-order coefficient, expurgation improvement, efficient-code construction, boundary p=1/2 result, extension to other channels, or conversion to separate security and correctness criteria is claimed.
- The quantum facts used here are Wilde's Lemma 4.1 and Eq. (3.12) (flags), Lemmas 4.2–4.3 (common test space and overlaps), Eqs. (6.12)–(6.14) (actual-marginal roots) and the binomial estimates of Lemma E.1. The signed operator bound in Lemma 4.4 is credited and reproved for convenience. The change is to replace the signed weights of Lemma 5.1/Appendix B and the uniform threshold loss in Proposition 6.1: the new filter acts directly on the slice and uses its degree only as needed by each count.
- Yuval Filmus, [Orthogonal basis for functions over a slice of the Boolean hypercube](https://arxiv.org/html/1406.0142v2), arXiv:1406.0142v2, §4, Definition 4.2 and Lemma 4.3 (Johnson association scheme), supplies the classical simultaneous spectral setting. Andries E. Brouwer, Sebastian M. Cioabă, Ferdinand Ihringer and Matt McGinnis, [The smallest eigenvalues of Hamming graphs, Johnson graphs and other distance-regular graphs with classical parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09011), §3, treats the Johnson scheme eigenvalues. The proof here derives the two needed eigenvalues directly from inclusion indicators and does not require a basis formula.
- Tulja Varun Kondra et al., [Sharp Quantum Capacity Thresholds](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01308v1), Supplemental Lemmas S1/S3, and Wilde, [An exponential strong converse for private communication over degradable quantum channels](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32515v1), §4.2 Lemmas 4.1–4.2, are the earlier signed-averaging framework. Their cube weights and strong-converse statements are not this fixed-size-layer O(log n) remainder. Hao-Chung Cheng, [Quantum communication: a discrimination approach](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40358v2), §4 Eq. (78), already gives the quantum erasure Gaussian coefficient with an o(√n) remainder for the indicated low-error range (as cited in Wilde Appendix D.5). Hongyu Mao and Kun Fang, [Reliability of quantum communication over erasure channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24054v1), addresses an exponential decay regime at rates below capacity and prime input dimension, not the fixed-infidelity remainder here.
