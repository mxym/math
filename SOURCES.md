# Sources, overlap, and scope

## Exact question

Sayantan Ganguly, [*Symplectic Matroids, Stabilizer Codes, and Quantum Secret-Sharing*, arXiv:2611.27300v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27300v2), 25 November 2026, [§VI, Open Problem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.27300v2#S6), first sentence:

> Is the distance unbounded over the representations of \(\mathcal{S}(G)\) when \(G\) ranges over the subdivisions of \(3\)-connected graphs?

The second sentence asks more broadly which rates and distances can occur in characteristic two. This package answers the first sentence over the fixed field \(\mathbb F_8\); it supplies one rate–distance family, not a classification or an optimality claim.

In the same manuscript, §II Lemmas 6–7 relate admissible ranks and paired-coordinate ranks to the representation; §III-C Corollary 1 gives distance exactly two on representable 3-connected simple graphs themselves. Example 1 gives the \(K_4\) incidence–flow base over \(\mathbb F_4\); the proof here uses its corresponding form over \(\mathbb F_8\). Example 2 is a single \(K_{2,3}\) realization of distance three, not an unbounded family of subdivisions of a 3-connected graph. §III-D Proposition 2 is a distance bound **under contraction**, not an amplification or a reverse construction. §IV-C Definition 4, Theorems 6–7 and Proposition 7 already give dealer shortening, the port quotient (including its sign), and supported port logical spaces. The use of those constructions here is a special case, not a new definition.

## Related constructions and precise overlap

- Timothy Y. Chow, [*Symplectic matroids, independent sets, and signed graphs*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(02)00568-X), Discrete Mathematics 263 (2003), 35–45, Theorem 2. This is the original signed-edge construction. Ganguly's companion [*Representability of Graphical Symplectic Matroids*, arXiv:2611.01241v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01241v2), Definition 2.10, records the tree/odd-unicyclic test, and Lemma 3.1 identifies admissible column ranks with the abstract ranks. Its characteristic-two one-row incidence–flow form is in Proposition 6.1 and Theorem 6.2; §6.3 studies subdivisions as a way to extend nonrepresentability obstructions, and proves the 3-connected simple representability classification. None of those results gives distance growth for a fixed represented 3-connected core.
- Pradeep Sarvepalli, [*Quantum Codes and Symplectic Matroids*, arXiv:1104.1171v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.1171v1), §III and §III-B: isotropic representation/code correspondence, graphical examples and code operations. The resulting distance is the usual symplectic normalizer-minus-stabilizer distance, not graph girth or the shortest admissible circuit.
- ChunJun Cao and Brad Lackey, [*Quantum Lego: Building Quantum Error Correction Codes from Tensor Networks*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.020332), PRX Quantum 3, 020332 (2022), §II including Theorem II.1 and Appendix D: joining stabilizer check matrices/encoding tensors by matching operator labels; ordinary code concatenation is a special case. Wolfram Helwig, [*Absolutely Maximally Entangled Qudit Graph States*, arXiv:1306.2879](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2879), uses full-rank graph-state cross cuts for maximal entanglement and threshold secret sharing. The construction here does not claim novelty for either stabilizer joining or the cross-cut method. Its local matrix has to satisfy the **additional** complete signed-transversal conditions to realize Chow's cycle and its required port-axis labeling.
- Iain Moffatt, Steven Noble, and Maya Thompson, [*Tensor Products of Multimatroids and a Brylawski-Type Formula for the Transition Polynomial*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-026-00865-3), Annals of Combinatorics (2026), §3 Proposition 7, §§4–5 and Theorems 14–15. These give related abstract two-sum, port-state, and star-product rank/polynomial rules for tight multimatroids, with comparisons to the older isotropic-systems and even delta-matroid compositions. Their results do not assert the distance of an isotropic representation of the edge-indexed Chow subdivision considered here. Equation (5) is a direct linear-port rank argument for this construction, including incomplete selections; we do not claim a new abstract two-sum theory.
- The [public Ganguly Question 9.2 note](https://www.trureturing.org/research/ganguly-2026-graphical-symplectic-joins/) is about representability of one-sums/bridge joins in the **companion** manuscript and is not the distance question in the present package.

## Bounded duplicate check

Checked 28 November 2026: current arXiv versions of both Ganguly papers, their named results above, the public Ganguly Question 9.2 note and indexing for Ganguly plus graphical codes, subdivision, concatenation, two-sum, and unbounded distance, and the cited code-joining and multimatroid literature. No matching fixed-field construction answering the first sentence was located. This is a bounded search, not an absolute priority assertion. The claim is limited to the simultaneous signed-edge compatibility and exact stabilizer distances proved here. No deployed system or scheme-security assertion is made.
