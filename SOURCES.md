# Source map and scope

1. Chongxu Ren and Hongbo Yu, [Fully Anonymous Perfect Threshold Secret Sharing: Explicit One-Bit Construction and Rate Amplification](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2202), last ePrint revision dated 2026-10-20; [verified PDF upload 2026-11-12](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2202/versions/20261112T135315Z.pdf). Definition 1 fixes exact product uniformity for unauthorized sequences and deterministic anonymous reconstruction. Theorem 1, Eq. (2), Algorithm 1, Lemmas 1–2 and §3.4 describe the common direction-space rate compiler. Its auxiliary is explicitly a one-bit primitive, used independently on ah bits. With a=m=ceil(log n), t=3, L≥2m, and the 2m-bit one-bit primitive they give, M= m ceil(L/m)+4m^2. Section 5.4, Eq. (15), records the alphabet lower bound; §6 (printed page 32) highlights the gap between logarithmic lower overhead and O(t^2 log^2 n) upper overhead, and calls out reducing the protection cost of the direction-space description. The present result uses their payload and basis compiler, replaces only the auxiliary with a single q^2-message primitive, and matches the lower order for t=3 only. It neither reopens perfect-threshold existence nor solves their general-threshold gap.

2. Roni Con, Ray Li and Noam Mazor, [Fully Anonymous Perfect Secret-Sharing](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2145), [PDF](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2145.pdf), ePrint revision dated 2026-10-01. §1.1 Definition 1.2 is the same perfect share and reconstruction model. Their Theorem 1.3/§3 and Propositions 3.4–3.5 give 2ceil(log n)-bit shares for one-bit secrets at threshold three and supply precedent for using a matching to repair the equality atom. Their theorem distinguishes one-bit secrets by affine/collinear and nondegenerate-quadratic/noncollinear patterns. It does not carry two field symbols to the decoder at this length order. Theorem 1.4 (general threshold existence) and Theorem 1.5/§2.2 (general access structures, via the two-minterm construction) already resolve the existence issues; §1.4 frames the follow-ups. Our matching is an additive shift factorization, used together with a family of secret-specific lines and exact off-diagonal compensation, not a claim to originate matching repair.

3. Runqing Qiu, Shixin Zhu, Yang Li and San Ling, [Optimal Ordering-Free Two-Dimensional Insdel Reed–Solomon Codes via Cross-Ratios](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21275v1), arXiv:2611.21275v1, 2026-11-16. §V, Lemma 22 and Lemma 24 connect ordered-triple affine ratios and Möbius maps, and Corollary 26 uses their degree-three single-orbit construction for a fully anonymous (1,3) **ramp** scheme. It does not supply exact product uniformity of every pair at reconstruction threshold three. The degree-five orbit labels used here separate q^2 secrets rather than distinguish evaluation orders inside one orbit; the duplicate-value branch and exact pair repair address the different exact-threshold condition. The elementary finite-field orbit and ratio facts are not claimed new.

4. Wataru Kishimoto, Koji Okada, Kaoru Kurosawa and Wakaha Ogata, [On the bound for anonymous secret sharing schemes](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-218X(01)00236-0), Discrete Applied Mathematics 121 (2002), 193–202. We use only the non-strict alphabet bound as quoted and mapped to fully anonymous schemes in Ren–Yu §5.4. It is a known lower bound, not a novelty claim. Ying-pu Deng, Li-feng Guo and Mu-lan Liu, [Constructions for Anonymous Secret Sharing Schemes Using Combinatorial Designs](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10255-006-0350-3), studies `Theta(|Sigma|)` for fixed n,t in the older anonymous-reconstruction model; it does not give product uniformity or the stated dependence on n here.

5. Allison Bishop, Matthew Green, Yuval Ishai, Abhishek Jain and Paul Lou, [Fully Anonymous Secret Sharing](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1984), §2.4 and Definitions 3.5/4.1. The origin of the perfect threshold question also recorded in [AICR c/0076](https://aicr.info/c/0076/). Its qualitative threshold-three question is already answered by Con–Li–Mazor; this package concerns the later quantitative gap in Ren–Yu.

Public-source searches for threshold-three multi-bit overhead, affine/cross-ratio threshold sharing, and citations to the two 2026 ePrint papers located the sources above but no duplicate exact pair-uniform auxiliary or O(log n) additive threshold-three overhead construction for arbitrary L. The claim is limited to the explicit construction and order comparison, not a priority assertion about inaccessible manuscripts, a tight constant, or an application-security statement.
