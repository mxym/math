# Sources and attribution

1. Yuan Li and Jing Zhang, [*Block Sensitivity and Certificate Complexity of Rotation Symmetric Boolean Functions*, arXiv:2610.17100v2 (8 November 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17100v2), [version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17100).
   - §10 (lines 1541--1546 in the HTML) explicitly asks whether the two global complexities agree for all sufficiently large lengths in each fixed even degree. §7, Remark 7.6 identifies beyond-pair control as the obstacle. The target is their consecutive family, not their separate short-length or nonconsecutive questions.
   - Theorem 6.17, Eq. (3) (lines 1289--1299), gives the block-sensitivity value and its length hypothesis. Its proof, Case 1 (lines 1304--1313), already proves joint freedom of the direct single-zero gaps in our §4 branch \(b\ge q+1\). Our §6 adapts their lower witnesses; the gap witness is used only when it fits, while the exceptional-run witness also covers the shorter case up to two plateau entries. Corollary 6.18 is their sensitivity formula, which is a different measure and not needed for our upper bound.
   - Their quadratic and consecutive quartic classifications are prior results (§§4--5), as are structured local formulas (§6.4), the general two-free-coordinate result and several residues (§7), the all-one odd-length local separation (§8), and the sextic residue-five result (§9). In particular, their Lemma 9.1 already uses the centered prefix-parity partition in long sextic runs. The new certificate step is the other branch: we use the same long-run mechanism at general even degree and add the inclusion-minimal short-cluster cover for arbitrary intervening short runs and zeros. Combining the two certificate branches with the eligibility condition and the matching witnesses gives the exact formula in both the original and shorter ranges.
