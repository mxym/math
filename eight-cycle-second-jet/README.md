# A quadratic deformation lifts the eight-cycle hub-spoke defect to O(q)

**Yongxian Zhang.** AI-assisted proof development and internal checks; no external peer review claimed.

We retain the complex, periodic single-shared-tensor eight-cycle model of [Gesmundo–Landsberg–Walter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09106), with equal physical and bond dimension q, and the notation of the [charge-and-hub note](../eight-cycle-charge-and-hub/README.md). Here F₈(q) is the largest rank of the odd/even flattening. The previous base witness gives rank at least q^4 minus its explicit defect bound via nonzero minors in signed-multiset types. Below we prove that for r = floor((q−1)/2), q^4−F_8(q) ≤ 3r if q is odd and ≤ 12r+4 if q is even. This is not general exactness.

The [addendum](Addendum.md) sharpens these bounds further to **r for odd q** and **8r+4 for even q** with two more sector certificates.

## Perturbation and retained coordinates
For label j≠0, let A_j(t) = E_{0,j} + c_j E_{−j,0} + t(d_j E_{j,j} + e_j E_{−j,−j}), with free coefficients c,d,e. Put A_0(t) = E_{0,0} + t sum_{j≠0} u_j E_{j,0}. Labels and states are in Z/q, and the column-coordinate convention is w=−v. The relevant structural symmetry is rotation (commutes with the matrix even after this relabeling), not charge; leaf diagonals for nonzero labels generally break charge.

At t=0, choose one base square invertible complementary minor B(0) of size equal to the base generic rank, working independently in base multiset blocks. In each zero-free balanced rotation orbit choose the rotation-eigenvector complements (non−1 eigenvalues) and use its alternating vector as the left/right kernel coordinate; all other zero-free blocks are nonsingular generically. All one-zero multisets that occur as intermediates below are nonsingular generically, so they are retained in full. In any remaining singular multiset choose any full-rank minor, deleting its unused rows and columns. Retain, in addition to B(t), the coordinates K on both sides from balanced zero-free multisets of either 2 distinct nonself opposite pairs or one nonself opposite pair repeated, and ignore any other kernel directions. The first type has 6 dims per pair-pair choice; the second type 2 per pair (length 2 and 4 orbits). There are no other zero-free kernels when q is odd; when q is even the omitted h-related type {h,h,a,−a} (h=q/2) adds 3r dims. The block decomposition and bounded omitted zero-containing dimensions follow from the earlier signed-multiset basis computation and exact modular certificates for the base witness.

## Support of the Schur computation
Through order two, any contributing trace walk visits the hub (a walk confined to a leaf would require a perturbation at every one of the eight positions). Its maximal hub-to-hub segments are hub monomers or excursions beginning with an outgoing spoke, possibly visiting diagonal leaf entries without changing leaves, and returning through a spoke. An excursion containing a nonzero label j in a leaf diagonal only uses spoke/diagonal nonzero labels in the same inverse class {j,−j}.

For entries between zero-free words, coefficients of t^1 vanish: seven cross-parity steps and one parity-preserving step never close. At order two, both perturbations must be on nonzero slices and hence leaf diagonals. Each excursion has length 2, 3 or 4, so contains both a row and a column position. Every tile has one nonzero inverse class; the sets of inverse classes appearing in row and column words agree. On the retained balanced K coordinates, each inverse-class set uniquely determines the inverse multiset, so the direct second-order compression D_2 is block diagonal.

For an entry between a zero-free word and a word containing zero, first-order nonzero contributions must have exactly one zero and one of two forms. A tilted A_0 in a spoke tile forces every remaining tile to be a dimer: a hub monomer would consume a position on one side only, and all such monomers are from the same side. The zero then replaces exactly one old letter. Alternatively a perturbed nonzero slice supplies a length-three excursion, all remaining nonzero positions being dimered. The excursion must contain two positions on the zero-free side and one on the zero-containing side, providing a single surplus, which requires exactly one hub monomer on the zero-containing side. Its two spoke-end labels are opposite; its leaf-loop label belongs to their inverse class. Thus the mixed word has the balanced source absolute-class pattern with one occurrence deleted, plus one zero. The multiset possibilities with actual signs are exactly among deletions of one letter of the balanced multiset. The other entries (first-order to a zero-free complement) are already zero. These arguments apply on both sides.

Balanced retained multisets cannot share such one-zero intermediates unless they are identical: delete one occurrence from a two-class pattern (2,2) to get (2,1) or (1,2), or from a singleton pattern (4) to get (3). The absolute classes determine the original retained balanced multiset. Each one-zero intermediate has either no opposite pair or a single nonself opposite pair and no nonzero self-inverse label. The base note certifies every one-zero type of this form as generically invertible (previous exceptions with one zero all require h).

The retained matrix has K blocks with coupling zero at t=0 and K×K first-order term zero. Over C(c,d,e,u)[[t]], its inverse complementary block exists. The Schur complement has form t^2 (D_2 − L_1 B(0)^(−1) R_1) + O(t^3). As B(0) is chosen independently within base multisets, and the intermediation involves only full one-zero multisets, this coefficient splits entirely by retained K multisets. Rotation commutes including at t=1 and column relabeling; K in rotation −1 involves only the −1 sector of the intermediary. No generic claim depends on higher-order terms.

## Exact certificate and uniform lifting
In each split Schur block, the only states that can contribute are the hub and the leaves of its 1–2 opposite pairs (every excursion's start and end enforce this, and a tilted zero in a contributing dimer also enforces it). Thus there are two universal rational blocks, equivalent up to label permutation to q=5 with labels {±1,±2}. The companion Python verifier `verify_second_jet.py` computes each t^k coefficient by enumerating closed walks with exactly k perturbed slices; −1 rotation coordinates are represented by alternating sums of word orbits per input and evaluation at representatives for outputs. For each old multiset S, it builds the intermediate list of words obtained by replacing one letter by zero, evaluates the first jets and the direct second jet, explicitly solves the base intermediary matrix, and computes the Schur coefficient over F_1009. The choices are c=(2,3,7,11), u=(13,17,19,23), d_j=u_j, e_j=u_{−j}+13, in nonzero label order. Six-dimensional block pivots: 840,221,513,810,128,399. Repeated pair pivots: (761,885) and (559,784). All intermediary solves succeed. These are upper-triangular elimination pivots after row swaps, so each symbolic Schur block determinant is a nonzero rational function over Q. The specializations may depend on which block is checked: the product of finitely many nonzero rational functions remains nonzero, also intersecting the base nonsingularity conditions. Thus all retained directions simultaneously lift at generic parameters over C, and an appropriate complex t attains at least the full retained size.

Since all retained K directions lift, the defect is at most the omitted zero-containing counts from the existing base certificates (3r odd; 9r+4 even), plus the omitted even h-related zero-free K directions (3r). Hence q^4−F_8(q) ≤ 3r for odd q, and ≤ 12r+4 for even q. No unproven claim about residual exact rank or optimality of positive multiples of r is made.
