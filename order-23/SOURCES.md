# Sources and scope

* Yuyuan Zhang, Mingzhen Lv, and Haitao Cao,
  [*On the possible cardinalities of quantum Latin squares*, arXiv:2607.19969v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19969v2).
  Section 5, Table 6, gives the order-23 attainable set as all integers
  from 23 through 529 except 24 and 528, with 528 alone in the uncertain
  column. Theorem 1.1(1), which cites their earlier sources, excludes
  \(v+1\) at any order \(v\). Theorem 1.2 itself excludes order 23:
  the other order-23 attainability claims used here are specifically
  those reported in the table, not an application of Theorem 1.2.
* David J. Reutter and Jamie Vicary, [*Biunitary constructions in
  quantum information*, Higher Structures 3(1), 109–154 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.21136/HS.2019.04).
  Corollary 3.2.1 and equation (41), with Figure 7(a), give the two-Hadamard
  construction. It is the mixed Schur product here after transposing their
  first Hadamard. The same construction appears in the
  [arXiv preprint 1609.07775](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07775),
  Corollary 17.
* Zhipeng Xu, [*New Cardinalities for Quantum Latin Squares of Order Six*,
  arXiv:2607.11800v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.11800v3).
  Section 3, Proposition 1 gives the mixed Hadamard Schur product, which we
  use. Section 7 uses a Hadamard and a row-permuted copy at order 6.
  Section 9 already constructs a single-double-ray example at order 6
  (cardinality 35), using a Butson matrix and its row-permuted transpose.
  Neither the mixed construction nor its general ability to achieve a
  single repeated ray is claimed new. The present contribution is the
  order-23 witness and its exact ratio-intersection certification using a
  two-value circulant with non-root-of-unity phase.
* John J. Benedetto, Robert L. Benedetto, and Joseph T. Woodworth,
  [*Optimal Ambiguity Functions and Weil's Exponential Sum Bound*,
  arXiv:1107.1887](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.1887), Section 2, records
  the two-value Björck sequence for \(p\equiv3\pmod4\) and credits
  Göran Björck, *Functions of modulus one on \(Z_p\) whose Fourier
  transforms have constant modulus*, Proc. Alfred Haar Memorial
  Conference (Budapest, 1985), North-Holland (1987), pp. 193–197. The
  present \(H\) uses this construction with \(p=23\) and argument reversal
  (the printed sequence puts the nontrivial phase on nonresidues; negation
  interchanges the two classes). Its orthogonality is also proved here
  without invoking the cited result.
* Yajuan Zang, Meihui Zheng, Zihong Tian, and Xiuling Shan,
  [*On the cardinalities of quantum Latin squares*, arXiv:2508.01972](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01972),
  Section 3, provides maximum-cardinality QLSs for order at least 4;
  529 is already known and not the question resolved here.
* Benjamin Musto and Jamie Vicary,
  [*Quantum Latin squares and unitary error bases*, arXiv:1504.02715v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.02715),
  Definitions 1 and 10 and Theorem 11 give the QLS axioms and a prior
  complex-Hadamard construction. It is not the exact two-Hadamard
  formula attributed above to Xu.

Prior-art check on 2026-11-27: checked the latest arXiv versions of
2607.19969 and 2607.11800; searched combinations of the exact title or
arXiv identifier with "528", "order 23", "single repeated ray", and
"Hadamard", and inspected the primary results above. No earlier
order-23 cardinality-528 construction surfaced in this bounded search.
The independent theorem here is existence of cardinality 528 at order
23; the rest of its spectrum is taken from the stated primary results.
No claim is made about unresolved cardinalities at other orders, nor
about a uniform near-maximum theorem for all orders.
