# Sharp full-kernel extraction with a bound on every pair codegree

**Yongxian Zhang.** AI-assisted proof development and internal checks; no external peer review claimed.

Let \(H\) be a nonempty simple \(3\)-uniform family. Assume that every pair of vertices is in at most \(D\) triples, where \(1\le D\le m\) and \(m\ge2\). Then the sharp worst-case fraction of triples one can keep so that **every** pairwise intersection, including the empty set, is a kernel of an \(m\)-petal sunflower in the output is \(\Theta(m^{-2}D^{-1})\). The constants are uniform. This neither assumes tripartiteness nor makes any two projections injective. See [the separate nonempty-only theorem](InternalSunflowersGlobalCapSharpNonempty.md). These results do not settle the unbounded-codegree question of [Janzer–Jin–Sudakov–Wu](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00493-026-00222-1#Sec6).

For the lower bound, color the conflict graph whose vertices are triples, with two adjacent when they share a pair. Its maximum degree is at most \(3(D-1)\), so a greedy coloring partitions the triples into at most \(3D-2\) linear subfamilies. The largest has size at least \(|H|/(3D-2)\). Apply the established [linear full-kernel extraction](LinearTripleInternalSunflowers.md) to preserve \(\Omega(m^{-2})\) of that class.

For the upper bound put \(n=m-1\), \(d=\min(D,n)\), and take three parts indexed by \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\). Include triples \((a,b,c)\) for which the residue \(c-a-b\) is one of \(0,\ldots,d-1\). Every pair between parts has exactly \(d\) completions, so the codegree bound is met and the family has \(n^2d\) edges. All pair codegrees are less than \(m\). Every singleton link has a side of size \(n<m\), and the whole family has no matching of \(m\) triples, so no intersection (even empty) can be witnessed. The optimum is exactly one edge. Since \(d\ge D/2\), this proves the matching order. The separate nonempty-only theorem uses a different matching upper example.
