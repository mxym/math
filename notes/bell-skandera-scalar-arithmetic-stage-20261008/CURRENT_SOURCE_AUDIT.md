# Bell–Skandera main problem: current-source preparation

Checked 8 October 2026, 16:26–16:29 UTC. Prepared while the preceding permanent
stage awaits coordinated source publication. No main-problem proof or
counterexample is claimed here, and no parallel research worker has been opened.

## Exact target

For every d>=1 and f(t)=1+c_1 t+...+c_d t^d with all c_i positive integers,
does real-rootedness imply that (1,c_1,...,c_d) is the face-cardinality sequence
of a finite simplicial complex? Equivalently all Kruskal–Katona lower-shadow
inequalities must hold. The constant coefficient is one; a general monic
polynomial is not the same hypothesis.

## Existing negative controls and scope

The inherited integral strict-Newton sequence (1,8,26,46,46,26,8,1) fails KK but
is not real-rooted: the two cubic factors have discriminant -31. It excludes
only a Newton/ULC-only reduction. It is not a Bell–Skandera counterexample.
A minimal-degree counterexample, if one exists, can be taken irreducible because
integer constant-one factors again have positive coefficients and negative
roots, and simplicial joins multiply f-polynomials. This reduction is already
in Bell–Skandera and must retain all coefficient-integrality constraints.
