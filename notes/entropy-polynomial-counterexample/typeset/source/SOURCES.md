# Sources and scope

1. Tanay Wakhare, *Iterated entropy derivatives and binary entropy inequalities*, Journal of Approximation Theory 307 (May 2025), article 106143.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jat.2025.106143
   - Published text: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021904525000012
   - Preprint snapshot: arXiv:2312.14743v2, 14 January 2025
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14743v2
   - https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14743v2
   - Target: Conjecture 2, equations (1.3) and (1.4), including every pair of integers k>r>=1 and roots counted with multiplicity.
   - The published statement and preprint statement have the same parameter range and formulas used in this package.
   - Theorem 3 also discusses the coprime-parameter route to the entropy inequality. Our counterexamples (11,10) and (20,19) are both coprime.

2. Boon Suan Ho, *A generalization of Boppana's entropy inequality*, arXiv:2601.19327v1, 27 January 2026.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19327
   - https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19327
   - Theorem 1 proves alpha_k h(x^k)>=x^(k-1)h(x) for every real k>1.
   - That theorem does not assert the high-order derivative root count of Wakhare Conjecture 2. The present counterexample is consistent with Ho's theorem.

The polynomial definitions and prior conjecture belong to the cited sources. The counterexample in this package is proved by its displayed arithmetic, not by a source's claim or a search-result summary. A search did not identify an earlier published counterexample, but exhaustive novelty verification is not asserted.
