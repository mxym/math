## Exact limits

The results concern one explicit rational-parameter planar product-digit family, its original natural distance probabilities, and the stated pin rectangle. They include superlacunary and fixed-ratio endpoint distinctions, positive masks with bounded continuous densities, exact exceptional-pin dimensions, and intervals of uniformly positive length in each studied pin's actual distance set. The interval location may depend on the pin.

Sharp nonmembership is restricted to pins with first coordinate in the horizontal support H, as specified in the paper. Off-H regularity has its own statement. At the fixed-ratio boundary D_K=0 and p_1 in H, essential boundedness remains open. The O(log q) norm estimate and double-exponential tail estimate are upper bounds only; no matching lower growth is claimed.
