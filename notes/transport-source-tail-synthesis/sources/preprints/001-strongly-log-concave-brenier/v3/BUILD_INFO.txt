Build information for version 3

Final document: 30 pages, US Letter, 11-point Latin Modern body text.
Toolchain: pdfTeX 3.141592653-2.6-1.40.26 (TeX Live 2025/dev/Debian),
LaTeX with AMS packages, BibTeX, and plain bibliography style.
Rendered for visual review using Poppler pdftoppm at 100 dpi.

Use sh build.sh in this directory. All source and bibliography inputs are local;
no external images, fonts downloaded at build time, or network calls are needed.
The packaged manuscript.bbl is generated from references.bib.

The supplied SHA256SUMS identify this exact artifact snapshot. Rebuilding with
another TeX distribution or at another time may change PDF binary hashes because
of font/package versions and embedded PDF timestamps or identifiers, without
changing the mathematical content. A separate-directory rebuild was checked for
matching page count and extracted text.
