# Dependencies, imported statements, and scope

The new upper theorem is conditional only on the established projection-body invariant calculus and the spectral reduction stated below. Its new product inequalities are proved in `proof.md` and replayed by the supplied exact checkers. This file makes those imported mathematical premises explicit; it does not introduce another theorem or a runtime requirement.

Exact verification requires only Python 3.10 or newer and its standard library. Optional PDF rebuilding requires a TeX distribution with `pdflatex` and the packages `fontenc`, `lmodern`, `amsmath`, `amssymb`, `amsthm`, `mathtools`, `geometry`, `microtype`, and `hyperref`. TeX is not a dependency of the exact mathematical checkers. The optional numerical discovery generators require SciPy; their output is not trusted until accepted by the supplied exact verifiers.

Throughout, affine equivalence means **invertible affine maps on affine hulls**. The dimension-graded class is generated by a formal point and finite joins and Cartesian products, followed by such affine equivalences. Rank-dropping affine maps are excluded. This is the class used by entry 005 v2; arbitrary images would be a different class and are not covered.

## Essential logical inputs

For a full-dimensional body of positive dimension d, the v2 convention is R(K)=|ΠK|/|K|^(d−1), g(d)=d^d/d!, a(K)>0, H=1/a and Q=aR/g(d). For a formal point, D=d+1=H=Q=1. All imports below use that normalization.

1. **005 v2, Lemma 2.1 and Sections 3–5.** R and a are invariant under invertible affine equivalence, and 1/(d+1)≤a(K)≤1. For positive-dimensional factors A,B of dimensions r,s, n=r+s, R(A×B)=R(A)R(B) and a(A×B)=[r a(A)+s a(B)]/n. Equivalently, H'=n/[r/H_A+s/H_B] and Q'=Q_A Q_B [g(r)g(s)/g(n)] [rH_B+sH_A]/n. For joins, (D,H,Q) updates as (D_A+D_B,H_A+H_B,Q_AQ_B), including joins with a point. These identities are the only inherited operations used in the new product/join induction.
2. **005 v2, Section 8.** Every class member has a finite, well-founded expression with dimension-preserving affine equivalences. Products with a point are redundant. Positive-dimensional members satisfy H≥2 by a new elementary induction from the interval H=2: joins add H and products average a. The package does not import a bound for arbitrary nonsymmetric bodies to obtain this class property.
3. **Spectral supplement, Theorem 2.1 and Section 3, equation (3.1).** For a dimension-graded join-closed family containing a formal point, put λ(K)=Q(K)^(1/(d+1)) and λ*=sup λ(K). If λ* is finite, the limit of sup R(K)^(1/d) equals eλ*. The new mixed invariant itself establishes λ*≤exp(49/1000), so the finiteness hypothesis is verified here. No attainment hypothesis or closure under products is required for that spectral theorem.

Weighted Jensen/Hölder, weighted power means, ordinary Stirling asymptotics, differentiation of the elementary logarithm inequality, the exponential series, and Machin's identity are standard analytic tools. The required uses and exact numerical bounds are proved within the supplied proof and component notes. They are not opaque solver or library dependencies.
