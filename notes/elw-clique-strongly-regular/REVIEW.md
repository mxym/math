# Independent AI proof audit: positive truncated clique square sum for every strongly regular graph
An independent AI-assisted internal review checked the proof and its cited mathematical inputs. This was not external peer review. **Outcome: no gap found** in the stated all-SRG inequality. Not a resolution on all regular graphs or all graphs. This report does not add an equality classification.

## Source and statement
[Elphick–Linz–Wocjan, Conj. 2, §4.2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05229) explicitly identify the all-SRG case as unresolved there. They already give the inertia bound from Cvetković, the reduction to d²+(ω−1)r², and the equivalence at the Delsarte endpoint to rμ≤dt(t−1). These should be credited; the endpoint inequality is not unconditional. Their Type C argument uses ω=1+d/t and ω≤f+1 simultaneously; it does not show the Delsarte endpoint inequality unconditionally. The present proof instead finds a valid interval whose endpoints both satisfy the desired concave inequality.

## Parameter and interval audit
For a primitive SRG, A²=(d−μ)I+(λ−μ)A+μJ. One has 0<μ<d (μ=d forces complete multipartite), the roots on 1^⊥ have opposite signs, and t>1: a connected noncomplete graph has an induced three-vertex path, so its minimum eigenvalue is at most −√2 by interlacing. Taking trace and eliminating n by the count (n−d−1)μ=d(d−λ−1) gives the manuscript's n,f,g,h,b formulas independently. In particular b=n−2d+λ and b+t−r−2=n−2d−2+μ are the complement's nonadjacent- and adjacent-pair counts. The first is positive for primitive graphs. The second need not be positive, but nonnegativity suffices.

If either restricted eigenvalue is irrational, they are conjugate over Q. Its irreducible factor occurs in the characteristic polynomial with common multiplicity f=g, and trace forces r−t=−d/f<0. In that branch t−r=μ−λ is still a positive integer despite generally irrational individual eigenvalues. In the other branch r≥t, rational algebraic integers are integers, so that t≥2. No spurious integrality assumption on f is imposed on actual graphs (all actual multiplicities are integers).

Turán applied in a neighborhood gives ω≥1+d/(d−λ) because the local degree is exactly λ and local clique size is at most ω−1; ω≥2 here. Applying Hoffman to the connected regular complement yields ω≤n(1+r)/(n−d+r)=1+d/t. On a clique the coordinate-restricted A+I is all ones, positive semidefinite; its supporting coordinate subspace meets the g-dimensional negative eigenspace only in 0. Therefore ω≤n−g=f+1, so precisely ω positive terms can be taken. F(x)=nd(1−1/x)−d²−(x−1)r² has F''(x)=−2nd/x³<0; checking a lower and a valid upper endpoint suffices even when neither is attained.

## Lower endpoint
I multiplied F(1+d/h) by h(h+d)/d and independently solved the counting identity for μ in terms of b; this gives B=dhb−(d+h)r² and B'=rth+r³t(h−1)/b²>0. At r<t, substituting b=1 and t=r+1+y gives a polynomial in r,y with 17 positive coefficients. Thus the conference branch is included. At r≥t≥3, direct substitution b=r−t+2 and multiplication by r−t+2, followed by shifts r=t+x and t=3+y, yields a nonnegative polynomial with 29 positive coefficients. This independently corroborates the shorter handwritten lower estimate. For t=2 the exact B(r) values are positive for integer 2≤r≤6. For r≥7, μ≤8 implies b≥2r; then h=r+2 and hb−r²≥r², d≥h since λ≥0. Every lower-end case closes.

## Upper endpoint
Direct rational cancellation gives F(1+d/t)=dr(dt(t−1)−rμ)/(μt). In particular all r<t are covered since r≤t−1<t(t−1). For remaining r>t(t−1), division in the first Krein inequality is by S=r−t²+2t>0 and gives the advertised interval rt<d≤K. Trace(A²) gives F(f+1)=gt²−nd/(f+1); dividing by gt² gives the exact threshold in the draft.

I checked the Krein bound from [Brouwer–Van Maldeghem, §1.3.4, PDF page 40](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/math/srg/rk3/srgw.pdf#page=40), including the sign under s=−t. The absolute bound 2n≤f(f+3) and Neumaier μ≤t³(2t−3) are [Prop. 1.3.14 and Thm 1.3.15](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/math/srg/rk3/srgw.pdf); using the latter only for primitive integral minimum −2 meets all of its hypotheses.

For 3≤t and t(t−1)<r≤t³, clearing the f-only sufficient bound gives J affine in d with J(rt)>0. At d=K, independently dividing SJ(K) by r²(t−1) gives a concave quadratic in r, second derivative −2. At both r=t(t−1) and r=t³ its expansion in t−3 has only positive coefficients, an alternative exact certificate to the author's estimate. For r≥t³, use f+1≥2n/f−2, as permitted by absolute bound. Independently clearing its excess over the required threshold yields the paper's concave H. H(rt) has the stated factorization, positive here. Computing H(K) from scratch, all five coefficients of the polynomial in r−t³ agree with the manuscript, including its constant term. Alternatively the polynomial after the simultaneous shifts (r−t³,t−2) has 39 nonzero coefficients, all strictly positive (minimum 3). All denominators cleared are strictly positive. The shared boundary r=t³ is covered; the other exceptional gap is exactly t=2, r∈{3,4,5,6,7}. Divisibility and μ≤8 leave only (r,μ)=(6,7),(7,7),(7,8) that could violate the Delsarte endpoint; f=57/8,23/3,22/3 respectively, impossible. Imprimitive SRGs are equal disjoint cliques or their complements, directly checked by their spectra. An edgeless graph with n≥1 has ω=1 and an empty positive sum. The statement requires at least one vertex; the literal zero-vertex graph has ω=0 and the formula with 1/ω is undefined.

## Supplementary independent checker
From this directory, use `python3 check_independent.py` with SymPy, no optimization flag. It derives the identities from the counting system rather than importing or invoking the author's script. Alongside the polynomial proofs, an independent finite parameter-grid stress test covers n≤1200, 2≤t≤60, 1≤r≤150 with integer eigenvalues subject to the specified feasibility filters (3,560 rows and 97,024 integral clique candidates), and all nonsquare formal n=1 mod 4 conference orders 5≤n≤1200 (283 rows and 5,929 clique candidates, exact comparisons involving √n). These rows can be impossible as graph parameters; the tests assume no existence and do not replace the analytic proof.

## Literature, with limitations
The relevant published and announced results have different scopes. [Kumar–Pragada, Theorem 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.19341) treat few triangles under quantitative conditions, and [Zeng–Zhang](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2025.01.037) cover few triangles and conditional line-graph cases for the higher-rank variant. [Liu–Tang–Zhang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18044) prove the other ELW conjecture, involving the total positive square energy and n² rather than nd. [Coutinho–Liu–Spier–Tang–Zhang](https://github.com/shengtongz/BN) explicitly say their rank-two result does not address up to ω positive eigenvalues. Bounded searches of accessible recent and older sources located no previous proof for every SRG. Historical attribution for the SRG reduction and known families is necessary; “first” is not certified.
