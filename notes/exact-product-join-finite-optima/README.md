# Sharp projection-body optima for arbitrary product/join trees, dimensions 1–48

**Supplement to entry 005, 8 October 2026.**

[Full theorem and proof](paper.md) · [Independent exact checker](code/check.py) · [Reusable exact certificate producer](code/build.py) · [Frozen 6,494-state certificate](certificates/frontiers48.json) · [Complete dimension-by-dimension optimum table](results/optima.tsv).

The class consists of polytopes generated by *arbitrary* finite Cartesian products, affine joins and invertible affine maps on affine hulls, starting from a point. Unlike 005's fixed-arity recursion results, subtrees can be heterogeneous and operations may be mixed in any finite order.

**Theorem:** The normalized projection-body maximum in this entire restricted class can be computed exactly by a two-coordinate rational Pareto-frontier recursion in every prescribed dimension. The posted certificate evaluates all dimensions **1 through 48**, with sharp attaining polytopes and no unverified numerical optimization.

At dimension 48, the sharp ratio to the simplex is

\[
\boxed{M_{48}=105488578125/34359738368>3.}
\]

An attaining body is the affine join of three copies of \(T_4\times T_4\) and two copies of \(T_5\times T_5\). Every dimension through 48 has an optimum attained by a join of points and products of two simplices; this is an **existence** theorem, not uniqueness for the full equality class.

The checker verifies 1,956,775 product/join candidates, exact frontier completeness, every witness's attainability, and every optimum fraction. No floats, solver outputs or `assert` statements are used in mathematical decisions. The standard geometric identities are imported from public 005 v2 (see proof attribution), not re-proved by code. This is model-assisted research, not external human review, Lean formalization, or a literature-wide priority certification.

The all-dimensional **algorithmic correctness** is proved mathematically, but the posted sharp-value table is finite through dimension 48. The unrestricted class of all convex bodies and the asymptotic optimal rate of the entire recursive class remain open.
