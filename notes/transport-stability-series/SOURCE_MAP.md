## Deliberate editorial differences

- All ASCII formulas are typeset as mathematics. Source comparison signs are rendered as `\asymp`; genuine ratio-one statements use `\sim`. Original numbered equations and section order are retained.
- Descriptive titles, concise abstracts, common notation, and a common provenance paragraph were added. Original obsolete research-status introductions and repeated source-hash metadata are represented by the preserved snapshots and current review-status documentation.
- Top-N: the source-support qualifier in Section 6.2 is made explicit as requested by the analytic review. The imported (P) statement remains explicit, with the precise upstream theorem and admissible extended-support class; its proof is not claimed to have been checked. The discussion of binary questions points to the second paper without using it in the proof.
- Binary mass: the obsolete statement that the top-N candidate awaits review is updated to its conditional PASS on (P), while retaining the lack of dependency. The final hash of the earlier source is moved to this manifest. The long fixed-revision GitHub URL retains its target with shorter displayed text.
- General moment: source density and all target-modulus definitions are expanded from their explicit second-paper definitions for standalone readability. The exact cross-paper references are Section 2.3, equation (2.5), and Section 3. Repeated old endpoint status is replaced by the final limited recheck; both repaired endpoint proofs are retained. (M) and (H) may occupy multiple display lines solely for layout.
- Bibliographies use conventional author/title entries and readable link labels. Repository identifiers are descriptive; no personal authorship attribution is invented.
- Proof prose is kept as close to the reviewed text as practical. There is no new source class, extra regularity assumption, leading-coefficient claim, formal-verification claim, or historical-priority claim.
