\begin{abstract}
For one fixed strongly log-concave product source with critical boundary density $x^{s-1}L(x)$, where $s=p/(p-2)$ and $L$ satisfies explicit differential slow-variation conditions, we determine the joint dependence of Brenier-map stability on target cardinality and Wasserstein distance. The sharp envelope is governed by the sum of the largest dyadic source weights, with no monotonicity assumption on $L$. A finite-step rearrangement inequality yields the upper bound; global convex potentials with exactly matched label masses realize arbitrary selected scales and give the lower bound with the full $2M+1$ atom cost. Consequences include logarithmic complexity cutoffs, a fixed-cardinality boundedness criterion, and a nonmonotone example excluding endpoint substitution. All two-sided statements for $N\ge3$ are conditional on the explicitly imported centered-potential estimate (P), supplied by manuscript 001 version 3, Theorem 1.1. Its proof is not reviewed here. Lower constructions do not use (P).
\end{abstract}
