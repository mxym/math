\begin{abstract}
For a fixed product source with vanishing boundary density and a general convex target moment budget, we give sharp Brenier-map moduli on two-atom target classes. The response $E(m)=\sqrt m\,\Phi^{-1}(1/m)$ and the source quantile $q(m)$ determine the fixed-mass formula, while an attained running maximum determines both the minimum-weight and unrestricted binary envelopes. The proofs realize the scalar bounds by genuine globally convex hinge potentials with exactly prescribed masses. Uniform continuity holds precisely when $\Phi(r)/r^2\to\infty$. Under additional differentiability assumptions an index principle recovers the power-moment phase diagram; separate comparison-only arguments establish logarithmic and stretched-exponential endpoint laws without those extra assumptions. The paper imports the all-directions halfspace estimate from the second paper of this series, Section 2.3, equation (2.5), and recalls its coupling calculation from Section 3. It does not use the centered-potential estimate (P).
\end{abstract}
