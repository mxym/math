\begin{abstract}
For a fixed strongly log-concave product source whose boundary distribution has exponent $\alpha>1$, we determine the sharp quadratic Brenier-map modulus on binary targets with a bounded $p$th moment. Uniform formulas cover a prescribed mass vector, a lower bound on positive atom masses, and arbitrary binary masses. An all-orientations weighted halfspace estimate gives a direct upper bound for changing support locations and masses, while globally convex hinge potentials give exact-mass lower realizations. The binary phase threshold is $\alpha=2s$, where $s=p/(p-2)$; a running maximum captures critical slowly varying corrections without assuming monotonicity. This differs from the $\alpha=s$ threshold for unrestricted targets and targets with at least three atoms. No centered-potential stability estimate (P) or top-N theorem is used.
\end{abstract}
