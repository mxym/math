# Flatness rigidity through three logical qubits

This is a partial result on Conjecture 1, **not** a proof in unrestricted dimension. Let a pure n-qubit state have stabilizer nullity s = n minus the binary dimension of its Pauli stabilizer labels (global signs ignored). If s ≤ 3 and the state is flat on its support in each basis of one complete stabilizer MUB, it is a stabilizer state. This covers n=3, arbitrary symplectic spreads, arbitrary choices of eigenvalue labels, and all ambient n. In particular a nonstabilizer example, if one exists, cannot have a support with at most four points, or even a support of affine dimension at most three. Here each support is labeled by the usual binary character labels of the measured abelian group. The proof below uses no numerical search.

**Fractional covers.** In V=F₂^(2s) denote a maximal totally isotropic subspace by L. Each L labels a joint Pauli eigenbasis. A fractional cover has nonnegative weights c_L such that each nonzero Pauli label belongs to Ls of total weight one. Zero-weight contexts impose no condition. Write D=2^s. Counting labels gives Σ c_L=D+1 when s>0. Put r_u=〈P_u〉 for any Hermitian Pauli representative. Character orthogonality, allowing consistent signs in the commuting subgroup, gives Σ_{u∈L} r_u² = D Σ_x p_{L,x}². Pure-state Pauli Parseval gives Σ_{u≠0}r_u²=D−1. If every positive-weight context is flat with support h_L then Σ_L c_L/h_L=2. In particular some context has h_L ≤ (D+1)/2. Also, for any u≠0 at least one such context contains u, so r_u is a signed integer divided by h_L, of numerator congruent to h_L modulo 2.

**Reduction to logical qubits.** Let A be the full stabilizer label subgroup of the pure physical state (all labels whose expectation has modulus one). This is isotropic. The state lies in one of its joint eigencodes; that code is the usual 2^s-dimensional logical space with symplectic Pauli labels W=A^⊥/A. One may equally make a Clifford change to fixed |0〉 ancillary qubits and s residual qubits. No nonidentity logical Pauli stabilizes the residual state: otherwise a representative of its logical label would extend A.

For each physical spread member L write t_L=|A∩L|. The image K_L of L∩A^⊥ in W is Lagrangian: the rank of pairing L against A is dim A−dim(A∩L), since L=L^⊥. Give this occurrence of K_L weight t_L/|A| and combine equal occurrences. For a nonzero logical Pauli coset w+A there are |A| physical labels to be covered, all nonzero. In each L containing that logical label in its image there are exactly t_L such representatives. The physical spread partitions the labels, yielding the fractional-cover equations on W. More generally a fractional physical cover also descends, with physical weights multiplying these factors.

For completeness, the measurements descend as well. The probability of a character of L is the Fourier sum of Pauli expectations in L divided by |L|, with signed lifts chosen to form a commuting group. Expectations outside A^⊥ vanish, since conjugating by some stabilizer negates that Pauli. The sign equations on A∩L force zero for any inconsistent character. For each consistent character restriction to L∩A^⊥ the probability equals the corresponding logical character probability divided by m=|L|/|L∩A^⊥|=|A|/t_L. Each logical outcome occurs m times (every character of L∩A^⊥ has m extensions to L). Hence flatness descends. This proof does not assume the projected Lagrangians are pairwise disjoint.

**Smallest support.** In the reduced system every support affinely spans F₂^s in its own character labels, because any containing affine hyperplane would give a fixed nonidentity Pauli eigenvalue. Thus min h_L≥s+1. For s=1 or 2 this contradicts min h_L≤floor((2^s+1)/2). For s=3 it forces min h_L=4. The four labels of such a support are affinely independent. By a Clifford basis/label change take this context to the computational basis with support S={0,e₁,e₂,e₃}, and write the amplitudes as z_x/2 for |z_x|=1.

Every unordered pair x,y in S has a distinct nonzero difference a=x+y. In a Pauli P_(a,b)=i^(a·b) X^a Z^b, the dot in the exponent can be taken modulo 2 (a different Hermitian sign is harmless). The only two terms in the expectation for this shift come from that pair. Depending on b·a being 0 or 1 one obtains, up to signs, Re( z_x* z_y )/2 or Im( z_x* z_y )/2; both kinds of b exist. Since every cover context has h in {4,…,8}, its expectation with modulus ≤1/2 belongs in absolute value to {0,1/7,1/5,1/4,1/3,3/7,1/2}. Squaring and checking the seven rational numbers shows two numbers from twice this set have sum of squares one only for {0,1}. Hence the relative phases all belong to {±1,±i}. After an overall phase, diagonal single-qubit Clifford gates remove the phases on e₁,e₂,e₃ independently.

**Excluding the tetrahedron.** The resulting state is Clifford-equivalent to the CCZ phase state ψ(x)=2^(−3/2)(−1)^(x₁x₂x₃): apply a Hadamard to one qubit of CCZ to obtain the positive uniform state on {(x₂x₃,x₂,x₃)}, four affinely independent points, then an affine label change. The property of admitting a fractional flat cover is invariant under this basis transformation.

Let Z={(0,b)}. The nonzero Pauli expectations of the CCZ state off the identity have magnitude 1/2 and support B={(a,b):a≠0 and a·b=0}. Indeed for a≠0 the sign of ψ(x)ψ(x+a) is positive except at x=111 and x=111+a. Its Walsh transform divided by eight has value 1/2 at b=0, absolute value 1/2 at nonzero b perpendicular to a, and vanishes otherwise. At a=0 only b=0 survives. In a flat context with h elements of support, its active count |L∩B| must equal 4(8/h−1); for integral h∈{4,…,8} this is either 4 or 0. On L the map (a,b)↦a·b is linear, since its polarization is the symplectic pairing. Its kernel has order 4 or 8 and contains L∩Z. The active count is |ker|−|L∩Z|. To reach 4 requires |ker|=8 and |L∩Z|=4. Consequently the projection onto a coordinates has exactly one nonzero direction a, and L∩Z identifies with a^⊥. Since all its elements have a·b=0, this forces L to equal the unique
  L_a={(0,b):b∈a^⊥} ∪ {(a,b):b∈a^⊥}.
The active label (a,0) can be covered only by L_a; each of the seven L_a must have weight one. For every fixed b≠0, however, the label (0,b) belongs to three of these contexts (the three nonzero a perpendicular to b). Its total weight would exceed one. This contradiction proves the theorem.

For the support corollary, a support in an affine space of dimension r supplies n−r linearly independent stabilizers in that measurement subgroup. Hence s≤r, so the theorem applies. Every support of at most four points has affine dimension ≤3. Stabilizer states themselves are flat in every stabilizer eigenbasis, by Pauli orthogonality.
