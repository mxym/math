# Literature and scope

- [A. B. Klimov, C. Muñoz and I. Sainz, arXiv:2610.10471v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10471v2), §III.3, Proposition 2, Proposition 3, Conjecture 1. One complete stabilizer MUB suffices in their conjecture, not all stabilizer bases. Their Prop. 3 analytically treats n≤2 and the nonadditive three-point branch at arbitrary n. Their introduction and end of Appendix E explicitly say a three-qubit exclusion argument can be carried out, omit that derivation, and report numerical enumeration; §IV.1 identifies the three-qubit support there as numerical. We do not claim priority for the three-qubit statement. The additional scope here is fractional-cover reduction, an explicit analytic s≤3 exclusion, computer-assisted s≤4, and the unrestricted-dimensional arithmetic obstructions in ARITHMETIC.md. The same paper proves the all-centered-supports-additive branch (Prop. 2) and a general cross-basis support-periodicity relation.
- [Measuring nonstabilizerness via multifractal flatness, arXiv:2305.11797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11797) treats the entire Clifford orbit/all stabilizer bases, a strictly stronger premise than a single spread.
- Targeted searches for the named conjecture, title, and arXiv ID did not return a general proof. This is limited literature review, not priority certification.
- This draft has an independent AI-assisted internal review, not external peer review or formal proof verification. General unrestricted Conjecture 1 remains open in this work.

- [H.-Y. Sun, arXiv:2610.12180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12180) studies full common unbiased states for several bases and extendibility, particularly in composite dimension six. Our supports may vanish, and our bases are a complete qubit Pauli spread. [Fiorentino and Weigert, support uncertainty](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/aca9a4) concerns measurement-support uncertainty for prime-dimensional sets without imposing flatness.
