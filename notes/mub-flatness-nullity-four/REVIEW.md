**Disposition: PASS for THREE, FOUR and ARITHMETIC, with the scope below.** An independent AI review, not peer review or formal proof verification. There is no general-resolution or historical-priority claim. Quantification in the first two parts is over fractional covers of nonzero Pauli labels by Lagrangians with nonnegative real weights summing to 1 at each label. ## Analytic gates

1. With full stabilizer A and Lagrangian L, T=A∩L, H=L∩A-perp, the pairing of A with L has rank dim(A)-dim(T). Hence H/T has dimension s=n-dim(A) and injects isotropically into the logical symplectic space. For a positive-weight L meeting a given nonzero logical coset, there are exactly |T| representatives of that coset in L. Summing physical weights over all |A| representatives gives |A|; dividing yields logical cover weight c_L |T|/|A|. No disjointness of the projected L is assumed. A consistent splitting of the commuting Paulis on H identifies the restrictions compatible with the physical stabilizer character on T with characters of H/T, up to relabeling. A logical outcome's probability is split into |L|/|H|=|A|/|T| equal probabilities; inconsistent outcomes are zero. This is a flatness-preserving reduction, not a postselection hypothesis. The residual pure state has no logical Pauli stabilizer. The s=0 case is already stabilizer.
2. Pauli Parseval and character orthogonality give the minimum-support inequality, with no integrality premise on cover weights. Every positive context's support affinely spans in the reduced problem, since an affine linear equation on its support gives a stabilizer. The three-qubit branch has a four-point affine frame. The divisibility/parity allowed-value check for a unique difference gives only the axial Pythagorean possibilities; this was also independently checked with rational arithmetic. The CCZ sign function changes at precisely two points under each nonzero translation; its Walsh spectrum has exactly the stated half-magnitude locations. The restriction of the binary quadratic form a·b to an isotropic subspace is linear. A flat active context must have exactly four active labels, so its kernel size (4 or 8) minus its Z intersection size (at least 1) equals 4, forcing sizes 8 and 4. The seven forced contexts overload each purely Z nonzero label threefold. This rules out even fractional covers.
3. For four qubits Pauli tomography gives h rho_xy in B^-1 Z[i], B=2^8·3²·5·7·11·13. There is also a short analytic alphabet proof rather than just the integer-circle scan: at primes fixed by conjugation every norm-one element has valuation zero. At a split prime over 5 or 13 numerator/denominator valuations are opposite and have absolute value at most 1, since both z and conjugate(z) are B-integral. All other valuations vanish. Since the Gaussian integers are factorial and have four roots of unity, this gives exactly i^u((2+i)/(2-i))^e((3+2i)/(3-2i))^f, e,f in {-1,0,1}, with unique exponents; the two ratios are (3+4i)/5 and (5+12i)/13. The same restriction on each pairwise relative phase proves exponent widths ≤1. A singleton difference enforces a Gaussian fourth-root ratio; the distinct-value counts in the scaled component check for h=5,6,7,8 are 17,14,13,11, respectively, and each yields only (1,0),(0,1). The gauge fixing requires simultaneous conjugation of the admissible cover when applying Clifford S gates and affine changes; this is permitted because nonexistence is proved across all covers, not just the seed's originally named spread.
4. For any spread, after fixing a seed all other Lagrangians are graphs of invertible-*difference* symmetric matrices; the matrices themselves need not be invertible. For each nonzero x the D products Mx are distinct. Fourier cancellation and x·Mx=diag(M)·x prove diagonal bijectivity. Chirp signs, normalization |T|²/(Dh), and v₂(T conjugate(T))=v_(1+i)(T) were checked. Odd h makes every complementary T nonzero modulo 1+i; the reciprocal sum forces h=1. Modulo (2), for even h the only potentially nonzero term is (i−1) times the stated parity bracket. If its coefficient is 1, the whole complementary measurement is occupied and v₂(h)=1; this contradicts 4|h or, independently, absence of full support. Diagonal bijection then forces vanishing XOR. For p=3 mod 4, norm-one seed phases are p-adic units, and the unnormalized chirp matrix and its inverse are p-integral. They preserve the minimum coordinate valuation (zero; ignore zero coordinates or give them +infinity). The occupied entries' common norm Dh/h_M therefore gives v_p(h_M)=v_p(h) at every member of the spread. The six-point XOR consequence creates a dependence among five nonzero points after translation, hence spans at most four dimensions. A stabilizer's flat measurement supports have power-of-two sizes, not six. No inference for arbitrary fractional covers is warranted by this complementary-basis argument.

## Independent finite certificate

- `independent_cover.py` does not call the original code. It enumerates the affine orbits using generators (transvections and one translation), compares their union with **all** full-span subsets of sizes 5--8 in 16 points, and checks they are disjoint. Orbit sizes, in the manuscript's order: **2688, 6720, 448, 6720, 4480, 1920, 840, 10080**. Counts per support size: 1,2,2,3.
- `independent_phase.cpp` regenerates the phase patterns with ternary component assignments and independently evaluates Pauli matrix elements by summing over all ordered computational inputs as integer bra/ket products, scale 65². It uses neither the original files at runtime nor the selected-endpoint sign convention. Its **4,223,337** distinct parameter patterns give survivor counts 1,4,4,16,16,64,56,64. The **sets of 225 individual (support-index,phase-code) tuples** from both implementations matched exactly, not just their cardinalities. Different iteration order does not affect the comparison. Screening the zero shift is unnecessary: dropping a necessary rejection only enlarges the set subjected to the subsequent complete cover impossibility check.
- The second stage of `independent_cover.py` parametrizes contexts by the X projection U and a symmetric bilinear form on U, counting 2295, with each nonidentity label contained in 135. Rather than transforming Pauli expectations, it constructs all 36,720 actual joint eigenbras as quadratic chirps on affine cosets; integer orthogonality and eigenvalue ±1 on a spanning generator set are checked for **each** basis. It computes exact squared overlaps with the 225 states. Nonnegative context weights force a unique incident context to weight 1, setting all intersecting alternatives to zero; consequently an uncovered label is a rigorous contradiction over real weights. Again **217** cases are impossible immediately and **8** after one forced context. No search was left undecided.
- Independent replay: `g++ -std=c++20 -O3 -Wall -Wextra -Werror independent_phase.cpp -o /tmp/ind-phase`; `/tmp/ind-phase > /tmp/ind-phase.out`; `python -O independent_cover.py /tmp/ind-phase.out` (Python 3 + NumPy). Neither program performs external reads apart from the Python stage's input data.

## Prior work and attribution

[Klimov, Muñoz and Sainz, §III.3, Props. 2–3 and Conj. 1; §IV.1; Appendix E](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10471v2) require a **single** complete stabilizer MUB, not all stabilizer bases. They establish the all-additive support branch, ambient one and two qubits, and exclusion of a nonadditive three-point support in arbitrary ambient dimension. Their intro and Appendix E explicitly say the three-qubit analysis extends/is possible but do not supply the derivation; they give numerical evidence. Their comparison of annihilators, support periodicity, and small-support bound in Appendix E predates this note. Do not present the ambient n=3 assertion as first. Their numerical five-qubit trajectories do not certify n=5. [Turkeshi, Schirò and Sierant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11797) prove a converse over the **entire** Clifford orbit, so it does not cover one specified spread. No independent primary source resolving the unrestricted single-spread conjecture or giving this four-logical-qubit certification was identified in targeted current searches; absence is not priority.
