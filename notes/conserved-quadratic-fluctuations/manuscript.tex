\begin{abstract}
For the three-dimensional excluded-product grand-canonical hard-sphere gas
with diameter $\eps$ and activity $\mu=\eps^{-2}$, we prove a functional
Gaussian limit for the maximal thirteen-dimensional quadratic family closed
under free transport and co-located elastic collision invariance. The limit
contains an initial vacancy bias and a finite-diameter deterministic
collisional drift; its Gaussian randomness is entirely the initial field.
The proof supplies the two-root connected estimate for speed-weighted passive
records, derives a uniform incidence law of large numbers, and uses exact
mechanical identities rather than an unbounded-test functional theorem.
A corollary treats per-particle thermal energy, intrinsic virial, and spread
about the center of mass. The assertion is conditional on the explicitly
stated, commit-pinned operational history package and the existing exactly
centered Schwartz functional theorem. True-flow distributional convergence,
first-moment transfer, and pasted prelimit covariance convergence are proved
separately. The available probability coupling does not establish true
prelimit covariance convergence, and does not imply its divergence. No
full phase-space mean correction is asserted.
\end{abstract}
