The conditional boundary is explicit. Sources F and V mean the public

\begin{center}\small\texttt{\srcpin}.\end{center}
Their upstream source U is \cite{U} at

\subsection{One auxiliary dynamics}
Choose the admissible cycle-excess cap $\Gamma$ and fixed number $L$ of slabs
from U before later fixed moment orders. Put $\tau=T/L$ and, up to integer
rounding,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:caps}
 A_L=|\log\eps|,\qquad \Lambda_i=A_i^{30},\qquad
 A_{i-1}=\Lambda_i^{30}\quad(i=L,\ldots,1).
\end{equation}
An incoming contact is accepted when adding its edge keeps the connected
collision component within $\Lambda_i$ labels and excess $\Gamma$ in slab $i$.
Excess is edges minus vertices plus components; repeated contacts are repeated
edges. Accepted C contacts scatter elastically; rejected contacts continue
straight. Only collision prehistory restarts at slab interfaces. Positions
and velocities are not reset, and overlaps are allowed. Expansion O contacts
are signed non-scattering contacts and are distinct from accepted C events.

\subsection{H1--H6}
\begin{enumerate}[label=H\arabic*.,ref=H\arabic*,leftmargin=2.8em]
\item\label{H:initial}\textbf{Initial and kinetic control.}
Ordered factorial densities normalized by $\mu^{-h}$ obey
$0\le F_{h,0}^\eps\le f_0^{\otimes h}$ and
$(1-C\eps)f_0\le F_{1,0}^\eps\le f_0$.
In particular $\sup_\eps\E(N/\mu)^p<\infty$ for each fixed integer $p$.
One-root birth inputs $g_i$ have a common Gaussian Bol envelope and converge
to $f_{i\tau}$ in a common smaller Bol norm, including a partial final slab.
The kinetic bounds propagate and provide global velocity moments.

\item\label{H:algebra}\textbf{Autonomous partition and output algebra.}
For a finite label set $V$, let $\Psi_C$ be autonomous capped evolution on
block $C$, $\kappa_C$ its connected-accepted-graph indicator, and
$\chi(C,D)$ the indicator that the two autonomous blocks have no cross-contact
acceptable under their prefix prehistories. For designated roots $S$ and
a bounded root-history test $K$,
\begin{align}
 1&=\sum_{\mathcal C\in\mathcal P(V)}\prod_{C\in\mathcal C}\kappa_C
                                \prod_{\{C,D\}\subset\mathcal C}\chi(C,D),
 \label{eq:partition}\\
 K(\Psi_V|_S)&=\sum_{\mathcal C\in\mathcal P(V)}\prod_C\kappa_C
          \prod_{\{C,D\}}\chi(C,D)K(\Psi_{\mathcal C}|_S).
 \label{eq:output}
\end{align}
Expanding incompatibilities into rooted forest blocks gives a finite retained
operator $\mathcal L_s$ and positive stopped domination $\mathcal E_s$ with
$F'_s=\mathcal L_sF+R_s$ and
$|R_s(K)|\le(\mathcal E_sF)(|K|)$.
The signed kernels are root-output pushforwards multiplied by physical
indicators; their weights are independent of passive records. Spectators
sum to one before integration. Choosing $j$ fresh labels contributes precisely
$\mu^j/j!$, using
\[
 (n-s)!^{-1}\binom{n-s}{j}=[j!(n-s-j)!]^{-1}.
\]
Required recorded lines continue to time zero. Forgetting records commutes
with output pushforwards and one-root regrouping; newly unmarked singleton
blocks sum to $g_i$. These are exact algebraic identities, not independence
of the actual gas at interfaces.

\item\label{H:remainder}\textbf{Unweighted remainder and complexity tails.}
For fixed factorial order and fixed finite bounded record count, the actual
and retained factorial hierarchies satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unweighted}
 \|F_h^\eps-\widehat F_h^\eps\|_{\TV}
       \le C_h e^{-c_h\Lambda_L},\qquad\Lambda_L=|\log\eps|^{30},
\end{equation}
also on partial final slabs. Finite factorial-cumulant inversion retains
connected diagrams. Complexity is
$\rho=|H_0|+\sum_i(|H_i|+r_i)$, with continuing/designated roots $H_i$,
initial-link incident lines $H_0$, and slab cycle excess $r_i$.
At every fixed order and prescribed $P$, choosing a fixed $K$ makes the
retained sum with $\rho>K$ be $O(\eps^P)$.
Nonphysical initial tails also admit an arbitrary-power auxiliary choice.
These choices do not alter the dynamics. No energy or Gaussian weight is
assumed for a general microscopic remainder.

\item\label{H:majorant}\textbf{Chronological coordinates and positive retained bounds.}
Every global birth has one independent phase coordinate; continuation adds
none. If $E$ is total birth energy, active energy never exceeds $E$.
Mergers of disjoint chronological components have $(s,\omega)$ coordinates
with Jacobian $\eps^2B\dd s\dd\omega$. A redundant contact is a determined
incoming-intersection indicator with update fixed by preceding data; a missing
branch is zero. At fixed complexity, let $m$ be birth count, $q$ continuation
count, $p$ physical initial-link count, $n=\sum_i n_i=m+q$, and
$a=\sum_i a_i=m-1-p$ for a connected one-root history. Then
\[
 n-a=q+1+p=O_K(1),\qquad
 \mu^{m-1}\eps^{2a}\eps^{3p}=\eps^p.
\]
There are $O_K(1)$ redundant contacts. Integrating one common translation,
or prescribing a root position and taking spatial suprema, bounds the birth
product by $C^m e^{-bE}$. Fresh-label factorials, ordered times, and pair
choices give the size majorant
\begin{equation}\label{eq:majorant}
 C_K(1+n)^{D_K}(C\tau\sqrt L)^n,
                       \qquad C\tau\sqrt L<1.
\end{equation}
It holds globally and at a fixed root position with a fixed small Gaussian
reservation. The exponential base and choice of $L$ are independent of later
fixed polynomial degrees and moment orders. For $h$ designated roots the
connected normalization is $\mu^{1-h}\eps^p$, as follows from the same
birth count; see \eqref{eq:scaling} below and F's imported package.

\item\label{H:trees}\textbf{Finite-tree convergence, completeness, and splitting.}
At fixed finite size and bounded birth energy, prescribed paths, times,
normals, and velocities converge as $\eps\to0$ away from the null grazing,
zero-relative-speed, coincident-time, and extra-contact sets. There are no
undeclared contacts for sufficiently small $\eps$. Physical initial links
retain their $\eps$ factor. Cyclic histories vanish qualitatively after
truncation and domination. The surviving signed C/O trees form the complete
leading class, with signs $(-1)^{\#O}$ and inputs $f_{i\tau}$; their unmarked
one-root marginal at time $t$ is $f_t$.

After unused records are projected out, reverse-chronological C/O cancellation
removes the latest contacts meeting no tested root. Deleting the last
remaining root contact splits its tree into two incoming components at their
common position, with fresh-label factorization
\[
 (j_1+j_2)!^{-1}\binom{j_1+j_2}{j_1}=(j_1!j_2!)^{-1}.
\]
This operation is made in the finite limiting tree class with a combined-size
cutoff, removed subsequently by absolute summability. Conditional spatial
tree measures are weakly continuous in root position with uniform
conditional and integrated Gaussian bounds. Their equal-position collision
product is defined by the common-translation coordinates, equivalently by
an approximate identity.

\item\label{H:coupling}\textbf{Full-time true/pasted coupling.}
On common initial data, the probability of a trajectory discrepancy anywhere
on $[0,T]$ is at most $C\eps^{21/20}$. The almost-everywhere elastic flows
and their outgoing right-continuous velocity versions are defined, and
deterministic-time collision sets are null.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Existing functional input F}
We use the already proved exactly centered functional theorem of F: the true
and the one fixed pasted signed-measure fields converge weakly in
$D([0,T],\cS'_\beta(\R^6))$, with the generalized $J_1$ topology induced
by the strong Schwartz dual, to a Radon law supported on strongly continuous
paths. Its finite Schwartz evaluations are centered Gaussian with covariance
\begin{equation}\label{eq:field-cov}
 \E G_t(\phi)G_s(\psi)
 =\int f_0 U(0,t)\phi\,U(0,s)\psi
  +\int_0^{s\wedge t}C_r(U(r,t)\phi,U(r,s)\psi)\dd r.
\end{equation}
Here $U$ is the existing backward propagator, and $C_r$ is its collision
noise covariance. Initial covariance is $\int f_0\phi\psi$ and the noise
is independent of the initial field. The static initial bounded-test CLT
and convergence of its covariances are used in their existing scope.
For clarity, the pinned initial proof gives
$\mu\|c_{2,0}^\eps\|_{\TV}=O(\eps)$.
No unbounded positive-time test CLT and no polynomial mapping property
of $U$ is included in input F.
The source's nuclear tightness proof also supplies tightness of both path-law
families along every sequence $\eps_n\to0$. This is the tightness used in
the product argument in Section~\ref{sec:process}.
