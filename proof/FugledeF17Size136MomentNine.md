# Size 136 in F_17^3: vanishing moments through degree nine

Assume a hypothetical spectral pair of size136=8·17. As in the [F17 dense proofs](FugledeF17Sizes187204.md), D(B)⊂U_{B'} and each member has plane cap17. A projective line meets each U in at least one site. With r uniform directions guaranteed on every projective line, the affine line cap is floor((170−9r)/17), initially nine. The exact planar direction-mask census in [verify-fuglede-f17-size136-cross.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size136-cross.js) shows any eight-site planar set of cap9 determines at least six directions (105 masks, 1413 states, maximum five when allowed at most five directions). Averaging gives r≥6 and line cap6.

The same checker records these cap-six facts:
1. at most eight directions allows at most nine planar sites, and at most nine directions allows at most ten sites (3003 masks,1226352 states and 5005 masks,7780278 states, respectively);
2. a nine-site set with ≤7 directions determines exactly six, and contains two five-site affine lines of distinct directions, each with sites an affine copy of H={0,±1,±4};
3. an eight-site set with ≤7 directions also determines exactly six and has two distinct-direction arms of length≥4, each an affine subset of H (1365 masks,168285 nodes,864 normalized completions).

The normalized search chooses a marked noncollinear triangle sent to 0,e1,e2, all masks containing its three directions, and every extension of the triangle compatible with the mask and line cap. Its final short arithmetic loop verifies that the difference of any two affine four-point subsets of H has at least nine points (translate and scale the second relative to the first, allowing all possible single deletions in H). The same lower bound holds if either arm has five points, by taking a four-subset.

Suppose a nonzero degree-nine homogeneous form F vanishes on U_B and some projective line L meets U_B in at most seven sites. In the partner B', every affine slice whose linear directions lie in L has at most nine points by the planar bound. At least nine slices contain eight or nine points: if at most eight did, the other at least nine have at most seven sites, giving total≤8·9+9·7=135<136. The eight- and nine-site slice structure supplies at least eighteen arms of length≥4 distributed over at most seven axial directions. Choose two parallel arms of direction e from distinct slices. Their pairwise cross differences plus the axis give at least ten directions on a radial projective line through e, so that line is a rational factor of F.

Let k≥1 be the number of distinct rational factors of F through e. A nonfactor line through e has at most 1+9−k sites of U_B, since away from e the radial linear factors do not vanish and only the degree 9−k residual form can contribute. Because every line meets U_B at least six times, k≤4. For k=1 all nonfactor radial lines have at most nine sites of U_B, so any corresponding affine plane in B' has occupancy at most ten. For k≥2 each such plane has occupancy at most nine. The pencil through an actual arm of length≥4 has total occupancy at most 17+17·10=187 at k=1, or at most 4·17+14·9=194 at k≥2. Either contradicts required occupancy ≥136+4·17=204. Thus under F every projective line meets U_B in at least eight sites.

Put N=|U_B| and n_L=|L∩U_B|. The incidence sum Σ_L(n_L−8)(n_L−9)=N²−289N+22104 is at least1224 for integer N. Only the nine potential rational factors contribute positively, each by at most (17−8)(17−9)=72, for upper648; contradiction. Therefore every degree≤9 moment form vanishes by its uniform-normal zeros. Plane-occupancy residue profiles now have degree≤6.

A useful further deduction is that whenever a projective line contains at most eight of D(B), the associated perpendicular parallel-plane profile of B is uniform8. Its occupancies n_t lie between0 and9 by the planar cap. The deficits 9−n_t have integer mass17 and their residue is a polynomial of degree≤6. If nonconstant, each value occurs at most6 times, so there are at least eleven nonzero deficits; twelve would already have mass≥6+2·6=18. For exactly eleven positive entries of sum17 with at most six ones, the only possible multisets are five ones and six twos, or six ones, four twos and a three. Their square sums29 and31 are both nonzero modulo17. But the squares are evaluations of a degree-at-most-twelve polynomial and must sum to zero modulo17. Thus the deficit profile is constant1, as claimed.

This is a structural reduction, not an exclusion of cardinality136.
