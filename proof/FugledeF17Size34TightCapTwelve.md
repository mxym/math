# Size 34: every tight pencil has at least twelve positives

Use the [size-34 no-layer and sharp pencil/plane reductions](FugledeF17Size34PencilCapSeven.md). Write the profile weight as w=(Σ g(t)²−68)/34; in scaled integral units q=17w, a tight pencil has total q=255. Every tight pencil has at least eleven positive profiles because of the U-pencil cap seven. We rule out exactly eleven.

In an eleven-profile pencil a reduced profile has degree≤9. Degrees one through five are absent, and all profiles take their least residues without zero lifts. For cap6 and mass34, the exact centered coefficient catalogue has 16 sextics (all w=2), 48 septics (minimum w=1), 272 octics and 3008 nonics. The attached verifiers produce these lists by interpolating from the first d+1 integer values in [0,6]. They solve two of those coordinates from the zero penultimate coefficient and a prescribed leading coefficient representative, and complete the list by input dilations. The exact projected profile identity in degrees ≥1 is homogeneous with zero right-hand side, while the constant coefficient must sum to 20 modulo17.

There are ten PGL slope-mask orbits in every branch. If the highest degree is nine, all eleven profiles are genuine nonics and translation centers them all: the leading nullspace has dimension one, two normalizations for translation and the degree-eight Vandermonde equation center the other nine. Common input dilation normalizes the leading scale, because ninth powers permute F17*. The verifier builds each coefficient-vector level through degrees seven to zero, solving a homogeneous Vandermonde system and filtering each per-profile list. It prunes using the actual minimum scaled weight on each surviving list and keeps exactly q total255. At degrees seven, six it counts 48017 and 108882 paths, and no paths at degree five; thus no congruence. See [verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-nonic.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-nonic.js).

If the highest degree is eight, at least ten profiles are genuine octics. The leading rank-nine system is a two-dimensional kernel, normalized up to eighth powers (eight scaling classes). Translate two true-octic centers away, and use the degree-seven relation to enumerate all other center coefficients, keeping every lower-sextic or lower-septic center if the one lower slot occurs. This gives 2448 choices per mask. From degrees six and five there are 1358219 and 469 partial paths, none in degree four. See [verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-octic.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-octic.js).

If the highest degree is seven, there are at least nine genuine septics and at most two lower sextics. The leading rank-eight system is a three-dimensional kernel (seventh powers arbitrary); remove two true-septic centers by a translation and solve the degree-six equation for the other coefficients, including the possible nonzero sextic leading values. This gives 85412 choices per mask. There are 48537 degree-five partial paths and no degree-four path. See [verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-septic.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-b11-septic.js). The lists in both mixed branches are filtered with by-position exact minima; zero or out-of-range candidates are not omitted on the basis of weight ordering.

The only remaining case, all sextic, has total ordinary weight 11·2=22>15 and is not tight. Hence every tight pencil has at least twelve positive profiles. This alone does not exclude size34.
