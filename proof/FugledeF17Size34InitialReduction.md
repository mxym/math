# Size 34 over F_17³: layers excluded, spatial plane and uniform-pencil caps eight, fourth moments

Suppose a spectral pair of size 34=2·17 exists. We use the preliminary geometric and Radon facts in [Ferguson–Iosevich](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1). For a member B, D(B) is its projective difference-direction set and U_B its uniform plane normals (every parallel plane in that direction contains two points). Spectrality implies D(B)⊂U of the partner. By pigeonholing the partner into the 17 affine planes of any specified tangent plane, every projective line meets U_B; in particular any spatial line lies in some affine plane uniform for its member. Thus each member has line cap two. No projective line is entirely in U_B (the affine pencil occupancy identity would fail for a line containing a member point), and initially plane cap is 17, since a planar set of size greater than 17 determines every direction. Set
```
w(v)=(Σ_t binom(n_t(v),2)−17)/17,  T=P w−15,  Σw=287.
```
The identities follow by counting a pair in the 18 perpendicular normal planes if its difference is a specified direction, and once otherwise; the total number of unordered pairs is 561. A layer has two full parallel 17-site planes, and weight 15.

## No layers

For a pencil with b nonuniform profiles, the reduced Radon identities bound profile degrees by b−2 (a sole nonuniform profile has constant residue). No genuine degree one has mass 34. The centered [low-degree catalogue](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-low-profiles.js) checks all degrees two through seven, allowing zero lifts; there is no true degree at most five and the degree-six and degree-seven centered catalogue has 16 and 80 profiles respectively, no lifts. Thus if a pencil has at least thirteen uniform normals, its only nonuniform profiles are layers. A member has at most one layer, since two independent layer directions would put all sites in a 2×2 affine grid of line fibers, each of size at most two.

A full affine plane of 17 sites with line cap two determines at least 17 directions: its 136 pairs give at most eight per direction. A full plane therefore forces a layer of the opposite member, and that layer in turn forces a layer of the first. The full planes then all lie in layers, because their tangent planes must contain the unique opposite layer normal and its perpendicular pencil has only one nonuniform point in the first member (the first layer normal). In particular any ordinary profile has no zero lifts.

To exclude simultaneous layers, choose the highest genuine ordinary degree R in one member B; such a profile exists since 287>15. Then R≥6, and its first nonzero moment form F_d has d=16−R, (1≤d≤10). Degree sixteen for a profile is impossible since its mass is zero in the field. Use mutually dual normal coordinates B=(x,f_s(x),s), A=(u,t,g_t(u)), each with two supported layers and full first coordinate over F_17. This coordinate choice follows because the layer normals are mutually perpendicular, and each is a missing difference direction of the opposite set; injectivity along that axis plus size 17 gives full graphs. Multiplying by the degree-one Lagrange indicator of either s-layer shows that the local horizontal moments through degree d−2 vanish (when this bound is negative no condition is needed), so F_d(u,v,w) has degree at most one in w.

The nonzero specialization F_d(u,1,w) is not identically zero as a polynomial in w for at least 17−d≥7 values of u=a, because at least one of its coefficients in w is a nonzero univariate polynomial of degree at most d. Fix such an a. For each x the two points of A with u−a t=x have difference normal represented by (a,1,w(x)); it is a zero of F_d. Thus w(x) must be the unique finite root for this a, independent of x; a nonzero constant specialization would already contradict existence. Consequently
```
g_{t_1}(x+a t_1)−g_{t_0}(x+a t_0)=c(a)
```
for all x. For two distinct good slopes a,b align the g_{t_0} arguments; subtraction makes the difference of g_{t_1} along the nonzero step (a−b)(t_1−t_0) constant. A nonzero step generates the additive group of F_17, forcing g_{t_1} to be affine, and then so is g_{t_0}. This is a full affine line of A, impossible. Thus there are no layers or full planes, and throughout the remaining argument there are no zero lifts.

## Pencil and plane caps

At most ten uniform planes are possible initially for a pencil: b≤7 would force all ordinary profile degrees ≤5. In fact there can be no b=8 pencil and only one PGL orbit is possible at b=9. The [exact Radon test](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-eight-nine-pencils.js) verifies this with the certified centered sextic and septic catalogues:
- At b=8 all positives are sextic, the degree-six kernel is one-dimensional, and centering the first two profiles forces the others centered. The constant equation forces every leading coefficient to be nonsquare.
- At b=9 a septic forces all nine profiles septic. The leading kernel is one-dimensional and its normalization uses the fact seventh powers permute F_17*. Translation of two centers and the degree-six congruence center the others. There are five centered profiles for each leading coefficient. Their remaining homogeneous signatures are tested by a meet-in-the-middle sum.
- If b=9 and all profiles are sextic, the leading kernel is two-dimensional. Centering two profiles and using the degree-five congruence leaves one center parameter. All choices of nonzero leading coefficients and that center parameter are tested, without using a constant-term restriction before shifting.

Across 17 PGL orbits for each b, the only physical projected grid occurs at b=9, with Boolean occupancies and complementary-to-U mask represented by [0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,11]. In particular U has pencil cap nine. The [planar cap and direction-mask verifier](verify-fuglede-f17-size34-planar-masks.js) shows that no cap-two ten-set has at most ten directions. A nine-set with at most nine directions has its mask complement in the orbit of [3,5,6,8,12,13,14,15,16], different from the survivor's complement-to-U mask. If a spatial 9-site affine plane existed, its difference directions would lie in the partner's pencil of up to nine U sites and hence equal those nine sites. The two orbit requirements contradict. Thus plane cap is eight. Now the lone b=9 projected grid is also impossible: two of its nine profiles have peak ten (verified by the same Radon test), violating the new plane cap. All uniform pencils therefore have cap eight. This leaves open the 8-site planes; the cap verifier records five normalized 8-site completions with exactly eight directions and one complement orbit.

## Fourth moments

A nonzero homogeneous degree-four form F vanishing on U_B would make U_B two-blocking. To see this, suppose a line contains only one uniform direction e. Its 17 parallel affine slices of the partner B' must all be full pairs in direction e, since every slice contains at most two points and the average is two. Their 17 quotient points modulo e have line cap four by plane cap eight, so determine at least fourteen directions by the [full planar graph bound](FugledeF17Size85DegreeEightGeometry.md).

Any two parallel pairs provide at least three finite transverse root directions of F, plus e. If F has at least two distinct radial line factors through e, a radial nonfactor has at most 1+4−2=3 U sites, so all fourteen quotient slopes force factors, impossible. If there are no radial factors, each chord has exactly three finite roots. The two pairs must have a common step up to sign, because the cross difference of two two-sets has size three only in this case. For any fixed quotient slope, the three finite roots then form a full arithmetic triple; equality of these triples forces equal step sizes up to sign (compare centered square sums). Therefore all quotient chords with the same slope agree up to sign. The [nine-site equal-chord census](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-moment-six.js) excludes any noncollinear nine-subset of these 17 quotient sites, contradiction.

If there is exactly one radial factor, divide it out. On all quotient slopes except this exceptional one, the degree-three residual has three distinct finite roots from any pair of full supports, and hence is monic axially after scaling. Again all ordinary chords connect same-step pairs and the ordinary graph is complete multipartite over the exceptional parallel groups, so all supports share a common step. The axial quadratic coefficient gives a linear root mean; after subtracting it, the linear coefficient is a quadratic form on the quotient taking one fixed nonzero value on actual intergroup chord differences (the centered triple has roots 0,±σ). There are at least five groups since each contains at most four of the 17 quotient sites. Pick four representatives in distinct groups and move one to the origin: their other three position vectors have Gram matrix proportional to I+J, of rank three over F_17, impossible in a binary space. Thus no line meets U_B in just one site.

Writing N=|U_B| and n_L=|L∩U_B|, now 2≤n_L≤8, and
```
Σ_L(n_L−2)(n_L−4)=N²−91N+2456 ≥ 386 .
```
Only rational factors of F contribute positively, so their total is at most 4·(8−2)(8−4)=96. This contradicts the identity. Thus no such F exists, and all homogeneous moment forms through degree four vanish (lower degrees can be multiplied to degree four). Size 34 is not excluded here.
