# Size 68: at most eleven uniform normals per projective line

Assume the size68 no-layer conclusions of [the moment-seven reduction](FugledeF17Size68MomentSeven.md). In particular there are no zero lifts, no nonconstant profiles of degree at most three, and the spatial line cap is four. A projective line with at least twelve uniform normals has b≤6 nonuniform profiles, and by the Radon Vandermonde degree bound they have degree≤4. There must be a nonuniform profile (no projective line entirely uniform). It must be quartic, so b≥6. Thus b=6, and the degree-four homogeneous relation forces all six profiles genuinely quartic because any five distinct fourth powers are independent.

For the projected occupancy function f(x,y) in the dual quotient plane, the integer Radon formula is
```
Σ_{i=1}^6 g_i(l_i(x,y)) = 20+17 f(x,y).
```
Each fiber projects from a spatial affine line, so f is an integer in [0,4]. The leading-coefficient congruence is a rank-five equation on six coordinates. Enumerate the ten PGL(2,17) direction-mask representatives, and the four classes of common leading-value scales modulo fourth powers, represented by 1,3,9,10. A single planar translation kills the degree-three coefficients of any two quartics (their linear forms are independent); the degree-three homogeneous relation then kills the remaining four by Vandermonde. Thus all profiles are centered. Enumerate every centered integer mass68 quartic (no lifts), grouped by leading coefficient. After taking five profile choices, the last quartic's lower coefficients are determined by the Radon congruence. Test the complete homogeneous congruence for every degree and require that last profile also have mass68.

The verifier first finds 64 centered quartic profiles with integer weights ranging from 4 to 11. The verifier [verify-fuglede-f17-size68-quartic-pencils.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size68-quartic-pencils.js) visits 55,398 profile combinations and finds 12 congruence-compatible tuples. For each, the integer Radon reconstruction violates the bounds 0≤f≤4, so no physical projection exists. Therefore every projective line meets U_B in at most eleven sites. In particular a tight pencil has b≥7, and a pair in which one member's highest reduced profile degree were at most four would be impossible: each positive such profile has weight≥4 by the exact centered quartic catalogue, whereas a tight pencil sums to26.
