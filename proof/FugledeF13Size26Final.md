# Size 26 in F_13^3: final pencil exclusions and secant-count contradiction

Assume a spectral pair of cardinality26. The [preceding geometric proof](FugledeF13Size26UniformPlaneCaps.md) gives no layers, affine line cap2, affine plane cap6, and at most six uniform normals in any projective pencil. Every six-cap with at most six directions uses exactly six, in the projective orbit represented by {0,1,2,3,8,∞}. We now tighten both the plane and pencil caps to four.

## The last Radon checks

Project along the annihilator of a pencil. If it has b nonuniform directions the profiles satisfy the integer Radon identity
```
Σ_i g_i(l_i) = 2(b−1)+13 f,       0≤ f ≤2,       Σ f =26.
```
Here f is the projected multiplicity. The homogeneous Radon congruences imply residue degree≤b−2 and independence of any j+1 distinct projective jth powers. Center each genuine degree j profile by translating its argument to kill degree j−1. Nonuniform profiles of degree≤3 are absent, and the possible profiles in every degree considered below can be enumerated exactly using bounded values at d+1 distinct inputs and polynomial interpolation.

1. **Pencil cap five and plane cap four.** For a pencil with six uniform directions (b=8) use residue degree≤6. The [six-uniform verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-six-uniform.js) covers the unique uniform mask orbit coming from a six-cap, with complement of size eight. If a sextic occurs, all eight profiles are genuine sextics; the leading vector has one free scalar. Translate to kill two profile centers; the other six centers are zero by the degree-five homogeneous equation. In the no-sextic branch, genuine quintic profiles, if present, occur in at least seven directions, and there can be at most one quartic. The analogous Vandermonde equations normalize the free leading and next coefficients; the routine enumerates any residual profile shifts as required. Pure quartic is the final case. Under cap6 these exhaustive tests give no admissible Radon projection for that orbit. Thus six-point affine planes are impossible and the plane cap improves to five. With this improved cap the generic six-uniform verifier covers all five PGL orbits of the eight nonuniform directions and again finds no projection. Hence every projective line meets either U in at most five points. The planar cap-two census for five points with at most five directions is empty (same marked-triangle normalization as the [small planar cap verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-final-small-caps.js)), which improves the plane cap to four. In particular no genuine quartic occupancy profile survives.
2. **Pencil cap four.** Suppose a pencil has five uniform directions (b=9). The cap-four degree≤7 census enumerates all 5^8 choices of values in {0,1,2,3,4} at eight distinct arguments, interpolates, and tests the remaining five values and total mass26. It finds one constant, 156 genuine quintics, 286 sextics and 3120 septics before centering. Their centered counts are respectively one,12,22,240. In the genuine septic branch all nine are septic, its top coefficient vector has one free scalar, and dilation normalizes it since seventh powers permute F_13*. Translation kills the first two centers; the degree-six equation makes the other seven zero. In the sextic branch, at least eight are sextic, with possibly one quintic; the leading and next homogeneous equations give all their permitted coefficients and shifts as in the verifier. Any cubic, quadratic or genuine quartic profile is impossible at the cap. The final all-quintic case is impossible by the pair budget below. The [five-uniform verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-five-uniform.js) considers the three projective orbits of nine nonuniform directions, tests all admissible coefficient vectors, and rejects the genuine septic and sextic branches. The following paragraph explains an additional necessary congruence used to keep those scans small.

For any projected pencil, put q_B(v)=Σ_t binom(n_t(v),2) for the occupancies in each normal direction. Summing q_B over its fourteen directions gives `binom(26,2)+13 T_B(d)`, where T_B(d) is the count of unordered pairs parallel to its annihilator d: a pair in distinct projected points contributes to exactly one direction, and a pair in the same projected point contributes to all fourteen. Subtracting 14·13 and dividing by13 gives Σ_v (q_B(v)−13)/13 =11+T_B(d). Thus even when individual normalized weights are fractional, the sum Σ_v(q_B(v)−13) is divisible by13. With d7 profiles, the residue of q_B is minus the sum of coefficient products a7 a5 (coefficient of t^12 in the square, divided by two), because the centered a6 vanishes. In the uncentered d6 branch it is −a6²/2. These give exactly the optional coefficient filters used by the verifier; translations do not change q_B. If all nine profiles are quintic, each contributes integer weight1 by the occupancy census, giving the impossible equation 9=11+T_B(d). We conclude that at most four normals in any pencil are uniform.

## Counting secants

Let B be one member. Its direction set D(B) is contained in the partner's U, so every projective line contains at most four D(B) directions. Fix a direction d∈D(B). The fourteen projective lines through d partition the rest of D(B); hence |D(B)|≤1+14·3=43.

Every two affine secants parallel to d lie in an affine plane, and their four endpoints are a cap determining at most four directions. Their six distances determine two repeated directions because any direction accounts for at most two unordered pairs. Two distinct repeated directions among four points force the four points to form a parallelogram: each repeated direction supplies one of the three perfect matchings, and two distinct perfect matchings supply its opposite sides. Thus those two secant vectors agree up to sign. Orient all secants parallel to d to have one common vector v≠0. Their endpoints are b_i,b_i+v.

Project the b_i along v. The distinct quotient points form a cap, since three collinear quotient points would lift six B points into a spatial plane, violating the plane cap four. For any two quotient chords in the same projective quotient direction, write their lifts as w and w'=λw+μv, λ≠0. The first chord gives the three transverse spatial directions represented by w−v,w,w+v, plus d; these exhaust its spatial projective pencil intersection. The second gives the same three transverse directions, so
```
{(μ−1)/λ, μ/λ, (μ+1)/λ} = {−1,0,1}.
```
The sums give μ=0, and the sums of squares give λ²=1. Therefore all projected chords in any direction have one fixed difference vector up to sign. The [exact equal-chord verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-equal-chords.js) normalizes a marked affine triangle (any putative eight-cap has one), then scans every further point in increasing order, requiring this fixed-vector condition. It visits 3134 states, reaches seven sites, and never reaches eight. Consequently each of the at most 43 directions of B has at most seven secants. But all unordered pairs of B appear once, so 325=binom(26,2)≤7·43=301, contradiction.

## Implication for F_13^3

The cardinality reduction of Aten et al., recalled in Ferguson–Iosevich [arXiv:2610.30712v1, Proposition 8.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1), gives tiling ⇒ spectrality, prime-power spectral cardinalities imply tiling, and all other spectral cardinalities are multiples 13m with 2≤m≤12. The [general bound of Malikiosis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43670-023-00078-2) excludes cardinalities strictly between 169−12√13 and169, covering 130,143,156. [The full theorem index](FugledeF13Theorem.md) gives the separate exact exclusions for cardinalities 26, 39, 52, 65, 78, 91, 104, and 117. Thus every nonempty spectral set in F_13^3 tiles and conversely. This theorem is dimension-and-field specific; it does not assert Fuglede for larger primes, higher dimension, or Euclidean space.
