# Size 26 in F_13^3: no layers, affine plane cap six, and uniform-pencil cap six

Assume a spectral pair (E,A) of size26. Use the general preliminary facts in [Ferguson–Iosevich, arXiv:2610.30712v1 §§8–9](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1): each member is an affine cap (no three collinear), each plane has at most thirteen points, D(E)⊂U_A and D(A)⊂U_E. Uniform means occupancy two at each of the thirteen parallel levels. Neither U contains a projective line: otherwise a pencil through an affine line with a member points would sum to 14·2, whereas it must equal 26+13a. A layered profile has exactly two thirteen-point affine planes.

For any normal pencil with b nonuniform profiles, project the member along the common annihilator of the pencil. The integer Radon identity is sum_i g_i(l_i)=2(b−1)+13 f; f takes values 0,1,2. After reducing profiles to polynomials over F_13, the Vandermonde homogeneous equations force degree≤b−2 for b≥2, and constant for b=1. Every nonconstant affine profile has mass at least 78. The [compact centered coefficient census](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-final-small-caps.js) shows no genuine quadratic or cubic profile of mass26 with cap13. (Translate the penultimate coefficient away, list all a≠0 and lower coefficients, and allow a zero lift only if mass is preserved.) Hence a pencil with at least nine uniform directions (b≤5) forces all nonuniform profiles constant modulo13. The only such nonuniform type is a layer, and the Radon constant equation 2(b−1)=0 makes b=1.

## No layers

This is the general p=13 version of §9 Lemma 9.1 of the above preprint. If E lies in two layers, each thirteen-point planar cap determines at least thirteen directions by pair counting: its 78 pairs use at most six pairs per direction. As the tangent projective line cannot be full in D(E), the union has exactly thirteen directions, each used by exactly six pairs and one singleton of each slice. The partner A has at least thirteen U normals in that pencil, so it is likewise layered by the preceding argument. Let n be the layer normal of E. For each of the thirteen tangent directions d, every normal in U_E∩d^⊥ must put the two singletons of the E layers on the same level: the other points are already paired parallel to d and every occupied level needs two points. The two singletons have a cross-layer difference not parallel to d, so there is at most one such normal per d. The missing tangent direction contributes at most thirteen U normals on the one remaining pencil line through n. Hence |D(A)|≤|U_E|≤26. But A's layers contribute thirteen tangent directions and the cross-layer directions are in bijection with a planar difference set Q−P of its thirteen-point caps. This has more than thirteen elements: otherwise the translated sets Q−p (p∈P) would coincide and Q would be invariant under a nonzero translation, forcing its thirteen points to form a full affine line. Thus |D(A)|≥27, contradiction.

In particular the uniform-pencil bound is now eight. Any eight-site affine cap determines more than eight directions (planar census below), so the plane cap is seven.

## Uniform pencils with seven or eight directions

For exactly seven uniform normals the seven nonuniform profiles have degree≤5; all genuinely quartic unless a quintic occurs, when all seven are genuine quintics by the leading Vandermonde equation. The centered mass26 quartic/quintic catalogs from the coefficient enumeration have respectively 9 and 36 profiles, all with peak≤6 (thus a cap6 catalog covers also cap7). Normalize each seven-direction mask by PGL, giving five orbits. In the quintic case its leading coefficients have a one-dimensional solution space, and a common planar dilation makes the last leading coefficient 1 since fifth powers permute F_13*. Translate the projected coordinates to kill the centers of its first two profiles, and the quartic homogeneous equation forces the remaining five centers zero. In the quartic case there are two free leading coefficients. After eliminating the first two centers by translation, the remaining center equations (cubic homogeneous congruence) solve four centers in terms of the last. The verifier [verify-fuglede-f13-size26-seven-uniform.js](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-seven-uniform.js) exhausts both branches and gets no polynomial Radon identity for any representative. Therefore exactly seven U normals is impossible.

For exactly eight uniform normals, the six nonuniform profiles are all genuine quartics. The leading coefficients are determined up to one nonzero scalar, and translation normalizes the first two centers; the remaining four centers vanish by cubic-column independence. The nine centered cap-seven profiles have constant 2 or 6. The constant relation requires 12+4k≡10 mod13 where k is the number of constant-six profiles, so k=6. Each such profile is a t^4+6 with a∈{7,8,11}. The verifier [verify-fuglede-f13-size26-eight-uniform.js](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-eight-uniform.js) normalizes an ordered triple of directions to x,y,x+y, exhausts all 165 choices of the other three slopes and all leading scalars (1980 possible leading vectors). Exactly 15 tuples satisfy the polynomial relation, on five normalized bad masks. Every reconstructed f has exactly one double site (its center), 24 single sites, and 144 zero sites. Each of the six bad Radon lines through the center has mass six.

## The cap census and elimination of the exceptional eight pencils

The verifier [verify-fuglede-f13-size26-planar-caps.js](verify-fuglede-f13-size26-planar-caps.js) exhausts normalized planar caps of six, seven, or eight sites using at most eight directions. Any such cap is noncollinear, so choose a marked affine triangle and send it to 0,e_1,e_2. Enumerate masks of eight directions containing the triangle's three slopes, and every compatible extension of the triangle, enforcing that no line has three sites. This exhausts the possibilities up to affinity (the program does not need to identify full affine orbits). It computes each actual direction mask's PGL orbit by normalizing all ordered triples.

There is no eight-cap. Any seven-cap uses exactly seven directions in a single orbit, none fitting any of the five possible eight-U masks (the verifier tests all subsets of the uniform complements). Such a seven-cap cannot fit a pencil with seven U either, so the plane cap improves to six. Likewise no six-cap fits any of the eight-U masks. Any six-cap must then use at most six directions, and the checker shows it uses exactly six in the unique orbit represented by {0,1,2,3,8,∞}. Its direction multiplicities are always (2,2,2,3,3,3). In particular every direction of a six-cap is used by at least two pairs.

Now suppose an eight-U pencil exists. Its projection has exactly one double site at its center, so its annihilator axis is realized by exactly one pair of member points. Each of the six bad central lines lifts to a spatial affine plane with six member points containing that pair: their occupancy is six by the associated constant coefficient. Each is a six-cap, which would require at least two pairs in the same direction as this unique pair. Contradiction. Thus both U sets meet every projective line in at most six sites, and affine planes have cap six. These reductions are used in the [final exclusion of cardinality 26](FugledeF13Size26Final.md).
