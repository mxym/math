# Scope, comparison and prior screen (2026-11-19)

1. **Question.** [J. Neunhäuserer, Recursive overlap Bernoulli distributions and an entropy concavity conjecture, arXiv:2609.05546v2, pp. 1–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05546v2). Conjecture 3.1 concerns ordinary Shannon entropy along a *single common parameter*, for each level n≥1 of every strictly increasing integer sequence a_i beginning at 2. There is no assumption a_{i+1}≤2a_i. Proposition 3.1 proves the binomial and fully nonoverlapping cases. If there are gaps in the indexed vector we take 0 log 0=0, equivalently ignore identically empty fibers. In PROOF.md the ambiguity cap two is an actual restriction, not a characterization of all recursions. The triple obstruction in SHARPNESS.md is to general **deterministic** observations only. It cannot arise from strictly positive weights.

2. **Prior discussion of the same conjecture.** The [author's MathOverflow thread and Amir's September answer](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/514729/a-recursive-overlap-construction-of-finite-bernoulli-sum-laws-and-an-entropy-con/515134) already write down the weighted-sum interpretation and the subset-count polynomial. The answer includes analytical work at two and three bits and reports a four-bit computational check. The exhibited **different-parameter** counterexample at three bits, weights (1,1,2) and (p₁,p₂,p₃)=(1/5+t,1/5−t,1/2), is already there. It is not on the common-parameter diagonal and does not refute Conjecture 3.1. Our deterministic-map obstruction, by contrast, has one common varying p but its output is nonadditive. No heterogeneously biased counterexample or first weighted-sum representation is claimed here.

3. **Existing entropy concavity and comparison results.** [Shepp and Olkin, Entropy of the sum of independent Bernoulli random variables and of the multinomial distribution](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Sum-of-idpt-bernoulli_rotated.pdf) include binomial diagonal concavity. [Hillion and Johnson, Bernoulli 23 (2017), Theorem 1.2](https://doi.org/10.3150/16-BEJ860) establish Shannon concavity even jointly in the parameters when the summands are the ordinary unweighted bits. Their theorem therefore handles some arbitrarily large fibers, not arbitrary positive integer multipliers or arbitrary output maps. [Wang, arXiv:2609.27433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27433) studies Rényi and Tsallis critical orders in that ordinary-sum setting. [Li, arXiv:2609.01529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01529) compares different Rényi orders for weighted Bernoulli sums, not derivatives in the common bias. The ordinary chain rule for entropy, conditional score/variance identities and Jensen's inequality are standard ingredients. The all-order binary-fiber theorem and failure of its extension to all three-point maps were not found in our searches; we make no priority guarantee.
