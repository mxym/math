# Internal mathematical review: two-point Bernoulli fibers and a three-point counterexample

Reviewed frozen PROOF.md 05d369e12696cee81eb8b8f943a38e80f84b0ef8af2b19ca29974464bf1069cf and SHARPNESS.md 680ca9e5e1752152cdc796e7c0946ab08533c4f80eca0348393f547d2d716c9c. Both PASS. The sharpness example is not a weighted subset-sum construction and does not refute the general conjecture. This is not external peer review.

The chain rule writes the entropy loss as conditional entropy on the fibers. Differentiating twice gives the curvature stated in the proof; the score-square terms from the two entropy differentiations differ by minus the mean conditional score variance. The powers of p and q are strictly positive in the interior. At every p the unconditional mean score square and the unconditional negative second log derivative both equal n/(pq). The split by the factors 1−log 2 and log 2 is therefore exact.

For any binary posterior, using −log(2t)≥1−2t in the variance expression leaves exactly t(1−t)(s₁+s₂)². In the second part, posterior odds across k,l are x^(l−k); multiplying by v_l/v_k at most x^((l−k)/2) leaves odds at least x^((l−k)/2) in favor of k. With z=(l−k)log x/2 the log posterior shifts are ±z−log cosh z, so the signed expectation is at least z tanh(z/2)−log cosh z. Jensen on the concave tanh restricted to [0,z] proves the claimed sign. The limiting cases x=1 and k=l produce exact zero.

The v ratio increases as the lower index decreases, hence is at most the k=1 expression. With y=√x, subtraction of its numerator from the denominator multiplied by y factors as (y−1)(y⁴−y³−y²−y+n−2). At n=5, setting y=1+b rewrites the latter factor as 1−2b+2b²+3b³+b⁴, nonnegative because 1−2b+2b²=(1−b)²+b²; larger n increases it. For n=2,3,4 the same algebra applies to the augmented score weights with n replaced by 5 while the original posterior (same ratio of word weights) is used. The extra term costs at most (5−n)log 2 times the nonsingleton probability divided by q². The forced endpoints yield exactly the stated positive curvature budget after multiplication by q². The inequalities hold already on replacing log 2 by 7/10; the strict endpoint contribution is enough. n=1 and reflected p≥1/2 are separately valid. Extension to the closed interval follows from strict interior curvature and continuity.

The original weighted-sum case is included by positive increments in the recursion and unique extreme subsets; the symmetry p↔1−p follows by image reflection and need not hold for an arbitrary observation. The separate 24-bit permutation has just one fixed word and its orbits have average Hamming weight 12. Removing the all-00, all-01 and all-11 orbit as three singletons leaves four singletons total. At p=1/2 each non-singleton mass derivative vanishes, and the four singleton score squares sum to 8n². Direct substitution yields 8n[(n−2)log 3−n], strictly positive at n=24 because the first three positive atanh series terms give log 3≥263/240>12/11. Comparable merged strings exclude this map from strictly positive additive observations.

[Neunhäuserer v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05546v2) is the exact named setting. The [author's MO thread](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/514729/a-recursive-overlap-construction-of-finite-bernoulli-sum-laws-and-an-entropy-con) already describes its equivalence to common-parameter sums and low-order checks, and [Hillion–Johnson](https://doi.org/10.3150/16-BEJ860) proves concavity for the unweighted Bernoulli sum. These are credited inputs, not new claims.
