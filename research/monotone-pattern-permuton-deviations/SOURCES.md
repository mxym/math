# Prior results and search scope

**The same model and encoding.** [Hoffman–Rizzolo–Slivken, *Scaling limits of permutations avoiding long decreasing sequences*, arXiv:1911.04982v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.04982v4), §2.1 pp. 3–4, already define precisely the canonical layers (successively peel left-to-right maxima) and record them as the two words read on the coordinate axes. These are exactly the longest-decreasing-ending heights and the two axis words in LDP.md §2, not new encodings. Their Theorem 2.1 gives a much finer **typical fluctuation** limit for the unweighted paths about the increasing diagonal: the eigenvalues of a traceless Hermitian Brownian bridge. We do not claim new unweighted diagonal concentration or their process-level limit. Their coupling controls the typical regime; our profile translation and robust canonical witnesses instead lower-count arbitrary macroscopic permuton neighborhoods at exponential speed.

**Direct size-three precedent.** Budzinski, Dubach, Féray, Kammoun and Maïda, [*Large deviation principles for pattern-avoiding permutations, and limit shapes for constrained Mallows permutations*, arXiv:2604.23751v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23751v1), use the same $q=e^{\beta/n}$ inversion bias. Their Propositions 4–5 and Corollary 20 give the direct LDP precedent, in excursion and two-measure coordinates for 231 and 321 respectively. Theorem 7 gives the explicit 321-avoiding near-uniform Mallows shape; Proposition 8, equation (6), gives its free energy. Our $d=2$ integral is algebraically the same; the checker also verifies the two-curve formula in exponentiated coordinates. Their results treat all single length-three patterns by symmetry, whereas our uniform and tilted statements cover the monotone family for every fixed length and not arbitrary length-four patterns. None of the 321 conclusions, logistic-shaped binary profiles, or their two-measure entropy is presented as new.

**Counts, other deviation scales, and existing tools.** [Regev, *Asymptotic values for degrees associated with strips of Young diagrams*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(81)90012-8), via the tableau correspondence, already obtains the unweighted exponential growth $d^2$ and substantially sharper asymptotics. [Madras–Pehlivan, *Large Deviations for Permutations Avoiding Monotone Patterns*](https://doi.org/10.37236/6225) determine exact exponential probabilities for a **single prescribed point** far from the typical diagonal/antidiagonal in the same monotone avoidance family. Moving one point is invisible in weak permuton distance; that probability is not determined by, nor identical to, this whole-measure LDP. [Atapour–Madras](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/combinatorics-probability-and-computing/article/abs/large-deviations-and-ratio-limit-theorems-for-patternavoiding-permutations/80A4BAB0A728DF541FD373284229BDD0) give earlier regional and ratio estimates. The inversion-generating function over an unrestricted multiset word is the classical MacMahon Gaussian multinomial, not a new enumerator; see [Canfield–Janson–Zeilberger](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.2089). Exponential tilting, hyperbolic-sine normalization, elementary Bernoulli conditioning and entropy of type counts are standard. A signed pair of uncoloured-word inversion statistics is exact only on the canonical subset: we do **not** identify the finite restricted partition function with the unrestricted Gaussian multinomial product.

**Adjacent results, not interchangeable.** [Borga–Féray–Winkler, §4.1, Problem 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12104v1), November 2026, explicitly credits the size-three case and asks which other uniform classes admit LDPs and their rates. A uniform draw from a pattern-avoiding class differs from drawing i.i.d. points from a fixed permuton. The repository’s [geometric-grid #222](../geometrically-griddable-permuton-ldp/README.md) uses a geometric trace model, [#226](../permutation-class-limit-law-counterexamples/README.md) shows that speed $n$ need not work for every exponentially growing hereditary class, and [#241](../small-class-permuton-large-deviations/README.md) gives a block-flow LDP for MSO regular position interpretations and therefore all small classes. For $d\ge2$ the present full classes have growth $d^2\ge4$ and are not small. Here there is a direct entropy of arbitrary increasing-colour marginals and an explicitly solved constrained Mallows tilt. The new steps are the two-axis entropy-preserving curve sorting/translation lower bound and using equality of signed-surrogate and canonical exponential free energies to transfer the logistic law for general $d$.
