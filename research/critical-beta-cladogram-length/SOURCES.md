# Sources and scope

* **Original question.** David Aldous and Svante Janson, [*The Critical Beta-splitting Random Tree II: Overview and Open Problems*, arXiv:2303.02529v3, §6.1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.02529v3#S6.SS1.SSS1). Equations (72)–(73) specify the leaf-up drawing; (75) leads to the proposed order in Open Problem 14. Problem 15 asks about its level-crossing profile. The same questions had numbers 11 and 12 in [v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.02529v2). The discrete and continuous trees are different objects. We prove a first-order result for the discrete drawing, not a fluctuation theorem.
* **First-order ingredients already known.** Formula (51) and Theorem 18 in [Aldous–Janson II, §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.02529v3#S4.SS1) distinguish a tagged-leaf occupation probability from the expected clade count per leaf, and identify its limit. See also [Aldous, Janson and Li, *The Harmonic Descent Chain*](https://doi.org/10.1214/24-ECP645). Heng Ma, [*The Height of Discrete-Time Critical Beta-Splitting Trees*, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14329v2#S1), proves a stronger maximum-height theorem than the moment bound we need. In particular, that theorem and the deterministic bound by (2n−2) times maximum height already exclude the proposed order, even before identifying the linear constant.
* **What follows directly from existing occupation results.** Anna Brandenberger, Byron Chin and Elchanan Mossel, [*Asymptotics for the harmonic descent chain and applications to critical beta-splitting trees*, §1.1 and Corollary 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24821v3#S1.SS1). The unnumbered statement immediately following (1.7) gives weighted convergence of the occupation probabilities for nonnegative weights of polynomial decay. Once the length is written as a sum of fringe heights, choosing weight E h(T_m)/m gives the **mean** limit directly. Corollary 1.4 gives the linear variance for every fixed size or shape count, an input to the random-profile limit. The separate result for the total length of continuous exponential branches (Corollary 1.5) is not the length here.
* **Nearby but different.** [Svihla and Lladser, *The Phylogenetic Structure of β-diversity: Covariance Matrix Sparsification of Critical Beta-splitting Trees*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-026-01655-x), Theorem 2.1, concerns the sum of the root-to-leaf **edge counts**, the external path length. Its variance result is not a new conclusion here. [Harmonic-descent occupation asymptotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00347), [extremal fringe shapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24973), [homogeneous-fragmentation asymptotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18320), and [the leaf-height Mellin analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12319) likewise study related objects.
