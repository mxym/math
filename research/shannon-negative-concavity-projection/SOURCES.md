# Formulations and preceding results

* **The conjecture refuted here** is [Madiman, Melbourne and Xu, *Forward and Reverse Entropy Power Inequalities in Convex Geometry*, arXiv:1604.04225v2, Conjecture 3.22](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.04225v2#page=29). It assumes joint symmetry and measure $\kappa$-concavity and asks for a reverse inequality in every entropy order $p\in[1,\infty]$ when the quantities are finite. Here only $p=1$ is needed. The same counterexample applies to the planar version of [Li, *Rényi entropy power inequality and a reverse*, arXiv:1704.02634v2, Conjecture 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.02634v2#page=7), whose order range includes $[0,\infty]$. Li's numbered statement does not say “symmetric”, but the subsequent planar formulation does; the witness satisfies both interpretations. The numbering here refers to the linked preprints.
* **An earlier unbounded ratio in a distinct class.** [Bobkov and Madiman, *On the Problem of Reversibility of the Entropy Power Inequality*, Theorem 1.3 and §3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.6807), constructed independent copies of a *one-dimensional* convex law whose sum and difference have arbitrarily large Shannon entropy power relative to a summand. This is an important antecedent, not an example satisfying the present joint hypothesis. Their density is proportional to $1/x$ on $(1,b)$; the independent joint density is proportional to $1/(xy)$ on its positive box. At $(a,c)$ and $(c,a)$ it has the same value, but at their midpoint a strictly smaller value by AM–GM if $a\ne c$. It is not even jointly quasiconcave, and is not centrally symmetric. The distinction here is the construction of a genuinely jointly $\kappa$-concave and coordinatewise symmetric law, not the mere fact that entropy ratios can diverge for heavy-tailed marginals.
* **Narrower positive statements.** [Ball, Nayar and Tkocz, *A reverse entropy power inequality for log-concave random vectors*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05926), posed the narrower Shannon norm conjecture for symmetric **log-concave** vectors and proved subadditivity for the $1/5$ power of this gauge. A [2026 working paper of Kominers](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7519179) reports the $9/16$ power for that log-concave class. Neither is contradicted, and the original log-concave norm question is not settled here. Likewise, the all-convex min-entropy result in MMX, Li's order-two result for log-concave densities, and the uniform symmetric convex-body cross-section problem discussed in [Li's lecture, slides 16–17](https://im.hit.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/ce/bd/cdd3e7af48ea8039008043fdde85/d0afd352-a3b2-415b-8b17-e2d2e0191d4a.pdf) are different statements.
* **Tools and search limits.** The convex-density/measure parameter correspondence is Borell's ([MMX, §3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.04225v2); see also the discussion around (10) in [Bobkov and Madiman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.6807)). Layer mixtures, entropy change of variables and continuity of $L^1$ translations are standard.
