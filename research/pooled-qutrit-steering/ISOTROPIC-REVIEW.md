**Verdict: PASS** for a positive but unspecified uniform gap. An independent reviewer checked the author's frozen analytic proof (SHA-256 ae16ac90f19f1207b5dd1b530aada224f45799a431e36a37d5f979be99c5d824) and derived the simpler three-phase proof used in [the main text](ISOTROPIC.md). * **Source and scope.** In the original Eqs. (20), (22), (23), (25), (32) and (33), the number selected across the entire list is fixed, not the numbers in individual bases. For each subset the projector sum is positive and its top eigenstate attains the maximum. The three-step isotropic list has a single interior Lorenz breakpoint. Concavity extends the inequality at that breakpoint to comparison with the actual concave lattice join, not with a nonconcave list of raw prefix suprema.
* **Probability measures.** The top-third fractional linear program and the cutoff formulation agree even for atomic measures or threshold ties. The integrands converge uniformly over the basis variable as the state and cutoff converge. Minimizing over one compact domain and maximizing over another preserves continuity, without swapping max and min. Weak compactness gives attainment. A pointwise upper bound on the continuous one-max-per-basis function that equals its full-support Haar mean forces equality at every state, including those in a fixed measure-zero flat torus.
* **Support and pair rigidity.** A nonnegative continuous cutoff excess of mean zero vanishes on the whole support, including bases of zero individual mass. In law a row can be made real and nonnegative by rephasing a flat input, without making simultaneous rephasing or independent-row assumptions. The order-three action yields at least one strictly positive deviation almost surely and two on a sign-center neighborhood. Linearity of expectation rules out every matrix without a monomial row. The alternative [Laurent proof](ISOTROPIC-PAIR-APPENDIX.md) was checked separately, including the pivot arc, both simple zeros, coefficient extraction, unitary zero patterns, and the unique order-three complex Hadamard.
* **From pairs to uniformity.** In dimension three, pairwise sharing of rays for orthonormal bases in the support forces a global common ray. At that state the mean of the maxima is one, contradicting Haar saturation. Compact attainment makes the non-equality a uniform separation over all setting measures.
* **Finite checks and prior art.** [The independent checker](independent_isotropic.py) performs [19,131 exact phase probes](ISOTROPIC-INDEPENDENT-CHECKS.txt) on explicitly unitary matrices, checks 490 sign-orbit centers, verifies the Fourier witness algebraically, and checks 329 finite menus and their cutoffs. These tests validate calculations, not the continuum theorem by enumeration. The original steering thresholds, earlier flat-torus geometry, one-per-setting incompatibility witness, and previous above-mean probability results are distinguished in [the source notes](ISOTROPIC-SOURCES.md). No earlier resolution of this particular pooled question was identified; that search cannot certify priority.
