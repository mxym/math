# Sources and exact scope

- Claude Carlet and Darrion Thornburgh, [Resolving a conjecture on quadratic APN functions and a new quadratic (n,n)-function associated to crooked functions](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23888v2), arXiv:2608.23888v2, 7 September 2026. Use Proposition 4.2 for the degree bound, Definition 5.1 for the adjoint, the proof of Lemma 5.2 for Walsh differences, and Theorem 5.9 (including its proof's polar-sum formula) for the APN/sum-free equivalence. Open Problem 5.10 requests crooked examples, and Gold Proposition 5.17 already gives the Gold subfamily. Proposition 5.11 records the transformation rule used for the optional EA-class interpretation. None of our claims depends on the general nonvanishing assertion following Theorem 5.5.
- Lilya Budaghyan, Claude Carlet and Gregor Leander, [Constructing new APN functions from known ones](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2008.10.001), *Finite Fields and Their Applications* 15(2), 150–159 (2009), original switched-cube construction. See also Emanuele Bellini, [Testing CCZ-equivalence to permutations via projections of Walsh zero spaces, with applications to APN functions](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10269v1), §7, for the parameterized formula. This note does not rediscover the APN map.
- Yves Edel and Alexander Pott, [A new almost perfect nonlinear function which is not quadratic](https://www.yvesedel.de/Papers/switch.pdf), §2 for the graph development and \(\Gamma\)-rank, and its six-variable switching-class invariants table for 1102 and 1146. Marcus Brinkmann and Gregor Leander, [On the Classification of APN Functions up to Dimension Five](https://www.nds.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/media/nds/veroeffentlichungen/2019/03/19/apn-self-archive.pdf), Table 5, is another early primary cross-check for those ranks. Our two computations regenerate the ranks.
- Christof Beierle, Philippe Langevin, Gregor Leander, Alexandr Polujan and Shahram Rasoolzadeh, [Classification of quadratic APN functions up to EA-equivalence](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22010376), v1 (2026), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The catalogue checks are optional; the supplementary 15 coefficients encode row 2 (one-based) of [QAPN-6-6](https://zenodo.org/records/22010376/files/QAPN-6-6?download=1). Row 2 decoded into 64 bytes has SHA-256 `5ab7a0bd635cdb2ee8c2520d9db65a7a4c7e9c28bdd9898ba042451476b7a1db`. For exact replay run `python3 verify_independent.py /path/to/QAPN-6-6`. On 27 November 2026 we checked both public versions of arXiv:2608.23888 and searched the exact paper identifier, “exclude parity adjoint”, “ortho-derivative”, “sum-free”, “non-Gold”, “1146”, and “Open Problem 5.10” in relevant combinations. The original paper reports the Gold case and a non-APN adjoint for the Kim example in v2. The sources located for the rank-1146 class and switched-cube family already establish the underlying APN class, but we did not locate a report of its APN adjoint or fourth-order sum-free ortho-derivative. This is not an absolute priority claim. The note neither resolves Open Problem 5.18 about self-adjoint examples, nor says that all switched-cube parameters in other dimensions have this property.
