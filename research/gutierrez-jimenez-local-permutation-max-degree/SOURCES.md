# Sources and precise scope

1. Jaime Gutierrez and Jorge Jiménez Urroz, [*Permutation and local permutation polynomials of maximum degree*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13370-025-01247-3), *Afrika Matematika* 36 (2025), article 45; [version of record HTML](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13370-025-01247-3), [preprint arXiv:2308.01258v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01258v2). Theorem 1 identifies the swap \(t\), §5 Eq. (3) defines the recurrence, Theorems 7–8 and the text before Theorem 9 give the settled low arities, and §6 Conjecture 1 asks for the maximum-degree assertion. The mathematically nontrivial claim concerns \(n\geq2\): the literal base \(f_1=X\) has degree 1. Section 6 separately asks for constructions over the prime field for arbitrary \(q\); its Theorem 6 has coefficients in the full target field, while Theorems 4–5 give prime-field coefficients in covered cases.
2. Same authors, [*Local Permutation Polynomials of Maximum Degree Over Prime Finite Fields*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40840-025-01825-5), *Bulletin of the Malaysian Mathematical Sciences Society* 48 (2025), article 40; [version of record HTML](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40840-025-01825-5). The introduction (HTML lines 62–78) formulates the prime-field existence question and gives Theorems 1–2: bounded arities in characteristic at least five and all arities when \(p\mid r\). Theorem 5 reuses the coprime-exponent range. Section 4 Conjecture 1, Eq. (6) (HTML lines 249–261), restates the identical iterated construction and explicitly separates the degree claim from the already-known local-permutation property. Its Corollary 2 treats the **non-iterated** expression \(t(\sum x_i^{q-2})\), not a counterexample to the recurrence. Section 3 discusses extension parameters not covered by either sufficient condition.
3. Raúl M. Falcón, Jaime Gutiérrez and Jorge Jiménez Urroz, [*An algebraic approach to Latin squares of prime power order by local permutation polynomials*](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.09086v1), arXiv:2510.09086v1 (2025). Its outlook and bibliography cite both preceding papers in the context of further multivariate problems. No same-scope answer to either question was found there.

The recurrence result is an exact test of its top coefficient, not a classification of all LPPs. The separate existence theorem does not claim priority for characteristic two, \(p\mid r\), the authors' coprime-exponent range, or their bounded arities. The new components needed for the previously uncovered range are the general Frobenius-orbit exchange for nontrivial extensions in characteristic at least five and the uniform characteristic-three recurrence criterion.

A bounded primary-source check on 2026-11-27 consulted the two versions of record, their preprint and follow-up, and searched for `"Permutation and local permutation polynomials of maximum degree" "counterexample" "Conjecture 1"`, `"s13370-025-01247-3" "counterexample" "conjecture"`, `"Gutierrez" "Jiménez Urroz" "local permutation" "q=25" conjecture coefficient recursive`, and `"local permutation polynomials of maximum degree" "prime field" Frobenius transposition construction`. We did not find the same counterexamples, rational coefficient criterion, or full prime-field existence result in the sources checked. This is not a claim of exhaustive bibliographic priority.
