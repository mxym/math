# Independent audit of the worst-case factor for random indivisible chores

**PASS for the stated worst-case theorem**. This is a separate internal AI review, not external peer review. The model uses full allocation, additive strictly positive costs, independent draws at all agent–chore coordinates from one common fixed nonatomic two-sided PDF-bounded law. For each c∈(1,2) one may choose a different law, then a subsequence of market sizes along which m/n≥c and failure has probability tending to one. Neither the probability for all sufficiently large sizes under one law, the behavior at m=2n−O(1), nor a threshold for natural distributions is determined.

## Source and quantifiers

[Manurangsi and Suksompong, IJCAI 2025](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/0444.pdf) and the [expanded SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics article](https://doi.org/10.1137/25M180161X), Thm. 1.1, give the fixed-distribution high-probability upper result for every m≥2n, along with a polynomial-time algorithm. Their Thm. 1.2 gives a different lower result: nonexistence for **every** underlying nonatomic cost law below (ν−ε)n, with ν≈1.1256 defined by their scalar equation. Lemma 3.1 is the single-round repeated-favorite obstruction. The last section asks whether the universal factor 2 can be lowered; it does not claim that the uniform-law threshold is two, or that there is such a single-law sharp limit in general. The new subsequence negates each proposed distribution-independent improvement. For any proposed c'≤1, choose a ratio between max(c',1) and 2; no literal extension of the construction to c'≤1 is needed. The familiar zero-cost empty market is irrelevant, since both dimensions diverge.

## Deterministic forcing

Consider one EF partition, with agent i's strictly ranked certified prefix. A singleton chore at any index in that prefix has cost to i less than the smallest possible total cost of any multichore bundle, by positivity and distinctness of the chores. Thus if there is at least one such singleton, the *earliest* singleton in i's complete ranking must belong to i. Take a round-(t+1) request for z. If any earlier ranked chore h is singleton, the globally earliest such h is also in the prefix, hence must be owned by i; but there is a request by another agent for h at time t, hence by induction h must belong to that agent. No contradiction rests on combining two different possible allocations. If no earlier singleton, then z itself, when singleton, is forced to i. Two different requesters therefore exclude a singleton. The round-1 empty antecedent and iterations on cycles cause no difficulty: this is induction in discrete time on a fixed finite graph, with no need for exclusivity of requests sent by one row to different chores. Every bundle is nonempty in an EF partition of a nonempty market; the deterministic maximum number of nonsingleton chores equals at most 2(m−n).

## Local limit and concentration

Use the actual random ordered lists, not independent Poisson variables at the market level. Write a=1/(m) for the one-position unconditioned hit chance conditional on an active row. Given b previously queried forbidden labels, for an untouched row the probability of hitting the next label at a prescribed rank conditional on avoiding those b is ρ/m+O_{K,b}(m⁻²), since pair hits of distinct labels are O(m⁻²). On a capped exploration only O(1) rows were removed, all remaining rows are still independent under the separate avoidance conditions, and a new hit's other positions are uniform distinct choices outside the revealed forbidden labels. Multinomial sparse cell counts therefore tend jointly to independent Poisson(ρ/c) variables indexed by rank. Conditional no-collision errors on bounded exploration size are O_{B,K}(1/m), including cross-collisions between two roots. The expected number of simple alternating paths of length 2t is bounded by n^t m^t [K(K−1)/(m(m−1))]^t, because each distinct row must meet its two specified labels, irrespective of activation; for odd lengths append nK/m. This yields uniform tightness of the exploration size without incorrectly assuming bounded degrees on item vertices. Fixed t,K are essential. Take n,m→∞ for capped trees first, then cap→∞.

A reached agent contributes a request at rank ℓ exactly if each of its earlier ℓ−1 new item subtrees supplies at least one request from outside that agent. The cavity condition is thus enforced and the subtrees are independent in the limit. Correlations of two requests made by the same agent are neither neglected nor relevant on one upward edge. Summing marked Poisson offspring gives λ, tail probabilities p_t and f_t. Because of exchangeability, the variance of the fraction is its one-root variance divided by m plus (1−1/m) times the two-distinct-root covariance, which vanishes by the joint exploration. Counting mean alone would not suffice; this verifies the necessary concentration.

## Constant choice and distribution

For any fixed c<2, ρ/c>0.45>1/e. Infinite-K iteration is monotone starting at 0; any limit below one would be a fixed point, contrary to max_{x∈[0,1]} −x log x=1/e. All fixed t values stay strictly below one. Hence first t can be chosen to beat the necessary nonsingleton fraction with strict margin, then K can be chosen finite by continuity and uniform geometric tails below one. This is not an interchange of infinite exploration depth and market limits.

The super-geometric scales obey z_{j+1}=z_j²; no larger band intersects [0,z_j), and the total lengths of smaller bands are O(z_j²). The constructed density is fixed, integrates exactly to one, has lower bound 1/H and upper bound B/H independent of j. The expected count below z_j tends to 1/100, with probability ≤2/100 of any such point eventually. The target-band binomial mean tends to 5(K+100); the one-sided bad probability is at most 4/M≤1/100 eventually by Chebyshev, with M the mean. Good probability ≥0.97 then permits *exact* independent Bernoulli 0.9 thinning, independent of the rank permutation by symmetry of iid nonatomic draws. Auxiliary randomness only discards admissible prefixes: if the original sample allows EF, no auxiliary outcome can ever contradict the deterministic counting condition. Integer floors give m/n≥c, m/n→c and both sizes divergent. No continuous-at-zero density hypothesis is in the source definition, so the oscillations are legitimate. The law may not be changed with j.
