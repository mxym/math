# Primary-source map

1. Claude Carlet and Darrion Thornburgh, [Resolving a conjecture on quadratic APN functions and a new quadratic (n,n)-function associated to crooked functions, arXiv:2608.23888v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23888v2), 7 September 2026. The [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23888) listed v2 as latest when checked 27 November 2026. The relevant locations in its HTML rendering are:
   - Introduction, around line 69; §5 Definition 5.1 and text immediately after (11), around lines 580–586; and the sentence after Theorem 5.5, around line 641: the nonvanishing assertion for quadratic APN and its purported inference from Corollary 4.9. Theorem 1 of our note refutes these assertions.
   - Lemma 5.2 and its proof: defining relation between exclude multiplicities and the mod-16 Walsh differences, used by the independent verifier.
   - Corollary 4.9 and the preceding component-degree argument: the valid result about each nonzero component of the ortho-derivative. We do not dispute this result.
   - Open Problem 5.7, around lines 653–655: degree of inverse components in odd dimension. Our counterexamples address a different statement and do not resolve it.
   - Theorem 5.5 itself (existence), Proposition 5.8 (degrees conditional on the normal), Proposition 5.12 (later explicit formula), and Corollary 5.13 (lower bound of \(n\) on the number of semi-bent components) are not invalidated by these counterexamples. Proposition 5.8 allows \(\varepsilon_F(u)=0\), in which case its low-degree branch applies to every nonzero mask. Neither the explicit formula in Proposition 5.12 nor the span argument in Corollary 5.13 needs the zero fiber to be a singleton.
   - Proposition 5.17, around lines 770–778: the Gold conclusion remains true; its last inference can be repaired without the disputed universal claim.
   - Introduction, around line 79, and Proposition 6.4 with proof, around lines 909–923: degree of the near-bent component indicator after restriction to a hyperplane. Theorem 2 gives the valid replacement and the dimension-ten example falsifies the unconditional degree claim.
   - [Version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23888v1), 24 August 2026, has the same nonvanishing and near-bent statements. The Gold result is numbered Proposition 5.12 there. Neither upstream manuscript is redistributed.
2. Emanuele Bellini, [Testing CCZ-equivalence to permutations via projections of Walsh zero spaces, with applications to APN functions, arXiv:2611.10269v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10269v1), 6 November 2026, §7. A recent explicit parametrization, including dimension ten, of the already-known switched-cube family \(x^3+a^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(a^3x^9)\); not a claim that the APN function itself is new. The original family (often labelled F4) is due to Lilya Budaghyan, Claude Carlet and Gregor Leander, [Constructing new APN functions from known ones](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2008.10.001), *Finite Fields and Their Applications* 15(2), 150–159 (2009). Our note gives its own APN proof and a standalone check.
