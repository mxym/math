# Research decision and verification boundary

The repository was checked at 01004eab75b21500ddffca2c7179ff8e125a040e before this cycle. The qutrit proof Release and metadata-corrected preprint DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23269470 were already published. No arXiv posting or peer review was inferred. The existing arbitrary-dimensional two-eigenvalue purity theorem and flat-minimum subclass were read and not repeated as new contributions.

The next attempt tested three-level spectra for violations of the general purity conjecture. Numerical ordering chambers were not exactly certified, so the optimization was discovery only. Completed cases were (m,n)=(4,4),(4,10),(4,15),(5,5),(5,10),(5,18),(5,20),(5,30), with respectively 105,741,1711,276,1176,3916,4851,11026 multiplicity pairs and 21 grid heights each. No certified counterexample or unrestricted upper bound resulted. The dimension-six optimization hit its bound and is not counted as complete. An exploratory weighted-graph route supplied no proved general lemma. These searches are not premises of any theorem in this directory.

Rather than add random restarts or assume a two-level maximizer, the direction was reassessed. AKW Section 6.3 gives a rigorous inner-polytope volume and numerical comparisons, while Tran's retrieved abstract gives the qubit volume order through two-sided bounds. The new route proves an all-fixed-local-dimension geometric law: a full-rank Schmidt test cuts out a containing affine simplex, and a uniform Schur-complement estimate shows that almost all of its volume is actually APPT. Dirichlet concentration yields the leading constant and typical spectral profile. The AS consequence uses Kondra et al.'s cited spectral-ratio criterion, not an assumption that AS=APPT.

The proof review checked the actual physical trace-pairing implication, the assignment-independent scalar matrix entry, the simultaneous operator-norm bound, the Schur complement, the affine volume Jacobian, the potentially negative least eigenvalue in the auxiliary simplex, exact Dirichlet dependence, all Gamma shifts, and conditioning on a subset of positive relative volume. The general purity conjecture remains open here.

The exact standard-library checker validates rational and symbolic identities and finite regressions. A second test runs it normally and under python -O, then deliberately alters its factorial formula, leading constant, and second-moment identity and requires each altered checker to fail for its specific mathematical mismatch. These ancillary checks do not replace the continuum analytic proof, and no Lean claim is made.

The published sources are for independent mathematical scrutiny. The full Tran PDF was not retrievable through the web tool; no exhaustive novelty certification is claimed. The new volume proof is separate from the immutable qutrit Lean proof and preprint, both unchanged. Future work on the general purity problem, AS leading coefficient, balanced local-dimension growth, or non-flat measures requires a new argument rather than a change in this theorem's quantifiers.
