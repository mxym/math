# Date-bounded search and scope (18 November 2026 UTC)

**External primary sources.** [Han–Zhan–Zhou–Zhu v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15716v1), posted 11 October 2026, Theorems 3–5, §VII (58), Remark 16, §VIII-A (61), was checked against the statement. The all-weights upper bound for orders ≥1, sharp exponential-product law, and all-dimensions unweighted threshold are theirs. Their negative direction uses a uniform simplex in sufficiently large dimension for each given subunit order. They explicitly ask for fixed-dimensional *unweighted* constants and order sets. This note characterizes all weights **quantified together** in each fixed n≥2; at equal weights it answers only a strictness region of that distinct open question. Direct substitution in their simplex formula shows the planar p=4/7 witness is not explained by the uniform triangle (all triangles affine-equivalent). This comparison does not rule out other published constructions.

The older unweighted p≥2 result is [Melbourne–Tkocz, §VI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.10930). [Fu–Li](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09206) studies entropy of i.i.d. positive sums, particularly in dimension one. Ball–Nayar–Tkocz entropy concavity and [Luo’s counterexamples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11418) involve the shape of entropy as a function of *positive* mixing coefficients, not this upper bound on signed differences. Searches using the primary paper's arXiv ID and exact title, English/Rényi spelling variants, dimension two/from below one, weighted differences of log-concave random vectors, perturbations and exponential products, and follow-up/preprint/announcement combinations did not turn up the present criterion or fixed-dimensional weighted conclusion. Titles and search hits are discovery aids; this is not a proof of novelty or universal coverage of unpublished announcements.
