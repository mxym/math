# Discovery and proof provenance

The independent research branch began from main `8353cff72` and checked
README, SOLVED_PROBLEMS, RESEARCH, the prior four-row proof and the actual
absence of repository AGENTS.md. The prior real axis and three-row complex
norm were retained as already completed work. Primary background was checked
in Carlen–Lieb–Loss, math/0508096, and the September 26 OpenAI/math four-row
permanent/permutation-moment source. A finite search does not certify priority.

Product-row maximization suggested five full-plane branches; its optimizer
output was not treated as proof. A symmetry-reduced fully-PPT SDP then supplied
candidate boundary Gram factors. That SDP was only a discovery tool. Its group
orbit system had twenty-three real Hermitian parameters; a kernel-restricted
dual was rounded and corrected by exact linear algebra over Q(√3).

The published data are exact rational/algebraic matrices. They are verified by
a separate full-matrix code path that does not trust the reduced orbit system,
kernel approximation, solver status or numerical eigenvalues. Its positive
factor identities and 65,536-entry operator identity imply the original matrix
inequality through the written product-vector argument. The full imaginary
parameter axis then follows analytically; finite t grids play no role.

The equality supplement was derived from the first exact positive Gram block:
its five difference columns force six two-column product equalities. An
elementary support lemma reduces every invertible boundary equality matrix to
a singleton row or column. The already proved three-row Hermitian identity
then forces the Fourier block, including at the transition. This is a complete
proof on the actual matrices; no numerical equality guess or unproved unitary
reduction is used. Its auxiliary checker exhausts the finite support space and
checks the specialized determinant and Fourier identities exactly.

Numerical discovery used NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, CVXPY and SDP solvers. None of
those packages is needed by the published verifier, which uses Python's standard
library and exact fractions only. Windows and WSL executed the same finalized
verifier independently with the same certificate and source hashes. This is
cross-environment reproduction, not independent human mathematical refereeing.
No new Lean formalization or kernel replay is claimed.

The nonreal full-plane candidate remains unproved. In particular a numerical
PPT-relaxation gap at another coefficient is not an original-matrix
counterexample. It is not a premise or a solved-problem claim of this package.

OpenAI AI assistants assisted derivation, discovery computation, coding,
