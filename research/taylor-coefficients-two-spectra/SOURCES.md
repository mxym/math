# Source and overlap record

- [Teng Zhang, arXiv:2601.07286v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07286v1), the sole arXiv version indexed at the time of this check. Problem 1.1 asks whether λ((A+B)^k) is weakly majorized by the singular values of the ordered coefficient Q_k, for arbitrary complex Hermitian inputs and k≥3. Orders one and two are immediate from the identities for the Hermitian parts. Theorems 3.2 and 4.3 in v1 address the cubic and quartic comparisons in all dimensions; the final article explicitly gives the corresponding norm consequences. No new claim for orders one through four is made here. Conjecture 5.5 in that preprint is explicitly about the **eigenvalues** of the Hermitian part R_k, for k≥5. It is not equivalent to the original singular-value question.

Unlike the January preprint, the publisher's indexed introduction specifically states that its §5 includes an exact example refuting the intermediate eigenvalue comparison at order five, but not the original singular-value question (now Problem 1.2). The journal version therefore does not leave the unqualified signed intermediate assertion open. Full publisher PDF not accessible through the checked routes; OpenAlex indexes only the older arXiv version as a free full-text copy. We checked the published indexed description and the actual arXiv full text, not every sentence in the paywalled final version.
- [Shivam Patel's MathDB solution](https://mathdb.com/p/372496/weak-majorization-of-higher-power-coefficients-in-the-expone) and [trureturing's independently documented formal statement](https://www.trureturing.org/research/zhang-2026-taylor-coefficient-weak-majorization/) both treat the concrete Pauli pair Z+cI, X with c<0. The centered summands have the same radius, and their Pauli axes are perpendicular. The MathDB proof gives the trace generating function exp(ct)[1+cosh(2t)−2cosh(√2 t)], extracts its even centered moments, and uses them to give strictly negative signed trace defects at every odd k≥5 once negatively shifted. It explicitly distinguishes Problem 1.1, which it does not settle. The trureturing page likewise leaves the even-order, PSD, and singular-value versions outside its result. We did not recompile its Lean. We do not claim first odd-order failure, first scalar-shift obstruction, or first use of this Pauli pair. The author's final acknowledgment of a fifth-order counterexample must likewise be credited, independent of the provenance of its actual matrices.
- **Direct and much earlier precedent for the scalar ingredient:** [P. J. Forrester and C. J. Thompson, *The Golden–Thompson inequality: historical aspects and random matrix applications*, §2.1, eqs. (2.4)–(2.5) and paragraph immediately after (2.6)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.2008). This treats arbitrary centered 2×2 Hermitian summands using Pauli matrices, expresses the trace by hyperbolic functions, and **explicitly states coefficientwise comparison for all even orders**, not merely the exponential inequality. After scaling by t, their normalized trace on the product side reads α cosh((a+b)t)+(1−α)cosh((a−b)t). Thus the even scalar moments and the inequality M_j≥h^(2j) are genuinely prior. Scalar shifts and corresponding signed **trace** conclusions follow by elementary algebra; the odd projection moment is an immediate consequence of the trace-derivative identity. The application here is to combine the scalar estimates with the two Ky Fan tests, get a stronger singular-value comparison for all orders, and complete the signed classification including its non-trace partial sums. Forrester–Thompson also discuss inequalities for norms of full exponentials; taking Taylor coefficients of an inequality of norms is not generally legitimate.
- [P. R. Halmos, *Two subspaces*, Trans. AMS 144 (1969)](https://www.ams.org/tran/1969-144-00/S0002-9947-1969-0251519-5/S0002-9947-1969-0251519-5.pdf): the two-projections block decomposition is classical. Extending a two-dimensional Ky Fan result to direct sums of the small blocks is a corollary. Neither decomposition nor its finite-dimensional proof is claimed new.
