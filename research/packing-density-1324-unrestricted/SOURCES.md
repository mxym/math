# Sources, antecedents and scope

- [The companion Bellman theorem (#211)](../packing-density-1324/PROOF.md) identifies the optimizer among layered permutons. See its [bibliography and numerical precedents](../packing-density-1324/SOURCES.md), especially Price's finite-layer calculations and the [Sliačan–Stromquist 2017 announcement](https://pp2017.github.io/assets/pdf/abstracts/talks/walter_stromquist.pdf) and [2018 journal bounds](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/4263/pdf). This addendum needs the companion theorem only after excluding nonlayered extremizers. No new density value is claimed.
- The existence of a layered optimizer comes from W. Stromquist's [1993 poset manuscript](https://walterstromquist.com/POSETS.DOC), with published formulation in [Albert–Atkinson–Handley–Holton–Stromquist, Theorem 2.2](https://doi.org/10.37236/1622). Its universal finite-optimum clause assumes every layer of each target pattern has size greater than one, not true of 1324. Their Remark 2.3 illustrates why that hypothesis cannot simply be dropped in general.
- R. W. Barton, [*Packing densities of patterns*](https://doi.org/10.37236/1833), Theorem 4 and proof: direct planar surgery on inversions by moving a point to have the same relations as the other one. [Stromquist's 2005 slides](https://walterstromquist.com/DensitiesAtAltitude.ppt), pages headed “Reid Barton's Proof” and “Proof, continued,” explicitly implement the two-clone comparison in posets. M. Bóna, B. E. Sagan and V. R. Vatter, [*Pattern frequency sequences and internal zeros*](https://doi.org/10.1006/aama.2001.0789), §7, in particular Lemma 7.6, compare poset counts under a cloning edit. Following Theorem 7.3, they explicitly conclude all finite exact optimizers for the **three-point** pattern 132 are layered. These are direct methodological precedents, not just similar topics. The finite proof here specializes their type of reasoning to the four-point diamond's exact slack.
- J. Krull, L. Pudwell, E. Redmon and A. Reimer-Berg, [*Packing patterns in symmetric words*](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/84/ajc_v84_p238.pdf), Australas. J. Combin. 84 (2022), p. 254, already describe the finite 1324-optimal permutation as layered. We give a proof with explicit quantifier over every exact optimizer for $n\ge4$, and do not claim priority for finite layering. The open questions on double lists in their §4 concern a different host model. Small maximal counts likewise predate this note (see [OEIS A342853](https://oeis.org/A342853)).
- A. Kabela, D. Král’, J. A. Noel and T. Pierron, [*Density maximizers of layered permutations*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.01723), §2 immediately before Lemma 6: a permuton is layered exactly when both its 231 and 312 densities vanish. The compactness and continuity step is standard permutation-limit theory; see [Hoppen, Kohayakawa, Moreira, Ráth and Sampaio](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2012.09.003). We assert only qualitative convergence in this topology.

**Scope check, 19 November 2026.** In addition to the full [targeted owner and source inventory for #211](../packing-density-1324/SOURCES.md), searches covered all-versus-some exact and near-optimal permutations, induced diamond posets, cloning and symmetrization, and unrestricted permuton uniqueness. The reviewed sources give strong finite precedents; they did not provide the two-root measure perturbation, unrestricted limiting uniqueness, or convergence here. Ordinary graph inducibility of the undirected diamond, unrestricted oriented graph problems, and induced diamond saturation of set families have different admissible objects or objectives. This is a bounded, non-exhaustive search, not a guarantee of priority.
