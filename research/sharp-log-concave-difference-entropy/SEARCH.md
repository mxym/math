# Scope of prior-art screening

This is a dated, nonexhaustive search record, not an assertion of priority.

**Repository.** The independent check inventoried the complete Git tree of `mxym/math` at [bfcce3b8](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/bfcce3b8ddf3659f165b1ba330943370a1fb6f5f), not just its checked-out directories: 21,953 paths and 14,507 distinct file objects. The preceding [27a4b321](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/27a4b32184df4ee69bbb18ab89cd97cf94bd58f1) tree and the trees of all 48 fetched tags were deduplicated by object identity (14,491 main objects and 277 additional tag-only objects). Search included preprints, notes, formalizations, verification records and source archives. This pass made 36,463 text visits, including text extracted from 162 PDFs; it opened 160 archives, including nested packages, and 103 standalone gzip members. The 16 additional files in the final tree all concern a four-qubit quantum measurement problem and were searched separately. The scanner ignored 18 opaque binary visits and 34 named split technical-storage chunks of Bapat or cyclotomic Lean proof evidence; it did not attempt image OCR.

The search combined author names and source identifiers with mathematical terms for difference entropy, shifts, log-concave distributions, concentration functions, majorization and Rogers–Shephard, including Chinese variants. Of 138 broader matches, the closest manuscript families were the classical *convex-body* Rogers–Shephard stability question; Brenier transport estimates for log-concave sources; the Shannon entropy deficit relative to covariance (no independent difference); and a Gaussian posterior mean-difference drift (not the entropy of a difference). No matching theorem surfaced. The two separate mathematical checkers in this package are not being described as a Lean formalization.

In addition to tag contents, screening covered 67 indexed PR titles/descriptions through #68, the names of 142 available branches, and the public releases list: 47 listed release descriptions with 175 named attachments. Of the 175 asset digests, 102 matched previously scanned Git blobs by SHA-256. All remaining 73 were downloaded read-only, digest-checked and screened, adding 10,947 text visits, 42 archives and 19 PDF extractions; the nine broad hits were solely Brenier transport material. All 48 tag trees were available, including the additional ECQC exact-replay maintenance tag, whose audit concerns manifest and replay integrity for quantum results. Neither the quantum entropy releases, the Wakhare entropy-root polynomial releases nor the chromatic log-concavity certificates concern these questions. Branch-only content outside the reviewed main/tag trees, private or deleted material and semantically equivalent statements without matching vocabulary may escape this search.

**Literature.** [Han–Zhan–Zhou–Zhu, §VIII and acknowledgments](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15716v1), [Melbourne–Tkocz, §6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.10930), [Melbourne–Palafox-Castillo, Thm 2.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.06997v1), and [Fu–Li v4 (9 November 2026), §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.09206v4) delimit the specific entropy question and its known cases. The independent search also checked related concentration results in [Bobkov–Marsiglietti–Melbourne](https://doi.org/10.1017/S096354832100016X), [Marsiglietti–Melbourne](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.25869v2), [Gaxiola–Melbourne–Pigno–Pollard](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.05793v1) and [Alqasem–Aravinda–Marsiglietti](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05439v1). Searches covered displacements, concentration/peakedness of symmetrized variables, subunit Rényi entropy, log-concave lattice differences and ROC comparisons. The statistical triangle inequality for a concave ROC is established prior work: see [Zhang–Mueller](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11336-003-1119-8) and [Bandos–Rockette–Gur, Appendix (A2)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2980542/).

The [proof](PROOF.md) and [review](REVIEW.md) explicitly identify the previously known entropy endpoints, monotone and symmetric subclasses, examples for sharpness, and equality theorem. The screening found no earlier explicit statement of the general discrete Shannon answer or the full asymmetric continuous one-dimensional subunit-order result, but cannot exclude earlier proofs or easy implications of results expressed differently.
