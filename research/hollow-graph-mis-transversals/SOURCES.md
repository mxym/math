# Earlier work and scope of the comparison

- [Erdős, Gallai and Tuza, *Covering the cliques of a graph with vertices* (1992)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(92)90681-5) study vertex sets hitting maximal cliques; their Problem 2 asks how a lower bound on the sizes of these cliques constrains transversal size. Their Theorem 5 gives graphs whose transversal is nearly all the vertices with smallest clique at least $n^{b/\log\log n}$ for an absolute $b>0$; that theorem does not impose *linear* smallest clique size. The first part of [Erdős Problem 611](https://www.erdosproblems.com/611) is the fixed-linear-size sublinearity question, in the complement graph. Different conventions for isolated-vertex cliques do not affect our graphs once $cn>1$. The [UCSD problem collection](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/SublinearCliqueTransversal.html) traces the question to Erdős's Visegrád 1991 conference contribution, published in the 1994 proceedings.
- [Joshua Cooper and Isaiah Hollars, *Hitting all maximal independent sets in $c$-hollow graphs* (arXiv:2607.15486v1, July 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15486v1), Conjecture 1.1, Lemma 3.1, Theorem 1.7 and Question 7.1, are the direct comparison. Their random reservoir makes all maximal independent sets large. Their core is the complete join of $m$ cluster graphs, each the union of $m$ cliques of size $m$. They obtain $\Omega_c(n^{1/3}/\log n)$ by a Janson-type estimate. The present proof uses a *single* cluster graph, cliques of size about $m\log m$, and a uniform covering argument. It improves that specific lower bound and yields the same quantitative improvement for $\max_G i(G)\tau(G)/n$, but determines neither extremal function.
- [Noga Alon, *Hitting all maximum independent sets* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05998v2) constructs a graph with linear independence number requiring $\Omega(\sqrt n)$ to hit its **maximum** independent sets. The hypothesis on all maximal independent sets here is different. [Bai, Chang and Yan, arXiv:2608.18963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18963v2) likewise concerns maximum sets and was withdrawn by the authors in September 2026; we use no claim from it.
- The simple upper bound obtained by sampling vertices and union-bounding over the maximal sets is a standard set-system observation, not a new upper bound on all graphs. The Chernoff and Hoeffding estimates in the construction are also standard.
