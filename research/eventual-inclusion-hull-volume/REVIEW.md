The quantified claim is the dimension-only threshold for expected unnormalized volume of an iid uniform sample hull that Rademacher (Mathematika 2012, §4 Q1) identifies for Meckes–Reitzner and Beermann–Reitzner (arXiv:1706.08342 §2) restate. It is stronger than the elementary pair-dependent asymptotic threshold and different from expected faces in the sample size, normalized fractions, and fixed-simplex counterexamples.

- Anchored determinant lower bound: a maximum absolute determinant basis (exists, positive by interior) gives all other columns coordinates of absolute value ≤1 via Cramer's rule; hence its determinant is at least |D|/2^d. Independent samples each in the indicated homotheties have probability (4d)^(−d²). The perturbation matrix has max row sum ≤1/2, so all eigenvalues of I+E have modulus ≥1/2; determinant and d! scaling give b_d as specified.
- For every exterior or boundary x, choose a separating/supporting affine height with minimum on K at zero. Between its minimum and maximum, cap volumes are continuous and strictly increasing. The upper half-height part D has at least 2^(−d) times cap volume by homothety toward a top-height point. Exactly d samples there and others outside the cap has probability at least 2^(−d(d+1)−2)/d!, since m≥2d. Conditional selected points are iid uniform in D. The half-scale anchored simplex about x lies below the old hull up to the null height interface; its volume after scaling, the D fraction, and event probability give c_d |K|/m.
- Uniform missing: a maximal simplex normalization gives containment in [-1,1]^d by replacing its basis vertices and an inscribed shifted cube with radius 1/(2d). J-grid hitting a fixed small homothety at each representative gives support error ≤2||v||_1/J, yielding lower homothetic hull inclusion 1−4d/J about the cube center. The stated expected missing-volume bound follows by Bernoulli and a union bound, uniformly over K, with explicit computable threshold. For an ordinary add-one sample, conditional exchangeability of second-half labels and any Carathéodory support of y show at most d+1 essential labels and zero when y was already covered in the first half. Integration gives the missing-fraction improvement in (3). Thus for the same n−1 old samples the specified outside-point expectation beats the additional ordinary draw by c_d|K|/[2(n−1)].
