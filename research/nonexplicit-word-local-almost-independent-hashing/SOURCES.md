# Sources and scope

- Yevgeniy Dodis, Shachar Lovett and Daniel Wichs, [Locally Computable High Independence Hashing](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/622), STOC 2026, [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3798129.3800855), [authors' full text (March 30, 2026)](https://cs.nyu.edu/~dodis/ps/twise.pdf), [ECCC TR26-059](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/059/). Definitions 3.1–3.2 separate adaptive input choice from adaptive key access and define efficient evaluation. Theorem 4.5 concerns expansion from perfect independence. Theorem 5.1 and its proof, pp. 10–11 in the authors' full text, provide the common-union selective lower-bound method: we use its first, stronger word-dependent expression as the antecedent, sharpen the stated near-optimal-key specialization, and make ceiling and distinct-query availability explicit. In its printed equations, using the floor for query count does not in general imply 2^(wt−nq)≤epsilon; this is an integer-rounding repair, not a claim the theorem's intended asymptotic conclusion is false. Their Theorem 5.2 also gives a different locality bound using support and a container argument; our separate address-pattern result adds a premise on the largest atom of the entire input-to-locations map and does not improve the unrestricted bound without that premise.
- In the same paper, Lemma 5.6 combines overlap-avoiding selection with robust output maps and an ideal-transcript argument using their Lemma 2.12 (H-coefficients). Theorems 5.7 and 5.10 give explicit bit-local and word-local constructions; the word construction assumes polynomial k and uses the width condition w=Omega(log²k+log²n) and theorem-level error 2^(-Omega(n)). Section 6 names closing the logarithmic gap for explicit almost-independent constructions at n-bit input/output as open and separately asks to unify constructions over word widths. Our whole-stacked-rank catalog is an existential, not polynomial-time, answer over the full width range for 2≤n≤k≤2^n. We do not claim to resolve the explicit problem or the k<n range.
- [AICR c/0066](https://aicr.info/c/0066/) and its [hub](https://aicr.info/p/almost-kwise-local-hash-locality/) index the explicit question. The hub's apparent gap at w=1 is absent in the stated equal input/output model: one-query support already forces t≥n at error below 1/2, and DLW Theorem 5.7 calls its O(n) order optimal. The analogous support argument at k fixed inputs forces at least kn random key bits at this error (correcting the hub's suggestion of logarithmic key dependence in this model). The word-local nonexplicit result does not refute the open efficient-evaluation question.
- Anna Pagh and Rasmus Pagh, [Uniform Hashing in Constant Time and Optimal Space](https://www.rasmuspagh.net/papers/uniform-jour.pdf), SICOMP 38 (2008), Theorem 1.1 and §3, already condition on a random choice of table addresses/auxiliary family that gives exact uniformity on a fixed set with high probability. Their stated failure is polynomially small in the query-set parameter for fixed constants, rather than the present uniformly quantified 2^(-input-bit-length) error with fixed constant-factor word space. We do not claim conditional exact uniformity or tabulation as novel.
- Kasper Green Larsen, Rasmus Pagh, Giuseppe Persiano, Toniann Pitassi, Kevin Yeo and Or Zamir, [Optimal Non-Adaptive Cell Probe Dictionaries and Hashing](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2024.104), ICALP 2024, §3; Tobias Christiani, Rasmus Pagh and Mikkel Thorup, [From Independence to Expansion and Back Again](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03676), STOC 2015. These are antecedents for expansion-based exact independence and word-level lookup. Their constructions do not supply the stated near-optimal-space, exponentially small-error, almost-wise nonexplicit locality formula.
- [Fast Bounded-Independence Functions and Their Duals](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol385-itc2026/html/LIPIcs.ITC.2026.8/LIPIcs.ITC.2026.8.html), ITC 2026, Theorems 1 and 21, treats constant-order exact independence and circuit size rather than this high-independence local word-probe measure.

A bounded duplicate search (2026-11-27 UTC) checked the primary paper, its linked versions and nearby work above, and queries combining its title or ePrint number with almost-independence, intermediate word width, nonexplicit locality, fixed-tuple rank, and static/adaptive access. We found the cited ingredients but no antecedent stating the same nonexplicit formula or whole address-pattern mass cutoff in these parameters. This is not a priority claim. All checkers use finite synthetic distributions or integer inequalities; no implementation or security deployment is investigated.
