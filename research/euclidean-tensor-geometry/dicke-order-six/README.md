# Dicke entropy formula in orders at most six
**Yongxian Zhang.** Research note (AI-assisted proof development and verification).

Let S be the set of permutations of a k-tuple with symbol counts c_z, L=|S|, p_z=c_z/k, and H=H(p) in base 2. For k≤6 the asymptotic induced matching number of S is 2^H. Thus every tensor over every field with support exactly S has asymptotic subrank 2^H. The general question is Oneto–Ventura (2025) Conj. 4.26 and CVZ Question 1.3.3.

Upper bound: For each term of the nth Kronecker power, the sum of information over its k party words is knH, where the information of a word w is -log₂ ∏ p_{w_i}. Some party word has information at most nH, and each party has at most 2^{nH} such words since each has product probability ≥2^{-nH}. Assign each term to one such party and word. Grouping terms by assignment gives slice rank ≤k2^{nH}. Subrank ≤slice rank, and induced matching size ≤subrank by local projection and diagonal rescaling.

Lower bound: Apply higher-order Coppersmith–Winograd as stated in Arunachalam–Vrana–Zuiddam, https://arxiv.org/html/1905.03148, Theorem 1.3. Distinct integer symbol labels make S tight via a constant-sum relation; translate the last encoding to make the sum zero. Let P be uniform on S, with H(P)=log₂ L and marginals p.

Fix a set R of pairs of words sharing a fixed coordinate (take it last by coordinate symmetry). Project differences to their first m=k−2 entries; the other entries are determined by the shared last coordinate and zero sum. Let r≥1 be the rational rank of these differences, so r≤m. The higher-order CW bound reaches H if each collision distribution Q on R with the prescribed marginals satisfies
H(Q) ≤ ((m+r) log₂ L − rH)/m.
For r=m the entropy chain rule gives H(X,Y)≤H(X)+H(Y)−H(Z)≤2 log₂ L−H, with Z the common coordinate.

For r<m reduce all projected differences modulo a prime. The rank of their modular span cannot exceed r because every integer (r+1)-minor vanishes. Enlarge to an r-dimensional subspace B, and let N_z(B) count pairs with common last symbol z and projected difference in B. Then H(Q)≤H+Σ p_z log₂ N_z(B). This is sufficient provided the exact integer comparison
k^{k(m+r)} ∏ N_z(B)^{m c_z} ≤ L^{k(m+r)} (∏ c_z^{c_z})^{m+r}
holds for every B. It follows by rearrangement, raising the entropy inequality to the exponential after multiplying by mk, and replacing kH by k log₂ k−Σ c_z log₂ c_z. dicke-six.py enumerates the usual unique RREF matrices over F_11 and every projective direction in each span (normalized in pivot coordinates). Zero directions and diagonals are included. It verifies all these inequalities for the nine listed order-six cases.

dicke-small.py uses the simpler bound H(Q)≤log₂ Σ N_z(B), so the integer test on maximum pair count M_r is M_r^{mk} k^{rk} ≤ L^{(m+r)k}(∏ c_z^{c_z})^r. It enumerates only spans generated by actual differences because the difference span of any R has this form. Ranks over F_101 may drop upon reduction, so the checker takes the maximum over dimensions at most r. Exact expected maxima verify each listed case.

The omitted constant and one-exception types are the trivial and known W states, respectively. Order two is just the rank of a diagonal or permutation matrix. Order three follows from the full-rank collision bound. Christandl–Vrana–Zuiddam already proved the binary (2,2) case; Arunachalam–Vrana–Zuiddam reported numerical verification for the balanced binary case up to order 2000 and proved it for all sufficiently large even orders. The exact certificates here are useful particularly for the repeated-symbol multicolor cases. This is special-case coverage, not a proof of the general conjecture.

To reproduce the finite checks, run `python3 dicke-small.py` and `python3 dicke-six.py` from this directory without `-O`, as they use assertions. Both require only Python's standard library. The latter exhaustively checks rational-rank upper bounds via finite-field subspaces; this choice of a finite field for the certificate places no restriction on the field of tensor coefficients.

## Literature update: a general proof

Wenqi Zhu, Chunlin Liu, Kai Zou, and Ping Nie, [*The Entropy Law for the Asymptotic Subrank of Dicke Tensors*](https://github.com/erenup/dicke-tensor-subrank) (November 2026), give a written proof for all multiplicity tuples over the complex numbers, with an induced-matching lower bound. Their method uses the entropy of quotient fibers under permutation symmetry and isolates candidates against the entire tensor-power support. The argument was checked here at the written-proof level; the asymptotic theorem is not a Lean-certified result. The induced-matching construction only uses the support, and the slice-rank upper bound also depends only on the support. For finite coefficient fields one can prove the upper bound after scalar extension to an infinite field. Hence their proof also yields the all-field, arbitrary-nonzero-weight variant of this note. This note is retained as an alternative proof or certificate for its narrower scope, not as a novelty or priority claim.
