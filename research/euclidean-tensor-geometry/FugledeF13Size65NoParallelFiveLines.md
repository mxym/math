# Two parallel five-point lines are impossible at size 65 in \(\mathbb F_{13}^3\)

Use the elementary spectral-pair notation of the [moment notes](FugledeF13Size65MomentsSeven.md). In a hypothetical size-65 spectral pair \((E,A)\), neither member has two parallel affine lines each containing five of its points. This is a restricted obstruction, not a nonexistence result at size 65.

By the [parallel-layer exclusion](FugledeF13Size65NoLayers.md), every projective line meets \(U_E\) and \(U_A\) at most ten times. Suppose \(E\) has two five-point affine lines parallel to direction \(d\). In their common affine direction plane, their cross differences determine at least nine distinct projective directions other than \(d\), by Cauchy–Davenport in a coordinate along the lines. Direction \(d\) also occurs. Since \(D(E)\subset U_A\), there are exactly ten uniform directions of \(A\) in this projective pencil, and the five-point coordinate sets have difference set of size nine. [Vosper's theorem](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-31.2.200) applies, since both sets have size five and nine is below thirteen minus one. They are arithmetic progressions with a common nonzero difference; the nine cross directions are a nine-term progression in the affine slope chart. Its complement in that chart is a four-term progression. These are exactly the four nonuniform projective directions in the pencil of \(A\).

The cross ratio of \(0,1,2,3\) in the ordering with value \(4/3\) is \(10\) in \(\mathbb F_{13}\), and inversion gives \(4\). Accordingly choose planar coordinate forms for the four nonuniform directions to be \(x,y,x+y,x+4y\). Cross ratio is preserved by the projective normalization. Projecting \(A\) along the pencil's annihilator gives a nonnegative integer multiplicity \(f(x,y)\). The four plane-count profiles \(g_i\) satisfy the [Radon identity](FugledeF13Size91WeightedProfiles.md)
\[
g_0(x)+g_1(y)+g_2(x+y)+g_3(x+4y)=15+13f(x,y).
\]
The usual Vandermonde relation shows each \(g_i\) has degree at most two modulo 13. If one has a nonzero quadratic coefficient, all four do, because squares of any three projectively distinct linear forms are independent. Otherwise each is affine; a nonconstant affine residue profile has total mass 78 or 91 with occupancies in \([0,13]\), not 65. All four nonuniform profiles would then be constant zero modulo 13, contradicting \(15\not\equiv0\).

Writing the quadratic coefficients in the indicated order, cancellation gives \((3b,b,9b,b)\), \(b\ne0\). Translate \(x,y\) to center the first two quadratics. Cancellation of the linear coefficients forces the other two centered there as well, since \(x+y\) and \(x+4y\) are independent. For each centered polynomial \(a t^2+c\) its least residues are forced, except that a zero can be lifted to 13; the total mass 65 determines the number of such lifts. The companion [exact verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size65-equianharmonic.js) tests all 198 centered constant tuples satisfying the constant congruence, with all 534 lift assignments. In every case the identity forces a negative value of \(f\). This contradiction excludes the parallel pair.

In particular every projective line meets \(D(E)\) and \(D(A)\) at least four times. Otherwise all thirteen parallel slices in one direction would have exactly five collinear sites (the [degree-seven note](FugledeF13Size65MomentsSeven.md) gives the elementary planar obstruction). Their directions would be drawn from at most three choices, giving two parallel five-point lines.
