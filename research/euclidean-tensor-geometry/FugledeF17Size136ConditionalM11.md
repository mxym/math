# Size 136 in F_17^3: conditional endgame if moments through degree11 vanish

Assume a spectral pair of size136, the affine cap and six-uniform-per-pencil lower bound of [FugledeF17Size136MomentNine.md](FugledeF17Size136MomentNine.md), at most one layer per member, and that all mixed moments through degree11 vanish. This note is conditional on the last hypotheses and does not prove the degree-eleven rigidity.

The plane-occupancy residues have reduced degree at most four. Weight is w_B=(Σ_t binom(n_t,2)−476)/17. As in other weighted arguments, P²=17I+J, P1=18·1, T_B=P w_B−36·1 and W=Σ_v w_B(v)=1124. Every positive normal of the partner defines a tight neighbor pencil of weight36. A layered profile weighs36, nonconstant affine profiles weigh12, and genuine profiles in degrees 2,3,4 have minima 8,6,4 respectively. The exact degree-specific minima are equal to those of mass153 by occupancy complement n_t↦17−n_t, which preserves the variance and thus the weight. Every member must have a degree≥2 profile, since otherwise all positive weights would be multiples of twelve, incompatible with W=1124.

For a member let R be its maximum genuine profile degree, among 2,3,4; call a profile high if its degree is at least two. The number h_B of high normals is at least 118,101,84 for R=4,3,2 respectively. Indeed the moment form F of degree d=16−R is nonzero, and its nonzero evaluation normals are exactly those of genuine degree R (the coefficient of t^R is detected by summing t^(16−R) times the residue profile). This form vanishes on all uniform normals, a six-fold blocking set. Its projective zero count is at most 17(d−1)+2. Subtracting from the 307 projective points gives the stated support bounds. To prove that bound, factor out all rational line factors. If a nonconstant residual remains, it has degree e≥2 and no rational line component; by the [Sziklai bound proved by Homma–Kim, stated in Homma's arXiv:1410.3174, Introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1410.3174#S1), its projective zero count is ≤17(e−1)+1. Distinct line components contribute at most17k+1 with k their number, and k+e≤d. If only line factors remain, let t be their largest concurrence (k≥2 distinct lines). A nonfactor radial line through a t-fold point meets F in at most 1+d−t points, so six-blocking implies t≤d−5. The union of the k lines has size at most both 18k−ceiling(k(k−1)/t) and 17k+1−(k−t)(t−1). The first subtracts repeated intersections by counting their pair multiplicity and using binom(m,2)≤t(m−1)/2; the second begins with the t concurrent lines, each other line meeting them at t distinct points. The verifier checks k≤d and t≤min(k,d−5), obtaining maxima 181,195,206 for d12,13,14, all below 17(d−1)+2. The one-line case is immediate.

For a tight pencil with no layered neighbor write b for the number of nonuniform profiles, h the number of high profiles, and S the sum of their squared weights. The degree≤b−2 Radon restriction and the weight minima now imply the following.
- b≤2 impossible; b3 all affine, h=0 and S=432.
- b4 all quadratic (an affine-only pencil would weigh48, and any quadratic requires at least four genuine second-degree coefficients), h=4 and S≤336. If needed, the exact centered (weight,constant) census strengthens S≤330: translate the first two centers away and the linear congruence forces the other two zero; the constants then sum to 24≡7 mod17 and weights to36. Exactly four such multisets survive, maximum squared sum330.
- b5 all cubic because the weight-minimum excludes only lower degrees, and any cubic requires five genuine coefficients. Then h=5 and S≤288.
- b6 if no quartic, all weights6, h=6, S=216. If quartic, all six are genuine quartics by the degree-four relation, and their centers all simultaneously zero by translation of the first two and the remaining independent four cubic columns. The naive maximum squared pattern (4,4,4,4,4,16) is infeasible by the accompanying exact census: enumerate 455 normalized masks, the leading quartic nullvector up to all nonzero scalars, a position for weight16, and the low profiles of weight4. The remaining quadratic/linear/constant congruences determine the potential weight16 profile. The checker tests 741720 low choices with zero survivors. Thus S≤314.
- b≥7 uses minimum weight4 and b≤9, so S≤240; h≤b≤9.

Therefore S≤432−αh, with α=19 for R=4, α=24 for R=3, and α=25 for R=2 (then only b3/4 can occur). In a tight pencil containing a layered neighbor, that neighbor is alone and S=1296, h=0; it costs an additive 864 over the previous linear envelope. Summing S against the opposite weights counts each high B normal with total coefficient36, and each layered B normal with total coefficient36, exactly by the polarity tight identities. Hence
```
||w_B||² ≤ (432/36) W − α h_B +864·(number of B layers)
        ≤ 14352 − α h_B ≤12252.
```
The worst is R=2 giving 14352−25·84=12252. Center both weight vectors. The norm √17 of centered polarity gives
```
w_E^T P w_A ≥ 18W²/307 − √17(12252−W²/307) > 36W,
```
as verified by integer squaring. This contradicts the equality from tightness and proves the conditional conclusion.
