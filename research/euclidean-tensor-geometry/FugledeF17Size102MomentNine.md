# Size 102 in F_17^3: moment-nine reduction and at most one layer

Assume a spectral pair of size102=6·17. Use the standard geometric preliminaries of [Ferguson–Iosevich, arXiv:2610.30712v1 §8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1): each member B has affine line cap6, affine plane cap17, D(B)⊂U of the partner, and no U contains a complete projective line. A layer means a normal with six full parallel affine planes of occupancy17 and other planes empty.

## Six-blocking under a putative low-degree polynomial

Assume some nonzero homogeneous form F of degree d≤9 vanishes on U_B. If a projective line contains at most five U_B sites, the corresponding affine slices of the partner B' contain at most six sites, all six-site cases collinear: every noncollinear planar set determining at most five directions has at most five sites by the marked-triangle census in [verify-fuglede-f17-size136-cross.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size136-cross.js). Averaging forces all seventeen slices to be collinear six-sets. Their support axes have at most five directions, so there are at least four parallel full six-supports with common direction e. Project along e; the four quotient locations form a cap, since three collinear supports would put eighteen points in a spatial plane. A four-cap determines at least four directions (three distinct parallel matchings on four sites are impossible in odd characteristic).

For two parallel six-supports, Cauchy–Davenport gives at least eleven cross directions, plus e. These are all in U_B. Thus at least four distinct radial projective lines through e are factors of F. Let k be the number of such lines. Every radial nonfactor has at most 1+d−k≤6 U_B sites, and any corresponding affine plane through an actual six-support of B' therefore contains exactly six sites: an outside point would give six distinct directions joining the support plus the axial direction, seven total. The eighteen incident affine planes through a six-support must sum to 102+17·6=204. But their occupancies sum to at most 108+11k. For k≤8 this is at most196, contradiction. If k=9 (so d=9 and all factors are radial), the projection of B' along e determines at most nine directions. It has at least seventeen sites by line cap6. More than seventeen sites determine all eighteen directions by pigeonholing into parallel lines. Exactly seventeen means every fiber is full; the projected set is then a cap because three fiber locations on a quotient line would exceed the spatial plane cap. Its 136 pairs determine at least seventeen directions. This contradiction completes six-blocking under any F of degree≤9.

## Moments through eight and at most one layer

For d=8 the standard projective incidence sum at line lower bound six and upper bound seventeen is
```
Σ_L (|U_B∩L|−6)(|U_B∩L|−8) = N²−235N+14736 ≥930,
```
where N=|U_B| and the lower bound is over integers. On nonfactors the summands are nonpositive, and up to eight line factors contribute at most 8·(17−6)(17−8)=792, contradiction. By their vanishing on uniform normals, every homogeneous moment form of degree≤8 is therefore identically zero.

If B had two distinct layers, project onto their independent normal coordinates. The projected occupancy array f has support X×Y, with six levels on each axis, each cell integer in {0,...,6}, and each row and column sum17. Its moment annihilator is precisely the set of arrays λ_X(x)λ_Y(y)Q(x,y) with a polynomial Q of total degree≤1; the barycentric weights λ are inverse level polynomial derivatives. This follows directly by product Vandermonde independence for separate degrees≤5 and total degree≤8, giving an annihilator of dimension three; the weighted evaluations of 1,x,y lie in it because each test product has total degree≤9, so at least one coordinate degree is≤4 and its barycentric sum vanishes.

The exact checker [verify-fuglede-f17-size102-two-layers.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size102-two-layers.js) enumerates all 49 affine classes of six-level sets and 1225 unordered pairs. It lists row polynomials of degree≤1 from the first two allowed integer values, after dividing by λ_Y, with all six integer values in range and sum17. Across rows the coefficient of y is constant after dividing by λ_X and the constant term is affine in x, so known rows force subsequent coefficients by interpolation. Enforcing partial column caps and final column sums gives visit counts [1225,2229,386,14,1,0,0,0,0,0], no complete array. Thus at most one layer exists per member.

## Moments through degree nine

If a nonzero degree-nine form F vanished on U_B, six-blocking and the incidence identity would give lower bound576 for Σ_L(n_L−6)(n_L−9). A factor with n_L≤14 contributes at most40, and any factor at most88, with no positive nonfactor terms. Hence at least five factors contain ≥15 U_B sites, since 4·88+5·40=552<576. Such a projective pencil has at most three nonuniform plane profiles; the Radon Vandermonde identity makes their reduced residues affine or constant. A nonconstant affine profile has least residue mass136>102, impossible. A nonuniform constant must be a layer, and the Radon constant identity 6(b−1)≡0 implies exactly one such profile. Thus the five heavy factors all contain the sole possible layer normal. A nonfactor radial through it contains at most 1+9−5=5 U_B sites, contrary to six-blocking. This proves moment vanishings through nine, equivalently residue occupancy degree at most six. The subsequent weighted proof excludes the cardinality.
