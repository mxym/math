# Size 51: uniform-pencil cap nine

After the size51 cap-nine plane theorem in [FugledeF17Size51MomentSix.md](FugledeF17Size51MomentSix.md), every occupancy profile has peak≤9, and projective lines have at most ten uniform normals. A projective line with ten uniforms has b=8 positive profiles, all of reduced degree≤6. There are no genuine degrees one through four under cap9, so the profiles are either all quintic, or all sextic: the presence of any sextic forces at least eight by independence of any seven sixth powers. We show that neither case satisfies the finite-field Radon congruence.

In the sextic case all eight profiles can be centered: one common translation kills two fifth coefficients, and the other six vanish by degree-five Vandermonde. Their leading relation has one-dimensional kernel, with two common scaling classes modulo sixth powers. There are 120 genuine centered cap9 mass51 sextic profiles. The verifier [verify-fuglede-f17-size51-any-sextic-pencil.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-any-sextic-pencil.js) filters coefficient options from degrees four through zero in all 17 projective slope-mask orbits, without imposing a tight-pencil weight constraint. The number of partial paths after degree four, three, two, one is respectively 5,1,1,1, and there are zero after degree zero.

In the quintic case there is exactly one centered profile per nonzero leading coefficient, by the cap9 mass51 catalogue. Normalize the common leading-coefficient vector up to fifth powers (all scalars), and enumerate the two-dimensional leading degree-five kernel with all eight coordinates nonzero. Remove two fourth coefficients by one planar translation. The remaining six coefficients form a one-dimensional kernel at degree four; their values uniquely set the translation center of each profile. The verifier [verify-fuglede-f17-size51-any-quintic-pencil.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-any-quintic-pencil.js) checks all 2890 admissible assignments across the same seventeen projective slope-mask orbits. None satisfies the remaining homogeneous equations in degrees three through zero.

Thus no projective line has ten uniform normals, and every uniform-pencil cap is nine. No size51 exclusion is claimed here.
