# A triangle transversal of size one in the complement implies the (2,k−1) Tihany case

**Theorem.** Let G be a finite connected double-critical graph: for every edge xy, χ(G−x−y)=χ(G)−2. If for some vertex v, α(G−v)≤2, then G is complete. Equivalently the hypothesis says that every triangle in the complement F of G contains v. This settles only a hereditary restricted family, not the general double-critical conjecture or Tihany splits other than (2,k−1).

## Proof

For a graph J write ρ(J)=|V(J)|−χ(complement J), the largest saving in a partition into cliques; a clique of size s saves s−1. Write ν(J) for the maximum matching size. Then ν(J)≤ρ(J) always, with equality when J is triangle-free.

The single-vertex case is immediate. Connectivity provides a neighbor y of each x otherwise, so the double-critical equality, followed by restoring y or x with a new color, gives χ(G−x)=χ(G)−1 for each x. Consequently ρ(F−x)=ρ(F) for each x, and ρ(F−x−y)=ρ(F) for each distinct nonadjacent pair in F.

Put H=F−v, triangle-free. Thus M=ν(H)=ρ(F), and ν(F)=M by monotonicity and ν(F)≤ρ(F). A vertex u can be exposed by a maximum matching of H if and only if vu is not an edge of F. In one direction an exposed u adjacent to v would augment the matching by adding vu. In the other, vu a nonedge gives ν(H−u)=ρ(F−v−u)=M.

Use the Gallai–Edmonds decomposition D,A,C of H, with D the exposable vertices, A=N_H(D) minus D and C the remainder. Thus N_F(v)=A∪C. Each component D_i of H[D] is odd and factor-critical. A maximum matching meets every vertex of A with an edge into a distinct D_i, is near-perfect internally in every D_i, and restricts to a perfect matching on C. If C is nonempty, choose an edge bc of that perfect matching. Replacing its pair by the clique vbc in the matching-derived clique partition yields saving M+1 in F, impossible. Therefore C is empty and M=|A|+m_D, where m_D=Σ_i (|D_i|−1)/2.

If some a∈A and z∈D were nonadjacent in F, double criticality would require saving M in F−a−z. In such a packing, all cliques have size at most 3 and at most one triangle is used, since F−v is triangle-free. If no triangle is used, every edge outside the D_i uses a remaining A-vertex (v has no D-neighbors; there are no edges between D_i), while the D_i give at most m_D edges, even after removing z. The saving is at most |A|−1+m_D. If the packing uses a triangle then it is vbc with b,c∈A distinct; after removing them the same budget bounds the saving by 2+|A|−3+m_D. Both are M−1. Hence A is complete to D. If A is nonempty then D is nonempty by definition, and A must be independent because H is triangle-free. If A is empty this is automatic. Thus F has no triangle at all.

Now apply Gallai–Edmonds to triangle-free F itself: since ν(F−u)=ρ(F−u)=ρ(F)=ν(F) for every u, its exposable set is V(F), so each component is odd and factor-critical. If a component of order 2j+1 contains nonadjacent x,y, deleting them lowers its maximum matching size from j to at most j−1 (fewer vertices), contradicting ν(F−x−y)=ρ(F−x−y)=ρ(F)=ν(F). Thus its components are cliques; triangle-freeness and odd order make them singletons. This proves the theorem.

**Corollary.** For any G in the hereditary class, if χ(G)=k>ω(G), some edge xy satisfies χ(G−x−y)≥k−1. Otherwise take an induced k-vertex-critical subgraph G′; it is connected and noncomplete. For every edge xy of G′, the assumed upper bound k−2 transfers to G′−x−y, and the reverse bound follows by adding x and y with new colors to any coloring. Hence G′ is double-critical. If the witness v was deleted, G′ has independence number at most 2 and any chosen vertex serves as witness. The theorem contradicts G′ noncomplete. The edge and its complement give the required partition.

**Constructive bonus.** The saving ρ(F) is the maximum of ν(F) and 2+ν(F−v−b−c) over edges bc in F[N_F(v)]. Find v by enumerating triangles and intersecting their vertex sets, or choose any v if F is triangle-free and nonempty. Hence matching computes χ on this class in polynomial time, and scanning the edges of G with the same procedure on G−x−y finds a partition. Empty graphs and empty triangle lists are handled directly.

## Nonoverlap family

For each k≥7 take r,s≥2 with k=r+s+3. Construct F from triangle T and separate cycles of lengths 2r+1 and 2s+1, joining each vertex of T to one distinct vertex on each cycle, with no additional edges. T is the sole triangle. The total n=2r+2s+5 is odd. A pairs-only saving is ≤floor(n/2)=r+s+2, and taking T and maximum matchings of both cycles realizes r+s+2. Any packing using T has no higher saving. Hence χ(G)=k for G=complement F. Also α(F)≤r+s+1, by bounding independently on T and the cycles, so ω(G)≤k−2. G is vertex-critical: deleting a cycle vertex permits a perfect matching of the resulting even path with T and a maximum matching of the other cycle; deleting a T vertex permits matching the two others to different cycles and matching each resulting even path perfectly. Both achieve unchanged saving r+s+2. G is connected by the complete cross-cycle adjacency and by neighbors of each T vertex on the cycles. F is connected, so G is not a nontrivial join. G has independence number three and an induced claw (T plus a cycle vertex without F-neighbors in T). It also has an induced four-hole (use two F cycle edges in distinct cycles), so it is not covered by even-hole-free, claw-free, independence-at-most-two, or Song's short-forbidden-hole results.

## Primary-prior-art screen and limitations

Balogh, Kostochka, Prince and Stiebitz handle independence number two for all Tihany splits, in their [quasi-line paper](https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/the-erdodouble-acutes-lov%C3%A1sz-tihany-conjecture-for-quasi-line-gra/); see also [Wang–Yu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08017). [Song's forbidden-holes theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11437) covers independence at least three and no holes of length 4 through 2α−1; the example above has a four-hole. [Even-hole-free result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20376) and [claw-free progress](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15164) do not entail this family; neither does [complete minors for low chromatic number](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.3133). Gallai–Edmonds use in Tihany-type matching arguments predates this proof; the particular triangle-transversal application and budget, not the decomposition, are the contribution. Searches for double-critical and Tihany combined with independence number three, pairwise/common triangle intersections, triangle transversals in the complement, and one vertex deletion did not identify the same theorem, but literature nonexistence has not been proved. This is a proof by matching and clique-partition budgets, not an application of ETG or a new general tensor method.

A possible next problem: an independent two-vertex triangle transversal in F fixes the same savings on deleting both vertices under double criticality. The exposed vertices of the triangle-free remainder cannot meet either apex. However, forced adjacency to the perfect-matching core C no longer follows, since deleting just one apex can leave triangles through the other. Do not claim the two-vertex extension.
