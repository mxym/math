## Four-blocking under forms of degree at most seven

Indeed any noncollinear triangle already determines three directions, and any fourth site would give a fourth direction (normalize a marked affine triangle and check its three slopes); so the entire cap is five. Thus averaging gives seventeen full five-supports, using at most three axial directions, so at least six are parallel to an axis e. In the quotient by e their locations have line cap3, since four full supports in one spatial affine plane would give twenty sites. Thus at least five distinct radial lines are linear factors of F.

If k is their count, each nonfactor radial line has at most 1+d−k≤3 U_B sites. An affine plane through a full five-support in B' with these directions contains exactly five sites: an additional outside point would give five transverse directions and the axial direction. For k≤6, the pencil through such a support has total occupancy at most 90+12k≤162, contradicting 85+17·5=170. For k=7 all factors are radial, so the quotient set of B' along e has at most seven directions. The image has at least seventeen sites by line cap5; more than seventeen determines every direction. If exactly seventeen, all fibers are full five-supports, giving quotient line cap3. Their 136 pairs need at least nine directions, since each direction permits at most 5·binom(3,2)+binom(2,2)=16 pairs. Contradiction in all cases. Hence U_B is four-blocking under F for every d≤7.

## Degree six

Under a nonzero degree-six form F vanishing on U_B, four-blocking gives lower bound 726 for Σ(n_L−4)(n_L−6). Nonfactor terms are nonpositive. A factor with n_L≤14 contributes at most80, and any factor at most143. Thus at least four factors have n_L≥15, since 3·143+3·80=669. Each contains the unique possible layer normal, which is outside U_B. Any radial nonfactor through that common point would have at most 6−4=2 U_B sites (even the weaker bound 1+6−4=3 suffices), contradiction. Hence all moments through six vanish.
