# Semisimple boundaries and exact codimension-five certificates

This continues the argument in [SalmonResidualSupportCodimensionFive.md](SalmonResidualSupportCodimensionFive.md).

## Semisimple projections with two missing minors

If at least three kernel coordinates vanish across the two modes, the image has dimension at most 35. Zero or one vanishing coordinate was covered previously. With exactly two vanishing coordinates there are three possibilities. If they are on the same side, two projected factors in that mode are proportional. The corresponding two rank-one matrix lines span a Segre line. One can choose any independent ordered pair of rank-one lines there and rechoose their C factors, holding the tensor fixed. This contributes two fiber dimensions beyond the eight rescalings; throughout this family the two zero kernel coordinates are retained. If the modes both have a missing minor with the same index, the other three factors span a two-plane on each side. Their three-dimensional matrix span meets the rank-one quadric of that \(2\times2\) compression in a plane conic, every point of which can vary in at least a one-dimensional component. Choosing three independent lines on this conic and rechoosing their C factors contributes at least three fiber dimensions beyond those rescalings; the unchanged fourth factor is outside the respective two-planes, and the collinear triples are preserved. An open neighborhood of the original choices can preserve all nonzero minors. These image dimensions are at most 34 and 33 respectively. If the missing indices differ, projective changes on each side and permutation of the common labels put them in the single orbit checked below; the independent C basis absorbs simple-factor rescalings.

## Five regularity checks

Take projection columns \(e_0,e_1,e_2,e_0+e_1+e_2\) on both sides, except replace the last column of A by \(e_0+e_1\) and of B by \(e_0+e_2\) for distinct semisimple missing indices; replace only A's last column by \(e_0+e_2\) for single-dual flag \(k_1=0\), or \(e_0+e_1\) for flag \(k_2=0\). Use the standard dual block \(a_1b_0c_0+a_0b_1c_0+a_0b_0c_1\) and the length-three block \(\sum_{i+j+k=2}a_i b_j c_k\), with singleton or shifted dual blocks as appropriate.

The companion [verify-salmon-codimension-five.js](verify-salmon-codimension-five.js) gives 20 quintic and four sextic independent integer Jacobian rows at every sample. Its minors modulo 101 are \(41,28,41,12,33\), respectively for distinct semisimple zeros, dual flags 1 and 2, two dual factors, and length three plus a singleton. Each local scheme cut out by these equations has tangent dimension at most \(64-24=40\), and contains \(X\) of dimension 40. Its local ring is regular and has \(X\) as a same-dimensional closed subscheme, so the ideals agree there. The rank test uses the same explicit sextics as the preceding compression-boundary proof. This exhausts all loci that could have dimension greater than 35, proving the stated bound.
