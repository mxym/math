# Sources and scope

- **[M]** Piotr Marszalik, [*The Price of Privacy: Randomness Complexity of Graph-Based Multi-Secret Sharing*, arXiv:2610.10002v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10002v1); also [ePrint 2026/2395, verified November 11 revision](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2395/versions/20261111T233940Z.pdf). Section II, Eqs. (1)–(3) give the model (pointwise correctness, joint-view privacy conditional on the local bit, and log of source support); Proposition 1 and Theorem 3 give exact deterministic/one-bit characterizations for arbitrary graphs. We credit and use the forest construction from Theorem 3. Proposition 12 and Lemma 9 supply the source-weight reductions except \(Q=(1/2,1/4,1/4)\). Appendix C states an exact general forced-edge normal form that searches complete-view transports, and in the \(K_4\) application it separately tests the uniform and \(Q\) cases. On the same model we show \(Q\) always reduces to two, and analyze the remaining uniform-three case block by block on cacti. Theorem 5 already constructs the five-point mod-three example on four vertices, and Proposition 6 uses two independent bits on paths in its \(K_4\) four-state scheme. We neither claim the \(K_4\) classification nor apply our cactus bound to arbitrary graphs. The motivating larger-graph direction is in §V.
- **[A]** Hari Krishnan P. Anilkumar, Aayush Rajesh, Varun Narayanan, Manoj M. Prabhakaran and Vinod M. Prabhakaran, [*Randomness Requirements for Three-Secret Sharing*, arXiv:2304.14934v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.14934v1), [ISIT 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT54713.2023.10206455). This is exactly the standalone triangle model; its tabulated classifications are cited, not redone. A triangle inside a larger cactus can have a flexible attachment and is not subject to the standalone lower bounds.
- **Earlier work in this repository.** The cycle and connected unicyclic notes set up signed ternary closure on a single cycle. The present block rule reuses that argument; the new steps are the simultaneous whole-view composition at cut vertices, exact two-forest bound independent of the number of cycles, the incompatible-source example, and elimination of nonuniform three-state sources on arbitrary graphs.
- **Bounded overlap check.** We checked both versions of [M], including the [author's companion repository](https://github.com/pjmarszalik/price-of-privacy-code), and searched as of 2026-11-28 for “conditional multi-secret sharing cactus”, “Marszalik unicyclic/cactus randomness support”, and “three-state nonuniform collapse”. We found no cactus classification or general elimination of \(Q\) there. Access-structure sharing of one secret, Shannon-entropy optimization, larger coalitions, and threshold sharing are different problems; we make no claim about them or absolute priority.
