# Source record and duplicate boundaries

PDF page locations below use zero-based PDF indices.

1. **Definitions and exact graph algorithm.** Divesh Aggarwal, Zihan Li, Saswata Mukherjee, Maciej Obremski, João Ribeiro, *Complete Characterization of Randomness Extraction from DAG-Correlated Sources*, [ePrint 2026/066](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/066) and [ECCC TR26-011, Revision 2 (2026-10-14)](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/011/revision/2/download/). The fixed text is the October 14 revision on the [ECCC record](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/011/).
   - Definition 1 (p. 2) uses strict path ancestors; Definition 17 (p. 24) preserves original views when restricting a graph, and its Algorithm 1 leaves G unchanged when called on all vertices. Definition 19 (p. 25), Definitions 23 and 25 (pp. 35 and 37) specify the two optimizations for N≥2.
   - §5.4 Lemmas 6–7 (pp. 35–36) already reduce uniform resilience to intact sets and state the greedy removal rationale. The paragraph after Lemma 7 (p. 36) claims O(|V|+|E|) including computing view counts from a topological order; Algorithm 2 (p. 37) line 13 prints a uniform score missing the self-count. §5.4.1 (pp. 37–38) already proposes a global-weight threshold scan for entropic resilience, but its formula before Lemma 8 is stated for arbitrary subsets and Lemma 8 quantifies over every maximizer. The threshold optimization after restricting to ideals is correct; the source itself ends §5.4.1 with the valid O(N(N+E)) time bound.
   - The present note does not claim the threshold method itself as new. It isolates the representation issue, proves conditional exact complexity for one scalar on raw DAGs, and gives deficit-sensitive exact and approximation guarantees. It says nothing about the paper's extraction impossibility/existence theorems or the explicit low-entropy open question in §1.4.

2. **HSE and its tripartite base.** Amir Abboud, Virginia Vassilevska Williams, Joshua Wang, *Approximation and Fixed Parameter Subquadratic Algorithms for Radius and Diameter*, [arXiv:1506.01799v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.01799), pp. 2–3 (the Hitting Set Conjecture) and §4, pp. 8–9 (HSE graph). Their graph encodes common coordinates by two-hop connections. The orientation, dummy-coordinate balancing and exact scalar padding in this note are spelled out to distinguish reachability cardinalities from their distance-based objectives. Their Appendix A, Proposition 2 explicitly gives the implication from falsity of the Orthogonal Vectors Conjecture to falsity of the Hitting Set Conjecture; we do not reverse it or replace HSC by SETH.

3. **All-vertex reachability counts versus a single optimization scalar.** Michele Borassi, *A Note on the Complexity of Computing the Number of Reachable Vertices in a Digraph*, [arXiv:1602.02129v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.02129), abstract and §2. It proves a SETH-conditional lower bound for the vector of per-vertex exact reachability counts, even on DAGs. Reversing edges changes out-counts into in-counts. Its reduction does not, by output type alone, imply hardness of either SHEDAG resilience scalar; the optimization could in principle be computed without those counts. The exact one-call reduction in this note fills that separate gap under HSC.

4. **Classical reachability sketches.** Edith Cohen, *Size-Estimation Framework with Applications to Transitive Closure and Reachability*, [JCSS 55 (1997), 441–453](https://research.google/pubs/size-estimation-framework-with-applications-to-transitive-closure-and-reachability/) ([author manuscript](https://graphics.stanford.edu/courses/cs321/Private/Readings/cohen1997.pdf)), gives near-linear relative reachability-size estimation. Edith Cohen and Haim Kaplan, *Summarizing Data using Bottom-k Sketches*, [PODC 2007, pp. 225–234](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~haimk/papers/p225-cohen.pdf), especially p. 225, discuss coordinated bottom-k union and directed-graph reachability. No new sketch primitive or first near-linear reachability-size estimator is claimed here; the application to the resilience objective uses finite dyadic ranks, order correction, and the deficit comparison proved in §4 of this note.

5. **Bounded duplicate check.** Public title/keyword search for SHEDAG resilience, exact raw DAG complexity, Hitting Set, maximum reachability cardinality, out-reach centrality, and the reachability-count literature, together with the latest ECCC revision and the repository's unified research tree (checked at `80b0f183f26f914035529e20fcbf8b010599c408`), did not turn up the single-score padding/HSC implication or the deficit application. This is not an absolute priority claim.
