# Primary sources and exact overlap

- **Shuo Tan and Syed A. Jafar**, [arXiv:2607.10414v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10414v3), §2.1 and Lemmas 1–3/Theorem 1, fixes the uniform binary average-case quantum random access code (QRAC) model and proves the exact quantum optima at redundancies 1 and 2. The original LP target here is its [October 21, 2026 author long version](https://tseason2022.github.io/papers/qrac/qrac-long-version.pdf): §4.2, Eqs. (59)–(61) and Theorem 2, gives exactly our (2) with \(c=D/2^n\); §4.3 identifies Delsarte; §4.4/Theorem 3 evaluates it for \(1/c=n+1,n+2\) at the corresponding odd/even Hamming lengths. Equation (78) already gives an \(O(n^{-2})\) quantum/classical gap on those two families. §4.5/Corollary 2 already determines the exact \((6,3)\) quantum optimum. The wider §4.6 comparisons are numerical, including some additional fixed-redundancy points. §5 discusses the Delsarte bridge and stronger constraints; we evaluate its existing LP in a further parameter range rather than claiming a new relaxation or a conjecture about the full quantum optimum. [The companion code](https://github.com/TSEason2022/QRAC-LP) computes the LP and exact finite certificates; computed values do not supply an all-parameter analytic formula.
- **Lei Yu and Vincent Y. F. Tan**, [arXiv:1910.09416v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.09416v1), §II-B Problems 1–2, Theorem 3, and Proposition 2/Appendix C Eqs. (84)–(85), are a close prior. Their problem uses the same Krawtchouk constraints on a rescaled spectrum but minimizes tail mass \(\sum_{j\ge2} u_j\), not mean distance. More precisely, an equality-diagonal vector in (2) gives \(u_j=q_j/c\) feasible there at density \(a=c\); the coefficient constraints are the same since \(K_r(j)=\binom nr\kappa_r(j)\). The ratio maximized in their Eq. (84), with \(x=2t/n-1\), is exactly \((1-c-f_c(x))/c\); their continuous maximizing \(t_2\) becomes our \(x_*\). We do not claim discovery of that rational optimization. The new finite statement certifies the optimum of the **full mean-distance** objective by the pointwise affine majorant (9) for all relevant rows and an attaining distance distribution (4), not merely a bound for the other objective. Yu–Tan's fixed-density limit already implies \(\liminf_n D(n,c)\ge 2(1-\sqrt c)\) for fixed \(c<1/4\) via \(D(n,c)\ge 1-c+c\Lambda(n;c)\); together with the two-shell upper and cap monotonicity, it also yields the qualitative growing-redundancy leading constant when paired with the Hamming construction. Our uniform quantitative rate and exact finite formula do not use that limiting argument.
- **Classical distance programs.** Fang-Wei Fu, Victor Wei, and Raymond W. Yeung, [2001 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00284-5), and Beniamin Mounits, [arXiv:0706.3295v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.3295v1), use Krawtchouk duality for lower bounds on actual minimum average distance. Mounits §4, in particular Corollary 2, Examples 5–6 and Theorem 12, is the direct predecessor of the Tan–Jafar Hamming-length evaluations. Our even \(s=2\) row gives precisely Mounits's even affine bound. At the odd Hamming cap \(c=1/(n+1)\) the \(s=3\) row reproduces the known value, even though Mounits obtains the odd bound via a dimension shift. The row inequality (9) treats every \(s>1\). Actual minimum average distances of sufficiently small codes, including size eight, have distinct prior classifications (Mounits Theorem 1 attributes those to Jaeger et al.). A feasible LP distribution here is not claimed to be realized by a classical code or quantum ensemble.
- **Other operational components.** Han-Hsuan Lin and Ronald de Wolf, [arXiv:2506.01903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01903), Theorem 2, introduce the full-string PGM reconstruction used here. Joseph M. Renes, [arXiv:1707.01114](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01114), Eq. (12), supplies the general measurement comparison used by Tan–Jafar; our binary derivation is included for self-containment. Ruho Kondo et al., [arXiv:2604.21274v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21274v3), §III-A.2 Eqs. (64)–(67), and Tan–Jafar long version §4.4 already use Hamming codes as classical random access codes. Kangqiao Liu [arXiv:2607.15617v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15617v1) establishes that the square-root bound is false in the general model, via other parameters. Seiseki Akibue et al. [arXiv:2606.12700v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12700v1) give distinct spectral converse bounds and the redundancy-two attaining codes. None of those constructions is claimed here.
- **Bounded overlap check, 2026-11-28.** We checked the above primary versions and the Tan–Jafar author long version and companion, and searched for exact Delsarte minimum-average-distance solutions in the \(1/c\le n+1\) small-code region and for high-rate PGM–Delsarte QRAC follow-ups. We did not find an all-parameter mean-distance LP evaluation in this region beyond the cited boundary cases. The novelty claim is limited to the exact finite evaluation/dual and the resulting explicit bound and obstruction; no claim of absolute priority or a resolved full quantum optimum is made.
