# Prior art and scope

- Ugo Montanari, *Networks of constraints: Fundamental properties and applications to picture processing*, Information Sciences 7 (1974), 95–132, [original paper](https://cse.unl.edu/~choueiry/Documents/Montanari-74.pdf), studies composition and intersection of binary constraints and series-parallel networks. Peter Jeavons, *Constructing Constraints*, CP 1998, LNCS 1520, 2–16, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-49481-2_2), explicitly connects these operations to series and parallel constructions. The network syntax and endpoint semantics are not new.
- Patrick Morton, *Solutions of diophantine equations as periodic points of p-adic algebraic functions. I*, New York J. Math. 22 (2016), 715–740, [paper](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2016/22-33v.pdf), §3, printed p. 727, (11)–(12), uses iterated resultants and a diagonal to capture periodic paths. Rin Gotou, *Dynamical Systems of Correspondences on the Projective Line I: Moduli Spaces and Multiplier Maps*, [arXiv:2109.06394, §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06394#S4.SS2), gives resultant composition. The raw path resultant is classical.
- Éric Schost, *Complexity results for triangular sets*, Journal of Symbolic Computation 36 (2003), 555–594, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0747-7171(03)00095-6), [author PDF](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~eschost/publications/trig.pdf), §5.1 Proposition 5 treats specialization of polynomial gcds and Bezout cofactors; §6 studies a degeneracy hypersurface. Hoon Hong and Jing Yang, [arXiv:2401.00408v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00408v2), give generalized subresultants for parametric gcd. Neither the generic gcd nor excluding bad specializations is presented as new.
- Manindra Agrawal, Nitin Saxena and Shubham Sahai Srivastava, *Integer Factoring Using Small Algebraic Dependencies*, MFCS 2016, article 6, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2016.6), discuss factoring with a given nondegenerate dependency involving a prime and complementary factor. Here a public catalogue constrains connected prime supports through hidden shared-variable witnesses; no dependency involving the entire complementary factor is supplied.
- Earlier repository [closed-walk anchors](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/c085586a3b5f3642a312b0ac1e254a135f278d05/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/bimonic-short-cycle-anchors) and [path bundles](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/857072dbafc13c7e1aca69e8291dfb1419fbf69d/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/bimonic-parallel-path-bundles) constrain one closed word or independent paths with common terminal values. The latter already uses the classical resultant linear combinations of Doug Ierardi and Dexter Kozen, [*Parallel Resultant Computation*, §20.2.4](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~kozen/Papers/resultant.pdf). The present fixture separates a coupled network from bundles of its constituent paths, not from every possible word over the alphabet.

The increment is the canonical-path pullback certificate and polynomial bit bound for explicit bimonic AND formulas in the stated path regime, followed by exact arithmetic membership. This comparison reflects bounded primary-source checking, not an assertion of global priority or a general constraint graph theorem.
