# Sources and claim boundaries

## Direct target

Shashank Kumar Ranu, Lewis Wooltorton, Alastair A. Abbott and Omar Fawzi,
[Randomness can be certified in energy-constrained semi-device-independent scenarios](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27357v1)
(arXiv:2608.27357v1, 27 August 2026).

- §§II–III, Eqs. (1)–(6): a common vacuum, a separate energy budget for each input, Bob's joint message/memory POVM, Eve's quantum side information, correlator magnitude, and generation input zero.
- Eq. (3): ideal correlator for the honest, unassisted regime. The new certificate uses this as benchmark data, not as the maximum over all entanglement-assisted strategies.
- §IV Eq. (13) and Appendix A: the dimension-independent hierarchy. The source's red Fig. 2 curve is reported positive to about 0.18, whereas its green fixed-qubit-source relaxation certifies to about 0.27 (Appendix B). Our analytic result, positive on the ideal curve for every w strictly below 1/5 and above zero, extends the reported red-curve interval at that benchmark. This is not uniform domination of the SDP, nor a comparison against all possible hierarchy levels or against the fixed-source curve. Appendix C already treats noise tolerance numerically; our formulas provide an analytic sufficient condition.
- §V explicitly says that extending its certified bounds to infinite-dimensional systems remains open after restricting the validity claim to arbitrarily large finite dimensions. Our separate operator proof addresses this extension for normal tensor-product states and local channels. The displayed finite-word problem itself permits arbitrary Hilbert spaces; the point is the direct realization-to-feasible-point proof under that physical model. We do not claim completeness of the hierarchy, a commuting-operator equivalence, results for singular states, or finite-round security.

## Nearby primary results

- Lang Li, Yutian Wu, Giulio Chiribella and Ravishankar Ramanathan, [On-Chip Quantum Randomness Amplification](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.12173v1), Supplementary Note 4, especially Eqs. (117)–(123) and Fig. 3: concurrent dimension-independent entanglement-assisted entropy certification, acknowledged in RWAF's Note Added. It uses an entanglement-assisted variational relaxation, compares min-entropy and von Neumann entropy and uses an input-dependent MDL score for amplification. Neither that result nor RWAF's existence of positive certification is claimed as new. We did not find either our explicit one-input error inequality or the proof for RWAF's own Eq. (13) in that comparison.
- Thomas A. Hahn, Efrat Gerchkovitz, Ester Yehuda and Rotem Arnon, [Certified Randomness Generation against Quantum Adversaries via Energy Constraints](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05766v1), introduction and §§II.1 and III.1.1: quantum side information, a qubit reduction based on two projectors, and von Neumann entropy bounds in a model that the introduction explicitly distinguishes from the more general shared-entanglement model of RWAF. The block-projection argument here instead retains the pre-existing memory by the shared marginal condition; no qubit-source reduction is claimed.
- Raffaele D'Avino, Gabriel Senno, Mir Alimuddin and Antonio Acín, [Entanglement in the energy-constrained prepare-and-measure scenario: applications to randomness certification and channel discrimination](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.27559v3), §IV.1 and Appendix B: correlations and guessing-probability bounds in the opposite direction, including the finite-dimensional equal-marginal formulation. The [TrureTuring deterministic-input dossier](https://www.trureturing.org/research/davino-et-al-2025-energy-constrained-deterministic-violation/) addresses a different, separable-threshold comparison; it is not the fully quantum certification bound proved here.
- Carles Roch i Carceller and Armin Tavakoli, [The role of entanglement in energy-restricted communication and randomness generation](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.27473v1), §III and Appendix A, journal [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4d80): vacuum-constrained entanglement-assisted communication and numerical upper bounds on entropy in the QRNG scenario, not the present analytic lower bound.
- Thomas Van Himbeeck et al., [Semi-device-independent framework based on natural physical assumptions](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2017-11-18-33/): vacuum/energy-constrained optical PM and classical-side-information certification already treat infinite-dimensional fields. The infinite-dimensional extension here is specific to the RWAF shared-entanglement/quantum-side-information SDP.
- A. de Oliveira Junior et al., [Quantum prepare-and-measure scenarios: concepts and methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20725v1), §IV.3: separates the average vacuum bound from dimensional truncation and distinguishes the shared-entanglement model. No identity with the present error curve was located.
- The conditional min-entropy/guessing equivalence is standard: König, Renner and Schaffner [arXiv:0807.1338](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1338) and Furrer, Åberg and Renner [arXiv:1004.1386](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1386). We give the binary trace-class proof in place of imposing a finiteness assumption.

This was a bounded comparison of the above texts and targeted queries for the source arXiv identifier, the energy-constrained prepare-and-measure analytic guessing bound with shared entanglement, and the infinite-dimensional extension. No direct duplicate was identified in the checked scope, which is not a worldwide priority claim. Standard two-projection calculus, Naimark dilation, and trace-class approximation are tools, not claimed novel. No result here is a deployment assessment.
