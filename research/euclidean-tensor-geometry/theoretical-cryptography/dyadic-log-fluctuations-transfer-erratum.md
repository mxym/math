# Erratum: one-place mark-transfer rate in the dyadic log-fluctuation theorem

In §5 of the [fixed original note](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/5040df62a2a08244b75347151306138b3db29c71/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/dyadic-log-fluctuations.md), replace the stated total one-place mark-transfer rate \(n^{-2+o(1)}\) by \(o(1/n)\), uniformly in the place. The Gaussian reference endpoint shell has probability \(o(1/n)\). The density-comparison contribution alone is \(n^{-2+o(1)}\), from \(n^{-1+o(1)}\) times the volume \(O((1+\log n)^{C_0}/n)\), and the norm-cutoff failure is superpolynomially small by §3. Summing the total over \(N=n/2\) places is still \(o(1)\), exactly what the mark-transfer argument uses. The joint log-volume CLT, hard-edge Poisson limit, and their asymptotic independence are unchanged.
