# Entropic share budgets for the Csirmaz access structures

We determine the exact distinguished-share budgets for the four-player Csirmaz structure under simultaneous entropy limits on leaked and unrecovered information. We also characterize, for every larger Csirmaz structure, the orders for which the minimum possible total share budget is attained at a critical leakage cap.

For a secret \(S\), let \(\eta\) cap \(I(S;X_U)/H(S)\) at every forbidden coalition and \(\delta\) cap \(H(S\mid X_Q)/H(S)\) at every qualified coalition. Put \(r=1-\delta\) and \(a=(r-\eta)_+\).

- For the two distinguished players \(u,v\) in the four-player structure, with \(u\) earlier in the defining order, the exact closed projection is
  \[
  x_u,x_v\ge a,\quad x_u+x_v\ge\max(3a,r),\quad 2x_u+x_v\ge r+2a.
  \]
  The last inequality matters: the symmetric converse would admit \((1/3,2/3)\) at \((\delta,\eta)=(0,2/3)\), but that vector is impossible.
- For \(k\) distinguished players and \(t=2^k-1\), at \(\eta=r(1-1/t)\) and \(r>0\), their total can reach the lower bound \(r\) in the closure exactly when the subset order is generated by assigning distinct powers of two to the missing participants. In that case the normalized budgets are proportional to those powers. Every other order has a strictly larger infimum.

Both results have linear constructions over any chosen finite base field (at rational parameters), together with converses for arbitrary finite, possibly nonlinear sharing distributions. The mechanism is an access-function entropy chain with an exact telescope; this yields asymmetric prefix inequalities at every \(k\). At perfect caps it recovers a projected convex hull of permutations of the known distinguished costs.

Here \(\delta=0\) means perfect reconstruction; a positive \(\delta\) is *not* a decoding failure probability. We make no claim about the whole convec region, the exact non-perfect projection at general \(k\), or the reverse-substitution factors in Khazaei's [ePrint 2026/2652](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2652/versions/20261111T084015Z.pdf).
