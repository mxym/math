The subspace checker independently obtains the entire reference set by brute iteration over a small integer cube and rank tests on each point, then compares this with enumeration by pivot-row coordinates. It includes signed rational slope coordinates, redundant columns, h=0, composite coprime atoms, and rational spans whose integer points are not generated by integer powers of the selected labels.
