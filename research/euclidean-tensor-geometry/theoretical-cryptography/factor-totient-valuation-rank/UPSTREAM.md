# Sources, comparison and nonclaims

* D. J. Bernstein, [*Factoring into coprimes in essentially linear time*, §7 Theorems 7.1 and 7.3, §9, Algorithm 13.2 and Theorems 13.3–13.4](https://cr.yp.to/lineartime/dcba-20040404.pdf), [published version](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgor.2004.04.009): the natural coprime base groups proportional valuation vectors. Applied to the pair (N,T), the elements with the same primitive valuation ray are exactly a ratio class, with its N exponent supplying the corresponding N block. Section 9 explicitly treats the two powers of one base using the gcd of their exponents; §13 gives the essentially-linear two-input computation. Neither this partition nor gcd-free bases are new here. The explicit J/W formula is a slower elementary route sufficient for the bounded candidate rays and bit-polynomial theorem.
* Erdős, Luca and Pomerance, [*On the proportion of numbers coprime to a given integer*, §2](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/PDF/gcdnphin14.pdf): the classical reduced ratio and its squarefree preimage; their Algorithm A uses a largest prime divisor. Extraction of an equal valuation-ratio block here is an elementary gcd/valuation computation, not a new generic factoring primitive.
* Ford, Konyagin and Luca, [*Prime chains and Pratt trees*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0473): the relation p | q-1 and the associated trees are standard. Here it is restricted to prime divisors of the one input. The bounded-ratio forest and bounded affine node forms are tools, not claims of first discovery.
* Źrałek, [*A deterministic version of Pollard's p-1 algorithm*, §6 pp.13–14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0707.4102v5), distinguishes unconditional expected time, ERH deterministic time and the deterministic single-totient results for products of two primes with multiplicities and three distinct primes with unequal exponents. Morain, Renault and Smith, [*Deterministic factoring with oracles*, §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.08444v2), include Landau's equal-order decomposition with multiple oracle calls and size criteria; the theorem here asks for one numeric value, not an oracle on arbitrary divisors. Hittmeir and Pomykała, [publisher](https://doi.org/10.3233/FI-2020-1891), study a stronger input including the totient's factorization.
* Previous results in this research line: [three components](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/e36b5afe51724bc4e0cba333b5be5c4d21a6b545/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/factor-totient-prime-graph-three-components) and [terminal exponent collisions](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/1b5bd61c9aebc7b96061a6e6b0ff61aeb0233e0c/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/factor-totient-terminal-level-collisions). They use exact products and specialized finite-fiber arguments. A negative displacement of the valuation ratio from 1 identifies terminal-exponent classes here as well. Neither component counts nor monochromatic terminal multiplicities are the rank criterion. No earlier theorem is needed to replay this proof.
* Renegar, [*On the Computational Complexity and Geometry of the First-order Theory of the Reals*, Part I](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/305759aa-f544-4006-aa85-f70ddce54e00/content): existential decision over real boxes with fixed dimension and polynomial degrees/bit heights. J. Milnor, [*On the Betti numbers of real varieties*, Theorem 2](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1964-0161339-9), bounds the total number of connected components of the full real polynomial zero set, hence its relevant isolated points. The differential argument uses the real implicit function theorem.

What is proposed is a promise criterion involving the incidence of valuation profiles across the actual trees, the corresponding full-rank location bound, and a strict-curvature argument for one missing rank. The comparisons above and targeted searches are not an exhaustive priority search. No positive density, infinite construction of prime stars, cryptographic insecurity, worst-case ordinary factoring or unrestricted one-value deterministic reduction is claimed.
