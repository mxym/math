# Primary-source scope and related results

- Filipe Martins Vono, [Antipodal necessity for rotation symmetric bent functions of degree at most three](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2844.pdf), [ePrint 2026/2844](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2844), version 4 dated November 8, 2026. Lemma 3.2 is the arbitrary odd-order balance-transfer result for quadratics; Corollary 3.3 gives the corresponding bent restriction for degree at most three. Lemma 3.1 and §6 identify why the cubic proof does not extend directly to quartics (halving can retain two antipodal pairs, and the derivative is now cubic). Appendix B.4 makes the latter limitation explicit. Our leading theorem makes this general fixed-space transfer sharp by realizing its failure at degree four with an actual bent parent. We make no claim to refute the homogeneous rotation-symmetric conjecture stated in its Introduction, equivalently [Zhang–Gao, arXiv:1303.2282v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.2282), Conjecture 1.1.
- Filipe Martins Vono, André Oliveira Naufel de Toledo, and Valéria Gonzaga Santos, [Modular Walsh rigidity for Boolean functions of degree at most four](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2900), ePrint 2026/2900. The November 21 revision abstract gives modular rigidity in 4m variables for m≥4 and odd modulus at least 2^(m−1), plus a 24-variable result. The first-version abstract, also visible in the [archive mirror](https://eprint.iacr.asia/2026/2900), explicitly already applies modular rigidity to a repeated-block restriction. We credit the modular approach, not claiming a new large-dimensional rigidity theorem. The current revision record and abstracts were accessible; an exact latest PDF could not be retrieved in the bounded search. Our four-variable rigidity and classification are proved directly (for arbitrary Boolean functions on that dimension), not inferred from the paper's m≥4 result.
- Vono, Santos and Naufel de Toledo, [Experimental Quantum Hardware Benchmarks of Flat Non-Clifford Hypergraph States, Antipodal Obstructions, and Nonlocal Games on IBM Heron r2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34683v1), §2.2 Eq. (3), already exhibits a degree-four nonbent function with the mod-3 residues of a bent spectrum, by tensoring a one-point perturbation of a four-variable affine function with a quadratic bent function. Neither the modular phenomenon nor the one-point examples alone are claimed here. Our counterexample starts with a *genuinely bent invariant parent*, and the four-variable statement characterizes all possibilities, not just a modular example.
- Vono, Naufel de Toledo and Santos, [Homogeneous rotation symmetric bent functions in twelve variables are quadratic](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2949), ePrint 2026/2949, submitted November 19, verified upload November 22. The source record already closes homogeneous n=12, including quartic; it is not an application claimed here. Our fully rotation-invariant 12-variable derivative example is nonhomogeneous and explicitly **not bent**. Our 12-variable bent restriction example requires only the order-three subgroup symmetry, not the full cycle.
- T. W. Cusick and E. M. Sanger, [Rotation Symmetric Bent Boolean Functions for n=2p](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.09313v1), Lemma 3.4/Theorem 3.8, give orbit congruence and short-cycle necessary conditions in n=2p. Lei Sun, Zexia Shi, Jian Liu and Fang-Wei Fu, [On the conjecture about the nonexistence of homogeneous rotation symmetric bent functions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-026-01848-4), give further partial orbit/derivative criteria (see also Vono v4 Introduction for exact nearby results). Our parity calculation is for the period-four fold in n=4ℓ^a, not a new short-cycle method or a new general homogeneity exclusion.
- Spread-line indicator Fourier calculations are standard partial-spread geometry. See Dillon's construction as presented in Claude Carlet, [Boolean Functions for Cryptography and Coding Theory](https://www.math.univ-paris13.fr/~carlet/book-fcts-Bool-vect-crypt-codes.pdf), §6.1, and Tu–Carlet–Zeng–Zhang, [k-idempotent Bent functions in Partial Spread class](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1361), for nearby Frobenius-invariant partial-spread bent constructions. Our 8-variable witness wires two four-dimensional spreads; the claimed result is its exact order-three fixed-space failure at minimum degree and dimension, not a new general construction class.

As of November 28, 2026, a title/identifier and keyword search for fixed-space restriction of invariant bent functions, modular Walsh rigidity, period-four folds, and partial-spread constructions did not locate the explicit sharp quartic bent-parent example or the exact period-four criterion below. This is a bounded check, not a categorical priority claim. No implementation, attack, protocol-security, or experimental conclusion is made.
