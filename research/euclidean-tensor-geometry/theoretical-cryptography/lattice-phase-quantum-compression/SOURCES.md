# Primary sources and scope

- **[FG]** Omri Farber and Gilad Gour, [Classical Communication Helps Single-Shot Quantum Compression, arXiv:2610.14785v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14785v1) (only posted version found as of 2026-11-28). Section II Eqs. (9)–(14) fixes the purified-source objective, root (not squared) fidelity, and quantum cost \(\log_2 m\) for an arbitrary integer dimension \(m\). Their Theorem 5/Eq. (31) gives the exact LO dimension, Theorem 7/Eqs. (48)–(49) the exact LOCC dimension, and Lemma 4/Eqs. (25)–(28) the clipped quadratic optimizer; these results are used, not claimed anew. Section VI.2 Eq. (97) records the common first three orders. Section VI.3 Theorem 10/Eqs. (100)–(101) finds the limiting ratio \(2/e\) for nonlattice spectra. The paragraph immediately following its proof (arXiv HTML line 531, between Eq. (110) and Eq. (111)) retains the same \(O(1)\) for lattice spectra without evaluating its cutoff-phase dependence. Equations (7) and (15) of this note give the lattice LOCC term and assisted/unassisted comparison, with the classical LO term rederived from Strassen's setting. The zero-variance formulas already appear in [FG, Eq. (111)]. We do not address their separate §VII question of exact finite-resource state merging when Bob has nontrivial side information.
- **Known spectral/one-shot results.** Dina Abdelhadi and Joseph M. Renes [arXiv:1905.08268](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08268), §III and Eq. (29), give the pure-state quadratic reduction of partially smoothed min-entropy and the second-order compression analysis, with a different error convention. Bartosz Regula et al. [arXiv:1711.10512](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10512), Theorem 2, evaluate the distillation norm used for the corresponding spectral formula; the exact spectral optimizer and operational reductions are not new here. For adjacent finite-resource problems see Thomas Theurer, Kun Fang, and Gilad Gour [arXiv:2312.17088](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17088) (single-shot entanglement manipulation), and Mario Berta, Hao-Chung Cheng, Roberto Rubboli, and Marco Tomamichel [arXiv:2608.27202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27202) (state-merging strong-converse exponent, not this fixed-fidelity lattice prefactor).
- **Classical source coding and hypothesis testing.** Ioannis Kontoyiannis and Sergio Verdú [arXiv:1212.2668](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.2668) (journal title: *Optimal Lossless Data Compression: Non-Asymptotics and Asymptotics*), §V and Theorems 16–17, give the relevant classical spectral counting and nonasymptotic/third-order bounds; the nonlattice exact constant is already known. Volker Strassen **[S]**, [*Asymptotic Estimates in Shannon's Information Theory* (English translation)](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~pmlut/strassen.pdf), Prague conference 1962 / proceedings 1964, Theorem 1.1 and §2 around Eqs. (2.23)–(2.36), already derives the periodic lattice cardinality correction and accounts for fractional cutoff mass. In our notation, putting \(d=h(\vartheta-1/2)\) gives the factor \(h e^{h/2} U_h(\vartheta)=e^{-d}[d+h/2+h/(e^h-1)]\), matching the uncollected expression after (2.36) in the translation. This classical LO factor and the existence of a classical phase are not new. Masahito Hayashi **[H]** [arXiv:1811.00262v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.00262v2), §IV Theorem 2/Eq. (46) and §II-C Eq. (12), explicitly gives an \(n\)-independent term with a function of the lattice span. We only correct the lattice-inclusive randomized-test statement in that fixed arXiv version: our Eq. (17) is a direct two-subsequence counterexample with \(P,Q\) normalized and \(V>0\), already at fixed error \(1/2\). [H, Remark 1] already notes a difference from Strassen's lattice term. The ordinary lattice continuity correction is classical; [H, §VIII Proposition 10/PDF Eq. (103) and §X-A PDF Eq. (131)] drops it. The [arXiv HTML rendering](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.00262v2) renumbers the expansion, constant, and two proof equations as (47), (13), (106), and (135), respectively. [FG]'s nonlattice use of [H] is not challenged by this example. No implication about other statements of [H] or an operational cryptographic task is asserted.
- **Recent lattice-prefactor precedent in a different model.** Theshani Nuradha, Sujeet Bhalerao, Christoph Hirche, Mark M. Wilde, and Mingze Xu [arXiv:2611.30038v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30038v1), Example 4.29/Eqs. (4.155)–(4.156), explicitly describe a periodic lattice prefactor for *symmetric Bayesian* quantum testing in the Chernoff regime. This is a direct precedent for the general phase phenomenon, not the fixed-type-I/central threshold or the LOCC-versus-LO ratio above.
- **Bounded overlap check.** On 2026-11-28 we checked the linked versions, references and follow-up searches for the Farber–Gour identifier/title with “lattice”, “quadratic smoothing”, “constant-order”, and “partially smoothed” terms; and searched Hayashi's identifier/title with “lattice”, “erratum”, and “hypothesis testing” terms. We found no matching LOCC/LO lattice-phase comparison or public separate erratum for the cited Theorem 2 in the material searched. This is not an absolute priority claim. The connected math research index was checked separately for overlapping compression/phase work.
