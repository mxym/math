# Provenance and scope

* The Sylvester determinant and the formula for a resultant as a product over the roots are classical; we state the specialised formula in the proof. Deterministic integer-root retrieval can be based on A. K. Lenstra, H. W. Lenstra Jr. and L. Lovász, [Factoring polynomials with rational coefficients](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~lenstrahw/PUBLICATIONS/1982f/art.pdf), Math. Ann. 261 (1982), 515-534, by reading off linear factors. We use only polynomial bit complexity. Primality testing uses M. Agrawal, N. Kayal and N. Saxena, [PRIMES is in P](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2004.160.781).
* Patrick Morton, [Solutions of diophantine equations as periodic points of p-adic algebraic functions. I](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2016/22-33v.pdf), New York J. Math. 22 (2016), 715-740, §3, printed p. 727, equations (11)-(12), already forms the recursive resultant for a fixed multivalued relation and substitutes the endpoint equal to the start to describe closed paths. Rin Gotou, [Dynamical Systems of Correspondences on the Projective Line I: Moduli Spaces and Multiplier Maps](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06394#S4.SS2), arXiv:2109.06394v1, §4.2 Propositions 4.10-4.11, Definitions 4.13 and 4.15, also gives composition and iteration of correspondences by resultants. The endpoint recursion and diagonal step are established, not originality claims. In this arithmetic promise theorem the bimonic hypothesis makes the affine specialized endpoint argument uniform, the words may come from a length-indexed catalogue, and a bounded word and coefficient-height calculation is combined with an actual prime-divisor walk certificate and exact membership.
* János Kasza, [On the graphs of primes determined by linear functions](https://ac.inf.elte.hu/Vol_043_2014/181_43.pdf), Ann. Univ. Sci. Budapest, Sect. Comp. 43 (2014), 181-191, DOI [10.71352/ac.43.181](https://doi.org/10.71352/ac.43.181), §1, connects a prime p to the prime divisors of F(p), then studies cycles, including Cunningham-chain cases. Here an edge is an exact root relation on a pair of primes rather than prime divisorship of F(p), and the task is to recover the support of the presented integer, not enumerate cycles in a range of primes. We do not claim the graph-of-primes viewpoint or traversal is new.
* Fixed points of compositions are standard in arithmetic dynamics, including semigroup actions; see J. H. Silverman, [Survey lecture on arithmetic dynamics](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/33176?nt=1), ICM 2022 vol. 3, pp. 1682-1710, §§2-3. See also B. Hutz, [Dynatomic cycles for morphisms of projective varieties](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2010/16-8v.pdf), NYJM 16 (2010), 125-159, for fixed points of morphisms. The directed polynomial-map special case exploits this elementary mechanism, not a new theorem about periodic points. The main result uses multivalued relations, and its anchor is a nonzero diagonal eliminant of the entire closed word.
* M. Agrawal, N. Saxena and S. S. Srivastava, [Integer Factoring Using Small Algebraic Dependencies](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2016.6), MFCS 2016, article 6, pp. 6:3-6:4, discuss supplied exact relations for two prime factors or for a prime and its cofactor. Direct substitution into such a given relation is already classical; their cyclotomic speed claims invoke other conjectural ingredients. Here the relations are public options at each unknown edge, the exact short word is unknown, and one finite anchor together with connectedness suffices for arbitrary support size.
* The [same repository's constant monomial fibre work](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/56a55c8a3a7b048a3c483fc52ed3e2fa1c0c08a0/research/euclidean-tensor-geometry/theoretical-cryptography/factor-bare-n-mixed-tree-constant-fibers/PROOF.md), §§1-3, propagates resultants along a fixed bounded-size tree and then studies a global monomial level. The present bimonic restriction is stronger on each edge, but a different promise permits a growing support; the finite anchor is inferred from a short closed word independent of the monomial equations. The established resultant/diagonal construction is used here for a specific length-uniform arithmetic problem; the claimed increment is the bit bound for growing catalogue words and the proof of complete factorization and exact membership via actual hidden prime-support walks. This is a bounded comparison, not a claim of an exhaustive search or global priority.
