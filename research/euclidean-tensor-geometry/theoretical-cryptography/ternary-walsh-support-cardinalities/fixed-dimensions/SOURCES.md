# Scope and source

The underlying open question is the p-ary Walsh-support-cardinality question
in Maxence Jauberty and Pierrick Meaux, *On the Cardinality of the Walsh
Support of a Boolean Function*, IACR ePrint 2025/1651, April 2026 revision,
Section 8, page 49:

https://opencryptology.org/2025/1651/versions/20260428T172000Z.pdf

The frozen parent directory at commit
`8d61365407515d98b2653444224b2146ccc62edf` proves the ternary union over
dimensions, the six global exclusions, and exact dimensions 1--3.  This
subdirectory adds exact fixed-dimensional positive results.  It does not
claim results for primes other than 3, vectorial functions, Boolean-valued
functions on ternary domains, or a complete classification in dimension at
least 8.

The four searches are deterministic witness discovery only.  All truth
tables and all data needed for exact replay are included.  No third-party
catalogue or unverified numerical computation is used.
