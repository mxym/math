# Size 221 in F_17^3: a cap-bootstrap exclusion

**Theorem.** There is no spectral subset of F_17^3 of size 221. This leaves the other cardinalities untouched.

Assume a spectral pair (E,A) of size 221=13·17. A Fourier zero gives uniform occupancy thirteen across its seventeen perpendicular affine planes, by irreducibility of the seventeenth cyclotomic polynomial. Thus D(B)⊂U_{B'} for the two members B,B', with D the difference directions and U the uniform normals. The standard affine plane cap is seventeen: a plane with ≥18 sites determines all 18 directions of its projective line and so yields a completely uniform pencil for the other member. Such a pencil is impossible because an affine line with a member sites has incident-plane occupancy sum 221+17a, which cannot equal 18·13. These are the general odd-prime reductions in [Ferguson–Iosevich, Lemma 8.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1), as used for p=11.

If every projective line has at least r uniform normals, the incident planes through any affine line have at least r occupancies of thirteen and all other occupancies at most seventeen. Hence every affine line in either member has cap floor((85−4r)/17). Initially r≥1, since the partner contains more than seventeen points, so for any affine-line direction d two partner sites have equal dot product with d and furnish a uniform normal in d^⊥. This gives initial affine line cap4.

**Planar census.** Every planar 13-site subset in F_17² of affine line cap4 determines at least nine directions. The independent exact verifier [verify-fuglede-f17-size221-planar.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size221-planar.js) works as follows: if such a set had at most eight directions, normalize a marked noncollinear triangle to 0,e1,e2. Its direction mask extends to an eight-element mask containing the triangle slopes 0,−1,∞; there are binom(15,5)=3003 possible masks. In each mask, enumerate the points compatible with the marked triangle, and extend them in increasing order, requiring every new pair's direction to remain in the mask and rejecting any insertion making five collinear sites. This visits 1,028,829 states, reaches maximum size nine, and finds no 13-site set. Calculations are exact in F_17.

Apply the census to each pencil: for the linear plane P belonging to its projective line, one of the seventeen translates of P contains at least thirteen partner sites by averaging. Choose thirteen; they obey line cap4 and give at least nine directions on the projective line in the first member's U. Thus r≥9 for both members; the pencil bound improves their affine line cap to two. Now any chosen 13-site slice is a cap, so has all 78 point pairs supported by directions contributing at most six disjoint pairs per direction. Therefore it determines at least thirteen directions. This gives r≥13 and affine line cap floor((85−4·13)/17)=1, impossible for either 221-point member. Contradiction.

For comparison, the general high-density result of [Malikiosis, Theorem 1.4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43670-023-00078-2) excludes sizes strictly between 289−16√17 and289. Since 289−16√17≈223.03, it does not exclude size221. No priority claim is made here.
