# Classical cyclotomic determinant obstruction for the finite-field cardinality sieve

This note applies an existing obstruction for odd generalized Hadamard orders, not a new nonexistence theorem. See [de Launey, *On the non-existence of generalised Hadamard matrices* (1984)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-3758(84)90062-4), as restated in [Egan, Flannery and Ó Catháin, Lemma 2.3 and Theorem 2.6](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.02717#S2.SS3). The self-conjugacy and determinant calculation is also explicit in [*A generalisation of bent vectors for Butson Hadamard matrices*, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16579#S2). We include the short calculation for the exact application.

## The obstruction

If B is spectral in a vector space over F_p with spectrum A and |B|=|A|=n, the matrix H with entries ζ_p^(a·b) satisfies HH*=nI. Its determinant Δ lies in the ring of integers Z[ζ_p], and
```
Δ · conjugate(Δ) = n^n.
```
Let ℓ≠p be a rational prime with ℓ^j≡−1 mod p for some j. The prime ℓ is unramified in Q(ζ_p), and the decomposition group of every prime above ℓ is generated by the Frobenius ζ_p↦ζ_p^ℓ. Thus complex conjugation, the j-th Frobenius power, fixes each such prime ideal. At one such ideal the two factors on the left have the same valuation, making their product's valuation even. On the right the valuation is n·v_ℓ(n), because ℓ is unramified. If n and v_ℓ(n) are both odd, this is impossible.

| Field order p | Spectral cardinality n | Prime ℓ | j with ℓ^j ≡ −1 mod p |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 17 | 51 | 3 | 8 |
| 17 | 85 | 5 | 8 |
| 17 | 119 | 7 | 8 |
| 17 | 187 | 11 | 8 |
| 17 | 221 | 13 | 2 |
| 17 | 255 | 3 | 8 |
| 13 | 65 | 5 | 2 |
| 13 | 91 | 7 | 6 |
| 13 | 143 | 11 | 6 |

In every listed row the exhibited prime occurs exactly once in n. In particular 51 is excluded without any restriction on plane profiles. The geometric proofs already in the repository for 85, 119, 187 and 221 remain independent proofs, not new cardinality exclusions relative to the classical theorem. The size-51 geometric reductions are retained as research notes. This test does not exclude 34 in field order 17, or 153 where the prime 3 occurs with exponent two. It also does not imply nonexistence of general order-34 phase-17 Hadamard matrices; indeed [dimension-four spectral counterexamples of size 2p](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08734) exist. The remaining issue in field order 17 is the three-dimensional size-34 case.
