# Size 136 in F_17^3: reducing completion to one layered direction per member

Assume the degree-nine vanishing and six-blocking uniform-normal bounds in [FugledeF17Size136MomentNine.md](FugledeF17Size136MomentNine.md). This argument excludes a size136 spectral pair if each member has at most one layered normal; it isolates that geometric input, and does not assume moment vanishings of degree ten or eleven globally.

Let V_B be the positive-weight support of w_B=(Σ_t binom(n_t,2)−476)/17. As before W=Σ_v w_B(v)=1124 and the projective polarity graph on V_E,V_A has neighbor total weight C=36 at each vertex. Moment9 gives reduced profile degree≤6. The weights are integers: the variance sum Σ_t(n_t−8)^2 is divisible by17 because the reduced polynomial square has degree at most12<16, and is even because with fixed total mass its parity equals Σ_t(n_t−8)=0. The exact low-variance checker [verify-fuglede-f17-size136-low-variance.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size136-low-variance.js) rejects all profiles of weight1 and degree≤6, and all profiles of weights1 or2 and degree≤5. Indeed prescribed weight w imposes Σ(n_t−8)^2=34w and Σ(n_t−8)=0. At these weights a deviation of +9 would already exceed the square budget, so each deviation is its unique signed residue in [−8,8]. Choose the first d+1 deviations with partial sum of squares≤34w, interpolate the remaining values, and check both equalities. The exact counts of tested initial assignments are 1124023 at (d,w)=(6,1), 209317 at (5,1), and 1668741 at (5,2), with no survivors. Thus the positive minimum is at least two through degree6 and at least three through degree5. The lower-degree minima from the mass153 complement argument are 12,8,6,4 in degrees 1,2,3,4.

A tight pencil without a layer has b>0 nonuniform profiles. Its spectrum of residue degrees is limited by degree≤b−2 (with constants only for b=1) from the Radon Vandermonde identity. If b=3 then all profiles are affine and S=432, where S is their squared-weight sum. Cases b≤2 cannot occur without a layer. For b4,5,6 the lower degree minima give maxima336,288,336, respectively. If b7 the degree≤5 minimum three gives S≤18²+6·3²=378. If b≥8 the degree≤6 minimum two gives S≤(36−2(b−1))²+4(b−1)≤512, with the maximum at b8. A tight pencil containing a layer has that weight36 profile alone and S=1296. Double-counting squared neighbor sums against opposite weights thus gives
```
||w_B||² ≤ (512/36)·1124 +(1296−512)·(number of B layers).
```
If both members' profile degrees are at most five, apply instead S≤432 for all nonlayer pencils: for b≥8 the minimum three gives S≤288, and the previous lower-degree estimates are unchanged. Then ||w_B||²≤(432/36)·1124+(1296−432)·(number of B layers).

If B has a genuine sextic profile, then its homogeneous moment form F of degree ten is nonzero and its nonzero projective evaluations are exactly the sextic profiles, by pairing the leading coefficient with t^10. The elementary projective degree-d bound is at most 17d+1 zeros: if there is a rational line factor, move it to infinity and apply the affine polynomial bound (d−1)·17 to the residual; if no line factor, count along the eighteen lines through one zero, at most 1+18(d−1), which is smaller for d≤17. Thus F at degree ten yields N=|V_B|≥136. If neither member has a sextic, but B has a genuine quintic, its corresponding moment form is degree eleven. The stronger line-union/Sziklai bound from [FugledeF17Size136ConditionalM11.md](FugledeF17Size136ConditionalM11.md) applies unchanged at this degree: with a nonline residual use the Sziklai bound plus the line-union bound; with all rational lines, maximum concurrence t≤11−5=6 and the same two elementary line-union bounds show at most172 sites. Hence N≥135.

For a fixed support V_B of size N, tightness gives 1_{V_B}^T P w_{B'}=36N. Center the vectors and use P²=17I+J. With V=307, this gives
```
||w_{B'}||² ≥ W²/V + (18W−36V)² N/[17 V(V−N)].
```
The right-hand side grows with N; if N=V tightness is already impossible by 1^T P w_{B'}=18W. At N136 the lower bound is greater than (512/36)·1124+784, the upper bound allowing one layer in the sextic case. At N135 it is greater than (432/36)·1124+864, the corresponding one-layer upper bound in the quintic case. The accompanying exact verifier checks both by cross multiplication. These contradictions eliminate genuine sextic or quintic profiles under the layer hypothesis. If neither occurs then both members have profiles of degree at most four, equivalent to moment vanishings through degree eleven; the conditional quartic endgame in [FugledeF17Size136ConditionalM11.md](FugledeF17Size136ConditionalM11.md) applies. Thus the only remaining input for a complete exclusion by this route is an at-most-one-layer theorem.
