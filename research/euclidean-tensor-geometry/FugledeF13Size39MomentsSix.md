# Size 39 in F_13^3: vanishing moments through degree six

Use [the degree-five and layer results](FugledeF13Size39MomentsFiveNoLayers.md) for moments through five, no layered direction, line cap3, plane cap13, and the conclusion that each member's uniform-normal set has at most eight sites on any projective line. Suppose a nonzero homogeneous form F of degree six vanishes on the difference directions D(B) of one member B. We first show that every projective line meets D(B) in at least three sites.

Suppose some projective direction line has at most two D sites. Then each of its thirteen parallel affine slices is a three-point collinear set (triangle requires three directions, and line cap3 gives at most three points; use the total mass39). These supports use at most two axes, so at least seven are parallel to an axis e. Project along e. Choose seven of these triple support locations, obtaining a set Q in the quotient affine plane, with no five collinear by plane cap13. In particular it is noncollinear and contains a noncollinear six-set. Such a six-set uses at least five directions by the [exact size-65 planar verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size65-moments7.js). Each quotient pair in Q determines a radial projective line through e with at least five transverse D points and the axis e, by Cauchy–Davenport for the difference of two three-sets in the axial coordinate.

Let k be the number of distinct rational linear factors of F whose projective lines pass through e. Then any nonfactor line through e has at most 1+6−k D points (outside e only the residual form can vanish). If k≥2, all at least five radial lines supplied by Q pairs are factors. If k=5 then at least nine radial lines have at most two D sites and are therefore uniform normals for B, by the slice argument above. This contradicts the at-most-eight U bound. If k=6 then all D lies on the six radial factors. However the quotient image of B has at least thirteen points (each fiber has at most three), is noncollinear (else B is coplanar), and determines at most six directions. In a noncollinear planar set with at most six directions, every affine line has at most five sites, by connecting its sites to an external point. This contradicts the exact planar census with at most six directions and cap5 (the [size-78 geometric checker](verify-fuglede-f13-size78-geometric.js) excludes ten sites).

It remains to exclude k=0 and k=1. The following two observations apply.

1. For any Q pair whose radial line is not a factor, the line contains at most six D points, so the axial difference of the two three-sets has size exactly five. This means both three-sets are arithmetic progressions with common step up to sign by [Vosper's equality theorem](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-31.2.200). Each three-point progression has its step unique up to sign, so all seven supports have one common step a: after discarding all pairs in at most one exceptional quotient direction, the surviving graph on Q is connected, being the complete multipartite graph of the partition into parallel lines in that direction. Thus if a quotient difference is represented as a nonzero scalar δ times a fixed direction vector, the five cross roots on that radial line are a translated five-term progression of step a/δ up to sign.
2. For each nonfactor quotient direction, all its pair difference vectors are equal up to sign. In fact, two pairs produce exactly the same five transverse roots (these are all zeros on their radial line). A five-term progression over F_13 has its step unique up to sign: center by its mean and note that the only multiplicative stabilizers of {0,±1,±2} are ±1. So their quotient displacement scalars agree up to sign. The [exact verifier](verify-fuglede-f13-size39-seven-quotient.js) enumerates a marked noncollinear triangle normalized to 0,e_1,e_2, three-point relations and all four additional sites. It applies the same-vector-up-to-sign condition in all but a possible exceptional direction, and allows at most four collinear sites in that direction. With one exceptional direction it finds a minimum of six determined nonexceptional directions. With no exceptional direction it finds 210 normalized completions and a minimum of twelve directions. These are ordered-coordinate, triangle-normalized counts and need not coincide with affine orbit counts.

Choose homogeneous projective variables (X,Y,Z) such that e=(1,0,0), with Y,Z quotient coordinates. Write F=Σ_{i=0}^5 c_i(Y,Z) X^i, each c_i homogeneous of degree 6−i; X^6 is absent since e∈D. If k=0, on at least twelve distinct projective quotient directions its restriction in X has five distinct roots, so c_5 is not identically zero and is nonzero at each such direction. The coefficient of X² after centering at the five-root mean is zero for a symmetric five-term progression. Clearing denominators shows that the homogeneous binary degree-six form
```
25 c_2 c_5² − 15 c_4 c_3 c_5 + 4 c_4³
```
vanishes at these directions and is identically zero. Reduce modulo the nonzero binary linear form c_5 to infer c_5 divides c_4. Thus the center −c_4/(5c_5) is a binary linear form, and can be sheared off in X. In these new coordinates each even coefficient vanishes at all the same directions and has degree at most six, hence vanishes identically. The centered form is c_5 X^5+c_3 X³+c_1 X. On each of the same directions the progression roots give 25 c_1 c_5=4 c_3². This holds identically since it is a degree-six binary identity. Therefore c_5 divides c_3, and the identity then also gives c_5 divides c_1. Hence c_5 is a radial factor of F, contradiction.

If k=1, divide off its unique radial linear factor and write the remaining homogeneous degree-five form as H. Its leading X^5 coefficient is a nonzero constant by existence of five distinct cross roots on a nonfactor line. Make it monic and shear X to remove its linear-in-(Y,Z) root center. At at least six nonfactor quotient directions the centered form has roots 0,±s,±2s. Consequently all even-in-X coefficients of H vanish identically (their degrees are at most five), and the remaining progression identity holds identically (degree four). Thus
```
H = X(X²−S(Y,Z))(X²−4 S(Y,Z))
```
for a binary quadratic S. The squared step statement in observation 1 says S(P−Q)=a² for every quotient pair outside the exception direction.

If S is nondegenerate, the nonzero level conic S(V)=a² has no line component: its projective homogenization in three variables has rank three. Consequently two distinct translates centered at P and Q intersect in at most two affine points: subtract their equations to obtain a nonzero affine linear equation (nonzero by nondegeneracy and P≠Q), then restrict to that line. But choose two anchors in the same exception-direction line if there are any two (at most four sites on that line, so at least three others must have the prescribed level relative to both). If all seven have distinct exception-direction lines, any two anchors have at least five other sites satisfying both levels. Contradiction in either case. The form S therefore has rank one (not zero since a≠0), and is the square of a binary linear form L, because it attains the nonzero square a². The five rational factors X, X±L, X±2L are distinct and concurrent. The sixth factor, the original radial factor, might also meet this concurrency point, but in any event there are at least nine nonfactor radial lines through it if the sixth is not concurrent, and otherwise eight. If the sixth is concurrent, all six factors are concurrent and D lies in their union; the quotient along that concurrency axis again has at least thirteen sites and at most six directions, already excluded above. With exactly five concurrent factors, all nine nonfactor radial lines contain at most 1+6−5=2 D points, giving at least nine uniform normals for B, contradiction.

This proves three-blocking under F. Apply the projective incidence identity
```
Σ_L (n_L−3)(n_L−6) = N²−113N+3294 ≥102
```
for integral N=|D(B)| (the minimum is attained at 56 and 57). Every n_L≤8 since D(B) lies in the partner's U set. Nonfactor contributions are nonpositive; each of at most six factors contributes at most (8−3)(8−6)=10, contradiction. Thus the homogeneous degree-six moment form of each member vanishes identically by uniformity on the partner's directions, implying all mixed moments through six. This does not yet exclude cardinality 39.

## A separate affine-plane bound

The same no-layer conclusion gives an affine plane cap of nine. Indeed every plane section has at most three collinear sites and at most eight difference directions, because its differences belong to one pencil in the partner's uniform-normal set. A [separate finite check](verify-fuglede-f13-size39-planar-cap.js) normalizes a noncollinear triple to \(0,e_1,e_2\). Extend their three directions to any of the 462 eight-direction masks, and extend the triple by all compatible sites in increasing order, discarding any extension introducing four collinear points. Exactly 252,724 recursive nodes are visited and the maximum plane-section size allowed by these conditions is nine. A collinear section already has size at most three.
