# Size 85 in F_17^3: both members are nonlayered

Assume the standard caps and at most one layer per member from [FugledeF17Size85MomentSeven.md](FugledeF17Size85MomentSeven.md), and the full planar seventeen-site line-cap-five direction lower bound fourteen from [FugledeF17Size85DegreeEightGeometry.md](FugledeF17Size85DegreeEightGeometry.md). This note excludes layered members without further moment assumptions.

## Full planes and layers occur simultaneously in the pair

If B has a full affine plane of seventeen points, its linear direction plane L carries at least fourteen D(B) directions by the exact full-graph certificate. Its partner's uniform-normal pencil over L therefore has ≥14 sites and by the previous K≥13 argument has only one nonuniform normal e, a layer, lying in L. Conversely from any full slice of that partner layer the same reasoning gives a layer of B. Thus either neither member has a full plane or both have a layer. Moreover all full affine planes of B are parts of its unique layer: every such plane's directions force the same unique partner layer normal e in its tangent plane, so every full plane normal of B lies in e^⊥. The full layers of the partner already give ≥14 U_B sites on that projective line. As this pencil has only one nonuniform normal, it must be B's layer and it is the only normal in the pencil that can have occupancy seventeen. Thus ordinary profiles never lift a zero residue to 17.

Suppose both members are layered. At least one nonuniform normal distinct from the sole layer exists in each member. Indeed, summing the weights \((\sum_t \binom{n_t}{2}-170)/17\) over all 307 normals counts each of the \(\binom{85}{2}\) point pairs 18 times, so the total weight is 710, while the layer has weight 30. Choose one member B and let R be the highest reduced degree of its genuine nonconstant profiles. The M7 conclusion implies R≤8, and the absence of affine or quadratic profiles implies R≥3. Thus d=16−R lies between8 and13. Pairing profiles with powers of their projection variable shows all moments of degrees less than d vanish, and the degree-d homogeneous moment form M_d^B is nonzero (its nonzero evaluation directions are exactly the degree-R normals). The inference from pointwise vanishing to an identically-zero multivariate form uses degree<17 in every variable. Choose mutually dual coordinates B=(x,f_s(x),s), A=(u,t,g_t(u)), with five supported levels s and five t. Here is the coordinate justification: the B layer normal is its third coordinate, hence not in U_B, so A projects injectively along that axis by D(A)⊂U_B. The partner layer normal lies in B's tangent plane by the previous paragraph, so choose it as the second coordinate axis in the B difference space and as the second dual normal coordinate for A. It is not in U_A, giving the analogous injectivity of B across that axis. Each plane layer has size seventeen, so both graphs use the full first coordinate field.

Multiplying by a degree-four Lagrange indicator in s isolates any given B layer and proves its local planar moments through degree d−5 vanish, because global moments through d−1 vanish. Therefore the w-degree of M_d^B(u,v,w) is at most four: a term with w-degree greater than four pairs at most d−5 powers of its planar coordinate within each layer, which sum to zero. The form vanishes at its layer normal with multiplicity at least d−4 (in particular at least four if d=8), because each term has aggregate u,v degree at least d−4. The specialization v=1 is injective on homogeneous polynomials of a fixed degree, so H(u,w)=M_d^B(u,1,w) is nonzero. Some coefficient of w is a nonzero polynomial of degree≤d≤13 in u, so for at least four slopes a∈F_17 the specialization H(a,w) is nonzero and has at most four roots.

For each such good a, slice A by u−a t=x at every x∈F_17. Each slice has five points, one per t, and all its difference directions lie among at most four values of w/v: differences have v=t−t' nonzero and normalized u/v=a; M_d^B vanishes on U_B including all D(A). This gives a planar set (t,g_t(x+a t)) with at most four determined directions. A noncollinear five-site planar set with at most four directions must be an affine cross. The standalone [verify-fuglede-f17-size85-affine-cross.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size85-affine-cross.js) normalizes a marked affine triangle, scans all 15 four-direction masks containing its three directions, visits 69 nodes, and obtains six normalized noncollinear completions, each with exactly one center of reflection. This is without mask orbit pruning. Since a centrally symmetric five-set in odd characteristic consists of its center and two opposite pairs, the classification is precisely a cross.

Let T be the five supported t levels. If T is not centrally symmetric, each of the preceding five-point slices is collinear: a noncollinear cross reflects to a centrally symmetric t-set, contrary to T. Choose any three distinct levels t_i. Collinearity gives an equation Σ_i r_i g_{t_i}(x+a t_i)=0 at every x and every good a, using nonzero r_i with Σ_i r_i=Σ_i r_i t_i=0 (the standard affine interpolation relation). If T is centrally symmetric, its center t_0 and its opposite pairs of t levels are unique (two centers would imply invariance under a nonzero translation of a proper nonempty subset of F_17). Choose one pair t_-,t_+ around t_0. In every affine cross these two levels reflect to each other, and the same linear relation holds also for a collinear slice. Thus for these three levels one has the equation above with r=(1,−2,1) ordered around the center.

Represent the three graph functions by their reduced univariate polynomials of degree≤16 and let d be their maximum degree. If d≥2, let A_i be their coefficients of x^d. For each good a the equation is a polynomial equality in x, since it vanishes on the whole field and its degree≤16. The coefficients of x^d,x^(d−1),x^(d−2) viewed in a have degrees at most zero, one, two respectively. There are at least three good a, so each vanishes identically in a. Their leading coefficients, after removing nonzero factors d and binom(d,2), give Σ_i r_i A_i t_i^j=0 for j=0,1,2. The three t_i are distinct and r_i nonzero, so Vandermonde forces all A_i=0, contradiction. Thus the three graph functions are affine, making a full seventeen-point affine line and violating cap five. This proves that neither member has a layer and therefore every plane profile has cap sixteen and no zero lifts, without assuming an octic profile.
