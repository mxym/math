# Projected Koszul rank certificates for the quartic permanent and determinant

**Yongxian Zhang.** AI-assisted proof development and internal verification; no claim of external peer review or priority. For the quartic generic permanent and determinant as polynomials in 16 independent variables, the complex symmetric border ranks are at least 41 and 42, respectively. This is not a result about the 4-mode permanent tensor, and neither rank is determined exactly. Ordinary Waring and cactus ranks have different bounds and are not being compared.

Let V be spanned by the 16 matrix variable basis vectors, let U have dimension 9, and take the explicitly generated integer map B:V→U below. Apply the second derivative catalecticant of F, polarise the quadratic, apply B to its second tensor factor, and wedge that factor on the left with the input exterior vector. The resulting map is S²V*⊗Λ²U→V⊗Λ³U. A pure fourth power has image contained in e⊗(B(e)∧Λ²U), so rank at most binomial(8,2)=28. By subadditivity on powers and closedness of rank ≤r via minors, the same rank bound scales by symmetric border rank. The second derivative image is generated by the 36 two-by-two permanental/determinantal minors, using complementary row and column pairs; derivative signs in the determinant case merely rescale individual columns. The matrix of the restriction to the 36 generators times Λ²U thus suffices. The polar convention below differs from the canonical symmetric injection by an overall scalar common to all these squarefree quadratics, harmless for rank.

Over GF(101), ranks are exactly 1141 and 1153 in the generator order and indexing below. Ranks over Q/C are at least these: any corresponding nonzero minor modulo 101 is an integer minor nonzero in characteristic zero. Thus the lower bounds are ceil(1141/28)=41 and ceil(1153/28)=42.

The contribution is the concrete projected certificates, not the general Koszul method. [Farnsworth, Theorem 1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/1505.05079) gives symmetric border rank at least 38 for the same quartic determinant. [Boij–Teitler](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.08896) review the distinction from ordinary and cactus ranks. [Ju et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18394) study a determinant tensor upper bound and its consequences for polynomial Waring rank, not this lower bound. The middle catalecticant alone gives rank 36 for the quartic permanent and determinant. No claim of global priority is made.

## Exact replay

Run `node verify_quartic_projected_koszul.js` (no dependencies). The entry ordering, projection matrix generator and modular row reduction are explicit; the verifier asserts the ranks 1141 and 1153. This verifies nonzero minors in characteristic zero, not equality of the complex flattening ranks.
