# Size 85 in F_17^3: two preparatory constraints for degree eight

These statements supplement [FugledeF17Size85MomentSeven.md](FugledeF17Size85MomentSeven.md). They do not prove M8 or exclude size85.

## Full planar affine cap-five sets need at least fourteen directions

Let S be a 17-site noncollinear set in F_17² with affine line cap5. If it determines at most thirteen directions, choose a missing direction as vertical, and write it as a full graph y=f(x). At least four finite slopes are missing. For each such slope s, f(x)−sx is a permutation of F_17, so the polynomial in s given by Σ_x(f(x)−sx)^j vanishes there whenever 1≤j≤4. Its degree is at most j−1 because the top coefficient Σ_x x^j vanishes. Therefore the four or more roots make it identically zero and all mixed local moments through four vanish. In particular moments x^a y for a≤3 imply the reduced graph has degree at most12.

Its degree is at least2 by line cap5. For graph degree d, a vertical scaling makes it monic, an x translation kills its penultimate coefficient, and an affine y shear removes coefficients of degrees zero and one. All these preserve the needed conditions. The three moments x^a y² for a=0,1,2 require coefficients 16,15,14 of f² to be zero (there is no second field-power wrap). Consequently d7 and d8 are impossible by their top coefficients. For d≤6 these three conditions are automatic. For d≥9 they determine coefficients 16−d,15−d,14−d in descending order from the higher coefficients: each equation is linear in its new coefficient, with coefficient 2 times the leading monic coefficient, and involves no lower coefficients.

The standalone [verify-fuglede-f17-full-plane-cap5.js](verify-fuglede-f17-full-plane-cap5.js) enumerates every remaining choice after this pruning, tests y³,xy³,y⁴ moments exactly, imposes line cap5, and counts directions. The largest branch d12 has 17^6=24137569 choices, of which 2760 pass the moments and cap. Across all branches, the final direction histogram is 100 with fourteen, 192 with fifteen, 1410 with sixteen, 1421 with seventeen, and none with at most thirteen. This contradicts the supposition and proves the claimed lower bound. As a consequence a 16-site planar cap4 set also determines at least fourteen directions: if it determines at most thirteen, choose missing vertical, and complete its missing x coordinate by giving it the y value minus the sum of existing y's. Each other originally missing slope remains missing, since its unique incomplete projection value is the negative sum of the already distinct values. Completion gives a full 17-site cap5 set, contradiction.

## Four-blocking for a degree-eight moment zero locus; conditional spatial cap4

Let a nonzero degree-eight form F vanish on U_B. If some U_B line has at most three sites, then the proof of the degree-seven reduction gives at least six parallel full five-supports in the partner B' with common direction e, and at least five rational radial factors through e. Let k be the number of all such factors. Each nonfactor radial has at most 1+8−k≤4 D(B') sites, so an associated affine plane through an actual five-support contains exactly the five sites, as any outside site would give six directions. For k≤6 the total pencil bound 90+12k≤162 is less than the required170. For k=8 all factors are radial; the quotient along e has ≥17 sites and at most eight directions, impossible by the same argument as for degree seven (if exactly17, line cap3 and pair counting give ≥9).

If k=7, any radial nonfactor has at most two U_B sites. Its slice family gives 17 collinear five-supports in B' with at most two possible axial directions, so at least nine of these supports share a single direction e'. Their quotient locations have cap3 and at least six directions (the planar census shows ≤5 directions allows at most five noncollinear sites, and nine sites cannot be collinear at cap3). Each pair of full five-supports gives at least ten D sites on its radial line, so at least six factors through e'. Two distinct points e and e' can share at most one radial line, whereas 7+6−1 exceeds degree8. Thus e'=e. There are at least nine full supports along e, whose quotient sites must have at most seven directions (every pair supplies a factor). But the planar cap-six census with at most seven directions classifies all 9-site cases as two five-arm crosses, incompatible with quotient cap3. This contradiction proves U_B is four-blocking under F8.

If the degree-eight moment forms of BOTH members are nonzero, each U is therefore four-blocking. Under this hypothesis no affine line has five sites. To see this, take a B' five-site arm of direction e, and let k count distinct factors through e of the other form F. The four-blocking lower bound gives k≤5 (nonfactor radial ≤1+8−k). At k≤3 the radial nonfactors have at most eight directions and plane occupancy at most nine ([exact planar census with the weaker cap six](verify-fuglede-f17-size136-cross.js)). The pencil through the arm includes at least four planes uniform for B' itself, of occupancy five, so its total is at most 4·5+(14−k)·9+17k=146+8k. For k≤2 this is below170. At k=3 equality170 would require all three factor planes full of seventeen B' sites, but each then determines at least fourteen directions by the graph census. Every such factor line therefore contains the unique possible layer normal of B by the degree-seven note. Two of them intersect at e∈D(B')⊂U_B, a uniform normal, so this is impossible. Finally k≥4 gives at most five directions on every radial nonfactor; every plane through the actual five-site arm then has just five sites, and total ≤90+12·5=150. Thus cap4 holds whenever both degree-eight forms are nonzero.

This leaves the desired degree-eight rigidity open; in particular the conditional spatial cap4 is not a proof that an octic residue profile cannot occur.
