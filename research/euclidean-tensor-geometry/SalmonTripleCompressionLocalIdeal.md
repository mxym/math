# The known Salmon equations generate the true local ideal on the generic triple compression

This upgrades the separate tangent computation for
\[
S=e_0^{\otimes3}+e_1^{\otimes3}+e_2^{\otimes3}+(e_0+e_1+e_2)^{\otimes3}.
\]
Let \(J=J_{5,6,9}\) be generated by the full known Strassen quintics, lifted Landsberg–Manivel sextics and lifted determinantal nonics. At \(S\), \(J_S=I_S\) in the ambient local ring, and the same holds on a dense open subset of the locus of border-rank-four tensors with all mode ranks three. This does not address the global homogeneous ideal. The local ring has tangent cone equal to a smooth affine factor of dimension 35 times the affine cone over \(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2\) in its Segre embedding.

## Why the quadratic jets are purely transverse

Let \(X\) be the reduced fourth secant cone (dimension 40). Use the preceding [triple-compression tangent result](SalmonTripleCompressionTangent.md): at \(S\) the true tangent has dimension 44, and we have 20 known equations with independent first derivatives. Let \(Y\) be their common zero scheme localized near \(S\); the Jacobian criterion makes \(Y\) smooth of dimension 44 there and \(X\subset Y\) scheme-theoretically. Let \(Z\) be the reduced locus in \(X\) with all mode dimensions exactly three. It is smooth of dimension 35 near \(S\): the supports are unique and vary regularly on the fixed-rank open, so \(Z\) is locally a bundle over three Grassmannians \(\mathrm{Gr}(3,4)\), with fiber the open rank-three portion of the degree-nine Strassen hypersurface in \(3\times3\times3\). At \(S\) the fiber is smooth by its explicit nonzero determinant derivative. Uniqueness/regularity of supports follows from choosing a nonvanishing flattening minor in each mode and using the resulting graph basis for its image; conversely the bundle with chosen supports maps back by inclusion. The locus \(Z\) is irreducible by this bundle description.

The preceding tangent argument proves \(\dim T_x X\ge44\) on a dense open of \(Z\) (varying the weights of the four symmetric frame powers); by upper semicontinuity this holds throughout \(Z\). Since \(T_xX\subseteq T_xY\) and \(Y\) is smooth of dimension 44 near \(S\), equality holds throughout \(Z\) in that neighborhood. Consequently every known equation, restricted to \(Y\), has vanishing differential along \(Z\), not merely at \(S\). If \(K\) is the ideal of \(Z\) in \(Y\) near \(S\), the injective conormal map \(K/K^2\to \Omega_Y^1|_Z\) for this smooth embedding sends the class of such an equation to its differential. The differential section is zero because it is zero at each closed point of the smooth, reduced \(Z\). Thus \(J\mathcal O_Y\subseteq K^2\). In particular all degree-two initial forms are pure quadratic forms in the nine transverse tangent coordinates: no smooth-direction or mixed terms can appear.

Explicitly, \(T_SX\) is the direct sum of the 26-dimensional compressed tangent and three six-dimensional single-outside symmetric blocks by the apolar-net proof. The tangent of \(Z\) consists of the compressed tangent and three three-dimensional blocks from shearing the supports; in each symmetric block their span is \(\langle E_{00}+\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top,E_{11}+\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top,E_{22}+\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\rangle\). The diagonal projection of this span is invertible (\(t\mapsto t+(\sum t_i)\mathbf1\)). Hence the pure off-diagonal symmetric \(3\times3\) matrices in each single-outside block give nine complementary transverse coordinates, arranged as three rows, one for each missing mode. In each row the column order is \((01,02,12)\); an entry is put in both symmetric positions.

## Nine independent quadratic initial forms from the quintics

Use output matrix modes A,B and slicing mode C. Fix coordinate slices \(X=T(e_0^*)\), \(W=T(e_1^*)\), and write
\[
 C(y)=X\operatorname{adj}(T(y))W-W\operatorname{adj}(T(y))X.
\]
Here \(e_i^*\) denotes the coordinate covector; \(\mathbf 1^*\) is the all-ones covector in the ambient 4-space. Consider the three quintic entries or sums
\[
 C(e_2^*)_{00},\qquad C(\mathbf 1^*)_{00},\qquad
 C(e_2^*)_{10}+C(e_2^*)_{01}.
\]
All have zero differentials in the full 64-dimensional ambient at \(S\); for the last expression the two individual gradients are opposite, supported solely on coordinate \((3,3,2)\). Because their full differentials vanish, the Hessian of their restriction to the smooth \(Y\) is precisely the ambient Hessian restricted to \(T_SY\), independent of any curvature of \(Y\).

Denote the transverse rows of A,B by \(a,b\), and \(D_{ij}=a_i b_j-a_j b_i\). On the nine transverse directions the degree-two terms of these three expressions are, respectively,
\[
  -D_{01}+D_{02}-D_{12},\quad
  -6D_{01}+2D_{02}-2D_{12},\quad
  -4D_{12}.
\]
These identities can be obtained by expansion of the \(3\times3\) cofactors. The companion [verify-salmon-triple-compression-jets.js](verify-salmon-triple-compression-jets.js) checks them with integer product-rule arithmetic on each basis direction and every sum of two basis directions, which uniquely determines a quadratic form. It also checks the zero full gradients. The coefficient determinant is \(-16\). Applying the same identities with cyclically permuted modes gives all nine \(2\times2\) minors of the \(3\times3\) transverse matrix in the initial ideal. By the previous section, these quadratic initial forms are pure in the transverse variables, not just their specialized restrictions to a normal slice.

## Initial ideal finishes the scheme comparison

The tangent graded ring of \(\mathcal O_{Y,S}\) is a polynomial ring in 44 directions. The quotient by the nine minors is the integral rank-one determinantal ring in the nine transverse variables, tensored with the polynomial ring in 35 free variables. It has dimension \(5+35=40\). The tangent graded ring of \((\mathcal O_Y/J)_S\) is a quotient of this domain. Its dimension is 40: Hilbert–Samuel dimension agrees with local Krull dimension, and Friedland–Gross set-theoretic equality says the support is the true fourth secant. Any nonzero ideal in the displayed finitely generated domain would lower dimension, so the quotient is exactly the domain itself. The local ring is therefore a domain (nonzero elements have finite adic order by the Krull intersection theorem and their initial forms multiply nontrivially). Thus \(J_S\) is already radical and equals \(I_S\). Equivalently the homogeneous ideal of the tangent cone after eliminating those 20 linear conditions consists exactly of the nine determinantal quadrics. The bad support of the coherent module \(I/J\) is closed; since it does not contain \(S\), it misses a dense open in the irreducible \(Z\).

No global equality, saturation, or priority claim is made.
