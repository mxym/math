# Degree six is necessary and sufficient locally on the (3,3,4) frame orbit

Let I be the complex homogeneous ideal of the fourth secant of the triple \(\mathbb P^3\) Segre. Let \(J_5\) be generated by all Strassen quintics, and \(J_{5,6}\) add the lifted Landsberg–Manivel sextic modules. At
\[
T=\sum_{j=0}^2 e_j\otimes e_j\otimes e_j + u\otimes u\otimes e_3,\qquad u=e_0+e_1+e_2,
\]
we have \(J_{5,6,T}=I_T\) but \(J_{5,T}\ne I_T\). The statement holds also on the orbit of \(T\), which is dense in the fourth secant locus supported on a \(3\times3\times4\) compression, and on each factor permutation of that orbit. In particular this is an all-singular-slice case, not covered by a \(4\times4\) inverse-slice chart. No degree-nine polynomial is needed locally at these points.

## Four explicit sextics

Number six slots 0 through 5. Assign index permutations of \((0,1,2)\) independently on each of two A columns (0,2,4), (1,3,5) and each of two B columns (0,1,2), (3,4,5); assign an index permutation of (0,1,2,3) on the C column (1,2,4,5), and set the C indices at the two remaining slots 0 and 3 to \(k\). Sum the products of the six tensor entries with coefficients equal to the product of the five permutation signs. Do this for \(k=0,1,2,3\). This defines four integer sextics on \(3\times3\times4\); extend to the full tensor space by ignoring the fourth A and B coordinates.

For an independent check of ideal membership, evaluate on a sum of at most four simple tensors and expand by assigning a summand color to each of the six slots. The five alternating columns give five determinants, so only assignments distinct on every column contribute; the C column uses all four colors. Coloring the slots as \((x,s,y,y,t,x)\) is forced: slot 0 cannot match 1,2,4 and hence matches 5, and slot 3 cannot match 1,4,5 and hence matches 2. Swapping the colors \(s,t\) leaves the product of A determinants invariant (the two change places with a factor −1 each), and likewise the B determinants. It flips the C determinant and preserves the two leftover C factors. Contributions therefore cancel in pairs. The polynomials belong to \(I_6\) on the compressed space. By Bates–Oeding [arXiv:1009.6181](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.6181), Prop. 2.1, that compressed \(I_6\) equals the Landsberg–Manivel sextic module. Their extensions are among its lifts.

## Rank certificate and local argument

Order tensor coordinates as \(16i+4j+k\). The companion script [`verify-salmon-compression-frame.js`](verify-salmon-compression-frame.js) evaluates the ordinary product-rule derivatives of the 16 entries of \(X\operatorname{adj}(Y)Z-Z\operatorname{adj}(Y)X\), with \(X,Z\) selected coordinate slices, \(Y\) one of eight specified slice combinations, and all three cyclic mode arrangements. It retains 20 independent quintic rows mod 101. Then it differentiates the four explicit sextics above by the same product rule, retaining four more rows. The 24 columns in pivot order are
\[
60,61,62,63,45,44,28,13,14,31,30,15,56,57,52,49,50,54,55,51,24,9,6,7.
\]
The resulting integer Jacobian minor is 59 mod 101, and hence is nonzero over \(\mathbb C\). All arithmetic is exact; there is no numerical tolerance and no dependence on a random draw.

All selected equations vanish on the irreducible affine secant cone, of dimension 40 (the upper bound by the parameterization by four simple tensors and equality from the disjoint tangent supports at the diagonal tensor, or from the commuting chart). At the displayed \(T\), their common scheme thus has Krull dimension at least 40, and its Zariski tangent space has dimension at most \(64-24=40\). Its local ring is regular and thus an integral domain; the prime ideal \(I_T\) defines a closed subscheme of the same dimension, so they are equal. This applies also to the full \(J_{5,6}\), which lies between the selected equations and I.

For necessity, \(J_5\) vanishes identically on the fixed compressed space, because in any slicing mode all slice matrices have a common nonzero left or right kernel. For a common right kernel, either the middle matrix has rank at most two, with zero adjugate, or rank three, with adjugate image equal to the shared kernel; both products vanish. The common left kernel case is transposed. Thus the gradients of all quintics are supported in the 28 out-of-compression coordinates. They also annihilate the eight independent tangent vectors obtained by moving each of the four A factors, or each of the four B factors, individually in the \(e_3\) direction while leaving other factors fixed. Independence follows from the four independent C factors and disjoint A-outside and B-outside supports. Quintic Jacobian rank is at most \(28-8=20\), and it is exactly 20 by the certificate. It cannot define the same local ideal as I, whose Jacobian has rank 24 here.

The displayed orbit is dense in the compressed secant locus: in a dense open set of the rank-four parameterization both lists of four projective A and B points are projective frames in \(\mathbb P^2\), and the C factors are independent. Projective changes send the frames to the three coordinate points and their sum; scalar rescalings are absorbed in the C basis. This also shows all points of this frame orbit have the same exact Jacobian ranks.

No global generator or priority claim; all conclusions here concern the indicated local orbit.
