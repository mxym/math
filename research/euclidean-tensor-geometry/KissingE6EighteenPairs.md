# Eliminating 18 antipodal pairs in the complete E6 branch

**Conditional theorem.** A 73-point six-dimensional kissing configuration containing a representative of every E6 root line has at most **17 true antipodal pairs**. This excludes a branch, not arbitrary 73-point configurations.

The [19-pair exclusion](KissingE6NineteenPairs.md) reduces the question to p=18. There are 18 unused signed original roots U and 19 nonroot outsiders. The [mixed-E6 intersection obstruction](KissingE6MixedIntersections.md) requires two vertex-disjoint negative edges among the outsiders. Alternatively absence of a matching would force a second E6 system with exactly 36 common roots, for which no abstract simply-laced subsystem of rank at most six exists. Disjointness of the two blocker masks on any negative edge, together with the [minimum five blockers](KissingE6GlobalMaskCapacity.md), bounds an edge endpoint by thirteen blockers.

Use the [previous exact census](KissingE6NineteenPairs.md), now with the sharpened seven-generator five-mask enclosure *from the outset*, and extend the positive upward ideals through size thirteen for edge endpoints. For completeness:

1. For each pair of endpoint types with total size ≤18, anchor the first and run the exact rational generator-product negative test over all second-chamber images. Saturate by the first stabilizer. Anchor a minimum type among all four endpoints; only second edges with both types at least the first need be considered. Enumerate distinct endpoints with no conflicting root signs, no forbidden small containment, union size at most eighteen, and a possible negative edge for each of the two chosen pairs. The 4,251 first-edge orbits give **43,503,701** anchored patterns.
2. Apply the conservative positive-product incompatibility tests to the four cross-edge pairs. From **5,413,031** passing patterns retain only their signed union, not their endpoint labels. The 538,263 indexed unions have **31,352** symmetry classes. Quotient only by actual E6 automorphisms, using dominant root sums and their stabilizers; complete each representative on unused axes to eighteen signed roots. This gives **562,866** completed universes up to symmetry. Dropping all endpoint labels makes the subsequent bound only weaker.
3. In each U enumerate candidate signed masks through size sixteen by transporting one-root extensions of the canonical upward ideals. Include the fork five-mask as an extension seed only. Empty spherical outer hulls cannot represent an outsider. Each signed mask has capacity one. Counting the resulting candidates discards **14,960** universes with fewer than 19. Next discard **499,954** for which some sign of U is in fewer than two candidate masks: deleting at most one blocking outsider and inserting that sign would produce a 73-point code with at least 19 pairs. Finally use the positive compatibility supergraph, including all relative chambers for each candidate pair. The remaining **47,952** universes all lack a clique of size 19 by the exact color-class branching algorithm. The three counts sum to 562,866: no universe survives.

The computations do not assert realizability of a single surviving local mask or matching. Every tested condition is necessary, and each discarded universe is ruled out by a proved upper bound. The sharp five-mask hull, rational arithmetic and geometric strictness are documented in the [preceding proof](KissingE6NineteenPairs.md) and the [boundary-exchange/census proof](KissingE6TwentyPairs.md).
