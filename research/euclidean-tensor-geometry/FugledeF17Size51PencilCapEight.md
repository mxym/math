Use the [affine plane cap nine](FugledeF17Size51MomentSix.md) and [uniform-pencil cap nine](FugledeF17Size51PencilCapNine.md). Each profile has reduced degree≤7 by the Radon Vandermonde bound, no degree≤4 can occur, and there are no zero lifts. These checks do not impose tightness: they rule out every pencil with nine nonuniform directions, and hence every U pencil has cap eight. Every tight pencil therefore has b≥10. No cardinality51 exclusion is claimed.

## The maximum degrees cannot both be seven

By the [absence of a degree-six or lower member](FugledeF17Size51NoSexticMember.md), suppose both maxima equal seven. Their positive weights are integers between 1 and 6 by the cap-nine profile catalogue in that proof. Each tight pencil now has at least ten positives and weight sum 21. The largest possible local squared sum is at most 83, by the integer partition check. Thus the norm on either side is at most 83·429/21 by weighted polarity double counting, and either support size is at least ceil(10·429/21)=205. At size 240 the centered indicator-vs-weight lower bound from the degree-six exclusion is already too large for the opposite weight norm, so both support sizes N,M lie in 205..239.

Each extra positive neighbor beyond ten costs at least one opposite weight. In both orientations the unweighted incidence E therefore obeys the upper bound
```
E ≤ min(10M+21N−4290,10N+21M−4290).
```
Centered polarity in contrast gives 307E ≥ 18NM−sqrt(17NM(307−N)(307−M)). The exact [support comparison](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-double-septic.js) checks all 630 unordered pairs with minimum positive cleared square gap 500548457. Thus at least one member has genuine degree eight or nine. The cardinality 51 remains open at this stage.
