# A weak length-17 M3 extension through total degree four

**Yongxian Zhang.** AI-assisted mathematical development and internal verification; no external review or priority claim.

This strengthens the [simultaneous quadratic-data barrier](Matrix3BorderApolarityQuadraticBarrier.md). For the same six degree-two ideals, there exists upper-Borel-fixed truncated ideal data through total degree four with the requested length-17 Hilbert function in every multidegree of that truncation, and all the Mańdziuk square inequalities in *both* total degrees four and five pass. This is neither a full multigraded ideal on the prescribed Hilbert scheme nor a point of its component of limits of distinct points.

The [standalone exact checker](verify-mm3-weak-truncation-quintic-square.js) lists additional cubic generators, all monomials, on top of the earlier six quadratic spaces. Rank over \(\mathbb Q\) verifies that each total-degree-three quotient is exactly 17. In every total-degree-four multidegree the lower-generated quotient has dimension at least 17 (actual range 17 to 31). Choose any larger ideal subspace giving quotient dimension exactly 17 in each such terminal multidegree. The lower inclusion constraints and these choices yield compatible multigraded form pieces in every available degree.

There are no ideal linear forms. Thus the square in total degree four depends only on the quadratic spaces and the square in total degree five depends only on the quadratic and cubic spaces. In each multidegree \(\mathbf d\) of total degree five, the checker computes the exact codimension of the sum of the images of products \(I_{\mathbf a} I_{\mathbf b}\) with \(\mathbf a+\mathbf b=\mathbf d\) and total degrees two and three. The least square codimension is 219 over pure quintics, 349 over pairs of nonzero multidegrees, and 516 over three nonzero coordinates. These clear the respective Mańdziuk thresholds 153, 289 and 425 ([general and specialized statements](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12121v1#S7)).

To obtain Borel-fixed truncated data, fix the six Borel-stable quadrics and let the other ideal pieces vary in the product of Grassmannians of their specified dimensions. Compatible multiplication inclusions are closed; requiring the square image ranks to be at most the necessary thresholds is also closed. This is a projective variety preserved by the connected solvable upper-Borel group, nonempty by the explicit calculation. Its Borel-fixed point gives the asserted fixed truncated data (it need not be the explicitly listed cubic monomials themselves). This argument cannot certify extendability beyond the truncation or smoothability.
