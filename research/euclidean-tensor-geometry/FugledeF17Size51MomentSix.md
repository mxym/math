# Size 51 over F_17³: degree-six moments, plane cap nine, and uniform-pencil cap ten

Suppose there is a spectral pair of size51=3·17. As in size68, D(B')⊂U_B, each projective line has at least one uniform normal, no projective line is completely uniform, affine planes initially have cap17, and affine lines have cap three because every line belongs to a uniform plane of its own member. Write W=429 and C=21 for total polarity weight and tight pencil weight. Indeed the plane-pair baseline is \(17\binom{3}{2}=51\); summing over the 18 normals perpendicular to a difference gives \(T=P w-21\), and summing all directions gives \(W=(\binom{51}{2}+307\cdot21)/18=429\). A layer means a profile supported on three levels each containing17 sites.

## No full planes or layers

There is at most one layer per member: two distinct layer normals give a 3×3 projected grid with spatial line cells of size at most three and hence size≤27. A line of at least thirteen U sites has at most five nonuniform profiles, of degree≤3 by the Radon Vandermonde constraint. A nonconstant affine profile has mass136, impossible. The exact centered degree-two and degree-three catalogue in [verify-fuglede-f17-size51-moment-six.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-moment-six.js) finds none of mass51, even allowing zero lifts. Thus on a line of at least thirteen U sites only a constant residue profile can be nonuniform, and then it is the unique layer.

A full 17-site plane with line cap three determines at least fourteen directions by the earlier full planar cap-five lemma in [FugledeF17Size85DegreeEightGeometry.md](FugledeF17Size85DegreeEightGeometry.md). Therefore a full plane of either member forces the opposite layer, and that layer likewise forces a layer in the first. Every such full plane's tangent contains the unique opposite layer normal, as it is the only missing U in its tangent. Thus all full-plane normals of a member lie in the pencil perpendicular to the partner layer, and this pencil has the member's layer as its unique nonuniform direction. Consequently ordinary profiles have no zero lifts.

The dual-graph layer argument of the size68 moment proof applies with three supported levels rather than four. In more detail, using dual coordinates makes the members B=(x,f_s(x),s), A=(u,t,g_t(u)) with full graphs at three levels in each member: the missing opposite layer normal gives the required injectivity along each of the two dual spatial axes. The nonuniform layer weight is C=21, smaller than W=429, so there is a genuine ordinary profile. Highest ordinary degree R≥4 and the nonzero moment form F_d with d=16−R has d≤12 (degree16 is excluded by mass zero in the field). Earlier moments vanish. Isolating each s-level by degree-two Lagrange interpolation gives vanishing local moments through degree d−3, so the degree in w of F_d(u,v,w) is at most two. Dehomogenize v=1, obtaining at least five slopes a for which F_d(a,1,w) is not identically zero. At each such slope, the three-point slices (t,g_t(x+a t)) for all x have ≤2 difference slopes, hence are collinear. Their fixed three-level affine interpolation relation for all x and three or more slopes forces all three graph functions affine: take the top three coefficients of the highest x-degree and compare the resulting polynomials of degrees 0,1,2 in a, giving an invertible Vandermonde system for their leading coefficients whenever the highest degree is ≥2. This would give a full line of seventeen sites, contradiction. Therefore neither body has any full plane or layer. All profiles henceforth use their least nonnegative residues.

## Projective and spatial caps

The exact mass51 profile catalogue in [verify-fuglede-f17-size51-moment-six.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-moment-six.js) finds only four genuine centered quartics (constant11, only nonconstant coefficient a_4 in {6,7,10,11}, peak11, weight4) and 32 genuine centered quintics (16 peak8, weight4, centered constant1; 16 peak13, weight5). Here w=(Σg²−153)/34. Every tight pencil has b≥8: if b≤5 it could only contain layers, and if b=6,7 each positive has degree≤5, weighs at least four, and the total exceeds21. The normalized triangle scan in the same verifier rules out 11 cap-three planar points with at most ten directions (1090 masks, 1599246 nodes). An affine plane of 11 or more sites has a nonuniform normal of its own member and determines at least eleven directions, meaning the opposite tight pencil would have at most seven positives. Therefore the spatial plane cap is ten. This removes all genuine quartics (peak11), so any projective line with b≤6 is now impossible, even if nontight, and U pencils have cap eleven.

The spatial plane cap further tightens to nine. First a tight pencil with b=8 cannot exist. A profile there has degree≤6, and the pencil cannot contain only degrees≤5 by weight minima. Then all eight profiles must be genuine sextics, since any seven distinct sextic powers are independent. In the 17 PGL direction-mask orbits, solve the rank-seven leading relation and examine two common scaling classes modulo sixth powers. A translation removes two penultimate coefficients; the degree-five homogeneous congruence then removes the remaining six. The centered mass51 sextic catalogue has 448 profiles with no lifts, minimum weight2, maximum8. Enumerate all potential positive profiles of total weight21 by successively imposing each homogeneous coefficient from degrees four down to zero. For each degree, choose pivot columns after sorting the constrained profiles by admissible value counts, enumerate all other coefficient choices, solve the pivot values, and filter every column's candidate profiles. Prune only if the sum of their minimal attainable weights exceeds21. The exact [verify-fuglede-f17-size51-tight-sextics.js](verify-fuglede-f17-size51-tight-sextics.js) replay has one path through coefficients 4,3,2,1 and zero constant-compatible paths. Hence every tight pencil has b≥9. The normalized planar scan rejects ten cap-three sites with at most nine directions (855 masks, 322816 nodes); an affine plane with ten sites thus forces at least ten directions in a tight opposite line, impossible. So the plane cap is nine.

In fact U-pencil cap is ten. A projective line with eleven uniform normals has b=7. Its positive profiles are degree≤5 and, with no quadratics, cubics, or quartics, all genuinely quintic. Translation centers all seven: kill two penultimate coefficients then use degree-four independence for the remaining five. Under the plane cap9 each of their centered constants is 1 by the exact profile census. Their sum 7 does not equal 3(b−1)=18 modulo17 in the constant Radon equation, contradiction.

## Conditional three-blocking at degree six and M6

Let F be a degree-six nonzero homogeneous form vanishing on U_B. A hypothetical projective line with ≤2 U sites gives seventeen collinear triple slices of B' (a planar noncollinear triple uses three directions). At least nine such full triple supports then share an axis e. Their quotient sites along e have line cap three because four full supports would give twelve sites in an affine plane. Each pair of these supports gives at least five distinct transverse directions by Cauchy–Davenport, plus e in U_B.

Let k be the number of distinct factor lines through e. If k≥2, every pair of supports forces its radial line to be a factor, since a nonfactor has at most 1+6−k≤5 U sites. But a nine-point quotient cap-three set requires at least eight directions, by the exact scan (238 masks, 9036 states for cap-three, seven slopes, target nine). Thus k>6 contradiction.

If k=0, each considered nonfactor radial has at most five transverse roots; each pair therefore realizes exactly five. By [Vosper's theorem](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-31.2.200) the axial triples are three-term APs with common step up to sign, and equal parallel quotient chords must coincide up to sign: their five transverse roots are the same complete set, and the sum of squares of deviations from its mean determines the squared AP step (the square sum of −2,−1,0,1,2 is 10≠0). The exact normalized marked-triangle equal-parallel-chord test visits 4090 states and excludes any nine-point quotient configuration. Thus k=0 is impossible.

If k=1, divide by the sole radial factor. The residual H has degree five. Since not all nine quotient sites are on one line, some chord is nonexceptional, so H is nonzero on e and may be made monic in the axial coordinate: otherwise it cannot have five finite roots on that radial. Partition the quotient positions into groups along parallel lines of the exceptional slope. Each nonexceptional chord between groups still yields five transverse roots and by Vosper all axial supports are APs of common step σ, using connectivity of the complete multipartite ordinary graph. The X^4 coefficient of H gives a linear root mean; make the shear to set it zero. Then its X^3 coefficient is a binary quadratic Q that takes the same nonzero value −5σ² on every actual quotient difference between groups, by the centered AP5 second elementary symmetric coefficient.

If Q has rank one, three representatives in distinct groups would be three equidistant points, which is impossible by the Gram matrix rank two of their two difference vectors, so at most two groups and ≤6 sites. If Q has rank two, there are at most three groups, as four such representatives give a Gram matrix of rank three in dimension two. Moreover a group of three would make Q constant on the entire line of differences from any outside anchor, forcing the exceptional direction isotropic and orthogonal to a vector outside its span, impossible in a nondegenerate binary form. Thus at most two sites per group, again ≤6. Rank zero is impossible by the nonzero constant. This excludes k=1 and proves that every line meets U_B in at least three sites under F.

With line cap at most eleven for U and a lower bound of three, the projective incidence sum Σ_L(n_L−3)(n_L−6)=N²−145N+5526 is at least270 over integer N. Only rational line factors can contribute positively, so the upper bound is 6·(11−3)(11−6)=240. Thus no F exists, and lower-degree forms cannot exist by multiplication. The usual moment forms vanish through degree six, implying profile degree≤9.

This does not exclude cardinality51.
