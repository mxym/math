# Infinite nonsquare equal-dimension cases of periodic twelve-cycle alternating quantum max-flow

Yongxian Zhang. AI-assisted proof development and internal checks; no external peer review claimed.

Use the single shared tensor model of [Gesmundo–Landsberg–Walter](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.09106#S1.SS4) over the complex numbers. Let F12(q) be the maximum odd-versus-even flattening rank of a twelve-cycle with physical and bond dimensions both q. Then
```
F12(q) = q^6  for q = 5^a 6^b k^2, with a,b nonnegative integers and k positive.
```
Equivalently the squarefree part of q belongs to {1,5,6,30}. The square case is already in GLW Remark I.6. The new ingredients are full nonsquare seeds in dimensions five and six. This does not determine the general twelve-cycle.

## A covariant finite seed

For q=5 or 6 put p=61 and identify both physical labels and virtual basis vectors with the residues modulo q. Write S for the virtual cyclic shift e_i -> e_(i+1). Take A_a = S^a A_0 S^(-a), where the two A_0 matrices (row order, with integer entries, not merely field elements) are

```
q=5:
41 27 49 50 11
26 42 45 33 23
21 34 45 60 14
55  2 11 51 26
11 38 27 23 10

q=6:
44 44 21 55 36 14
33 39 60  6  6 14
28 49 31 46 31 32
48 10 48 22 24 19
15  8 20 25 12 15
 3  6 50 13 53 51
```

The flattening is M(u,v)=tr(A_(u0) A_(v0) ... A_(u5) A_(v5)) for two length-six words. Simultaneous cyclic rotation R through one position preserves each entry by cyclicity of trace, and simultaneous addition T of 1 to all labels preserves each entry by simultaneous conjugation. Hence the linear map M commutes with the permutation representation of G=C6 × Cq generated by R and T. This holds modulo 61 as well. Since 6 and q divide 60, this representation splits into the characters over F_61.

For each orbit choose its least base-q index, least-significant digit first and lexicographic order on group parameters (r,t), first r then t. Let g be the least integer ≥2 of multiplicative order 60 modulo 61. For a character chi_(h,k)(r,t)=g^(10hr+(60/q)kt) and an orbit representative v, the orbit sum has coordinates chi_(h,k)(r,t) on the image R^r T^t v. It is well-defined if the character restricts trivially to the stabilizer; otherwise no vector of that character lives on the orbit. This gives one basis vector per admissible orbit in each eigenspace (the action on this vector has inverse character). Evaluating at the representative gives its coefficient. Consequently the block entry for admissible orbits u,v is
```
sum over distinct w in G.v of chi_(h,k)(r_w,t_w) M(u,w),
where w=R^(r_w) T^(t_w) v.
```
The total dimension of all blocks is q^6. The script uses six-matrix product tables to evaluate every trace and row pivot elimination to check the block determinants exactly modulo 61. In order h=0,...,5, with columns k increasing, the determinant arrays are

```

```

Therefore the integer matrix M has nonzero determinant modulo 61 in both dimensions, so the same witnesses give full rank over the complex numbers. Run the replay with:

```sh
c++ -std=c++17 -O3 -DQ=5 verify.cpp -o verify5
c++ -std=c++17 -O3 -DQ=6 verify.cpp -o verify6
./verify5
./verify6
```

The output includes each block dimension, rank and determinant and ends with `defect 0` for each dimension. Witness parameters are explicit; finite-field arithmetic is used only to certify nonvanishing.

## Infinite propagation

Tensor two slice families: use slices A_a ⊗ B_b indexed by pairs (a,b), with product physical and bond dimensions. The trace of a product factors, so the alternating flattening is a Kronecker product of the two flattenings, up to row and column permutations. Full rank multiplies. Finally a square physical dimension k² has a witness using the k² matrix units of Mat_k (padded to bond k²), whose flattening is a permutation: a row specifies edges (a_i,b_i), and cyclic multiplication forces the unique column edges (b_i,a_(i+1)). This also proves the square case directly. Combining arbitrary numbers of the seeds and this square witness yields the claimed infinite families.
