# Prior work and limits

- [Ma and Zhang, *Maximal input entropy for binary two-deletion and two-insertion channels*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01064v1), especially Lemma 2.1 and Theorem 5.1. Their result already determines the full embedding histogram of the alternating observation for **every** insertion radius. Their Corollary 5.2 is the radius-three specialization; their following paragraph gives the three histogram entries also at radius four. These formulas do not assert that the alternating observation maximizes entropy at higher radii. The radius-two maximizer theorem and radius-three conjecture have different scopes. Our contribution is the first-order comparison to the actual maximum over all binary hosts at fixed higher radius and the resulting structure of every maximizer, not the alternating baseline or its histogram. The [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01064) displayed v1 when checked.
- Singhvi, Sabary, Bar-Lev and Yaakobi, [*Conditional Entropies of k-Deletion/Insertion Channels*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3581849), [open text](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.10026v2), give the posterior per observation and the dual perspective of output uncertainty under exactly $k$ omissions in a fixed host. At radius one their Theorem 7 gives the exact insertion maximizers; their balanced-**constant-run** comparison at a fixed number of runs is not a classification of optimal **alternating-segment** counts for higher fixed radii. Their §VII-A highlights higher-error extensions.
- Atashpendar et al.'s [fixed observed pattern asymptotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11609) take the length of the *longer unknown word* to infinity with observed length fixed, in the deletion direction. This note instead fixes the insertion radius, lets the long received host grow, and optimizes over the posterior on **shorter** inputs. Neither the uniform-prior channel capacity nor independent-per-site deletion probabilities are being optimized.
- Own-repository antecedents: the [exact three-insertion comparison](../three-insertion-entropy/README.md) already uses the tree on the constant-run and alternating-segment partitions. Section 1 reuses that argument and derives a uniform estimate at all fixed higher radii; it is not a new incidence-tree observation. Its insertion-positive result up to radius three is compatible with the present failure for $k\ge4$. The [no-least-posterior note](../deletion-posterior-no-least/README.md) concerns the opposite deletion posterior and uses the one-doubled-bit pattern for a higher-Rényi comparison. That structural pattern is not new. The separate [three-deletion counterexample, #158](../three-deletion-entropy-counterexample/README.md) also uses such an observation, but with a longer unknown input of length 67; it neither implies nor uses the current fixed-insertion-radius asymptotic.

The source papers, ancillary and related author project pages, and targeted searches for maximum output Shannon entropy for exactly $k$ omissions in a fixed host, optimal alternating segments, and a fixed-radius asymptotic coefficient did not expose this all-host comparison. This is a bounded screen, not a proof of priority. No author is claimed to have conjectured optimality at every higher insertion radius.

[Kanoria and Montanari](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.5546) develop asymptotic capacity expansions when deletions occur independently with small probability. That is not the maximized per-word output entropy with exactly a fixed number of omissions used here.
