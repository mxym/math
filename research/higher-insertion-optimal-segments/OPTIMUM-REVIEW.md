1. The run/alternating incidence tree and entropy inequality are the same as the verified radius-three proof, with the hypergeometric expectation now r(r−1)k(n−k)/(n(n−1)). Conditional segment outputs are individually recoverable from the overall output and segment counts. For the segment term, adjacent omitted sites imply an omitted span with at least two sites, hence at least one free pair beyond the parity minimum; its fiber contains as many embeddings as there are retained-length-plus-one gaps. Multiple adjacent pairs can only cost O_k(log n/n²) in the expected lower bound, by two-edge occupancy counting. Replacing (l−1) log max(1,l−k+1) by l log l incurs at most log n+O_k(1) per segment including the short segments. This gives the uniform analytical lower bound. Alternation agrees with its leading term and stated error.
2. In the balanced construction, native double copies are disjoint and at linear distance from each other and the ends. For maximal defect increase each native bond survives intact; every other output equal bond uniquely stands for one skipped symbol. Candidate reconstruction of the host index increases strictly in its output position at fixed number of earlier native bonds. The one-native-loss event forces loss at the same native double, as the entire rank window is alternating, and the collapsed skeleton has maximal defects, so exactly two lifts. In the sole-adjacent-pair event all retained anchors are pinned by their rank windows; the local pair gives n/b+O_{k,b}(1) embeddings retaining all anchors. Additional embeddings project to at most one mask on each of the D collapsed skeletons, since its required defect increase is again maximal. At most 2D lifts are possible, without needing to assume a putative new mask erases only one native double. Both rare frequencies and the error for intersections are justified by the finite number of close/marked occupancy constraints. The checks in optimum_check.py are additional exact sanity checks, not part of the proof.
3. The run term alone first gives D=O_k(log n) among maximizers. With that bound the scaled error in the comparison tends to zero, forcing 2D−(k−1)log(D+1)≤o(1), hence D=O_k(1). Now the error is uniformly O_k(log n/n²) for maximizers. The discrete objective increases until the crossing, then decreases. An integer tie would give (b+1)^(k−1)=4 b^(k−1), so coprimality forces b=1 and k=3; neither is an allowed tie. There is a fixed positive gap to all other integers. Interchanging the balanced construction and uniform upper bound establishes both the leading coefficient and the eventual exact count of alternating segments. The entropy deficit bound and curvature establish the claimed O_k(sqrt(n log n)) deviation; it is not a proof of an exact rounding rule.
