# Prior work and search boundary (18 November 2026)

The source of the minimal-vertex-cut problem is [P. Erdős, Problems and results in combinatorial analysis and graph theory, Discrete Mathematics 72 (1988), 81–92](https://webmail.math-inst.hu/~p_erdos/1988-27.pdf). He credits Nešetřil and records Seymour's three-interior-vertex melon construction. [Erdős Problem 150](https://www.erdosproblems.com/150) marks the original “limit below two” question solved. [Bradač, JGT 108 (2025), 817–818](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23207), [accessible paper](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/a7a8d5cf-2693-4a66-abc4-5e8b1c23cc7f/content), gives a limit argument via fixed terminals, Fekete’s lemma and parallel composition. We do not claim those ideas, nor a first refutation of the older guess about the value 3^(1/3). On a single graph marked minimal separators and inclusion-minimal disconnecting sets need not coincide. Our two-/three-/four-shore reduction treats global cuts separately rather than citing an identification of the two finite counts.

[Fomin, Kratsch, Todinca and Villanger (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1137/050643350) and [Fomin and Villanger, Treewidth computation and extremal combinatorics](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-012-2536-z) used upper bounds for the number of minimal separators in exact algorithms. The latter paper gives the golden-ratio general exponential upper bound, still cited by recent work. Gaspers and Mackenzie gave another proof of the upper estimate and an improved finite-template lower bound; §4.4 of [Gaspers’s Dagstuhl open-problem report](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/04dagstuhl-reports/volume08/issue10/18421/DagRep.8.10.63/DagRep.8.10.63.pdf) explicitly asks to improve either bound. Their [published conference chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-53174-7_9) and [JGT 87 (2018), 653–659](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.22179) report base **1.4457**. Their [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01203) instead prints 1.4521 for a different older construction. Do not describe that larger number as the published record: unrestricted switching paths invalidate the earlier counting argument (see [O. Lapidus’s independent account, §3](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/56b6357e01dbaea0266fe701/t/61141966cc01a74ed5c0ab1b/1744538904776/Olle_Lapidus.pdf)). In the published design a middle-layer set system switches off selected paths. A [public account of the corrected gadget (Golovach, SGT 2018, slides 41–47)](https://www.lirmm.fr/~goncalves/sgt2018/uploads/Enum-15-06.pdf) details nine gates per side, 126 middle paths indexed by 4-subsets, and terminal gluing. The arbitrary-cell lifting is an extension of their mechanism, not the invention of selectors or of parallel repetition. The rooted connected-shore bound binom(a−1+s,s) used when there are at least four components is exactly Lemma 3.1 of [Fomin–Villanger (published author PDF)](https://fedorvf.github.io/articles/2012/2012n.pdf), with their b=a−1, f=s. We give a self-contained frontier proof; the four-component budget 4a+s≤N is the application used in our reduction to clean cuts.

[ESA 2026, Faster Exponential-Time Approximate Counting via Bounded Self-Reductions](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol388-esa2026/html/LIPIcs.ESA.2026.104/LIPIcs.ESA.2026.104.html) still uses the golden-ratio extremal estimate; its smaller time base counts approximately, not an improved bound on the actual number of separators.
