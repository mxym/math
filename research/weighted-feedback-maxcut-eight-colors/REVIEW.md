1. In [BEKSZ Conjecture 1.3 footnote 3](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), after homogenizing to sum W, the bound is 2W/5 + (8/15) E|wt(S)−wt(Sᶜ)|. The signed internal-weight difference is one-half the signed-degree sum, edge by edge, yielding H. This check does not draw any unrestricted coding consequence.
2. Identifying a pair of weight zero does not lose W and permits all former cuts constant on the pair. For conditioned remainder T, averaging the equal-sign outcomes gives max(|T|,d_i+d_j), versus max(|T|,|d_i−d_j|) for opposite signs; replacing by equal signs increases K. The quotient stays loopless precisely because the contracted pair has weight zero. Repeated contractions within independent classes reduce the weighted statement to orders ≤8; deleting only absent support is not substituting an unweighted graph.
3. If there were a least-order counterexample of order at most eight, normalize C=1. The normalization polytope is compact (singleton cuts and nonnegativity bound every coordinate); convex H attains its maximum at a vertex by its vertex decomposition. A zero coordinate gives a smaller counterexample even if its cut value drops below 1. At a positive vertex the active cut vectors span or else a two-sided feasible perturbation exists. A full set of linearly independent cuts in the hyperplane u·x=1 affinely spans a facet; CUTP is full-dimensional via cut differences. Conversely a facet with positive normal has positive rhs and spans linearly after normalizing. This avoids incorrectly asserting exhaustive strict facets of order 8 alone suffice. Small-order uniform 2+2 cut cut-probability=2/3, while majorizing the degree sequence by (W,W,0,...) gives K≤W. Cases W=0 and n≤1 are harmless.
