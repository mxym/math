# Inputs, attribution and limitations

- [BEKSZ original revision](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), Conjecture 1.3 and §11. The first authors proved the equal-weight complete-graph case and the equal-weight ≥10⁹-edge case. The [all-unweighted note (#110)](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/110) is distinct. The weighted graph in this draft may have arbitrarily heterogeneous weights and need not itself have bounded order, but its positive-edge graph must have ≤8 colors.
- Numerical rows of the Christof–Reinelt enumerations come from the corresponding classes (vertex permutations + switchings) files of the original [Heidelberg SMAPO dataset](https://comopt.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/software/SMAPO/cut/cut.html): [order 5](https://comopt.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/software/SMAPO/cut/swcut_5), [order 6](https://comopt.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/software/SMAPO/cut/swcut_6), [order 7](https://comopt.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/software/SMAPO/cut/swcut_7), [order 8](https://comopt.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/software/SMAPO/cut/swcut_8). The distribution includes none of these catalog files. After checking each raw file’s format and its ≤0 right sides, inputs.py canonicalizes the ordered row IDs and coefficient lists as compact ASCII JSON followed by one newline and checks the four published digests in that script. This is not a raw HTTP-body hash; harmless whitespace differences do not matter.
- [Deza and Dutour Sikirić](https://doi.org/10.1111/itor.12194), especially §3 of [open text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.05407), furnish the indispensable confirmation for n=8, where the original repository used tentative language. No completeness claim about order 9 or higher follows. Switching/permutation reduction and convex facet geometry are standard. Evaluation of the BEKSZ objective is a separate calculation.
- Targeted title/author/7/23/mean-cut-imbalance and weighted MaxCut searches of ECCC, the conference paper and more recent public work did not identify this eight-color specialization. In the same repository, #110 resolves the all-unweighted specialization, not the weighted-color claim. No external review or guarantee of priority.
