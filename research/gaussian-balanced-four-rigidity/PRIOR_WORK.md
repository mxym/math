# Finite primary-source comparison

Heilman, *Euclidean Partitions Optimizing Noise Stability* (2014),
arXiv:1211.7138v2, Definition 1.7 and Conjecture 3 (pp.36–37), asks that a
four-cell equal-mass first-Hermite optimizer be **simplicial conical**.
The separate regular-cone definition is stronger. The note's rank theorem
does not prove origin conicity; its central classification proves that
conicity would suffice for the regular-tetrahedral conclusion.

Khot–Naor, *Approximate Kernel Clustering*, Mathematika 55 (2009),
arXiv:0807.4626v2, supplies the unconstrained propeller calculation and
unrestricted conical reduction. The three-cell bound is classical and
reproved here. Empty cells in that reduction do not satisfy four equal
positive prescribed masses.

Heilman–Jagannath–Naor, *Solution of the Propeller Conjecture in R^3*,
DCG 50 (2013), 263–305, Theorem 1.1 and §3, concerns unrestricted cell
masses. Its spherical stationary equations are not the nine priced
equal-mass equations studied here. The full unrestricted bound permits
three propeller cells and an empty fourth. OpenAI/math family 096 has
the same scope distinction and is not needed as an input.

Heilman, *Stable Gaussian Minimal Bubbles*, arXiv:1901.03934v1 (2019),
Lemma 13.1, studies a particular unused-coordinate vector field whose
second derivative cancels. Lemma 4 permits arbitrary label perturbations
of score vectors and derives a Laplacian bound; it does not attribute
the general spectral conclusion to that special variation.

Mulgund, arXiv:2609.28452v2 (2026), Theorem 1.1/§6, compares maxima of
centered unit-variance Gaussian vectors and equal-energy signal detection.
It does not assert that arbitrary prescribed-mass optimal moments have
equal norms or zero prices. It is not used in our proof.

The companion unequal-mass counterexample (commit 4371b0a) does not
refute the present equal-mass target. Fixed-mass duality/cylindrical
structure already appear in our balanced-fans package and are rederived
without claiming them as new. Gaussian isoperimetry is standard imported
work. Empty-kernel and PDF tooling are explicitly adapted from the
companion package.

A user-authorized GPT-6 Luna High scout checked the listed primary
sources. No identical fixed-mass rank-three theorem or this combination
of conditional central classification and nine-parameter local isolation
was located there. This is a finite comparison; no global priority or
claim that all later literature has been searched is warranted.
