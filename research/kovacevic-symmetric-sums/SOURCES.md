# What was known, and what was checked

- M. Kovačević, [*On the maximum entropy of a sum of independent discrete random variables*](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0040585X97T99054X), [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.01138). Theorem 2.1 supplies this note's candidate distribution and value, and establishes the unrestricted $n=2$ case. Theorem 2.2 covers $(n,r)=(3,2)$. Theorem 2.3(a) proves the sharp bound whenever all but at most two marginals are supported on the endpoints (arbitrary biases allowed); (b) gives a related ternary class. “Endpoint-supported” differs from “symmetric about $r/2$”: a symmetric input can use many interior symbols, and an endpoint law may be biased. Neither arbitrary-alphabet class contains the other in general. The binary unrestricted maximum is the classical Shepp–Olkin–Mateev result.
- M. Kovačević, [*Maximum Entropy of Sums of Independent Ternary Random Variables*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spl.2026.110867), [PDF, arXiv:2605.11831v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.11831). **Theorem 7 in §4** proves the unrestricted ternary ($r=2$) maximum for all $n$ and exhibits an attaining law. Section 5 gives examples obstructing its conditional ultra-log-concavity proof for larger alphabets, not counterexamples to the entropy conjecture.
- E. Ordentlich, [*Maximizing the entropy of a sum of independent bounded random variables*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2006.872858), and Y. Yu, [*Maximum entropy for sums of symmetric and bounded random variables: A short derivation*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2008.917660). These establish the **differential**-entropy maximum for bounded symmetric inputs and supply the signed-sum concentration lemma used here. The continuous and finite-alphabet problems have different objective functions; the finite-alphabet proof must also handle atoms exactly at the integer interfaces.
- D. Kleitman, [*On a combinatorial conjecture of Erdős*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9800%2866%2980027-3). For the explicit odd-diameter bound see also H. Huang, O. Klurman and C. Pohoata, [*On subsets of the hypercube with prescribed Hamming distances*, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.05989). The present proof rederives the needed signed tail comparison from that theorem; it does not claim the tail comparison is new.

## Search scope and limitations

The search covered the original conjecture, citing and author/title/identifier searches, bounded symmetric discrete sums, quaternary or larger alphabets, the Shepp–Olkin–Mateev problem, and discrete analogues of Ordentlich and Yu. No prior statement of this precise at-most-one-asymmetric finite-alphabet result, or unrestricted resolution for all $n,r$, was located. This is not a priority guarantee.

The repository scan was pinned to [main 8ab22d9](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/8ab22d9ed3ab8b8e16b8d9f14c84b22b6a248570): 22,013 tracked paths including notes, preprints, 12,604 formalization paths and 148 release paths. Across that tree and 48 tags, 14,567 distinct main blobs and 277 additional tag blobs were inventoried; supported text, archived documents and text-extractable PDFs were searched by relevant terms including author names, paper identifiers and English and Chinese phrases. The independent pass processed 35,460 textual streams, 162 PDFs and 160 archives. All 175 assets of the 47 published releases were accounted for by SHA-256 matching to already searched repository data or by separately checking and searching the 73 unmatched assets. Two nonpublic draft assets were not inspected. No corresponding manuscript was located.

Immediately before preparing the draft, the live main ref advanced to [74b7275](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/74b727591b452f1f8a5e45b73704fb44e350832c) (22,023 tracked paths). All ten changes were additions under the planar $\ell_p$-sections project; a separate content check found no match to the entropy-of-sums question. No existing paths changed.

Such searches do not detect all paraphrases, unindexed work, image-only pages, non-text binary material or deleted/unmerged branches. The review and search were AI-assisted and are not independent external peer review. The cited results, including the original construction, remain credited to their authors.
