# Earlier work and the additional step

- [Nederlof, Pawlewicz, Swennenhuis and Węgrzycki](https://doi.org/10.1137/22M1478112) connected large subset-sum fibers with a small set of attainable sums in their work on bin packing.
- [Jain, Sah and Sawhney, *Anticoncentration versus the Number of Subset Sums* (2021), p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07726v2#page=2) conjectured $\delta(\varepsilon)\le2\varepsilon$ in the implication $\rho\ge2^{-\varepsilon n}\Rightarrow K\le2^{\delta(\varepsilon)n}$. This is equivalent to $K\rho^2\le1$. Their conjecture is stated just before their Eq. (1.2), not by Eq. (1.2) itself, which gives the weaker proved bound $O(\sqrt\varepsilon)$. Their well-separated repeated-weight blocks already explain why the conjectured exponent is sharp.
- [Jiange Li, *Multiplicative comparisons of Rényi entropies for weighted Bernoulli sums*, arXiv:2609.01529v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01529v2), Conjecture 1.1, formulates the endpoint problem as $H_0\le2H_\infty$ and credits **Mokshay Madiman** for communicating it. Li's Theorems 1.4 and 1.5 concern positive Rényi orders and a logarithmic-factor endpoint bound. They do not give the advertised exact endpoint inequality for arbitrary weights.
- [The earlier repository note (#95)](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/1a73fcf02f417ccbf6fc05a8a601426e0eb3c449/research/two-weight-subset-sum-anticoncentration) proved the exact inequality for at most two distinct nonzero absolute weights, including all commensurable ratios. The sign reduction, one-type estimates, exact predecessor count in a rectangle, separate residue bounds, and doubled-length cyclic-binomial calculation are reused here. The proof identifies their use explicitly. Products of independently encoded blocks are elementary; that principle is not the new ingredient.

**What this note adds to #95.** The two-coordinate bound is strengthened from the constant 1 to $8/9$ and applies to cosets of arbitrary integer steps, even when the coordinates have a common divisor. A full one-dimensional relation lattice projects to just such a subgroup on the two coordinates selected from the count box. The improved constant offsets a possible factor $9/8$ from the remaining coordinates; this yields the unrestricted-in-multiplicity conclusion for any number of types having at most one independent rational relation. Highly commensurable inputs need not satisfy this hypothesis.
