# Source and priority notes

1. A. A. Gorodilova, *A note on the properties of associated Boolean functions of quadratic APN functions*, Prikladnaya Diskretnaya Matematika 47 (2020), pp. 16–21, [DOI 10.17223/20710410/47/2](https://doi.org/10.17223/20710410/47/2), [journal record and full text](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/pdm691), [arXiv:2005.10788v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.10788). The exact target is **Conjecture 1**, section 1.2, printed p. 18 / [preprint PDF p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.10788#page=3): a quadratic APN map in odd dimension has \(\deg \varphi_F<n\), equivalently even Hamming weight. The source defines the affine derivative offset before §1 including the convention \(\varphi_F(0)=1\); its “quadratic” means exactly degree 2, so there is no \(n=1\) case. Its Theorem 1 includes the odd-dimensional bijectivity of \(\Phi_F=\pi\). Its Proposition 1 proves the even-dimensional counterpart. Its §2 Step 2 describes the odd-dimensional plane partition used here. The proof in this package rederives the facts it needs, then identifies a trace rather than relying on a computer search.
2. C. Carlet and J. Thornburgh, *Resolving a conjecture on quadratic APN functions and a new quadratic \((n,n)\)-function associated to crooked functions*, [arXiv:2608.23888v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23888v2), establish oddness of the exclude multiplicities more generally (Proposition 2.1) and use the second coordinates of the same plane sums to study quadratic parity functions (Theorem 4.8, Eq. (9)). Their prominent proof of “Gorodilova's conjecture” means **Conjecture 2** in source 1, the degree of the **ortho-derivative components**, distinct from the degree of the affine **offset** \(\varphi_F\) in Conjecture 1. No offset trace or conclusion of Conjecture 1 was located in their Theorem 4.8, Corollary 4.9, or nearby results. Their parity and plane identities are precursors to the proof here, not claimed as new.
