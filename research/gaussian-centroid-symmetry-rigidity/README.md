# Gaussian centroid symmetry–rigidity

**Research status:** a complete analytic conditional-variance theorem,
with standalone Lean 4 kernel proofs of its finite binary-code
isometry ingredient. Not a claim of historical mathematical priority,
full formalization, or independent human peer review.

## Main new mathematical bound

For an arbitrary measurable Gaussian partition A=(A_i) into
k cells of mass 1/k, put

- b_i = integral_(A_i) x dgamma_d(x);
- a_i = k ||b_i||, mean a_bar = (sum_i a_i)/k;
- V(A) = (sum_i (a_i-a_bar)^2)/k;
- U_k = h_k^2/k, where h_k=k phi(Phi^{-1}(1-1/k));
- L=log k and P(A)=sum_i ||b_i||^2.

The exact algebraic identity

    k*(U_k-P(A))+V(A) = h_k^2 - a_bar^2

makes the role of unequal centroid norms explicit.

For L>=100 and d>=L^2/log L, the
spherical-cap proof gives the global inequality

    k*(U_k-P(A))+V(A) >= 2L^2/d - 28.

When d/L^2 -> c in (0,infinity), the constant-sharp
asymptotic statement is

    liminf [k*(U_k-P(A))+V(A)] >= 2/c.

This applies to ALL measurable equiprobable Gaussian
partitions, not just smooth, conical or Voronoi ones.

## Symmetry breaking is quantitatively necessary

Any partition with all conditional centroid lengths equal,
including any Gaussian-isometry-equivariant partition whose
symmetry acts transitively on its cells, has V(A)=0.
For those partitions the dimension loss is at least 2/c
in k-normalized squared-centroid energy, **twice the
coefficient of the previous general-partition converse**.

Suppose A_k in dimension d_k~c(log k)^2 has score at least
F_infty(k)-C/k, where F_infty is the dimension-unrestricted
global optimum. The regular simplex / Gumbel comparator yields

    liminf V(A_k) >=
        max(0,2/c-C-2(1-gamma)).

If the right side v_star is positive, for every
0<v0<v_star the proof forces at least

    (v0/4+o(1))*k/log k

cells to have a conditional centroid length that differs
from the mean by at least sqrt(v0/2).
This rules out a completely homogeneous or transitive
near-optimal geometry in the specified dimension regime.

These are necessary structural conditions, NOT an
identification of the exact Gaussian optimizer.

## What is NOT proved

The full Gaussian spherical-cap theorem, Berry–Esseen
statements, Gaussian integration and existence of an
optimal partition are NOT Lean formalized in this note.
The analytic inequality depends on the previously
published and auditable spherical-cap and Gaussian
extreme-value lemmas. We do not claim exact global
Standard Simplex optimality, sharpness of the dimension
coefficient, or full historical mathematical novelty.
