Finite weighted caterpillar terminals realize all densities simultaneously by replacing with rounded proportions of actual caterpillars, using multivariate hypergeometric convergence. Conversely uniform leaf weights yield the genuine finite induced tree conditional on no sampled terminal repeating; the probability of repetition is O(j²/n). This establishes the pass to arbitrary limiting profiles without assuming an abstract representation theorem.

I independently recomputed the root-binomial recurrences. Every mixed split other than 1+(j−1) fails the caterpillar test, while 3+3 exactly induces E6. The tolls for D4,D5,D6 are respectively 6r²,10r²,15r²−10r³ (r=pq). For k=4, applying the child inequality is sufficient by subadditivity of power 2/3. For k=5 the same child estimates give multipliers p+3q and q+3p, both at least 1. For k=6 the B expression combines the binomial mixed terms into r² and the one-in-side terms yield exactly 4pq on each isolated u² and v². In the balanced branch both multipliers are at least 1 using p²+4pq−1=q(3p−1). In the remaining range the small branch uses u/r≤p/q, q≥2/3 and 4pq≥2p/(3q) plus the tangent-line bound at r³. The larger branch has multiplier at least 1. Sharpness on two-terminal skeletons and monotonicity in S prove the entire indicated arcs, not a claim about other horizontal coordinates.

For k≥7, the balanced split within the small root child changes C_k only if k or k−1 samples fall in it, and adds (5/16)a⁶ to E6. The Cat derivative at small p is −k(k−1)p+O(p²); shifting down by p^{k−2} brackets the exact target by an amount asymptotically k(k−1)p^{k−1} versus a smaller cost at most (k+o(1))p^{k−1}. Continuity gives exact limiting horizontal equality. The root E6 change is O(p^k), strictly smaller order than the new positive p⁶ term. This is valid separately for every fixed k and all sufficiently small positive p, not a uniform threshold in k or all p. The published 2025/26 Brosch–Puges Conjecture 1 explicitly asserts all k≥4, already supplies the double-caterpillar formula and certifies specific points (including midpoint k=4,5,6); the complete arcs and higher-k counterexamples are the relevant scope.
