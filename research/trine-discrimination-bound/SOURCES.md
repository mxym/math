# Sources and prior-art comparison (18 November 2026)

**Target.** Mihály Weiner, [*Classical, quantum, and general probabilistic state-discrimination profiles*, arXiv:2609.17920v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17920v1). [§6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17920v1#S6.SS1) and [§6.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17920v1#S6.SS5) give the definitions and Conjecture 6.3; Appendix A gives the pure-triple case, removal of a shared PSD component, and mixed-state equality examples. In this notation the exact qubit value and explicit trine lower bound are earlier results. The paper’s dimension-independent upper bound is (3√15−9)/4, improving 2/3, still larger than (3√3)/2−2. It does not prove the all-mixed upper bound. No unequal-prior or general profile-region characterization is asserted here. We follow the paper’s *unnormalized* success SDP, not the conventional success probability divided by three. The arXiv history displayed only v1.

**Method background.** The un-tilted three-setting chained Bell maximum is known. In particular see I. Šupić, R. Augusiak, A. Salavrakos and A. Acín, [*Self-testing protocols based on the chained Bell inequalities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.09220), which gives chained Bell SOS decompositions and self-testing. The present operator bound includes a nonzero signed term proportional to the sum of the three Alice marginals: a bound by 3√3 on the chain alone does not give the claim for success exceeding 2. We do not claim to introduce chained Bell inequalities, purification, POVM duality, or the general SOS/NPA method. M. Navascués, S. Pironio and A. Acín, [*A convergent hierarchy of semidefinite programs characterizing the set of quantum correlations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4290), supplies background for the numerical discovery phase, not a necessary assumption in the exact proof.
