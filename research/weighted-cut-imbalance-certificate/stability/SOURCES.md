# Where this result fits

**Original problem and extremizers.** Mark Braverman, Klim Efremenko, Gillat Kol, Raghuvansh R. Saxena and Zhijun Zhang, [*Round-vs-Resilience Tradeoffs for Binary Feedback Channels*, ITCS 2025, Conjecture 3 and footnote 2](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2025.22), identify the uniformly weighted triangle and five-clique as equality examples. In their [ECCC full version, revision 1](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), see Conjecture 1.3 and §11, including the equal-weight clique and large equal-edge cases. For random signs, twice the internal-weight imbalance equals the signed degree sum; this gives exactly the normalization used here, with the new quantitative conclusion concerning the graph's *edge weights*.

**Prior public repository results.**
* [#110](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/110) treated the equal-edge case, including equality for unweighted simple graphs.
* [#115](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/115) proved the weighted inequality for **eight-colorable positive support**. That bounded-color restriction is not made here.
* [#123](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/123) proved the weighted inequality on **all finite graphs** with a finite rational certificate. We reuse that [certificate](../certificate.json) and its prefix majorization lemma rather than present the underlying inequality or its coding implication as a new result.
* [#127](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/127) classified **exact equality** at all graph orders and all real nonnegative weights; it established that the only extremizers are the uniformly weighted triangle and five-clique, up to isolates. This gives no stated quantitative uniform error constant. We reuse its tight-corner list and majority-function calculation.
* The present follow-up proves the deficit-to-distance bound with an absolute constant independent of graph order, using strict corner slacks, one majority type per certificate leaf, and reconstruction of the physical edge weights. A fixed-order result with an order-dependent constant already follows from a finite polyhedral subdivision of the normalized compact weight simplex. On a cell with zeros, interpolation bounds the mass on nonzero vertices by the deficit divided by the least positive vertex deficit; a zero-free cell has positive minimum. Convexity and affinity on the cell put the convex hull of its zero vertices in the zero set. This reasoning is an elementary fixed-dimensional error bound, in the spirit of [A. J. Hoffman, *On Approximate Solutions of Systems of Linear Inequalities*](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/049/4/V49.N04.A05.pdf). It supplies no common constant as the number of vertices grows.

**Search scope.** Checked the ITCS publication and original ECCC record, primary-source searches for BEKSZ, one-round feedback, the $7/23$ threshold, weighted cut imbalance, equality and stability about the two clique examples. Related max-cut or degree-imbalance stability statements for different functionals or random models do not yield this estimate. We did not find an externally published statement of the present order-independent bound. For the repository see the [whole-repository and release history](../SOURCES.md) and [equality comparison](../equality/SOURCES.md), which include earlier preprints, formalizations, tags, archives, and supplemental release assets. The full delta from #123 through [main at #130](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/5ac928b56f6e87907a1a9fe95afef3c1bd30bc37) adds the equality package, random-chores and Cayley-tree packages, not an intervening quantitative weighted-cut stability result. The next changes were checked file by file: [#131](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/131) adds 13 Cayley-tree entropy files; [#132](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/132), merged at [2fb97442](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/2fb97442e4bc091e4507dca10b96b8f8da095ec9), adds 11 files on the distinct BEKSZ degree-square inequality (ITCS Conjecture 5 / ECCC Conjecture 1.5), proving it for product weights or when all but at most three vertices have the same weighted degree. It neither supplies nor is implied by this stability estimate for Conjecture 3. A bounded search cannot establish historical priority.

The public proof fixes the math typesetting, spells out the model and constants, and incorporates that audited calculation. The audit's note on malformed draft delimiters concerns the reviewed draft, not the public typesetting. Both verification scripts retain their audited bytes.
