**Prior and scope.** [Braverman–Efremenko–Kol–Saxena–Zhang, ITCS 2025, Conjecture 3 and footnote 2](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2025.22), and their [ECCC revised text](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), identify uniform $K_3,K_5$ as tight examples. Their weighted conjecture is exactly the cut–Rademacher inequality under unit total weight, not the claimed stability result. Repository [#123](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/123) gave the global inequality and [#127](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/127) the full equality classification; neither stated the quantitative conclusion. For any *fixed* order an $O_n(\delta)$ distance bound is an elementary consequence of finite piecewise linearity on the compact weight simplex plus equality classification (subdivide into affine cells, interpolate strict vs tight vertices). This is also in the spirit of [Hoffman's classical polyhedral error bound](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/049/4/V49.N04.A05.pdf). Such a fixed-order result does not control the constants as the graph order grows. The distinguishing assertion must be the uniformity over **all** finite graph orders, not merely linear stability at fixed order. The ITCS/ECCC follow-up and related cut/degree-stability search did not reveal the stated uniform result.
