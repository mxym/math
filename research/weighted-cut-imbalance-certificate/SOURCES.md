# Primary sources, earlier cases, and search limits

1. **Original graph conjecture and coding equivalence.** M. Braverman, K. Efremenko, G. Kol, R. R. Saxena and Z. Zhang, [Round-vs-Resilience Tradeoffs for Binary Feedback Channels, ITCS 2025](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2025.22): Conjecture 3, its footnote 2, Theorems 2 and 4. In the [ECCC full version, revision 1](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), see Conjecture 1.3, Theorems 1.2/1.4, and Theorem 9.1 for the precise statement with a fixed gap below the endpoint. In particular, it is their already-proved reduction, rather than a new protocol argument, that turns the graph theorem into the channel result. Their §11 establishes the equal-edge clique case, recalls the tight examples on 3 and 5 vertices, and proves the equal-edge case with at least $10^9$ edges.
2. **Same-repository precursors.** [#110](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/110) proves the equal-edge case for all sizes and studies equality there. [#115](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/115) proves the eight-colorable case for unequal weights using positive cut-polytope facets. The current proof has neither restriction and needs neither previous note nor a facet table. In particular it makes no completeness assumption about any facet catalog at orders 9 or higher.
3. **What the statement is not.** [Gupta, Guruswami and Zhang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05673) use a bounded number of feedback rounds depending on the fixed gap, not exactly one feedback round. [Gupta and Zhang, ITCS 2025](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2025.60), study list decoding with noiseless feedback after individual bits. Neither is the one-round weighted Max-Cut assertion. [Gutin and Yeo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05536) investigate other lower bounds on weighted Max-Cut. The [2026 paper on the imbalance conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09191) concerns differences of endpoint degrees across individual edges, not the difference between the internal weights on opposite sides of a random cut.
4. **Targeted external search.** Public title/citation, one-round binary corruption, $7/23$, cut-versus-imbalance, Rademacher weighted degrees, follow-up ECCC and conference/preprint searches on 2026-11-19 did not identify an existing proof of unrestricted Conjecture 1.3. That does not establish priority or exclude unindexed, recent, unpublished or differently worded results. Majorization, random cuts, linear inequality aggregation, convex interpolation and exact certificate checking are classical mathematical tools; we claim no novelty for them as standalone methods.
