# Sources and overlap limits

1. **Original examples.** M. Braverman, K. Efremenko, G. Kol, R. R. Saxena and Z. Zhang, [Round-vs-Resilience Tradeoffs for Binary Feedback Channels, ITCS 2025](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2025.22), Conjecture 3 and the paragraph immediately following its displayed inequality on p. 22:4. In the [ECCC full version, revision 1](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/179/revision/1/download/), see Conjecture 1.3 and §11.1 (equal-weight cliques), as well as §11.2 for the large unweighted case. The authors already exhibited the equal-weight triangle and five-clique. Our statement about all nonnegative real weights is a *necessity* assertion, not the discovery of either example.
2. **Earlier results in this repository.** [#110, unweighted](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/110) classified the equality examples for all equal-edge simple graphs. [#115, eight-colorable case](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/115) established a restricted weighted lower bound. [#123, full weighted bound](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/123) proved the general inequality and explicitly did not classify all extremizers. This addendum depends on #123, not on a new coding reduction or a complete catalog of cut-polytope facets.
3. **External search.** Title and author searches, including weighted cut–imbalance equality/uniqueness, the three- and five-clique extremizers, and the one-round adversarial binary corruption model and its 7/23 threshold, were checked against conference, ECCC and preprint records through 2026-11-19. We did not locate a pre-existing unrestricted weighted equality classification. Nothing here excludes an unindexed, unpublished, recent or differently stated result.
