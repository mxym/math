# Independent proof review: exponential dimension/alphabet tradeoff

**Verdict:** PASS on the stated asymptotic inequalities. This is an independent AI review, not external peer review or a proof of historical priority. Inspected proof SHA-256: `6cce43355c7ecc410152d200cd921880d97c8171675e0ff32cca8d7669ad6532`.

1. **Why the image cone is closed.** A linear image of a PSD cone need not be closed, so one cannot assume SDP strong duality. Here each sequence of representing PSD parent tuples is bounded: for one included setting the sum of its convergent marginal effects equals the total sum of the parent tuple. Every parent component lies between zero and that common total in Löwner order. Finite-dimensional subsequence compactness gives simultaneous limits, preserving all equalities. If the target marginals sum to the identity, every representing cone point is already a POVM. Unrestricted finite parents with stochastic classical postprocessings are equivalent to product-index parents: refine each parent outcome by the product distribution of its individual response kernels. Thus no restriction on the number of parent outcomes has been imposed.

2. **Independent certificate derivation.** For the linear map taking a parent to its marginals on S, the adjoint takes H to the tuple $(\sum_{i\in S}H_{i,x_i})_x$. The product PSD cone is self-dual in the *real* trace pairing on complex Hermitians. Separation of a point outside a closed cone now yields an H in the dual with *strictly negative* pairing on the measurement array. Zero padding H outside S and repeating restricted words over all full words is equivalent in both directions. Similarly zero padding an individual measurement's outcome labels cannot hide incompatibility: every parent effect below a zero marginal is zero. No positive-definiteness of effects or tester is required.

3. **Sign-fiber count.** One Hermitian matrix has $d^2$ real coordinates. Characteristic coefficients of $\det(tI+H)$ are real polynomials of degree at most d in those coordinates. Nonnegative coefficients imply no root at $t>0$, hence no negative eigenvalue; Hermiticity and the plus sign are essential. The non-leading coefficients suffice even if the determinant vanishes. The witness can depend on S: one first fixes the measurements, then asks if their *set* of all complete simultaneous sign strings contains one qualifying string for each separate incompatibility question. This is not a test using one witness for the entire complex or multiplying a separate quantified-block cost for every S. The polynomial count is $m=1+nqd^2+dq^n$, total degree at most $D=\max(2,d)$; both parameter counts are $p=nqd^2$. I checked the typeset [Hrubeš, CCC 2024, Appendix A, pp. 13:11–12, (4)](https://users.math.cas.cz/~hrubes/PDFs/HardSub.pdf): it bounds exactly those full ternary sign sets by $m^{(p+1)^2}D^{O(p)O(p)}$, ascribed there to [Basu–Pollack–Roy, 2nd ed., Theorem 14.16](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-33099-2). The implied constants are universal. No explicit numerical constant can be read off the displayed big-O.

4. **Entropy budget and quantifiers.** Write $b=\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\gtrsim2^n/\sqrt n$; all $2^b$ middle-layer choices preserve all singletons for $n\ge4$. For $B\le2^n$, every $qd^2\le B$ has $p\le nB$, $\log m=O(n^2)$ and $\log D=O(n)$. The number of integer pairs is at most $B^2$. A union *before* selecting the complex bounds the log count by $2\log B+O(n^4B^2)$. With $B=c2^{n/2}n^{-9/4}$, a sufficiently small absolute c makes this at most b/2 for all sufficiently large n. Thus the very same complex excludes all pairs below the curve; failure probability is at most $2^{-b/2}$ for the specified uniform middle-layer ensemble, not for an arbitrary random-complex model. At fixed q and $d\le2^n$, $\log m=O_q(n)$, so universality forces $b\le C_qn^3d^4$, i.e. $d\ge c_q2^{n/4}n^{-7/8}$. Larger d satisfies it automatically. This is a worst-case lower bound, not an optimal exponent or an explicit hard complex. Label symmetry neither creates nor destroys a realization.

5. **Prior and upper bounds.** [Kunjwal–Heunen–Fritz, 2014, §§III,V](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.5948) already prove binary universality at unbounded dimension by direct sums over minimal nonfaces; their optimization question for a given complex is not completely solved here. [Andrejić–Kunjwal, 2020, immediately after Corollary 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.00785) explicitly observe that the same direct sum needs only one qubit per minimal nonface. Minimal two-element nonfaces are ordinary incompatible pairs. Distinctness is immaterial to lower bounds: allowing coincidences only enlarges the count. For the prior binary upper bound, if literal distinctness is desired (cone vertices might otherwise coincide), add a single classical 1D summand whose binary outcome bias distinguishes all vertices. Compression preserves nonfaces; block-diagonal extension preserves faces. The unrestricted qubit construction in [repository #118](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/118) handles the opposite resource endpoint; [#119](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/119) is an explicit 144-vertex obstruction using pairwise compatibility of binary qubit measurements, not this asymptotic higher-order result. [Heunen–Fritz–Reyes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3698) concerns *sharp* observables and ordinary graphs. [Carmeli–Heinosaari–Toigo](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02985) is prior on incompatibility witnesses. [Skrzypczyk–Hoban–Sainz–Linden](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10085) count the outcomes of an implementing *parent* for a given jointly measurable family; the present q bounds the individual *child* settings. I did not identify an external prior establishing these explicit profile-counting or exponential worst-case resource results for arbitrary finite-outcome POVMs. This observation is not a guarantee of priority. The previous same-project full-tree/48-tag/47-release (175 assets) scan found no earlier such result; current checked `main` `45e49af8f0271d64eac7a3a7b6c4a028d34d71aa` adds #116–120, of which #118/119 are the nearby results above.
