# Source anchors and literature scope

- Quan Geng, Wei Ding, Ruiqi Guo and Sanjiv Kumar, [*Optimal Noise-Adding Mechanism in Additive Differential Privacy*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/geng19a.html), AISTATS 2019, PMLR 89:11–20, [camera-ready PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/geng19a/geng19a.pdf), [arXiv:1809.10224v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.10224v2). The §1.1 footnote 1 (printed p. 12) singles out the decreasing-distribution condition as a candidate for removal. The privacy constraint is equation (7), printed p. 14 (PDF page 4); Property 2 is on that same page; Theorem 2 on pp. 15–16 establishes the restricted atom plus uniform form; Theorem 3 on pp. 17–18 gives the \(p>0\) optimizer parameter; Corollaries 4–5 give the absolute and squared cost. This note takes that theorem with its actual hypothesis and determines exactly when its \(|x|^p\) optimum survives without the hypothesis. It neither contradicts the theorem as scoped nor attributes a stated conjecture about the exact unrestricted optimum.
- Aras Selvi, Huikang Liu and Wolfram Wiesemann, [*Differential Privacy via Distributionally Robust Optimization*](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.2023.0218), *Operations Research* 74(1):356–376 (2026 issue, online 2025), [accepted manuscript](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/accepted_version_manuscript.pdf), §5.1, Figure 4 and accompanying text. They already report numerical certificates that nonmonotone additive noise can improve on monotone noise for approximate DP, with \(\varepsilon>0\) in the displayed cases; the pinned [GitHub supplement](https://github.com/selvi-aras/DP-via-DRO/blob/ef353ca5ba42ec8e8467e623cba68f6b54fe7e3d/GitHub%20Appendices/GitHub_supplement.pdf), heading “Monotone Distributions,” gives the specific \(\ell^1\) case \((\varepsilon,\delta)=(3,0.3)\). We do not claim first observation of nonmonotone improvements. Our result here is an analytic parameter-wide criterion for \(\varepsilon=0\) and power losses, with exact exceptions.
- Jordan Awan and Aishwarya Ramasethu, [*Optimizing Noise for f-Differential Privacy via Anti-Concentration and Stochastic Dominance*](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-1624.html), JMLR 25(351):1–32 (2024), [PDF](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume25/23-1624/23-1624.pdf), Lemma 3, Theorem 5, Corollary 11, Theorems 16 and 24, and Example 4. Equation (10) here is a direct integrated consequence of their anti-concentration principle plus the TV triangle inequality, as shown in the proof. Their concluding discussion in §6 explicitly notes continuous mean absolute and squared error as future objectives in the tradeoff-function framework. Their log-concave CND optimality for continuous noise requires domination of a specified infinitely divisible tradeoff curve at **all** scales \(t\ge0\), stronger than (1). The other optimum is for integer-valued noise. Neither contradicts this theorem for a single shift budget and all real shifts up to the given sensitivity.
- Quan Geng and Pramod Viswanath, [*Optimal Noise Adding Mechanisms for Approximate Differential Privacy*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2015.2504972), IEEE TIT 62(2):952–969 (2016), is the related integer-valued theory, not this all-real-shifts problem.
- James Melbourne, Mario Diaz and Shahab Asoodeh, [*Optimality of Staircase Mechanisms for Vector Queries under Differential Privacy*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14597v1), proves a monotone reduction and staircase optimality for pure \(\varepsilon\)-DP, not \((0,\delta)\)-DP. Yu Wei, Alexander Bienstock and Antigoni Polychroniadou, [*Asymptotic Optimality of the High-Dimensional Gaussian Mechanism and Improved Low-Dimensional Mechanisms for Differential Privacy*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08681v1), addresses spherical mechanism design, asymptotics and composition for approximate DP; not the scalar power-loss dichotomy.

It did not identify a matching analytic dichotomy. No exhaustive priority claim is made.
