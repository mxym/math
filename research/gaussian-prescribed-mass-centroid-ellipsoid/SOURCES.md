# Sources and provenance

Author metadata uses the personally confirmed profile in repository
`AUTHOR.md`, confirmed on 8 October 2026. The mathematical manuscript's
SHA-256 is
`bef41c3cdf68e11ee5b4d8eb31742952170e1dcca1d510f336f31b497e156126`.
TeX, PDF and extracted text are generated by `build.py`; its fixed
metadata date is not a disclosure timestamp.

The non-elementary source is Emanuel Milman and Joe Neeman,
*The Gaussian Double-Bubble and Multi-Bubble Conjectures*,
Annals of Mathematics 195 (2022), 89–206,
[DOI 10.4007/annals.2022.195.1.2](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2022.195.1.2).
The exact read version is [arXiv:1805.10961v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10961v3),
PDF SHA-256 `2bebde484903a994dcc198294e404adb4e6d20ad689cd2ba93671e6cbbae1220`.
Theorem 1.1 supplies the perimeter minimum and Proposition 2.4 supplies
the established model-profile Hessian; both are explicitly credited.
Our text/code supply the first-moment comparison and are not copies of
that proof. The normalization is also independently derived in the paper.

The equal-mass precursor is our immutable release
[gaussian-balanced-simplex-all-k-v1](https://github.com/mxym/math/releases/tag/gaussian-balanced-simplex-all-k-v1),
commit `ebfd90558439afca21babeb7b77c8d8837ea745f`. This package
generalizes its proof mechanism; its uniform corollary is not counted
as another independently solved conjecture.

Additional sources and the exact limits of status/equivalence checking
are in `LITERATURE_STATUS.md`. Prior conjectures and the unequal-mass
Euclidean counterexample are cited separately in the manuscript.

The base algebra/real-analysis sources and proof-closure replay come
from our equal-mass package. `AdditionalRadial.lean` is the new arbitrary
initial-derivative extension. `results/lean.json` records compiler and
dependency provenance plus all five source/log hashes. Its analytic
endpoint formalized field is false. Two complete-source internal model
reviews are archived; they do not replace external human peer review.
