# Exact source, prior work and limits of the search

- **Primary target.** Jean-Christophe Bourin and Teng Zhang, [Matrix Triangle Inequalities: Past, Present, and Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30538), [Conjecture 8.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30538v1#S8), submitted October 21, 2026; the version history showed v1 only as checked on November 19, and the authors list acceptance in *Reviews in Mathematical Physics*. The conjecture applies every symmetric (two-sided unitarily invariant) norm to the positive root of the *operator* mean of the two absolute powers, for finite real p≥1. Its proposed conclusion is that one obtains a norm invariant under unitary conjugation. It does not assert two-sided invariance of the result. We disprove convexity, not conjugation invariance. **Corollary 6.5 of the same paper already proves the whole p=2 case**. If the outer norm is the Schatten p-norm at the same exponent, tracing the pth power gives its usual norm at every p; this is why the result here must be read with the universal norm quantifier.
- **Nearby, inequivalent questions.** In the same source Theorem 8.5 treats unitary-orbit domination by real and imaginary parts, not the norm axiom. Problem 8.6 asks for triangle inequalities and optimal constants more broadly and is not solved here. Theorem 7.7 and Proposition 7.8 compare an arithmetic symmetric modulus of a sum with the **norm of the sum** of individual symmetric moduli, not the **sum of their norms**. In Proposition 7.8 the individual operator norms are both 2 and the left side is 2√2, so that pair does not refute normhood. See also Bourin and Lee, [Triangle inequalities for the operator symmetric modulus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19607), and Teng Zhang, [Operator symmetric moduli and sharp triangle inequalities](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70672). General unitary-orbit counterexamples (such as the [MI-06 solution](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/matrix-inequalities-and-norms/MI-06/solution.tex)) are a different order comparison.
- **The operator-norm functional is old.** El-Haddad and Kittaneh, [Numerical radius inequalities for Hilbert space operators II](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/90412), Studia Math. 182 (2007), Theorem 1, (6), take α=1/2 and r=p. Their estimate says that the numerical radius is **bounded above by** the functional in our operator-norm example. It does not assert that the upper bound itself is a norm; nonconvexity of that upper bound does not contradict their estimate.
- **Classical convexity mechanism.** Hansen and Tomiyama, [Differential Analysis of Matrix Convex Functions II](https://emis.dsd.sztaki.hu/journals/JIPAM/images/228_08_JIPAM/228_08.pdf), Theorem 3.4: even order-two matrix convexity on the whole real line forces a function to be quadratic (including degree below two). Thus the failure of whole-line matrix convexity of x↦|x|^p for p≥1 different from 2 is not a new result. The rank-one large-block test in the alternative proof extracts precisely a scalar matrix-convexity inequality; unitary change of basis tests any vector. The main finite witnesses show the stated norm-triangle failures directly without requiring the classical classification.
