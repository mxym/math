# Independent check of the binary three-deletion counterexample

The scope matches the first assertion of Ma–Zhang Conjecture 6.2, arXiv:2611.01064v1. The observed binary word has length 64, and its possible uniform input supersequences have length 67. For each possible set of three new positions and each of the eight bits assignments, place the observed bits in the remaining positions; this enumerates each pair (input, deletion mask) exactly once. Thus multiplicity equals the embedding count, and the common normalization is 8 choose(67,3).

I ran an independently written enumeration that opens the three positions in increasing low-bit index order in the observation's fixed-length integer representation before OR'ing the three added bits. It gives exactly the recorded histogram for each word and strictly compares the integer moment products (no entropy rounding). Leading-zero words are interpreted at the same fixed length, so integer representation is injective. For positive multiplicities e, entropy is larger exactly when the product of e^e over the possible inputs is smaller, since their total multiplicity is identical.

PASS: the word (01)^16(10)^16 has strictly greater posterior input Shannon entropy than (01)^32 and, by bit complementation, also than its complement. This supplies a counterexample to alternating maximality with exactly three deletions, but does not determine any true maximizer or the least counterexample length. It does not affect the distinct three-insertion result. This is an independent internal check in the AI-assisted workflow, not external peer review.
