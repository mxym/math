# Prior work and scope

- Ma and Zhang, [*Maximal input entropy for binary two-deletion and two-insertion channels*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01064v1): Lemma 2.1 gives the posterior; Theorem 2.2 settles the separate two-deletion comparison; Theorem 5.3 already determines the alternating three-deletion histogram at every observed length; Proposition 6.1 covers all observations through length 12; Conjecture 6.2 first asserts alternating optimality at **every** observed length with exactly three deletions. The counterexample concerns only this conjectural assertion, not any of their proved results. We credit their use of exact histogram products for finite Shannon comparisons (§§4.2, 6.1). Their [arXiv history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01064) displayed v1 when checked.
- Atashpendar, Roscoe and Ryan, [*Information Leakage Due to Revealing Randomly Selected Bits*](https://orbilu.uni.lu/bitstream/10993/21251/1/information_leakage_due_to_revealing_randomly_selected_bits_preprint.pdf), §3.6, studied the alternating-maximizer hypothesis for posterior Shannon entropy; Atashpendar et al., [*From Clustering Supersequences to Entropy Minimizing Subsequences for Single and Double Deletions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00703), discuss it in the deletion direction. Singhvi, Sabary, Bar-Lev and Yaakobi, [*Conditional Entropies of k-Deletion/Insertion Channels*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3581849), [open text](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.10026v2), give the embedding posterior and the one-deletion alternating Shannon theorem. None of these lower-radius positive results is contradicted.
- Atashpendar, Mestel, Roscoe and Ryan, [*A Proof of Entropy Minimization for Outputs in Deletion Channels via Hidden Word Statistics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11609), analyze the distinct limit of fixed observed length with growing transmitted length and formulate a conjecture about positional autocorrelation. The [separate length-348 refutation of that autocorrelation statement](https://www.trureturing.org/research/atashpendar-mestel-roscoe-ryan-2018-kappa-alternating-minimizer/) concerns that coefficient. It does not establish a strict Shannon comparison at exactly three deletions. The [Ma–Zhang project dossier](https://www.trureturing.org/research/singhvi-sabary-barlev-yaakobi-2024-deletion-channel-max-input-entropy/) also discusses a largest-multiplicity obstruction and a failed prefix-increment inequality for a different question. Neither is the entropy comparison here.
- Repository [#155](../deletion-posterior-no-least/README.md) exhibited deletion-posterior majorization and higher-Rényi obstructions; the centered doubled-bit pattern is an antecedent to the present construction, not a new pattern. It did **not** compare Shannon entropy after three deletions. [#147](../deletion-posterior-majorization/README.md) concerned the opposite, constant-word majorization extremum. [#157](../three-insertion-entropy/README.md) handles the shorter-input posterior after three **insertions**. Thus the two assertions of Conjecture 6.2 have different answers.
