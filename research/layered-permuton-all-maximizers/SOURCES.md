# Sources, antecedents, and scope of the search

- **Question and definitions.** Adam Kabela, Daniel Král’, Jonathan A. Noel and Théo Pierron, [“Density maximizers of layered permutations,” EJC 29(3) (2022), P3.56](https://doi.org/10.37236/10781), [full text](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v29i3p56/pdf/). Section 5, **Problem 3**, asks whether a layered target admits a nonlayered optimal permuton. The preceding paragraph distinguishes the known finite counterexample; Section 2 uses vanishing 231 and 312 densities to characterize layered permutons. Their Theorems 3–5 concern layer counts, a different issue. This proof answers Problem 3 for order at least two, not their other numbered problems.
- **Finite antecedent.** M. H. Albert, M. D. Atkinson, C. C. Handley, D. A. Holton and W. Stromquist, [“On packing densities of permutations,” EJC 9 (2002), R5](https://doi.org/10.37236/1622), [full text](https://www2.math.ethz.ch/EMIS/journals/EJC/Volume_9/PDF/v9i1r5.pdf). Theorem 2.2 gives a layered maximizer at every finite order for layered targets, and all finite maximizers are layered if none of the target layers is a singleton. Remark 2.3 gives a nonlayered finite maximizer of order 20 for 43215. Neither existence of a layered optimizer nor this sufficient finite condition alone characterizes all limits of unrestricted near-optimal sequences.
- **Cloning.** W. Stromquist's [1993 manuscript on packing posets](https://walterstromquist.com/POSETS.DOC) is an antecedent of the layered-existence theorem. R. W. Barton's [*Packing densities of patterns*, Theorem 4](https://doi.org/10.37236/1833) uses finite planar surgery on inversions; [Stromquist’s 2005 slides](https://walterstromquist.com/DensitiesAtAltitude.ppt) present Reid Barton's cloning argument for incomparable points. Related finite replacement arguments appear in M. Bóna, B. E. Sagan and V. R. Vatter, [“Pattern frequency sequences and internal zeros,” especially Lemma 7.6](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/bsagan/Papers/Old/pfs-pub.pdf). The preceding [1324-specific argument in this repository](../packing-density-1324-unrestricted/ALL.md) already uses the second variation with oppositely ordered copies. The present transfer of labelled completion events to an entire inversion interval, and the replacement argument allowing arbitrary runs of singletons, apply beyond that special case. For related sufficient stability criteria in the **unlabelled graph** setting see H. Liu, O. Pikhurko, M. Sharifzadeh and K. Staden, [JLMS 108 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12777); order of layers is not recorded by the underlying unlabelled graph, and no such stability criterion is used here.
- **Recent source.** J. Borga, V. Féray and P. Winkler, [*Permutons*, arXiv:2611.12104v1, 9 November 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12104v1#S3.SS2). Section 3.2 treats order at least two and states the existence of a layered sequence attaining the packing density, as well as the known results on layer counts from Kabela et al.; it does not state that every optimal permuton is layered. We checked the full survey for its packing/layered discussion, bibliography and later open-problems section, alongside the original question and finite theorem. Searches using the authors, problem wording, inversion symmetrization, and later work on packing and stability did not locate an independent general answer. Pattern avoidance, graph inducibility, and optimization of Schubert specializations are different questions.
