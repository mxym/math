# Source mapping and prior work

* N. Fernando and B. Kousik, *A further study of polynomial \(g_{n,q}\) over finite fields*, [arXiv:2606.01037v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01037v1), 31 May 2026, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01037). Definition 1.1 in §1 defines a multivariate permutation polynomial by the fiber cardinality \(Q^{k-1}\) over \(\mathbb F_Q\), and Definition 1.2 gives the stronger slice-wise local notion. Equation (1.1) defines the univariate \(g_{n,q}\). In §5 the authors define \(\sigma_\ell=\sum_{i=1}^k X_i^\ell\), \(\ell\ge1\), and \(g_{n,q}^{(\ell)}=g_{n,q}\circ \sigma_\ell\) just before Theorem 5.9. The exact target **Conjecture 5.23** and its reduction **Remark 5.24** are on PDF p. 17 of v1 (end of §5). They quantify over \(q=p^m,Q=p^e\) and do not require \(m\mid e\). The proof here also covers the univariate case \(k=1\).
* Theorem 5.9 of the same preprint already proves the coprime case and its local-permutation conclusion. Theorem 5.11 rules out local permutations for noncoprime powers. Its Theorem 5.21 excludes several noncoprime cases for balanced multivariate PPs, and Remark 5.22 already rules out a nonpermuting univariate postmap. It is the **remaining noncoprime case** for multivariate PPs, for arbitrary arity and without admissibility restrictions, that the free action resolves at once. The eight *univariate* questions of §6 are distinct.
* The finite-field facts about the cyclic multiplicative group and the character orthogonality used in the two proofs are standard. The source cites R. Lidl and H. Niederreiter, *Finite Fields*, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press (1997), Theorems 6.33–6.34, for exact counts of diagonal equations. The present proof does not need an explicit diagonal-equation count. The checker extracts actual \(g_{n,q}\) examples by elementary expansion of the defining sum and removal of powers of \(X^q-X\). It does not transcribe source tables or redistribute external data.

On 2026-11-27 we checked the current public arXiv version and searched the title, arXiv ID, and exact “Conjecture 5.23” label with “\(\sigma_\ell\),” “sum of powers,” “balanced,” “Fourier,” “roots of unity,” and “permutation polynomial in several variables,” including related work on diagonal equations. We found the original partial results but no matching documented resolution of the exact conjecture in that bounded search. The proof intentionally makes no originality claim for group actions, character convolution, or the mixed-exponent observation separately.
