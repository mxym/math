# Internal adversarial review: four factors in Duchi's expected-norm comparison

**Disposition: the four-factor mathematical claim passed independent internal review.** The requested literature supplements are included in the accompanying manuscript. This is an independent AI review, not external peer review. No assertion of priority. The claim passed is: for each integer population n≥5 there exists a population of n real rational PSD contractions, dimension (n)₄, whose ordered four-factor product has strictly greater *mean operator norm without replacement* than with replacement. Since prior work proves the universal inequality for product lengths ≤3, four is its smallest *possible* failing length. Not proved: population four, a minimum dimension, the exact optimal ratio, failure for every norm, a new general disproof, or a low-dimensional AlphaEvolve benchmark.

## Audit trail and independent arithmetic

Run the independent check with `python -B -O check.py`; expected output ends in PASS. Both the source verifier and this independent verifier passed in optimized mode and both use explicit exceptions, not assert. The latter imports no author code; it multiplies scaled integer 2×2 matrices and obtains each norm from the integer square root of its squared Frobenius norm, checking rank ≤1 and exact squareness. This differs from the author's cosine/dot-product objective and polynomial interpolation.

* The vectors are rational because they are powers of (99+20i)/101, of modulus one. Multiplying each local template by 101⁷ gives an integer matrix of rank one with positive trace ≤101⁷. Thus each genuine block is a rational PSD contraction, including the two non-idempotent weighted blocks; the full matrices are not asserted to be rank-one projections.
* On each label injection block the product in the specified order is either zero or a rank-one outer product scaled by the weights and the absolute successive overlaps. The block diagonal product norm is its maximum block norm, not the sum of them. Reversing the four slots applies the reflection with matrix entries (cos(3θ),sin(3θ); sin(3θ),−cos(3θ)); the reflected slot weights match. Each pair has exactly one endpoint-order representative since the endpoints are distinct. This proves the dimensional factor 2·(n)₄/2. Every partial placement of k occurring labels extends to an ordered four-slot block when n≥4. If that extension is not retained, its reverse is retained with identical norm, even for a word using only one or two labels.
* For each restricted-growth equality pattern using k labels, the number of ordered population words is (n)ₖ, with no extra factorial. Distinct words have a common value S=(99/101)⁹. Grouping the 256 possible nonzero-slot words into 15 classes, the independent program confirms every entry of the small-angle loss table, not only grouped totals. Symbolically expanding (n)ₖ coefficient-by-coefficient gives n⁴(S−R)=(2n/D)(a n²−bn+e) with exactly the four printed integers. The coefficients at n=5+x are 82396441351706403933495 (x²), 370679468818936234086162 (x), 71561165382278964018427 (constant), all positive. This is one rational angle for every n, with n-dependent concrete matrices; no exchange of n and angle limits is needed.
* As a separate direct check, for n=4,5,6 the code compares the product norm on *every ordered population word* both with the full block list and with the halved one, including the actual zero blocks. Counts: 256, 625, 1296 words; compressed ambient dimensions 24, 120, 360. For n=5 it obtains exactly S−R=143122330764557928036854/140853128766496215082650125. At n=4 this particular family gives S−R<0 and does not answer the population-four question.
* Three zero operations should not be confused: (i) zero *slots* for omitted labels in an injection block are necessary; (ii) appending the same zero ambient summand to each matrix preserves every product norm and therefore extends the example to each larger *dimension*; (iii) appending whole zero matrices to a fixed five-label *population* does not supply the proof for larger populations. For n labels obtained this way, S_new=(5)₄/(n)₄ S₅ and R_new=(5/n)⁴ R₅; already n=6 has S_new−R_new=−180935550930908251822771337/1460365239051032757976916496. The paper's construction rebuilds the symmetrization at each n, as required.
* For any fixed n with δ=S−R>0, the perturbation (1−ε)Aᵢ+εI with rational 0<ε<min(1,δ/8) is positive definite and still a contraction. Each length-four product changes in operator norm by ≤4ε (telescoping); the gap changes by ≤8ε. This standard perturbation is not new; compare Liu Prop. 8.2.

## Scope of original statements and method provenance

1. [Duchi, COLT 2012, p. 1, Eqs. (2) and (3)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v23/duchi12/duchi12.pdf) defines ordered uniform sampling, not an unusual averaging convention: the without-replacement normalizer is (n−m)!/n! and the with-replacement normalizer n⁻ᵐ. Eq. (3) takes the operator norm of the product; inserting this nonnegative scalar into (2) makes the outer absolute value immaterial. The unmodified Recht–Ré Eq. (1) is a different comparison of norms of matrix averages. For historical precision use “Duchi asked” rather than attributing a stronger explicit assertion to the commentary.
2. [Israel–Krahmer–Ward, Prop. 3.2 and Thm. 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.0333) cover m=2,3 for all n≥m and all unitarily invariant norms; m=1 is equality. Their discussion immediately after the proof highlights new repeated-index terms at length four, not a four-factor result. Together with a single four-factor witness this gives the stated exact threshold for existence.
3. [De Sa, NeurIPS 2020, §2.3](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/42299f06ee419aa5d9d07798b56779e2-Supplemental.pdf) already disproves the norm-inside comparison for six matrices sampled six at a time in dimension 1440. The permutation block maximum of planar rank-one projectors is his device, based on Recht–Ré's tight frame. His five-factor result in §2.1 instead concerns a norm outside the averaging. It neither gives the current four-factor result nor an original general conjecture left open to be disproved here.
4. A direct separation of De Sa's *specific* template (reviewer's derivation): at four factors let c=cos(π/n), n≥4. Each distinct ordered word has optimal score c³ since distinct rays have absolute overlap at most c and four consecutive rays suffice. Each one- or two-label pattern scores at least c³ using adjacent rays. The six three-label patterns can be embedded in three consecutive rays to score at least 3c²+2c³+c²cos(2π/n) in sum: the three one-hold paths score c² each, the two no-hold backtracks score c³ each, and the triangle scores c²cos(2π/n). This total is 6c³+2c²(1−c)². Thus the unmodified regular-frame template does not fail at four for any n≥4. This is not a statement about all symmetrized projections.
5. **Prior work identified during review and now cited in the manuscript:** [Zehua Liu, arXiv:2611.07649v1, 5 Nov 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07649v1) uses the same inner-norm objective, permutation direct sums and exact word-type enumeration. Theorem 4.2 and Proposition 6.2 establish the graph criterion and exact ratio; Corollary 6.3 gives six-factor examples for n≥11 at ρ=1/2. Theorems 5.3, 6.1 and 7.2 study other growth regimes; the family uses equal-weight projections with a path-supported Gram matrix throughout, 0<ρ≤1/2. In particular, this is much closer than unrelated norm-outside work and is now cited.
   
   Explicit distinction (reviewer's calculation, independently checked): take m=4 in Liu's (20), write q=1/ρ≥2, and normalize by WOR as in Liu. The resulting n⁴·WR/WOR equals q³(n)₁+(3q²+3q+1)(n)₂+(3q+2)(n)₃+(n)₄. It is increasing in q, and at q=2 exceeds n⁴ by 2n³+6n²−n>0. Thus Liu's *stated* path-Gram parameter range cannot yield a four-factor violation for any admissible n. This independently establishes that specializing that formula is not the result here. The candidate uses weighted nonorthogonal planar rays, missing-label blocks and all n≥5 in a separately enumerated polynomial. Describe this as a shorter-length refinement of existing counterexample methods, not invention of the method.
6. [Lai–Lim 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/lai20a.html) and [Zhong, October 2026, Eq. (1)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04874v1) are norm-outside/ordered-matrix-average results; Zhong's quantitative sufficient large-population regime does not bear on this analysis. [Georgiev et al., Problem 6.47, Dec 2025 v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02864v3) considers a quantitative infimum in specified dimension. Its m≥4 open-status sentence is not a reliable description of the general historical status in view of De Sa, and its low-dimensional optimization cases are not settled here.
