# Prior work, version distinctions, and bounded search

**Original questions.** In [Balko–Scheucher–Valtr, latest arXiv revision v2 (7 February 2022)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.12533v2), the Open problems paragraph on p. 6 asks whether the limiting coefficient exists for all fixed dimensions and hole sizes. The adjacent Problem 1 asks specifically for its value for empty tetrahedra in a tetrahedron. The [2023 journal record](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.21088) confirms the model and scope. Exact problem numbering and simulation values here refer to the full author preprint. The [previous SoCG paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2020.14) already provides the upper bound through islands; Theorem 1 of the full paper gives the matching lower order, used in this note for positivity. Its Theorem 7 proves existence and body-independence for planar holes, and Theorem 6 computes the planar quadrilateral value. Those results, and simplex-size existence in all dimensions, are not new here.

**The tetrahedron value and its precursors.** [Reitzner–Temesvari, Theorem 1.4 and §3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.08734), [journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1215/00192082-11081246), on empty simplices, already establish the required hyperplane-section representation and the limit for \(k=d+1\). We evaluate that integral using the classical simplex slice formula ([Lasserre, Theorem 2.2 and the Curry–Schoenberg attribution](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.4617)). [The early EuroCG 2021 extended abstract, Corollary 2.3](https://page.math.tu-berlin.de/~scheuch/publ/bsv-tightbounds-eurocg21.pdf) described the lower bound 3 as tight at a tetrahedron. In the subsequent full paper the authors no longer state that equality: they report a simulated tetrahedron range of 3.095–3.097, state a strict comparison with the ball, and pose the exact value as Problem 1. This note uses the later formulation, not the superseded equality wording. It does not infer the answer from the simulation.

**Late-2026 and related scopes (checked 19 November 2026).** Searched both without a date limit and specifically for July–19 November 2026, including revised preprints, expected empty convex polytopes or simplices, leading coefficients, simplex densities at zero, fourth moments of hyperplane sections, Fischer triangles and the exact coefficient or its decimal expansion. Compared the original [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12533) and the [2026 department publications index](https://www.mff.cuni.cz/en/kam/research/archive-of-publications/publications-2026). Among the nearest results:

- [Andričík et al., August 2026 revision v2, Theorems 1.1–1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.08762v2) maximize the number of holes for **deterministic planar point sets** in the absence of larger holes. They do not give these expected iid limits.
- [Bhattacharya–Das–Islam–Sen, Theorems 1–2, Computational Geometry 130 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comgeo.2025.102197) bound the change in empty-simplex counts upon deleting a point from a configuration. The result is not this leading coefficient.
- [Beck, full original preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.07952), [2026 journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11009-026-10277-8), computes selected positive metric moments of a random simplex in various bodies. [Mannion](https://doi.org/10.2307/1427809) also studies volume moments in a tetrahedron. These concern the volume of the random tetrahedron itself, not the expected number of **empty** ones or the integral of the fourth power of section area.

No matching theorem for either claim was located in the material checked. Sources can be unindexed, inaccessible, or use another formulation, so this is not a priority determination.
