# Source and scope comparison

1. **Question and sharpness.** A. Paone and M. Paone, [*Line-Graph Signature Beyond the 2-Core*, v1.3](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/line-graph-signature-beyond-the-2-core/reader/), [deposited version](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21706797), Conjecture 5.4 is the universal fixed-rank question. Proposition 5.2 gives the known rough upper bound. Theorem 5.3 gives the sharp cactus lower examples at all positive ranks, based on A. Paone's [amplifiers](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/unbounded-signature-line-graphs/reader/) for odd ranks and a neutral square extension for even ones. At rank zero, the isolated vertex suffices. Their incidence identity and rooted reductions, including singular-port cases, are predecessors of the method. The universal-constant conjecture in [Akbari et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2025.114953) was already refuted: see the earlier Paone work and [Francis and Uptain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22874), whose constructions and bridge-response criterion also predate this note.
2. **Exact special cases.** Paone and Paone, [*Line-graph inertia of roses and generalized theta graphs*](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/line-graph-inertia-roses-generalized-theta/reader/), obtain exact inertia formulas for those two families, not merely an inequality. Their Proposition 10.1 gives a sufficient residue-count condition for the fixed-rank inequality on bridgeless cacti; Remark 10.2 explains the multiport limitation. Their [*Response Protection for Line-Graph Equality Families*](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/response-protection-line-graph-equality-families/reader/) deals with closure under particular equality-preserving attachments and one-/two-edge extensions of those protected graphs. We do not assert exact inertia, response protection, new extremal constructions, or an equality classification.
3. **The graph grammar and inertia algorithms are established.** See R. Duffin, [*Topology of Series-Parallel Networks*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-247X(65)90125-3). For a finite 2-connected block with no $K_4$ minor, the ends of any specified edge can be chosen as the two terminals; see D. Eppstein, [*Parallel recognition of series-parallel graphs*, Lemma 9](https://ics.uci.edu/~eppstein/pubs/Epp-IC-92.pdf), and the block-level $K_4$-minor criterion summarized at the [beginning of §5 of this Combinatorica article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00189-5). A connected graph with cut vertices need not have a two-terminal expression for the whole graph. M. Fürer, C. Hoppen and V. Trevisan, [*Efficient diagonalization of symmetric matrices associated with graphs of small treewidth*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02515), Theorem 1, compute the inertia of a **given** bounded-treewidth matrix by congruence and tree-decomposition dynamic programming. We do not claim new general algorithms for computing inertia or finding decompositions: our finite upper comparators prove one inequality simultaneously over **all** such graphs. Earlier [Wang–Fan line-graph signature bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1003) use cycle residues in the line graph, not the cycle rank of the root.
4. **Within this repository.** [#143](../line-signature-cyclomatic-kernel/README.md) provides the finite-order extremal reduction for arbitrary fixed cycle rank and its unrestricted result through rank five. [#146](../line-signature-cactus-simple-kernel/README.md) gives the one-port palette and proves the bound for every cactus with arbitrary boundary locations and trees; it separately treats simple suppressed kernels. All cacti are $K_4$-minor-free; the converse fails, for example for a diamond. A simple suppressed kernel need not be $K_4$-minor-free, e.g. the graph $K_4$. The present all-treewidth-two extension uses two-port states and unrestricted blockwise compositions; it is not the cactus proof relabeled. The repository's [finite series/parallel dictionaries](../minimal-cut-series-parallel-dictionaries/README.md) and [parallel templates](../minimal-cut-parallel-nonattainment/README.md) use related network composition but count minimal vertex cuts, not inertia.
