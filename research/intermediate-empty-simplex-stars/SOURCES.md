# Sources and scope (19 November 2026)

**Original question.** Matthias Reitzner and Daniel Temesvari, [*Stars of Empty Simplices*, arXiv:1808.08734v2, Conjecture 1.6, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.08734v2), conjecture exponent \(d-k\) for the expected **maximum** over \(k\)-subsets, for iid uniform points in a fixed convex body and \(1\le k\le d-1\). The present result disagrees exactly for \(2\le k\le d-1\); it is not a deterministic worst-case assertion. Their Theorem 1.1 already proves order \(n\) for \(k=d\), and Theorem 1.5 proves the stated order for \(k=1,d>2\); its planar upper bound contains a square-root logarithm. Their Theorem 1.4 concerns the *total* number of empty simplices. The small-cluster marking strategy and its unique-base reduction already appear in their proof of Theorem 1.1, especially (4) and Lemmas 2.1–2.2. The present lower bound instead uses rare flat intermediate bases with controlled proper faces, thin-slab completions, and an independent-pool uniqueness estimate; the upper bound uses conditional moments across all completion-overlap patterns.

**Source versions.** Conjecture numbering refers to that checked preprint. [Reitzner's CRM slides, 16 October 2023, slide 24/26](https://www.crm.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Matthias_Reitzner_Empty_Simplices.pdf) also display the conjectured order. His [Oberwolfach Report 54/2018, pp. 3265–3266](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46776?nt=1) explicitly distinguishes the unproved intermediate cases from the endpoint results. The [2024 journal article](https://doi.org/10.1215/00192082-11081246) and its [TU Wien institutional abstract](https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/196465) were checked at record/abstract level. The journal full body was not accessible in this check; no claim that it kept identical numbering or wording is made.

**Nearby work.** [Bárány–Marckert–Reitzner](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.3928) study empty triangles with a common edge; [Temesvari](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07481) studies moments of the maximal number of empty simplices sharing a facet (a base of \(d\) vertices). Neither is the intermediate degree here. The deterministic planar problem about the minimum over all point sets of the largest star is different again.

**Recent work.** Searches both without a date cutoff and specifically over July–19 November 2026 used the paper title, authors, conjecture, intermediate degree, common-face/edge and expected maximum/star formulations, and the special case of empty tetrahedra sharing an edge. Compared the [original version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08734). [Andričík et al., v2 of 3 August 2026, Theorems 1.1–1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.08762v2) treat maxima of total planar hole counts over deterministic configurations excluding a larger hole; the revision does not give the common-base iid maximum. [Bhattacharya–Das–Islam–Sen, Theorems 1–2, Computational Geometry 130 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comgeo.2025.102197) bound changes in empty-simplex counts under point deletion, not the growth rate here. No matching intermediate-degree exponent was located in the checked material; indexing gaps, inaccessible sources and different terminology cannot be ruled out.
