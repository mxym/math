# Primary sources, related results, and scope (2026-11-19)

* [Heilman, JGT, *Independent Sets of Random Trees and Sparse Random Graphs*](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23225), §§3, 7 and Lemma A.1, analyzes the size-biased/planted tree and credits Bedrosian for the complete exact expected count. [Basit–Galvin](https://doi.org/10.37236/9896) note the Matrix–Tree variance comparison, and their Problem 17 and Heilman Q8.3 are a *different* path-normalized macroscopic lower-bound question. The critical lognormal window and qualitative supercritical collapse are already in [repository #130](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/130); these are not new claims. We do not claim the unnormalized count is small.
* [Martin–Reiner, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0302213) supplies the exact planted degree generating polynomial, with earlier degree-aware complete multipartite enumeration by [Richard P. Lewis](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(99)90111-5). The empty-bin leaf formulas in ENTROPY are direct consequences, not new enumeration. [Heilman](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06724) studies tree-concentration arguments and planted model statistics more generally. A primary-source scan did not locate the displayed comparison of typical *coefficient size* via its leaf-test event or the sharp sparse coefficient loss; the finite leaf count alone is not original.
* Canonical pressure is prior: [Janson's conditioned Poisson fringe theorem, 7.12(ii), Ex. 10.2](https://www2.math.uu.se/~svantejs/papers/sj264.pdf); [Ralaivaosaona–Šileikis–Wagner](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00467), §6.1, on log counts and fringe recursions at unit fugacity; [Dembo–Montanari–Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.4821) on factor-model free energies and marginal recursions, and [repo #125](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/125) for the PGW formula, global differentiability and fixed-parameter pressure deviations. [Pemantle–Peres](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548313000345) gives environment concentration of log partition as a spanning-tree edge-Lipschitz function. The independence ratio is due to classical maximum independent set results (see Heilman Theorem 2.10 and [Pittel](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoap/1029962872)). Such pressure/density facts are inputs; convex duality is standard.
* Expressing log-partition coefficients as connected induced-subgraph count combinations is an established principle. [Patel–Regts, §3–4, especially Lemma 3.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.01167) credit earlier Csikvári–Frenkel additivity; their independence-polynomial application is explicit. Use a self-contained inclusion–exclusion proof here only for convenience. The Cayley forest extension count is classical. The sixth-degree arithmetic in the present Cayley model can be obtained either by enumerating all trees on up to six labels with this principle or by expanding the independent Poisson fringe moments; see the exact reproducible programs.
* Sharp coefficient extraction from a hard-core model tuned to a target mean using a size local limit is standard. [Davies–Perkins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04984) and [Jain–Perkins–Sah–Sawhney](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01161) treat given-size counting for bounded-degree graphs, with thresholds depending on the maximum degree. [Jenssen–Perkins–Potukuchi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09709) use binomial local limit extraction on the hypercube. [Fang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20961) treat forest cardinality fluctuations in other fugacity ranges. A general fixed-degree assertion with constants depending on maximum degree is not automatically a theorem here: random tree maximum degree is unbounded and $k/n$ is allowed to decrease arbitrarily slowly. The paired fringe and conditional smoothing are a simple way to obtain matching power-law prefactors without a full size LCLT.

The new candidate is the precise relative sparse coefficient size on *every* supercritical sublinear sequence, the separate exact-layer entropy curve and strict annealed gap, not another first claim for those inputs.
