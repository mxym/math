# Source, scope and novelty checks

## Original scope and results

* [Passerini–Severini (2008 preprint, published 2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.2597) originated the star question in the context of the trace-scaled combinatorial Laplacian. The later authors' attribution is in their introduction, reference 8.
* [Dairyko, Hogben, Lin, Lockhart, Roberson, Severini & Young, arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.00420), [published 2017 record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2017.01.037), [author-hosted text](https://jupiter.math.nycu.edu.tw/~chlin/Publications/Articles/entropy.pdf), [UCL accepted-manuscript record](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1526412/). In arXiv v1 the respective conjecture numbers are 1.2 (minimum S over connected graphs), 1.3 (maximum S over trees), and 3.3 (minimum Hα, α>1, over connected graphs); Thm 3.8 already proves strict minimum for α=2. They proved almost-all Shannon and computed small n. Numbering here is strictly that of arXiv v1; the publisher API full text was not available in the present check.
* [Lin–Zhou (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2018.04.004) supply bounds in terms of graph parameters, discussing the general star statement as conjectural. [ETNA paper on paths/rings](https://emis.de/ft/18679) separately restates the three problems and proves approximations, not universal extrema. [Simmons–Coon–Datta](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.07906) discuss star minimization but do not resolve it; [their normalized-Laplacian variants](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01142) are a different entropy. [Liu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09766) compare structural information to von Neumann entropy. [Dong et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13884) optimize degree-based entropy, which is not our objective.

## Prior tools and mathematically implied cases

* [Csikvári (2011), Theorem 8.1](https://real.mtak.hu/14004/1/application_Kelmans.pdf) gives the needed coefficientwise Kelmans comparison for any simple graph, not just trees or adjacent vertices. We apply the operation while retaining connectivity, not a blanket assertion that Kelmans preserves connectivity.
* [Jozsa–Mitchison](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4922317) study Shannon entropy as a function of the elementary symmetric functions with fixed sum; monotonicity is prior. [Mohar (2007), citing Zhou–Gutman](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2006.12.005) proves coefficientwise star/tree/path inequalities. Thus the tree path maximum (and tree special case of the star minimum) is an application of older general results.
* [Sakai–Iwata, Theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.00019) explicitly gives, at a prescribed order α, the minimum higher-order Rényi entropy at the vector with one heavy atom and all other atoms equal. With α=1 and support length n−1 this plus strict Shannon optimality yields every higher order, by strict monotonicity of entropy on that one-parameter curve. With α=2 and the known H₂ star theorem it already implies all higher orders >2. Neither this norm comparison nor the α≥2 graph range should be marketed as an independent technical breakthrough. The substantive graph result sought is the universal Shannon cone bound (5). The proof file gives its own short zero-counting argument for the order transfer.
* [Threshold spectrum background](https://academic.oup.com/qjmath/article/73/2/609/6432406): threshold spectra from degrees or birth sequences are classical, and the prefix contrast basis here is proved directly.
