# Prior work, model, and search scope

1. **Fixed cycle rank.** A. Paone and M. Paone, [*Line-Graph Signature Beyond the 2-Core*, v1.3, author text](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/line-graph-signature-beyond-the-2-core/reader/) ([deposited version](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21706797)), pose the conjecture in §5, Conjecture 5.4. Their Lemma 2.2 gives the incidence shift and Lemma 3.5/Proposition 3.6 give the nonsingular tree recursion and the exact arbitrary-pendant-forest response formula. Proposition 5.2 gives the upper bound $f(c)\le c$; Theorem 5.3 attains $\lfloor(c+1)/2\rfloor$ at every $c\ge1$. Its odd-rank amplifier comes from A. Paone's [earlier construction](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/unbounded-signature-line-graphs/reader/); the even-rank construction adds a neutral 4-cycle module. Section 9 already proposes kernel enumeration, explicitly notes the $2c-2$-vertex bound on the unsplit degree-at-least-three kernel, and reports counts at small cycle ranks. Neither that observation nor a graph-order census bounds the attached trees or decorated chain lengths in a maximizer. We claim no new lower construction, no new branch-count observation, and no new result at $c=0,1$.
2. **Path reduction and related classes.** A. Paone and M. Paone, [*Line-graph inertia of roses and generalized theta graphs*](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/line-graph-inertia-roses-generalized-theta/reader/) ([deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21744051)), §§3–4, give the balanced path pieces, endpoint parity constraints in the singular cases, and residual compression. They compute signatures on roses and generalized thetas and discuss selected bridgeless cacti. Their [*Response Protection for Line-Graph Equality Families*](https://aletheia-technologies.it/en/research/response-protection-line-graph-equality-families/reader/) ([deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21793638)) proves a response invariant under four-subdivision and specified module attachments, and covers certain edge extensions. Four-subdivision and the undecorated path response are not new here.
3. **Different original conjecture.** S. Akbari, C. Elphick, V. Kumar, S. Pragada and Y. Tang, [*A new conjecture on the inertia of graphs*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2025.114953), posed the stronger uniform line-graph bound later refuted by A. Paone's examples. [Francis and Uptain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22874) independently give an unbounded construction. Our result concerns the subsequent fixed-rank question, not those refutations. Earlier line-graph bounds by Wang and Fan count cycles of specified residues in the **line graph**, not the cycle rank of its root; see Paone–Paone §8.2 for the distinction.
4. **Scope of the additional arguments.** Tree-signature cost bounds, extremal replacement by a leaf or by nothing, and the Loewner rounding of marked runs turn the proposed kernel search into a finite bounded-order extremal problem. Positive definiteness in the only possible exceptional forms gives the exact upper values through $c=5$ for all orders. The numbers do not rest on the attached small exact checks.
5. **Search limits (19 November 2026).** The authors' posted versions, indexed papers and follow-ups on line-graph signature/cycle rank and signless-Laplacian spectra at 2 were checked; e.g. [Liu and Liu](https://combinatorialpress.com/ars-articles/volume-120-ars-articles/on-the-signless-laplacian-spectra-of-bicyclic-and-tricyclic-graphs/) concern spectral radii/spreads rather than signature. No earlier proof of the same extremal reductions or unrestricted upper range was located. In mxym/math a full text, PDF and nested-source-archive scan of the tree at [#137](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/a989f66ed8f8da70e07cf6c23ec16d2edc86c570) and 48 prior tags covered 15,080 distinct main blobs and 277 tag-only ones (35,915 text units, 162 PDFs, 160 archives, no extraction error). All 36 additions between that tree and [#141](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/2294baf5b871c6367694f99dc9c022dbe4deede3) were screened separately; [#142](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/85b67a88d41ab008692867e113796cf183c055d4) adds seven unrelated Gaussian-comparison files. Search included notes, preprints, research, formalizations and release registers. The available release materials were cross-matched with Git blobs by digest; the 73 additional publicly retrievable unmatched assets were independently digest-checked and screened (10,756 texts, 19 PDFs and 42 archive containers). The separate weighted-cut tag concerns graph cuts; two historical draft release assets were not accessible. This is a bounded check, not a novelty guarantee.
