# SC0 gate audit for the two-effective-hour research stage

15 October 2026. Yongxian Zhang (张永贤), South China University of
Technology. No external funding. OpenAI AI assistants assisted. Original
material all rights reserved; third-party rights retained.

The original target is unchanged: for every set X, every total binary
class C of finite actual VC dimension d, and every finite C-realizable
labeled sample S, one preconfigured total decoder rho must admit a labeled
subset T of S of size at most d with rho(T) consistent with ALL of S.
Improper output is allowed. No bits, message order, multiplicity, input
domain, sample size or search path may be supplied to rho.

| Gate | Exact status after the present mathematics |
|---|---|
| SC0, full arbitrary-domain original target | Open; no universal construction or legal counterexample |
| SC1, every finite total class on every finite domain | Open; five listed complete finite positive controls do not supply the universal quantifier |
| SC2, simultaneous compatibility on the entire partial-sample poset | Open; this is the substantive missing existence step |
| SC3, finite-to-arbitrary-domain compactness | Conditional bridge available, with the original finite premise still missing; already known in the literature |
| SC4, legal refutation | None; no certified impossibility at k=actual VC in the general improper model |
| New major problems solved in this role | Zero |
| Independent mathematical review, Lean proof, publication or Release | None |

The mature finite existence controls are (n,d,number of concepts)
(8,2,34), (10,2,49), (12,2,65), (16,2,105), and (12,3,207).
Every one has an explicitly listed legal decoder and a separately checked
plain-integer deletion DAG covering every realizable partial sample by
induction. Direct full partial-sample enumeration was additionally done
for the four controls with n<=12. It was NOT done for the n=16 control.
The first two n=12,d=2 SMT attempts ended unknown; their original limits
remain recorded, and the later CNF positive certificate supersedes no
negative mathematical conclusion because none was made.

The exact fractional reformulation and dual are auxiliary. Its natural
polytope has a fractional vertex already at k=d=1; fractional feasibility
at the general target parameter k=d is not proved. The strict Warmuth
weighted dual obstruction concerns k=1<VC=2. It does not refute SC0.

The fixed-decoder graph criterion at k=1 and coNP-completeness analysis
at k=2 concern testing a GIVEN preconfigured decoder. The hard instances
have a trivial good size-two decoder; they are not existence-hardness
instances. The proposed complexity proof is author-only, with its input
table size, witness size and full reduction quantifiers stated. It should
receive a separate review before any novelty or public-theorem status.

Forced-label normalization, the conditional fresh-message augmentation
lemma, the finite column quotient, and finite d-maximal reduction are
complete elementary arguments within their stated scopes. Fixed-reference
unanimity rules, including two-reference rules on the listed n=16 class,
are insufficient. The two-reference exclusion has exhaustive author-only
integer profile controls; that same class has a verified general size-two
decoder. The prescribed recursion in the already withdrawn 2022 claim
also gives explicit incompatible samples, even after allowing improper
reconstruction. These are restrictions of proposed routes.

No finite partial-concept impossibility, full-row matching, no-result SAT
run, ordered/proper compression restriction, maximal/maximum confusion,
independent-coordinate decoder choices or unreviewed external claim has
been substituted for the original global target. A further successful
attack would need a new compatibility or augmentation invariant for ALL
partial samples. The failure of the examined routes is not evidence that
the full problem is impossible.
