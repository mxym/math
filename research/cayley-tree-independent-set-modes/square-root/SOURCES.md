# Prior work and scope

* [Heilman, *Independent Sets of Random Trees and Sparse Random Graphs*, Question 8.2](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23225) asks about the mode in a sampled uniform labeled tree. [The earlier repository result](../README.md) identifies the common linear-scale center and gives exponential deviations for every fixed relative error; it does not give the centered second-moment estimate here. See also its [full reference discussion](../SOURCES.md).
* [Ralaivaosaona, Šileikis, and Wagner, *A Central Limit Theorem for Almost Local Additive Tree Functionals*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-019-00622-4), [arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.00467), Lemma 3 and §6.1 equation (44), provide polynomial level-width moments and exponential boundary influence for the hard-core log message. Their equation (12), based on Janson, also compares a single truncated conditioned-tree neighborhood to a Kesten neighborhood. Their Theorem 10 establishes a CLT for the log **number** of independent sets in a random conditioned Galton–Watson tree. It does not state the present environmental variance/bias bounds for their conditional **mean size**. Neither the generic stabilization idea nor the width bounds are new.
* [Janson, *Simply generated trees, conditioned Galton–Watson trees, random allocations and condensation*](https://www2.math.uu.se/~svantejs/papers/sj264.pdf), Theorem 7.1 and Example 10.2, identify the conditioned Poisson model and its Kesten root limit. [Andriantiana, Misanantenaina, and Wagner](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08290) study the mean independent-set size itself and deterministic extremal comparisons.
* [Fang, Lu, Nevo, Yao, and Zheng](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20961v1), Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 5.1: large-forest unimodality and conditional hard-core size CLT with linear variance. Their work already gives all modes within $O(\sqrt n)$ of the **individual forest's own mean**, and even $o(\sqrt n)$ using their uniform CLT and unimodality (the interval argument is in the review). [Li, Vallier, and Zhang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07943) state unimodality for every forest. The matter here is variation of the individual means about the **fixed** $\mu n$ under the random-tree law; it is not a new unimodality or within-tree CLT.

The earlier full repository and release-content check is documented in [the parent source note](../SOURCES.md); the only changed files since its exact checked main are the 14 files of that parent result in #125, ending at main b6a1035af890ab2b87a7585414d2bb6676526810. We also checked the primary works above, their local-limit comparisons and more recent results under Cayley-tree mode, conditional occupancy, typical independent-set size, stabilization and average independent-set size. This describes the search performed, not an exhaustive historical-priority certification.
