# Independent internal audit: uniform approximate Bell-to-Bell overlap

**Verdict: PASS on the mathematical statements, subject to the interpretation and attribution below.** This is an AI-assisted internal review, not external peer review or a priority certificate. The original conjecture for arbitrary fixed non-prime-power dimension is *not* established. No external review has been performed; no priority claim is made.

This report concerns the arguments reproduced in [COMPRESSION.md](COMPRESSION.md) and [CONTROLLED.md](CONTROLLED.md). Their division and wording have been edited to state the two witnesses separately, identify the extra entropy guarantee, and clarify the attribution.

## Primary-source checks and scope

* Puchała et al., [Phys. Rev. A 92, 032109, §V](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.92.032109) use mutual maximal entanglement across two global splittings. Życzkowski later explicitly restated the unitary displaceability interpretation in [Quantum version of the Euler’s problem, §III(d)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03903#page=4). Brahmachari et al. [§III.A and Eqs. 17–18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.13307#page=4) formulate UBB for arbitrary global unitaries, vectorize the local unitary tests to squared norm d, and use elementwise conjugation on the second test in (17). None asks about only the members of a fixed Bell basis. Puchała et al.'s other conjectures on measurement entropy are not this statement.
* For the **specific** projectivized left orbit through the identity, see [Smith §4.3 and Thms. 4.10–11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.04005#page=12). The orbit consists of scalar multiples of unitary matrices, is compact monotone orientable spin, and has minimal Maslov number 2d. The action of every global unitary is Hamiltonian (choose any Hermitian logarithm). [Iriyeh, Thm. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.0777#page=5) treats powers of 2, and Prop. 9 separately proves d=3 nondisplaceability. [Evans–Lekili, Prop. 1.3.5(c)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.05842#page=7) and [Ex. 7.2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.05842#page=52) give nonzero Floer cohomology over a field of characteristic p when the local dimension is a power of p. Nonvanishing forces intersection with every Hamiltonian image. This is an existing exact prime-power result, not a newly proved geometric theorem. Smith's mixed-prime vanishing is for every coefficient field, relative spin structure and **flat line bundle**. Neither vanishing nor its scope warrants asserting displacement by any Hamiltonian or by any linear unitary.
* [An–Đoković, Thms. 3.4 and 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.1915#page=11) apply to a simultaneous-conjugation representation on a direct sum (not just a single matrix). Setting entry (j,i) in the (i,j)-th matrix to zero makes the quotient torus weights exactly one copy of each x_j−x_i. Its signed characteristic number has magnitude d!. Thus both existence and the signed count for the controlled-gate intersection are prior geometric consequences. [Bykov, Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1419#page=24) explicitly constructs this flag as the Lagrangian of orthogonal lines in a product of projective spaces. Self-intersection ±χ is the standard normal/tangent bundle consequence; do not attribute a quantitative Bell-gate theorem to Bykov.
* A finite search under UBB, mutual entanglement, projective-unitary orbit displaceability, controlled-gate orthogonalization and orthogonal flag intersections did not identify the same all-dimension projection-and-rounding bounds in an earlier paper. The compression is an elementary consequence of the published prime-power theorem; limited search is not proof of original discovery or of the status of every special case. The sharp controlled example is a standard local transverse perturbation (equivalently a flag height function); no broad new intersection theorem is established.

## Independent mathematical checks

1. Write the Bell amplitude as max of the absolute Hilbert–Schmidt pairing divided by d; the square is the fidelity. Equality in Cauchy–Schwarz is proportionality and the phase can be absorbed. Extend a complex-linear HS-unitary V by the identity on *all entries outside the corner*, not by a state-space tensor product. In dimension q prime power the projective conclusion gives X unitary with the image unitary as well: both have Frobenius norm √q, so the projective scalar has modulus one. Put q=d+k with 0≤k<d. The complementary blocks C,D,E of both matrices are identical. Block unitarity therefore gives AA*=BB*=I−CC* and A*A=B*B=I−D*D, both ≤I, with at least d−k eigenvalues 1. The common trace s is at least d−k>0, including singular corners. Normalizing A,B by √s gives equal input/output reduced density matrices on each respective subsystem, the right one being the transpose in this vectorization. No equality *between* the left and right matrices is claimed. Their common operator-norm bound is 1/s≤1/(d−k); hence all positive-order Rényi entropies and the von Neumann entropy are at least ln(d−k). Equal marginals alone already hold for any eigenvector of V; it is their **simultaneous high entropy** that matters.
2. Take SVD phase midpoints for the contractions A and B, independently. The average of the four real parts of the pairings of the corresponding unitaries is Re Tr(B*V(A))=s, so b_d≥s/d≥1−k/d. Squaring gives the guaranteed Bell target fidelity. The two existential witnesses need not be the same output of the construction; avoid saying that they *must* be different or that the theorem excludes a common witness for some V. If exact UBB holds, a common witness always exists.
3. The smallest prime power **not below d** works and is <2d by the next power of 2. In [Baker–Harman–Pintz, Thm. 1](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/83.3.532), set x=d+2d^0.525; x^0.525/d^0.525→1. This puts a prime ≥d within 2d^0.525 for *every* sufficiently large d. Thus k/d=O(d^(-19/40)), uniformly in V. For k/d≤1/2, −ln(1−k/d)≤2k/d. No finite threshold or optimal exponent is needed. [Li v8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04458v8) announces 0.52 but is not needed. Group n input copies by party to apply the arbitrary-dimensional theorem for d0^n; a collective witness is allowed. No multiplicativity or single-copy conclusion follows.
4. The controlled-gate section vanishes exactly when the U_i x_i are orthonormal. Orthogonal lines modulo d input phases describe a flag; the skew-Hermitian infinitesimal frame rotations modulo diagonal phases identify its real tangent bundle with the sum of Hom(L_i,L_j) for i<j. This identification is global, so the Euler number is ±d!, with positive sign after orienting compatibly. A nontransverse zero set need not have at least d! points by Poincaré–Hopf alone. If transverse, isolated simple zeros are finite and there are at least d! flags. Each zero supplies a phase family containing d orthogonal physical Bell states, not necessarily a basis of d², and different flags do not automatically give orthogonal states to each other.
5. For generic independently chosen U_i, the submersion (U,F)↦(U_i L_i)_i to the product of projective spaces is transverse to the embedded output flag manifold. Its normal has real dimension d(d−1). To check equivalence with transversal zeros of the section, use local unit vector frames v_i near an orthonormal output tuple. The differential of the off-diagonal Gram entries ranges freely: for each pair j>i vary v_i in its v_j direction, using no reverse coefficient, with each free complex coefficient affecting just one pair. Parametric transversality gives almost every gate. For sharpness, rescaling by 1/t removes the identically zero t=0 section; smooth extension follows by integrating the derivative in t. At t=0 the off-diagonal entries vanish exactly for eigenflags of H. In frame e^(sK) the derivative of e^(−sK)He^(sK) is [H,K]; hence the (j,i) component is multiplied by i(c_i−c_j)(h_j−h_i), nonzero on each complex coordinate. Locally each zero persists uniquely by the implicit-function theorem (and is the original eigenflag); uniform convergence on the complement of fixed neighborhoods on the compact flag manifold rules out all other zeros. Thus d! is attainable for an explicit class of **transverse flags**, not a universal exact generic count or bound on the number of physical states.

## Reproducible finite illustrations

The accompanying [primary script](check.py) passes both normally and with optimization `-O`; its fail-closed checks contain no Python `assert`. The [independent script](independent.py) (SymPy 1.14, Python 3.12) also passes both. It uses a complex rational example with distinct left and right reduced matrices, a reflection on HS matrix space, fixed complementary entries of 7×7 unitaries, and two unitary roundings on each side. The four normalized real pairings are 43/51, 401/425, 401/425, 43/51 and their average is 67/75≥5/6. Neither rounded input in this construction has an exactly unitary output; this does **not** exclude some other exact Bell solution for this gate. It also constructs the normal Jacobians from commutators and checks their determinants at each eigenflag through d=4. No finite script verifies Floer cohomology, the universal existence theorem, or priority.
