# Independent internal review: growing order addendum

Pass for GROWING.md `9e97aab1dafbdc6faac70c8b3395c2726e41dabbbecbcf6b8afecd387d5ab88b`. This builds on the already reviewed compact-order proof. The result requires dimension at least 2 and asserts a sufficient growth window, not its optimality.

Brunel's Theorem 4 (display (10), journal preprint arXiv:1704.01620) gives exactly a dimension-uniform normalized missing-volume bound at k^(-2/(d+1)) with excess tail O(exp(−ckt)). Integrating that tail gives the squared missing moment. For the inverse moment, Z≥1/2 allows −log Z≤2(1−Z) and the tail integrates when 2a<ck. On the middle band the inverse is at most s0^(−a), while its probability is exponentially small uniformly in k. On the small band the block exponent r/d is linear in k, so integrating below s0=(2D)^(-d) contributes at most 2 after choosing a≤εk small; denominator and boundary factors in the displayed integral are correct. Combine the three bands to obtain (G3).

The upper ordinary add-one bound uses the L² missing estimate, not merely its first moment; Cauchy–Schwarz and (G3) produce the factor exp(C(|p|+1)n^(−α)). For the exterior, intersect the uniform pathwise cap gain with the high normalized volume event at 1−O(n^(−α)); clipping the credited interval means its log magnitude is O(n^(−α)). This yields the reciprocal exponential lower factor. For fixed B the lower dominates by n^α; for κ small with 2Cκ<α/2, the loss ratio tends to zero and the surviving bound is at least order n^(−1−α/2). Both moment ranges are o(n) because d≥2. At each n the fourth-moment constant may depend on n; Hölder still gives a local o(q) loss. Crucially the partition also enforces (P_n+1)log λ≤log2 so the final factor converting q to F_p(λ) stays bounded uniformly in n; telescoping preserves the claimed increment coefficient. No dimension-one extension from the concentration argument is asserted.
