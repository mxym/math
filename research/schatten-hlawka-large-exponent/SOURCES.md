# Sources, prior work and search coverage (2026-11-18)

- **Original problem:** K. M. R. Audenaert and F. Kittaneh, [arXiv:1201.5232v3, §8.2 Problem 7, Eq. (48)](https://arxiv.org/html/1201.5232v3); [published text](https://www.impan.pl/shop/publication/transaction/download/product/111309), *Banach Center Publications* **112** (2017), 15–31, §8.2 Problem 6, Eq. (48), pp. 25–26. This asks for the best dimension-independent constant in the inequality of the draft, for arbitrary complex rectangular matrices (not just three diagonal ones). The same source gives the operator-norm obstruction and Hilbert benchmark and recalls the Adamović multi-vector Hilbert inequality (50), suggesting generalizations to any number of matrices. The present all-finite-list upper bound is uniform in that number but need not be sharp.
- **Direct inputs, not contributions of this note:** E. Esa, [proof/blueprint](https://github.com/savarin/hlawka-schatten/blob/79aa498bfcf7b22bd91d771fb32ec278e2d4704b/BLUEPRINT.md); [checked external registry entry](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-09-25-000006&version=1), and the author's [statement/evidence inventory](https://gist.github.com/savarin/a0ee61cfc02db7a458aaa1e0c0f11e20). Prior: unrestricted finiteness for all real 1<p<infinity, trace endpoint failure, scalar compact-ratio Bregman/Mazur transfer via Hermitian dilation, and the sharp one-dimensional compact cyclic variational formula for complex diagonal matrices at p>=256. The diagonal constant works in every size and is sharp already in each fixed size at least three. The prior rough bounds include 939p/2000 < K_p <= p. Existing formalization is external and applies to its named prior statements only; we have not rebuilt it or formalized our new estimates.
- **Earlier near-optimal test and cutoff refinement:** Esa and contributors, [September 2026 analytic proof for p>=90](https://gist.github.com/savarin/4621846808053fe597d1a59f1b7acbbd), [simplified proof and certificates](https://gist.github.com/savarin/c718e63210ad761c2ab3a393df8b09f4). Among other things the witness t=p^(-1/p) already implies liminf K_p/p >= 1/(3 log 2), and their explicit lower bound on the ratio contains a negative logarithmic correction of the same order. The present note does not claim this witness, its leading lower slope, the diagonal reduction, or O(p) diagonal growth are new. The sharper conclusions offered here are the matching leading *upper* slope, exact logarithmic and constant terms with controlled errors for the true diagonal optimum, eventual uniqueness and the constant shift in the optimizing cyclic parameter. No optimal cutoff is claimed.
- **Classical scalar background:** the equal-sign two-sided Young remainder in Section 1 is classical; see F. Kittaneh and Y. Manasrah, [*Improved Young and Heinz inequalities for matrices*](https://www.zuj.edu.jo/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/yousef-manasrah.pdf), and the two-sided formulation in [*A refinement of the Kittaneh-Manasrah inequality*](https://www.creative-mathematics.cunbm.utcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/2011_vol_20_2/creative_2011_20_2_157_162.pdf). The proposed unrestricted upper bound is obtained by giving Esa's scalar comparisons explicit constants, not by a new noncommutative transfer method.
- **Opposite endpoint:** [Colbrook, original MI-25 solution](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/tree/main/matrix-inequalities-and-norms/MI-25), gives a distinct failure at p=1. [*An endpoint lower bound for Schatten–Hlawka constants*](https://evidencepress.org/releases/schatten-hlawka-endpoint/), [archived candidate](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23999268), Nov 2026, already derives a logarithmically corrected lower bound at p down to 1 for unrestricted matrices and optimizes the parameter in Esa's 2x2 trace-endpoint family. Its direction, witness, scale, and claims differ; our elementary upper bound does not close the near-1 gap.
- **Adjacent work:** D. Marinescu, C. P. Niculescu, [*A New Look at the Hornich–Hlawka Inequality*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00025-025-02464-9), and [*Constructions and reductions on the Hornich–Hlawka inequality and its generalizations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23785) investigate the constant-one Hlawka property and relatives; not the large-p deficit optimum. The [formal challenge for arbitrary operators at p>=256](https://prove2.me/theorems/d897cb43-a38c-4a3b-b9cd-211dab9660a0) explicitly labels diagonal = unrestricted as a conjecture, not a proof. This note does not settle it.
- Searches used combinations of Schatten, Hlawka/Hornich, deficit, cyclic, supremum, maximizer/unique, optimal parameter, asymptotic/logarithm, large exponent, linear growth, multi-summand, Adamović, Bregman/Mazur, alongside source/blueprint and follow-up gists. No matching prior expansion, uniqueness theorem, or explicit simultaneous dimension/summand independent order bound was identified in this bounded literature pass. There is no global priority guarantee.
