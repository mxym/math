# Problem and comparison with previous results

- [M. Sonnleitner, *A limit theorem for Hausdorff approximation by random inscribed polytopes*, arXiv:2508.16442v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.16442v1#S1). The introduction immediately before Remark 2 asks for higher-dimensional analogues of the polygonal vertex-factor laws, for sampling either inside the polytope or on its boundary. The paper's main Theorem 1 is a Gumbel theorem for *smooth positively curved* bodies under the particular optimal surface density. An adjacent question concerns other sampling densities in the smooth case; it is **not** answered here. The [arXiv history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16442) lists only v1; the [author's publications page](https://sites.google.com/view/mathias-sonnleitner) lists the [Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. version](https://doi.org/10.1090/proc/17703). The precise problem location above is in the inspected arXiv text.
- [H. Bräker, T. Hsing, N. H. Bingham, *On the Hausdorff distance between a convex set and an interior random convex hull*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-applied-probability/article/on-the-hausdorff-distance-between-a-convex-set-and-an-interior-random-convex-hull/114E2BAAC5A8FEFDC4F327BDAD7FDF3E), Adv. Appl. Probab. 30 (1998), Theorem 3: two-dimensional interior-sampling product limit for polygons, with explicit angular factors. [J. Prochno, C. Schütt, M. Sonnleitner, E. M. Werner, *Random approximation of convex bodies in Hausdorff distance*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-025-03186-7), Math. Ann. 392 (2025): polygonal surface-sampling factors and expected error, the interior-polygon antecedent, and higher moments (Remark 2 of [arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.02870)). Independent local Poisson factors at planar corners, the vertex-maximum identity and their utility for moments are prior ideas. The point here is all higher-dimensional fixed polytopes for both samplings.
- [V.-E. Brunel, *Uniform behaviors of random polytopes under the Hausdorff metric*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.01504), §5 and Corollary 2 give upper moment rates of order $n^{-q/d}$ for bounded-complexity polytopal supports and uniform volume sampling. Our contribution is not the exponent alone. [P. Calka, J. E. Yukich, *Variance asymptotics and scaling limits for random polytopes*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.08025), Theorems 1.2–1.4, establish transformed vertex-local boundary limits for volume Poisson processes in **simple** polytopes and variance asymptotics for face counts and volume. These are important one-vertex scaling predecessors; they do not state the present total-variation joint Hausdorff law for arbitrary polytope types and the two sampling measures. [Reitzner, Schütt and Werner, boundary samples of a simple polytope](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-022-00460-2), concern expected facet counts and volume difference rather than this Hausdorff limit.
- The multivariate Mecke identity is standard; see [G. Last and M. Penrose, *Lectures on the Poisson Process*, Ch. 4](https://stoch.math.kit.edu/img/Last/lastpenrose2017.pdf) or [the authors' book page](https://stoch.math.kit.edu/297.php). Here it turns the finite random contact set at each polytopal vertex into equation (1).
