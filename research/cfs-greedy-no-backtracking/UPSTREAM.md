# Primary source and exact scope

Claude Carlet, Serge Feukoua and Ana Sălăgean, [The stability of the algebraic degree of Boolean functions when restricted to affine spaces](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-025-01702-z), *Designs, Codes and Cryptography* 93 (2025), 4799–4832. See especially journal Theorem 5 (sufficient monomial condition); Algorithm 1, FunctionWithoutDegreeDrop, selection steps 4–17 and arbitrary completion steps 18–20; Proposition 6 (termination on specified parameters); and Remark 2 (the omitted r=n−3, n≥9 range). The journal article does not assert failure in this range. The [authors' preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.20211) has different proposition numbering; the construction and Remark 2 can also be located by name. No third-party files or implementation are included here. The original parameter line did not advertise r=n−3 as guaranteed; like Remark 2 we consider running the same selection rule unchanged in that family.
