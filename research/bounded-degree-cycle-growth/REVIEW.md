# Independent internal review of bounded-degree cycle growth

Reviewed PROOF.md SHA-256 af2fc8b72dcfdab1f220fede2d62768c3a752de43e115618a35b56951eb7d6b1. The proof supports its stated scope: 2^{c_D n^gamma} distinct simple cycles for 3-connected simple n-vertex graphs of maximum degree ≤ D, for each fixed integer D≥3 and sufficiently large n; no exponential-in-n bound and no assertion about distinct vertex sets.

- Ear supply: every cycle terminal has noncycle incident edge and is in a component with ≥2 terminals (a chord or a noncycle component must meet C again). In a rooted bounded-degree tree the deepest eligible subtree has ≥2 and ≤D+1 terminals, because each child contains ≤1. A closest pair in that subtree has no interior terminal; removing the subtree gives disjointness and each original component contributes ≥t/(D+2). Interior paths are genuinely off C.
- Chord order: the block count follows from 2P≥200s^4 and s≥3, ensuring ≥(s+2)T per block. An in-block ear toggle acts on a separate arc; otherwise the distinct complementary indices meet the monotone-subsequence criterion. For the increasing case an odd number of disjoint rung switches produces paths to the opposite rail joined by the off-rail cap, with a unique rung set.
- Nested order and bridges: coordinates along each rail are strictly increasing and on a whole rung/cap are constant. A span at least s+3 leaves at least s+2 whole interior integer columns, so an exterior path and each permissible switch parity give distinct cycles. In the small-span regime, after deleting the two opposite center vertices, the split of H separates strict left/right and possibly the open center rung. The last left visit to the first outside-left H visit gives one bridge portion, no interior in H. This supplies a genuine two-track replacement inside the slab: a half-rung when it ends in the open center, straight complement when approach/departure rail match, or untouched central full rung joining complementary tracks when rails differ. The explicit spans leave both attachments in the slab. Distinct centers use distinct bridges, so the outside portions are disjoint, and the two end caps close the tracks into one simple cycle for either pairing. An off-H edge uniquely detects each toggle.
- Chen–Xu–Yu's published circumference bound uses b=2(D−1)^2+1 and exponent log_b(2); floor threshold results in gamma=log_b(2)/4. The h-regular cycle powers ensure nonemptiness eventually for every fixed admissible triple; if deletion disconnects, at least two cyclic gaps have h/2 deletions each. Knor's 1994 p.321 printed statement and definition of min/max degree inequalities were read directly. Thus the conjectured upper polynomial bound fails for every triple. Faudree §10 pp. 135–136 separately quotes that conjecture and explains how the cubic theorem solved the Barefoot–Clark–Entringer conjecture, not this fixed-minimum-degree statement. The 1997 Aldred–Thomassen cubic proof supplies central ideas and is credited, and the stronger literature in the cubic case is not superseded.
