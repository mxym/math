# Provenance, comparisons, and search limits

**Source problem.** Naomi Dvora Feldheim and Ohad Noy Feldheim, [*Mean and minimum of independent random variables*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-021-2195-0), *Israel Journal of Mathematics* **244** (2021), 857–882; [arXiv v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.03004). In §1.1, Conjecture 1 asks about every fixed pair of positive weights and independent, individually unbounded laws. Their Theorem 1 proves the equal-weight case, originally posed by Noga Alon. Their Theorem 2 covers arbitrary positive weights, including in higher dimensions, for identically distributed independent inputs. Their §1.4 and subsequent proofs already use log-tail geometry, inverse functions and convex minorants; we do not claim these ideas, or the dimension and norm reductions the authors anticipated, as new. The use of a finite inverse-level hull here is coupled to a clipped two-factor moment and a random exchange of the interpolation roles. Their §1.1 Conjecture 2 is stated for natural-number dimension; the norm ratio equals one in dimension one.

**Classical anti-concentration input.** The atom estimate conditional on the role coins is the classical Sperner antichain bound for positive subset sums, a point version of [Erdős, *On a lemma of Littlewood and Offord* (1945)](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1945-04.pdf). The proof includes the maximal-chain count. No new Littlewood–Offord bound is asserted.

**Related results examined.**

* [Steinerberger, *A sharp estimate for probability distributions*, §§1.2–1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00663) considers an iid absolutely continuous law and a supremum over thresholds, giving a bound in terms of median and density. The further exponential-extremality questions there concern a *rate* relative to threshold; this proof of an infinite limsup does not settle them.
* [Barnard and Steinerberger, *Three convolution inequalities on the real line*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.08731), prove estimates on convolution and autocorrelation integrals; they do not compare the weighted-mean survival with the same-threshold product survival of two arbitrary unequal laws.
* [Alon, Feldman, Mansour, Oren and Tennenholtz](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.09548) introduce models for admission to evolving social groups. [Feldheim and Feldheim's quantile-admission paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.05550) analyzes convergence with candidates drawn independently from one underlying law. Those results concern the motivating model and do not prove the heterogeneous weighted tail comparison; the present note claims no new admission-process result.

**Status check (2026-11-19).** We examined the original article and preprint, authors' related publications, forward citation records and searches using author names, the title and DOI, heterogeneous weighted averages and product tails, asymptotic minimum/mean comparison, and recent work on these statements. No separate prior proof of the displayed unequal-law, unequal-weight assertion was identified. Citation indexes differ and can be incomplete; not finding a proof is not a priority guarantee.
