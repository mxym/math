Exact published scope is Feldheim–Feldheim §1.1 Conjecture 1 (fixed positive weights, two independent individually unbounded nonnegative variables, strict survival tails, limsup only). This is an internal mathematical check, not external peer review or a priority claim.

1. Eventual ratio boundedness extends over a compact threshold interval because both survival functions have positive minima there. On a positive-time atom, X≥t,Y>t forces the weighted mean strictly over t, and symmetry bounds each individual survival jump ratio by C. Thus the chosen right-continuous log tail has jump ≤2log C. The strict rectangles are compatible exactly with the weighted threshold.
2. Inverse levels reach each threshold and tend to infinity, with overshoot at most B. The finite upper concave hull has integer data contacts, nonnegative nonincreasing slopes, and horizontal terminal faces contribute no height. A long positive face gives an exact secant; short positive faces grouped to length in [N,2N) give at most two units of relative excess if the first/last slopes are close. If no group or face works, the geometric slope drop uniformly bounds total rise, including the last incomplete group, contradicting unbounded inverse levels. The selected secant yields s(b−a)=j−i≥N. Looking up the next inverse integer level bounds the tilted product between the claimed endpoint-to-supremum constants despite possible jumps and plateaus.
3. The clipped exponential identity follows pointwise on Z>a and by Tonelli. Its strict t convention is correct even at endpoints. Weighted clipping on X>a,Y>a lies below clipped W; after factoring by independence and dividing by the product of the individual suprema, its right side is at most C(1+min(Iu,Iv)). The left endpoint neighborhoods immediately before b give nonzero lower bounds for both normalized integrals, across atoms as well, using backwards exponential control. Both integrals are therefore uniformly bounded.
4. The rectangle bound applies to both roles. Their fair average yields one-half of each joint log deficit, so the expected deficits remain uniformly bounded. Conditional on roles all Bernoulli subset weights are strictly positive; identical subset sums form an antichain and the stated Sperner bound applies for every real weight, surviving mixing over roles. The finite normalized support at fixed depth has a true positive minimum spacing among distinct points. First take sufficiently many good points by choosing the depth; then choose N large enough. Their disjoint backward small intervals force the normalized area above its bound. No limit is interchanged.
5. Positive multiple weights follow by grouping independent coordinates; the group's survival probability is at least the product, so the inequality direction is correct. No specified rate or full-limit assertion.
