# Sources and scope

- [Potapov] Vladimir N. Potapov, [*On the Fourier Entropy–Influence Conjecture for Boolean Plateaued Functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563), [v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21563v1), 18 September 2026. Equation (1) and Theorem 3 prove the lower mean-Hamming-weight bound for Boolean supports; Proposition 6 gives its upper counterpart by input reflection, and Lemmas 1–2 supply the binary triangular Gram matching used here. Proposition 4 and Theorem 2 give the plateaued spectral-support degree bound and the already sharp constant 4. Section 5 Proposition 10 proves μ_f(pq)≥q^d μ_f(p) only for p,q≤1/2, and Corollary 2 consequently uses final parameter u≤1/4. Our additions are the full thinning range, anisotropic and general-grid sharp comparison, equality criteria, and nonattainment at positive entropy. We do not claim the original Potapov inequality or the constant 4 as new. In Section 5 the printed identity with f⊕1 has a simple notation slip: reflection of *the inputs* gives μ_f(1−p), whereas complement of the output gives 1−μ_f(p); neither slip is needed for our proof.
- Irit Dinur, Yuval Filmus and Prahladh Harsha, [*Agreement Tests on Graphs and Hypergraphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09426), SIAM J. Comput. 54(2), 279–320 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/21M1397684). Claim 6.8 (Biased Schwartz–Zippel) gives the p-biased absolute nonvanishing bound min(p,1−p)^d cited by Potapov, not the ratio of nonvanishing probabilities at two different biases studied here.
- Anurag Bishnoi, Pete L. Clark, Aditya Potukuchi and John R. Schmitt, [*On zeros of a polynomial in a finite grid*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06020), Combin. Probab. Comput. 27(3), 310–333 (2018), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548317000566). Their Theorem 1.2 (generalized Alon–Füredi) gives absolute support-size bounds for polynomials on arbitrary product grids, also treating individual degrees. Their proof tools include the standard interpolation/top-coefficient identity. We use that identity in a weighted rank sum and compare two parameterizations of the same support, not an absolute support-size bound.
- Zhaole Li and Deng Tang, [*A note on the FMEI of the Boolean functions in the Generalized Maiorana–McFarland construction*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2026.02.052), Discrete Applied Mathematics 387, 106–115 (2026). As already used by Potapov, their plateaued examples give entropy/influence ratios approaching 4; Fourier min-entropy equals Fourier entropy for the flat spectrum in question. We claim only nonattainment of 4 at positive entropy and do not reproduce their construction.

A bounded search (2026-11-27) for Potapov's title and arXiv identifier in combination with “thinning,” “equality,” and “support”, and for “p-biased” polynomial “nonvanishing” and weighted/generalized Alon–Füredi finite-grid results, turned up the primary papers above and no matching sharp relative theorem with equality classification. This is not a claim about unpublished work. The fixed common-bias case is intentionally stated as a direct extension of Proposition 10, not a new result on the general Fourier Entropy–Influence conjecture.
