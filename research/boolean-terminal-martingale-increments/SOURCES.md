# Sources and exact scope

- Omid Etesami, Ji Gao, Saeed Mahloujifar and Mohammad Mahmoody, [ePrint 2021/1464, author full version](https://opencryptology.org/2021/1464/versions/20240114T061328Z.pdf), TCC 2021, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-90453-1_25). Appendix C, printed pp.44–45, Definition 51 takes the absolute size of the observed increment, with w_0=E[w_1] and w_(n+1)=w_n, so a stopping rule can use the current value and a non-stop gives a zero gap. Theorem 52 compares the μ-dependent fixed-threshold result and the expected-gap bound; the displayed open question directly below it asks for probability times threshold Ω(μ/sqrt(n)). The displayed sentence does not restate the Boolean terminal hypothesis; the surrounding intended mathematical setting uses Boolean Doob martingales. Without any terminal condition an interior constant process disproves the bare sentence, not the intended claim. No reduction or protocol material in Appendix C enters the proof here.
- [AICR c/0030](https://aicr.info/c/0030/) gives precisely the Boolean terminal condition and observed-prefix absolute jump. The result here supplies the threshold dependence and constant in that mathematical statement. This note does not address its separate requests about implementing anything in a cryptographic model, nor does it claim a tight minimax product for very small μ.
- Richard Cleve and Russell Impagliazzo, *Martingales, Collective Coin Flipping and Discrete Control Processes* (1993 manuscript), as cited in the primary Appendix C's Theorem 52, establish the constant-start gap phenomenon. Hamidreza Amini Khorasgani, Hemanta K. Maji and Tamalika Mukherjee, [Estimating Gaps in Martingales and Applications to Coin-Tossing](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01694), [author full version](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/haminikh/Martingale__Full_Version_2_0_.pdf), study the expected stopped gap; their lower estimate 2μ(1−μ)/sqrt(2n−1) concerns a different objective. Amos Beimel et al., [Tighter Bounds on Multi-Party Coin Flipping, via Augmented Weak Martingales](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2017/168/), generalize the centered martingale gap estimate to suitably constrained weak models (Theorem 1.3 and its formal version). This note has no such generalization; it uses ordinary conditional martingale orthogonality.

The tools in the proof are standard (stopped orthogonal increments and first passage). A bounded overlap search (2026-11-27 UTC) checked the above primary sources, related citations, and combinations of Boolean terminal, martingale gap, first passage, small mean, stopped variance, and probability times threshold. No equal general fixed-threshold bound with the explicit terminal-impurity correction was found. This is not an exhaustive search or a priority claim. The two checkers enumerate abstract finite probability spaces only.
