# Primary references and exact overlap

- Daniele Bartoli, Alessandro Giannoni, Giuseppe Marino and Alessandro Neri, [*An infinite family of non-extendable MRD codes*, arXiv:2603.27748v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.27748v2). The target is **Sec. 5(ii)**, the question about norm-one $U_\delta$ when the restricted code is MRD, especially in odd extension degree. The displayed family is in Sec. 3, initially over degree five, after Proposition 3.4 (normalization to the trace-zero hyperplane). Proposition 3.6 gives a degree-five scatteredness test; Propositions 3.7–3.8 exclude pseudoregulus and Lunardon–Polverino extensions for degree five, while Theorem 3.12 proves a nonextendability family in the specified powers of 3. Those results and their original parameters are not claimed here. Our main statement is a uniform large-degree cutoff for the displayed binomial family and an exact counting bound, not a classification of arbitrary MRD codes. The separate $m\ge6$ pseudoregulus exclusion extends the coefficient method of Proposition 3.7. The binary $m=5$ calculation is a finite supplement and recovers its coefficient obstruction.
- Stanley E. Payne, [*A Complete Determination of Translation Ovoids in Finite Desarguian Planes* (1971), printed pp. 328–331](https://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLINA_1971_8_51_5_328_0), specifically the Main Result under condition (3)' (printed pp. 328–329 and proof thereafter): if $g$ is an additive permutation over $\mathbb F_{2^m}$ and $g(x)/x$ permutes its nonzero elements, then $g(x)=c x^{2^s}$, $c\ne0$, $\gcd(s,m)=1$. [Original scan](https://www.bdim.eu/item?fmt=pdf&id=RLINA_1971_8_51_5_328_0). The coding geometry form for actual $\mathbb F_2$-subspaces, rather than only their projective point sets, is restated in the Introduction of [arXiv:2004.13101](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.13101). Proof Sec. 5 explains the hypothesis map and the linear change of coordinates in full. Payne's theorem is used only for the analytic binary nonextendability conclusion, not the all-field cutoff; the seven finite extension tests do not rely on it.
- Hiroaki Taniguchi, [*D-property for APN functions from $\mathbb F_2^n$ to $\mathbb F_2^{n+1}$*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-023-00627-5), treats restrictions of Gold maps to the binary trace-zero hyperplane. Claude Carlet and Enrico Piccione, [*On vectorial functions mapping strict affine subspaces of their domain into strict affine subspaces of their co-domain, and the strong D-property*](https://doi.org/10.3934/amc.2024025), [full-text author preprint](https://opencryptology.org/2023/928/versions/20230615T070428Z.pdf), Proposition 5.4, Remark 5.5 and Sec. 5.3 Theorem 5: for the Gold cube they imply surjectivity of $(x,y)\mapsto (x+y)^3+x^3+y^3=xy(x+y)$ on the **binary trace-zero hyperplane** for $m=6$ or $m\ge8$. Indeed for quadratic maps their restricted D-property is surjectivity of the polar image; for Gold maps it suffices to test this hyperplane. Thus the binary large-degree nonexistence implication is already available from their theorem once (3) is applied. The binary exception at $m=7$ is also noted computationally (a missing nonzero polar value) in [*On Dillon's property of $(n,m)$-functions*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-024-00730-1); our small tables are explicit rechecks, not priority claims for binary D-properties. The all-prime-power determinant count and the extension statement are separate.
- Henning Stichtenoth, [*Algebraic Function Fields and Codes*, 2nd ed.](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-76878-4), Sec. 3.4–3.5 (Hurwitz genus formula, higher ramification and Hilbert's different formula) and Sec. 5.2 (Hasse–Weil). The local contributions for the curve in this note are derived explicitly rather than assuming an unramified or tame cover.
- Giovanni Longobardi and Valentina Pepe, [arXiv:2609.35617v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35617v2), on the possible projective point sets of maximum scattered linear sets in degree five, following [Lia–Longobardi–Zanella, arXiv:2507.23409v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.23409v2), Corollary 7.2. The distinction between point-set equivalence and equivalence of defining vector subspaces is discussed explicitly in the Introduction of 2609.35617v2. None of our claims outside the binary case assumes that point-set classification alone implies $\Gamma\mathrm L$ equivalence of subspaces. Julien Molina, [arXiv:2602.10929v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10929v2), Sec. 5, computes the ordinary generalized rank weight distributions of MRD codes; this is a different issue and we make no new claim about Sec. 5(v) of Bartoli et al.

Bounded searches used the source arXiv number and authors, the norm-one trace-zero MRD binomial and Moore determinant, rank-$m-1$ scattered spaces, binary Gold restricted D-properties, and Artin–Schreier trace cutoffs. The strongest identified overlap is the binary D-property above and the established full-graph theory. No exhaustive priority claim is made. Non-scatteredness is a statement only about the corresponding rank distance, not about a cryptographic scheme.
