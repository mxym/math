# Source and scope

1. Jack Doerner, Iftach Haitner, Yuval Ishai and Nikolaos Makriyannis,
   *From OT to OLE with Subquadratic Communication*, [IACR ePrint
   2025/1722](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1722), revised 2026-01-05,
   [fixed public PDF mirror](https://opencryptology.org/2025/1722/versions/20260428T172000Z.pdf).
   See §3.1 for notation and §5.3.5, paper p. 39 (PDF p. 42),
   Definition 5.36 and Conjecture 5.37. Bibliographic proceedings record:
   [CCS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3719027.3765225). The numbering
   and statement cited here are the public ePrint's, not an assertion
   about the proceedings text. The public text explicitly writes
   \(a,b\in\mathbb N\) and \(\widehat\alpha-\alpha\).
2. [AICR c/0074](https://aicr.info/c/0074/) is a secondary problem
   transcription. It already identifies the sign reversal, changes
   the sign in its transcription, and retains the natural-coefficient
   comparator. Sign recognition is not credited as new here. The
   fixed-domain obstruction and the rational formulation are separate.
3. Bounded check on 2026-11-27: examined the ePrint revision record,
   fixed text and public problem page; searched the exact title
   “Distance from lines is resilient to modulo,” “Conjecture 5.37,”
   and those terms with “rational,” “counterexample,” “modular,” and
   “determinant.” No matching correction or theorem in this precise
   weighted-agreement formulation was located. The determinant
   identity and counting prime divisors are standard; in particular,
   the source's Proposition 3.6 bounds collisions by applying a
   prime-divisor count to differences. The new use here is the
   weighted-incidence witness for an optimized comparison line.
4. J. Barkley Rosser and Lowell Schoenfeld, *Approximate formulas for
   some functions of prime numbers*, Illinois J. Math. 6 (1962), 64–94,
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1215/ijm/1255631807), Corollary 1.
   It gives the prime-counting inequalities used after (2) with
   natural logarithms; no prime-density estimate is new here.

This note neither supplies nor assesses a security guarantee for
any protocol in the source, and the rational comparator is proposed
as a mathematical formulation in this note, not attributed to
the source authors.
