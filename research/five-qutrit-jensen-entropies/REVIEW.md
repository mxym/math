# Scope of the independent internal review

**Result: no proof gap found in the two baseline statements.** The mathematical draft and this separate adversarial check were AI-assisted. Neither the read-through nor the accompanying scripts constitute external peer review, formal verification, or certification of priority.

1. **States and spectra.** In units of $1/120$, the pole spectra are $(12,12,96)$ and $(8,8,104)$, and the trine spectra are $(8,12,100)$. They are strictly positive and have trace one. Individual matrix entries need not be rational. Independently factoring all 25 upper-block characteristic polynomials gives the signed spectral multiplicities $(-6,24,-36,24,-6)$ at $8,9,10,11,12$. The flag gives $(9,-36,54,-36,9)$ at $96,98,100,102,104$. These are multiplicities in the **signed contrast**, not probabilities.
2. **Sign and factor of 16.** The zero sum cancels the endpoint traces, so the entropy-gap contrast is *minus* the negative-entropy midpoint contrast. The two fourth differences have respective steps $t/2$ and $t$, and hence integral volumes $t^4/16$ and $t^4$. For Tsallis negative entropy the fourth derivative is positive decreasing. The ratio at the worst endpoints of the two intervals is $8^{4-q}>64>24$, larger than the factor required to dominate the flag. At $q=1$ the derivative is $2/z^3$. At $q=2$ the contrast is not strictly positive: the divergence itself is exactly one quarter of squared Hilbert–Schmidt distance.
3. **Rényi quantifiers.** The upper-block trace contrast for $z^q$ is strictly positive when $0<q<1$, because the fourth derivative is negative. The trace-one embedding does not commute with the logarithm, so an additional step is needed. With $q$ fixed, expanding the trace of the midpoint power and then the logarithm gives an $O_q(\varepsilon+\varepsilon^{2q})$ remainder. This is $o_q(\varepsilon^q)$ over the 25 pairs. It is not an estimate uniform in $q$ near either endpoint. The review covers only the order-dependent assertion in [Section 3](PROOF.md#3-rényi-choose-the-scale-after-choosing-the-order).
4. **Prior work.** [Sra's Theorem B.1 and Proposition 2.5](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00602v1) respectively supply the four-point lower bound on witness size and the five upper-block geometry for log-determinant divergence. [Zhang's Example 9.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08164v1#S9.SS3) already uses the scalar supplement and gives a strict five-point contrast for Shannon, hence also for both families in a neighborhood of $q=1$. His published matrices give $\sum_i c_i P_i=\operatorname{diag}(1/4,0,-1/4)$, so their order-two Tsallis contrast is $-\tfrac12\|\sum_i c_iP_i\|_\mathrm{HS}^2=-1/16$ with unnormalized trace. By continuity that particular contrast is nonpositive near two. See [the source comparison](README.md#what-was-known) for additional classical and qubit results.
