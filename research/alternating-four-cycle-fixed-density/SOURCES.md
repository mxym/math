# Problem, prior identities and scope

* **Question answered.** József Balogh, Bernard Lidický, Dhruv Mubayi, Florian Pfender and Jan Volec, [*Semi-Inducibility of some small graphs*, arXiv:2601.03433v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03433v1), Definitions 1–2, Question 1, Theorem 1.3, §4. This is the source of the proposed near-equal red clique construction and of the positive reciprocal-density results. The two decimal comparisons in [WITNESS.md](WITNESS.md) refer specifically to outputs reported after Figure 4 of v1, not to Theorem 1.3 or to a verified numerical certificate. The [author-hosted manuscript](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~mubayi/papers/Semi_Inducibility.pdf) is also publicly accessible.
* **Broader problem not answered.** Abdul Basit, Bertille Granet, Daniel Horsley, André Kündgen and Katherine Staden, [*The semi-inducibility problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09842), Problem 9.3 asks for the maximum over all red/blue hosts at each prescribed density. Refuting a proposed maximizer does not determine this function. The unrestricted optimum is different. Ordinary induced path density is a different problem again.
* **2-switch interpretation and prior identities.** An alternating four-cycle presents a possible 2-switch. Adrián Pastine and Victor N. Schvöllner, [*The 2-switch-degree of a graph*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23327), Theorem 6.1, Corollary 6.2 and Proposition 6.6, give the corresponding induced-subgraph count, complement invariance and component decomposition. In their convention each switch yields four labelled copies of the fixed alternating pattern used here. Thus the colour symmetry and the fact that cross-component contributions depend only on the two edge counts are not new. See also Michael D. Barrus, [*On 2-switches and isomorphism classes*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2012.04.014). The application here is a strict, density-preserving replacement of two unequal red cliques.
* **Nearby questions.** [Deng and Hou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23351) determine profiles for the semi-induced four-vertex star. [Deng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27911) treats the blue–blue–red path at unrestricted density. [Bodnár and Pikhurko](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24336) treat unrestricted four-vertex semi-inducibility. None of these statements evaluates the fixed-density alternating four-cycle profile.
