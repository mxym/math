For p<1 the expectation over a uniform tagged leaf counts an m-clade with probability m/n. The exact harmonic-size chain gives, conditionally on entry, a geometric bound at the dyadic hazard on visits below the next bin; returns upward cannot occur. Weighted bin sums of L^q norms converge when p<1, and the constant-size variances and boundedness give convergence for finite truncations in all L^q. At p=1 the statistic per leaf equals the quenched conditional mean depth; the annealed depth estimates and higher moments of maximum depth give the asserted L^q via conditional Jensen and interpolation.

For p>1, the Ma Lemmas 2.1–2.3 coupling indeed provides the conservative interval paintbox, conditionally independent unit-exponential rescaled clocks, and exact e^(−t) survival for two sampled tags. The finite-block size-biased tag formula converts the empirical p-sum to a conditional mean of (M/n)^(p−1) over nonsingleton M. Using M≥2 permits the stated factor of two bound by (M−1)/(n−1), concavity handles r in (0,1], and the resulting L^q time envelope is integrable uniformly in n. At any fixed time, empirical masses converge in ℓ¹ by truncating true masses; H_{m−1}/log n tends to one on each positive mass. The random integral therefore converges in each L^q by Minkowski and dominated convergence. Conditional clock variance bounded by B_n/log n gives L² de-Poissonization; bounded higher norms and interpolation transfer it to all finite L^q. These are fixed real regimes. The integral itself was previously identified by Bertoin ALEA 2012 §3.4 as the area at index 1−p, and its exact continuum moments are not new. Both the left/right normalization of the dislocation measure and the variance algebra check (p=2 gives mean 1, variance 2/11). Fuchs–Steel Thm 11 and preceding moments already yield the integer-p annealed mean by the MRCA counting relation, not the tree-wide conditional averaged limit. Ma’s clock device and BCM weighted sums likewise require explicit credit.

For the window, normalized sample log-size at time v log n tends to (1−v ζ(2))_+: the tagged mass subordinator SLLN inserts log p/log n→−v ζ(2), then conditional binomial concentration for v ζ(2)<1 or Markov for v ζ(2)≥1. The harmonic number replacement error is uniformly O(1/log n). The exchangeable-tag Jensen step is valid without independence of the average, and the survival-derived bound for v≥1 is integrable. The change of variables gives ζ(2)^(−1) ∫_0^1 x f(x) dx. The independent conditional clocks have squared-weight sum bounded by ||f||∞² X_n(1)/log² n; conditional Jensen plus interpolation applies even for signed f. On compact λ ranges, Lipschitz constant of the random transform is controlled by X_n(1) in L^q, so a finite mesh proves uniformity. This is a log-size profile, distinct from decrement or fluctuation analyses.
