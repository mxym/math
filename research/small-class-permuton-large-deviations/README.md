# A speed-$n$ permuton LDP for every small permutation class

Take a uniform permutation of length $n$ from any infinite hereditary class of upper growth strictly below $\kappa\simeq2.20557$. Its permuton satisfies a large-deviation principle at speed $n$ along **all** sizes. This gives a positive family for [Borga–Féray–Winkler, Problem 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12104v1#S8), alongside the prior geometric-class result and the known failure of universal speed $n$. It does not classify all exponentially growing classes.

The [proof](LDP.md) first treats two fixed MSO-defined orders on all positions of a uniform regular word. That statement is by supported length residue; a bijection onto a hereditary permutation class removes the residue dependence. Cutting a word into $K$ equal-sized blocks loses at most $1/K+o(1)$ in the permuton metric. In distinct blocks, even the *atypical* order comparisons are known exactly from the two marked positions' finite states. Each block's entropy is maximized over feasible circulations on a finite graph. Equations (4)–(5), or the equivalent local maximization immediately after (5), specify the rate. This is a variational formula, not an assertion of a closed elementary expression or fast numerical algorithm. Neither the group-buffer proof of typical shapes nor independent-sampling entropy relative to a fixed permuton proves this tail result.
