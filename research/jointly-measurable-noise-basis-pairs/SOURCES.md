# What was known, and what is established here

* [Designolle, Farkas & Kaniewski, *Incompatibility robustness of quantum measurements: a unified framework*, New J. Phys. 21, 113053 (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.00448). Equations (44)–(45) define the two-parent, compatible-noise problem; (50) gives an earlier universal lower bound. Equations (59)–(64) give the parent of the **mixture** used in this proof, constructed for generalized robustness. Equations (69), (73)–(75), (77) give the exact mutually unbiased basis (MUB) and qubit results. Section IV.C.2 after Eq. (84) conjectures optimality of pairs of MUBs among all pairs of rank-one projective measurements under jointly measurable robustness. That conjecture is the question addressed here. The same section's separate conjectures for depolarizing noise and probabilistic noise are not consequences.
* [Carmeli, Heinosaari & Toigo, *Quantum incompatibility witnesses*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 122, 130402 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.130402), and DFK (74): DFK credit this work for prior use of their parent for the complementary mutually unbiased measurements. The MUB pair's exact jointly measurable robustness is not new.
* [Kiukas, McNulty & Pellonpää, *Amount of quantum coherence needed for measurement incompatibility*, Theorems 1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.07239). Their Theorem 1 and DFK (74) already imply the **one-sided compatibility** used here. In the $P$ basis, the correlation matrix with diagonal 1 and other entries $-1/(d-1)$ defines the Schur multiplier $X\mapsto(d\Phi_P(X)-X)/(d-1)$. Applied to a mutually unbiased basis $Q^0$, this gives $(I-Q^0_j)/(d-1)$. DFK (74), normalized as a noise POVM, makes this compatible with the diagonal complement $(I-P_i)/(d-1)$. Kiukas et al. transfer that compatibility from a maximally coherent basis to any measurement in the second argument. Our finite Kraus sum is a direct proof of the needed one-sided fact, not a priority claim for it.
* [Tavakoli, Farkas, Rosset, Bancal & Kaniewski, *Mutually unbiased bases and symmetric informationally complete measurements in Bell experiments*, Appendix B.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.03225). Their formulas (B76)–(B81) treat jointly measurable robustness of the special class of mutually unbiased measurements (including higher-rank examples). Proposition B.12 gives a **generalized-noise** universal lower bound; its Eq. (B84) specializes at equal outcome counts to the same mixture parent as used here. That is not a universal compatible-noise parent for its residuals.
* [Designolle, *Most incompatible measurements and sum-of-squares optimisation* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.10381) develops universal bounds for several noise models, including an all-binary observation; [Designolle & Farkas, *k-fold unbiased measurements and maximal incompatibility* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20728) treats, in particular, generalized robustness for three unbiased measurements. [McNulty, Townsend & Kiukas (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21169) concerns a fixed Schur decoherence channel. We did not find the rank-one basis-pair minimax statement under jointly measurable noise in these sources.

**Increment and limits.** The mixture parent, the one-sided joint compatibility, the MUB values and the qubit optimum are earlier work. Decomposing the residuals of that parent and crossing the one-sided joints gives their missing joint parent for arbitrary pairs of bases. This note does not classify extremizers, equate generalized and jointly measurable robustness for every pair, or optimize over arbitrary-outcome-count POVMs at fixed dimension.
