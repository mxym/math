# Sources and prior-work boundary

* [Mostafa Hayajneh, Saja Hayajneh and Fuad Kittaneh, *On some classical trace inequalities and a new Hilbert–Schmidt norm inequality*, Math. Inequal. Appl. 21 (2018), 1175–1183](https://files.ele-math.com/articles/mia-21-81.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7153/mia-2018-21-81). Conjecture 2 (p. 1177) contains the logarithm and the two-interval spectral condition. Theorem 3 (p. 1180) proves its Hilbert–Schmidt case; Theorem 2 in that same section is an ordinary trace comparison. Neither theorem is contradicted here.
* **Distinct nonlogarithmic question.** Conjecture 1 on the same page compares two powers $s,t$ with no logarithm. [trureturing's dossier](https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Blueprint/D5/S3/Quantum/Matrix/HayajnehConjectureRefutation.html) already gives a trace-norm counterexample to that question at $(1/2,3/4)$, with two rational positive definite matrices. That example also uses the standard two-singular-value identity; this identity is not a contribution of the present note. [Repository PR #98](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/98) classifies the norms for the nonlogarithmic comparison at a fixed interior pair and explicitly distinguishes Conjecture 2. Neither result exhibits the logarithmic counterexample proved here.
* The authors' [*New Hilbert–Schmidt norm inequalities for positive semidefinite matrices* (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43036-023-00251-3) presents a generalization comparing sums of two squared norms. In the displayed comparison in its abstract, equating the two positive matrices and taking the middle matrix Hermitian makes the two summands adjoints before taking norms. That specializes to nonlogarithmic Conjecture 1, not logarithmic Conjecture 2.
* [Liu, Wang and Sun (2017), p. 1019 and Theorem 1](https://files.ele-math.com/articles/jmi-11-77.pdf) affirm a different conjecture also numbered “2”, for four positive semidefinite matrices with pairwise commutations as specified there; there is no logarithm. [S. Hayajneh (2024), *On a trace inequality due to Ando–Hiai–Okubo trace inequalities*](https://www.pmf.ni.ac.rs/filomat-content/2024/38-19/38-19-20-22715.pdf) compares traces of products of matrix powers. These statements are distinct from a comparison of sums of singular values.
* A citation-index cross-check also identified [*Remarks on a Question of Bourin for Positive Semidefinite Matrices*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00025-023-01870-1) and [*Norm inequalities for positive semidefinite matrices and a question of Bourin III*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11117-022-00895-0). The questions in their publisher abstracts concern expressions in powers of two matrices compared with bounds involving their sum, rather than the logarithmic comparison here.

**External search scope.** The original statements and published proofs were checked in the 2018 paper. Searches on 18 November 2026 combined the article title, authors, DOI, conjecture number, “logarithmic”, “log A”, “mixed-power”, “unitarily invariant”, “trace norm” and “counterexample”; the named trureturing result and citing records from Semantic Scholar and OpenCitations were also screened. No prior counterexample to the precise 2018 logarithmic conjecture was identified in that material. Some related works were assessed via their publisher abstracts, and indexing and nomenclature vary. This is not an exhaustive priority search.
