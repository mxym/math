# Prior work, scope and search limits

* [Busch and Schmidt, Coexistence of qubit effects](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-009-0109-x), [arXiv Theorem 3, (55)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.4167): necessary and sufficient pair criterion, including biased and singular effects. The determinant radical uses its nonnegative square root. This note factorizes that known criterion; it does not supply a new pairwise coexistence condition. [Yu, Liu, Li and Oh](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.1538) give an alternative criterion used in the independent exact regression.
* [Forster (2002)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022000002000193) supplies the spectral lower bound for strict sign-rank. [Alon, Moran and Yehudayoff, Appendix A](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v49/alon16.pdf) give an additional derivation. Neither that lower bound nor the large sign-rank of a Hadamard sign matrix is a new result here.
* [Paley (1933)](https://doi.org/10.1002/sapm1933121311) gives the seed construction. [Goethals and Seidel](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1970-067-9) and [Kharaghani, Cor. 2](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788700028044) give symmetric Hadamard square matrices with constant diagonal; the reshuffled tensor-product construction is classical. The application is to pairwise compatibility of **unsharp as well as sharp** binary qubit measurements.
* [Heunen, Fritz and Reyes (2014), especially Thm. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.3698) already rule out universal realization by **sharp PVMs** in fixed dimension, using differing neighborhoods of action vertices among control vertices. The optional all-subsets example in our proof uses the same combinatorial idea. Their sharp-measurement bound does not apply to arbitrary binary POVMs. [Kunjwal, Heunen and Fritz (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5948) give all finite joint-measurability structures in growing Hilbert dimension.
* [Andrejić and Kunjwal (2020), Defs. 1–3 and Conj. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.00785) study many binary examples (notably all four-vertex structures), but the conjecture itself does not fix the number of POVM outcomes. [Kunjwal (2025), Defs. 5.1–2 and §5.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.10063) restates it for finite N. The positive finite-outcome construction in [repository PR #118](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/118) grows the alphabet with the input complex; the present binary impossibility concerns a stricter question.
* [Grinko and Uola](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07711) give an unbiased finite-family criterion and a biased necessary condition; [Yoshino et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38836) treat coplanar unbiased measurements. [McNulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15954) uses graphs of anti-commutation to study robustness, not the complete pair-compatibility sign table. Qualitative fixed-complexity semialgebraic graph counting is standard (see [Sauermann](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.11575)); it does not give this explicit sign-rank inequality or these graphs by itself.
* Searched combinations of qubit effects, finite binary POVMs, coexistence/compatibility/incompatibility graphs, forbidden realizations, sign-rank, Forster, Hadamard, measurement dimension, VC dimension and arbitrary joint-measurability structure. No earlier presentation of this particular application was located. This is not an exhaustive priority determination.
