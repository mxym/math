# Model, priority boundaries and direct width-two implication

**Earlier permutation results.** [Hoffman, Rizzolo and Slivken, §2.1 and Theorem 2.1, arXiv:1911.04982v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.04982v4) give the canonical layer pair of words used here and the unweighted path fluctuations about the diagonal as the eigenvalues of a traceless Hermitian Brownian bridge. Donsker scaling and the type A chamber in the unweighted model are not new. Their untilted weak path convergence by itself does not estimate probabilities with the stretched-exponential precision at issue here. [Regev](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(81)90012-8) supplies more than the polynomial-accuracy unweighted normalization used in (2). [Janson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.08427) obtains inversion and other pattern-count distribution limits for uniform 321 avoidance on their typical scales. [Diaconis–Graham](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1977.tb01624.x) compare Kendall distance and Spearman's footrule; only the elementary inequality footrule $\le2\operatorname{inv}$ is needed here, not a new distance bound.

[Budzinski, Dubach, Féray, Kammoun and Maïda](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23751v1), [published conference version](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2026.18), study the weak Mallows bias at forbidden length three, with explicit bias-dependent limit shapes and exponential free energies (full preprint Theorem 7 and Proposition 8). Repository [#243](../monotone-pattern-permuton-deviations/README.md) obtains the full fixed-monotone-family permuton LDP and its exponential-order free energy, including $2\log d$ for the sign here. Repository [#246](../monotone-pattern-mallows-prefactor/README.md) treats the **opposite sign**, $q=e^{+\beta/n}>1$, and obtains a polynomial correction with a transmission determinant. It does not imply the negative-tilt spectral coefficient by continuation in the bias. The exact pointwise decomposition/localization of the canonical score, rough-penalty control of unbounded incoming states and the two-half matching construction in GROUND.md concern this new finite-permutation transfer.

**Width two already reduces to a classical tilted path.** [Cheng, Elizalde, Kasraoui and Sagan](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2013.07.026) study the 321 inversion polynomials via two-coloured Motzkin paths and other encodings. In the two-word encoding the relative low-letter count $h$ goes up/down/stays according to the two letters. Matching upper-letter ordinals strictly in the two words is exactly a nonnegative excursion with two flat-step colours except that only one is allowed at zero. Its area $\sum_t h(t)$ is the inversion count, by summing the difference of low-letter positions. Dividing the total weight by $4^n$ gives a lazy walk of increments $\pm1,0$ with probabilities $1/4,1/4,1/2$ and a reduction of the flat probability at zero.

[Hryniv–Velenik](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0310217) establish the $\lambda^{-1/3}$ height scale for ordinary positive walks with linear area tilt. For the precise constant [Ioffe–Shlosman–Velenik, *An invariance principle to Ferrari–Spohn diffusions*, Theorem 2](https://www.unige.ch/~velenik/papers/ISV14.pdf) prove convergence both of the rescaled principal log-eigenvalue and of the rescaled normalized ground state of the symmetric positive-half-line transfer with linear area cost $\lambda h$. Here $\lambda=b/n$ and step variance is $1/2$, so their Airy operator is $-\tfrac14\partial_h^2+h$ after scaling and has eigenvalue $4^{-1/3}|a_1|$.

For clarity on finite length and the different zero boundary, write $T_\lambda$ for that strictly positive-half-line transfer, with symmetric half-potentials, spectral radius $r_\lambda$, and normalized Perron vector $v_\lambda$. Allowing both zero flats and shifting heights up one gives $4^{-n}Z_n\le e^{\lambda n}r_\lambda^n$. For fixed small $\eta>0$ their ground-state convergence gives some height $h\le\eta\lambda^{-1/3}$ with $v_\lambda(h)^2\ge c_\eta\lambda^{1/3}$, using the positive limiting mass on $[\eta/2,\eta]$. Force $h$ initial up and $h$ terminal down steps, and in between stay strictly positive. The middle $(h,h)$ entry of $T_\lambda^{n-2h}$ is at least $v_\lambda(h)^2 r_\lambda^{n-2h}$, because the symmetric lazy-walk transfer is positive semidefinite (the free lazy convolution is, as are its compression and the multiplications on both sides by the same positive diagonal). Entrance and exit cost at worst a factor $4^{-2h}e^{-O(\lambda h^2)}$. Dividing log costs by $n^{1/3}$, taking $n\to\infty$ and then $\eta\downarrow0$, recovers the coefficient in (1) at $d=2$ from this prior result. No claim of first discovery for the rank-two coefficient or its immediate tilting corollaries.

**Many-line and spectral ancestry.** [Ioffe–Velenik–Wachtel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04444) analyze noncolliding line ensembles above a hard wall with area self-potentials, obtaining Dyson Ferrari–Spohn limits. The $B$-by-$B^2$ scaling, killed Brownian Feynman–Kac operators and ground-state method for such transfers are direct precedents, not a new general diffusion principle. Here the underlying finite objects are uncoloured permutations: matching equal ordinals is nonlocal in word time, their difference walk has correlated coordinates on a trace-zero hyperplane, and its effective potential is the sum of gaps over *pairs*, not a common one-body wall potential. The ground-state-squared bulk law is familiar in Ferrari–Spohn theory; MESOSCOPIC.md transfers that density to the empirically sampled signed displacement of the actual permutation and its canonical layer, not to the process at a specified time. The proof does not infer an exponentially small finite-permutation probability from a usual weak invariance principle for whole conditioned paths.
