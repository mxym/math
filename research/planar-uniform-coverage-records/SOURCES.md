# Sources and scope (screened 19 November 2026)

* [Penrose, *Record times for coverage thresholds and maximal spacings*, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19104v3#S2) is the primary statement. Its [version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19104) lists v1 on August 19 and v3 on September 16, 2026. Theorems 2.1–2.3 handle interior/spacing records and full-domain records with strict dominance. Immediately after Theorem 2.3, the author separates the uniform planar full-domain case, predicts the boundary constant for k ≥ 2, and asks for a perimeter-dependent mixture at k = 1. We use his geometric uniqueness (Prop. 5.2), radius strong law and exponential moment bounds (§4, especially Lemma 4.10). His §3 already gives a compensator and martingale approach; our use of martingales is not itself a new principle.
* [Penrose, *Random Euclidean coverage from within*, Theorem 3.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-022-01182-5) already supplies the one-time planar mixed radius law for smooth domains. [Penrose–Yang, *Random coverage of a manifold with boundary*, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19278v2#S2) specifies the boundary coefficient in Theorem 2.1 and interior intensity in Proposition 2.11. The authors already describe the interior versus boundary interpretation in Remark 2.3. These formulas are inputs, not claimed as new. They do not by themselves identify a record frequency for nested samples. The unrelated intermediate-target question in their Remark 2.4 is not answered here.
* [Penrose–Higgs, *Random coverage from within with variable radii, and Johnson–Mehl cover times*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10687-026-00529-8) studies fixed-time coverage probabilities for random radii and growth cover times. It does not analyze the strict-decrease count in one nested sequence of equal-radius coverage thresholds. We do not settle the general variable-radius regularity question. [Deheuvels–Devroye](https://luc.devroye.org/devroye-maxkspacing.pdf) study strong laws for maximal one-dimensional spacings, not this planar record count.
* Searches for the exact paper and identifiers, the conjecture's terms, perimeter-dependent record counts, cover-radius records, maximal spacings, and competing interior/boundary winners included the July–19 November 2026 window and a follow-up search restricted to dates after v3. The above are the closest located primary results. [Submission histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19278) distinguish the July 2026 Penrose–Yang revision from the September 2026 Penrose record paper. No exact parallel solution was identified. Unindexed, private or inaccessible results are not ruled out.
