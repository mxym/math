# Source locations and attribution

- **Fionnuala Curran, [Quantum randomness under unambiguous attacks, arXiv:2601.16343v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16343v4).** Use v4, not the January v1. Section 2, Eq. (3) fixes the inconclusive probability and maximizes unconditional correct probability. Section 3.1, Theorem 3 and Eqs. (14)–(19), give the value and weighted-diagonal iff over rank-one PVMs in the range stated immediately before that theorem. Appendix C.2 gives the filtered lower bound and its exact sum-of-squares remainder at (129)–(141) and dual at (142)–(149). In the shared full-rank range, its $\Lambda\sqrt\rho$ is exactly this note's $A_Q$. Eq. (132) explicitly uses full rank; Eq. (142) divides by the square roots of tail eigenvalues. The last paragraph of Sec. 4 identifies non-full-rank states, more general measurements, and maximum confidence as directions to explore. This note gives a direct support-space adaptation of the FRIO theorem to the singular zero-padded Naimark problem, not a novelty claim for the full-rank projective formula, nor an analysis of the paper's QKD application or its unambiguous, joint device, or noise models.
- **Kenji Nakahira, Tsuyoshi Sasaki Usuda and Kentaro Kato, [Discrimination between geometrically uniform quantum states with inconclusive results](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.86.032316), Phys. Rev. A 86, 032316 (2012).** Theorem 2 is the source of Curran's filtered/clipped decomposition, explicitly cited next to Curran's Eq. (129). The published abstract confirms the analytical all-rate result for a fixed geometrically uniform pure-state set; in particular the cyclic/Fourier symmetric attaining ensemble is within that discrimination setting. That is an optimization over the discriminator for a given symmetric ensemble, not the informationally complete measurement optimization or simultaneous balance statements below. **E. Bagan, R. Muñoz-Tapia, G. A. Olivares-Rentería and J. A. Bergou, [arXiv:1206.4145v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.4145v1), Eqs. (6)–(7)**, also reduce arbitrary FRIO to a filter followed by minimum-error discrimination. The filter paradigm is prior work.
- **Ulrike Herzog, [arXiv:1206.4412v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.4412v3), Sec. IV.B (HTML IV.2), Eqs. (65)–(68).** Gives the same piecewise value over the full reject range for the fixed symmetric pure ensembles with two distinct coefficient magnitudes, including more labels than the support dimension. Gives arbitrary symmetric amplitudes at the minimum-error and maximum-confidence endpoints. **L. F. Melo, O. Jiménez and L. Neves, [arXiv:2502.00148v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.00148v1), Sec. II.3, Eqs. (6)–(13)**, explicitly allow linearly dependent cyclic equiprobable states ($N\ge n$ in their notation) and give state-separation/conclusive-discrimination constructions for them. Their earlier [Phys. Rev. Research 5, 043149 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043149), Sec. II, treats $N$ symmetric qubit states. Neither consideration of dependent symmetric ensembles nor their use to attain symmetric FRIO values is new here.
- **Sarah Croke, Erika Andersson, Stephen M. Barnett, Claire R. Gilson and John Jeffers, [Maximum Confidence Quantum Measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0604026)**, formulate maximum-confidence discrimination. **Herzog, [arXiv:1202.5435v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1202.5435v2)**, optimizes its failure rate and treats arbitrary equiprobable symmetric pure ensembles including linearly dependent ones. The rank/support maximum-confidence plateau for the symmetric attaining family is not new; a general rank-one frame with nonuniform support overlaps need not be optimal there.
- **Shuyang Meng, Fionnuala Curran et al., [arXiv:2307.15708v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15708v2), Phys. Rev. A 110, L010403 (2024).** The $Q=0$ projective endpoint is already known. See also the repository's pinned [weighted MIC theorem and its source note](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/8a7a0af8cf975ea707c6eaa7a645efe6ad1d85ee/research/weighted-mic-randomness-attainment): this earlier construction yields an extremal rank-one MIC with a uniform diagonal against any prescribed nonzero PSD weight. We use it as an input, not as a newly derived construction. The related [Gutierrez Anco–Nemoz–Brown](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16447v2) work discusses secure randomness under different, stronger side-information assumptions; those are not assumed here.

The additional conclusions of this note concern the statistical and tomographic constraints on **one** trusted rank-one measurement: balance against every distinct positive spectral projector is necessary and sufficient for simultaneous FRIO optimality, with the corresponding two-rate lower frame bound and nonattained MIC minimax infimum. Searches combining FRIO and fixed-rate discrimination with informational completeness, intrinsic randomness, tight frames and simultaneous optimality did not reveal these statements in the papers above. The [official Curran dissertation defense abstract](https://www.icfo.eu/es/event/4346/fionnuala-curran/) describes FRIO for projective measurements, separately from its discussion of extremal nonprojective measurements. The linked full dissertation was not accessible in this check; no exhaustive priority claim is made.
