# Sources and exact scope

- Sudharshan Rajagopalan and Nitin Saurabh, [*On Non-Deterministic Representation of Boolean Functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11966), [arXiv:2611.11966v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11966v1), 9 November 2026. §3 Proposition 22 proves only singleton indicators attain maximal degree n; Lemma 4 gives the exact-nonzero nullspace condition, and Lemma 24 gives its unit-vector/row-space criterion. These are the direct precedents for the cube case of the single-hit identity, not results we claim as new. Fact 13 is the familiar maximum-of-complement formula for rational degree. In contrast to their Theorem 3, our formula is a deterministic upper envelope at each support cardinality, not a statement about the random-support average or its asymptotics.
- Anurag Bishnoi, Pete L. Clark, Aditya Potukuchi and John R. Schmitt, [*On zeros of a polynomial in a finite grid*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06020), Combin. Probab. Comput. 27(3), 310–333 (2018), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548317000566). Theorem 1.2 and its grid-polynomial discussion abstract the exact Alon–Füredi minimum nonzero support size with individual degrees. That minimum-support input and top-coefficient interpolation are standard. Here we apply them to exact supports via the dual single-hit obstruction, yielding the complete worst-case nondeterministic degree profile and a critical-support description.
- Alexandr Valyuzhenich, [*Optimal functions with spectral constraints in hypercubes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10995), [v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.10995v1), *Discrete Mathematics* 347(3), 113816 (2024), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2023.113816). His Theorem 2(2) at the lower spectral endpoint 0 is precisely the minimum-support classification used in §4. We use the supports of his blocks, not their scalar normalizations. The extra step here is the deletion-of-one-point classification for *maximal nondeterministic degree at the critical cardinality* and the count by signed coordinate partitions.
- Robin Kothari, Matt Kovacs-Deak, Daochen Wang and Rain Zimin Yang, [*Rational degree is polynomially related to degree*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08727), arXiv:2601.08727v3, 28 September 2026. The independent general questions about rational degree, deterministic query complexity, and effective hypercube Nullstellensatz are not resolved by the present support-size calculation.

A bounded check on 2026-11-27 used “non-deterministic degree”/“nondeterministic degree” with “support cardinality”, “maximal”, “support size”, “Alon–Füredi”, the exact Rajagopalan–Saurabh title or Proposition 22, and weighted finite-grid evaluation duality. It found the primary ingredients above but no matching exact worst-case profile and all critical support counts. The arguments here are elementary combinations of those ingredients. No priority claim is made.
