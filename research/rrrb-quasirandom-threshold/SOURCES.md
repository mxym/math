# Sources and scope

1. Abdul Basit, Bertille Granet, Daniel Horsley, André Kündgen and Katherine Staden, [*The semi-inducibility problem*, arXiv:2501.09842v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09842v2). Theorem 1.9 treats the RRRB-pattern without a prescribed density. Theorem 2.7 fixes the fraction of **red** pairs and establishes quasirandom optimality, stability and a finite error estimate for $p\ge(1+\sqrt2)/4$. Theorem 2.6 gives the resulting positive quantum-graph example. Their §7.2 uses a degree/codegree relaxation; §9.1 asks whether their density interval can be extended. Our contribution here is a sharp asymptotic threshold, the nonunique endpoint, and the profile on $[1/2,1]$, not the original question or the previously established high-density result. We do not claim the finite constants in their theorem.
2. Xizhi Liu, Dhruv Mubayi and Christian Reiher, [*The feasible region of induced graphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.16203), develop the surrounding fixed-density framework. The matrix manipulations and inequalities in our proof are elementary; no general-purpose operator inequality is claimed as new.

Related but different objectives include [Balogh–Lidický–Mubayi–Pfender–Volec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03433) on prescribed-density patterns including the alternating four-cycle; [Bodnár–Pikhurko](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24336) on unrestricted semi-inducibility; [Deng–Hou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23351) on a fixed-density four-vertex star; and [Deng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27911) on a blue–blue–red path at unrestricted density. The repository's [alternating four-cycle note](../alternating-four-cycle-fixed-density/README.md) is about **RBRB**, not the three-red one-blue pattern studied here.
