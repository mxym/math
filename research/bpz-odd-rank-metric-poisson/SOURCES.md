# Research question and prior art

1. Martino Borello, Olga Polverino, Ferdinando Zullo, [*On the existence of linear rank-metric intersecting codes*](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02004v1), arXiv:2604.02004v1, 2 April 2026. Corollary 3.5 and §4 identify the extremal parameters and the equivalence with scattered dimension \(m+3\) in \(\mathbb F_{q^m}^3\). Corollary 4.2 already treats all even \(m\geq6\). Remark 4.4 and §6 (HTML lines 352–354 and 429) explicitly leave the odd-degree range open. The present result supplies all sufficiently large odd degrees for each fixed \(q\), plus three small examples at \(q=2\); it does not address the separate length \(2m-4\) question, nor assert every odd \(m\geq7\).
2. Stefano Lia, Giovanni Longobardi, Giuseppe Marino, Rocco Trombetti, [*Short rank-metric codes and scattered subspaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01315), [SIAM J. Discrete Math. 38 (2024), 2578–2598](https://doi.org/10.1137/23M1574749). Theorem 3.5 constructs scattered dimension \(m+2\) under explicit arithmetic conditions; that dimension is one less than the target here. The rank-weight and duality language used here is standard.
3. Alessandro Giannoni and Alessandro Neri, [*A probabilistic construction of maximum \(h\)-scattered subspaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32737), October 2026; Alessandro Giannoni, [*Evasive subspaces, minimal rank-metric codes and subspace designs via auxiliary fields*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08627v1), November 2026. These provide large-ambient-rank constructions (ambient dimension over the extension field grows, with extension degree fixed). They do not give the present theorem with ambient dimension three over the extension field and \(m\) growing. Giannoni's [*The extremal gap for scattered \(q\)-systems*](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23156v1) concerns a different cutoff \(h=m-3\) and different ambient dimensions.
4. Anina Gruica, Alberto Ravagnani, John Sheekey and Ferdinando Zullo, [*Rank-Metric Codes, Semifields, and the Average Critical Problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.07193), [SIAM J. Discrete Math. 37 (2023), 1079–1117](https://doi.org/10.1137/22M1486893); Anina Gruica, Anna-Lena Horlemann, Alberto Ravagnani and Nadja Willenborg, [*Densities of codes of various linearity degrees in translation-invariant metric spaces*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-023-01236-2). These give related density frameworks and discuss other asymptotic regimes. The matrix ensemble here is extension-field linear with both coordinate length and extension degree growing, a fixed number of rows and a fixed positive rank cutoff at the critical relation in Theorem 1. This is not the fixed-short-side MRD density problem and does not assert an MRD limit.
5. The exact presentation of the trace pairing, Gaussian binomial estimate and alternating inclusion-exclusion in the proof uses only standard finite-field linear algebra and elementary counting. Full attribution for the original extremal coding problem is in item 1.
