# Sources and priority

- **Yueying Lou and Qichun Wang, *Search for 9-Variable Boolean Functions with the Optimal Algebraic Immunity-Resiliency Trade-Off and High Nonlinearity*.** *IEICE Transactions on Fundamentals*, E107-A(8), 1382–1385 (2024), [DOI 10.1587/transfun.2023EAL2111](https://doi.org/10.1587/transfun.2023EAL2111), [publisher full text](https://globals.ieice.org/en_transactions/fundamentals/10.1587/transfun.2023EAL2111/_f). The open problem at the end of §3 asks for a 3-resilient nine-variable Boolean function of algebraic immunity 5 and nonlinearity greater than 224. The 224 result in the paper is for the full nine-coordinate rotation-symmetric class. Here a function invariant under a seven-cycle on only seven coordinates answers the unrestricted question.
- **Andrey Khalyavin, *The constructing of 3-resilient Boolean functions of 9 variables with nonlinearity 240*.** [IACR ePrint 2007/212](https://eprint.iacr.org/2007/212), [PDF](https://eprint.iacr.org/2007/212.pdf), §7, printed p. 6, second explicit example, the one with two seven-variable subfunctions having nonlinearity 56. [witness.txt](witness.txt) transcribes the 16 hexadecimal groups of that table verbatim, with only whitespace normalized; the ePrint lists the source as CC BY. Khalyavin established the function's spectral parameters, not the degree-four annihilator ranks checked here.
- **Ziad Saber, Mohammad Faisal Uddin and Amr Youssef, *On the Existence of (9, 3, 5, 240) Resilient Functions*.** *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 52(5), 2269–2270 (2006), [author-hosted paper](https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~youssef/Publications/Papers/On%20the%20existence%20of%209-3-5-240%20resilient%20functions.pdf). Provides earlier witnesses of degree 5, resiliency 3 and nonlinearity 240. Degree 5 is not itself evidence of algebraic immunity 5. Their data are not needed to run the certificate.
- **Palash Sarkar and Subhamoy Maitra, *Nonlinearity Bounds and Constructions of Resilient Boolean Functions*.** CRYPTO 2000, LNCS 1880, pp. 515–532, [paper](https://static.aminer.org/pdf/PDF/000/120/440/nonlinearity_bounds_and_constructions_of_resilient_boolean_functions.pdf). Its nonlinearity bounds include 240 for the nine-variable 3-resilient case. The needed divisibility bound is reproduced in elementary form in §1 of the proof.

The joint answer is a property check of a pre-existing function, not a new construction or a claim about maximum nonlinearity of arbitrary balanced nine-variable functions. Searches through 27 November 2026 for the Lou–Wang DOI and “3-resilient”, “240”, “algebraic immunity 5”, Khalyavin's witness word, and citation/Title combinations located the earlier spectral witnesses but no primary source stating the joint result for this witness.
