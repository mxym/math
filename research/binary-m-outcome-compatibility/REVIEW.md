# Internal review

**Scope:** one binary POVM and one arbitrary $m$-outcome POVM on a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space of dimension at least two, with the same uniform-outcome visibility. The independent internal AI review found no gap in the threshold proof. This is not external peer review or proof-assistant verification.

- **Quantifiers and support functions.** The binary marginal is a single difference constraint. All order-interval support functions are evaluated at the same Hermitian matrix $T$, and interval inclusion is equivalent to pointwise support-function dominance. Zero effects remain counted; a singular order interval can be restricted to its support.
- **The main inequality.** The fidelity variational objective is for unsquared fidelity. Its scalar estimates apply in the eigenbasis of each auxiliary operator, not to products of noncommuting matrix lower bounds. Rectangular polar isometries preserve all nonzero singular values, including when the witness support is not invariant under the original effect. The compressed effects sum to $R=|T|$ on its support.
- **An independent argument.** The [alternative proof](TWO-PROJECTION-PROOF.md) avoids fidelity altogether: dilation preserves the trace norm, the two-projection decomposition reduces the problem to intersecting two real intervals, and convexity proves they intersect for every block parameter. The argument does not assume that the individual blocks reduce $T$.
- **Sharpness and crossing.** The known padded qubit pair attains the support bound in the direction of a trace-norm-one flip. Compression preserves its obstruction after extension to larger dimensions. The crossing is unique, with no division by zero at the endpoints. Neither proof asserts uniqueness of maximizers of incompatibility.
- **Computation.** Both standard-library scripts run with Python's assertions disabled. The main script certifies scalar identities symbolically and exercises singular, genuinely complex examples. The independent checker tests the two interval radii and compares the sides of the crossing via exact rational arithmetic. Continuous and all-dimensional conclusions rest on the mathematical proofs, not the finite tests. [Outputs](CHECKS.txt) · [Scope of the prior-work search](SOURCES.md).
