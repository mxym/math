# Independent internal proof checks

**Verdict: pass in the stated finite-dimensional model.** An independent AI reviewer checked the common-visibility characterization, the full two-visibility frontier, the interior equality characterization, the three-label splitting, the four-label counterexample to universal qubit-block necessity, and primary-source scope. No external referee report, formal verification or priority certificate is represented here.

1. **Limit of witnesses.** For an incompatible fixed pair, strict support separation compares $a\operatorname{Tr}AT$, not automatically $a\|T\|_1$, with the sum of the interval supports. After normalizing, compactness yields a nonzero limit of trace norm one. Taking both visibilities to the common threshold, or holding $b$ fixed at any interior value, forces sharpness of every inequality. No interior contact at $a=1$ was presumed.
2. **Non-invariant support.** Initial compression of $B_j$ to the support of $|T|$ is legitimate through rectangular polar decompositions; invariance is deduced only at the end. Both fidelity inputs are positive definite on that support when $0<b<1$, so the variational optimizer exists. The scalar chord slack is exactly $(q-p)^2x(1-x)$ after squaring. All other inequalities have nonnegative gaps, the chord factors are positive, and the product slack vanishes only if $xy=0$. Each label can have a different minimizing basis. Its transforms by $R^{1/2}$ need only span, not be orthogonal.
3. **Projections and reduction.** Each transformed vector is a simultaneous eigenvector of the two compressed effects at eigenvalues in $\{0,1\}$, of product zero. An extremal compressed expectation for the *full* positive contraction gives the same full-space eigenequation. This precludes hidden couplings to the complementary subspace. The converse uses a single normalized sign witness and counts the equal ranks of $P$ and $SPS$ separately; they are orthogonal.
4. **Three versus four.** The $(i,k)$ block of $S^2$ has just one potentially nonzero summand for distinct labels when there are at most three. The directed range spaces therefore split orthogonally within each label; $S$ interchanges opposite ranges. For four coordinate projections any commuting projection is diagonal, and the four nonzero cross entries of the given rational orthogonal matrix force it to be scalar. Zero padding does not change the invariant subspaces.
5. **Examples and consistency.** The independent standard-library script checks the matrix modulus by squaring an explicitly positive candidate, rather than diagonalizing numerically; it checks a rational four-dimensional irreducible model, a mixed-coordinate six-dimensional three-label example, endpoints and strict scalar slack. These finite regressions illustrate the proofs and do not establish universal quantifiers.
