**Simple suppressed kernel.** The parent reduction permits eliminating branch decorations as before. After even paths, deleting the h marked vertices and at most 2r endpoints of a basis of r even constraints leaves an unconstrained coordinate subspace. Every even edge touches that set of deleted vertices by the support of its row vector. For a cubic simple kernel the remaining adjacency is simple subcubic, with at least 3b/2−3h−6r edges; the two largest of ten induced matchings cover at least a fifth. The signed edge differences are null vectors (diagonals both one), mutually orthogonal within each matching. Two induced matchings have no alternating cycle or three-edge path, giving independence by leaf stripping. Their span has zero signature by the sign involution exchanging block signs; interlacing then gives the stated bound (and the trivial dimension bound for r+h≥b/4). Higher-degree splitting into trivalent trees keeps a simple kernel; length-four connectors enforce equality and contribute zero signature before compression, so the sum of the d−2 unit diagonals is the original d−2. The splitting preserves cycle rank and previous marks/constraints. The original graph being simple is not sufficient for its suppressed kernel to be simple. Andersen's coloring theorem and previous path identities are credited tools. The author's exact simple-kernel replay passed all 2960 instances.

**All cactus graphs.** One central copy plus one port copy for every original cycle at a vertex preserves the cactus block tree, after length-four equality connectors; collapsing the equal coordinates reproduces its original degree diagonal and adjacencies. Rooted messages compare doubled signature minus three times the local cycle rank, so each child budget is charged just once. Generalized Schur elimination at a compatible terminal gives the indicated shift. An incompatible terminal pins one external functional to zero and creates a net-zero hyperbolic pair (or does not change the host in the zero-function case); this costs at most one in signature by interlacing. Drawing one dummy exterior scalar H=[0] at the end is legitimate since exterior forms were arbitrary: the bridge makes it +1, which pivots and removes exactly the root preload and adds +1 to the signature.

For point vertices x is nonzero because 3x is odd. The budget partitions for sums of costs 0 through 3 yield exactly the maxima 0, 2/3, 2, 4; the low-cost pivots dominate the four responses. A single-mark removal has Schur difference with eigenvalues 1 and 2/y−1. For two marks along an even path their diagonal contributions add after the signed equality; the rank-one bound is independent of the surrounding host. For odd links the two-by-two determinant signatures are correct: type (1,1) on b=e a has nonnegative inverse quadratic form and one available signature loss; types (1,3)/(3,1) permit a signed link because its Schur-difference determinant is 3/(product−1)−1≥0; type (3,3) leaves a negative semidefinite correction which can be discarded after two pivots and a leftover hyperplane restriction. These relations still hold with coincident boundary functionals. The port is never removed when erasing the consecutive marks of the open cycle. The bare-cycle four residues give the stated scalars/equality; the single-mark menu allows both odd links, either even relation, or opposing even relations. The exact replay finds minima g at costs 0,2,4,6 equal to 1,3/11,−1/5,−3, with exactly two incompatible states at cost 4 and none outside the allowed bounds.
