# The unique saddle on a two-coordinate family of central cube sections

Rotate a central hyperplane of the unit cube from the normal with all entries equal toward the normal with just two equal nonzero entries, keeping both coordinate blocks constant throughout. [Ambrus and Gárgyán, §5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2024.109524) proved that the section volume has an interior critical point along this arc and asked whether it is the only one. This note answers **yes, for every dimension n ≥ 4**: the volume decreases strictly to that point, then increases strictly. The two larger entries in its unit normal tend to approximately 0.64017405 as the dimension tends to infinity.

The existence of the section and its saddle behavior in the full sphere are **previous results** of Ambrus and Gárgyán. Dimension four was previously classified by [Ambrus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14778). The result here is uniqueness along the **two-large-coordinate** arc, up to permuting/signing the normal, and the limiting location. It does **not** settle the general local-extrema Conjecture 1.3 or the separate question for three or more large coordinates.
