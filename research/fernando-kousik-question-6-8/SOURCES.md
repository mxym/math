# Sources and scope

1. N. Fernando and B. Kousik, *A further study of polynomial \(g_{n,q}\) over finite fields*, [arXiv:2606.01037v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01037v1), 31 May 2026, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01037). Equation (1.1) defines \(g_{n,q}\) by translation. **Question 6.8**, [PDF p. 18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.01037v1#page=18), fixes \(q=4\), \(e\ge3\), \(n=1+2q+q^2+2q^3+2q^{e+1}\) and asks when the displayed polynomial is a permutation of \(\mathbb F_{4^e}\). Section 1 of the proof derives the formal polynomial independently and proves its functional congruence with the displayed formula modulo \(X^{4^e}-X\), the equivalence needed by the question. Table 1 records the positive finite instance \((e,n)=(4,2201)\).
2. N. Fernando and X. Hou, *From \(r\)-linearized polynomial equations to \(r^m\)-linearized polynomial equations*, Finite Fields and Their Applications 37 (2016), 14–27, [DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2015.08.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2015.08.003), [arXiv:1503.03162v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03162), Table 1 (PDF p. 10). This earlier table already lists the \((2201,4;4)\) instance without an explanation in that row. No new positive finite instance is claimed here. The new conclusion relative to those tables is that all other extensions in Question 6.8 fail, including the unbounded range.
3. A. Weil, [*On Some Exponential Sums*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.34.5.204), Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 34 (1948), 204–207. We apply the complete additive Weil bound \((D-1)\sqrt{|K|}\) for a nontrivial additive character of a finite field and a polynomial of degree \(D\) prime to the characteristic; here \(D=9\) and the characteristic is 2.

On 2026-11-27, a bounded search using the title, arXiv ID, exact “Question 6.8,” \(g_{2201,4}\), the trace/Frobenius expression and permutation-polynomial terms located the existing finite instance above but no matching proof of the full classification. The frozen package addresses Question 6.8 alone. No third-party data or code are distributed.
