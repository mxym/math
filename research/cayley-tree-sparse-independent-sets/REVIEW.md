* **Model and finite moment:** Normalize X by its exact positive expectation for 1≤k≤n−1, not by a path count. Fixed-set planting followed by forgetting the set gives dQ/dP=X/E X. With overlap j the allowed-edge graph is a join of the outer clique and the interior disjoint union of j isolates and a balanced complete bipartite graph. Its Laplacian yields exactly the displayed probability, including j=0 and j=k; no zero-size class is used with negative multiplicity in the proof. Through the critical scale 2k<n eventually. Exact Prüfer enumeration separately confirms even feasible disconnected cases outside that range.
* **Second-moment asymptotics:** The hypergeometric mean M=k²/n and variance M(1−k/n)² n/(n−1) are exact. For diverging M, the log error terms of orders M/n and M²/n² vanish. In the split at 4eM the positive centered overlap is controlled by its factorial-moment mgf on the bulk; on the rest the point probability is at most 4^{-j} and the integrand squared is ≤exp(2kj/D), D=n(1−k/n)². Since k/D→0 this is a summable geometric tail with cutoff going to infinity. For bounded M the uncentered mgf bound directly applies; subsequences cover all oscillations. Thus the limit of E_P (X/E X)² is exp(t⁶/2) when k/n^{5/6}→t<∞. Distributional convergence of the integrand alone would not prove this expectation, and this expectation alone would not identify the critical law.
* **Planted law and uniform-occupancy CLT:** The planted degree vector is described by the full degree-generating polynomial of Martin–Reiner Theorem 4. I also derived it from the directed matrix-tree minor by a one-dimensional Schur complement (see companion derivation). It gives an equality of multiplicity distributions, not of ordered Prüfer sequences. In the collision martingale the conditional variance uses sum of squared previous occupancies = j−1+2C_{j−1}; the centered conditional variance sum has L² norm O(sqrt n), negligible compared with n/2. The unconditional law of each increment before centering is Bin(j−1,1/n); uniformly bounded fourth moments imply conditional Lindeberg in probability by Markov. This works when the number of unrestricted draws is merely asymptotic to n.
* **Uniform covariance, all complements:** Put m=n−k, u=m−1, b=k−1. Exactly Var_Q W = [binom(u,2)+ub](n−1)/n²+binom(b,2)(m−1)/m²+u b(b−1)k/(n²m). The only nonzero off-diagonal covariance has a shared unrestricted draw and two distinct restricted partners, and its coefficient counts *ordered* pairs. This gives O(n+k²/m) with constants independent of m≥1. For pairs involving a restricted draw only, O(k+k²/m+k³/n²)=o(n) at k=O(n^{5/6}), justifying the author's short planted-CLT reduction without an independence assumption. My independent coupling proof also gives a centered comparison to the null CLT. At m=1 the planted tree is a star; the formula correctly gives zero variance. No moderate-density expansion is used near k=n.
* **Likelihood and strict separation of regimes:** At critical k∼t n^{5/6}, the standardized tree collision W has mean shift s=t³/sqrt(2) under Q and asymptotic unit variance. Matching the critical E_P Y² via bounded continuous truncations h_B yields joint convergence to (Z,exp(sZ−s²/2)). The truncation is essential, since rare extreme trees can invalidate convergence of untruncated exponential moments of W. At supercritical k≤n−1, Δ²/n and Δ²/(k²/m) diverge, hence separation of the two measures using a midpoint; Y→0 in P-probability, not L¹, with divergence of E_P Y² by Cauchy–Schwarz. This is not a claim that X=0 with high probability. At k∼u n^{2/3} the *different* ratio X/path tends in L² to exp(u³/2) by exact finite expectation cancellation and the subcritical concentration. No statement simultaneously uniform over random profiles or identifying higher lognormal moments is made.
