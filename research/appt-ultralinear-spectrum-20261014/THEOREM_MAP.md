# APPT orders faster than the local dimension: proof obligations

Prepared before the manuscripts, 14 October 2026. This is a further analysis
of the same APPT entropy direction, not a newly counted major conjecture.

| Statement | Required bridge on the actual object | Verification boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1: uniform sharp peak-or-bulk moment law, alpha/m->infinity | Exact finite V* from the prior all-order theorem; exact scalar projection maximum; a width split makes the peak and bulk unable to both contribute at leading order. Keep u_D and (m+1)/(m-1) without exponent expansions. | Ordinary proof; no added Lean root or audit. |
| F2: simple relative entropy law, max{2/m,log u_D-log D/alpha} | Additive logarithmic accuracy from F1; the positive bulk benchmark diverges in moment scale; divide only after proving relative accuracy. | Ordinary all-aspect asymptotic, not an exact finite optimum. |
| R1: complete all-aspect sharp-moment near-optimizer classification | Actual physical interval/mean; explicit width and mixing defects; size-biased upper-edge layer; coupling suppresses one mechanism when the other is active. Include oscillating phase ratios and alpha much larger than D. | New ordinary physical spectral criterion, not complete Lean. |
| R2: complete leading entropy-optimality criterion | Actual first and middle contributions; uniform logarithmic exclusion of all other exceptional values using the physical second-eigenvalue gap and a separate large-peak-log case. | Weaker than sharp-moment optimality; a physical projection sequence separates them. |
| Exact finite lower witnesses | Rank-rounded projection-only family and pure spike; the prior all-order proof's trace/rank control in the comparable range. | Existing all-unitary constructions reused, not invented here. |

The inherited all-order theorem is a clearly identified mathematical input;
its full earlier proof is not silently re-certified by this continuation.
Fixed m, general finite exact extrema, absolute separability and complete
formalization remain outside these new statements.
