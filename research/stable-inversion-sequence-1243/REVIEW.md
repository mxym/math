* For fixed black data, the threshold rank is nonincreasing with the row. Setting the forbidden row and column gaps to zero is sufficient: every eligible row has infinitely many available candidate columns; once the finite black rows end all remaining white values are eligible. The increasing white tail is eventually the identity, because only finitely many values were assigned before it. A previously assigned white point cannot lie southwest of a black point, by transitivity of its witnessing pair. Conversely the witness rectangle of any supposed 1243 makes its fourth value eligible in its third row, which greedy minimality forbids.
