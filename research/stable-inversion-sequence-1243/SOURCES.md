# Primary sources and scope of the comparison

- **The exact question.** Anders Claesson, Svante Linusson, Henning Úlfarsson and Emil Verkama, [*Inversion monotonicity in subclasses of the 1324-avoiders*, November 6, 2026, v2, §8, Problem 8.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01143v2#S8), request the stable sequence for the **single** forbidden pattern 1243 and give its first ten entries. Their Proposition 5.1 treats existence of limit sequences; Theorem 5.10(a) gives the ordinary partition generating function for the **pair** of forbidden patterns 1324 and 1243, a different class. The answer here is not that product.
- **Earlier reduction of this question.** Emil Verkama's KTH licentiate thesis, [*Inversions in the 1324-avoiding permutations*, chapter 3, printed p. 50](https://diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A2050045/FULLTEXT01.pdf), gives the decomposition $C_{1243}(q)=(P(q)-1)\sum_{\ell\ge1}q^{\binom\ell2}+\sum_{k\ge0}i_k(1243)q^k$, where $P$ is the ordinary partition generating function and $i_k$ counts indecomposable avoiders of any length, with $k$ inversions. The first contribution and reduction to the second are prior. The new multiple series does not leave the $i_k$ as unknown coefficients; subtracting the known first contribution recovers their generating function.
- **The filling is not new.** The rank-preserving boundary and smallest-eligible-value continuation are the finite filling of Julian West's bijection between 1234- and 1243-avoiders. See Miklós Bóna's [author manuscript, §2, Lemma 2.2 and Example 2.3](https://people.clas.ufl.edu/bona/files/alternating.pdf), explicitly crediting West. The last rank-two value to the left is the smallest earlier endpoint of an increasing pair. West's ordinary Wilf equivalence is by length and is not an inversion equidistribution (already the single excluded word in size four has different inversion count). The present use of the stable increasing tail makes the finite row and column gaps independently admissible, then marks the inversions of the forced filling by a bilinear gap statistic.
- **Other refinements compared.** [Chan, *An infinite family of inv-Wilf-equivalent permutation pairs*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2014.08.031), studies inversion-weighted transversals and equivalences; this is relevant methodology, not a stable series for the isolated 1243 class. [Gao–Kitaev–Zhang, *On pattern avoiding indecomposable permutations*](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/s2/s2.pdf), enumerate by length and include descent refinements. [Callan](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3857) treats the simultaneous avoidance of 1243 and 2134 by length. [Albert et al., *Combinatorial exploration*](https://akc.is/papers/038-Combinatorial-exploration.pdf), give length specifications, not the coefficient series here.
- **July–November 19, 2026 gate.** The exact problem and other results in the November revision cited above were checked against September's [Meng v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06717v2) and the [author's companion manuscript listing](https://lsmeng.github.io/ai-for-math.html), which concern the low- and high-inversion regions of **1324** avoidance; [Verkama's November 12 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17237) treats connectivity constraints on inversion graphs. Searches of stable inversion sequences, the quoted ten terms, single 1243 and its reverse-complement 2134, inversion-refined Wilf equivalence, rank/Ferrers fillings, enumeration schemes, and more recent versions of the source question did not identify this positive series elsewhere. The comparison is limited by the indexed literature and the sources accessible at the check date.
