# Independent internal review: fixed-q inversion weights

Reviewed DICHOTOMY.md SHA-256 31000d011b90560bb3b5c64d01f49ff6f676e5630dffa454d1ea100cf10efbd5. The stated analytic rates and exact polynomial degree follow from the proof. This review is internal AI verification, not external peer review or priority assurance.

* In the included-layered-class case, standardizing the two position blocks preserves avoidance and within-block inversions; the binary word of value allocations has Gaussian-binomial aggregate cross weight. This gives submultiplicativity after a fixed normalization (not an assertion that arbitrary direct sums are allowed). The zero/one block recurrence supplies the exponential lower bound. For an identity forbidden, ranks by longest increasing subsequence put all points into finitely many decreasing chains, hence the quadratic inversion energy dominates n!.
* Avoidance of a sufficiently long direct sum of descents bounds the number of nonsingleton sum components. Each length-l indecomposable needs at least l−1 inversion-removing adjacent swaps, since without crossing any given cut the left value-set could never be corrected to the smallest l-prefix set. The reduced core therefore has length at most k+m−1. Any disjoint family of the closed position-and-value inversion intervals is ordered in both axes and forces the indicated layered pattern, so the greedy interval-hitting argument gives at most m−1 cut points. If both inverted entries have displacement at most H, then their positions differ by less than 2H and the entire interval is within 3H of either position. Deleting high-displacement entries by footrule and one position neighborhood per cut leaves an increasing subsequence. Choosing exceptional positions, values and their assignment gives a valid overcount exp(O(sqrt(k) log(k+2))), not relying on unique certificates.
* Truncating every insertion gap at maximum forbidden-pattern length preserves the existence of every forbidden witness (and the chosen core stays fixed). For a legal truncated tuple the coefficient-count contribution has its exact polynomial form once n>k+C, including the all-bounded tuple whose approximating polynomial is zero. More than r free gaps exhibit in order r descents separated by as many singleton positions as needed for the chosen forbidden layered pattern. Conversely r−1 isolated descents with all r gaps unrestricted avoid every basis pattern by membership in the layered class and the inversion total. All degree-r−1 leading coefficients at positive q have the same sign. Coefficients grow at most subexponentially in k; the entire difference is supported at k≥n−C and the exponential tail bounds apply also when the polynomial is evaluated to the left of its onset. This proves both the radius-one holomorphic statement and the rate O(u^n) for every u>t on the smaller disk |q|≤t.
* Linusson–Verkama's exact low-inversion coefficient formula gives the displayed polynomial correction after summation and should be credited as such. The core envelope justifies controlling the missing k>2n−7 range all the way to the disk boundary radius chosen strictly below one. Taking q<u<sqrt(q) indeed makes the error negligible relative to q^(n−1). The resulting monotonicity is for the single weighted sum, not coefficientwise and not the unweighted growth problem.
