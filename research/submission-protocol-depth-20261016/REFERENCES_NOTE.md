# Closest algorithmic and universality predecessors

Symmetric compression is prior work: Plesch--Buzek (0907.1764v2), Baertschi--Eidenbenz (1904.07358), and most directly Chen--Xiong--Khadiev--Wu, Efficient quantum compression for identically prepared states with arbitrary dimension (2307.12024v2, 29 August 2026), whose sections III--IV explicitly contain arbitrary-D occupation/CG compression and efficient approximation.

The finite gate compilation input is Dawson--Nielsen, The Solovay--Kitaev algorithm (quant-ph/0505030v2), Section 2 Definition 1 and Theorem 1: a fixed inverse-closed instruction set in SU(2), projective treatment of global phase, and the total gate/compile overhead. These hypotheses are retained in the pure-circuit v3 source.

Blume-Kohout--Croke--Gottesman, Streaming universal distortion-free entanglement concentration (0910.5952v1), already treats coherent reversible streaming universal concentration of unknown bipartite pure states and perfect EPR output under ordinary LOCC with variable yield. The independent reviewer directly checked its Theorem 1. Our fixed global-U resource and all-psi fixed Bell target differ; no claim of first streaming or first unknown-pure concentration is made.

Rizzo--Lami--Eisert--Leone, Universal quantum coding (2609.38038v1), already has local-Schur universal coding, finite-shot and second-order achievability and rank-sensitive sample complexity. These are not novelty labels for our different globally preprocessed minimax task.

Mandal--Piveteau--Pfister--Renes, Efficient capacity-achieving entanglement generation with application to pure-loss Bosonic channels (2610.03674v1), provides recent algorithmic context; only its primary abstract was checked in this author continuation.
