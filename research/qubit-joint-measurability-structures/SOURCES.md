# Primary sources and boundaries

1. N. Andrejic and R. Kunjwal, [*Joint measurability structures realizable with qubit measurements: incompatibility via marginal surgery*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.043147) ([full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.00785)). Definitions 1–3 distinguish general POVMs from binary qubit POVMs and define joint measurement by product-space marginals. Conjecture 4 in §VI asks for all joint-measurability structures on a qubit, without an outcome restriction. Conjectures 1–3 concern narrower families of binary measurements. The paper already gave single-qubit binary constructions for all N-cycle and N-Specker scenarios, every four-vertex structure and some larger examples. Those special cases with binary outputs are stronger within their scope.
2. R. Kunjwal, [*Quantumness via Discrete Structures*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.10063), §5.3, p. 70. The author explicitly describes the conjecture for any **finite** number of vertices and reports it open as of the 2025 habilitation. The binary criterion is item 3 in the preceding list, not a qualifier on the ensuing conjecture. Chapter 5 defines vertices as different measurements; here each measurement has private, distinct rank-one effects.
3. R. Kunjwal, C. Heunen and T. Fritz, [*Quantum realization of arbitrary joint measurability structures*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5948). The earlier universality theorem assigns binary POVMs and assembles obstructions for minimal incompatible subsets in Hilbert direct sums. It gives no constant dimension bound independent of the complex. We instead keep the dimension at two and allow the outcome lists to grow. C. Heunen, T. Fritz and M. L. Reyes, [*Quantum theory realises all joint measurability graphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3698), realize graphs by sharp observables with a variable Hilbert space; for sharp measurements pairwise compatibility already implies global compatibility.
4. P. Skrzypczyk and D. Cavalcanti, [*Loss-tolerant Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen steering for arbitrary-dimensional states: Joint measurability and unbounded violations under losses*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.04926), §VII, Eq. (18), and Appendix E, Eqs. (E1)–(E2). The efficiency threshold 1/M for M lossy rank-one bases without common rays is already known: below or at threshold there is a flagged single-measurement parent, while above it the lossy family is incompatible. This ingredient is **not** the new claim. On a nonface one may alternatively recover each lossy basis by coarse-graining the chosen representative setting: keep its two selected signed outcomes and send everything else to null. The construction here organizes the **complements of the maximal faces** into single many-outcome measurements for the vertices. This lets the same flagged basis serve any number of settings and gives a common qubit parent for every and only the required subfamilies.
5. T. Yoshino, K. Tojo, K. Hagihara, A. Tanaka and T. Tomita, [*Joint measurability of coplanar POVMs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38836). Their perimeter criterion for coplanar unbiased binary qubit measurements settles Conjecture 2; D. Grinko and R. Uola, [*Compatibility of Binary Qubit Measurements*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07711), had previously given a counterexample to Conjecture 3. Neither result is a universality theorem for arbitrary complexes. D. McNulty, [*A Graph-Theoretic Approach to Quantum Measurement Incompatibility*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15954), treats graph invariants of incompatibility robustness for binary observables with prescribed commutation/anticommutation, a different realization problem.

No bound independent of the complex on the number of outcomes, infinite-family extension, sharp-measurement realization, Bell violation, or experimental and noise-robust contextuality statement is asserted here.
