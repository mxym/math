# Source and scope

* Yuan Li and Jing Zhang, [Sensitivity of Rotation Symmetric Boolean Functions via Binary Necklaces](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17006v2), 8 November 2026, Section 7 defines and treats the consecutive quartic case, and Section 10 explicitly leaves open arbitrary quartic supports and asks about dependence on difference sets and short orbits. This work follows their convention of counting distinct orbit monomials, and supplies the width-five section, not their general problem.
* Yuan Li and Jing Zhang, [Block Sensitivity and Certificate Complexity of Rotation Symmetric Boolean Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17100), 2026. The companion treats the consecutive case and other complexity measures; it does not cover these offset supports.
* Sergei Sergeev and Hans Schneider, [CSR expansions of matrix powers in max algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.2534), reference for the standard eventual cyclicity framework. We use finite exact power equalities for the specific graphs and do not claim a new max-plus theorem.

Searches through 27 November 2026 combined the exact title, Li–Zhang, quartic MRS, nonconsecutive sensitivity, all four offset forms, widths, max-plus and the fractional slope. No prior result giving this full width-five classification was located. Individual Boolean functions and general max-plus techniques are not claimed as new.
