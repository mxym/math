The two different point/enumeration pipelines returned matching strict degree-sum margins for each model: 148,673 eligible smooth charts with minimum gap 18, including nonflex embeddings, and 87,360 nonsplit-node charts with minimum gap 150. The verifier also regenerated both conic outsider-triple distributions and direct 66-point Sidon witness. The review checked the characteristic-two Weierstrass normalizations, completeness of |2O+T| modulo translations, the implicit nonflex equation, the node reduction and the exact hypergraph-counting comparison. This determines the maximum and equality case **only** for nonzero polynomial supports of degree at most three over GF(64), with addition in the affine plane.

The dedicated July–21 November search found no identical degree-three support classification. Recent [Dobbertin Walsh spectrum, arXiv:2611.18907v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18907v1), §9, constructs Sidon sets from APN hyperplane slices, and [Thornburgh, arXiv:2609.27731v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27731v1) bounds different ambient dimensions. The older [Schoof cubic classification](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(87)90003-3) supports the geometric viewpoint, not the Sidon exclusion. [Forey–Fresán–Kowalski's published article](https://javier.fresan.perso.math.cnrs.fr/sidon.pdf) and [Jacobian graphs](https://javier.fresan.perso.math.cnrs.fr/jacobiangraphs.pdf) concern addition in generalized Jacobians; the frozen text's earlier arXiv reference 2103.04917 is a withdrawn predecessor, not an asserted proof input. [Nagy's conic result](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2024.106001) supplies historical context for the already known ellipse-plus-nucleus example; it does not classify all degree-three supports. Repository searches did not locate this classification. These are bounded overlap checks, not a global priority claim.
