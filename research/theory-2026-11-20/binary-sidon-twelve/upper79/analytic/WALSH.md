# Independent signed-arithmetic route for the four low quarter profiles

Choose the largest internal quarter $a$ **over all maximum $m$-halves**. The 80 and 46/47 second/fourth slabs, $s(10)=34$ and the six pair sums no larger than $m$ give exactly six quarter sizes (n80shapes.py). On the four covered here, let $n_i$ be the sizes and $w_i$ the four signed Walsh coefficients at a character of $U=\mathbb F_2^{10}$. For a nonprincipal frequency the four quotient-signed sums are bounded by $2m-80$; the two-layer bound is $|w_i|+|w_j|\le2a-m$ whenever $n_i+n_j=m$. Center all odd layers' XORs to zero, then root the first $e-1$ even layers at a member; these are precisely three independently specifiable affine layer translations. Up to row reduction, the tuple of even XORs has 5 types when $e=2$ and 67 types when $e=4$; at each step enter any old span value or a fresh basis element. Thus the four profiles have 5+67+5+5 types. Tags $b$ are restrictions of the frequency to that span. Each occurs $Q/2^{\mathrm{rank}}$ times, $Q=1024$, and $w_i\equiv n_i-2\,u\cdot\mathrm{XOR}(S_i)\pmod4$.

The equality rows besides counts, tags, and $\sum w_i=Q$ for rooted layers are $\sum w_i^2=Qn_i$, $\sum w_i^4=Q(3n_i^2-2n_i)$, $\sum w_i^2w_j^2=Q n_i n_j$ and $\sum_u \prod_{j=0}^3 w_j(u)=0$ (the last sum is over frequencies). These follow by orthogonality: sets of within-layer pair sums are disjoint, and no four-tuples using all four distinct layers sum to zero.

The indicator signals on $U$ for the signed certificates are:
- $P_i=1_{S_i}$, and its complement $p_i$, with masses $n_i,Q-n_i$.
- $D_i=1_{\Delta S_i}$ for within-layer sums of distinct pairs, mass $\binom{n_i}{2}$. Its Walsh value is $(w_i^2-n_i)/2$. The four $D_i$ have disjoint supports; $d$ is the indicator of the complement of their union.
- $C_{ij}=1_{S_i+S_j}$, mass $n_i n_j$ and Walsh $w_iw_j$, because all these cross-sums are distinct. For each partition $0j|kl$, $C_{0j},C_{kl}$ have disjoint supports (no four-layer collision); $c_j$ is the complement of their union.


At $u\ne0$ the Fourier transform of a complement is the negative of the given signal or disjoint signal sum. **At zero instead use its full mass.** For any above indicator $T$ and any $h$ in the XOR span, $\sum_u(-1)^{u\cdot h}\widehat T(u)$ lies between 0 and $Q$ by Fourier inversion. For any two indicators $T,S$, the analogous Fourier sum with product $\widehat T\widehat S$ is $Q$ times their translate intersection cardinality, between 0 and $Q\min(|T|,|S|)$. These bounds also apply to a signal paired with itself.

For each of the 82 modes the supplied integer combination has unrestricted multipliers on equality rows, nonnegative multipliers on upper-bound rows, a nonnegative coefficient on every allowed nonprincipal character profile, and a strictly negative right-hand side after subtracting the actual principal term. This is an integer contradiction. Python's arbitrary-precision checker rebuilds all columns, legal signs and bounds, and checks the complete mode cover. Four subsets of a genuine 66-element Sidon set test all used arithmetic row types (ranks 2,4,2,2). These eliminate the two $a=28,m=46$ profiles and the two $a=29,m=47$ profiles, without using the Jaffe alternative.
