# A 66-point binary Sidon set cannot have a 32-point codimension-two section in dimension twelve

**Theorem.** If S is a Sidon subset of the twelve-dimensional binary vector space and |S|≥66, every affine subspace of codimension two contains at most 31 members of S.

This is a necessary structural condition; it neither classifies all size-66 sets nor constructs or rules out 67 points. It does not lower the unrestricted upper bound of 78. No symmetry of the hypothetical S is assumed. Symmetries are used only to identify equivalent markings of a fixed smaller set. All finite searches use binary XOR, integer bitsets and exact widths. No oracle for the classification of 46-point sets is needed.

## Dependencies and scope

We use the [certified 11-dimensional maximum and 10-dimensional source census](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/8022fae01a2c79356c0cb3c9123a778bac0fb986): the maximum in V11 is 48; in V10 the maximum is 34 and for sizes 32,33,34 the complete affine classifications have 200,7,1 representatives. That source proves coverage by labeled hyperplane incidence masses. Its d10.txt is copied unchanged, SHA-256 a12772fa637227ee3b017cbe35edfab700d14c935ae34daafc531cb56dbc7d98. The present computation does not rely on whether any 32-set is called a Gold graph, or on the asserted order of any catalogue automorphism group.

## Four quarters

It suffices to consider |S|=66: from a larger counterexample retain 32 points of its alleged large section while taking a 66-subset. Choose a codimension-two affine space meeting S in a set A of size r≥32. The four parallel affine spaces may be identified with V10, using binary quotient labels 00,01,10,11. The other three quarters are U,V,W. For any set D let ΔD be the unordered sums of two different points. Any of the four quarter sets is Sidon, and their four pair-difference sets are pairwise disjoint (two equal internal differences would give a forbidden quadruple). Conversely, for a *two-layer* split, being individually Sidon with disjoint internal differences is sufficient as well as necessary: the only possible four-point collisions not internal to a layer have two points in each layer.

The V10 source bound leaves only r=32,33,34. Every other quarter U up to translation is Sidon with ΔU disjoint from ΔA. Any affine re-identification of A extends to the entire ambient space, and translating just U in the two-quarter anchor extends by a shear in the quotient labels. In the subsequent opposite-layer search the full remaining half is allowed to move. Permuting the three nonzero quotient labels changes no existence question.

For each of the seven r=33 sources and the sole r=34 source the complete 12-mate list is empty. If r=33 the remaining 33 points therefore split 11+11+11. The exact numbers of translation classes of 11-mates on the seven sources (file order) are 2,2,2,2,0,0,54. Direct comparison of their difference sets gives no triple of pairwise disjoint ones in any class. Repeating a translation class cannot help: its nonempty difference set would repeat. If r=34, the 32 remaining points must split 11+11+10. Here the 11- and 10-mate counts are 2 and 22; again no three of these required sizes have pairwise disjoint differences. The program checks every positive listed mate directly and every necessary disjointness combination. It does not assume that three independent choices of translations in the quotient suffice to give an actual four-layer Sidon set: disjointness alone already fails.

Now r=32. If any other quarter has at least 14 points, keep a 14-subset U of it. The hyperplane containing A and the *whole* U-quarter has at most 48 original points by the certified 11-dimensional maximum. Therefore the original opposite half has at least 18 points, no matter how many U-quarter points were discarded. Hence H=A+U (two layers, 46 retained points) requires a compatible opposite 18-subset. All such marked anchors have been exhausted. Otherwise the other three quarters sum to 34 and each is at most 13; at least one is exactly 12 or 13. Keep its entire U. The hyperplane containing A and U has exactly 44 or 45 original points, hence requires an opposite 22- or 21-set respectively.

## Exhaustive opposite-layer search

Given a fixed A in Vd, define a graph on vectors, with an edge xy iff x+y is nonzero and not in ΔA. An admissible opposite B is exactly a clique without any four distinct members of zero XOR. Translation preserves this condition. Order candidate differences by decreasing number of common neighbours of 0 and the difference, breaking ties by the integer encoding. For each possible least internal difference d of B, choose either endpoint as the origin, hence 0,d∈B. Later vertices and pair edges must use later differences. rootpairs.cpp imposes this progressively: after processing d it deletes every edge of difference d among the remaining vertices. On adding x it removes the prospective vertices x+y+z for each previous pair y,z, as these would violate the four-sum condition. The recursion inherits old exclusions. Proper greedy color classes of the candidate graph bound the clique size above; no optimality of the coloring is assumed.

At a terminal, compare the candidate to its translate by d and emit only the smaller sorted set. Since the unique pair realizing the least difference is the same up to its two endpoints, this emits each translation class once. No translate ties occur for |B|>2, since a nonzero translation preserving a Sidon set must pair its points into at most one pair. For the secondary orbit computation, even k uses the least translate through a point; for odd k translating by the XOR of all points gives a unique XOR-zero representative. The two normalizations are explicit: the implementation re-normalizes the complete list before applying the 12 basis maps.

The second full terminal solver (split.cpp) uses a different decomposition. Choose the last nonzero internal character maximizing the absolute Walsh value of H, and let C,T be its two sides in V10. Split the target opposite layer into L,R along the same direction; by translating just that opposite layer one may assume k=|L|≥|R|=t−k. Every k=ceil(t/2),...,t is tested. D(L),D(R) must each avoid D(C) union D(T), as well as one another. Additionally L+R must avoid C+T. These and the separate Sidon conditions on L,R are sufficient, by the four possibilities for quotient labels. For L root the *least numeric* difference d at 0,d and construct the entire graph for this d directly. As L grows, remove four-point collisions and repeatedly shrink the R pool by removing C+T+x for each x in L. After L is fixed, recurse over every R-subset of the required size under the remaining difference constraints. Valid proper clique color bounds only reduce the search. This split solver is not a replay of the first full V11 graph and does not use its degree order, prune threshold or nodes. It lifts each nonempty hit to V12, directly validates its pair sums and reports all translation classes. Every one of the 312 independent jobs instead returned exactly zero across its entire k range. The optional third implementation raw.cpp merely roots at 0 and does not use least differences or the hyperplane split. flag --raw asks for a fresh through-zero cross-check as well; it is not needed to reproduce the two complete solvers.

## Replay

Use Python 3.10+ and a GNU C++17 compiler on x86-64 (no third-party libraries, downloads or solver). From this directory:

    python3 -O verify.py
    python3 -O verify.py --raw

Both modes regenerate the complete raw layer arrays, check their digests, recompute all marking orbits and execute both complete 312-job terminal searches. The second invocation additionally runs the independent through-zero raw searches. Expect minutes to tens of minutes or more depending on CPU; raw is slower. The verifier works in a fresh temporary directory and refuses silent missing or extra keys, truncated output, or per-array mismatches. Sources use binary XOR as ordinary vector addition. Counts in endpoints.json identify first-solver nodes; split_ledger.json records the independently completed parameters, normals and full terminal transcripts. Compiled source disallows NDEBUG. For an independent replay of the prior dimension census, see the pinned source above.

**Positive terminal control.** positive.txt gives an explicit 46-point hyperplane anchor and a compatible opposite 16-set, taken by deleting two points from the 48+16 construction. The verifier directly tests the resulting 62-set (1891 distinct pair sums), then checks that the two search routines both recover the planted 16-set up to translation. Neither solver merely tested for absence on the negative terminals. This control does not assume a previously proved uniqueness theorem.
