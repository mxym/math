# Stability one point below the maximum binary Sidon set in eleven dimensions

**Theorem.** Every 47-point Sidon set in $V_{11}=\mathbb F_2^{11}$ has a unique extending point, and its extension is affinely equivalent to Chen's 48-point set. Up to affine equivalence there are precisely three such 47-sets. Thus there are **no inclusion-maximal 47-sets** in dimension eleven. The theorem is unrestricted; it does not assume the 47-set has any symmetry. Consequences include all 33 binary $[46,35,5]$ code classes, all three binary $[47,35,6]$ code classes, the exclusion of 79 Sidon points in $V_{12}$, and two corresponding distance-table equalities. No exact value for $s(12)$ is claimed.

This package builds on the [unique optimum-48 classification](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/a78febb46b89666125b6fc483fd14322b5f593bc), the [9D and 10D input census](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/8022fae01a2c79356c0cb3c9123a778bac0fb986), and the [46/29 and 47/30 exclusions](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/1b0d053b4eca9c8c0fe364673cbaa7df06a2d574). In particular the last reference excluded every stand-alone 46-set of maximal half 29 and every 47-set of maximal half 30, not just those arising from an ambient 80-set.

## 1. Marking a maximum half

Call the maximum cardinality of an affine hyperplane section the maximum half. For an $n$-point Sidon set in $V_r$, its off-principal Walsh sums have second/fourth moments $2^rn-n^2$ and $2^r(3n^2-2n)-n^4$. Both count ordered tuples with zero sum by orthogonality. If the maximum half is $m$, then $n\bmod2\le W^2\le(2m-n)^2$ at every nonprincipal frequency. Summing the resulting quadratic slab shows that at $n=47,r=11$, $m\le28$ would give the impossible positive excess $953,856$. For $m=29$ delete any point outside a 29-half to obtain a 46-set of maximum 29, also impossible. The previous packet handles $m=30$. So $m\ge31$, while $s(10)=34$ implies $m\le34$.

For the $m=31$ case choose a largest 31-half $A$ whose own maximum half $q$ is as large as possible **across all 31-halves**. We have $q\ge20$ since $A$ contains a 29-subset. For clarity here is a short, independent proof that every Sidon 29-set in $V_{10}$ has a 20-half. Center its XOR to 0 by translation. If the maximum were $\le19$, the nonprincipal Walsh coefficients could only be $-7,-3,1,5,9$. Let $h$ indicate membership of the centered origin, and $t$ count distinct unordered triples with sum zero. Such triples are disjoint (or two distinct pairs have equal sum); $t\le9$. If $h=1$ then $t=0$ by Sidon. After subtracting the principal term, the five moments of orders $0,1,2,3,4$ give the five multiplicities by a nonsingular Vandermonde system. Its right side is $(1023,1024h-29,1024\cdot29-29^2,1024(85h+6t)-29^3,1024(3\cdot29^2-58)-29^4)$. For $h=1$ the multiplicities are $(238,-21,540,84,182)$; otherwise they are $(386-12t,32t-229,724-24t,4,138+4t)$, forcing $t=8,9$. Counting the principal character and the Walsh values $-3,5$, exactly 32 or 64 frequencies would have $W\equiv29\pmod8$. But all character weights $k=(29-W)/2$ are even, and $1+\binom{k}{2}\pmod2$ detects this residue. As a function of the frequency its degree is at most two: $\binom{k}{2}$ is the sum of pairwise products of the corresponding linear bits. It is nonzero at zero. A nonzero multilinear degree-at-most-two polynomial on ten bits has at least $2^8$ ones: take a highest-degree monomial of degree $d\le2$; summing the function over its $d$ variables yields 1 for every assignment to the others. Contradiction.

Write the four $V_9$-coset layers as $C,U,V,W$ of sizes $q,31-q,k,16-k$, with $k\ge8$. Every pair of quotient points is an affine hyperplane in $V_2$, so $q+k\le31$. Thus $20\le q\le23$ and $8\le k\le31-q$: ten shapes. For each nonzero internal character, the two-layer $C,U$ Walsh restriction is $|w_C|+|w_U|\le2q-31$ by maximality of $q$. For $C,V$ the same bound applies when $q+k=31$. Otherwise $|w_C|+|w_V|\le15$, because $2\cdot31-47=15$ bounds the absolute values of all four signed quotient Walsh sums; averaging two sign choices cancels the other two terms, and both relative signs on $C,V$ are possible. These bounds include their automatic parity improvements. For instance $q+k$ even forces the effective bound below 15 to be at most 14. If any $|w_C|>2q-31-((31-q)\bmod2)$ then the first side is impossible; both drivers record this exact anchor-level rejection.

The two independent searches use the entire $V_9$ census for $C$. An affine coordinate change of $C$ extends to the whole space. By quotient-dependent affine shears plus a translation, $U,V$ can be translated independently without changing $C$ (the fourth translation is then determined). Normalize each of $U,V$ by translating the endpoints of its least nonzero difference to $0,d$ and choosing the smaller of the two residual orientations. Every unordered pair sum of $C$ must be disjoint from those of $U,V$, and $U,V$ must have disjoint distinct-pair sums. When the latter two roles are identical use unordered pairs. The final layer $W$ is selected inside $V_9\setminus(C+U+V)$; its internal distinct pair sums must avoid those of the first three. These conditions exactly exclude a zero-sum quadruple: possible quotient multiplicities are 4, 2+2, or 1+1+1+1. Finally test all nontrivial Walsh sums of the full 47-set against 15. The engines use different least-difference orders, proper graph-color branching, Walsh computations, treatment of candidate pair sums, hole masks, and final-layer recursion. No quadratic filter, node cap, external optimizer or randomized cutoff is used. Some branches generate $V$ already conditional on the selected $\Delta U$ rather than filtering at the join; this changes neither the pairs nor the final candidates. The ledger records every one of the 9,200 shape/source keys in both implementations, including 6,831 exact first-side rejections; the other 2,369 keys were submitted to both complete engines, not estimated by sampling.

## 2. Large 32-, 33-, or 34-halves and rigidity

Independently of the previous low search, fix each of the 200, 7 and 1 source classes of sizes 32, 33 and 34 in $V_{10}$, respectively. The opposite layer need only be a Sidon $(47-|A|)$-set disjoint in difference support from $A$: this is necessary and sufficient for a two-layer Sidon set. The pair-rooted search (unique least difference) and point-rooted search (all origins, canonical translation at leaves) separately enumerate **all** eligible opposite layers up to translation. Both return zero at 206 sources. At size-32 sources 198 and 199 they give respectively 992 and 1,984 distinct normalized 15-sets and yield the identical labeled lists. Each listed 15-set is exactly one point deleted from an opposite 16-set of the known extremal 48 configurations. The check constructs 186 explicit 48-set representatives (62 and 124 respectively), verifies the invertible affine map of each to Chen, and equates the entire normalized deletion-list with both fresh output lists. This is stronger than merely comparing their cardinalities; a blanket assertion that an arbitrary 47-set extends was not used.

The complete affine group of Chen has order 80 and exactly three point orbits of size 16. For one deletion per orbit the checker enumerates every additional point: outside the remaining 47 vertices, an eligible point must not be a sum of three distinct remaining vertices. The singleton list is exactly the point just deleted, in each case. Their maximum half is 32. Distinct point orbits therefore give inequivalent 47-sets, since a hypothetical puncture isomorphism would take the unique extending point to the unique extending point and hence extend to an automorphism of Chen. This proves the stability theorem.

For each of the three punctures we also looked for a 17-point Sidon set in a parallel opposite 11-dimensional layer. The two-layer criterion is again exact; translate the opposite layer to contain 0. Both the least-pair-root search and an independent all-point-rooted search over the entire 2048 positions return **zero for each of the three classes**. Consequently a Sidon set of at least 64 in $V_{12}$ has no 47-point hyperplane section: at least 17 points in its opposite layer would supply a forbidden 17-subset. In particular ≥65 already excluded 48 from the preceding classification, so its maximum half is now at most 46. This is stronger than a statement about one favored coordinate or graph representation.

## 3. Code classification and the twelve-dimensional bound

A binary $[46,35,\ge5]$ code corresponds to a 47-point Sidon set with one marked point, modulo affine equivalence preserving the mark: translate the mark to zero, take the other 46 as columns of a rank-11 parity check, and use the absence of dependencies of sizes 1–4. Conversely, coordinate equivalence of codes corresponds to a permutation of columns and invertible row operations. Every 47-set spans, since $s(10)=34$. By the unique 48-extension the two marks in Chen, deleted point first and retained code origin second, form an ordered pair of distinct points. Under its full automorphism group the 2,256 ordered choices split as 27 orbits of size 80 and six of size 16. Thus exactly 33 inequivalent $[46,35,5]$ codes occur. For each orbit representative the checker finds 756 weight-five words by matching a triple of parity columns to a pair with the same sum; their supports span dimension 35. In particular the stated distance is attained, and any linear one-coordinate extension to distance at least six must agree with the parity functional on a spanning set. It is therefore the parity extension. Puncturing an arbitrary $[47,35,\ge6]$ code injectively gives one of the preceding codes; homogeneous parity checks $(x,1)$ on a 47-set $S$ exhibit its even extension (with the translated origin coordinate reinstated). Two such parity extensions are equivalent exactly when their 47-sets are affine equivalent: equivalence induces a linear map on the parity-check span mapping the entire affine span $x_{12}=1$ bijectively to itself. There are therefore exactly three binary $[47,35,6]$ codes, all even. The distances 5 and 6 themselves were already known in [Grassl's $[46,35]$](https://www.codetables.de/BKLC/BKLC.php?q=2&n=46&k=35) and [$[47,35]$](https://www.codetables.de/BKLC/BKLC.php?q=2&n=47&k=35) tables; these statements concern complete classification, not new distance values.

Suppose $N=79$ in $V_{12}$. Center the XOR at zero. If the maximum half were at most 45, the allowed off-zero Walsh values would be $-9,-5,-1,3,7,11$. Then $P(w)=-(w+9)(w-3)(w-7)(w-11)$ is nonnegative at every possible value. On summing off-zero and using the four moments (including the centered third moment), one obtains $-8,346,624$ if the origin belongs, or $-16,014,336+294,912t$ otherwise, where at most 26 disjoint triples sum to zero. Both are negative, contradiction. By the opposite-17 test and the preceding exclusion of 48, $m=46$. Take the maximum inner half $a$ among all these 46-sections. Its lower slab gives $a\ge28$. The opposite 33 points split into two quarters of sizes at most $46-a$, one at least 17, so $a\le29$. The preceding 46/29 exclusion rules out $a=29$. For $a=28$ the two possible quarter profiles are $(28,18,17,16)$ and $(28,18,18,15)$. The signed certificate uses the same Fourier indicator masses and complete within-span tag enumeration as the prior $s(12)\le79$ proof. More explicitly, choose translations centering the one odd layer's XOR and rooting the first two of the three even layers. The one remaining translation is determined. (Exactly three translations are fixed: one odd and two even.) Up to a choice of basis the three labeled even XORs admit 16 possibilities, hence 32 certificates over the two profiles. The off-principal signed quotient cap is 13 and any layer pair with total 46 obey $|w_i|+|w_j|\le10$. Global exact totals of $1,w_i^2,w_i^4,w_i^2w_j^2,\prod_i w_i$ and spectrum tag counts are determined by Sidon and orthogonality. For the four layer, internal-difference and cross-difference indicator sets, the disjoint sumsets in corresponding quotient pairs and their complements, inversion on $h$ in the XOR span gives upper/lower bounds on each indicator and pairwise translate intersection. Each integer certificate uses all profile multiplicities nonnegatively and upper-bound multipliers nonnegatively, and yields a strictly negative residual RHS. The bundled verifier reconstructs all signed columns and row sides exactly, and two genuine 63-point subsets of the known 66-set with full-rank three even XORs test the underlying arithmetic. Alternatively Jaffe's no $[29,11,10]$ result excludes $a\le28$ outright by the residual argument in the preceding packet. Thus there is no 79-set. This leaves **$66\le s(12)\le78$**, not a decision at 78 or 67.

Consequently $d_2(78,66)=4$ and $d_2(79,66)=5$: a rank-12 parity check of a hypothetical $[78,66,\ge5]$ would give 79 Sidon points after adjoining zero, and any $[79,66,\ge6]$ punctures to such a code. Distance four at length 78 follows from 78 spanning check columns in $x_0=1$; distance five at length 79 is given by [the table's construction](https://www.codetables.de/BKLC/BKLC.php?q=2&n=79&k=66), or by shortening the preceding packet's explicit $[80,67,5]$. The resulting 79 columns are bundled explicitly: the verifier checks rank 13 and all 3,160 distinct pair sums including zero as the additional 80th point. The corresponding [old length-78 range](https://www.codetables.de/BKLC/BKLC.php?q=2&n=78&k=66) was 4–5. No claim about a maintained table having accepted these research calculations is implied.

## Reproducibility

Run `python3 -O replay.py --stage audit` for source hashes, every per-engine stored trace, explicit high maps, actual 47-point positive controls at the relaxed maximum 32, the automorphism/orbit computations, the two independent 17-opposite searches on all three punctures, 32 signed certificates and two positive signed-spectrum controls. Run `python3 -O replay.py --stage full --jobs 2` to regenerate the 208 large-source jobs and all 9,200 low-source jobs, or use `--stage high` and `--stage low` separately. Requires only C++17 and the Python standard library. Additional lower bound $66$ and initial exhaustive catalogues have direct source links above. All source-key results are deterministic; incomparable node counts are not used to establish equality. The public size-80 exclusion is a prerequisite, not recomputed here.
