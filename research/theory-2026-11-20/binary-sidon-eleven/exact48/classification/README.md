# The 48-point Sidon extremizers in eleven binary dimensions
**Theorem.** Every 48-point Sidon set in $\mathbb F_2^{11}$ is affinely equivalent to Chen's set. Consequently the binary linear codes of parameters [47,36,5] have exactly three coordinate-permutation equivalence classes; there is exactly one [48,36,6] code. Among *all* six-bit APN functions, the maximal Walsh linearity 32 is attained in one CCZ class, the known Gold extension ($D_7$). In dimension 12 the unrestricted maximum Sidon cardinality lies between 66 and 81 inclusive. This last interval is not an exact determination.

This is a finite, computer-assisted classification. It does not presume a symmetry of the extremizer, the quadraticity of the APN function, or any construction of the linear code. All statements concern binary linear codes unless specified otherwise. The result is supported by the replay below, not claimed as independently peer reviewed.

Let $V_j=\mathbb F_2^j$. A Sidon set has no four distinct elements summing to zero; write $\Delta A$ for the sums of unordered pairs of distinct elements of $A$. The previously certified value $s(11)=48$ and the source orbit census in dimensions 9 and 10, with complete replay by labeled incidence masses, are available in [the pinned proof](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/8022fae01a2c79356c0cb3c9123a778bac0fb986). We reuse its exact files d9.txt, d10.txt (SHA-256 verified). We distinguish new classification from the prior value theorem and from the classical [Chen construction](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.133262).

## The reduction to a short shape list

For $S\subset V_d$, $w(u)=\sum_{x\in S}(-1)^{u\cdot x}$. At $u\ne0$ this character cuts $S$ into layers of size $(n\pm w(u))/2$. From orthogonality and the Sidon property the second and fourth moments off zero are $P_2=2^dn-n^2$, $P_4=2^d(3n^2-2n)-n^4$. The constant $3n^2-2n$ counts the ordered zero-sum quadruples of at most two distinct repeated elements.

For $n=48,d=11$ we have $P_2=96,000,P_4=8,650,752$. Suppose every half contains at most 29, i.e. $|w(u)|\le10$ off zero. Set $g=\bigoplus S$. If $g=0$, all the coefficients are divisible by four, hence $P_4\le64P_2$, impossible. Otherwise on $H=g^\perp$ the nontrivial coefficients are divisible by four and have magnitude at most 8. On the complementary 1024 characters they are $2\pmod4$ and satisfy $4\le w^2\le100$, so $w^4\le104w^2-400$. Orthogonality on $H$ gives $T=\sum_{u\in H\setminus 0} w(u)^2=1024(48+2[ g\in\Delta S])-48^2$; the two off-diagonal ordered solutions with sum $g$ exist exactly when $g\in\Delta S$. Thus $P_4\le64T+104(P_2-T)-409,600\le7,700,480$, a contradiction. By the certified $s(10)=34$, the largest half has one of the five sizes $m=30,31,32,33,34$.

**High marks.** For $m=32,33,34$, fix a representative of the 200,7,1 affine classes respectively of size $m$ in $V_{10}$. Independently translate the opposite layer $B$; the resulting set is Sidon exactly when $B$ is Sidon and $\Delta A\cap\Delta B=\varnothing$. The first enumeration includes $0\in B$ and keeps its lexicographically first translate through zero, requiring also that the maximal section is $m$. The second uses the least-difference-pair normalization and never imposes a maximality condition. Both enumerate the entire affine translation classes, not only the first witness, and compare representatives after per-layer canonical translation. For every source but two there are no completions; for source indices 198 and 199 in the combined zero-indexed catalog there are 62 and 124 respectively. Every one of the resulting 186 configurations is mapped to the same specified Chen set by its own invertible binary affine map, checked on all 2048 words.

**Low marks.** Among all sections of maximum size $m=30$ or 31 take one whose own largest internal section has greatest size $a$. For $m=31$, applying $(w^2-1)(w^2-7^2)\le0$ under a putative maximum 19 to its internal 10-dimensional Walsh coefficients and summing contradicts the exact $P_2,P_4$, so $a\ge20$. For $m=30$, if $a\le19$ then $|w|\le8$. On the orthogonal complement of the 30-set XOR the coefficient is $2\pmod4$, hence $|w|\le6$ off zero. On this complement the nontrivial second moment is at least $512\cdot30-30^2$. Therefore $P_4\le64(1024\cdot30-30^2)-28(512\cdot30-30^2)=1,503,600<1,893,360=P_4$, impossible (the bound on the orthogonal complement includes the zero-XOR possibility).

The four quotient layers have sizes $a,b=m-a,c,d=48-m-c$ with $c\ge d$. Every pair sum is at most $m$ because any two quotient cosets form an affine hyperplane. This gives the following exhaustive list of possible $(a,b,c,d)$ and Walsh limits for the pairs of sets $A,U$ and $A,V$:

For a pair of total size $a+x=m$, every internal half has size $\le a$ by the **global choice** of $a$ across all maximum $m$ sections. Hence for every nonzero character of $V_9$ the sum of the magnitudes $|w_A|+|w_X|$ is at most $2a-m$. ## Finite tests and scope

For fixed $A\subset V_9$, the nonzero quotient directions supporting $U,V$ are independent. An affine shear fixing $A$ can translate $U,V$ independently, also translating the final layer $W$ by the sum of the translations. Thus it is complete to enumerate $U,V$ up to their own translations. A zero-sum of four distinct points has quotient multiplicities four in one layer, two in each of two, or one in each of all four. Consequently each layer must be Sidon, the six pairs of distinct-pair-sum sets must be disjoint, and $W$ must be contained in the hole set $V_9\setminus(A+U+V)$. These conditions are sufficient as well. The goal at a completed $W$ is checked again against **all** nonzero Walsh characters: a Sidon completion whose largest half exceeds the present $m$ is not a witness of this maximal-mark case.

Both implementations normalize a nontrivial translation orbit by the unique pair realizing its least difference (degree order vs. ordinary numerical order); put that pair at $0,d$, forbid all earlier pair differences, and retain the smaller of the two translates related by $d$. They use different clique branching (full proper greedy coloring vs. branching on the vertices outside $t-1$ stable sets), different character arithmetic, independent hole masks and independent fourth-layer recursion. Their cutoffs are mathematical bounds, not time or node caps. For small first lists the second layer can be generated conditional on the first's used differences rather than by a later disjointness join; this changes neither completeness nor the set of admissible pairs. The first algorithm optionally filters an $m=31$ prefix by a quadratic parity identity: the opposite size is 17, so translate its XOR to zero. At every $u$, its weight $k$ is even, $w=17-2k$, and $\binom k2\equiv k/2\pmod2$ is a binary polynomial of degree at most two without constant term in $u$. When the known coefficients of the size-31 prefix and the global bound $|w_{31}|+|w_{17}|\le14$ force $k/2\bmod2$, record the corresponding linear equation in 55 monomial coefficients. Inconsistency forbids the prefix. The second algorithm deliberately does not use this filter. Neither uses it in the $m=30$ cases. A size-32 control from Chen's witness forces each solver to return an actual separately revalidated 48-set.

## Consequences of the classification

1. There is exactly one affine class of optimal 48-Sidon sets in dimension 11. By adjoining zero to parity-check columns, binary [47,36,5] codes correspond to such sets with a distinguished point, modulo affine changes taking distinguished point to distinguished point. Chen's affine group has order 80 and three orbits of 16 points. For each possible target of the chosen origin the group routine grows a partial isomorphism between equal-size binary subspaces. It tries every image of the next independent source point consistent with membership of every vector in the enlarged span. Rooted color filters do not identify vertices: they perform whole-space Walsh transforms, mix the resulting values pointwise in the dual and transform back; every such round commutes with change of binary basis (the dual action is contragredient). Modular 64-bit arithmetic likewise preserves these equalities; any color collision can only cost extra search. At full dimension the point permutation is emitted. Each map is generated on exactly one branch, since the next source vector is chosen deterministically at every node. Thus the reported stabilizer is exact, not an estimate from color fingerprints. It follows that the three rooted code classes are all of them. [Batten, Davidson and Storme](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.825813) previously produced three inequivalent codes *within their construction*; that is different from an unrestricted classification.
2. Any binary [48,36,6] code punctures injectively to a [47,36,$\ge5$] code. In every one of the 48 possible roots of Chen the resulting 47-column binary kernel has 846 weight-five words, spanning rank 36. This is checked by matching every triple of columns with the unique pair of the same sum, then binary row reduction of their 47-bit support vectors. For an extension attaining distance six, the added coordinate must be 1 on all these words; the parity linear form has exactly those values on the spanning set and is therefore forced. The common even extension is represented by check columns $(a,1)$ for all $a\in A$; translating $A$ gives row equivalence. There is precisely one binary [48,36,6] code. Its 6,768 weight-six supports span it and form a $2$-$(48,6,90)$ design: list all equal-sum pairs of disjoint unordered triples and deduplicate their six-point unions, then check generation rank and each of the 1,128 coordinate-pair counts. A design automorphism preserves the generated code, and a coordinate permutation preserving the code preserves its minimum supports. Finally a permutation preserving this code induces a linear automorphism of its parity-check column span, preserving the spanning affine hyperplane of the points $(a,1)$; this is exactly an affine automorphism of the 48-set. Therefore the full design automorphism group has order 80. The uniqueness assertion here is only for the design from such an extremal code; it does not classify unrestricted designs of these parameters.
3. The graph of an arbitrary six-variable APN function is a 64-Sidon set in $V_{12}$: two pairs with the same nonzero sum would give four roots of one derivative equation. Its linearity is the maximum absolute Walsh character sum on the graph over characters with nonzero output component. The other nontrivial characters (input only) sum to zero. A maximal character of absolute value $L$ cuts $32+L/2$ graph points into one affine hyperplane. Thus $s(11)=48$ gives $L\le32$, with equality exactly when a section has 48 points. By the classification, that section is Chen. The separately certified [unique 16-point Chen extension](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/285278f551737055845329881b14771bdda2d91e) therefore makes the graph affinely equivalent to the known Gold extension; graph equivalence is CCZ equivalence. The original APN function need not be quadratic. The result classifies only maximal-linearity maps, not all six-variable APN functions. The equivalence to $D_7$ in [the September 2026 catalogue, v2 Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22394v2#A1.T1) is also checked by an explicit rank-12 linear map. The [prior census, Table 1 row 7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-020-00427-1) determined that class's 19 EA classes, with degrees 2,3,4 occurring 1,15,3 times. No new EA-internal enumeration is claimed.
4. Any Sidon set of at least 65 points in $V_{12}$ cannot have a maximal 48-section: a 17-element subset of its opposite layer would give seventeen distinct 16-element subsets. By the Chen completion theorem all sixteen-subsets would have to be translates of one another. Two different 16-subsets of a 17-set have intersection 15, whereas two distinct translates of a Sidon 16-set meet in at most two points. Contradiction. Thus its maximal half has at most 47. Section “82 via signed profiles” gives $s(12)\le81$, not its exact size (known lower 66).

## 82 via signed profiles

For a hypothetical 82-Sidon set, the elementary second/fourth moment slab forces largest half $m\ge47$, hence $m=47$ by the preceding corollary. Inside a 47-half the same slab with odd coefficient parity forces its largest internal half $a\ge29$. The other 35 points split into two quarters, one of size $\ge18$, forcing $a\le47-18=29$. Thus the unique shape is $(29,18,18,17)$.

Write the four $V_{10}$ layers as $S_i$, sizes $a_i$, and $v_i(u)=\sum_{x\in S_i}(-1)^{u\cdot x}$. Normalize the XORs of both odd layers (indices $0,3$) to zero and choose $0\in S_1$: three of the four quotient fibers can be translated arbitrarily by an affine map; the fourth receives their XOR. For even layers $1,2$ write $g_i=\bigoplus S_i$, which is unaffected by translation. The five possible labeled configurations in their span, up to basis change, are $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,2)$. For a given $u$, tag $b$ is its restriction to $G=\langle g_1,g_2\rangle$. The signed Walsh values satisfy $v_i\equiv a_i-2u\cdot g_i\pmod4$ for even layers, $v_i\equiv a_i\pmod4$ for odd layers. For $u\ne0$ all four signed quotient sums of the $v_i$ have magnitude at most $12$, and for each pair of total 47, $|v_i|+|v_j|\le11$ by maximal $a$. The independent verifier enumerates the full signed integer profile space under exactly these conditions.

With $Q=1024$, the global rows are $\sum v_i^2=Qa_i$, $\sum v_i^4=Q(3a_i^2-2a_i)$, $\sum v_i^2v_j^2=Qa_i a_j$ (disjoint pair sums), $\sum\prod_i v_i=0$ (no four-layer collision), constant and tag counts, and $\sum v_1=Q$ by the rooted point. For $h\in G$ let $\chi=(-1)^{u\cdot h}$. Fourier inversion gives $\sum\chi v_i/Q=1_{S_i}(h)\in[0,1]$ and $\sum\chi v_i v_j/Q=1_{S_i+S_j}(h)\in[0,1]$, since all cross sums in a fixed pair are distinct. On two complementary cross pairs these indicators sum to at most one, by no four-layer collision. For $h\ne0$, $\sum\chi v_i^2/Q$ is twice $1_{\Delta S_i}(h)$, and their sum is at most two. All inequalities used in the five integer separators follow from these facts.

Subtract the explicit $u=0$ profile. Each certificate is an integer linear combination of remaining rows, with nonnegative coefficients for upper-bound rows, checked to be nonnegative on every admissible column and strictly negative on the RHS. For the five label configurations the column counts are 112,220,220,225,441 and the RHS values are $-6018080,-2883484,-2883484,-1586268,-3602900$. A 64-point subset of the explicit known 66-Sidon set independently tests the signed formulas at a genuinely rank-two pair of even XORs. The witness files reproduce this finite arithmetic with Python integers.


## Reproduction

Python 3.10+ and a C++17 compiler are sufficient. No Python packages beyond the standard library, network access, SAT solver or precompiled executable are required. Run from the directory containing this file:

    python3 -O run.py structural ledgers high derived corollaries --jobs=2
    python3 -O run.py 30 31 --jobs=4

The first command verifies both input-catalog hashes and every archived raw shard header/stop status, the complete key coverage, independent per-key count agreement, every aggregate, and both corrected caps. It then redoes the high-mark enumeration, maps, code/design calculations, signed 82-point proof and APN graph equivalence. The second reruns both independent low-mark engines and checks every per-anchor recomputed count (not just the final absence of witnesses) against its original frozen ledger. It skips only immediate impossibilities proven by the anchor's own exact character magnitudes. To distribute work, pass --engine ex31 or --engine second48 with stage 30 or 31; maximum-30 is the costly stage. Neither search has a time or node cutoff. The supplied ledger captures separate per-job timing where available, but elapsed time depends on CPU, compiler and concurrent load.

The source anchor censuses themselves can be regenerated from the pinned earlier nine- and ten-dimensional classification in the introductory paragraph. The unique-opposite-16 subdirectory is included as the unchanged standalone companion; its README intentionally states the narrower result of that earlier stage. SHA256SUMS identifies all the delivered contents except itself. Old exploratory spreadsheets, unstable floating-point discovery models and superseded bounds are not inputs to this proof.
