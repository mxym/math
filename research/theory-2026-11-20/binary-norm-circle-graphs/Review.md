# Independent review, 2026-11-20 UTC

The proof was checked directly: trace polarization has only the exponent (1+2^m) contributing, the translated quadratic form has sign sum (-q) in the claimed cases, and an (m)-dimensional singular space would force sign sum (+q). The affine-chart cases reduce to the ellipse, parabola and inverse function. The frozen archive was also unpacked into an empty directory, its manifest verified, and `python3 -O check.py` reproduced all five lines of `controls.txt`. The theorem is analytic; those finite checks are controls, not the proof.

The July–20 November 2026 pass used indexed arXiv and IACR results for APN graphs, affine graph projections, regular hyperovals, binary Sidon sets and norm-one sets, including preprint version histories. No match for the projection obstruction was located. [Thornburgh, arXiv:2609.27731v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27731v1) gives a Sidon upper bound in dimensions congruent to 3 modulo 4 and a nonlinearity corollary, rather than a graph obstruction for an even-dimensional norm circle. The distance paper mentioned in the frozen text is by [Maria Mihaila and Darrion Thornburgh, arXiv:2509.02280v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02280v3), last revised in June 2026; it covers distances between APN functions, including odd-dimensional inverse functions, not the exterior-chart theorem. [Nagy's preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05887) has two 2022 versions, followed by the journal article cited in the proof. GitHub code searches for the exact theorem wording and for regular hyperovals did not find it already in the repository. This is a bounded check, not a global priority claim.
