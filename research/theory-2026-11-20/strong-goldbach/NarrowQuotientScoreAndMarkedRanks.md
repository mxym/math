# A subpower quotient score and genuinely signed rank transfers

This gives an exact centered change of the joint Goldbach ledger, with no new large-prime quotient rows. It makes the whole fixed-target low-rank, polynomially rough quotient sector nonpositive on reflected ranks three and four, apart from a priced narrow prime collar. Separately, an actual marked part of the resulting narrow quotient component cancels rank by rank, with large physical contributions of both signs. The higher-rank and small-quotient-factor terms remain.

## 1. Reference and new fixed-target observable

Keep the exact setting of [JointShortDivisorProjection](JointShortDivisorProjection.md), including \(N=2x\), \(L=\log x\), \(T=\log L\), \(M_N=\mathfrak S(N)x/(2L)\), actual primes \(x^{.10}<s<x^{.14}\), and the physical conditions \(n=sq\in(x/2,x]\), \(m=N-n\), \((n,N)=1\). Write \(W_s(q)=(1-t)(1-2t)^2\), \(t=\log s/\log n\). Retain
\[
 B_q=x^{.501},\quad B_m=x^{.5+T^{-20}},\quad z_q=\exp(L/T^3),\quad
 z_m=\exp(L/T^{23}),\quad \rho_j(y)=1_{P^-(y)>z_j},\quad
 K=\lfloor T^2\rfloor.
\]
For \(\delta=T^{-1/2}\) and \(X_0=x^{1-\delta}\) define the genuinely narrower quotient score
\[
 C(q;s)=\sum_{d\mid q,\ d< s q/X_0}\mu(d).
                                                                    \tag{1}
\]
There is no replacement of the actual equation \(m+sq=N\). Keep \(T_m(m)=\sum_{d\mid m,\ d<m/B_m}\mu(d)\) on the other side. Write \(T_q(q)=\sum_{d\mid q,d<q/B_q}\mu(d)\), and keep \(T_m(m)=\sum_{d\mid m,d<m/B_m}\mu(d)\). On central \(m\) put \(\zeta=m/B_m\) and define the prime-free reflected correction
\[
 S(m)=\sum_{d\mid m,\ d<\zeta}\mu(d)
                  \sum_{r\mid d,\ r\ \mathrm{prime}}2\,1_{r\ge\sqrt\zeta}.
                                                                    \tag{2}
\]
It is exactly zero on prime \(m\). On each physical row put \(\mathcal F(q,m;s)=T_q(q)T_m(m)+(C(q;s)-T_q(q))S(m)\). Then the rigorous new identity is
\[
 Z_N:=\sum_{s,q\ \mathrm{prime}} W_s(q)\Lambda(m)
    =\sum_{\substack{q,m\ \mathrm{squarefree\ composite}\\
                     \rho_q(q)\rho_m(m)=1,\ P^+(q)\le B_q}}
                  W_s(q)(\log m)\,\mathcal F(q,m;s)+O_A(xL^{-A}).     \tag{3}
\]
The reflected largest-factor restriction is not imposed in (3): the newly admitted reflected high-semiprime correction is kept literally, and all reflected giant rank at least three terms still vanish. On \(P^-(q)>x^\delta\), \(C=1\) exactly. If in addition \(\Omega(q)\le4\), then the whole reflected rank-three and rank-four sector away from the half-prime collar is nonpositive; the actual collar for those fixed ranks is \(o(M_N)\). The original sufficient upper threshold is above \(J M_N\) by \(.010M_N\), with \(J=\log(7/5)-.1408\), and still needs strict margin. Neither a separate negative subset nor the signed nulls below are independent reserve.

## 2. Exact distribution beneath the short divisor cutoffs

We use two harmonic facts at a subpower endpoint. The uniform masked Mertens bound in [PrimeSupportedSmoothNeckCancellation](PrimeSupportedSmoothNeckCancellation.md), Section 4, holds for polynomial masks \(H\) at every \(u\ge \exp(T^4)\); its proof uses ordinary zero-free-region Mertens and the Rankin parameter \(1/T\) on the inverse Euler factors at primes of \(H\). Hence \(\sum_{d\le u,(d,H)=1}\mu(d)/d=O_A(L^{-A})\), and the corresponding maximal \(\mu(d)\) bound on intervals of scale \(u\) is \(O_A(uL^{-A})\). We also need \(\mu(d)/\phi(d)\) at subpower scales, where the polynomial-endpoint form of the Goldston–Yıldırım lemma is not being quoted. Convolve the masked \(\mu(d)/d\) with the multiplicative ratio whose coefficients at \(p^j\) are \(-1/((p-1)p^j)\) for \(j\ge1\), \(p\nmid H\), and zero at forbidden primes. These coefficients have bounded absolute mass even with the factor \(d^{1/4}\), as is seen directly from their Euler product. Splitting at \(\sqrt u\) proves \(\sum_{d\le u,(d,H)=1}\mu(d)/\phi(d)=O_A(L^{-A})\) whenever \(\sqrt u\ge\exp(T^4)\), including every endpoint used below. Strict endpoints have the same bound because their possible omitted single term is log-small. Bounds in this paragraph are uniform at any of the allowed polynomial masks.

We first prove the centered null \((C-T_q)S\) with both rough guards, but *temporarily without* \(P^+(q)\le B_q\). Both prime restrictions can be omitted **exactly**: \(C-T_q=0\) on every true prime quotient, and \(S=0\) on every true reflected prime. Open the true divisors in the two cutoffs and the two Bonferroni rough polynomials of degree \(K\), with \(e_q\le x^{T^{-1}}\), \(e_m\le x^{T^{-21}}\). Opening \(S\) merely gives one additional prime label \(r\mid d_m\), of log-power multiplicity, and the literal test \(m\le B_m r^2\) along with \(m>B_m d_m\). The reduced classes for \(n=sq\) are the same as the Joint note: \(n\equiv0\bmod s\operatorname{lcm}(d_q,e_q)\) and \(n\equiv N\bmod \operatorname{lcm}(d_m,e_m)\), each modulus coprime to \(N\) and to one another on the actual support. The density is \(\phi(N)/(N s\operatorname{lcm}(d_q,e_q)\operatorname{lcm}(d_m,e_m))\). With old \(T_q\) the floor count exponent is at most \(1-.501+1-b_m+T^{-1}+T^{-21}+o(1)<1\) with fixed reserve (using the harmonic \(1/s\)); with \(C\) it is at most \(.14+\delta+1-b_m+T^{-1}+T^{-21}+o(1)<1\). At fixed \(e_q\) the decomposition \(d_q=a k\), \(a\mid e_q\), \((k,e_q)=1\), followed by the exact conditional harmonic, gives masked \(\mu(k)/k\) log-small in both cases. For \(C\) the minimum endpoint is \(\gg x^{\delta-T^{-1}}\), and for \(T_q\) it is polynomial. The exact moving cutoff for \(C\) is \(d_q<n/X_0\). The fixed-label integrals retain every interval. The usual Cauchy–Shiu factorial estimate restores both rough guards: the extra score \(S\) is bounded by fixed divisor powers, so the same proof and log reserve apply.

## 3. Actual large-quotient removal for this prime-free test

If \(p\mid q\) is prime and \(p>X_0/s\), a composite \(q=p u\) has \(C(q;s)=0\): the prime \(p\) is too large to occur in (1), and all divisors of \(u\) are included in it. Such a \(p\) cannot repeat, and is unique. To restore the original \(B_q=x^{.501}\), it therefore suffices to remove the unique actual prime
\[
                       B_q<p\le X_0/s .                              \tag{3}
\]
Here \(V=x/(s p)\ge x^\delta\), with \(u\asymp V\) on the literal central interval; in particular \(u\ge2\), so these are not a hidden prime-quotient term. Also \(p^2>x\), so it is never in \(u\). Both \(C(q;s)\) and the quotient roughness test use only the cofactor \(u\) (\(p\gg x^\delta\)). Here \(T_q=0\); and \(S=0\) on reflected primes, so no extra primality exclusion is needed for this particular correction.

Open the true \(d_m,r\) in (2) and rough label \(e_m\), retaining the two literal reflected endpoints, and use reduced modulus \(D_m=\operatorname{lcm}(d_m,e_m)\) below \(x^{1/2}/L^{C_A}\) for all fixed \(A\). The true source is \(n=p(su)\) with the large **actual prime** \(p\) as its SW factor. Both source lengths are polynomial: \(p>B_q\), \(s u\ge s>x^{.10}\). The signed quotient divisor label \(d\mid u\) from (1) and the rough label \(e_q\mid u\) belong entirely to the \(s u\)-coefficient after separating the product tests. That coefficient is divisor-bounded. The model before these comparisons are restored is the literal reduced \(1_{(n,D_m)=1}/\phi(D_m)\), with polynomial target mask \(N\).

The endpoints (3) and (1) use the independent cuts \(s p\le X_0\) and \((s p)(u/d)>X_0\), respectively; the latter is the actual \(n/d>X_0\), not a free approximation to that inequality. Round the factors in short relative meshes on the prime-axis \(p\) and their respective cofactor labels \(s\), \(s(u/d)\), preserving true SW of \(p\) on independent intervals. A wrong inclusion in either comparison requires an actual divisor of \(n\) in a relative \(O(L^{-F})\) strip about \(X_0\). Counting by that divisor first gives \(O(xL^{-F}+x^\delta)\) possible rows before fixed divisor weights. Global divisor moments and Cauchy give arbitrary log reserve for these literal rows, even with \(\tau(N-n)^C\) from the reflected short tests. The centered error is paid by the harmonic divisor moment in \(D_m\). The separately moving reflected prefixes and smooth weights use the Shiu and log-relative meshes of the Joint note. Thus the SW prime and all single-slot divisor weights remain genuinely independent, with a log-power number of pieces. Shape selection causes no hidden modulus-sized loss.

Restore the actual tests in the coprime model and fix \(s,p,D_m,e=e_q\). The fixed mask is \(H=N D_m\) on \(p,s,u\). If \((s p e,H)>1\), the row is empty. Split \(d=a k\) with \(a\mid e\), \((k,e)=1\), so \(u=e k v\), and \(\mu(d)=\mu(a)\mu(k)\). All actual rough prime-powers are included correctly because no condition \((v,e)=1\) is imposed. In this opened form the short divisor inequality is exactly \(v>a X_0/(s p e)\). Split at \(k=\sqrt{V/e}\). In the shorter part count coprime \(v\), with weights of uniformly bounded log-power variation, by density \(\phi(H)/H\) and floor \(O(\tau(H))\). The cost \(\sqrt{V/e}\,\tau(H)\) is log-small relative to \(V/e\): for polynomial masks \(\tau(H)\le\exp(O(L/T))\), whereas \(V/e\ge x^{\delta-T^{-1}}\). The bound on \(\tau(H)\) follows, for example, by separating primes below \(L^{1/2}\) (at most \(L^{1/2}\) harmless powers) and above it (at most \(O(L/T)\) factors counted with multiplicity). At a continuous \(u\) the remaining masked \(\mu(k)/k\) cutoff is the minimum of \(\sqrt{V/e}\) and \(s p u/(a X_0)\), both of scale at least \(x^{(\delta-T^{-1})/2}/O(1)\). It is log-small by Section 2. In the longer part fix \(v\) first, using its exact scalar lower bound. Then \(k\) has a genuine moving interval \(k\asymp V/(ev)\), with the uniform mask \((k,eH)=1\); this length scale is at least \(\sqrt{V/e}\). Signed masked Mertens before harmonic summation over \(v\) gives arbitrary log reserve. Across the \(a\)-choices, the total model bound is \(O_A((V/e)\tau(e)L^{-A})\). Summing \(s,p,D_m,e\) costs only harmonic log powers. This proves the signed giant-quotient term in the **prime-free** correction is null, and hence removes it from the exact centered change. The squareful rough rows in that change have arbitrary log reserve, by the same support Cauchy bound as in the Joint note. Together with the original exact joint identity before eliminating its semiprime collar, this proves (3).

This argument cannot replace \(T_qT_m\) by \(CT_m\) on the old domain: for the latter \(T_m\) equals 1 on reflected primes, and the new giant-quotient term restricted to **true prime \(m\)** is not evaluated by the product-BV calculation. The correction (2) is what avoids that distinct signed debt.

## 4. Pointwise rank sign and its actual collar price

On squarefree \(m\) with a prime \(r>B_m\), the full sum of (2) is over divisors of the genuine cofactor \(v=m/r\), since none carries \(r\) and all of them lie strictly below \(\zeta\). For \(\omega(v)\ge2\), the additive prime-marked Möbius sum vanishes exactly: fix a marked prime and cancel the complete sum over the other prime bits. So this correction adds **no** reflected high rank \(\ge3\) rows. For a high reflected semiprime it is the exact nonpositive number \(-2\,1_{v\ge\sqrt\zeta}\), with \(T_m=0\). For a surviving near-half semiprime, \(T_m=1\) and \(S=0\) because neither prime is below \(\zeta\); this is precisely the old \(o(M_N)\) collar.

For a squarefree quotient \(q\) with \(P^+(q)\le B_q\) and \(\Omega(q)\le4\), \(T_q\le1\). For ranks two and three no divisor of size rank minus one can be below \(q/B_q\), so this is immediate. For rank four, if \(k\) singleton primes lie below \(q/B_q\), at most \(\min\{\binom{k}{2},3\}\) pairs can qualify: an eligible pair uses eligible singletons, and there is at most one short member of each of the three complementary pairs because \(B_q^2>q\). Thus \(T_q=1-k+k_2\le1\). On \(P^-(q)>x^\delta\) we have \(C=1\), so on such low ranks a new high-reflected semiprime coefficient \((1-T_q)S\) is nonpositive.

For reflected ranks three and four with every factor strictly below \(\zeta\), we have \(S(m)\le T_m(m)\le0\). At rank three all singleton divisors are eligible and no pairs are, so \(T_m=-2\); at least one singleton exceeds \(\sqrt\zeta\), giving \(S\le-2\). At rank four, call a prime large if it is at least \(\sqrt\zeta\), small otherwise. Two small primes always form a short pair, and two large primes never do. No triple is short, and if \(j\) pairs are short then \(T_m=-3+j\) with \(j\le3\) by complement. There must be at least one large prime since \(\zeta^2<m\). If it is the only one, all three small-small pairs are short, so no large-small pair can be short and \(S=-2\le T_m=0\). If there are two large ones, one pair is small-small and \(S=2(j-3)=2 T_m\). If there are three large ones, every short pair is mixed and again \(S=2(j-3)=2 T_m\). If all four are large, \(S=-8\le T_m=-3\). On the quotient macro low ranks just specified, the new polynomial is
\[
     \mathcal F=S+T_q(T_m-S)\le S+(T_m-S)=T_m\le0.                   \tag{4}
\]
The comparisons use the literal strict cutoff, including equality at \(\sqrt\zeta\) on the large side.

The omitted prime collar for these fixed ranks is small **on the actual targets**, not just in an averaged exponent simplex. It consists of \(m=r v\) with a true prime \(r\in[\zeta,B_m]\subset[x^{1-b_m},x^{b_m}]\), \(\Omega(v)\le3\), and \(\Omega(q)\le4\). The coefficients are absolutely bounded on these ranks. For fixed \(s,r\) use the same primitive affine forms \(q=q_0+rj\), \(v=v_0-sj\) with determinant \(N\), at free scale \(Y\asymp x/(sr)\), exact target masks and local factors as in the Ungated note. Apply corrected Henriot with the multiplicative local weights \(\lambda^{\omega(q)}\rho_q(q)\), \(\lambda^{\omega(v)}\rho_m(v)\), \(\lambda=1/\log T\), and multiply the bound by \(\lambda^{-7}\) to cover the actual fixed ranks. The Euler factors cost \(O(1)\) since \(\sum_{z_j<p\le 2x}1/p\ll\log T\), and the uniform growth constants do not increase when \(\lambda\le1\). At fixed \(s,r\) the resulting bound is \(O(\mathfrak S(N)Y L^{-2}(\log T)^7)\). The true prime harmonic in \(r\) is \(O(T^{-20}+L^{-A})\); multiplying by \(\log m\) and summing the bounded \(1/s\) harmonic gives \(O(T^{-20}(\log T)^7 M_N)=o(M_N)\). This is a **prime** collar, not an absolute cap for all raw integer divisors near half-scale.

The conclusion only signs the stated low quotient and reflected ranks. For example, five logarithmic factors near .176 (sum .880) give \(T_q=1-5+10=6\), \(C=1\). With three reflected factors near one third, \(T_m=-2,S=-6\), the new coefficient is \(18>0\). Reflected high-semiprime factors near .33 and .67 with that same quotient profile give \(10>0\). These are strict factor-profile signs, not occupancy statements. In particular high reflected semiprimes are a genuinely new physical term for the remaining quotient (higher ranks or small-prime shell); one may not treat them as uniformly favorable using (4).

## 5. Actual marked rank cancellation and paired mass

For a physical quotient define the literal multiplicity
\[
 \omega_s^*(q)=\#\{v\mid q:\ v\text{ prime},\ x^{.4992}\le s v\le x^{.4994}\}.
\]
Then \(\omega_s^*\le2\) because \(v\ge x^{.3592}\) and \(q\le x^{.90}\). On any such mark \(q=v w\) with \(w\ll x^{.5008}\) and \(v<x^{.40}\), so \(q\) is automatically composite and has \(P^+(q)\le B_q\), without an artificial roughness model. For **each fixed reflected rank** \(k\ge2\), on squarefree \(m\) with \(P^+(m)\le B_m\), the weighted marked sum
\[
  \sum_{\substack{\Omega(m)=k,\ m\ \mathrm{squarefree}\\ P^+(m)\le B_m}}
 W_s(q)(\log m) C(q;s)\rho_q(q)\rho_m(m)\omega_s^*(q) G(m)
                       =O_{A,k}(xL^{-A})                           \tag{5}
\]
holds for either genuine reflected test \(G=T_m\) or \(G=S\). There is no claim that \(T_q G\) admits this statement; its divisor cutoff is much longer. Nor is \(k\) allowed to grow in (5).

Open \(C\) on the actual cofactor \(w=q/v\), since \(v\) is too large to occur in (1), and open the rough \(e_q\mid w\) by Bonferroni. The true modulus for \(m\equiv N\) is \(s v\operatorname{lcm}(d,e_q)\le x^{.4994+\delta+T^{-1}}<x^{.5-c}\) eventually, with fixed gap. Here \(s,v\) are distinct polynomial prime labels and both exceed every prime in the lcm; they are uniquely recoverable from the modulus by their bands. The remaining label count costs fixed divisor powers. The literal \(C\) cutoff remains the prefix \(m<N-X_0d\). Replace the true reflected \(k\)-prime source by its reduced coprime model using Maynard full-scale BV: group it as its **largest actual prime** \(r\), of length at least \(x^{1/k}/O(1)\), times its actual remaining prime product, of length \(\ge x^{1-b_m}\). The genuine SW factor \(r\) and both polynomial lengths are real; keep each actual prime factor above \(z_m\) and coprime to \(N\), and squarefree distinctness by the ordered factors. The cofactor coefficient is divisor-bounded. There is no prime SW assertion for an arbitrary conditioned other slot.

For fixed \(k\), the internal subset divisor tests of \(G\) are finitely many. A condition \(d_m<m/B_m\) in them is the actual complementary factor product \(m/d_m>B_m\). Its log-mesh boundary certifies a real divisor of \(m\) in a relative \(O(L^{-F})\) strip about \(B_m\), costing \(O(x L^{-F}+x^{.51})\) rows before weights. The extra \(r_i\ge\sqrt\zeta\) in \(S\) has boundary \(m\) in a relative strip about \(B_m r_i^2\), with \(r_i\mid m\); opening the true factor gives \(O(x L^{-F+O(1)}+x^{.26})\) rows, since \(r_i=x^{.25+o(1)}\) there. The largest-factor ordering across the two source slots changes only if two actual polynomial prime factors have relative distance \(O(L^{-F})\). Summing over an integer factor \(r\ge x^{1/k}/O(1)\) and its possible partner near \(r\), followed by the remaining integer quotient, bounds this by \(O(xL^{-F+1}+x^{1-1/k+o(1)})\). These estimates count physical integers before the bounded-divisor label weights. Cauchy with global divisor moments for \(m,N-m\) pays the actual weighted edge; the model pays the same boundary with its harmonic modulus weights. Thus independent relative meshes preserve genuine SW on the true largest-prime slot. The reflected moving prefix at each literal modulus and smooth weight is handled by the usual Shiu log mesh; label-weighted error follows by Cauchy against the unweighted BV estimate, as in JointShortDivisorProjection.

In the restored coprime model, fix actual \(m\) and \(s,v,e_q\), and discard \((m N,s v e_q)>1\) exactly as required by actual units. With \(d=a k'\), \(a\mid e_q\), \((k',e_q)=1\), the reciprocal factor is \(1/(\phi(s v e_q)\phi(k'))\) since \(s,v>x^\delta\). The finite \(\mu(k')/\phi(k')\) tail masked by \(N m s v e_q\) has cutoff \(\gg x^{\delta-T^{-1}}\), and is log-small by Section 2. This proves the uniform null (5). Possible common prime divisors of \(m\) with \(v\) in the uncensored geometric family are handled by this exact reduced model, not assumed absent. Restoring exact roughness on \(q\) uses the same factorial error, with \(\omega_s^*\le2\) and bounded rank-test weights.

For \(G=S\) equation (5) is directly a signed part of (3) after writing \(\mathcal F=C S+T_q(T_m-S)\). For each fixed rank, it permits a nonnegative multiplier \(1-\omega_s^*/2\) on the \(C S\) term there, deleting actual two-mark rows in this term and halving single marks. It does not delete the remaining \(T_q(T_m-S)\), and need not preserve the earlier pointwise sign accounting on each individual row. It is not an exact-one mark cancellation or an additional independent negative reserve.

There is genuine paired mass in (5), not just a formal zero. On the fixed actual reflected prime pattern \(m=r_1r_2r_3\) with exponents in the disjoint interior bands \((.318,.322),(.328,.332),(.346,.354)\) and \(m\in[1.1x,1.4x]\), both \(T_m=-2\), \(S=-6\). This pattern has cardinality \(\gg x/L\), uniformly under \((m,N)=1\): choose slightly narrower subintervals for the first two true primes, then ordinary PNT for the third in the literal interval \([1.1x/(r_1r_2),1.4x/(r_1r_2)]\), and harmonic prime summation. All these primes are distinct from the whole \(s\)- and \(v\)-bands. For fixed \(s,v\) with \(x^{.4992}\le s v\le x^{.4994}\), apply the dimension-one lower sieve to \(w=(N-m)/(s v)\) for the actual reflected pattern and the congruence \(m\equiv N\pmod{s v}\), sifting up to \(x^\delta\). An auxiliary modulus \(d\le x^{.0004}\) gives total modulus \(s v d\le x^{.4998}\), so the averaged discrepancy over all the true \(s,v\) and all sieve divisors is arbitrarily log-small by the same actual prime-product BV. The base labels are unique and distinct from all sifted primes. Each \(m\)-prime on this pattern is disjoint from every divisor used, so its reduced principal is exactly the pattern size divided by \(\phi(s v d)\). The local function for sifted primes not dividing \(N\) is \(1/(p-1)\); primes dividing \(N\) never divide \(w\) on the unit support. The lower fundamental lemma tends to the dimension-one main (its ratio parameter \(.0004/\delta\to\infty\)), uniformly in \(N\). The sifted Euler product is \((e^{-\gamma}+o(1))\mathfrak S(N)/\log(x^\delta)\), by the explicit local comparison in PrimeSupportedSmoothNeckCancellation and the negligible harmonic sum of primes dividing \(N\) above the cutoff. The harmonic sum over genuine \(s,v\), excluding \(p\mid N\), is bounded below by a positive constant by ordinary polynomial-interval PNT; the width in \(\log_x(s v)\) is the fixed \(.0002\). This gives \(\gg \mathfrak S(N)x/(\delta L^2)\) actual counted tuples. For each \(q=v w\) there, every prime factor exceeds \(x^\delta\), hence \(C=1\), and \(W_s\log m\gg L\). The negative contribution in the actual marked rank-three sum is therefore at least \(c M_N/\delta\) in magnitude with a fixed \(c>0\), for either \(G\); equation (5) forces comparable positive mass in the same complete marked rank, necessarily on the real small-quotient-factor shell. Other reflected patterns of rank three are retained in (5). This argument does not compare the two signs on the selected interior pattern alone, though the same proof of (5) admits that fixed geometric restriction as well.

The unmarked higher-rank and small-quotient-factor terms, and the corresponding new reflected high-semiprime correction, are not controlled by these identities. No unconditional binary Goldbach conclusion follows.
