# A complementary prime transfer for both new high-reflected ranks

This is an exact signed replacement within the quadratic ledger of [SymmetricQuadraticAndGrowingEvenRanks](SymmetricQuadraticAndGrowingEvenRanks.md). It treats both new high-reflected additions, including the former adverse example with a rank-five quotient. The quotient can have any rank on the macro-rough part of the new sign statement. The remaining true small-factor signed part is not bounded here.

## 1. Statement

Keep the quadratic note's true even target \(N=2x\), \(L=\log x\), \(T=\log L\), \(M_N=\mathfrak S(N)x/(2L)\), physical \(s,q,m\), \(W_s\), \(B_q,B_m,z_q,z_m\), exact roughness, and original unit mask. In particular \(B_q=x^{.501}\), \(B_m=x^{.5+T^{-20}}\), \(z_q=x^{T^{-3}}\), \(z_m=x^{T^{-23}}\), \(\delta=T^{-1/2}\), \(X_0=x^{1-\delta}\), and \(C(q;s)=\sum_{d\mid q,d<sq/X_0}\mu(d)\). Write \(m=r v\) for an actual reflected squarefree composite with a prime \(r>B_m\). That prime is unique. In the new quadratic high-reflected part of rank two or three, set \(G(m)=-S_2(m)\ge0\), explicitly
\[
 G(r v)=
 \begin{cases}
  8\dfrac{\log v}{\log m}(1-\dfrac{\log v}{\log m}),&v\text{ prime},\\[4pt]
  16\dfrac{(\log v_1)(\log v_2)}{(\log m)^2},&v=v_1v_2,\ v_1<v_2\text{ primes}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Since \(T_m=0\) at this high reflected factor, the actual signed addition on the old quotient support is \(-\sum W_s(q)(\log m)G(m)(C-T_q)\) there. Other high reflected ranks give no additional quadratic term.

For composite \(q\) define \(U(q)=1_{P^+(q)>B_q}\) and the literal complementary count
\[
 V(q)=\#\{u\mid q:\ u\text{ prime},\ q/u>B_q,\quad
              u\in(z_q,x^{\delta-2/T}]\cup(x^\delta,\infty)\}.
\]
The inclusive low and strict high conditions leave the gap \((x^{\delta-2/T},x^\delta]\). The exact replacement for the high-reflected signed addition, after restoring its genuine rough guards and unit mask, is
\[
 -\sum_{P^+(q)\le B_q} W_s(q)(\log m)G(m)(C-T_q)
  =\sum_{\text{all composite }q} W_s(q)(\log m)G(m)C(q;s)(U(q)-V(q))
             +O_A(xL^{-A}).                                         \tag{1}
\]
Both sides retain the same genuine high reflected rank-two and rank-three families, summed together or separately. On every \(q\) with \(P^-(q)>x^\delta\), \(C=1\) and \(U-V\le0\), **without a rank restriction**. If \(U-V>0\), the entire positive contribution of those rows to (1) is \(O(T^{-1/2}M_N)\). Thus the only other positive part needs \(C<0\) and \(V>U\), in particular at least two actual prime factors below the quotient score cutoff. It is not controlled by the present transfer.

## 2. The true reflected source and the two signed nulls

We first show the signed sum of \((C-T_q)G\) with \(q\) unrestricted in largest factor is \(O_A(xL^{-A})\), and then the same for \(C V G\). True prime \(q\) terms vanish in either (\(C=T_q=1\), and for a prime \(q\) its sole prime divisor has quotient 1). The genuine reflected source is \(m=r v\), with \(r>B_m\) an actual prime. For rank two, the second slot is true prime \(v>z_m\); for rank three it is the squarefree product of two genuine primes \(>z_m\), with its literal weight \((\log v_1)(\log v_2)\) in the \(v\)-coefficient and smooth \(1/(\log m)^2\) outside. Distinct order is unique. The unit condition \((m,N)=1\) is imposed in the product coefficients. For polynomial \(v\ge x^{.0005}\), use [Maynard's full-scale convolution BV, Lemma 8.9](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A39a52f47-3770-4ddd-b7a2-b2a4f7300931/files/srj4305733) with the actual \(r\) as the SW slot, both slots polynomially long, and the literal reduced principal \(1_{(m,A)=1}/\phi(A)\) for true moduli \(A\). The \(r\)-lower bound makes the chosen high prime unique, regardless of the cofactor size or internal label. The central range and moving endpoints may be separated by the same Shiu log-relative meshes used in JointShortDivisorProjection; fractions and log factors here are smooth on their literal ranges. Unit exclusion for the SW prime only removes \(O(L)\) prime values.

For \(v<x^{.0005}\), fix the actual \(v\) first. If \((v,A)>1\), a row in the reduced class \(m\equiv N\pmod A\) is impossible, as it should be in the principal. Otherwise the true residue is \(r\equiv N\overline v\pmod A\). Use ordinary maximal prime BV at scale \(x/v\ge x^{.9995}\), with arbitrary reduced residue. All moduli about to be used lie eventually below \(x^{.4995}\), in fixed power reserve here; the maximal endpoint is a literal \(r\)-prefix after division by the fixed \(v\). The true extra target prime exceptions \(r\mid N\) are excluded with power-small cost (these primes are large). Weighted discrepancy errors at each \(v\) cost \(O_A((x/v)L^{-A})\); their sum, including the rank-three coefficient, is paid by fixed divisor/log harmonic moments of \(v\). The coprime model in this case integrates the unprogressioned true prime \(r\)-density before the same harmonic computation, so it is also possible to use actual \(\pi\) in interval partial summation. It is not asserted that the two-polynomial-length convolution theorem covers near-unit \(v\).

In both branches use exact roughness on \(m\) and replace the \(q\) rough guard by the Bonferroni polynomial with degree \(K=\lfloor T^2\rfloor\). The same factorial error proof from JointShortDivisorProjection applies to these physical rows by Cauchy–Shiu and fixed divisor weights; the extra \(G\) is bounded and \(U,V\) are bounded by \(\tau(q)\). Opening the \(q\)-score divisor \(d\) and roughness label \(e\) (with only primes \(\le z_q\)), the unrestricted null has modulus \(A=s\operatorname{lcm}(d,e)\). For \(T_q\) use the actual \(d<q/B_q\) (hence \(s d\le x^{.499}\)); for \(C\), use the actual \(d<n/X_0\). The \(C V\) null opens an additional **true prime divisor** \(u\) of \(q\) and uses \(A=s\operatorname{lcm}(u,d,e)\). Its literal complement \(q/u>B_q\) is the moving prefix \(m<N-s u B_q\), so \(s u\le x^{.499}\) and \(A\le x^{.499+\delta+T^{-1}}<x^{.4995}\) eventually. Any \(u\) equal to \(s\) gives the genuine squared prime in the core modulus; it is not discarded. The weight on each \(A\) has at most fixed divisor-power multiplicity of labels, hence the usual weighted Cauchy interpolation with the trivial Shiu bound preserves arbitrary log reserve (or the corresponding ordinary prime bound for fixed short \(v\)). All polynomial-source moving cutoffs are treated by the previous short relative meshes; at each \(A\) the physical strip and principal cost \(O(x L^{-F+O(1)}/\phi(A))\), and all additional label and interval counts are log powers.

In the principal of the unrestricted null, fix the actual source \(m\) and \(s,e\) after restoring the separate weights. As in the Narrow Quotient note, split \(d=a k\) with \(a\mid e\), \((k,e)=1\). The possible factor \(1-s^{-1}1_{s\mid k}\) in \(1/\phi(s e k)\) costs a power saving; the remaining masked \(\mu(k)/\phi(k)\) harmonic has polynomial cutoff for \(T_q\), and cutoff at least \(x^{\delta-T^{-1}}/2\) for \(C\). These are uniformly log-small, including the exact \(m,N\) reduced mask. One can equivalently compute the harmonic pointwise in the continuous \(m=r v\) integral for the short-\(v\) branch, retaining the correct \(v\) mask.

For the marked null include \(u\) in the fixed labels. On \(u>x^\delta\), it cannot divide \(d\) and shares no prime with \(e\). On the lower band \(u\le x^{\delta-2/T}\), split \(d=a k\), \(a\mid u e\), \((k,u e)=1\), so \(a\le u e\le x^{\delta-1/T}\). On either band the modulus is exactly \(s u e k\), with \(k\) coprime to the full core \(s u e\) (\(s>x^\delta\)); in the upper band the split just uses \(a\mid e\). Thus \(1/\phi(s u e k)=1/(\phi(s u e)\phi(k))\) **also when \(s=u\)**. The harmonic in \(k\), with mask \(m N s u e\), has its actual moving cutoff \(n/(X_0 a)\) at least \(x^{1/T}/2\) in the lower band and \(x^{\delta-T^{-1}}/2\) above. Both are well past \(\exp(2T^4)\): use the subpower uniform \(\mu/\phi\) estimate of NarrowQuotientScoreAndMarkedRanks, Section 2. Harmonic summation of the remaining labels costs only log powers; \(1/\phi(su)\le 1/(\phi(s)\phi(u))\) suffices for the absolute sums. This proves that the actual marked signed sum of \(C V G\) is log-small, uniformly in the moving target and genuine source weights. Any temporarily admitted squareful \(q\) rows on the restored rough support have arbitrary log reserve by the same square-count and Cauchy–Shiu divisor moments as in JointShortDivisorProjection; the extra bounded-divisor coefficients do not change that estimate.

On the omitted high quotient composites \(T_q=0\) by complete cofactor Möbius cancellation, so subtraction of that part from the unrestricted null yields the \(+C G U\) representation. Subtract the verified \(C G V\) null to get (1). This change does not affect other rows of the quadratic ledger.

## 3. The actual positive gap is small

If \(q=p w\) has the unique giant \(p>B_q\), then every prime factor \(u\mid w\) satisfies \(q/u\ge p>B_q\). If \(P^-(q)>x^\delta\) at least one such factor is in \(V\), and if \(q\) has no giant \(U=0\). This proves the macro sign. More generally \(U>V\) implies \(U=1,V=0\), and every prime factor of the actual cofactor \(w>1\) lies in the gap. Since a product of two gap primes exceeds \(x^\delta\) eventually, its true score is \(C(q;s)=1-\#\{u\mid w:\ u\text{ prime},\ u<n/X_0\}\le1\). Thus only its positive part, at most 1, is charged on this gap. The harmonic volume of all such nonunit \(w\) is
\[
 \sum_{w>1,\ P^-(w)>x^{\delta-2/T},\ P^+(w)\le x^\delta}\frac1w
   \ll \sum_{x^{\delta-2/T}<u\le x^\delta,\,u\text{ prime}}\frac1u
   \ll T^{-1/2}
\]
by Mertens or ordinary prime reciprocal PNT. All algebra is on actual quotient prime factors, not on independent samples.

At fixed \(s,w,v\) on these physical rows the remaining true primes \(p,r\) satisfy \(s w p+v r=N\), with \((s w,v)=1\) and \((s w v,N)=1\). Indeed any shared prime in the coefficients divides \(N\) and violates the exact unit mask. Write \(p=p_0+v j\), \(r=r_0-s w j\), \(p_0\equiv N\overline{s w}\pmod v\), \(r_0=(N-s w p_0)/v\). The forms are primitive and have determinant \(N\). Since \(p>B_q\), \(s w\le x^{.499}\); since \(r>B_m\), \(v\le1.5x^{.5-T^{-20}}\). Thus the free interval has length \(Y\asymp x/(s w v)\gg x^{.001}\) at comparable height. The coefficients have fixed polynomial heights. Apply corrected Henriot with a fixed sufficiently small polynomial roughness majorant for each **true** large prime \(p,r\); its Euler factors cost \(O(1)\), with the target unit masks and \(\mathfrak S(N)\) as in UngatedReflectedRankProjection. The extra local ratio at factors of \(s w v\) is \(1+o(1)\) uniformly: \(s\) is a true macro prime, every prime of \(w\) is in the high gap, and \(v\) has at most two prime factors, each \(>z_m\). At such slope primes exactly one form is a nonzero constant, and the same corrected discriminant terms vanish at target primes. The bound is \(\ll Y\mathfrak S(N)/L^2\), with absolute uniform constants for these fixed height/free-length exponents. This counts each original orientation (unique \(p,r\), canonical \(v\)); it is only an upper on actual prime pairs of the two affine forms, not an asymptotic.

For the semiprime weight, the remaining reflected cofactor harmonic is bounded because \(G\ll\log v/L\) and \(\sum_{v\ \mathrm{prime},v\le2x} (\log v)/(L v)\ll1\). For the triprime weight use \(G\ll(\log v_1)(\log v_2)/L^2\), whose two genuine prime harmonics are each bounded. Summing \(s\) with \(1/s\), multiplying by the physical \(\log m\), and paying the gap harmonic above gives \(O(T^{-1/2}M_N)\). This prices every positive \(U>V\) row. If the product \(C(U-V)\) is positive otherwise, necessarily \(C<0\), \(V>U\); if at most one distinct prime below \(n/X_0\) divides \(q\), its score \(C\) is 1 or 0, never negative. Thus this remaining positive term is on genuinely different low-factor profiles than the gap, and no bound for it or for the full higher-rank signed ledger is claimed.
