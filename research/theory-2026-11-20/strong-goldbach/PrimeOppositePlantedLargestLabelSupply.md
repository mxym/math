# Planted square-root P4 gives many distinct actual first-step large labels

Written research note, 2026-11-24. This is a genuine supply across many prime roots, not a claim that one root reaches all the labels or that they lie in any single terminal component. No priority claim.

Fix \(0<a<b\) and \(A>0\). Take the actual prime-root interval \(I=(a\sqrt N,b\sqrt N]\) for sufficiently large even \(N\), and the admissible planted profile from [the planted square-root P4 theorem](PrimeOppositeSquareRootPlantedP4.md): \(M=dm\le(\log N)^A\), \(\operatorname{rad}(d)\mid m\), \(r\bmod m\) with \((r,m)=(N-dr,M)=1\). Put \(L=\log N\), \(Y=|I|\), \(X=\int_I dt/\log t\), and \(B_0=2XS_1/(\varphi(M)L)\), with the singular factor \(S_1\) of that theorem. Count actual distinct primes \(p\in I\), \((p,N)=1\), \(p\equiv N-dr\bmod M\), whose quotient \(Q=(N-p)/d\) is squarefree, has all prime factors at least \(N^{.05}\), and has at most four of them. The theorem gives at least \((3/40+o(1))B_0\) such rows.

**Result.** At least \(.04B_0\) of these actual rows have largest quotient prime \(q(p)>N^{.2502}\). They yield \(\gg N^{.2502}\) distinct genuine labels \(q(p)\). If at least half the retained rows have \(q(p)\le N^{.4}\), those labels alone have a fixed positive reciprocal sum, and occur in \(\gg\log N\) separate dyadic scales. Otherwise the labels with \(q(p)>N^{.4}\) include \(\gg N^{.4}/L^2\) distinct primes. All statements are uniform in the profiles for fixed \(a,b,A\), and include \(d=m=1\).

## 1. Exclude a narrow range of nearly quarter-sized maxima

If \(q(p)\le N^{.2502}\), then \(Q\asymp N/d\) with at most four factors forces \(q(p)\ge (N/(2d))^{1/4}\), for sufficiently large \(N\). Every mark in this narrow range lies in the original long-mark interval, so apply the [planted switched affine cap](PrimeOppositeSquareRootPlantedP4.md) to \(Q=qj\) with \(j\equiv rq^{-1}\bmod m\). The actual progression parameter has length \(Y/(Mq)\), and the local normalization \((\varphi(M)/M)(S_2/S_1)=1\) is exact; the negligible aggregate floor is unchanged. The old one-subtraction denominator is
\[
 J(v)=\tfrac54v^2-\tfrac12v^2\log v-v+\tfrac14,\qquad
 v=\frac{.249-\tfrac12\log q/L}{.05}.
\]
Uniformly over the near-quarter marks, \(2.47\le v\le2.5\) for large \(N\), hence \(\log v<1\). The lower \((3/4)v^2-v+1/4\) increases there, and at \(v=2.47\) it equals \(2.355675\). Thus the actual switched upper coefficient \(1/(4(.05)^2J(v))\) in units \(B_0/q\) is \(<43\). The harmonic sum of the true marks is at most \(\log(.2502/.25)+o(1)<.0008+o(1)\), uniformly for \(d\le L^A\). The number of removed rows is at most \((.0344+o(1))B_0\): each is counted for its genuine maximum, and allowing all divisibility by such marks can only enlarge this upper. The remaining coefficient is at least \(.075-.0344+o(1)>.04\).

## 2. Count labels using their actual prime-root multiplicities

For \(N^{.2502}<q\le N^{.4}\), switch \(Q=qj\) as above but sift both true forms \(j\) and \(N-dqj\) only at primes \(\ell\le K=N^{.02}\). Every retained row survives: \(j\) is rough and the prime root \(p\) exceeds \(K\). Primes dividing \(M\) are already excluded from both forms, and no other sieve prime divides \(dq\) for large \(N\). The full two-root denominator, with its exact collision whenever \(\ell\mid N\), is \((1+o(1))(\log K)^2/(2S_2)\); use the same Euler convolution as in the original planted proof. In units \(B_0/q\) this gives coefficient \(1250+o(1)\), since \(X\sim2Y/L\) and the same local profile identity cancels the change in \(M\). The individual Selberg error \(O(K^2 L^C)\) is \(o(B_0/q)\) uniformly throughout this band: \(Y/q\gg N^{.10}\) and \(M\le L^A\). In particular at most \(1300 B_0/q\) retained prime roots use any one such label. If this side has at least \(.02 B_0\) rows, its distinct labels have reciprocal sum at least \(.02/1300\). Each is \(>N^{.2502}\), so there are \(\gg N^{.2502}\) of them. In each dyadic scale in this polynomial band, primes have reciprocal sum \(O(1/L)\) by the prime number theorem or Chebyshev, forcing \(\gg L\) actual occupied scales.

For \(q>N^{.4}\), a true retained label is prime to \(M\) and \(N\); in addition to the fixed root class modulo \(M\), we have \(p\equiv N\bmod q\). Their genuine CRT class contains at most \(Y/(Mq)+1\le Y/(M N^{.4})+1\) roots in \(I\). As \(Y/(M N^{.4})\to\infty\) and \(B_0\gg Y/(ML^2)\), \(.02B_0\) rows on this side imply \(\gg N^{.4}/L^2\) distinct labels. This also dominates \(N^{.2502}\).

In the actual directed factor graph with arcs \(p\to q\) when \(q\mid N-p\), each counted label comes from a real arc. Since the planted primes are only polylogarithmic and the remaining quotient is rough, this \(q\) is also the largest prime factor of the full complement. Any forward-closed set containing the entire retained source set contains the supplied labels. This only concerns the indicated many-source set; the theorem gives no implication for the size of one root's terminal component or for a three-factor bound.
