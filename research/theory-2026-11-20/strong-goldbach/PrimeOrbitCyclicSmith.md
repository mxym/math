# Feedback generators and a Smith obstruction on genuinely cyclic labels

Use the directed graph p → prime divisors of N−p. Let C be any finite forward-closed set of distinct odd primes coprime to even N, with labels <N−1. For each row put A_{pq}=v_q(N−p), d_p=Ω(N−p), and B=I−A. Let G be the subgroup generated by the labels of C in U_N/{±1}, with U_N=(ℤ/Nℤ)^×. The following facts are exact at each center, even with prime rows present.

1. Every feedback vertex set F (a set meeting every directed cycle in C) generates G by the residues of its own labels.
2. Let the cyclic strongly connected components be D, and write B_D=I−A_{DD}. Put j_D=corank_ℚ B_D, t_D=the product of its nonzero Smith factors (empty product 1), J=∑_D j_D, T=∏_D t_D. Write the invariant factors of G in **decreasing divisibility** order b₁,b₂,… . Then
     
       ∏_{i>J} b_i  |  T,         T ≤ ∏_{p on a directed cycle} √(1+d_p²).       (1)

   Thus **transient vertices do not contribute even if the full forward closure is enormous**. In particular, if J=0 and G=U_N/{±1}, then φ(N)/2 divides T for N>2. The number of vertices on directed cycles is ≫log N/loglog N as N grows, since d_p≤log N/log 3 and φ(N)≥√(N/2). If one or more diagonal SCC blocks is singular, (1) instead uses the invariant-factor tail. J is a sum of diagonal-block coranks, not necessarily the corank of the whole matrix; when J is at least the number of invariant factors this divisibility may be vacuous.

**Why full residue generation is forced at a hypothetical Goldbach failure.** Fix any ε>0 with η=1/(4√e)+ε<1. By [P. Pollack, *Bounds for the first several prime character nonresidues*, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.05035), at every sufficiently large modulus N, *each* nonprincipal Dirichlet character has more than N^κ prime nonresidues ≤N^η, where κ=κ(ε)>0 and a nonresidue means the value is neither 0 nor 1. No cubefree or conductor restriction is needed in this theorem. Choose all primes ≤N^η coprime to N as seeds, and if desired delete any ≤⌊N^κ⌋ of them **from the seed list**, not from the factor graph. The remaining seeds generate U_N: a proper subgroup would be annihilated by a nonprincipal character, yet even initial deletions cannot exhaust that character's nonresidues. Annihilating characters exist since a nontrivial finite abelian quotient has a nontrivial character.

At a hypothetical exception N, every encountered complement is composite. Every factor is still odd, coprime to N, and ≤(N−3)/3. For large N the prescribed seeds are <N−1, so their joint forward closure satisfies the hypotheses with G=U_N/{±1}. Consequently (1) pertains solely to **cycles of genuine composite rows** at such a center; F must generate the entire signed unit group with no size condition on the closure. This is a statement about a forward graph, not that its terminal exceptional components separately generate G or carry all cyclic vertices. At represented centers, genuine Goldbach two-cycles can contribute, so their presence in examples is not evidence of a counterexample or of any classification. No upper bound on cyclic support or J is asserted.

### Feedback and exact group-rank consequence

Map the j-th generator of ℤ^C to the residue of its label in G. The rows of B map to the identity because the complementary product equals −p modulo N and signs are quotiented. Thus G is a quotient of the group with presentation rows B. In the acyclic complement of a feedback set F evaluate labels in reverse topological order: each label is, up to sign, the product of its outgoing targets with multiplicities. This proves generation by F.

It follows that |F|≥max_ℓ dim_{𝔽ℓ}(G/ℓG). For G=U_N/{±1}, its binary dimension is exactly t+u−δ where t is the number of distinct odd prime divisors of N, and u is 0,1,2 according as v₂(N)=1,2,≥3. Here δ=0 if −1 is a square modulo N, and δ=1 otherwise. Indeed the odd prime-power unit groups are cyclic of even order; the 2-power unit group has the indicated dimensions modulo squares. Quotienting by −1 kills at most its single binary class.

### Proof that transient vertices can be discarded in (1)

Order all SCCs topologically. Then B is block triangular. Its acyclic singleton blocks are [1], since there are no self-loops for labels coprime to N. Apply integral unimodular row and column changes **within** each block to put every diagonal block in Smith form; the off-diagonal blocks may be large but triangularity persists, and the presented group is isomorphic. The new generators therefore also map to G. Let H⊂G be generated by the images of the J generator columns corresponding to the zero diagonal Smith entries of the diagonal blocks. In G/H these generators vanish. Drop their columns and the corresponding rows: dropping the rows merely relaxes relations. The remaining square presentation, still block triangular, has nonzero determinant of absolute value T, so |G/H| divides T.

For each prime ℓ let b_i have ℓ-parts ℓ^{a_i} with a_i nonincreasing. The ℓ-primary part H_ℓ is at most J-generated (and embeds in G_ℓ). For h≥1, the dimension of (ℓ^{h−1}H_ℓ)[ℓ] is at most both J and the dimension of (ℓ^{h−1}G_ℓ)[ℓ], the latter being the number of i with a_i≥h. Summing these dimensions over h gives log_ℓ |H_ℓ| ≤∑_{i≤J} a_i. Hence v_ℓ(|G/H|)≥∑_{i>J} a_i, proving the divisibility in (1).

Finally t_D is the gcd of absolute maximal-rank minors of B_D, with the usual empty-minor convention. At least one such minor is nonzero, and its absolute value is at most ∏_{p∈D}√(1+d_p²) by Hadamard (all factors ≥1). This proves the inequality. The elementary φ(N) bound follows by multiplying φ(q^v)²/q^v=q^{v−2}(q−1)²≥1 except for q^v=2, where it is 1/2. The attached finite checker tests feedback generation on full small genuine-prime graphs and checks synthetic singular-block couplings against exact integral minors. It does not claim to test a Goldbach exception.

### Related 2026 work

The directed divisor graph is not new. The [Kelanemer SSRN record](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7274460) already describes a recursive prime-divisor graph, terminal SCCs, and an exponent matrix; its full text was not accessible here. The [AMRAL reverse-structure program](https://amral.evemisslab.com/en/links/#goldbach-41) takes several distinct cover and transport approaches. [Nelson, v2 dated 16 November](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14580), studies a presentation by equal-additive-sum relations, not complement factorization relations. The result here is specifically the signed-unit quotient and invariant-factor tail in a full forward graph; no priority or general closure claim is made.
