# Polylogarithmic exact profile in the new P4 windows

Written research corollary, 2026-11-24. Combines the [short-window P4 theorem](PrimeOppositeSubSquareRootShortP4.md) and the [exact planted transfer](PrimeOppositeSquareRootPlantedP4.md). No new P3 claim.

Let \(N\) be sufficiently large and even, \(L=\log N\), and fix \(0<a<b\) and \(A>0\). The source window \(I\) is either \((aN^{.49},bN^{.49}]\), or \((x,x+N^{.495}]\) for any \(x\in[a\sqrt N,b\sqrt N]\). Let \(Y=|I|\) and \(X=\int_I du/\log u\). Choose integers \(d,m\ge1\) with \(M=dm\le L^A\), \(\operatorname{rad}(d)\mid m\), and a residue \(r\bmod m\) with \((r,m)=(N-dr,M)=1\). Let
\[
 B_0=\frac{2X}{\phi(M)L}S_1,\qquad S_1=S_N\prod_{\substack{\ell\mid m\\\ell\nmid N}}(1-1/(\ell-1))^{-1}.
\]
Then at least \((.08+o(1))B_0\) actual primes \(p\in I\) with \(p\equiv N-dr\bmod dm\) have a quotient \(Q=(N-p)/d\) which is squarefree, \(P^-(Q)\ge N^{.04}\), and \(\Omega(Q)\le4\). In particular \(Q\equiv r\bmod m\) and \(v_\ell(N-p)=v_\ell(d)\) for every \(\ell\mid m\). Uniformity is for fixed \(a,b,A\).

Proof transfer: use exactly the short-window numerical parameters \(D=N^{.244},\mu=.04,\beta=.314,T=.216\). Embedding the divisibility test for \(Q\) into the profile progression multiplies each combined BV modulus by \(M\); it is still at most \(N^{.244}L^A\), strictly below the effective .245 full-scale BV and .2445 partitioned Perelli–Pintz–Salerno ceilings of the two source windows. Uniformity is by maximum over reduced classes and injectivity of the added fixed modulus. Removing Mangoldt prime powers now costs at most \(M D P^{1/2} L^C\), still power-saving for true prime scale \(P\) as in the short-window proof. The fixed log-height of \(M\) only asks for a larger arbitrarily available logarithmic saving. All omitted primes \(\ell\mid m\) are below all sieve cutoffs and multiply the base normalization by exactly the stated factor \(S_1/S_N\).

For large \(q\mid Q\), switch \(Q=qj\) and \(j\equiv r/q\bmod m\), then sieve the affine variable of length \(Y/(M q)\). Every prime of \(m\) has both affine values forced nonzero. Its omission changes the full two-form normalization to \(S_2=S_N\prod_{\ell\mid m}(1-\nu(\ell)/\ell)^{-1}\), with \(\nu(\ell)=1\) if \(\ell\mid N\) and 2 otherwise. The already audited local identity \((\phi(M)/M)(S_2/S_1)=1\) exactly cancels the changed main scales, including the prime 2. Omitted primes are below \(N^{.04}\), so the one-root telescoping loss and the function \(J(v)\) are unchanged. Keeping \(K^2=N^{.489}/q\) gives the same total absolute affine floor error \(O(N^{.489}L^C)=o(B_0)\), since \(B_0\gg Y/L^{A+2}\). The short-window rational margin \(>.0878\) is therefore unchanged.

For squarefree deletion on \(Q\), whose interval has actual length \(Y/d\), split square divisors \(s^2\mid Q\) at \(s=N^{1/3}\). The lower range costs \(O(Y/(dN^{.04})+N^{1/3})\). In the higher range write \(Q=c s^2\), \(c\le N^{1/3}\), and sum length \(O(Y/\sqrt{dNc})\) plus each floor; this yields \(O(N^{1/3}+Y/(\sqrt d N^{1/3}))\), also \(o(B_0)\). The same signed-to-unweighted Richert conclusion follows. The prime-factor bound counts only \(Q\), not the planted factors already in \(d\).
