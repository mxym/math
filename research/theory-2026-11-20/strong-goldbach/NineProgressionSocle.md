# A fixed family of seven prime-support rings detects a missed even integer in every residue class

In the ordinary unthinned odd-prime ring a missed large multiple of six forces a nonzero socle cubic at the variable cutoff N+3; see [CubicPrefixSocle.md](CubicPrefixSocle.md). Here is a different, explicitly **restricted-support** theorem for the other residue classes.

For each u∈{1,2,4,5,7,8} choose once and for all the prime q=q_u in this table:

    u (mod 9)   1   2   4   5   7   8
    q          19  11  13   5   7  17

The fixed support A_u consists of q together with every odd genuine prime congruent to u+3 or u−3 modulo 9. Both ordinary classes are units mod 9 and q is the ONLY retained label in class u. Write G_u^{≤s} for its finite variable prefix binary-move ring over any field: include every equal-pair relation on the allowed labels, without truncation on pair weights. At every sufficiently large even N with N≡2u mod9 put s=N+q. There are two central ternary monomials M_+,M_- at weight s such that

    (x_t:t∈A_u, t≤s) (M_+−M_-)=0           in G_u^{≤s}.

If no pair of allowed labels sums to N, this cubic f is nonzero. In particular missing N on the complete unthinned genuine primes suffices. Then its annihilator is the homogeneous maximal ideal of this particular restricted prefix; f²=0 and depth there is zero. Combining the six fixed rings here with the single unthinned ring for 6|N gives a common finite-family conditional criterion for all sufficiently large even N. No assertion about depth of the *unthinned* ring at N+q for 3∤N follows. The converse of the conditional criterion is not claimed. Even a true unrestricted representation of N does not have to belong to our chosen A_u.

## Central giants in a fixed prime progression: only a qualitative exceptional-set bound is needed

Put I=[s/8,5s/8]. For each sign consider JUST the central primes in one residue a=u±3 mod9, with hyperedges for all triples of them of total s. For p,r in this progression the complementary label s−p−r is automatically in the SAME progression, since s≡3u≡3a mod9. For all sufficiently large s, each sign hypergraph has one component containing (1−o(1)) of all its prime labels, uniformly along this progression of totals.

Here is a proof in terms of [CubicGiant.md](CubicGiant.md) §§2–4, including the fixed-modulus changes; it does not assume an almost-all Goldbach theorem in progressions. Suppose a union C of components and its complement D each have ≥δs/log s labels. The actual mixed prime-triple count between C and D with a third prime in I is exactly zero, since the complementary label automatically has residue a. Therefore §2 there, *without restricting the third weight further*, bounds the corresponding normalized finite-modulus model mixed mass by O(w^(−c))+o_w(1). To lower-bound the model let W=∏_{ℓ<w}ℓ and record endpoint classes modulo 3W, so the class mod 9 is known. Fix w and pass to a subsequence for weak limits as in §4. The sum of the endpoint measures is given by PNT in fixed progressions at 3W. At 3 the two endpoints have the SAME FIXED lift a modulo 9 and the third reduced class is automatic; this is a one-state positive constant kernel. At 2 one has one odd state. At all ℓ≥5 the finite walk is exactly that in §3 there, including absolute spectral gap, bounded-below stationary density and total normalized mass. Conditioning the two endpoints on their fixed reduced class mod9 costs only fixed positive factors; the archimedean walk is unchanged. The resulting limiting mixed mass is bounded below by a positive constant depending on δ but not w, from the Poincaré bound in §3. This contradicts the model upper bound after w is chosen sufficiently large. Thus no such partition persists for any fixed δ>0. Finally if the largest component misses a fixed positive fraction of the vertices, either take it as C when it has positive macroscopic size or form C by successively accumulating small components until enclosing, say, one third of the mass. This gives the prohibited partition. PNT implies the total sector size is ∼s/(12 log s), so its giant exists and the discarded labels have logarithmic mass o(s). Only this density error, not a superlogarithmic one, is used below. Connectedness of triples sharing labels translates to binary moves within the same sign and prefix.

## Uniform physical charts, including the fixed pivot
For t=q, use exactly the three-move chart of [SquareZeroBridge.md](SquareZeroBridge.md), replacing the distinguished 3 by q (everywhere in its affine formulas as well). Take a,b,b'≡u+3 and p≡u−3 mod9. Then c=s−a−b≡u+3, and q+a−p, b+p−b', b'+c−q are all u−3. For t≠q in the support the four-move chart in [CubicPrefixSocle.md](CubicPrefixSocle.md) becomes

    (t,a,b,c) → (y,v,b,c) → (q,h,v,c) → (w,z,h,v) → (t,r,h,z),
    c=s−a−b,   v=t+a−y,   h=y+b−q,   z=q+c−w,   r=a−y+w.

Impose a,b,y≡u+3 and w≡u−3 mod9. Then c≡u+3, v≡t∈{u±3}, h≡u+6≡u−3, and z,r≡u−3. Thus **all** intermediate labels belong to A_u, not merely the endpoints. At 2 impose odd inputs. For ℓ≥5 the same sequential local choices work for every s,t,q: in the four-move chart choose a≠0, b outside {0,s−a}, y outside {0,t+a,q−b}, w outside {0,q+c,y−a}. For the three-move chart choose a≠0, b outside {0,s−a}, p outside {0,q+a}, b' outside {0,b+p,q−c}. This includes possible ℓ=q or ℓ|t. Every imposed class mod9 is reduced; it is not necessary to assume any distribution in a moving modulus. The archimedean boxes in CubicPrefixSocle.md work with error 19/s instead of 3/s, so for s≥19000 every moving label is positive and ≤s and all six endpoints lie in I. The exceptional q itself is fixed and lies in the prefix. Literal checks for each class and band are included in check_nine_socle.py.

After substituting fixed residue classes, both systems have fixed pairwise nonproportional linear parts and intercepts O(s). The finite-complexity [Tao–Teräväinen theorem 1.6](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1404) therefore supplies the uniform genuine-log-weighted lower bound ≫s⁴ in each chart: the admissibility above gives the finitely many small-prime lower factors, and pairwise independence gives large-prime factors 1+O(ℓ^(−2)). Proper powers in any moving form cost O(s^(7/2)log^9 s).

To remove central labels outside the giants with just the o(s) log-mass estimate, fix one of the six endpoint forms L, and write H(l) for the sum over chart input quadruples with L=l of the product of the genuine-prime weights of all other forms, including the imposed mod9 restrictions. The doubled system consisting of two copies joined on L (count the one weight of L only once) has seven free integer variables and fixed finite complexity. Indeed L is primitive and has a coefficient ±1, so we may use its value as one coordinate in each unrestrained copy. In each copy every other form has nonzero linear part in at least one of that copy's three remaining free coordinates. Across copies these blocks are disjoint, so the doubled list, including L just once, is still pairwise nonproportional. Upper-bound the positive weighted count by dropping all progressions and using von Mangoldt in the original joined coordinates; affine unimodular change, positive convex box intersection, O(s) intercepts, and the finite-complexity theorem give uniformly

    Σ_{l prime}(log l) H(l)² ≪ s⁷.

The implied constant is independent of t,s because the fixed linear parts bound all normalized local factors uniformly (the exceptional small primes are finite, and vanishing is harmless for an upper bound). If E is the set of excluded labels, Cauchy yields Σ_{l∈E}(log l) H(l)=o(s⁴). Do this six times. A chart survives for each t at one common threshold, so any two chosen monomial representatives of the central giants satisfy the stated annihilation. Endpoints and all intervening binary moves stay in the finite variable prefix.

If N has no allowed prime pair, no triple at s can contain q. Its three remaining labels, all u±3, have sum 3u modulo 9 precisely when all three have equal signs. A binary move fixes one label and cannot cross signs. Thus the two monomial classes differ, over every field. Annihilation by all prime variables gives the conclusions as in CubicPrefixSocle.md.

## Local and logical limitations
The particular requested cross-sign pair at N is locally admissible: modulo 9 take u+3,u−3, at 2 take an odd pair, and for any ℓ≥5 choose x outside {0,N} with second coordinate N−x. CRT combines these. For N>2q the ONLY allowed pairs for this weight are a cross pair of the two ordinary progressions; a pair containing q would need its mate in q's singleton residue. A deficit of such restricted pairs can therefore cause a socle even though another genuine-prime residue pattern does represent N. Conversely our theorem does not say a representation forces a zero socle, and it supplies neither positivity nor a depth lower bound for the restricted rings. [PrimePrefix317.md](PrimePrefix317.md) already warns that even complete unthinned finite prime prefixes can have depth zero at a cubic weight above the variable cutoff. This result uses only the displayed prime progressions, not an identification of their rings with the full prime ring or its quotients.

The restricted binary target is a special case of [Refined Goldbach conjectures with primes in arithmetic progressions, Conjecture 1.1](https://math.ou.edu/~kmartin/papers/goldbach.pdf). Its eventual pointwise truth is not an input. Our contribution here is the conditional socle obstruction in these six explicitly specified allowed-label rings, not a new formulation of refined Goldbach.
