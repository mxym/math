# The full prime toric ideal needs only arity four; all sufficiently heavy four-moves split into three-moves

Let P be all genuine odd primes, R=k[x_p:p∈P] over any field, I its ideal of all exact binary equal-sum moves, and J the kernel of x_p↦u^p v. An **arity-r move** replaces r genuine prime labels in a list by r genuine prime labels of the same total; only arity two is a physical relation in I. On the infinite support:

1. J is generated by exact moves of arity ≤4. Indeed any two lists of common degree d≥4 and total are connected by ≤2d−5 moves of arity ≤4.
2. There is an absolute S₀ such that every move of arity four at additive weight S≥S₀ can be replaced by at most THREE moves of arity ≤3. This holds uniformly for all pairs of endpoint quartets.
3. Consequently J is generated by the quadrics, the cubics, and a finite collection of quartics of weight <S₀. To prove quadratic domainhood in [Nelson Q8.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14580v2#S8) it is sufficient (and necessary) to check that *all* cubic fibers and those finitely many low-weight quartic fibers are connected by **binary** moves. All degrees and sums in assertions 1 and 2 refer to actual labels and moves; there is no cancellation or localization argument. We do not derive quadratic connectedness of all cubics, identify an explicit S₀, or claim all low-weight quartics have been eliminated.

### Exact arity bound from the ternary theorem

Set A={(p−3)/2:p∈P}; 0∈A. [Helfgott's ternary Goldbach theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.05438) in its odd-prime form gives 3A=ℕ₀: for e≥0 write 2e+9 as three odd primes (9 and the other small cases are included). For any four available indices a₁,…,a₄, write their sum as b₁+b₂+b₃ in 3A; this gives a legal quartic move to (0,b₁,b₂,b₃). In any d-list distinguish one such zero and keep it. Repeat on four remaining entries, keeping previous zeros untouched, so after at most d−3 moves the list has d−3 fixed zeros and three other entries. Do this independently to both d-lists being compared. The remaining triples of equal total connect by one legal ternary move. Reversing the second path proves the distance bound. This argument works by the same reasoning for any A containing 0 with hA=ℕ₀, yielding degree h+1; no novelty for the general additive-basis observation is asserted. Conversely, arbitrary toric polynomials are polynomial-linear combinations of matching-bidegree monomial differences since distinct u^n v^d are linearly independent; telescoping the paths generates these differences in the ideal. For d=2,3 use the unrestricted equal-sum moves directly.

### Uniform common anchor at every sufficiently large four-weight

Fix two quartets of total S, and choose one smallest prime p of the first and q of the second. Thus 3≤p,q≤S/4. We seek genuine odd primes

    r, a, S−p−r−a, b, S−q−r−b.

If found, the first quartet moves, keeping p, to (p,r,a,S−p−r−a); the second, keeping q, moves to (q,r,b,S−q−r−b). These two quartets have the common label r and so differ by one ternary move. This explicitly gives the three moves, including repeated-label cases.

Take r,a,b independently in [S/5,S/4]. Then both dependent labels belong to [S/4,3S/5], the volume is a fixed positive multiple of S³, and the five (r,a,b)-linear parts are distinct and pairwise nonproportional. At 2 use all odd values and S even, p,q odd. At every odd prime ℓ choose r nonzero, then a outside {0,S−p−r} and b outside {0,S−q−r}; these choices work even at ℓ=3. All normalized local factors have uniformly positive product, since pairwise independence at all large ℓ gives 1+O(ℓ^(−2)) uniformly including special residues p,q,S. The fixed-complexity [Tao–Teräväinen Theorem 1.6](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1404) gives therefore ≫S³ von Mangoldt-weighted tuples, with its o(S³) error uniform in the moving offsets p,q=O(S). A fixed proper prime-power value in any form leaves O(S²) remaining tuples, so removing them costs O(S^(5/2)log^O(1)S). Thus all sufficiently large S admit the gadget, with no assertion here of an effective threshold.

An explicit low-weight observation: if every even from 6 through S−6 has a representation by two odd primes, every quartet of total S can immediately be moved, holding any one of its labels p, to (p,3,b,c) with b+c=S−p−3≥6. Hence it connects to every other quartet by ≤3 ternary moves via the anchor 3. The [Oliveira e Silva–Herzog–Pardi verification](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-2013-02787-1) through 4·10¹⁸ therefore covers that initial range of the *ternary-move* claim, not the hypothetical intervening finite gap to S₀ and not universal binary-move connectedness. A genuine unresolved quartic (for three-moves) thus needs a missed smaller even target; no such quartic is exhibited. Finite VARIABLE prefixes, which may clip necessary large labels in an individual compression, are not claimed to have Markov degree four.

### Comparison

Nelson v2 studies the physical quadratic prime ring, a distinct assertion from a four- or three-move generating bound for J. Classical finite projective monomial-curve / higher sumset regularity methods (for example [Castelnuovo–Mumford Regularity of Projective Monomial Curves via Sumsets](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00009-023-02482-3)) address the broader connection between sumsets and graded algebras. We make no priority claim. This arity bound is not a binary Goldbach proof and does not turn the missing-3 bridge in the cubic sector into a representation. It isolates the remaining infinite family of physical Markov tests in degree three.
