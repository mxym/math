# A genuine four-tag rank-at-most-seven count is programmable, but not yet sign-paid

Written research note, 2026-11-24. Keep the original same fixed \(x+y=N\), central \(J\), target unit rows, sharp CQ detector, and the genuine middle rank-five score \(D_0\) with \(m_0=m(D_0)=969075913/7200000000\). This extends the [earlier four-tag obstruction](CQFourTagRankSevenPaymentObstruction.md): a *real* positive-power quotient-sieve factor can be allocated to the previously forbidden \(Q_2\) slot by working box-by-box with the published Iwaniec modified lower weights. On a fixed disjoint physical angular box this proves an actual unweighted broad four-tag rank-five-through-seven count. It does **not** prove a genuine signed CQ gain because dropping zero-score rank seven still cannot be paid with the present reverse upper coefficient 1.672. The latter conclusion holds for any four-tag angular boxes using the same direct broad linear lower and Prop. 5.2 rails, even allowing sieve factors in \(Q_2\).

## 1. Joining the real tags preserves the relevant support

Use logarithmic exponents throughout; \(x=N\) is the analytic scale. An ordered vector \(t_1\ge t_2\ge\cdots\) belongs to the well support at exponent \(E\) if \(\sum_{i<j} t_i+2t_j<E\) for all \(j\). This is [Lichtman (6.1), (6.11)](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08522v1#S6). Fix four genuine tag exponents \(a_1,\ldots,a_4\) all below \(E\), with \(S=\sum a_i\). The sorted merged vector of the tags and sieve factors belongs to the well support at \(B=S+E\). At an original sieve factor the preceding tags cost at most \(S\). At an added tag of exponent \(a\), if a sieve factor \(t\) precedes it and is last in the earlier merged order among sieve factors, its well inequality gives \(\sum_{t_i\text{ earlier}}t_i<E-t\le E-a\). Adding at most \(S-a\) for the preceding tags and \(2a\) proves the asserted inequality. If no sieve factor precedes, the bound follows from \(a<E\) directly. The proof works also with apparent equal exponent upper endpoints, under any total tie order.

Fix independently named ordered half-open angular intervals of radius \(10^{-6}\) about exponents \((.0012,.025,.033,.0408)\). Their sum is \(.100\pm.000004\); the smallest tag is above \(\beta\) and below the earlier three-tag grid's smallest possible .002. This box has \(E=.4506\), quotient rough cutoff \(z=.2251\), and geometric level \(S+E=.5506\pm.000004\). The quotient cutoff is larger than every tag and larger than \((1-S)/4\) by a fixed margin. The actual residue is \(a=N\), so use \(\alpha=1\), exceptional parameter \(\theta=7/32\). For the factorization of named-prime boxes below, reserve the slightly bigger reference \(\mathcal B=.551\). With \(\delta=10^{-6}\), the merged prime-prefix vectors lie below the support bound \(\mathcal B-2\delta\) with a fixed margin. Their largest width is \(\le .22512\). In [Lichtman Lemmas 6.3–6.4 and Proposition 6.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08522v1#S6), the critical exponents are
\[
 v=2\mathcal B-1=.102,\qquad
 u=\min\!\left(\frac{1-(1+\theta)\mathcal B}{3/2-2\theta},
                         \frac{2-\theta-2\mathcal B}{3}\right)

\]
If the largest exponent in a merged box is in \([v,u]\), it qualifies as the \(b\) factor in Proposition 6.5(i). If it is below \(v\), the third is also below \(v<u-v\), while twice the second is below \(2v<1-\mathcal B-2\delta\). There are at least four factors, including the actual named tags. Lemma 6.4 applies and gives all three rails (6.3), also at the very small sieve prefixes. The source factor \(H_0\) is the genuine smallest of the \(D_0\) primes, on dyadic scales from \(N^{.04}/O(1)\) to \(O(N^{.105})\); the auxiliary parameter \(A=H_0N^{-\delta}\) is in the required range. These test the real residue \(N\), with no variable residue replacement.

## 2. Lifting the factorizations to the standard modified *lower* coefficients

This does not assume Iwaniec's weights are triply well-factorable. In fact Lichtman expressly distinguishes them from that class [arXiv:2309.08522, §§2, 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08522v1#S6.SS1). Use the published explicit box representation of the original modified linear weights \(\widetilde{\lambda}^{\pm}\) in [Lichtman, *A modification of the linear sieve, and the count of twin primes*, §5.1, (5-17), (5-18)](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.02851v2#S5.SS1), with the actual component formulas (5-14), (5-15). Equation (5-17) applies to the **original lower** family as well as the upper; the programmable improved bound Theorem 2.12 in that same paper is for an upper family and is not invoked here. The original lower formula is a weighted finite sum of fixed rough-prime vector boxes in \(\mathcal D_r^-(D)\), with signs and factorial multiplicity as indicated there. Each contribution is a convolution of independent prime-box factors with one genuine small-prime fundamental-lemma factor of support \(\le D^\varepsilon\) from (5-15); in the lower family the parity choices are those already built into (5-17). The vector boxes are within \(\mathcal D_r^{\rm well}(D)\) by (5-18). Their actual prime-width upper endpoints cost only the fixed \(1+\tau\) enlargement of the nominal exponent (\(\tau=\varepsilon^9\)). Ordinary lower main and error are exactly those of the original Iwaniec modified lower theorem (the same as for ordinary linear sieve, with an arbitrarily small fixed loss). No box is removed and no signed coefficient is replaced.

For each fixed rough-prime vector and fixed physical tag box, append the four independent named tag factors **before** choosing a factorization. Use their exponent upper endpoints and those of the rough-prime coordinates in §1. Take the Iwaniec construction parameter small enough that the total upper-endpoint inflation is \(\ll10^{-5}\), and the low-prime tail exponent \(E\varepsilon \ll\delta/10\). The resulting factorization is a partition of the coordinate labels. Retain that **one** partition throughout the independent product box; it does not depend on which actual primes appear. The fixed-factor differences at upper endpoints cost \(O(1/\log N)\), and strict margins absorb them. One may work with the combined monomial last rail \(H_0^{1+2\theta}Q_1 Q_2^{5-2\theta}Q_3^{2+2\theta}\), whose exponents on all three upper levels are positive, so no lower-endpoint substitution is required. ## 3. Actual broad count, and why the current reverse upper still does not certify the sign

Use the true quotient floor \(N^{.2251}\). Because \(2z<E\), the original lower density is strictly positive and in this range its normalized leading coefficient is \(2\log(E/z-1)/E\). The arbitrary fixed small loss in the modified lower can be made smaller than half this positive coefficient. The four named true primes are the unique first four primes, because every further quotient factor exceeds \(z\). The quotient is \(>1\), and the cutoff forbids four further primes; normal squareful trimming gives genuine ranks five through seven. Harmonic weights for the named primes are positive and the fixed ordinary source mass is \(m_0>0\), so this is a positive **true broad count** in each reflected orientation. Its least tag below .002 makes it literally disjoint from every earlier 40,604-cell receiver. Its small third angle \(.033+10^{-6}\) also forces failure of the former two-tag \(C\) cutoff, for which the accepted threshold is at least \((1-t-v)/5\). Ranks five and six have the true favorable negative sign on this branch. Rank seven has zero CQ score and remains in the broad count. No true favorable signed lower follows without removing it cheaply.

Even changing the four-tag angular box cannot make the present uniform reverse coefficient \(1.672\) pay for this design. For a general four-tag box write \(X=1-S\), normalized \(a=E/X\), and \(t=z/X\). With a genuine rank-at-most-seven roughness guarantee one needs \(t\ge1/4\). The first three full-level rails imply \(B\le(5-\theta)/8=153/256<.6\). Indeed \(b>2B-1\) from the first two, while \(\theta+2B+3b<2\) follows from the next and \(H_0>Q_1\); eliminating \(b\) gives the bound. Since \(S+E\le B\), \(a\le(B-S)/(1-S)\le B<.6\), and positivity requires \(t<a/2<.3\). The actual rank-seven ordinary density per named harmonic is at least
\[
 m_7\ \ge\ \frac{9}{4X}(1-3t)^2
\]
by the exact remaining 3-prime simplex as in the [prior four-tag note](CQFourTagRankSevenPaymentObstruction.md). This is a lower on **ordinary mass**, not on the actual reflected wrong-rank count. The broad linear coefficient at any allowed \(a\) is \((2m_0/(Xa))\log(a/t-1)\). For \(2<a/t\le2.4\), \(\log(s-1)/s\) increases by its elementary derivative. Hence
\[
 \frac{L_{\rm broad}}{m_7}
 \le \frac{40m_0}{27}\,
      \frac{\log(.6/t-1)}{(1-3t)^2}
 \le \frac{40m_0}{27}\,
      \frac{.6/t-2}{(1-3t)^2}
 \le \frac{256m_0}{27}<1.277 .
\]
The last comparison is equivalent to \(288t^3-192t^2+42t-3\ge0\). At \(t=1/4\) both this polynomial and its first derivative vanish and its second derivative is strictly positive throughout \([1/4,.3]\). Thus even the exact \(m_7\) quoted into the available reverse upper budget \(1.672m_7\) cannot certify a positive residual, even ignoring any other cost. This says nothing about the actual rank-seven count hitting its available upper. The construction settles the implementability of positive-power \(Q_2\) packing for a new disjoint broad four-tag branch; the remaining obstacle is a considerably sharper actual reverse count or a rank-sensitive signed mechanism, not generic triply well-factorable lower weights.
