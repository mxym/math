# CQ fixed-target relaxation: no-pair rank-one witness preserving one-arm calibration and fixed CRT data

CQ reference commit [989aea5](https://github.com/mxym/math/commit/989aea5aebbf6116d633a6c296ec02f568bf91d2). This is a new model for the sharp cubic–quintic score, not the repo's earlier [Pan/semiprime quotient marginal witness](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/989aea5aebbf6116d633a6c296ec02f568bf91d2/research/theory-2026-11-20/strong-goldbach/JointMarginalParityWitness.md). It is a **relaxed pairing of actual integers**, never a claim that the physical N has no prime pair. It isolates what a genuine two-arm comparison must overcome.

## Statement and scope

Let N tend to infinity through even integers; fix a central J compactly in (1/3,2/3), and an arbitrary **fixed** even modulus Q (not growing with N). Given any fixed η>0, a nonnegative model pairing measure can be supported on actual integers x∈NJ, y∈N(1−J), with |x+y−N|≤ηN, x+y≡N (mod Q), and (x,N,Q)=(y,N,Q)=1. The actual integer labels, ranks, least primes and CQ score are retained; x+y=N and full global coprimality are not. Each CRT row/column count and each CRT row/column V-weighted moment at the opposite arm is **preserved exactly relative to the base stratified product law**, even with arbitrary tests on the source arm. Its calibrated main terms agree asymptotically with the stated actual one-arm compact-source transfers. It gives **exactly zero** true-prime-by-true-prime mass, and HH=o(1) on normalized M units, while satisfying the stated scalar positive-block, high-negative-union, tiny-union, tiny/high intersection and true middle-prime rebate inequalities with slack. It does not assert all DHR cellwise capacity bounds, all growing-modulus joint distribution, or a global family of hypothetical failures consistent with true dyadic prime-pair averages.

## Ordinary fixed-CRT means

Write L=log N, V,Π,H=V−Π as in CubicQuinticSharp, and normalize individual functions by L, e.g. v(n)=V(n)/L, h(n)=H(n)/L, π(n)=Π(n)/L. On a fixed macroscopic interval I let μ_r=(L/|I|)Σ_{n∈I,n≡r (Q)}δ_n. For fixed Q, uniformly over its finitely many residues,
\[
 \int v\,d\mu_r=\begin{cases}2/\varphi(Q)+o(1)&(r,Q)=1,\\o(1)&(r,Q)>1,\end{cases}
\qquad
 \int\pi\,d\mu_r=\begin{cases}1/\varphi(Q)+o(1)&(r,Q)=1,\\0&(r,Q)>1.\end{cases}
\]
The prime statement is ordinary fixed-modulus PNT. For v, repeat the one-arm short completion in DyadicExterior, now fixing r. Use the smooth taper F_h with F_h'(0)=−2 and replace own log by L as in sharp CQ; the absolute taper/scale comparison only improves under a congruence restriction. At fixed h the literal divisor d≤N^(1/2−h); open μ² by e²|n for e≤N^(h/4), discarded physical squares cost o(N), since division counts give O(N^(1−h/4)+sqrt N) and fixed divisor powers cost N^o(1). Counting simultaneous n≡r (Q), [d,e²]|n gives exact principal 1/lcm(Q,[d,e²]) when compatible, floor atom ≤1; the number of counted labels is o(N/L^C) for any fixed C. Restore the omitted square labels only in the principal: inserting e^(1/4), off d Euler factors are 1+O(p^(−7/4)), on d the extras are O(p^(−3/4)); summing harmonic d weights costs O(log N) for fixed Q, hence Rankin tail o(1) after the exterior L.

Pull out density 1/Q. For p∤Q the one-arm factor in the d-variable s near 1 is 1−p^(−2)−(1−1/p)p^(−s) (same as DyadicExterior). For p|Q and r a unit, p is forbidden in d,e and the factor is 1. For p|r, writing p^j||Q, if p²|r with j≥2 then V vanishes identically. Otherwise forced n divisible by p has factor c_p(1−p^(1−s)), with c_p=1−1/p if j=1, or c_p=1 if j≥2 and p²∤r. This exactly accounts for the squarefree condition. Extract ζ(s)^{-1}. The regular factor A(s) converges absolutely on a strip left of 1 (outside the finite Q factors use 1+O(p^(−2)+p^(−1−Re s))), with A(1)=Q/φ(Q) on units, 0 on other classes. The short signed completion is unchanged for fixed Q: convolve the absolutely summable regular factor with μ, apply ordinary signed Mertens, and cut the log moments at exp((log L)^4) with arbitrary-log tail. Since ζ^{-1} has simple zero at 1, zeroth moment is 0 and first log moment is −A(1). Taylor of L F_h(log d/L) gives main 2A(1)/Q, higher terms o(1). For detail, on the short interval absolute harmonic coefficients are O(log D) by convolution, giving O((log D)^3/L)=o(1) for the quadratic remainder; for the long part uniform Mertens gives arbitrary log saving by partial summation. Finally h→0. Thus h has mean 1/φ(Q) on units and o(1) elsewhere.

For any fixed compact true angle/rank source Z, with all factors >N^δ for a fixed δ>0, normalized z=Z/L has mean m(Z)/φ(Q)+o(1) in each unit class and is zero in the others eventually. Standard independent prime harmonics proof: partition into true ordered angle cells, select largest prime slot, use PNT at that genuine slot in the prescribed unit progression (fixed Q), and sum the positive harmonic data for the others; literal boundary collars vanish after refining the cells. This is ordinary one-arm, not a fixed-target pair theorem. For T=H1_{P^-≤N^(1/5000)}, the same mean holds by subtracting the compact rough complement from H. Its mass t obeys −.0064<t<0 by the exact polynomial and factorial error in ExplicitTinyUnion (writing β=1/5000, t=−32β+288β²−(2720/3)β³+(3040/3)β⁴ within 10^−12). All means are independent of the chosen fixed macroscopic interval.

## Positive law and exact invariants

Use two reflected intervals I_1=NJ, I_2=N(1−J). For spatial width ≤η, partition J into finitely many intervals of length ≤η, apply the same construction on each matching reflected pair, and average with weights |J_j|/|J|. All limits unchanged, and each resulting pair obeys |x+y−N|≤ηN. The following defines the law on one such pair of intervals. For the r→s=N−r residue blocks retain (r,N,Q)=1 (symmetrically for s), and put
\[
D_Q(N)=\#\{r\pmod Q:(r(N-r),Q)=1\},\qquad S_Q(N)=Q D_Q(N)/\varphi(Q)^2>0.
\]
This is the finite CRT singular factor. Base law is ν_0=(Q/S_Q)Σ_{r allowed} μ_{1,r}⊗μ_{2,s}. Its normalization is on M-scale **weighted moments**, not total probability; its one-sided means are as in the preceding section and finite N μ measures are finite. In particular only both-unit blocks contribute to the leading HH, VV, Π×V, and compact-source transfer.

Let P be the physical positive rank-two/three weight from WiderPositiveBlock (both one-arm exponent floors .123, using its one-arm support). It is nonnegative; write p=m(P)>2.0377 by DyadicExterior. On a unit residue in each arm, set π_r=∫πdμ_r, p_r=∫(P/L)dμ_r, and take
\[
 f_r=1_{\text{prime}}-(\pi_r/p_r)1_{P>0}+d_r1_{Z_r}.
\]
Here Z_r contains those n≡r (Q) divisible by ℓ² for any fixed odd prime ℓ∤Q. V,H,Π vanish identically there. Its count mass grows like L/(Qℓ²), while count masses of primes and the compact positive P support bounded (ordinary PNT and compact angular densities). Thus choose d_r making ∫f_r=0 exactly and |d_r|=O_Q(1/L)<1; π_r/p_r→1/p<1 and supports disjoint. On nonunit residues take f_r=0. This gives **exactly**
\[
 |f_r|\le1,\qquad \int f_r=0,\quad \int v f_r=0,\quad \int \pi f_r=\pi_r,\quad\int h f_r=-\pi_r
\]
on each unit block. Define ν by multiplying each base block by 1−f_{1,r}(x)f_{2,s}(y)≥0. Hence same exact residue equation, nonnegativity, and prime-by-prime identically zero (both prime f=1), without fabricating any prime labels.

For arbitrary source test g(x), projected rows with other-arm U(y)=1 or v(y) are **exactly** the same in ν and ν_0 because the perturbation factors and ∫U f_{2,s}=0. Same with arms reversed, and separately in every CRT block; in particular any fixed divisor/residue indicator in the other arm determined by Q can multiply U. This includes all fixed prescribed tests inside that locally based linear information. Asymptotically each compact-source scalar ∫ z(x)v(y)dν=2m(Z)+o(1) (and reflected), since there are D_Q unit blocks each contributing (m(Z)/φ)(2/φ), with prefactor Q/S_Q. In particular VV=4+o(1), ΠV=VΠ=2+o(1), and ΠΠ=0 exactly. Thus HH=o(1) by the actual algebra H=V−Π. This is exactly the leading CQ identity if the hypothetical target has no prime pairs. It is **not** claiming that normalized raw count marginals of the physical x+y=N line equal ν_0, only that the indicated perfectly measured model rows and certified weighted leading terms do.

## Numeric checks on the existing paid supports

Integral notation in this section omits normalization by L on weights, i.e. all pairs use the corresponding scaled versions. In the model P×P equals p²−1+o(1) because ∫f·P/L=−π_r exactly per unit residue. Thus >3.1522+o(1), exceeding the actual certified 1.2976 lower. (No claim of its precise DHR layerwise count.) Also P×Π and Π×P equal p+1+o(1). This last number is a **transverse genuine mixed observable**: it is not among the automatic preserved row calibrations. An actual fixed-target upper under the no-pair hypothesis by less than (p+1−ε)M, if obtained, would reject this particular witness; it would not automatically prove Goldbach or reject all transverse witnesses.

Let A=[−H]_+1_{P^->N^.105}, B=[−H]_+1_{N^.040<P^-≤N^.105}. Their supports have f=0 wherever score nonzero. They are disjoint from P-support and primes, and fixed-floor compact with a=m(A)<.08472. Every score in a mixed moment with A or B retains the base value at leading order. In particular:
- High negative union is −∫(A H+H A+A A)=−2a−a²+o(1)>−.176618, stronger than its prescribed -.493039 lower. The signed union formula is exact because on high-negative arm H=−A and the intersection is subtracted once.
- Signed tiny union is 2t−t²+o(1)>−.012841, stronger than -.456 lower. Indeed f·T=0 (P has large least floor; primes outside tiny; auxiliary Z has H=0). A-tiny intersection score is −2at+o(1)<.001085, below .218. These formulas use actual scores at all model labels, not imposed signs on tiny rows.
- Middle prime crosses in both directions are m(B)+o(1). The actual repo's uniform true middle lower with lower certificate c=33390005752037/10^15>.0333 implies m(B)≥c. To see this last step (only for ordinary mass), dyadically average the genuine source B×Π cross: B has fixed positive least prime floor and bounded rank, Π prime, so separated ordinary measures on the wedge converge by PNT/angle boxes exactly as in DyadicExterior; shared factors cost o(X²) as one label macro rough, and the singular-series average of M matches wedge area. Thus its limit is m(B). This uses the true cross theorem only for its consequence on the one-arm ordinary mass. The model's B×B overlap is m(B)^2 and keeps the exact signed union transfer: <−B,H>+<H,−B>−<B,B> = −4m(B)+<B,Π>+<Π,B>−<B,B> at leading order. Hence no isolated rebate is incorrectly added.
- Disjointness P-by-P from the other paid sets persists because physical type conditions on the model labels are unchanged. Thus the three paid support inequalities and no-pair assumption are compatible despite no strictly positive global HH. The toy exterior pays the corresponding deficit by a true adjustment of *coupling of types* in the model, not by changing any signed one-arm input.

## Scope and a mixed condition that rejects the model

The correction on each residue block is the rank-one factor −f_r⊗f_s, invisible to every test with the opposite factor 1 or V (even source tests arbitrary and CRT-block-specific). Finite local congruences or narrower geometric wedges do not repair that blindness, even with no prime-prime rows and the listed scalar budgets. A useful fixed-target input must sense selected **mixed two-arm correlations** not generated by these projections, e.g. joint positive block vs prime occupancy/negative sign at the same reflection, or exploit exact x+y=N at a changing analytic scale. Model may violate DHR-refined layerwise constraints, noncompact rank-cut covariance bounds, or any new mixed estimate; it claims neither preservation of all those nor an impossibility theorem for actual integers on the exact affine line. Keep Q fixed for each result, no increasing-Q estimates. The physical target's dyadic average known positive is entirely consistent: this is a relaxed model of an individual hypothetical failure, not a proposed positive-density sequence of real failures.

The [companion true mixed-sieve estimate](CQActualMixedPrimePositiveBlock.md), §5, shows that this particular construction does not satisfy the genuine prime-versus-rough upper bound. The construction is therefore not a witness against a proof that also uses that bound.
