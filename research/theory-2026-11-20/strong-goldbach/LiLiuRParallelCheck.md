# 21 November literature check: the full Li–Liu spectral hypothesis R

[Yilin Liu, *Failure of the Li–Liu spectral hypothesis R under Dirichlet GRH*, Hexagon 2611.00788v1 (21 November 2026)](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00788v1), Theorem 1.1, gives a conditional obstruction to the **full operator-norm** route in [Jiamin Li and Jianya Liu, *A Conditional Proof of the Goldbach Conjecture*](https://mrc.sdu.edu.cn/english/info/1018/1514.htm). For any fixed \(D>0\) it constructs many represented even targets at which, under GRH for the primitive conductors dividing the target, \(\sqrt{N}\|T_N\|>(\log N)^D\). The targets are not counterexamples to Goldbach; on the same constructed moduli the preprint gives actual prime pairs without GRH.

We checked the key bilinear entry against Propositions 5.4 and 7.4 and §7.2.2 of the original. Hardy reproduction with the two real Mellin test vectors cancels the two \((1+s)\) factors and selects the two opposite congruences. Here the first test is at \(u=m/N\to0\), with \(m\) on a polylogarithmic scale, and the second is at \(u\) near 1. Odd-character orthogonality then compares the counts with \(N-m\) and \(N+m\); the ordinary diagonal is absent. The continuous beta model vanishes on this entry: its three convolutions have zero total integral, so the pole is canceled, and the surviving sample at 1 vanishes because the second test is one at both relevant reflected points. The Hardy norm factor follows by substituting \(\Phi_f(u)=\sqrt u f(u)\) into the original norm formula. These are the two noninterchangeable supports used in Yilin Liu's Lemma 2.1, not the fixed central test of the original prime-pair theorem.

Consequences for the original version: the new lower bound contradicts the uniform R norm criterion (2.30) if Dirichlet GRH holds. It also contradicts the S norm criterion (2.28) for any admissible \(h\) eventually at least \(\sqrt{N}/(\log N)^B\) for a fixed \(B\), by choosing \(D>B+1\) and using the elementary \(\mathfrak S(N)\ll\log\log N\). It does not disprove S for every slowly growing \(h\), does not control the central one-sided Hardy entry, and does not apply to the separate arithmetic [exact delta cover](ExactDeltaLinearRemoval.md). This is a comparison of a new primary preprint with the original hypotheses, not a new solution or a criticism of unrelated Goldbach claims.
