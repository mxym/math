# Complete classification allowing one arbitrary large prime label

**Theorem.** Over every even center N, the only exceptional autonomous components (EACs) of Bożek's Goldbach factorization graph having **at most one prime above 53**, with no size limit on that prime, are the component at 128 with labels {3,5,7,11,13,23,29,41} and that at 2200 with labels {3,13}. Both have no prime above 53 and both centers admit genuine Goldbach pairs. Thus every EAC at a hypothetical counterexample must have at least two primes above 53. The result concerns components, not a stronger range of verified Goldbach centers.

We use the graph orientation, all-height compact classification and published S-unit table provenance of [the compact-core theorem](EACCompactSmooth.md), and the separate [three-label arbitrary-third-prime exclusion](EACThreeLabels.md). Bożek's [Theorem 3.7](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09900v1) already classifies the two-label EAC. It suffices, therefore, to check a possible EAC with precisely one label r>53 and at least three small labels from the set P of 15 odd primes through 53.

Since r cannot divide the difference of two labels in P, at most one small label p has r dividing N−p. There must be such a label by strong connectivity and since r does not divide N. Thus at least two small rows are 53-smooth. The compact-core theorem lists all 36,476 candidate centers by normalized differences and the complete von Känel–Matschke 16-prime S-unit table. Their maximum is 4,020,958,408,470. This is a complete reduction from arbitrary N and not an assumed numerical search range.

For each center, peel every P-factor from every odd small prime row coprime to N. A completely smooth row is eligible if its complement is composite (count multiplicity). At the unique remaining small row p using r, the stripped cofactor h has to equal $r^e$ for some e≥1. Check each e with $59^e\le h$, computing the integer e-th root and verifying its exact power. Initially allow even composite candidate roots r≥59 to avoid any dependence on primality testing. Retain candidates satisfying the row multiplicity ≥2, $N-r\ge3$, and gcd(r,N)=1. Necessarily N−r is supported wholly in P and has at least two factors counting multiplicity. Form the directed graph containing precisely the previously determined eligible small rows plus the rows p and r. To produce an EAC containing r all outgoing factors of every vertex reachable from r must in turn be complete eligible rows in that graph; additionally every such vertex must return to r. Completeness alone already fails in every cell.

The standard Python checker imports the pinned public-table reader from `check_eac_sunits.py`. Download the original `abc53.original.txt` as specified in the compact-core theorem; no second table is required for this extra step. Run:

    python3 -O check_eac_one_large.py /path/to/table-directory

The replay reconstructs all 36,476 centers directly from the hash-verified table. There are 311,308 possible (p,r) extensions after the small-row filters, 14,457 surviving the large-row smoothness test, and **zero** with factor-closed reachability from r. A positive control finds the genuine component {3,13} at N=2200 when the small alphabet is restricted to 3,5,7,11, making 13 its one external label. This step, the unbounded three-label proof and Bożek's size-two classification together exhaust all numbers of small labels; none imply that every possible EAC has this shape.
