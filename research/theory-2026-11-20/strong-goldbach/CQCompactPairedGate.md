# CQ same-target compact covariance gate and high-positive reference audit

Research note, 2026-11-24. The result is a reduction to one concrete **real two-arm signed pairing** on the current fixed target, plus an audit of why the existing high positive prime lower cannot supply it by mass matching. No new positive reserve, no hypothetical relaxed coupling and no Goldbach conclusion.

## 1. A compact-only exact reformulation of the unchanged canonical Y debit

Work on the same actual rows x+y=N, x/N in the fixed central interval J, (x,N)=1; let L=log N and M=|J|N S(N). Use original sharp H=V_raw−Π. For genuine weights D,E define
\[
 B_N(D,E)=\frac{\langle D,E\rangle+\langle E,D\rangle}{2M}.
\]
For nonsymmetric J the two orientations are taken separately or averaged as written, not declared equal. Let β=1/5000, T=T_β, and H_c=H−T (the true compact floor >β). Let Γ=A_{\rm old}+W+Z+G be the previously paid nonnegative negative-arm magnitudes. Let Y=B_0+C be the actual unpaid middle/deep negative magnitude. The supports of Γ,Y,T are pairwise disjoint **on an arm**, with H=−Γ and H=−Y on their respective actual supports. Write y=m(Y), g=m(Γ), w=m(T). Reserve .0378 refers to prior paid sectors only. Define the compact, signed receiver
\[
 K=H_c+\Gamma+Y,\qquad B=K-Y/2=H_c+\Gamma+Y/2,\qquad b=m(B)=1-w+g+y/2.
\]
Here K equals the original sharp score off the paid arm types, tiny arm and Y, and is literally zero on each of those three types. No proof that K has a sign is used. B is a different variable from the old middle B_0.

Let Δ_Y be the increment of the original H,H sum on the actual union with a Y arm after the paid sets (including whole tiny union) were removed. Then
\[
 \boxed{\quad \Delta_Y/M=-2B_N(Y,B).\quad}                       \tag{1}
\]
This is true without no-pair and is exact for the literal finite weights. To see it directly, if exactly one arm is Y, its contribution is −Y K on that orientation; K vanishes on already paid other arms and on Y itself. A both-Y row gives +YY and is included only once. No previously paid positive shell meets a Y row because both arms there have positive actual H. Thus the union's contribution is −2B_N(Y,K)+B_N(Y,Y)=-2B_N(Y,B). In the other derivation, use the published genuine compact raw transfer B_N(Y,V_raw)=2y+o(1) in the original ledger. With j(D)=B_N(D,Π+T), the same original formula is
\[
 \Delta_Y/M=-4y+2j(Y)-v-w_Y+o(1),\quad
 v=B_N(Y,Y),\quad w_Y=2B_N(Y,\Gamma).
\]
Since \(V_{\rm raw}=\Pi+T+H_c\), \(j(Y)=2y-B_N(Y,H_c)+o(1)\), which gives exactly the signed conventions of (1). Thus eliminating prime and tiny is not dropping or estimating either arm: it uses their same-target sum.

## 2. A single measurable mean-zero receiver and quantitative gate

Let R=R_{.04}=L times the genuine squarefree rough indicator, with true least >N^.04, including the actual rank-one primes, and let r=m(R)>0. Define \(\widetilde B=B-(b/r)R\), whose genuine ordinary mean is zero. This centered probe is **not** part of the canonical physical union; its small correction is included exactly below.

For the literal receiver R and any genuine nonnegative compact source D, [the previously checked ratio-10 bound](CQTrueSignedRoughTailEnvelope.md) at tested level N^.40 on the same target gives
\[
 |B_N(D,R)-r m(D)|\le(\bar c-\underline c)m(D)/.04+o(1),\quad
 r\ge\underline c/.04,\quad
 (\underline c,\bar c)=(.5614584359419,.5614605206538).
\]
Apply this only to genuine D≥0, here Y or the high A below. No sieve is applied to a noncompact tail or to the signed B. In particular
\[
 B_N(Y,B)=by+B_N(Y,\widetilde B)+\epsilon_Y,\qquad
 |\epsilon_Y|\le (3.72\cdot10^{-6})by+o(1).                       \tag{2}
\]
The relative correction follows from dividing the true linear width by r and uses b>0. The error may differ across targets and orientations; there is no assertion of its sign.

Paying just this Y union with the genuine .0378 reserve is **equivalent**, at fixed nonzero asymptotic margin, to \[
 B_N(Y,\widetilde B)\le .0189-by-\epsilon_Y
\]
by (1)-(2). The prior ordinary floor \(y>.3537083851\), \(w<-.006388\) and the true paid rank-five part \(g\ge (2/3)(.475)^4\) show
\[
 by> .43052675553,\qquad
 (1-3.72\cdot10^{-6})by-.0189>.41162515.
\]
Consequently an isolated payment necessarily requires \(\boxed{B_N(Y,\widetilde B)<-.4116+o(1)}\) on the actual target. This numerical necessary gate is not a sufficient cutoff; the exact sufficient statement can use \(.0189-(1+3.72\cdot10^{-6})by\) in (2). The needed proposition is now entirely between two genuinely compact angular scores on the original ordered reflected rows. It requires a negative pairing of a genuine positive source with a mean-zero *signed* receiver; it is not a test of one-arm densities. This reformulation by itself neither proves that pairing nor falsifies payment at hypothetical no-pair targets. In particular it does not establish a whole-exterior result. For the genuine M-weighted dyadic average, product angular averaging of the already-known components in the original ledger gives −2by<−.86105 in Δ_Y units; it is then not legitimate to claim universally negative B_N(Y,\widetilde B) of the needed size.

## 3. What the high positive prime reference actually buys, exactly

Let \(A=P_{>.156}\) be the actual genuine high positive rank-two/three angular source (not \(A_{\rm old}\) inside Γ). Its ordinary a=m(A) satisfies \(1.819<a<1.827\), by the directed sorted angle cells below. The known true high prime lower is \(u_A=B_N(A,\Pi)>1.31+o(1)\) on a no-pair target, and is \(u_A+.61R_J/M>1.31+o(1)\) in general. Write \(\tau_A=2B_N(A,T)\), \(\lambda=y/a\), and use the literal **shared signed receiver**:
\[
 u_Y+\tau_Y/2
  =\lambda(u_A+\tau_A/2)-B_N(Y-\lambda A,H_c)+o(1).             \tag{3}
\]
This is obtained by applying true compact raw transfer separately to Y and A, then subtracting; the ordinary mean of Y−λA is zero. It therefore uses the same target, same orientation and same true compact receiver in both high and middle/deep arms. The high and Y one-arm supports are physically disjoint. If desired H_c can be centered by its own ordinary coefficient times R in (3) with only a ratio-10 correction bounded by \((3.72\cdot10^{-6})(1-w)\,[y+\lambda a]\), by applying the rough bound to Y and A separately; no signed source hypothesis is needed.

The exact bridge needed to close via (3) is
\[
 \lambda\tau_A/2-B_N(Y-\lambda A,H_c)-(v+w_Y)/2
 \ \ge\ (2-u_A/a)y-.0189+o(1).                                  \tag{4}
\]
With only the certified no-pair lower \(u_A>1.31\), a sufficient version replaces its right side by \((2-1.31/a)y-.0189\), a strictly positive requested gain \(>.43378\). This is a burden of that **use of the lower bound**, not a necessary threshold with the unknown actual \(u_A\). In particular simply assuming equal mass-normalized joint projections on the high/Y supports, or setting the compact contrast and high signed tail crossing to zero with no justification, does not make the lower available on the prime axis enough. Conversely the weaker true lower does not exclude that the actual high prime moment might be large enough on some targets.

## 4. Shared-tag opening audit and precise next proposition

On target units any common true prime label of x and y is exactly impossible: p|x,y implies p|N, contradicting (x,N)=1. “Shared” can only mean common angle banks, or using the same test receiver, not the same true prime. For distinct actual p,q not dividing N, the unweighted two-mark rows p|x,q|y have a single CRT class modulo pq. Incorporating (x,N)=1 by inclusion-exclusion gives \(|J|\varphi(N)/(pq)+O(2^{\omega(N)})\). For a fixed bank \(N^{.156}<p<N^{.17}\) and a tiny bank \(N^\varepsilon<q\le N^\beta\), the sum of these bare remainder counts is a power smaller than N; this does not estimate a signed CQ score. To use the rank-two subcase x=p r with r a genuine prime, opening the q bank leaves progressions of length \(\asymp N/(pq)\) for r. A direct floor-error lower linear sieve forcing r prime by its actual square-root cutoff would have ratio at most \(2(1-t-s)/(1-t)<2\) for \(t=\log p/L, s=\log q/L\ge\varepsilon>0\), even before a simultaneous rough test of y/q. This statement concerns that particular naive lower: if rank three is allowed and one merely asks for at most two factors in r, the lower sieve no longer has this specific obstacle, but one still has to compute the *signed* cofactor and original (3) cannot be recovered from bare count. Also the actual prime arm Π is disjoint from a true tiny q bank since it lies at central scale. Therefore opening two low tags or applying the old nonnegative third-label rejection does not produce the needed signed contrast. No impossibility for a deeper analytic method is asserted.

The smallest target-specific next proposition that avoids any new noncompact tail/prime bounds is: **on actual hypothetical no-pair targets, prove \(B_N(Y,\widetilde B)\le .0189-(1+3.72\cdot10^{-6})by\) with a fixed favorable margin, or measure its exact rough-error-adjusted version in (2)**. To use the high-positive route specifically, establish (4) instead, using an actual two-arm estimate for its signed combination, not more separate prime lower or rough counts. This pays only the Y union as defined; it does not pay the whole remaining exterior. Fallback is retaining the independent .0378 reserve, not increasing any tiny constant, and marking this specific compact pairing unresolved.
