# A signed rough self-pair at .110, with the original low mixed strip

Keep exactly the original sextic \(H\), score
\(S=G_b+W,\ b=.003\), fixed even \(N\), central \(J\),
\(L=\log N\), \(M=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\), and
physical pairing \(x+y=N,\ x\in NJ,\ (x,N)=1\).
There is no change to the original rough correction \(Q\).

Let \(\mathcal Z\) be the **whole** set of true rows with
both least prime factors \(>N^{.110}\), without any rank
or sign restriction. Keep from the previous mixed
Q-boundary chambers only their original tagged angle
\(.080<t\le .110\), including both orientations; call
that union \(\mathcal C_{\le .110}\).
All other original conditions, including the actual
tag quotient floor .170 and untagged floor .1251,
are unchanged.

**Proposition.** The signed covariance of \(S\) on
\(\mathcal Z\) is \(>.935M+o(M)\); the signed covariance
on \(\mathcal C_{\le .110}\) is \(>.090M+o(M)\).
These disjoint sets replace the old \(\mathcal E_Q\)
and both old \(\mathcal C_Q\) orientations, with lower
\(1.025M\) instead of \(.637M\) before the smaller
literal exterior. This is a gain of \(.388M\) in the
same comparison, with the changed physical support.
There is no lower bound on that exterior and no
Goldbach conclusion.

## 1. Actual positive covariance through one common physical ambient

Write \(a=.110\) and \(\theta=.499\).
On these true rough arms the .003 gate passes,
\(W=0\), and \(S=H+Q\). Thus \(S_+=H_+\) and
\(S_-=[-S]_+\le[-H]_+\) there. The score on a
prime is zero, and a nonsquarefree coordinate
also has zero. No deletion of a nonzero score
with the wrong sign is built into the ambient.

Use the full actual rough pair ambient with
\(P^-(x),P^-(N-x)>N^a\).
The literal pair Selberg upper at support \(N^\theta\)
from [the positive-arm proof, §1](RoughPositiveCredit.md#1-the-genuine-pair-ambient)
gives
\[
  \#\{\text{actual ambient rows}\}\ \le
     (C+o(1))M/L^2,\qquad
  C=\left(a^2\int_0^{\theta/a}\rho_2(z)\,dz\right)^{-1}.
                                                                    \tag{1}
\]
Allowing the physical target-unit condition to be dropped
can only increase this count. At primes of \(N\) the two
affine roots coincide (including 2), and elsewhere there
are two actual roots. The fixed support gives the true
\(O(N^{.998}L^{10})\) square floor, and its normalized
denominator converges uniformly through individual
targets to the Dickman-2 integral in (1), with its
exact \(\mathfrak S(N)\) multiplier as in the cited
proof. The checker integrates a downward-rounded
argument to give an upper bound for \(C\).

Use true ranks two and three only as nonnegative
witnesses for the positive part, without changing the
actual score on the rest. For a threshold \(\lambda>0\)
let \(m_\lambda\) be the ordinary unopposed normalized
mass of the true witness ranks where \(H/\log n>\lambda\).
All factors are \(>N^a\). Actual product BV on
these genuine bounded-rank angle boxes and the
actual opposite rough quotient gives the lower
\((k\,m_\lambda+o(1))M/L^2\) for its marginal within
(1), with \(k=.549/a\), as justified in
[the one-by-two transfer, §2](OneByTwoTagCredit.md).
There are no outer tags here: the level is
\(N^{4a}\le N^{.499}\), and the real residue
\(N\bmod d\) uses the same reduced source and target units.
Taking the same lower on the reflected interval uses
its actual equal length, not an assumption that \(J\)
is symmetric.

The two witnesses of thresholds \(\lambda,\mu\)
therefore have actual simultaneous count at least
\([k(m_\lambda+m_\mu)-C]_+M/L^2+o(M/L^2)\).
Sum the lower layer cake on 160 equal positive
amplitude increments. The explicit certified
ordinarily unopposed masses use the same genuine
ordered rank-two and rank-three angle simplex
boxes as [the preceding positive-arm proof, §3](RoughPositiveCredit.md),
now with real floor .110.
For rank two the exact amplitude is \(1-F(u)\),
where \(u\) is the smaller own-log angle.
For rank three it is
\(1-\sum_{i=1}^3 [F(u_i)-F(1-u_i)]\);
the function in square brackets is decreasing.
The checker takes the lower score over the whole
ordered angle box, the lower harmonic from the
same actual prime factors, and drops cells with
insufficient lower amplitude. Strict bin boundaries
give slack for the uniform \(O_J(1/L)\) own-log change.
Consequently the true positive product on these
two witness ranks alone exceeds \(1.5797M+o(M)\).
The witnesses are not assumed independent; (1)
is the same ambient in both marginals.

## 2. The full negative crossing on the new self-pair

An actual opposite-sign product is bounded using a
true rough receiver, not a model pair distribution.
For any nonnegative compact positive-floor weighted
source \(Z\) the genuine product-BV linear upper at the
same level \(N^{4a}\) gives coefficient \(U=.575/a\)
for the actual remote \(P^->N^a\) condition.
All source and receiver roles below use that same
positive estimate in their actual orientation.

On ranks at most seven we have \(S_+\le\log n\).
For ranks two and three this follows respectively
from \(1-F(u)\le1\) and the displayed rank-three
formula (or, for a giant, from monotonicity of \(F\)).
At rank four use
\((528-1680q)\prod u_i\le108/4^4<1\) on the positive
part, where \(q=\sum u_i^2\ge1/4\).
Ranks five through seven are nonpositive for \(H\)
before clipping. The high even ranks are zero; at
this rough floor the only remaining high odd rank
is nine, by \(9a<1<10a\).
Let \(m_j\) bound the unopposed normalized ordinary
negative mass at rank \(j\); at rank nine use the
absolute mass \(m_9\). Applying the genuine upper
with \(S_-\) as source and the above positive cap
pays the opposite-sign products with the positive
arm of rank at most seven. For the positive rank-nine
exception, apply it instead with that prime-product
source, and use \(S_-/\log n\le 2^9\) pointwise
on the other arm. Thus the total bilateral adverse
debit is at most
\[
           2U(m_3+m_4+m_5+m_6+m_7+m_9+2^9m_9)M+o(M).
                                                                    \tag{2}
\]
All source weights in these bounds are continuous
positive parts on genuine finite-rank angular groups,
up to the piecewise constant actual Q cutoff, so
the same true-box product-BV transfer applies.
This does not assume any opposite-sign occupancy
factorizes. Upper bounds for the uncompensated
negative ordinary mass are valid because \(Q\)
only clips it.

Here are explicit bounds for every term of (2).
For prime rank-one or rank two the negative
part is zero. In rank three with no giant, let
\(z_i=1-2u_i\). Then \(0\le z_i\le1-2a=.78\),
\(\sum z_i=1\), and
\(z_i^4(11-7z_i^2)/4\le z_i\), so the score
is nonnegative. With a giant, writing \(u,v\)
for the smaller two angles, the score is
\(1-F(u)-F(v)+F(u+v)\).
It is increasing in each small angle on
\([a,1/2]\) since \(F''\ge0\) there.
The score at \((a,.14)\) is positive, so
a negative requires \(u,v<.14\), and its
magnitude is bounded by \([2F(a)-1-F(2a)]_+\).
The ordinary two-angle density with the giant
chosen once is \(du\,dv/(2uv(1-u-v))\).
Using the full small rectangle gives
\[
      m_3\le[2F(a)-1-F(2a)]_+
                     \frac{(.14-a)^2}{2a^2(1-.28)} .
\]

For \(j=4,5\), put \(A_j=1-ja\) and
\(\bar q_j=ja^2+2a A_j+2 A_j^2/(j+1)\).
These are the total excess and the mean of \(q\)
on the uniform excess simplex, after the
\(\prod u_i\) in the original score has canceled
the true harmonic denominators. On the four-variable
simplex \({\rm Var}(q)=2A_4^4/175\) by the elementary
Dirichlet moments. Write \(d=\bar q_4-11/35\)
and use \(\mathbb E X_+=(\mathbb E X+\mathbb E|X|)/2\),
with Cauchy for the second term.
The exact rank-five bound from the
[original slice formulas](Proof.md#2-the-whole-rough-negative-arm-comparison)
has concave coefficient \(10080\sqrt q-1056/\sqrt q\);
use Jensen on that same genuine ordinary simplex.
This gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 m_4&\le\frac{1680 A_4^3}{4!3!}
            \frac{\sqrt{d^2+2A_4^4/175}+d}{2},\\
 m_5&\le\frac{A_5^4}{5!4!}
              (10080\sqrt{\bar q_5}-1056/\sqrt{\bar q_5}).
 \end{split}
\]
The same literal slice formulas give
\(m_6\le40320(1-6a)^5/(6!5!)\) and
\(m_7\le(2112/a^3+80640\cdot7)(1-7a)^6/(7!6!)\).
For rank nine the absolute high-odd envelope is
\((16896/9^4+322560/9^6)\log n\), by AM–GM on
its four and six smallest angles. Its ordinary
density is bounded by
\((1-9a)^8/(9!8!a^9)\). This accounts for rank nine
in both places of (2); no other nonzero rank is possible.
The outward bilateral debit in (2) is \(<.64395M+o(M)\).
Combining with §1 proves \(>.935M+o(M)\) on the whole
actual \(\mathcal Z\), since products with both scores
of the same sign that were not used are nonnegative.

## 3. Exact partition replacement

The old rough self block \(\mathcal E_Q\), both arms
\(>.1251\), lies entirely inside \(\mathcal Z\).
For the old mixed Q-boundary pair, its untagged arm
is \(>.1251\), and the least actual angle on its tagged
arm is its named \(t\) (the quotient floor is .170).
Thus its portion with \(t>.110\) lies entirely in
\(\mathcal Z\); the portion \(.080<t\le.110\) lies
entirely outside. The endpoint belongs **only** to
the latter. The latter has the same original signed
lower as in [the Q-boundary proof](QBoundaryMixedChambers.md)
with the literal tag sum shortened to the first
30 cells. Its true capacity lower and full adverse
rank upper are checked with that same finite replay,
giving \(>.090M+o(M)\) for its two actual orientations.

The old \(\mathcal F_{\rm all}\) is **entirely**
disjoint from \(\mathcal Z\), not just after a
rank restriction: on each of its arms the two
actual least tag angles have sum \(\le .170\),
whereas any \(.110\)-rough arm has first-two sum
\(>.220\). It is also disjoint from the retained
old low mixed portion by the previous partition.
All the later exact small-tag or signed adverse
chambers have at least one genuine prime \(\le N^.080\);
neither new set has such a prime. Consequently the
two new signed credits replace only the old
\(.470+.167\), with no new intersection or
double count. They remove those previous
whole sets and the genuine enlargement.
