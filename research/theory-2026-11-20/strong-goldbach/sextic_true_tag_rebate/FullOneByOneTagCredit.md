# The complete true one-by-one chamber by opposed signed transports

Keep the fixed large even target \(N\), the original central interval
\(J\Subset(1/3,2/3)\), \(L=\log N\), \(M=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\), and
the identical sextic \(S=G_b+W\), \(b=.003\), of the
[full medium comparison](FullMediumCohort.md). Every pair uses
\(x+y=N,\ x\in NJ,\ (x,N)=1\). Let \(\mathcal C_{11}\) be the
**entire true chamber** where both coordinates have exactly one
prime factor in \((N^{.003},N^{.080}]\) and every other prime
factor \(>N^{.080}\), with squarefree support.

**Proposition.** Uniformly through actual even targets,
\[
                   \sum_{\mathcal C_{11}} S(x)S(y)>.0705M+o(M).
\]
There is no assumption on either actual sign or completed rank.
The chamber is disjoint from all other exact tag counts, but not
from the old complete two-tag ambient at the larger tag cutoff.
To use this lower in a ledger retaining that ambient, remove
\(\mathcal C_{11}\) from the old ambient first, as detailed in §4.
No sign is asserted about the remaining exterior.

## 1. Literal variables and the longer-level actual source

Here \(S=H\) on both sides: \(Q\) requires least angle \(>.1251\),
\(W\) requires an actual first-two sum at most .065, and the .003
gate passes. Lower-rank notation below is not a condition in the
theorem. A nonsquarefree row gives zero; a prime cannot occur in the
chamber because of its actual mandatory polynomially small tag.

Put \(a=.080\). For the named tag \(p\) in \(x=pq\), write
\(t=\log p/L\) and define
\[
              g_t(s)=\frac{F(s)-F(s+t)}{t},\qquad
          F(s)=44(1/2-s)_+^4-112(1/2-s)_+^6 .
\]
From the true divisor sum the normalized weight on this arm is
\[
       \frac{H(pq)}{Lt}
       =\sum_{d\mid q}\mu(d)g_t(\log d/L)+O_J(L^{-1}).            \tag{1}
\]
This is a true one-tag divided difference, not a sign approximation
or a change in the roughness condition. The uniform error follows
from the fixed positive tag floor and bounded number of factors.
Here \(g_t\ge0\) because \(F\) is decreasing, and \(g_t=0\)
on \(s\ge.5\). The errors in replacing own-log coordinates
are \(o(M)\) on the genuine weighted paired upper of §2 below
(apply it with \(d=1\)), both before and after multiplication
by a bounded normalized score.

Write \(X_\ell\) for \(H\) restricted to the chamber's one-arm
condition and completed rank at most seven, and
\(X_+=[X_\ell]_+\), \(X_-=[-X_\ell]_+\).
The complementary score \(X_h\) is restored in §3. Partition the
tag angle into .0002 cells \((t_-,t_+]\) from .003 to .080.
For each such (left source) cell \(i\), both \(X_\pm\) on that cell
admit the absolute reduced-model progression transfer at the
actual residue \(N\) to modulus \(N^{\theta_i}\), where, in units
of \(10^{-4}\), the level chosen is
\[
 P=10000,\quad t_-=i/P,\quad t_+=(i+2)/P,\qquad
 P\theta_i=\min\{5000+\lfloor i/2\rfloor,\,
               \lfloor(40000-6(i+2))/7\rfloor\}-4 .             \tag{2}
\]
In particular \(\theta_i<\min(.5+t_-/2,\,4/7-6t_+/7)\),
with fixed positive room, throughout the whole range.

This uses [Fouvry, *Autour du théorème de Bombieri–Vinogradov II*,
Corollaire 1 and (1.3)](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1987_4_20_4_617_0.pdf),
on the *actual* outer short prime \(p\), and the *actual* grouped
product \(q\) of the remaining primes. The two modulus rails are
\(X^{1/2}V^{1/2}\) and \(X^{4/7}V^{-6/7}\), with fixed power slack,
where \(V\) is the short-slot length and \(X=4UV\) in the notation
of that corollary, \(U\) the long-slot length. The short slot
has length at least \(N^{.003-o(1)}\). The argument is the physical
fine-cell application in [the quartic tiny-opponent proof, §1](
../quartic_tiny_opponent/Proof.md#1-a-real-over-root-distribution-bound-for-the-actual-source),
now with the two actual rails chosen separately for each angular
cell by (2). In detail, subdivide every true prime variable into
multiplicative intervals of ratio \(1+L^{-C}\), with \(C\) fixed
arbitrarily large. The normalized signed profile and its positive
parts are Lipschitz on these fixed-floor finite-rank boxes.
Freeze the weights in that box; the grouped coefficient on \(q\)
is divisor-bounded and the short coefficient is a prime interval
satisfying Siegel–Walfisz, including auxiliary-prime omissions as
in (1.3). Rectangular cells meeting the two actual spatial
endpoints are discarded at divisor-moment cost \(o(M)\) by
a sufficiently fine mesh. For the retained cells \(UV=(1+o(1))x\);
thus \(X=4UV>N\) literally, because \(J\Subset(1/3,2/3)\).
The physical residue in Fouvry is \(N\), not a small representative.
The long coefficient is not required to be a prime or to satisfy
Siegel–Walfisz. The restrictions to the actual source units are
retained in both factors and in its reduced model.
Taking the phases of the errors as the divisor-bounded modulus
weights in the corollary gives the absolute form; the multiplicities
and fixed log weights in the present use are absorbed using its
arbitrary logarithmic saving. The same holds with \(1-J\).

## 2. Opposed signed divisor layers and one common affine upper

Write \(y=u d r\), where \(u\) is its actual tag and \(d\mid y/u\)
is the squarefree term of (1). The original floor for the
quotient is \(a\). On an opposing tag cell \(j\), use angle
\(v=\log u/L\in(v_-,v_+]\) and a divisor cell \(s=\log d/L\) of
width .0005 with upper endpoint \(s_+\le.5\).
Write \(e_a(s)\) and \(o_a(s)\) for the even and odd ordinary
reciprocal divisor densities. They are the sums of
\(1/k!\) times the \(k\)-fold convolution of
\(1_{s>a}\,ds/s\), separated by actual \(k\) parity, and
\(d=1\) is a separate even unit atom. These are distributions of
**labels**, not distributions assumed on the actual reflected pair.

For a fixed test product \(ud\) the remaining quotient \(r\)
is genuinely \(a\)-rough. Using (2), put \(D=\theta_i-v_+-s_+\)
and apply the linear sieve at level \(N^D\) whenever \(D>0\).
The actual moduli \(ud\ell\) never exceed \(N^{\theta_i}\).
As in [the signed subroot transfer](MediumSemiprimeAdverse.md#2-both-signs-in-the-ordinary-subroot-transfer),
the reduced progression model keeps the real source and target
units, with root \(1/(p'-1)\) at tested \(p'\) off the actual
needed masks. Omit the tag \(u\) from the sieving list and
exclude its true square afterwards. The other prime factors
of \(d\) all exceed \(N^a\); possible squares in either
coordinate have aggregate \(O(N^{1-.003+o(1)})\) plus an
\(N^{1/2+o(1)}\) floor, with harmless fixed divisor and log
weights. The excluded factors of the source also have positive
polynomial floors and affect the normalized local factors only
by \(1+o(1)\). The genuine factorization weights \(u,d,\ell\)
have divisor-bounded multiplicity at a given actual modulus,
even with the angle and sign restrictions. The outside
\(1/\varphi(ud)\) is \((1+o(1))/(ud)\) on these genuine labels.
Removing the target primes from the real label harmonic does not
change its positive-angle limits; the target Euler factor remains
the actual \(\mathfrak S(N)\).
Use \(k=e^{-\gamma}f_{\rm lin}(D/a)/a\) and
\(U=e^{-\gamma}F_{\rm lin}(D/a)/a\), with \(k=0\) for
\(D\le2a\). For \(0<D<a\) the same true upper \(U=2/D\)
uses the smaller prime cutoff \(N^{D/2}\).
No linear estimate is used when \(D\le0\).

There is also a true upper for **either** nonnegative part of
this layer, obtained without progression transfer. Fix the
actual labels \(p,u,d\); they are disjoint between the two sides
by the target-unit condition, and \((u,d)=1\) by the actual
placement of the tags. Work on the primitive line
\(p q+ud r=N\), with true free length
\(|J|N/(pud)+O(1)\). Both actual quotients \(q,r\) are
\(a\)-rough; neither is treated as a prime. Use the actual
dimension-two Selberg square at support
\[
             D_2=.499(1-t_+-v_+-s_+).
\]
At primes of \(N\) the two bad residues coincide, including 2;
at primes of \(pud\) one slope is zero with nonzero constant
on that coordinate; elsewhere there are two bad residues.
The extra local factor from the fixed tags is \(1+o(1)\)
because their actual factors exceed \(N^{.003}\).
The genuine affine root calculation and normalized Dickman-2
denominator are precisely those of [the common tagged ambient](
TaggedNegativeCredit.md#1-one-common-real-ambient-for-each-two-cell-pair).
This yields the upper coefficient
\[
          C_2=\left(a^2\mathcal G(D_2/a)\right)^{-1},
          \qquad \mathcal G(w)=\int_0^w \rho_2(z)\,dz .            \tag{3}
\]
The true floor \(O(N^{2D_2}L^{10})\) is power-small relative to
**each** actual free length, since \(1-t_+-v_+-s_+\ge.34\).
Summing these uniform relative errors uses the true reciprocal
\(p,u,d\) harmonics. The upper may drop the remaining target-unit
condition, whereas the subroot and overroot lower always retain
it. No assertion about a moving residue for primes is used in (3).

For any cell \(i\), let \(m_+,m_-\) be the ordinary unopposed
coefficients per unit tag width for \(X_+,X_-\) in that cell.
Let \(c_\pm\) be caps for \(X_\pm/(Lt)\) there.
The actual tag factors cancel their reciprocal prime harmonics,
leaving \(dt\,dv\); positive- and negative-source caps in (3)
give \(c_+ C_2\) and \(c_- C_2\) on the same actual layer.
For the directed contribution of \(X_\ell(x)H(y)\)
the genuine lower coefficient in a non-atomic cell is bounded
below by the following integrated expression:
\[
 g_v(s)\big[
 k(m_+e_a+m_-o_a)
       - o_a\min(U m_+,c_+C_2)-e_a\min(U m_-,c_-C_2)
 \big].                                                        \tag{4}
\]
Here \(m_\pm\) in (4) denote averages on the **whole** fixed
left tag cell. All remaining constants \(k,U,C_2\) for a given
left tag cell and opposing two cells are chosen at the safe levels
just specified, so are independent of the value of the left tag.
When \(D\le0\), take both adverse pieces from the affine bound
and take zero positive credit. The atom at \(d=1\) contributes
the genuine lower \(g_v(0)(k m_+-U m_-)\)
with \(D=\theta_i-v_+\).
Equation (4) follows by the lower on each sign-agreeing
positive term and the chosen upper on each sign-disagreeing
term, before subtraction. The angular integrations of the
nonnegative counts can be made first on fixed finer boxes
or bounded by their whole-cell enclosures.

One may choose *either* proved upper in each adverse term
of (4). In the certificate the fixed choice is made by comparing
the numerical midpoint model of the two options, merely to
select a useful proved inequality; no midpoint value is used
as a bound. All terms selecting the progression estimate are
collected into the single coefficient multiplying \(m_+\) or
\(m_-\), respectively, before outward interval evaluation.
This retains their genuine signed cancellation in the one
ordinary source moment. The rest use the genuine affine uppers.
 
For each ordered pair of fixed tag cells take the better of
the forward bound just proved and its reflected counterpart
(with actual intervals \(J,1-J\)). There is a single chosen
orientation per pair of physical tag cells, not two contributions
from that pair. The two directed expansions pay the entire
opponent in their chosen orientation; their only missing
part consists of rows which are high on the chosen source arm.

## 3. Outward replay, including every remaining physical rank

Use the slice bounds from the [negative one-by-three proof, §2](
NegativeOneByThreeCredit.md#2-actual-pointwise-estimates-and-the-ordinary-inputs).
In particular \(c_-=15.4\) suffices for all low one-tag ranks.
Ranks two and three have positive part bounded by \(g_t(0)\).
At rank four the positive part is bounded by
\([528-1680(t^2+(1-t)^2/3)]_+((1-t)/3)^3\);
take \(c_+\) as the maximum. Ranks five through seven are
nonpositive. The actual ordinary \(m_+\) uses rank two
\(g_t(0)/(1-t)\), the rank-three two-variable quotient
\([g_t(0)-g_t(z)-g_t(1-t-z)]_+\) integrated against the
true reciprocal simplex, and the exact rank-four positive
slice with quotient harmonic denominators cancelled.
For \(m_-\), subtract the ordinary signed full one-tag
mean from \(m_+\), with an interval error of the ordinary
absolute mass of \(X_h\). The full ordinary signed mean
is the actual reciprocal convolution with an even unit
atom and parity signs on the opened \(d\), and remaining
rough cofactor. These are the same genuine unopposed means
as in the previous one-tag proofs; no fixed-target pairing
is evaluated by those means.

For \(X_h\) all even ranks above six vanish. Only odd
\(r=9,11,13\) can remain, since the quotient has floor \(a\).
Writing \(k=r-1\), \(T=1-t\), the old true signed derivative

     1056\,2^{k-5}(T/k)^3+80640\,2^{k-7}(T/k)^5 .

The ordinary high mass \(m_h\) multiplies these caps by

For receiver uppers \(U_\ell,U_h\), open the one actual tag
and use the ratio-four linear upper at exponent .320 and
floor .080 on the quotient, multiplying by the relevant
pointwise caps. Its actual modulus is at most \(N^{.400}\),
so ordinary product BV applies to the genuine finite-rank
source, exactly as in the earlier one-tag receiver.
Missing high rows when choosing the orientation in §2 have
total possible adverse magnitude at most
\[
                  (2U_\ell+U_h)m_h M+o(M).                     \tag{5}
\]
Indeed either low-high orientation occurs at most once and
the high-high portion occurs only once. The same true
support is used in both the ordinary mass and the positive

## 4. Moving the old overlap once

In the [old complete tagged block](CompleteTaggedCredit.md),
all true quotient factors after its two named least factors
exceed \(N^{.110}\). Therefore an original .002 two-tag
cell is of actual .080-count one exactly when its first
cell is at or below .080 and its second cell lies strictly
above .080. The half-open .002 grid agrees literally with
the .080 threshold. The old overlap with \(\mathcal C_{11}\)
is exactly the old ordered pairs of those cells.
One can omit precisely those pairs in the old nonnegative
cellwise witness lower, and keep its old global high-odd
debit once. The analogous zero-count mask is the set of old
cells with both actual names above .080. In particular
the aligned-mask replay gives \(>.1332M\) on the old
complete block with both \(\mathcal C_{11}\) and the full
zero-by-zero chamber removed. No other true row of the
old block is discarded; the omitted pairs are paid by
the corresponding replacement bounds. The two new
actual whole chambers are disjoint from each other.
