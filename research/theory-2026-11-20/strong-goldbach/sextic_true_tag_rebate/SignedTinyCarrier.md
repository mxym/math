# Signed tiny arms of the jointly compensated degree-six score

Use the genuine fixed-target \(N,L,J,M,\Pi,R_J\), own-log score \(H\)
and the **narrow jointly paid** compensation of
[Theorem B of the ordered-mark proof](OrderedMarkCovariance.md):
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 B_0=\{p\ {\rm prime}:N^{.010}<p\le N^{.012}\},\quad
 k_0(n)=\#\{p\in B_0:p\mid n\},\\
 Q_{\rm rough}=[-H]_+1_{P^->N^{.1251}},\quad
 A^0=(k_0-\binom{k_0}{2})H,\quad
 H_C=H+Q_{\rm rough}-A^0.
 \end{gathered}
\]
This is literally the original sextic \(H_C\), not the nearby
affine or quartic companion. All pairings preserve target units.
Put \(I_b(n)=1_{P^-(n)\le N^b}\), and use the actual carrier window
\(D=(.026,.048]\).
For each sufficiently small fixed \(0<b\le .0003\), let \(\mathcal C_b\)
be the following disjoint union of actual central source integers
\(n\) with \(P^-(n)>N^b\):
* \(n\) has at least one true prime factor with angle \(\log p/L\in D\),
  with **either** sign of \(H_C(n)\);
* \(n\) has no such prime factor, but has an actual divisor with angle
  in \(D\), and \(H_C(n)<0\).

**Theorem.** Uniformly in all sufficiently large even targets
at each sufficiently small fixed \(b\), the three disjoint
actual pair regions
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
  P^-(x)\le N^b,\ y\in\mathcal C_b, &
  P^-(y)\le N^b,\ x\in\mathcal C_b,\\
  P^-(x),P^-(y)\le N^b &
\end{array}
\]
have signed \({H_C(x)H_C(y)}\) contributions respectively
\(>68bM,\ >68bM,\ >5700b^2M\).
Every score and all its multiplicities are retained on these
regions, including both signs on the entire tiny arm.
In particular the third region is not added to any part of
either one-sided region. All other actual rows remain unpriced:
this includes the one-tiny rows where the opposite carrier
condition fails, and all rows where neither arm is tiny.
There is no sign claim on the whole residual or a binary conclusion.

## 1. Actual small-mark transfer against a carrier source

On a tiny arm \(Q_{\rm rough}=0\). Its score is
\(H_C=(1-k_0+\binom{k_0}{2})H\) and the multiplier is
nonnegative and uniformly bounded, since \(k_0<100\).
The same uniform absolute bound \(|H_C|\ll |H|\)
holds everywhere. In the following take a true small-tag bank
\(B=(N^a,N^b]\) on primes with \(a=b^C\), where \(C>1\)
is a fixed constant chosen in §3. In the order of limits
\(b,a\) are fixed before \(N\) grows.

Let \(Z(y)=H_C(y)1_{y\in\mathcal C_b}\).
For a product \(h\) of \(s\) distinct \(B\)-tags,
\(1\le s\le5\), and a product \(g\) of \(j\) distinct
\(B_0\)-tags, \(0\le j\le2\), open the real receiver
\(x=hg\,v\). Its divisor profile is, with angles
\(t_\ell\) of these \(s+j\) genuine labels,
\[
 \Delta_{\boldsymbol t}F(w)
       =\sum_{E\subseteq[s+j]}(-1)^{|E|}F(w+\sum_{\ell\in E}t_\ell).
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
One may use first a fixed smooth subroot taper of \(F\), then
remove it using the genuine marked absolute tails in the degree-six
base. Here \(Q_{\rm rough}(x)=\Pi(x)=0\) because of the actual
\(B\)-tag. The physical modulus is \(hgd\), \(d\le N^{.5}\),
with \(\alpha=\log(hg)/L\le .024+5b\le.0255\).
The \(B,B_0\) labels are disjoint. Repeats with \(d\) may be
restored in the analytic test because they impose a genuine
\(p^2\mid x\) for a fixed polynomial-sized label;
the true mass and corresponding reciprocal models are
power-small with fixed divisor weights. Further squarefree
and own-log reductions are those of the sextic base.

Choose a genuine short source group \(P\mid y\) with angle
\(\beta\in D\). On a source with a prime carrier, use all
eligible primes as alternative choices with the reciprocal
of their actual count. On the additional negative-source rows,
use all eligible actual divisors in \(D\) with that reciprocal
normalization. Thus their total selection weight is one,
without postulating a unique carrier. The source has fixed
positive prime floor \(b\), hence bounded rank at fixed \(b\).
For each chosen group, group its actual prime factors into
the short coefficient and the remaining ones into the long
one. Their true product-box approximations and arbitrary
auxiliary-unit Siegel--Walfisz are precisely as in
[the ordered-mark proof, §2](OrderedMarkCovariance.md):
fix all but one genuine prime of the short group and apply
maximal prime Siegel--Walfisz to the last polynomial-sized prime.
This argument allows any fixed finite group size.
Signs, the conditional extra-source negative part, and the
mark-count reciprocals are fixed bounded angle profiles in
each cell. Carrier-choice walls are actual fixed subset
sum boundaries. To discard a width-\(\varepsilon\) wall,
fix its true divisor and apply the positive sextic paired
envelope to the two affine forms after extraction, costing
\(O(M/P)\) on that fiber at fixed source floor and rank.
The true reciprocal harmonics of its marked prime slots
then cost \(O_b(\varepsilon)\) by conditioning on all but
one angle. Thus finitely many wide angular cells, and then
log-fine spatial/angle cells, justify the true source
profile approximation; take the wall width down to zero
after the large-target limit. Actual squares and spatial
boundaries are handled by the same real product-box estimates.

The \(hgd\) modulus is separated with first slot \(d\)
and second slot \(hg\). Fourier inversion on the fixed
smooth taper separates each summand of (1) into a
\(d\)-weight and an \(hg\)-weight, including the actual
chosen subsets in that latter weight. The two **literal**
Fouvry C.1 rails from
[the primary moving-residue theorem (1.4), p.619](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1987_4_20_4_617_0.pdf)
have fixed power reserves:
\[
   -\beta+1+\alpha\le .9995,\qquad
   \tfrac32\beta+\tfrac78+2\alpha\le .998.
\]
On the actual central source boxes its analytic scale is
\(4UV=(4+o(1))y>N\), so the residue is the admissible
actual \(N\), not a bounded surrogate. Use the usual weighted
true discrepancy on these separated dyadic groups.
The model is the true \(1/\varphi(hgd)\) on the reduced
mask \(N y h g\). Its zeroth signed \(d\) moment is zero
and first-log moment has coefficient
\(-\mathfrak S(Nyhg)=-(1+o(1))\mathfrak S(N)\).
Each added prime is polynomially large at the fixed floor,
and overlapping target/source primes omitted from the harmonic
have power-small mass. Consequently the actual pairing for
each fixed \(s,j\) has principal \(-M_Z\) times
\[
 \frac{1}{s!\,j!}
 \int_{(a,b]^s\times(.010,.012]^j}
     \Delta_{\boldsymbol t}F'(0)\prod_\ell\frac{dt_\ell}{t_\ell},
 \qquad
 M_Z=\mathfrak S(N)\sum_{y\in N(1-J),(y,N)=1}Z(y).                \tag{2}
\]
Both factorials are the literal unordered-set counts.
This result is valid with reflected interval and arms.
It retains all signs of \(Z\) and all signs on the marked
opposite arm. The errors are \(o_{a,b}(M)\) for these
fixed \(s,j\), after taper/angle limits as above.

Let \(d(a,b)\) be the signed coefficient multiplying \(M_Z\)
after summing (2) with \((-1)^{s+1+j}\) and the leading minus sign.
All displacements in (2) are at most .0255 and strictly below
the half seam. Since \(F'\) is a polynomial of degree five
there, terms with \(s+j>5\) vanish, and terms with \(s\ge2\)
are \(O(b^2)\). Put \(\ell=-\lim_{b\downarrow0}d(0,b)/b\).
Its exact expression is
\[
 \ell=78-
       \int_{.010}^{.012}[F''(u)-F''(0)]\,\frac{du}{u}
       -\frac12\int_{(.010,.012]^2}\Delta_{u,v}F''(0)
                                                    \frac{du\,dv}{uv}.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
Indeed the first small difference is \(-t F''(w)+O(t^2)\).
Here \(F'''(u)\le1152\) through .012, and
\(F^{(4)}(u)<0\) through .024, by the elementary sextic
derivatives. Thus \(\ell\ge78-1152(.002)=75.696\),
and \(d(a,b)=-\ell b+O(b^2+a)\).
This coefficient includes both actual \(B_0\) corrections.

## 2. An ordinary signed mass on the whole geometric source

On \(\mathcal C_b\) the rough correction is identically
zero: any proper carrier divisor in \(D\) forces an actual
factor below the rough threshold. Also there are no prime
integer rows on this source. The extra composite-only
carriers in \(\mathcal C_b\) have \(H_C<0\), so it suffices
to estimate the ordinary signed mass on the full true prime
hit \(D\), without a sign restriction.

The ordinary geometric calculation of
[the full geometric-bank proof, §§1–2](../quartic_tiny_opponent/WholeGeometricBanks.md)
applies to the same genuine prime-slot split with the sextic
\(F\). In detail, write
\(\nu_D=1_D(t)dt/t,\ \sigma=\exp_*(\nu_D),\ \tau=\exp_*(-\nu_D)\).
On the true no-hit support the ordinary sextic divisor score
has limiting angular coefficient
\[
 -\int_{a_1+a_2<1}F'(a_1)d\sigma(a_1)d\tau(a_2)
       +[(F\sigma)*\tau]_{\rm dens}(1).
\]
This is the ordinary prime-product angular limit, obtained
by the actual signed divisor and complementary prime
harmonics. The shifted Dickman/Buchstab transfer from the
cited proof requires only bounded \(F'\); its boundary
density at total one is retained. The positive cutoff and
tiny-factor tails used in that calculation apply also to
this fourth-order half-zero profile by its stronger
Fourier decay.

Let \(X\) have law \(e^{-\nu_D(D)}\sigma\). Its cumulants
are \(\kappa_j=(.048^j-.026^j)/j\).
The untruncated polynomial mean \(-\mathbb E P_6'(X)\),
with \(P_6\) the full sextic, is \(1+T\), where
\[
 T=78\,\mathbb E X-576\,\mathbb E X^2+1504\,\mathbb E X^3
                       -1680\,\mathbb E X^4+672\,\mathbb E X^5.
\]
On \([1/2,1]\), \(P_6'\ge0\), so passing to the sharp
zero beyond half can only lose a lower bound at \(X>1\).
That tail, the signed cut \(a_1+a_2\ge1\), and the
boundary density at one each require at least 21 actual
bank labels in their respective convolution series
(the sum of 20 angles is \(<1\)).
They have absolute combined upper \(<10^{-4}\), using
\(\nu_D(D)<.62,\ |F|\le1,\ |F'|\le43\),
\(\|\nu_D^{*j}\|_\infty\le(.026)^{-1}\nu_D(D)^{j-1}\)
and the polynomial derivative tail, exactly as in
[the rational checker](check_signed_tiny_carrier.py).
The total ordinary whole divisor-score mean is
\(-F'(0)=1\), by the same calculation with the bank empty.
Subtracting the no-hit lower gives a true ordinary mean
\(<-1.098+o(1)\) for \(H\) on the entire prime hit.
This includes all positive rows in the hit.

Finally the entire ordinary absolute \(A^0\) mass is
\(<.194M\). Indeed the true signed ordinary coefficient
is \(-m_1^0+m_2^0<-.141\) and
\(M_{[-A^0]_+}<.167M+o(M)\) by the narrow jointly paid proof;
use \(|A^0|=2[-A^0]_++A^0\).
The true source floor \(P^->N^b\) costs only
\(O(b^\gamma M)\) in the ordinary absolute mass,
from the genuine one-arm Fourier envelope.
The additional negative carriers can only improve the
bound. Thus for all sufficiently small fixed \(b\),
\(M_Z/M\le-.90+o(1)\) in either central interval.

## 3. The literal tiny union and its sixth-order remainder

Write \(j_B(n)=\#\{p\in B:p\mid n\}\).
The actual hit indicator equals the first five
inclusion–exclusion terms plus \(E_6(n)\), with
\[
                     |E_6(n)|\le\binom{j_B(n)}6 .
\]
For each product \(h\) of six such genuine primes,
impose its actual divisibility. In the positive Fourier
envelope extract all six factors
\(|1-e^{i\xi\log p/L}|\) on that arm, not their trivial
bound 2. Corrected Henriot on the true quotient lattice
gives the usual two-root factor times \(1/h\) and
frequency exponent \(c=3/2\) per arm, which has slack
above \(4/\pi\). If both remainders are present, impose
both actual six-tag products \(h,h'\), and use the
two affine forms on the genuine lattice \(hm+h'm'=N\);
its length is \(\asymp_J N/(hh')\).
The true products are coprime on target units. At primes
dividing either leading coefficient just one root remains,
at target primes the two roots coincide, and at all
other primes the usual two distinct roots apply.
All coefficients and interval lengths have fixed power
slack because each \(h\le N^{6b}\).
The bounded \(H_C/H\) factors do not alter this estimate.
Use the true full score, not any assumed conditioned
distribution of the marked prime factors.

Uniformly for \(T=|\xi|\), the **whole** small-prime
phase harmonic satisfies
\[
 \sum_{p\le N^b}\frac{|1-e^{i\xi\log p/L}|}{p}
  \ll \begin{cases} bT,& bT\le1,\\1+\log(bT),&bT>1.\end{cases}
\]
For the first part use \(T\log p/L\) and the usual
prime log harmonic. For the second split at \(N^{1/T}\);
below it use the same log bound and above it use Mertens.
If the splitting point is below 2 then \(T>L/\log 2\),
and the full reciprocal sum to \(N^b\) gives the claimed
bound as well. Thus the estimate introduces **no**
\(\log(b/a)\) loss at large frequency.
Since the degree-six transform decays as
\((1+T)^{-5}\) (also after bounded own-log dilation),
the single six-tag frequency integral is
\[
 \int_0^\infty (1+T)^{c-5}
  \bigl(bT\,1_{bT\le1}+(1+\log(bT))1_{bT>1}\bigr)^6\,dT
                              \ll b^{4-c}=b^{5/2}.
\]
Use the \(1/6!\) unordered-set bound and true positive
weights to sum the actual labels. With both remainders,
the two independent frequency integrals give \(O(b^5M)\).
For a single remainder the other arm uses just its
fixed integrable sextic envelope. Replacing the true
small-factor gate \(I_b\) by the \(B\)-hit loses at most
\(O(a^\gamma M)\) on the actual paired scale, by the
genuine small-prime bound, for some fixed \(\gamma>0\).
Choose \(C>3/\gamma+3\) so this is \(O(b^3 M)\).
For a product of two hit indicators, replace actual
indicators first by the two true bank hits, and then
expand. Since each true hit indicator is at most one,
the propagated remainder is bounded by
\(|E_6|+|E'_6|+|E_6 E'_6|\). Thus no B-harmonic factor
depending badly on \(a\) enters either error.

Applying (2) and (3) now gives
\[
   \langle I_b H_C,Z\rangle=d(a,b)M_Z+
                                 O(b^{5/2}M)+o_b(M).
\]
The same comparison has the actual reflected orientation.
Since \(-M_Z>.90M+o(M)\) and \(\ell\ge75.696\),
both actual signed crosses exceed \(68bM\).
Their source has actual least factor \(>N^b\), so
the reflected crosses are disjoint.

## 4. The doubly-tiny actual remainder

On both sides use the same first-five genuine \(B\)-mark
expansions and the degree-two \(B_0\) correction.
Each selected group \(hg\) is nonempty and has at most
seven actual named primes. Its total angle is at most
.0255. Order the two total angles with fixed slack and
apply the [ordered two-mark factorization](OrderedMarkCovariance.md)
to these two true groups. The proof there uses only a fixed
number of named polynomial-size prime slots, arbitrary-unit
Siegel--Walfisz of the chosen short group, and the two
C.1 rails. It therefore applies unchanged at up to seven slots:
the second rail is at most \(.875+3.5(.0255)<1\).
The physical product-angle diagonal is \(O(\eta M)+o_\eta(M)\)
by the actual two-quotient envelope and the genuine
reciprocal harmonics of at most fourteen angles, all
above the fixed artificial floor \(a\); take \(\eta\downarrow0\)
at fixed \(a,b\). Model diagonals have the same vanishing
positive harmonic measure. The two signed reduced
derivatives are exactly the finite sums defining \(d(a,b)\).
There is no \(Q_{\rm rough}\) term on either marked arm.

The same true one- and two-remainder prices in §3 give
\[
  \langle I_b H_C,I_b H_C\rangle
      =d(a,b)^2M+O(b^{5/2}M)+o_b(M).
\]
Since \(\ell^2\ge 5729.884416\), this exceeds \(5700b^2M\)
for every sufficiently small fixed \(b\).
This region has both actual least factors \(\le N^b\)
and is disjoint from both one-sided comparisons just proved.
