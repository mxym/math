# One replacement for all nonzero-tag self-pairs

Use the same fixed even \(N\), \(\log N=L\), central interval \(J\),
true target-unit pairing \(\langle C,D\rangle=\sum_{x\in NJ,(x,N)=1}
C(x)D(N-x)\), and \(M=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\) as in the
[full medium comparison](FullMediumCohort.md). Keep its exact score
\(S=G_b+W\), \(b=.003\), and the same genuine tag interval
\((N^b,N^{a}]\), \(a=.080\). The score is zero on non-squarefree and
non-\(N^b\)-rough arguments. Let \(I_0=1_{P^->N^a}\),
\(U=I_0 S\), \(Y=(1-I_0)S\), and \(Y_j\) be \(Y\) on actual
tag count \(j\) (with \(Y_{\ge4}\) for all counts at least four).
Set \(V=H+\Pi\), \(A=I_0V\), \(\Pi(n)=1_{n\ {\rm prime}}\log n\),
and \(T=1_{P^-\le N^b}H\). In particular \(A\) is the literal
\(V_0\) of the [exact dimension-two comparison](ExactDHRZeroTag.md).

**Proposition.** On the full original nonzero-by-nonzero chamber,
\[
                 \langle Y,Y\rangle>(3.785+o(1))M.
\]
The two remaining unestimated-by-chamber crosses in the earlier
partition in fact have a uniform adverse upper:
\[
  \langle U,Y_1+Y_2\rangle+\langle Y_1+Y_2,U\rangle
                   \le (-5.427+o(1))M .                         \tag{1}
\]
Both orientations of the entire original \(01\) and \(02\)
chambers are included. The upper results from retaining the
same explicit \(R_J\) term on both sides of an exact comparison.
It is not a binary lower or an attainable target for a new
unconditional lower in these same chambers.

## 1. A genuine signed receiver for the entire zero-tag raw score

For an actual compact nonnegative finite-rank product source \(Z\)
with a fixed positive floor, let \(m(Z)\) denote its ordinary unopposed
mass in units \(|J|N\) (use the equal-length reflected interval where
appropriate). Then in either orientation,
\[
   3.2598\,m(Z)+o(1)\le \frac{\langle Z,A\rangle}M
                       \le3.7257\,m(Z)+o(1).                    \tag{2}
\]
The coefficients do not depend on the compact source or its bound
\(Z\le K L\). This applies by true finite-rank and floor limits to
the source terms used below.

Indeed fix first a collar width \(\delta>0\) and a level exponent
\(\theta<.5\) with \(\theta>.5-\delta/2\). On \(s=\log d/L\le.5-\delta\),
open the real Möbius label \(d\mid y\). It is \(N^a\)-rough, so
the actual quotient is sifted to \(N^a\). Use the linear sifting
functions of [the exact linear comparison](ExactLinearZeroTag.md)
at true modulus \(d\ell\le N^\theta\), with actual product
Bombieri--Vinogradov on \(Z\), as in the [compact-source
transfer](../halfzero_detector/OneArmRoughDebit.md#3-genuine-signed-transfer-and-a-genuine-upper-of-the-overlap).
There is no need for a prime on the source arm. The residue is
literally \(N\). Group the tested actual moduli with their usual
fixed divisor weights. At a compatible tested prime the genuine
reduced class has density \(1/(p-1)\), except that primes of \(N\)
or of the source integer are omitted. Indeed, in the reduced
principal, all such shared source primes cost \(o(1)\): if the
source floor is \(\eta>0\), for a fixed source integer their summed
reciprocal loss is at most
\(L^C\sum_{p\mid x,\ p>N^\eta}1/p=o(1)\) in the divisor and sieve
label harmonics, with fixed \(C\). The analogous additional
polynomial \(d\)-prime and target omissions are power-small;
small primes of \(N\) are kept in \(\mathfrak S(N)\).
The prime factor \(\varphi(d)^{-1}\) can be replaced by \(d^{-1}\)
because every factor of \(d>1\) exceeds \(N^a\).
The original square and own-log restoration has \(o(M)\) cost
at the fixed source floor and rank: a true square on the
\(N^a\)-rough arm costs \(O(N^{1-a}+N^{1/2})\) before
the harmless logarithmic and bounded-score factors.

For \(d\) in the terminal collar keep its actual affine progression
\(x+d r=N\) and the two genuine roughness tests: \(r\) is \(N^a\)-rough,
and \(x\) has the source's positive floor. On this fibre the
positive fixed-power Selberg square used in the
[physical affine proof](FullZeroTagAffineSelf.md#2-the-genuine-dimension-two-lower-and-upper-on-the-same-fibre)
at any fixed exponent below \((1-s)/2\) gives count
\(O_{\eta,J}(M/(dL^2))\). For this upper one may sift both forms
at the smaller floor \(\min(a,\eta)\), or at a smaller fixed
positive floor if desired; the exact local form roots have one
coincident root at primes of \(N\) and two otherwise. No tested
prime then divides \(d\). The factor \(Z/L\) is bounded at the
fixed compact floor and rank, and \(F(s)\le44(.5-s)^4\).
The total positive terminal error is therefore \(O_{Z}(\delta^4)M\);
the label reciprocal mass is bounded by the compact \(N^a\)-rough
harmonics. Take \(N\to\infty\) first, then \(\theta\uparrow.5\)
and \(\delta\downarrow0\), **before** removing compact source
floors. The previously proved genuine two-arm physical small-prime
and rank envelopes then extend the result to \(T\), the stopped
remainder, and the taper defect appearing below; no uniform
\(Z\)-dependent collar constant is passed to those limits.

For explicit coefficients let \(B_\pm\) be the ordinary signed
and unsigned reciprocal-label densities of
[the affine comparison, §2](FullZeroTagAffineSelf.md#2-the-genuine-dimension-two-lower-and-upper-on-the-same-fibre),
where the subscript \(+\) means unsigned and the subscript \(-\)
means signed. There is a separate even unit atom. Write
\(E=(B_++B_-)/2\), \(O=(B_+-B_-)/2\).
With \(D=.5-s\), the limiting lower is
\(k_1(.5)+\int_a^{.5} F(s)(k_1(D)E-U_1(D)O)\,ds\);
the upper is \(U_1(.5)+\int_a^{.5} F(s)(U_1(D)E-k_1(D)O)\,ds\).
These linear bounds apply on the actual even and odd label counts
before subtraction. \(k_1\) increases and \(U_1\) decreases as
level increases, so the lower level endpoint works in both the
lower and upper signed enclosures. The model integrals at \(D=0\)
converge since \(F(s)U_1(D)\le 88D^3\) there.
Likewise the ordinary mass of the actual signed raw arm is
\[
            m(A)=B_+(1)+\int_a^{.5}F(s)B_-(s)B_+(1-s)\,ds .
\]
This last formula is one-arm only, from the ordinary compact
prime-product harmonics. The attached outward checker gives
\(m(A)<3.503\), lower coefficient \(>3.25984865\), and
upper coefficient \(<3.72566993\). Use only (2) and \(m(A)<3.503\).

## 2. Full nonzero-tag square, including W and the physical endpoint

On central arguments the exact identities are
\(U=A-\Pi+Q\), \(Y=V-A+W-T\). No genuine prime is present on

and floor limits, \(\langle V,Z\rangle/M=m(Z)+o(1)\) in either
orientation for \(Z=A,W,T\); also \(\langle V,V\rangle/M=1+o(1)\)
by the original half-zero calibration. Thus
\[
 \frac{\langle Y,Y\rangle}{M}=
 \frac{\langle A,A\rangle}{M}+1-2m(A)
 +\frac{\langle V-A,W-T\rangle+\langle W-T,V-A\rangle
              +\langle W-T,W-T\rangle}{M}+o(1).                 \tag{3}
\]
Retain the actual raw lower \(11.745M+o(M)\) from the exact DHR
calculation, on the very same \(A\). Its original cap and
restoration have already been charged.

 -.226339<c<-.226337,\quad |m(T)|>.2263381,\quad
