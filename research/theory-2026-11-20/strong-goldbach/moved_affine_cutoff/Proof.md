# A stronger central semiprime reserve and a wider paid affine strip

Let \(N=2X\) be an individual large even target,
\(J\subset[1/2,2/3]\) a fixed nondegenerate interval,
\(x\in NJ\), \(y=N-x\), \(L=\log N\), and
\(M_N=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\). Write
\(\Pi(v)=1_{v\ {\rm prime}}\log v\) and
\(R_J(N)=\sum_{x\in NJ}\Pi(x)\Pi(y)\).
All analytic pairings below retain \((x,N)=1\).
The difference in the true binary prime atom with this
unit mask is \(O(L^2)\).

**Theorem 1.** Let \(D_2(a)\) be the true squarefree
semiprime source \(x\in NJ\) whose two actual prime
factors exceed \(X^a\), weighted by \((\log x)\Pi(y)\).
For all large even \(N\),
\[
                         R_J(N)+D_2(.204)>.755 M_N .
                         \tag{1}
\]
This is a less rough but quantitatively larger anchor
than the same previous central count at \(.252\).
It imposes no claimed positive lower on \(R_J\).

**Theorem 2.** Fix \(0<\lambda\le.001\),
\(\rho=.542\), and \(\tau=.040\), and define
\[
 k=\frac1{1-\lambda},\qquad F(t)=(1+kt)C(t),
\]
with \(C\) a fixed smooth cutoff equal to one through
\(.4584\), supported in \([0,.4588]\), and \(0\le C\le1\).
The original \(P_F(v)\) uses the true prime marks
\(N^\lambda<r<v\) and the true divisor score
\(\sum_{d\mid v/r}\mu(d)F(\log(dN/v)/L)\).
Split it as \(P_F=L_F-E\), with \(L_F\) the literal
score on \(r\le N^\rho\) and \(E\) the nonnegative
high affine term. Let \(W_0\) be the actual subscore
with \(N^\lambda<r\le N^\tau\).

* In the original \(L_F(x)L_F(y)\), the signed
  sub-block with at least one mark in \(W_0\) is
  **unconditionally** equal to
  \[
    k^2\{2(\tau-\lambda)(\rho-\lambda)
                   -(\tau-\lambda)^2\}M_N+o(M_N)>.0406M_N .
    \tag{2}
  \]
* Separately, at a target with \(R_J(N)\le .01M_N\),
  the true high semiprime source gives
  \[
                \langle E,\Pi\rangle>.0244M_N,\qquad
                \langle E,P_F-W_0\rangle<-.0059M_N,               \tag{3}
  \]
  where \(\langle A,B\rangle=\sum_{x\in NJ,(x,N)=1}A(x)B(y)\).
  Thus moving the affine cutoff and paying (2) does not
  consume all conditional adverse weight in this oriented
  pairing. The residual in (3) can still contain a
  semiprime on the other arm when a lower mark has been
  removed; no exact rank or total low–low sign is asserted.

The estimates refer to the same physical profiles and
halves of the mark windows; one does not add an overlapping
sub-block or compare cutoffs from different scores.

## 1. Stop the actual forced-prime packing earlier

Use equations (2)–(4) of
[the original central rough lower](../central_rough_chen_2663/Proof.md)
and the audited baseline construction at \(a_0=.252\) in
[the real least-factor trim](../least_factor_trim_2685/Proof.md).
In their notation the positive base at \(\beta=25/224\)
has the same \(b_0\) coefficient, and the actual lower
remaining at cutoff \(a\) has coefficient \(b_0-H(a)\).
The forced-prime ranges are included below the true
cutoff \(\alpha\) simply by truncating their last bin.
Use \(\alpha=a=.204\) now, a fixed positive value in
\((\beta,a_0)\); only the last allowed range of the original debit changes.
All retained labels use the
same originally checked prime \(p_1\) prefix tests
and the actual common residue \(N\); the ordinary-BV
branch that only starts at \(.254\) is not used here.

For clarity, the exact gain \(H(a_0)-H(a)\) is on the
original marked **upper debit**, not a new sieve main in a
different family. The original residual \(R(t)\) over
\([a,a_0]\) equals \(A-Bt\), where
\[
    A=1-1.00001(7/32)/2-.00005,\qquad B=5/2 .
\]
The crossover \((1-1.00001(7/32))/4\) is below \(a\);
the prescribed residual roughness exponent is
\(R(t)/3<t\) on this interval. Here the upper linear
sieve function at \(s=3\) gives
\[
 H(a_0)-H(a)=\int_a^{a_0}\frac{2\,dt}{t(A-Bt)}
       =\frac2A\log\frac{a_0(A-Ba)}{a(A-Ba_0)} .
       \tag{4}
\]
These are exactly the branch, full modulus and
first-square conditions of the earlier argument,
not an extension of it to a new exponent rail.
Fixed packed bin and sieve tolerances are taken
before \(N\to\infty\), inside the strict final
numerical margin.

At \(a=.204\) the true rough positive count has at most
four prime factors with multiplicity, since \(5a>1\).
The actual integers containing a prime square in this
rough range have count \(O(N^{1-a})\) and contribute
\(o(M_N)\) with the log weights. The finitely many
central opponent primes dividing \(N\) have total

[the verified actual 3.446 angular upper](../angular_prime_first_composite/Proof.md).
It applies to every fixed rank \(j\ge2\) in compact

has zero measure. The normalization of the source is
the ordinary genuine prime-product angular integral
in this same central interval, with the true log
weights uniformly relative \(1+O(1/L)\).

Write the ordinary ordered angular densities as \(I_j(a)\).
The increment to the rank-three debit is
\[
 I_3(a)-I_3(a_0)
    =\int_a^{a_0}\frac{\log((1-2t)/t)}{t(1-t)}\,dt .
\]
For rank four the density on the ordered simplex,
in its three independent coordinates, is
\(1/(t_1t_2t_3t_4)\).
This follows, as for rank three, by ordinary PNT on

\[
                      I_4(a)\le
             \frac{(1-4a)^3}{144 a^3(1-3a)} .
\]
The use of \(X^a\) instead of an own-log threshold

The already certified baseline coefficient

The [exact rational certificate](check.py) proves this

This proves (1), with the same central weights and no

## 2. The unconditional wider true stripe

All divisor arguments on a genuine high mark
\(r>N^\rho\) are at most \(\log(N/r)/L<.458\).
The exact affine part of \(F\) therefore gives
\[
       E(x)=k\!\sum_{\substack{x=r m,\ r>N^\rho\\
                                  r\ {\rm prime},\ m>1}}
                                      \frac{(\log r)\Lambda(m)}L .
\]
The high mark is unique. The unconditioned
[high-window harmonic](../nearfull_high_vaughan/JointGiantBothHighReduction.md)
gives \(\sum_{x\in NJ,(x,N)=1} E(x)
=(1-\rho)k|J|N+o(N)\). That mean itself has no narrow
profile or \(\rho\le.518\) hypothesis.

Repeat the proof of the
[evaluated stripe](../nearfull_high_vaughan/WideGroupedMarkCovariance.md)
with the present parameters. The essential modulus ceiling
for \(W_0\) is \(N^{\tau+.4588}=N^{.4988}\), strictly below
the root. Thus it uses ordinary genuine prime BV for
\(\langle W_0,\Pi\rangle\) and actual product BV for the
\(E\) source against these same real divisors; no
over-root or new uniform residue assertion is needed.
As there, the principal has the exact \(Nx\) mask in
the \(E\) source. Its factor \(c(x)-1\) is subsumed by
the summable prime-power harmonic
\(\sum_{m=p^j}(\log p)|c(p)-1|/m\) and the actual
\(r>N^\rho\) prime. The true logarithmic weights and the strict
central endpoints are unchanged.

Write \(\Delta=\tau-\lambda\). The resulting genuine
identities are
\[
 \begin{split}
 \langle W_0,\Pi\rangle&=-k\Delta M_N+o(M_N),\\
 \langle E,W_0\rangle&=-(1-\rho)k^2\Delta M_N+o(M_N),\\
 \langle W_0,P_F\rangle&=k\Delta M_N+o(M_N).
 \end{split}
\]
For the third, the full
[one-mark transfer](../nearfull_high_vaughan/WholeMarkPrimeTransfer.md)
has \(a_F=1-(1-\lambda)k=0\); its wide variant requires
only \(\lambda+.4588<1/2\), not \(\rho+\lambda>1\).
Reflection gives the same coefficients for the reversed
pairings without identifying the individual rows.
Since \(L_F=P_F+E\), the \(W_0\)-by-\(L_F\) coefficient
is \(k^2\Delta(\rho-\lambda)\).
The grouped calculation of the \(W_0\)-by-\(W_0\)
term has coefficient \(k^2\Delta^2\); its product
caps are automatic here. Exact mark-pair
inclusion-exclusion now proves (2). The certified
lower holds for every fixed \(0<\lambda\le.001\).

## 3. A conditional high anchor after this same deletion

Suppose \(R_J(N)\le .01M_N\). In (1) subtract the real
rank-two tail with smaller prime \(m>N^{.457}\) by the
same 3.446 angular upper. Its coefficient is at most
\[
             3.446 \log((1-.457)/.457)+o(1).
\]
This is the genuine least-angle integral
\(\int_{.457}^{1/2}du/[u(1-u)]\) and the same fixed
outward collar for own log versus target log.
The certificate gives a strictly larger than \(.1508M_N\)
raw weighted mass on the retained true semiprimes
\(m\le N^{.457}\).
Their actual larger prime is
\(r=x/m\gg_J N^{.543}>N^\rho\).
On each such squarefree row, writing
\(u=\log m/\log x\), one has
\[
       \frac{E(x)}{\log x}
         = k\,\frac{\log x}{L}u(1-u)
         \ge k\{.204(1-.204)-o(1)\}.
\]
This uses monotonicity of \(u(1-u)\) below \(1/2\)
and the actual lower threshold \(m>X^{.204}\).
Thus \(\langle E,\Pi\rangle>.0244M_N\) eventually.

The same whole one-mark transfer, now with actual
source \(E=-H_F\), gives
\(\langle E,P_F\rangle=-\langle E,\Pi\rangle+o(M_N)\).
After removing the exact \(W_0\) term from this
pairing, the positive correction is at most
\[
        (1-\rho)k^2\Delta M_N+o(M_N)
              \le \frac{.458\cdot .040}{(.999)^2}M_N+o(M_N)
              < .0184M_N
\]
eventually. The strict difference proves (3).
In particular no comparison of a signed model with
a positive actual row is used to obtain this margin.
