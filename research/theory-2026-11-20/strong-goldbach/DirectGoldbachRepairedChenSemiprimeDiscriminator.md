# A fixed-target weighted semiprime discriminator and a repaired double-sieve budget

Set L=log N, S=the ordered Goldbach singular series (2C(N) in Li), X_N=S N/L². The boundary m=N−p<N/L^A, or p<N/L^A, costs O(N/L^A) rows and O(N/L^A) score, taking A>4 fixed. Remove q|N exceptions by dropping p|N, at most O(L). Nonsquarefree complements that survive the rough cutoff have total O(N^(1−a)), since some prime ℓ≥N^a has ℓ² dividing the complement and ∑_{n≥N^a}n^−2≪N^−a. Every score multiplicity on a surviving row is bounded by a constant depending only on a (at most 1/a prime factors).

## Literature gap and scope of the result

[Wu 2008 Lemma 2.2](https://www.numdam.org/article/AA_2008__131_4_367_0.pdf) and [Runbo Li's published paper, Lemma 3.1 and §4](https://imar.ro/journals/Mathematical_Reports/Pdfs/2026/1_2/3.pdf) need the pointwise repair documented in [the primary formal definitions and theorem statement](https://github.com/sthubbar/goldbach-lean/blob/4501f42c25c951fc2e461801ec661fdb3969a022/entries/wu2008/ChallengeWu2008.lean) and the linked completed proof. Repaired R9 excludes both marked primes in its sieve set. Repaired R10 and R11 exclude q1. Repaired R11 goes up to N^(1/2−3a), not N^(1/2−2a)/q3. We do NOT use Li's 1.9728 as a proved lower for the repaired score. Below independently bound all three repaired costs unconditionally, then supply a conditional transfer if the unaffected Li numerical analytic estimates (the terms S1–S12) are accepted in their intended definitions. There is also a weaker completely independent fallback below. No claim is made that all numerical claims in either prior paper have been independently re-audited.

## Corrected pointwise score and actual semiprimes

Let a=100/1149, b=50/309, τ=1/2−3a. In repaired Wu score R=3R1+R2−R3−R4+R5+R6−2R7−R8−R9−R10−R11, use the singleton instance of the repaired theorem. Its value on any actual squarefree m of rank≥3 is ≤0. On a prime m≥N/L^A it is 4. On an actual semiprime m=qr≥N/L^A, q<r, its value is 4 W(log_N q), with W=0 below a, 1/4 on [a,b), 1/2 on [b,τ), 3/4 on [τ,1/3), and 1 thereafter. Indeed r≥√m>N^(1/3); R5/R6 require both smaller than N^τ. R7/8/9 require r<(N/q)^.5, impossible since q r²=m r≥m^(3/2)>N. R10/11 each require four named factors. Thus only the singleton marks and the two base sieves contribute with the stated values. The pointwise theorem's exceptional non-squarefree error is o(X_N) as above. Therefore writing A_W for the actual charge ∑_{q<r,qr=N−p}W(log_N q) with p,q,r genuine primes, and G for ordered genuine prime pairs, G ≥ R/4 − A_W − o(X_N).

## Corrected R9, R10, R11 actual support and unconditional upper bounds

Write each row m as the named mark product times v, retaining multiplicities of actual marks. R9 names q1,q2 with N^b≤q1<N^τ≤q2<(N/q1)^.5 and v free of primes below (N/(q1q2))^.5 after excluding the two named primes. On squarefree rows this simply means P^−v≥(N/(q1q2))^.5, since v has neither mark. Since v≤N/(q1q2), v is prime or 1 except a possible equal-square endpoint, already absent. The v=1 branch has m≤N^((1+τ)/2) and is already removed at m<N/L^A. R10 names a≤t1<t2<t3<t4<b, where ti=log_N qi. R11 names a≤t1<t2<t3<b≤t4<τ. In both latter cases the sieve excludes q1 and is strict below q2, so squarefree rows imply P^−v≥q2. To majorize, allow repetitions in v after dropping its exclusions q1,q2,q3,q4; all primes in the resulting source still exceed (1−o(1))N^a. Variables v in the two quadruple families are of polynomial size with a fixed margin: product of named labels ≤N^(3b+τ)<N^.725, and m≥N/L^A. In R9 the residual prime likewise is polynomial. Target divisors are removed with separate unit masks on the variables.

Actual upper proof: form positive outer rectangularization at ratio 1+L^−B across the three or five coordinates. Keep boxes meeting the physical region; in R10/11 relax P^−v≥q2 to the lower q2 box endpoint. Product/order/angular walls change by O(L^−B) relatively and the exponent domains by o(1). The enlargement may put m just above N, harmless with sifting |N−m| for an upper bound, excluding m=N by the unit restriction. Bounds for total source mass follow from ordinary PNT on prime slots and the uniform Buchstab upper Φ(Y,Z)≤(1+o(1))Y/log Z on these compact fixed-exponent ranges (use ω(u)≤1 for u≥1, and u>1 with fixed slack for v in R10/11). Because the total is nonnegative and only angular regions are being upper bounded, the outer boxes introduce no hidden signed seam. Keeping finitely many upper angular rectangles before taking N→∞ gives the same integrals; outside walls can be sent to zero after the estimate. Log-fine boxes ensure product scale ≥N/L^A and allow generalized BV at D=N^.499.

Explicit primitive distribution input: [Polymath, *New equidistribution estimates of Zhang type*, Theorem 2.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.0811v3), in the classical Motohashi formulation for convolution at its actual length; see also [the existing product-scale application](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/main/research/theory-2026-11-20/strong-goldbach/FullScaleConvolutionBV.md). In each rectangular box one genuine independent prime variable is the Siegel–Walfisz slot; convolve the remaining variables into one divisor-bounded coefficient, with no SW demand on it. All lengths polynomial, product comparable to physical full scale up to fixed log powers; by dyadic subdivision and arbitrary log reserve BV controls the log-many boxes at modulus D=N^.499 uniformly in the admissible target residue N mod d. The rough cofactor sequence is allowed to be non-SW. Its extra coprimality mask with N is separate; the genuine prime SW slot survives omission of the O(log N) target prime divisors and the usual auxiliary coprimality (at most τ(d0) prime values omitted). The reduced total T_d can be replaced by whole source mass T with aggregate error, over sieve coefficients at most 1, bounded by T·O_a(L N^−a): for each source m all distinct prime divisors are ≥(1−o(1))N^a and there are O_a(1), and ∑_{d≤D:ℓ|d}1/φ(d)≪L/(ℓ−1). Thus eliminating reduced-source dependence never silently inserts density for a mark dividing d.

Upper linear sieve for actual p=N−m uses z=D^.5 and F(2)=e^γ. Prime exceptions p<z were already removed globally; the source's bounded multiplicity keeps the deletion negligible. The product for primes ℓ∤N is V_N(z)=(1+o(1))e^−γ S/log z uniformly in N; missing ℓ|N above z contribute o(1). Hence the genuine prime-opposite upper is (2/.499+o(1)) S T/L. As all proof inputs are available at D=N^(1/2−ε) for each fixed ε>0, taking limsup and then ε↓0 gives factor 4. No uniform logarithmic power to the exact square-root boundary is required. This argument upper bounds only these actual costs, not primes opposite arbitrary nonsifted signed sets.

The raw source counts ≤N/L times the following integrals, plus o(N/L). For R9 integrate t2∈[τ,(1−t1)/2] against 1/[t1 t2(1−t1−t2)], as all three slots prime. Inner integral gives

## Conditional-on-remaining-Li-estimates criterion and unconditional paid seam

Li's reported evaluations §§4 (9)–(30) for unaffected terms S1–S12, if admitted as written for their intended sieve definitions, give the score with the final three terms omitted a lower bound 11.103466 C(N)N/L² (add 2.835087+.193502+.183611 to the unrounded reported signed 7.891266). Independently pay the actual seam N^.49≤q<r≤N/q. ## Independent elementary Chen fallback (no Li numerical claims)

Let α=.102=51/500, β=1/3, D=N^.499, z=N^α. On squarefree complements m from the actual prime p, rough at z, set C(m)=1−j/2−1_T/2, j the number of distinct factors in [z,N^β), T the actual exact three-factor population with one factor there and the other two ≥N^β. On nonrough m set C=0. For ranks≥3: j≥2 is automatically nonpositive; j=1 means rank exactly three (three factors ≥N^β plus any positive extra factor would exceed N), and T pays it; j=0 is impossible. On prime C=1 away from negligible endpoints, and for actual semiprime the smaller-label charge is 1/2 for α≤t<β, 1 for β≤t≤.5, zero below α. Again the second factor is >N^β at m≥N/L^A. Thus G≥ ∑_{p} C(N−p)−A_C−o(X_N), exactly on physical prime support.

Lower rough main from ordinary BV and lower linear sieve gives coefficient ≥(2/d)log(d/α−1), where d=.499: f(s)≥2e^γ log(s−1)/s follows by derivative and F(s)≥2e^γ/s. The singleton half-debit is ≤K+U, K=∫_α^β dt/[t(d−t)] and, letting r=(d−t)/α−3, U=∫_α^(d−3α) (r²/4−7r³/72+r⁴/96)dt/[t(d−t)]. Indeed s=(d−t)/α<4 here, F(s)=2e^γ/s up to s=3 and its correction factor is ∫_3^s log(u−2)/(u−1)du. For v=u−3 in [0,1], log(1+v)≤v−v²/4 and 1/(2+v)≤1/2−v/6; multiplying and integrating proves the upper correction. The named prime q and sieve divisor are coprime since q≥z, and each physical modulus has only O(1) possible such q, so summing ordinary BV directly pays the remainder. Marked uniform main has factor 1/(q−1); PNT sum produces dt/t. No stronger prime distribution theorem is used for this piece.
