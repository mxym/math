# Both true high labels above the square root in the near-full score

Addendum to [the high-cut calibration](Proof.md). Use its literal even \(N\), \(C_0>1\), \(L=\log N\), \(\ell=\log L\), \(S=\exp(\ell^4)\), \(B=N/S\), \(I=[N/S^{C_0},B]\), \(J\subset J_0\Subset(0,1)\), \(U=\exp(\ell^3)\), \(z=\exp(\ell^2)\), and its original lower \(W=\sqrt N/S^{C_0+10}\). Keep \(x+y=N\), \(T_y=y/B\), and the strict cutoff \(Q_y(m)=\sum_{d\mid m,d<T_y}\mu(d)\). This addendum, unlike the original all-even calibration in that proof, assumes \(\operatorname{rad}(N)\le L^{B_0}\) for fixed \(B_0\).

There is a fixed \(\delta>0\) such that one may replace **both** high-label cutoffs in its calibrated near-full identity by
\[
                             W'=\sqrt N\exp(L^\delta).
\]
In particular, on the same actual near-full cohort, the expression for \(C_I\) up to \(o(N)\) is
\[
 \sum_{\substack{x=A f\in NJ,\ y=m C h=N-x,\ C h\in I\\
              (xy,N)=1,\ P^-(AC)>z,\ f,h>W',\ f,h\ {\rm prime}}}
       (\log f)(\log h)H_U(A)H_U(C)Q_y(m),\qquad
       H_U(A)=1_{A>1}\sum_{d\mid A,\ d\le U}\mu(d).                 \tag{1}
\]
The second \(H_U\) has exactly the same convention at \(C=1\). Both true high labels exceed a subpower factor times the root individually, stronger as a localization than a restriction on their product. No bound on (1), no conclusion for arbitrary radicals, and no transfer of this statement to the low-cutoff raw ledger is asserted.

## 1. The two single-prime drift arms on the same physical score

Use the true rough Vaughan decomposition as in the high-cut proof, with \(\mu\)-cutoff \(U\) and \(\Lambda\)-cutoff \(W\) or \(W'\). The small \(\Lambda\) term is identically zero on either original prime leg, of length \(N^{1-o(1)}\). Changing \(W\) to \(W'\) in either Type I leg changes only its term with a short \(\Lambda\) factor. After retaining actual prime logs on the opposite leg, its signed drift, up to its overall sign, has the common form
\[
 \sum_{\substack{u s+v q n=N,\ \{u,v\}=\{1,m\}\ {\rm in\ a\ fixed\ orientation}\\
    1\le m\le S^{C_0},\ s,q\ {\rm prime},\ W<q\le W'\\
    P^-(n)>z,\ \text{actual central, giant and unit masks}}}
     (\log s)(\log q) Q_y(m) \sum_{d\mid n,\ d\le U}\mu(d).        \tag{2}
\]
Here there are two genuinely distinct forms: \(x=s,y=m q n\) with \(u=1,v=m\) for the right drift; \(x=q n,y=m s\) with \(u=m,v=1\) for the left drift. At \(m=1\) the displayed orientations are just their respective original roles; that row is outside the original \(m\ge T_y\) anyway. The \(y\) determining \(Q_y(m)\) is the original \(N-x\) in either form. For (2) one has \(n=N^{1/2+o(1)}\) uniformly, since \(x,y\) are central and \(m=N^{o(1)}\); hence the \(1_{n>1}\) is unnecessary here. The prime \(q\) and left actual source \(s\) in this drift are not replaced by unrestricted integers unless an explicitly stated expansion or absolute upper is in use. Any genuine prime powers in the precursor drift terms cost \(N^{3/4+o(1)}\) for \(q\) and \(N^{1/2+o(1)}\) for the long \(s\): count actual multiples in whichever physical leg contains the power, using the fixed divisor envelope. The literal \(m\) range is \(T_y\le m\le y S^{C_0}/N\). Fix it and \(d<T_y\) within the \(y\)-mesh of the high-cut proof, using the actual multiples \(m\mid y\) and the original short score divisors dividing \(m\) and thus \(y\) to pay the moved cutoffs. All fixed log-power losses are taken uniformly while \(0<\delta<.01\).

Use the fixed-color first-active bank cover \(C(n)\) from the [prime-column estimate](../polynomial_prime_columns/UntwistedNearRoot.md) with the same bank size range, each family demanding a hit in every color. Actual primes in these banks exceed all of \(m,U\), are \(>z\), and are less than \(q\). Each bank prime is a target unit. Choose a fixed comparison precision, then its fixed \(K\) colors, then \(s_0\asymp_K\log L\) disjoint families. Let \(\kappa>0\) denote the ensuing model failure exponent below; take \(\delta<\min(.01,\kappa/4)\) **after** these choices.

## 2. The true missed-bank upper in each drift

Open the absolute divisor weight on \(n\) by \(d\le U\), and bound \(|Q_y(m)|\le\tau(m)\) before estimating. At fixed \(m,q,d\), write \(n=d n'\) and \(b=v q d\). Compatibility requires \((u,b)=1\), since \(m,q,d\) are target units. On the actual equation
\[
      n'\equiv N\bar b\bmod u,\qquad u s+b n'=N,
\]
parametrize \(n'=n'_0+u j\) in its real interval. The length in \(j\) is at most a constant times \(X=N/(m q d)=N^{1/2+o(1)}\), and \(s\) is a primitive affine form in \(j\) of step \(b\), with constant term prime to \(b\). The bank status is unchanged when dividing \(n\) by \(d\). At every bank prime \(\lambda\) both steps \(u,b\) are units and the roots of \(n'=0\) and \(s=0\) in \(j\) are distinct, since otherwise \(N=0\bmod\lambda\).

Apply a universal one-form upper \(\beta\)-sieve of depth \(N^{.001}\) on \(s\), omitting the bank primes and using all other sieve primes up to \(N^{.000001}\). Here all bank primes are eventually below this cutoff. Use its positive majorant only on rows also satisfying exact bank-prime avoidance of \(s\) and the actual failed-cover mask on \(n'\). Open this positive weight by the outside-bank sieve, and express failure of the banks as a log-power number of Boolean avoidance patterns; add avoidance of \(s\) at **every** bank prime. The latter joint root conditions use one or two roots at each bank prime, and can be opened by successive Bonferroni degrees \(\lfloor L^{.1}\rfloor\). At each fixed outside-bank label, count ordinary CRT in \(j\), with exact product since the alphabets are disjoint. The floor sum per \(m,q,d\) is \(N^{.001+o(1)}\). The high-degree model tails are factorially small by \(\ll\sum_{\lambda\in{\rm bank}}2/\lambda\ll\log L\), even after the signed Boolean expansions. After summing \(q\le\sqrt N\exp(L^\delta)\) and the subpower \(m,d\), the floor part is power-small and the factorial tails give arbitrary fixed log accuracy. This procedure does not assume a prime lower bound or use a sieve estimate in a short unknown progression.

The prime-bank local factor is \(1-1/\lambda\). Conditional on it, the chance of avoiding the second root is \(1-1/(\lambda-1)\), with no collision as observed. Thus the fully reassembled bank model is the prime-bank product times failure probability \(O(L^{-\kappa})\) under independent divisor parameters \(1/(\lambda-1)\); each color has fixed positive reciprocal mass. The outside-bank sieve main is bounded by its dimension-one Euler product with uniform constant, with root 0 for each prime dividing \(b\) and 1 elsewhere. Multiplying the restored prime-bank product gives \(O((b/\varphi(b))/L)\). Both reassembled factors are nonnegative. Multiplication by \(\log s\le L\) and then \(\log q\) leaves the bound
\[
 \ll N L^{-\kappa}(\log L)^{O(1)}
       \sum_{m\le S^{C_0}}\frac{\tau(m)}m
       \sum_{d\le U}\frac1d
       \sum_{\substack{W<q\le W'\\q\ {\rm prime}}}\frac{\log q}{q}
   +o(N).
\]
Here \(b/\varphi(b)\ll\log L\) is uniform by the standard local Euler estimate; Chebyshev and partial summation give the last sum \(O(1+\log(W'/W))=O(L^\delta)\). This is \(o(N)\) in both actual orientations.

## 4. Re-evaluate the overlap of the two short parts at the new cut

\[
                      a_1 f_1 e_1,\qquad m c_1 h_1 e_2.

## 5. Optional larger short cutoff and both upper endpoints
