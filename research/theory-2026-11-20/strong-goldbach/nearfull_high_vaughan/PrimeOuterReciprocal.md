## Proof

The score is
\[
 \sum_{\substack{x=A f\in NJ,\ y=m C h=N-x,\ C h\in I\\
                 (xy,N)=1,\ P^-(AC)>z,\ f,h\ {\rm prime}}}
   (\log f)(\log h)H_U(A)H_U(C)Q_y(m)
\]
restricted only by actual intervals on \(f,h\), including the high-label lower \(W\). Limits in all stated boxes force \(A,C=N^{\Omega(1)}\): for (1) each is at least \(N^{.486-o(1)}\), and any supercritical rail (2) with the starting \(f,h>\sqrt N\,N^{-o(1)}\) bounds their maximum exponent strictly below 1. Consequently \(A,C>1\) is automatic. Do not drop or modify the \(H_U(1)\) convention in the full score; it is simply irrelevant on these boxes.

Freeze the actual \(m\)-window and the genuine strict short cutoff in \(Q_y(m)\) on the same real \(y\)-mesh of the [high-cut proof](Proof.md). The changed rows obey \(m\mid y\) or \(d\mid y\) for a true short-range cutoff divisor, and their absolute global count relative to the contour collars is exactly as there. All coefficient multiplicities are bounded by fixed divisor powers on \(x,y\). Thus fixed divisor moments and the central smoothing leave arbitrary-log error, including the case of smaller real \(J\).

Open the two signed short labels \(d,e\le U\) from \(H_U(A),H_U(C)\), and keep both \(z\)-rough (otherwise no possible original row). Next open the true roughness on \(A/d,C/e\) by the original two successive Bonferroni truncations with squarefree labels \(e_1,e_2\) on primes at most \(z\), excluding those of \(N\). The first omitted tails are estimated before using progressions by actual \(e_i\mid x\) or \(e_i\mid y\), and the physical divisor moments give the same arbitrary-log accuracy. The sizes and counts of all \(m,d,e,e_1,e_2\) choices are \(N^{o(1)}\) (for every fixed precision), and all extra log weights only fixed powers. Write the exact enlarged product coefficients
\[
                 F=f d e_1,\qquad H=m h e e_2 .
\]
The two remaining coordinates are free integers at the equality \(F a+H c=N\). Here \(f,h\) remain **true primes** and are the independently dyadized outer variables; their weights are literal prime logarithms, not prime main terms. At fixed other labels their product relabeling to \(F,H\) is injective separately and costs \(\ell^2\) norms \(F_{\rm scale}^{1/2}L^{O(1)}\), \(H_{\rm scale}^{1/2}L^{O(1)}\). There is no inverse reduction that needs equidistribution of \(f\) or \(h\). Require both products to be target units and keep \((F,H)=1\), as forced by the original equation and units.

The exact \(x\)-unit condition is treated as in the reciprocal proof: expand by \(u\mid N\) on the free coordinate \(a\), which forces \(u\mid c\). Truncate \(u\le \exp(L^{3/4})\) by the same uniform all-target Rankin divisor argument and the actual divisor-moment envelope. The central smooth function gives the nonzero Poisson phases
\[
       \frac{N}{u F H}\widehat{\Psi}\left(\frac{jN}{u F H}\right)
                       e_H\big(j(N/u)\overline F\big).
\]
Apply [Bettin--Chandee, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.00769) with the actual phase multiplier \(N/u\) and outer modulus \(H\). Product scales \(F,H\) differ from those of the genuine \(f,h\) only by \(N^{o(1)}\), with the same harmless subpower \(u\). Smooth dyadic separation of each nonzero frequency factor and its tails is identical to the reciprocal proof, giving (2); all fixed outer counts are \(N^{o(1)}\), so a fixed power saving remains. On all of (1), the case above product exponent 1 has \(g\le1.028+o(1)\), \(b\le.514+o(1)\), and the first rail is at most \(-.0019+o(1)\). Below 1 one has \(g\ge1-o(1)\) from the literal \(W\), so the second line of (2) also has uniform strict room. This proves a power-small nonzero contribution for (1).

For the principal, **recombine all temporary enlarged-product dyads before summing \(d\)**. These auxiliary dyads were used only to estimate the nonzero sums. The real \(f,h\) box or interval restrictions are independent of \(d\), and the physical \(x,y,m\) gates in the modeled integral are likewise independent of \(d\) at fixed central point. The zero density of the exact unit sample is
\[
                \frac{\varphi(N)}{N}\frac{1}{m f h\, d e e_1 e_2}.
\]
Retain every compatibility condition: at fixed \(m,f,h,e,e_1,e_2\) the only \(d\)-dependent conditions are that it is \(z\)-rough and \((d,N m h e e_2)=1\). The \(d\)-prefix is the full actual \(d\le U\), so its uniform rough signed harmonic \(\sum\mu(d)/d\) is arbitrary-log small by [the all-even high-cut proof, §1](Proof.md). For \(U=\exp(L^\nu)\) this bound is stronger at the longer prefix; \(U\) is still subpower and polynomial mask sizes are allowed. Restore the true \(Q_y(m)\) at its original endpoint (or retain it on the paid mesh). Sum the remaining reciprocal \(e,e_i,m\) divisors and the genuine weighted \(f,h\) prime harmonics at log-power cost, not by the subpower cardinality of the labels. The zero term is therefore arbitrary-log small as claimed.
