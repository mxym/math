# Single-tag compensation of the full rank-three arm and a higher-rank debit

Let \(N\) be an individual even target, \(L=\log N\), \(x+y=N\),
\(x\in NJ\) for a fixed nondegenerate \(J\Subset(0,1)\), and
\(M=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\). Every actual pairing
\(\langle A,B\rangle=\sum_{x\in NJ}A(x)B(N-x)\) below
keeps the actual unit condition \((x,N)=1\).
Use the original wide affine profile and physical marks of
[WideLowNoHighRemainder](WideLowNoHighRemainder.md):
\[
 \lambda=.006,\quad k=(1-\lambda)^{-1},\quad
 F(t)=(1+kt)C(t),\quad v=.498,\quad w=.499,\quad \rho=.518.
\]
Here \(C\) is fixed smooth nonincreasing, equal to one through
\(.486\) and zero from \(.490\). For the allowed smooth cap in
\(B\), take a fixed nonincreasing \(\chi\) which is one through
\(.4985\) and zero already from \(.498505\). Thus \(T=\mathcal L-B\)
is the exact no-high remainder of that theorem for one allowed
choice of smooth cap, throughout this argument. No \(F\)-profile
or genuine high-prime gate is changed. Recall \(T=P_F+E-B\),
where \(E\) is the nonnegative true high affine score and
\(P_F\) has only actual prime marks on composite integers.
For the exact short log-minus-mark weight \(A_F\) of that
theorem, \(P_F=A_F-F(\log(N/n)/L)\Pi\) except on the actual
proper-prime-power divisibility support described there.
Also \(T(n)=0\) on every central integer having a prime factor
\(>N^\rho\).

Keep the first-arm actual prime \(q\) of
[WideNoHighRankTaggedCross](WideNoHighRankTaggedCross.md),
with \(\alpha=\log q/L\in I=[.5022,.5086]\) and \(x=q m\).
Require \(m\) squarefree. Let \(V_j(x)=T(x)\) on this chart
with \(\omega(x)=j\), \(j=3,4\), and \(P^-(m)>N^{.014}\),
and zero otherwise. Let \(V_{\rm high}(x)=T(x)\) on the same
actual \(q\)-chart with \(\omega(x)\ge5\) and \(P^-(m)>N^{.045}\),
and zero otherwise. These three first-arm classes are disjoint.

On the second arm use the single literal bank
\[
 {\cal P}=\{p\ {\rm prime}:\ .00051\le\log p/L\le .00060\},
 \qquad W(y)=\#\{p\in{\cal P}:p\mid y\},
 \qquad V=\log(20/17).
\]
Put \(T^{[\ge4]}(y)=1_{\{y\ {\rm squarefree},\,\omega(y)\ge4\}}T(y)\).

**Theorem.** At every sufficiently large even \(N\), for
this same exact \(T\),
\[
 \begin{split}
  \langle V_3+V_4,W T^{[\ge4]}\rangle
           &>.0030\,M,\\
  \langle V_3+V_4+V_{\rm high},W T^{[\ge4]}\rangle
           &>.00045\,M.                                        \tag{1}
 \end{split}
\]
The first line is contained in the second sector, not a separate
credit to be added. The proof bounds the full negative first-arm
rank-three debit, the full signed first-arm rank-four receiver,
and in the second line all actual higher ranks within their stated
rough floor. It leaves the entire right squarefree rank-at-least-four
score, positive or negative, rather than selecting its favourable
rows. If the bank is narrowed about a fixed interior point before
\(N\to\infty\), both strict signs persist with positive constants on
replacing \(W\) by the literal indicator of hitting the bank.
No estimate is claimed for the remaining part of the original
\(T\times T\) or for a prime-by-prime count.

## 1. A uniform real source and the tagged \(A_F-B\) moment

On the stated first-arm \(q\)-chart, with \(m\) squarefree
composite and \(P^-(m)>N^{.014}\), the exact formula is
\[
             T(x)=-L\alpha(1+k(1-\alpha))\mu(m).
\]
Indeed a proper divisor of \(m\) has profile argument at
most \(.4838\), the entire divisor has argument at least
\(.4914\), and all other physical marks are either zero
or in the exact \(B\)-cap; see
[the full rank chart, §1](WideNoHighRankTaggedCross.md).
It is enough to work with nonnegative sources
\[
  Z(qm)=L\alpha(1+k(1-\alpha))\,g(m),\qquad 0\le g(m)\le1,
\]
where \(g\) is supported on these genuine squarefree composite
cofactors and \(Z=0\) off the true prime interval. Write
\(M_Z=\mathfrak S(N)\sum_{x\in NJ,(x,N)=1}Z(x)\).
The source has at most one possible \(q\), and its log-weighted
prime slot and bounded shorter coefficient are separated.
Each \(Z\) used below selects an actual rank or a union of actual
ranks on which \(T\) has the same sign, possibly with the
stronger least-factor condition.

We will use the centred product discrepancy of this source at
actual reduced residue \(N\bmod Q_0\) for **all**
\(Q_0\le N^{.5020}\). For \(Q_0\le N^{.4996}\) use
[WholeMarkPrimeTransfer, §1](WholeMarkPrimeTransfer.md).
On the remaining dyads use the primary Fouvry–Radziwiłł
comparison with literal residue \(N\), as in the
[full rank chart, §2](WideNoHighRankTaggedCross.md).
The true long \(q\)-slot has its ordinary prime
Siegel–Walfisz property with the actual auxiliary-unit masks,
and the shorter bounded coefficient needs no such condition.
The fixed-aspect residue extension is exactly the one proved
there. Its rails are strict:
\[
          (56\cdot.5086-17)/23=.4992<.4996,
          \qquad .5020<.5022 .
\]
As there, freeze common spatial arguments and genuine product
endpoints with arbitrarily log-fine boxes. The actual modulus
divisor powers are met by interpolation between the arbitrary-log
absolute discrepancy and the usual \(NL^{O(1)}\) crossed positive
divisor moment bound. Discarded collars have the same physical
divisor envelopes and the reduced model has only reciprocal

piecewise smooth angular weights of the modulus labels, and
with the actual spatial points restored in the reduced model.
Every distribution application below groups these labels first.

is power-small, by the number \(O(N^{1-.00051})\) of these

prime-power marks \(j\le N^\lambda\) have \((j,p)=1\).
In particular the actual moduli in \(A_F\) are \(p d'\)
or \(p d'j\), bounded by \(N^{.49660}\).
For \(B\), opening the subsets of this single tag yields

\[
      F(\kappa+\log d'/L+u)\,
      \chi(\log(r d')/L+u),\qquad u=0,a_p,
\]
with signs \(+,-\). The mark \(r>N^\lambda\) is genuine,
distinct from \(p\), and the actual modulus is \(p r d'\),
bounded by \(N^{.4996}\) (even with the largest \(w\)-cap).
Thus all actual moduli are in the stated distribution.
The repeated-tag-square extensions used to group these scores

## 2. The genuine high \(E\)-payer for the same tag

If \(E(y)>0\) and a genuine bank prime divides \(y\),
then \(y=r p^j\) with \(r>N^\rho\) prime and that very
same bank prime \(p\); no second bank prime can occur.
Terms with \(j\ge2\) were paid by \(p^2\mid y\) above.
Otherwise \(r=(N-x)/p\) prime and its weight is
\(k a_p\log r\le k a_p L\). To upper-bound this true
weighted count, use the dimension-one upper linear
sieve for the quotient \(r\), with level
\(D_p=N^{.5020}/p\) and sifting range \(\sqrt{D_p}\).
The genuine \(r>N^\rho\) exceeds that range. Only after
majorizing prime \(r\) may its high restriction be dropped.
The actual overlapping class for a sieve prime \(p\) is
\(p^2\mid y\); it is retained and its conditional model
density is \(1/p\), not \(1/(p-1)\).
Source primes and target primes are omitted in the
**reduced principal**; the latter can also be omitted
in the literal sieve since \(y\) is a target unit.
All other tested primes have conditional density
\(1/(\ell-1)\). The finite \(\mathfrak S(Nx)\) correction
is \(1+o(1)\) relative to \(\mathfrak S(N)\) on this
bounded macro source. The additional tag Euler factor
in the Mertens product is
\((p-1)^2/(p(p-2))=1+o(1)\).
These local models are uniform and keep all actual
\(p\mid d\) classes in the analytic expansion.

The source dispersion of §1 for the actual
moduli \(p d\), grouped with their divisor multiplicity,
gives the upper sieve main \((2+o(1))M_Z/(p\log D_p)\),
before the weight \(k a_p L\), since
\(F_{\rm lin}(2)=e^\gamma\). This is an estimate on
the summed expanded model, not a claimed uniform
error at each tag. The exact prime harmonic yields
\[
       0\le \langle Z,WE\rangle
          \le (.00242+o(1)) V M_Z,\qquad
       \frac{2k\cdot.00060}{.5020-.00060}<.00242.                \tag{3}
\]
In particular the \(E\)-term is not set to zero just
because the first bank is occupied.

## 3. All actual tagged squarefree rank-three rows are priced

The tagged squarefree ranks one and two contribute zero
to \(T\): rank one cannot have a tiny factor, and at
rank two the other prime would exceed \(N^\rho\).
For a tagged squarefree rank-three row on which \(T(y)\ne0\),
write \(y=p s r\), with \(S=\log s/L<R=\log r/L\).
There is exactly one tag; otherwise the last prime is
too large and \(T(y)=0\). Both \(s,r\) are genuine macro
factors because \(R\le \rho\) on the nonzero support and
\(S+R=1-\kappa-a_p\). In particular \(R>v\) for large \(N\).
Set \(H_0(t)=1+kt-F(t)\) (nondecreasing) and
\(K(t)=1-\chi(t)\) (nondecreasing).
If \(S\le v\), the larger mark gives exactly
\(-L R\,\Delta_{a_p}H_0(S+\kappa)\) and the smaller
gives exactly \(-L S F(\kappa+a_p)K(S+a_p)\).
These follow respectively by taking the full two-factor
difference on the uncapped mark, and noting that every
divisor containing \(r\) is outside \(F\) on the capped mark.
If \(S>v\), both marks are uncapped and the other macro
is outside \(F\) in each. Their total contribution is
\(-L k a_p (S+R)\). Thus \(T(y)\le0\) on every such row.
No unpriced rank-three transition term is being omitted.

For the magnitude upper fix the actual \(p,s\) of
each chart and upper-sieve the genuine remaining prime
\(r=(N-x)/(ps)\) with \(D_{p,s}=N^{.5020}/(ps)\),
\(z_{p,s}=\sqrt{D_{p,s}}\).
Since \(r>N^v\), this is a positive upper on the
true rank-three rows; other prime angle conditions for
\(r\) only enlarge it when dropped. The \(d\)-sum imposes
the literal \(p s d\) modulus, and varying levels are in
its coefficients, not in the fixed separated source.
For every nonzero component below, \(S>.4853\) for large
\(N\), whereas the largest factor of \(m\) has angle
at most \(.4978-.014=.4838\). Thus \(s\) is disjoint
from the true source. All primes tested by the sieve
have angle at most \((.5020-.4853)/2<.014\), and hence
are also disjoint from \(x\). The repeated tag class has
density \(1/p\) as in §2. The other tested prime densities
are \(1/(\ell-1)\) for \(\ell\nmid N\), zero for target
divisors. The actual factor \(s\) is larger than the
sifting cutoff. Uniformly, the upper sieve model is
\((2+o(1))M_Z/(ps\log D_{p,s})\) at the aggregate level
after weighting. The absolute discrepancy from grouping
the true labels \(p,s,d\) is \(o(M)\) by §1.
As before, a positive upper for the weights themselves
pays the \(O_{F,\chi,J}(1/L)\) changes from normalizing
the spatial shift \(\kappa\) or restoring angular endpoints.

There are four elementary upper costs, all relative to
\(V M_Z\). The harmonic of the actual smaller macro prime
uses \(dS/S\) only in the expanded upper sieve main.
The two log weights from \(Z,T\) are as stated, so the
\(T\)-log cancels the \(L\) in \(\log D_{p,s}\).

* Where \(S\le.490\), the giant term is supported at
  \(S>.486-a_p-\kappa>.4853\). There \(R/S<1.063\) and
  \(\log D_{p,s}/L\ge.0114\). Cap \(H_0\) constantly after
  \(.491\) for the integral bound; its variation is below
  \(1.50\). Therefore \(\int\Delta_{a_p}H_0\le a_p\cdot
  1.50\). The upper cost is at most
  \(2(1.063)(.00060)(1.50)/.0114\).
* Where \(.490<S\le v\), the giant difference is exactly
  \(k a_p\), and \(R/S<1.05\). The width is at most \(.008\)
  and the denominator is at least \(.0034\).
  This cost is at most \(2(1.05)k(.00060)(.008)/.0034\).
* The smaller mark has factor \(S\), canceling the
  \(1/S\) in the harmonic, and its support has width
  at most \(a_p-.0005\le.00010\) below \(v\).
  Here \(F(\kappa+a_p)<1.001\) for large \(N\).
  The cost is at most \(2(1.001)(.00010)/.0034\).
* Where \(S>v\), necessarily \(S<.5\).
  The normalized weight divided by \(S\) is at most
  \(k(.00060)/.498\) and the width at most \(.002\),
  with residual denominator at least \(.0014\).
  This last cost is at most \(2k(.00060)(.002)/(.498\cdot.0014)\).

Their sum is \(<.234\). Consequently the entire true
tagged squarefree right rank-three contribution satisfies
\[
     0\le \langle Z,W\,1_{\{y\ {\rm squarefree},\,\omega(y)=3\}}
                       |T|\rangle
                \le (.234+o(1)) V M_Z .                          \tag{4}
\]
All upper sieve arguments use the true fixed-target rows,
not an independence estimate of two factor ranks.

Actual nonsquarefree \(y\) has total \(o(M)\) in the signed
cross by the fixed-\(m\) mixed affine bound and the smooth-cap
envelope of [the true rank pressure, §3](WideNoHighPrimeRankPressure.md).
Explicitly the true \(q\)
is bounded by a polynomial-rough constraint in corrected
Henriot on \(q,N-mq\). A prime \(p\le N^\eta\) on the
second form is extracted at its true progression, resetting
its residual weight at all powers; its harmonic costs
\(O(\eta(1+|t|))\) at frequency \(t\). The fixed cap's double
Schwartz envelope for \(T\), the bounded \(W\) and summable
finite-rank \(m\)-harmonic give \(O(\eta M)\).
Outside that tiny range, repeated squares affect
\(O(N^{1-\eta})\) physical integers with subpower weights.
Take \(N\to\infty\) and then \(\eta\downarrow0\).
This is unchanged if \(g(m)\) restricts to a signed-parity
rank subcohort.

Combining (2)--(4) with the exact vanishing of ranks below
three now gives the uniform, genuinely one-sided estimate
for **all** physical right squarefree ranks at least four:
\[
  .9994\, V M_Z+o(M)
       \ \le\ \langle Z,W T^{[\ge4]}\rangle
       \ \le\ 1.238\, V M_Z+o(M).                                \tag{5}
\]
For the upper use \(\beta\le1\),
\(k(.00009)/V\le k(.00060)<.00061\), and
\(.00061+.00242+.234<.238\).
For the lower the discarded rank-three contribution was
nonpositive and \(E\ge0\), so no expected subtraction is
imputed to either of them.

## 4. The actual source balance, including its extra high ranks

The unconditioned source limit for \(\omega(m)=j\ge2\),
with first-arm least angle \(a\), has angular density
\((1+ks)J_j(s/a)/s\) in \(d\alpha\), where \(s=1-\alpha\)
and \(J_j(u)=u I_j(u)\) in
[the full rank chart, §4](WideNoHighRankTaggedCross.md).
This follows by ordinary PNT in the last genuine prime,
with reciprocal harmonics in the others and the actual
\(q\); thus it keeps the same fixed spatial \(J\) and
target units. It is an unconditioned mass used in (5),
not a substitute for the signed joint estimate.
The signed factor from \(T\) itself is \((-1)^{j+1}\).
In particular
\[
 J_2(u)=\log(u-1),\qquad
 J_3(u)=\int_{1/u}^{1/3}\frac{\log((1-2t)/t)}{t(1-t)}\,dt .
\]
For \(a=.014\) on the whole \(I\),
\(J_2(u)<3.56\) and \(J_3(u)>5.424\),
as checked by the [rational certificate](check_wide_single_tag_compensation.py).
Using (5) on the negative first-arm rank three and positive
first-arm rank four, their combined integrand has coefficient
(in units of \(V(1+ks)d\alpha/s\)) at least
\[
                  \mu_*:=.9994\cdot5.424-1.238\cdot3.56 .
\]
Since \(V>.16\), \(1+ks>1.49,\ 1/s>2,\ |I|=.0064\),
this proves the first inequality of (1).

For the added rougher actual class of first-arm ranks \(\ge5\),
take \(u=s/.045\in[10.92,11.063)\).
The full signed and unsigned numerators after its genuine
rank truncation are, respectively,
\[
 D(u)=\rho_{\rm D}(u-1)-1+J_2(u)-J_3(u),\qquad
 A(u)=u\omega_{\rm B}(u)-1-J_2(u)-J_3(u),
\]
where \(\rho_{\rm D}\) is Dickman and
\(\omega_{\rm B}=\sum_{j\ge1} I_j\) is Buchstab, in the normalization
of the full rank chart. The Dickman identity there follows
from \((uI_j)'=I_{j-1}(u-1)\) and the initial \(I_1=1/u\);
summing without signs proves the displayed positive Buchstab
identity as well. These are finite sums on all
the physically allowed ranks in this chart.
For \(2\le u\le3\), \(u\omega_{\rm B}(u)=1+\log(u-1)\);
it is \(\le .57u\) since
\(.57u-1-\log(u-1)\) has minimum
\(.57-\log(100/57)>0\). The positive delay equation
\((u\omega_{\rm B})'=\omega_{\rm B}(u-1)\) extends
\(\omega_{\rm B}\le .57\) to every \(u\ge2\) by induction.
On the present interval the exact rational integral bounds give
\(J_2(u)>2.294\) and \(1.91<J_3(u)<1.949\).
With \(0\le\rho_{\rm D}\) this gives \(D(u)>-.655\)
and \(A(u)<1.102\).

Apply the lower in (5) to positive \(T(x)\) inside
this added source and the upper to its negative \(T(x)\).
Writing its two positive masses in angular units as
\((A+D)/2\) and \((A-D)/2\), the signed lower from this
**whole** additional first-arm class is
\[
  .9994\,\frac{A+D}{2}-1.238\,\frac{A-D}{2}
            =1.1187D-.1193A
            >-1.1187(.655)-.1193(1.102).
\]
It is disjoint from the original ranks three and four by
actual rank, not by labels from a formal expansion.
The compounded lower coefficient in units of \(M\) is thus
\[
  \bigl[\mu_*-1.1187(.655)-.1193(1.102)\bigr]
       (.0064\cdot1.49\cdot2)(.16) >.00045 .
\]
This establishes the second inequality of (1) with no
higher-rank tail removed on the right.

For the literal unweighted bank, \(0\le W-1_{W>0}\le
\binom W2\), so the correction is bounded by a sum over two
distinct actual second-arm tags. At fixed physical \(m,h\)
for these tags \(h=p_1p_2\), apply the same primitive affine
sieve envelope to \(q=q_0+h t,\ (N-mq_0)/h-mt\).
Their determinant has absolute value \(N\), both are
primitive, and the true interval after positive enlargement
has length \(\asymp_J N/(mh)\) of fixed positive power.
At a tag the first form is a nonzero constant and the
second has one root; extract its weight once at all
valuations. The \(m\)-factors are likewise real leading
primes with bounded local costs and target primes retain
their zeros. This gives \(O(M/(mh))\) after the two genuine
exterior log weights and the fixed Fourier moments, as in
[the full rank chart, §5](WideNoHighRankTaggedCross.md).
The true finite-rank \(\sum 1/m\) is bounded and the
two distinct tag harmonics cost \(O(V^2)\). At any fixed
interior center the proof of (2)--(5), with its new \(V\),
has the same numerical bounds per \(V\). Thus the
unweighted excess \(O(V^2 M)+o(M)\) is smaller than the
strict linear-in-\(V\) margins after narrowing the bank
and only then letting \(N\) grow.
