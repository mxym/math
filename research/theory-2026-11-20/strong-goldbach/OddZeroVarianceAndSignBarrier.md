# An odd-character-only fixed-target criterion and two obstructions to its sign

**Prior odd-character projection.** The use of odd characters to extract a smooth fixed-target Goldbach correlation by subtracting the arithmetic diagonal is already explicit in [Jiamin Li and Jianya Liu, *A Conditional Proof of the Goldbach Conjecture*, Lemma 3.1 (public November 2026 manuscript)](https://mrc.sdu.edu.cn/english/info/1018/1514.htm). Their positive asymptotic assumes an additional spectral hypothesis beyond Dirichlet GRH. Here the narrower assertion concerns the one nonperiodic zero-gap window and its causal filter, together with the two specifically stated limitations. No novelty is claimed for odd-character orthogonality itself. For the parity-weighted ratios approach see [Gou–Wang–Xu, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18825v1#S3).

Assume GRH for the primitive conductors dividing an even modulus $N$, including zeta. The shrinking-window detector and notation are those of [LiveRealGapDetector](LiveRealGapDetector.md), whose genuine arithmetic response is large on infinitely many targets but is not known to be positive on all. Write $L=\log N$, $\Phi=\varphi(N)$, $M(t)=m(1/2+it)$, $c_0=\int f(u)^2du$, $c_1=\int f(u)^2\log u\,du$. Use $Z_N^{\mathrm{all}},Z_N^{\mathrm{odd}}$ for its same-primitive-character two-zero statistic with **no** parity signs: the subscript selects all or just odd characters and the normalization remains $1/\Phi$ in both cases (not $2/\Phi$). Let $Z_N^\pm$ denote the earlier parity-signed version. Thus $Z_N^\pm=Z_N^{\mathrm{all}}-2Z_N^{\mathrm{odd}}$.

## 1. Eliminating the two-family cancellation

For any $0<Y\le N$ the untwisted full character energy is
$$ \frac1{Y\Phi}\sum_{\chi\bmod N}|P_\chi(Y)|^2
 =\frac1Y\sum_{(n,N)=1}\Lambda(n)^2 f(n/Y)^2. $$
Both supported operands are below $N$, so $n\equiv n'\pmod N$ means equality. The right side equals $c_0\log Y+c_1+O_f(Y^{-1/2}L^C)$ uniformly on $N^{0.4}\le Y\le N$: the RH prime number theorem for $\vartheta(x)$ and partial summation supply the prime terms, proper powers cost $O_f(Y^{-1/2}L^C)$, and primes dividing $N$ cost $O_f(Y^{-1}L^C)$ after removing their powers in the previous error. Integrating over $Y=Ne^{-s}$ multiplies the constant by $K(0)$ and $s$ by $iK'(0)$. For the explicit detector (3) of the linked note, $K(0)=O(L^{-6})$ and $K'(0)=O(aL^{-6})$: here $a=L/4$, $t=0$ stays a fixed distance from the nonzero gap-window center, both square roots in $U_v$ are real there, and their derivatives are bounded uniformly. The inverse measure has norm $N^{o(1)}$. Applying the untwisted version of the explicit formula and deleting the complement of $I$ gives
$$ Z_N^{\mathrm{all}}(K;I)=-(N/\Phi)m(1)^2+o(1),\qquad
 \boxed{\mathcal D_N=-2\Re Z_N^{\mathrm{odd}}(K;I)+o(1).}                \tag{1} $$
The real arithmetic comparison, the proper-power test, and the positive calibration of the linked result now apply to an **ordinary average over one parity**, not the difference of two unknown averages. A bound $\Re Z_N^{\mathrm{odd}}\le-\delta$ uniformly for some $\delta>0$ would suffice. No such strictly negative bound has been proved.

The near-full-conductor cutoff of the live detector also survives here. In the earlier proof for the signed family $E_{m'}$ factoring through $m'\mid N$, the unsigned analogue does not vanish at $s>\log(N/m')+h$. Instead the full unsigned family then has only its arithmetic diagonal, exactly $(\varphi(m')/\Phi)(c_0(L-s)+c_1+O_f(Y^{-1/2}L^C))$. From its nonprincipal energy subtract $(\varphi(m')/\Phi)(c_0(L-s)+c_1)$ at **all** scales. The earlier bound $O_f(L^Ce^{-\max(s,\log(N/m'))})$ for the remainder holds, plus $O_f((\varphi(m')/\Phi)N^{-0.2}L^C)$ on the late scales; the explicit negative principal term there remains just as before. On early scales the added model is bounded using $\varphi(m')/\Phi\ll L e^{-\log(N/m')}$. Under the same divisor Möbius inversion the model coefficients sum to the proportion of conductors in the subset (and are in $[0,1]$ for each aggregate), since $\sum_{m'\mid q}\mu(q/m')\varphi(m')$ counts primitive characters of conductor $q$. The integrated model is $o(1)$ from $K(0),K'(0)$. The extra uniform error after integration and absolute divisor multiplicity costs at most $N^{-0.2+o(1)}\sum_{D\mid N}2^{\omega(D)}/D=N^{-0.2+o(1)}$. The earlier signed and present unsigned deletions combine to give (1) on just the odd characters of near-full conductor $q>N\exp(-B(\log\log N)^4/(\log\log\log N)^2)$, with unchanged $o(1)$.

## 2. Termwise sign locking cannot prove this estimate

One can choose a smooth nonnegative live weight for which $M(\gamma)=e^{ic\gamma}b(\gamma)$ with real $b\ge0$: choose $e^{x/2}f(e^x)$ to be a translate by $c$ of the autocorrelation of a nonnegative real smooth bump. For example $c=\log(0.53)$ and an autocorrelation positive on $|x|<0.12$ supported on $|x|<0.16$ give the support and lower interval in the live construction after harmless rescaling. The Fourier transform of the autocorrelation is nonnegative; the function $f$ remains smooth and compactly supported in $(0.40,0.65)$. A natural attempt at (1) is to make every individual off-diagonal term have nonpositive real part throughout $I$ by imposing
$$ H(t)=\Re[e^{i(L+c)t}K(t)]\le0\quad(t\in I). $$
Suppose the delay measure is supported in $[0,S]$, $S\le(1-\eta)L$ with fixed $\eta>0$, and $|K|\le\epsilon$ on the whole real complement as in the live construction. Then **all** of $H$ has $|H|=O_\eta(\epsilon)$ and the odd sum in (1) has absolute real part $O_f(\epsilon L^2)$. In particular termwise locking cannot provide the desired negative constant.

Here is the sharp-order elementary inequality used. If a bounded real Fourier–Stieltjes function has spectral support $\{\sigma\le|\xi|\le\beta\}$, $0<\sigma\le\beta$, then $-\inf H\le C(\beta/\sigma)\max(0,\sup H)$. Indeed average $H$ against translates of the nonnegative mass-one density proportional to $\sigma\,\mathrm{sinc}^2(\sigma t/4)$ (here $\mathrm{sinc}(u)=\sin(u)/u$); its Fourier support lies strictly inside $(-\sigma,\sigma)$, so every such convolution is zero. If the largest magnitude is negative, take a point arbitrarily close to that negative magnitude $M$; by Bernstein's inequality for bounded entire functions of exponential type $\beta$, $\|H'\|_\infty\le\beta M$. On an interval of radius a fixed small multiple of $1/\beta$ one has $H\le -M/2$. The averaging kernel centered there assigns this interval mass $\gg\sigma/\beta$, and the rest is bounded above by $\sup H$, proving the estimate. The order is sharp even for trigonometric polynomials: one minus the normalized Fejér kernel $(1/n)|\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}e^{ijt}|^2$ has no zero-frequency term, spectrum in $1\le|\xi|<n$, supremum 1 and infimum $1-n$. For the phase-locked $H$ above use $\beta=L+c$ and $\sigma=L+c-S$, positive and comparable to $L$ for sufficiently large $N$. The comparator only rules out a **termwise** sign over a whole real interval with a uniform complementary bound, not sign-sensitive cancellation evaluated at actual zeros.

## 3. An actual off-diagonal overdispersion, not a pseudoprime countermodel

For contrast take the **unfiltered** fixed central-scale statistic in the odd family. Write $\mathbb E_-$ for the probability average over odd characters modulo $N$ and $d_\chi$ for the primitive conductor. For fixed real smooth nonnegative $f$ as above define $X_\chi=\sum_j N^{i\gamma_j}M(\gamma_j)$, and let $R_f(N)=T_N(N)$ include the usual unit-restricted prime powers. Then
$$
 \mathbb E_- |X_\chi|^2 = c_0L+c_1-R_f(N)+o_f(1),\qquad \mathbb E_- X_\chi=o_f(1).            \tag{2}
$$
For the second moment use $2\mathbf1_{\mathrm{odd}}=1-\chi(-1)$, the arithmetic diagonal above, and the uniform prime explicit formula, with no pole for an odd character. The first moment on the arithmetic side is zero exactly when $N$ is large, since odd-character orthogonality selects only the residues $1,-1$ and neither occurs in the support. The smoothed explicit formula supplies its vanishing error. The Poisson diagonal (one term per zero, respecting multiplicities) has
$$ \mathbb E_-\sum_j|M(\gamma_j)|^2=c_0\mathbb E_-\log d_\chi+O_f(1).                       \tag{3}$$
Indeed use the standard even-test Weil explicit formula for $|M(t)|^2=m(1/2+it)m(1/2-it)$ on the real line. Its integral divided by $2\pi$ is $c_0$ by Mellin Plancherel; the odd archimedean term is fixed; and its prime sum has only the fixed finitely many prime powers $p^j\le0.65/0.40$, hence none here. There is no zeta pole for odd characters. The same $O_f(1)$ works for any other fixed compact support ratio, by trivial bounds on that finite prime sum.

Take $N_y=2\prod_{\sqrt y<p\le y}p$. In the odd family the local component at each odd $p$ remains uniform since another odd prime supplies equal numbers of both parities. Its omission probability is exactly $1/(p-1)$, and the conductor has no factor 2. Thus
$$
 \log N_y-\mathbb E_-\log d_\chi=\log2+\sum_{\sqrt y<p\le y}\frac{\log p}{p-1}
      \sim\tfrac12\log y\sim\tfrac12\log\log N_y .
$$
By the true classical upper-bound sieve ([Deshouillers–Granville–Narkiewicz–Pomerance, §3, (2)](https://dms.umontreal.ca/~andrew/PDF/GoldbachII.pdf)), $R_f(N_y)\ll_f\prod_{p\mid N_y,p>2}(p-1)/(p-2)=O_f(1)$; the product is bounded since $\sum_{\sqrt y<p\le y}1/p\to\log2$. Proper powers add $O_f(N_y^{-1/2}\log^2N_y)$. Consequently
$$
 \boxed{\operatorname{Var}_-(X_\chi)-\mathbb E_-\sum_j|M(\gamma_j)|^2
        \sim \tfrac12 c_0\log\log N_y>0.}                                                  \tag{4}
$$
This is a positive signed off-diagonal excess on a genuine sequence of integer moduli under the stated GRH, independent of whether their Goldbach counts vanish. It excludes a naive uniform determinantal/sub-Poisson variance estimate with bounded additive error for the *raw odd-character family at this boundary frequency*, not the arithmetically corrected and filtered statistic (1), nor any standard fixed-test limiting unitary law. In particular an asymptotic normalized by the full mean zero density, with only $o(\log N)$ precision, cannot see this obstruction.
