# Actual G23 prime/composite inner product: close slopes and tagged factor chambers

Written research note, 2026-11-24. Use \(P=2^j\), \(L=\log P\), \(y=e^{L^\delta}\), \(z=e^{L^{.24}}\), \(\kappa_z=\prod_{\ell\le z}(1-1/\ell)\), and the true weights/ranges of [the true prime-layer note](G23TruePrimeReflectionLayer.md). Write \(m=P-pk\), \(m'=P-p'k\), where \(p,p'>P^\sigma\) are true original slopes, \(m\) is prime and \(m'\) is the rough composite argument. The actual signed summand is
\[
 kR_z(k)W(p,k)W(p',k)C(P,pk)C(P,p'k)1_{m\ {\rm prime}}R_y(m')1_{m'\ {\rm composite}}\lambda(m').
\]
The weights apart from \(k\) and \(\lambda\) are in \([0,1]\). Both \(m,m'\asymp_J P\) on support. There is no diagonal \(p=p'\). In a dyadic first slope block \(p\asymp U\), physical windows force \(p'\asymp_J U\), \(k\asymp_J K=P/U\), and \(U\ll\sqrt P\). There are \(O_J(L)\) blocks.

**1. Near-diagonal deletion.** For any fixed \(A>0\), the absolute contribution of \(0<|p-p'|\le pL^{-A}\) is
\[
 O_{A,J,\sigma,\delta}(P^2\kappa_z V_y(P)L^{-A-2}),\qquad V_y(P)=\prod_{3\le\ell\le y}(1-1/(\ell-1)).
\]
In particular for \(A=2\) this is little-oh of the genuine prime-layer energy scale \(P^2\kappa_z/L^3\). Here is a genuine fixed-target sieve proof. At fixed slopes sieve \(k\) at divisor level \(P^{1/100}\) and fixed small prime cutoff \(W_0=P^\theta\) with \(\theta<\sigma\). The true exclusions are \(k\neq0\) through \(z\), \(m\neq0\) through \(W_0\), \(m'\neq0\) through \(y\). Below \(y\) there are generically three bad roots, with the two nonzero roots coinciding precisely at odd divisors of \(p-p'\). Between \(y,z\) there are two bad roots and above \(z\) only one. At 2 the roots coincide; a prime 3 exhausting all residues makes the true set empty, and otherwise is just a bounded local factor. Comparing the remaining generic factors to the corresponding powers of \(1-1/\ell\) and using convergent \(O(\ell^{-2})\) errors gives survival product at most \(O(\kappa_z V_y(P)S(|p-p'|)/L)\), where \(S(h)=\prod_{\ell\mid h,\ell\ge5}(1+c_0/\ell)\) with fixed \(c_0\). In the nonempty case the product is at least a fixed inverse log power. The same elementary Selberg square as in G23TruePrimeReflectionLayer.md compares its denominator with the full Euler product by the expected log-divisor \(O(\log W_0)\); choose \(\theta\) small enough relative to the divisor level so that Markov puts half the mass below it. Its coefficients are \(\ll L^C\); CRT yields \(O(\nu(d))\) floors and the bound \(\nu([a,b])\le[a,b]\) yields total \(P^{.04+o(1)}\) per slope pair. Thus the fixed-pair count is \(\ll K\kappa_z V_y S/L+P^{.04+o(1)}\).

For the genuine prime-pair sum, expand \(S(h)=\sum_{d\mid h}g(d)\), \(g(d)=c_0^{\omega(d)}/d\) on eligible squarefree \(d\). For \(d\le U^{1/10}\), use Brun–Titchmarsh for \(p'\equiv p\bmod d\) with \(p\gg_JU>d\) prime and interval length \(O_J(UL^{-A})\). Its length divided by \(d\) is \(\gg U^{.8}\), giving \(O(U L^{-A-1}/\varphi(d))\). See [Montgomery–Vaughan, The large sieve, Theorem 2](https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/large_sieve.pdf). There are \(O(U/L)\) first primes and \(\sum_dg(d)/\varphi(d)<\infty\). At large \(d\), each nonzero \(h\ll_J U\) has total \(g(d)\) for \(d>U^{1/10}\) bounded by \(U^{-.1}(1+c_0)^{\omega(h)}=U^{-.1+o(1)}\); even all integer pairs here give a power saving. The weighted genuine prime-pair sum of \(S\) is \(O(U^2L^{-A-2})\). Multiply by \(O_J(K)\) for the weight: one block contributes \(O(P^2\kappa_z V_y L^{-A-3})\) plus at most \(P^{1.54+o(1)}\) in floors by \(K U^2=P U\). Summing blocks proves the claim.

**2. Real signed factor chamber.** Fix \(\varepsilon,\eta>0\) and integer \(s\ge1\). Let \(\mathscr D\) select squarefree \(D=q_1\cdots q_s\le P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\) with true tagged primes \(q_i>P^\eta\). On the true chamber \(m'=Dr\) with \(r\) prime (e.g. \(D\) consists of the \(s\) smallest genuine factors), the exact sign is \((-1)^{s+1}\) and the absolute contribution is
\[
 \ll_{J,\sigma,\varepsilon,\eta,s}\frac{P^2\kappa_z}{L^3}
     \sum_{D\in\mathscr D}\frac1D+P^{2-\varepsilon/2}.
\]
Restricting rank to squarefree is permitted but not needed for the upper bound. In particular \(D\) a true prime with angle \(a<\log D/L\le b\) contributes at most a fixed multiple of \(P^2\kappa_z/L^3\) times \(\log(b/a)+o(1)\). The constants can be held fixed while \([a,b]\) varies inside a fixed compact subinterval of \((0,1/2)\). Fixed ordered Riemann angular regions for several tags similarly give the ordinary harmonic product volume.

To prove this, fix \(p,p',D\). If \(p'\mid D\) there is no true row because \(p'\nmid P\); otherwise \(k=k_0+Dh\) in one residue \(p'k_0\equiv P\bmod D\), with actual interval length \(O_J(K/D)\) in \(h\). The two prime forms are \(P-pk_0-pDh\) and \((P-p'k_0)/D-p'h\). Choose \(W_0=P^\theta\) with small fixed \(\theta<\min(\eta,\sigma,1/10)\); then \(D,p,p'\) are units at all sieve primes, both forms exceed the cutoff (\(r\asymp_J P/D\)), and the root set is exactly that of the true prime-layer triple through \(z\), then that of the two affine prime forms through \(W_0\). In fact the second affine root can be multiplied by \(D\) in the local residue field to obtain \(m'=0\). Roots coincide at 2 and at odd divisors of \(p-p'\) as above; a locally impossible case at 3 is deleted. The survival product is \(O(\kappa_z S(|p-p'|)/L^2)\). Use divisor level \(P^{\varepsilon/16}\), shrinking \(\theta\) for the same Selberg denominator argument. The true fixed-tuple count is \(\ll (K/D)\kappa_z S/L^2+P^{\varepsilon/4+o(1)}\), with the latter term the fully expanded CRT floor. The pair average of \(S\) without restricting the slope gap is \(O(U^2/L^2)\) by the same small-divisor and large-divisor argument. With the weight \(K\), this gives \(O(P^2\kappa_zL^{-4}\sum D^{-1})\) per block. Since \(\#\mathscr D\le P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\), the floors per block total at most \(P^{\varepsilon/4+o(1)}KU^2\#\mathscr D\ll P^{2-3\varepsilon/4+o(1)}\); sum dyadic blocks. This does not apply any prime-distribution theorem at modulus \(D\); the lattice was switched first. General even \(P\) with \(\mathrm{rad}(P)\le L^B\) have the same bound with an extra explicit factor \(\prod_{\ell\mid P,\ell\ge5}(1+c_1/\ell)\) in the main term, since the only change is collapsed roots at those primes.

**3. True squareful reflections negligible.** The rough \(m'\) can be squareful only if some prime \(r>y\) has \(r^2\mid m'\); at most \(O(P/y)\) integers \(m'\le P\) do so. For each \(m'\), at most \(1/\sigma\) choices of \(p'\mid P-m'\) occur, each fixing \(k\). The total \(k\)-weighted count over first slopes at that \(k\) is \(O_J(P)\) even with integer slopes. The absolute squareful contribution is \(O(P^2/y)=o(P^2\kappa_z/L^3)\).

These are absolute deletions and genuine fixed-rank upper estimates; they leave the sign and size of even-rank minus odd-rank compensation open for substantial squarefree chambers. They are not the missing full G23 inner product.

## Upgrade: tags down to the literal rough threshold, and variable rank

In the tagged-product bound one may replace the polynomial floor \(P^\eta\) by the real \(R_y(D)=1\), and take \(\mathscr D\) to be any set of distinct such integers \(2\le D\le P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\) (squarefree if desired); the constant no longer needs \(\eta\) or the number of tags. The same bound applies to either parity subset, with exact sign \((-1)^{\Omega(D)+1}\) for each true representation. Use \(\theta<\sigma\) as before. The only additional local analysis is at a sieve prime \(\ell\mid D\): since \(p'\nmid D\) in every nonempty tuple, the actual progression makes \(k\) a constant unit modulo \(\ell\), while \(m=P-pk\) is constant. If the latter is zero, there are no actual rows because the sought \(m\asymp P>W_0\) is prime. Otherwise \(k,m\) pass automatically, and the affine form \(r=(P-p'k_0)/D-p'h\) contributes one genuine root. Since every such \(\ell>y\), modifying the original generic local factor changes the product by at most \(\prod_{\ell\mid D}(1+O(1/\ell))=1+o(1)\) uniformly: \(\sum_{\ell\mid D}\ell^{-1}\le O(L/y)\). Local products remain bounded below by an inverse log power in the nonempty case. Hard floor bounds and the summation over distinct \(D\)'s are unchanged. For \(\operatorname{rad}(P)\le L^B\), \(y\) exceeds the radical and the same analysis applies with the previous explicit target divisor multiplier.

Thus, with at most one \(D\) chosen per true representation, **all** squarefree composites whose largest prime is \(>P^{1/2+\varepsilon}\) are covered by choosing \(D=m'/P^+(m')\); terms with \(m'\asymp P\) have \(D\le P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\). This is useful for genuinely sparse D-families of controlled harmonic mass; allowing all rough \(D\)'s gives a much larger harmonic mass and does not bound that whole chamber at the prime-layer scale.
