# The half-envelope on the same high four-tag exterior

Retain exactly the actual signed score, all cutoffs, and the
literal high-arm exterior of [the high four-tag proof](Proof.md).
In particular neither arm lies in the already charged rank
5–7 four-tag cohort. The actual high-arm sum is
\[
                           >-.124M+o(M)
\]
in place of \(>-.262M+o(M)\). This is a replacement on the same
set, not an extra chamber. The low-rank debit does not change.

For the odd ranks \(r\ge9\) in that proof, differentiate the
largest \(k\) box convolutions in the centered density \(b_r\).
The two derivative orders in the original sextic envelope
are \(k=r-5\) and \(k=r-7\); both are positive. Since the
derivative of a centered uniform of width \(u\) is a
difference of two endpoint masses divided by \(u\), the
result is exactly \(2^k\) translates of the remaining
**nonnegative** density, with \(2^{k-1}\) positive signs
and \(2^{k-1}\) negative signs, divided by the product of
the differentiated lengths. Multiplicities, even when
translate locations coincide, are retained. At a fixed
argument both signed sums lie in the interval from zero
to \(2^{k-1}\) times the supremum of that remaining density.
Their difference is therefore at most that last value
in absolute value, not twice that value.

The remaining density is bounded by the largest remaining
inverse-length bound in the sense used in
[the original sextic envelope](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/Proof.md):
its supremum is at most the inverse of the longest remaining
box, since all other densities convolved into it have
total integral one. Thus the two original triangle bounds
each improve by exactly a factor \(1/2\):
\[
 |H_r|\le1056\,2^{r-6}\prod_{i\le4}u_{(i)}
                  +80640\,2^{r-8}\prod_{i\le6}u_{(i)} .
\]
No earlier cancellation of the signs of these translates
was used in the \(2^{r-5},2^{r-7}\) factors of the old high
proof. We have not altered the pointwise score \(H_r\).

Consequently replace \(P_i\) by \(P_i/2\) in that proof
and divide both rows \(\overline m_i,U_i\) of its table by
two. These are linear operations on its **same actual**
ordinary envelope and signed-contact receiver majorants;
all actual smallest \(j\) labels, source units, literal

\(\sqrt{U_i\overline m_i}\). The true old-low cross upper
halves, whereas every high/high overlap upper
\(\min(U_i\overline m_j,U_j\overline m_i)\) quarters.
Each physical orientation and the two high/old-low
subtractions are kept exactly as before.
