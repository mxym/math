# Fixed-target control of deep-minor zeta packets and rational skirts

This uses a **local frequency** theorem of Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Tao (MRT), not their almost-all-Goldbach conclusion. It applies at **each individual even target**, but does not sum a logarithmic gain over a polynomial number of packets. It gives a necessary delocalization of any cancellation at an exceptional target, not positivity of the whole statistic.

Use the actual off-critical-line-zero weights \(B_*\), compact positive ratio band \(\mathcal B_X\), and \(X=N-1/2\), \(G,w\) of [the unconditional cardinal note](UnconditionalCardinalZetaBand.md). Put \(e(v)=e^{2\pi i v},\ L=\log X\). Choose nonnegative \(k\in C_c^\infty((-1,1))\) such that \(\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}k(x-j)=1\); take \(h=\lfloor X^{3/10}\rfloor\) and the genuine smooth partition on \(\mathbb T\),
\[
 K_j(\alpha)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}k(h(\alpha+m)-j),\quad 0\le j<h.
\]
The normalized sum-ordinate is \(\alpha=(\gamma+\gamma')/(2\pi X)\). Let \(g\ge0\) be smooth, supported in \((.6,.74)\), and identically one near the reflected support \(1-\mathrm{supp}(w)\). Define \(S_v(\alpha)=\sum_p(\log p)v(p/X)e(p\alpha)\), and
\[
 Z_j=\sum_{\mathcal B_X}B_*(\rho,\rho') K_j((\gamma+\gamma')/(2\pi X)),\quad
 T_j=\int_0^1 G(2\pi\alpha)K_j(\alpha)e(N\alpha) S_w(-\alpha)S_g(-\alpha)\,d\alpha.
\]
There is an unconditional **absolute sum of transfer errors across the entire mesh**:
\[
                     \sum_{j<h}|Z_j-T_j|\ll X^{.94}.                     \tag{1}
\]
Thus any bounded weighting of the fine packets, even chosen after observing their signs and depending on \(N\), retains the original arithmetic phase with an \(o(X)\) total error.

Fix \(A>0\) and a sufficiently small constant \(0<\epsilon<1/200\). There is \(B=B(A,\epsilon)\), as large as needed, with the following properties. Let \(\mathcal R\) be all reduced points on \(\mathbb T\) whose denominator is at most \(L^B\), together with reduced points of denominator \(2d\) for odd \(d\le L^B\). Write \(U=X^{-1/6-2\epsilon}\), \(D=X^{-8/33-4\epsilon}\), and call a packet near if \(\|j/h-r\|\le U\) for some \(r\in\mathcal R\); otherwise call it deep. Then:

1. The near packets taken together carry \(X\mathfrak S^*_{\lfloor L^B\rfloor}(N)\int w+O_A(XL^{-A})\), positive of order at least \(c_w X\) for large \(B,X\). The enlarged zones capture no problematic skirts: the sum of \(|Z_j|\) over **all** packets with centers of distance between \(4/h\) and \(U\) from some \(r\in\mathcal R\) is \(O_A(XL^{-A})\).
2. For every interval \(J\subset\mathbb T\) of length \(D\),
\[
          \sum_{j/h\in J,\ j\ {\rm deep}} |Z_j| \ll_{A,\epsilon} X L^{-A}.  \tag{2}
\]
The interval can include near points: only its deep fine centers are charged. This bounds arbitrary signed subcollections of roughly \(hD=X^{3/10-8/33-4\epsilon}\) fine packets simultaneously without multiplying by that polynomial number. A partition into at most \(\lceil D^{-1}\rceil+1\) such coarse tiles covers the circle. For an arbitrary fixed desired exponent \(a\), use \(A=a+2\) (and its corresponding \(B\)). Even an adaptively chosen collection of at most \(L^a\) coarse tiles cannot carry more than \(O(X/L^2)\) absolute mass of the deep **signed packet sums**. At any target with \(R_w(N)=0\), (1), the partition and item 1 force negative deep real part of order \(X\). Thus its cancellation cannot be confined to any such polylogarithmic collection. This is not a bound on \(\sum |B_*|\) of microscopic zero pairs.

### Proof of all-mesh transfer

For one \(j\) use the log-delay test of the unconditional cardinal note with compact symbol \(P_u(\lambda)K_j(\lambda/(2\pi))\), \(\lambda=(\gamma+\gamma')/X\). The cutoff to \(|\gamma|,|\gamma'|\le X^4\), Taylor remainder through order 24, nonstationary \(u\)-phases, pole and single terms are exactly as there; \(h\le X^{.4}\) and all real centers stay bounded. Each component is supported at \(|\lambda-\tau_j|\le 2\pi/h\), with at most two periodic components, and \(\lambda\) is in the fixed positive compact interval of the cardinal note. **Use this restriction before taking absolute values of the off-line Taylor terms.** For comparable ordinates in the remaining positive band set \(a_\rho=X^\beta\). By \(2ab\le a^2+b^2\) and the ordinary local zero count, uniformly in the center,
\[
 \sum_{|\gamma+\gamma'-X\tau_j|\le 2\pi X/h}a_\rho a_{\rho'}
    \ll (X/h+1)L\sum_{\gamma\asymp X} X^{2\beta} \ll (X/h)X^{2.11}L^{O(1)},
\]
where both ordinates on the left lie in fixed compact positive multiples of \(X\). Integrating Ingham's classical density theorem as in the unconditional note gives the latter bound: on \([1/2,1]\), \(2\sigma+3(1-\sigma)/(2-\sigma)\le 7-2\sqrt6<2.11\), and on the other half use the ordinary count. The second derivative in the \(u\) phase has size \(\gg X\). Thus the off-line Taylor term of order \(m\ge1\) costs at most \(O_m(X^{-3/2}(h/X)^m(X/h)X^{2.11} L^{O(1)})\), which is \(O(X^{.62})\). Uniformly the main \(m=0\) term is exactly \(Z_j\); no amplitude on the critical line is assumed.

On the arithmetic side keep the **exact** inverse transform \(b_{u,j}(s)\) of \(P_u(\lambda)K_j(\lambda/(2\pi))\). For fixed derivative orders in \(s,u\) it and its derivatives are \(\ll_D h^{-1}(1+|s|/h)^{-D}\), by convolution with the modulated rescaled transform of \(k\). Work first on \(|s|\le 2hX^\xi\), \(\xi=1/200\); tails are power-small to any prescribed power as in the cardinal note. Set \(Y=Xe^{s/X}\), \(y=p-a,\ Y-y=p'-a'\). Replace \(s\) by \(Y-X\) in the inverse kernel, \(u=y/Y\) by \(p/X\), and the exterior Jacobian/dilation weight by 1. Their errors in the arguments or multiplier are respectively \(O(h^2X^{2\xi-1}),O(hX^{\xi-1}),O(hX^{\xi-1})\). Correct the cell lengths to \(p/X,1-p/X\) at the latter precision. There are \(O(XhX^\xi)\) pairs of integers and each continuous kernel error carries \(O(1/h)\); the combined cost is \(O((h^2 X^{3\xi}+hX^{2\xi})L^2)\). The number of **proper** prime powers in central intervals is \(O(\sqrt X)\); at each one the summed absolute kernel weights of its possible opposite integer partners are \(O(1)\) by the displayed Schwartz bound, so their cost is \(O(\sqrt X L^2)\). Smooth compact factor \(g(p'/X)\) can be inserted at arbitrary power saving, since it equals one on a fixed surrounding neighborhood. Inside each remaining cell the computation of (5) in the cardinal note gives *exactly* the inverse Fourier coefficient of \(G(\lambda)K_j(\lambda/(2\pi))\) at \(p+p'-N\); the aperture \(A_u\) cancels. Fourier inversion with \(\lambda=2\pi\alpha\) is exactly \(T_j\) in the displayed sign convention. All errors per packet are \(O(X^{.63})\); summing \(h\) proves (1) with slack. This argument also transfers an individual smooth rational mask of scale \(X^{.4}\) at error \(O(X^{.91})\), by either the previous note or the same estimates (then \(h^2X^{3\xi}=X^{.815}\)).

### Primary local bounds and the two kinds of region

We invoke [Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Tao, *Correlations of the von Mangoldt and higher divisor functions I*, Proposition 3.4 (i),(ii)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01315); the [authors' copy has the full hypotheses at (62)–(63)](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/radziwill/correlations.pdf). At scale \(Y\asymp X\), for \(f(n)=\Lambda(n)1_{(Y,2Y]}\) and \(Q_0=(\log Y)^{B_0}\), their bounds include:

* Over the **whole** range \(Y^{-1}(\log Y)^{B'}\ll|\theta|\ll Y^{-1/6-\epsilon_0}\) about any reduced \(a/q\), \(q\le Q_0\), the integral of \(|S_f(a/q+\theta)|^2\) is \(\ll Y(\log Y)^{-C}\).
* For any \(Y^{-1/6-\epsilon_0}\ll|\beta|\le1/(qQ_0)\), the same bound holds on \(|\theta-\beta|\ll Y^{-8/33-\epsilon_0}\).

In both cases choose \(B_0\) sufficiently large depending on \(C\) and then \(B'\) sufficiently large depending also on \(B_0\). We use the actual bounds on frequency integrals; it is not legitimate to cite the authors' averaged correlation corollary as a bound at our fixed \(N\).

The sharp dyadic support suffices for smooth \(S_v\). Place the support of each of \(w,g\) inside a single \((Y/X,2Y/X)\) for fixed \(Y\asymp X\). Multiplication by \(v(n/X)\) is convolution in frequency with a periodized kernel bounded by \(C_M X(1+X\|\alpha\|)^{-M}\) (Poisson summation). Its mass outside \(\|\alpha\|\le X^{-.9}\) is smaller than any prescribed power. Minkowski and a slightly enlarged frequency region give the local \(L^2\) bounds with this cushion; the tail times the global norm is negligible. In deleting prime powers use their global **squared** Parseval norm \(O(\sqrt X L^2)\). Conjugation or \(\alpha\mapsto-\alpha\) does not affect the estimates since the weights are real.

For deep centers choose \(C=A+3\) in the second MRT bound, with \(\epsilon_0=3\epsilon\) sufficiently small; take \(B\) larger than the required \(B_0+1\). If \(J\) contains a deep fine center \(\alpha_0\), approximate it by Dirichlet's theorem as \(a/q+\beta\), \(q\le \lceil (\log Y)^{B_0}\rceil\), \(|\beta|\le1/(q(\log Y)^{B_0})\) (one can use the integer floor in both Dirichlet and MRT; alternatively take the largest integer \(M\le Q_0\), for which the Dirichlet bound is \(1/[q(M+1)]<1/(qQ_0)\)). This rational is in \(\mathcal R\), thus \(|\beta|>U\gg Y^{-1/6-3\epsilon}\). Every support of a charged fine packet lies in a neighborhood of \(\alpha_0\) of radius \(D+2/h+X^{-.9}\ll Y^{-8/33-3\epsilon}\). Cauchy–Schwarz on this one physical interval bounds \(\sum_{j\in J,\,deep}|T_j|\) by \(\|G\|_\infty\int_E |S_w S_g|\), because \(K_j\ge0,\ \sum_jK_j=1\). The two local MRT norms give the claimed bound; (1) absorbs all chosen transfer errors at once.

For the skirts take \(C=A+2B+10\) in the **first** MRT bound and use a fresh, sufficiently large \(B_0>B+2\); this does not expand the actual set \(\mathcal R\) being summed. Apply it with \(\epsilon_0=\epsilon\). On the charged supports the offsets, even after the smoothing cushion, range from \(2/h\) to \(2U\), strictly between the required boundaries. There are \(O(L^{2B})\) rationals. Sum the local square bounds and use Cauchy and the nonnegative packet partition as before. This proves the asserted collective skirt estimate.

Finally compare the sum of all near \(T_j\) to the **ordinary-circle integral** with the earlier disjoint flat-top mask centered on exactly \(\mathcal R\) at scale \(H=X^{2/5}\). Their multipliers agree within \(c/H\) of each rational (both one) and outside its \(2U\) neighborhood (both zero); the difference is bounded. Their difference therefore lies in the same first-MRT range, now from \(c/H\) to \(2U\), so its integral is \(O_A(XL^{-A})\) after summing all rational skirts. Each narrow rational mask's physical and genuine zeta integral agree to \(O(X^{.91})\), so [the unconditional polylogarithmic rational-main theorem](UnconditionalCardinalZetaBand.md) evaluates their combined integral with exactly \(\mathfrak S^*_{\lfloor L^B\rfloor}\). Together with (1) this proves item 1; the positive uniform lower bound follows from the cited relative tail bound and \(\inf_{2\mid N}\mathfrak S(N)>0\). For the total note that \(\sum_jT_j=R_w(N)\) **exactly** by parity and the \(\pi\)-periodization of \(G\). Thus the asserted necessary delocalization follows for every target with zero weighted count. The phase across the unestimated polynomial number of coarse tiles is retained in (1), not estimated away or replaced by a mean-square statement about targets.

### Scope compared with other recent estimates

[Pintz–Althöfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15559v1) and [Althöfer–Chen–Perelli–Pintz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09386v1) bound exceptional target sets in their respective settings. Here the target is never averaged, but the bounded contribution is only a local frequency integral and its counterpart in the ordinates of actual zeta zeros. In particular the transfer does not prove a full fixed-target minor-arc estimate or the removal of any exceptional target.
