# A signed debit for every opposing rank when one arm has four small tags and rank 5–7

Use exactly the original sharp sextic \(H\) and the current
\(S=G_b+W\), \(b=.003\), of the
[three-by-three comparison](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/ThreeByThreeTagCredit.md),
on the genuine fixed even target \(N\), \(x+y=N\), \(x\in NJ\),
\((x,N)=1\). Write \(L=\log N\), \(M=|J|N\mathfrak S(N)\).
The [smaller tiny debit](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/ImprovedTinyCovariance.md)
and all previously priced physical chambers keep their meanings.

Let \(\mathcal Y\) be the actual squarefree \(n\) of rank 5, 6,
or 7, with all prime factors \(>N^{.003}\) and **at least four**
actual factors at most \(N^{.080}\). Put \(Y=1_{\mathcal Y}H\).
On these \(n\) one has \(Y=S\le0\). Indeed \(Q=0\) and the
tiny gate passes, and \(W=0\) because after its two named least
primes the quotient still has a real prime \(\le N^{.080}\),
whereas the required \(W\) floor is at least \((.555-.065)/3>.163\).
The sign follows from the exact original rank 5–7 formulas.

**Proposition.** The complete signed sum on the original pair rows
where **either** coordinate lies in \(\mathcal Y\) is
\[
                        >-.191M+o(M).
\]
There are no restrictions on the other coordinate's rank, signs,
tags, or completion. The union is disjoint from the previously
priced rough, two-tag, mixed boundary, one-by-two, two-by-three,
and three-by-three chambers, including
[the complete two-by-three chamber](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/FullTwoByThreeTagCredit.md).
A \(\mathcal Y\) arm cannot be \(N^{.1251}\)-rough, cannot be the
mixed arm with least angle in \((.080,.125]\), and has too many
actual \(\le .080\) factors for any of the other chambers. It is
also disjoint from the separate
[medium semiprime debit](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/MediumSemiprimeAdverse.md),
where neither coordinate has four such factors.
Thus one may subtract .191 from a preceding coefficient and remove
this entire union from its still-unpriced exterior. This is **not**
a better coefficient with an unchanged exterior. Any later
charge of \(\ge4\)-tag rows must exclude this union. In particular,
this does not price higher odd ranks on the four-tag arm when
neither coordinate is in \(\mathcal Y\).

## 1. A cheap receiver on precisely the four least labels

Let \(a=.003,\ z_0=.080,\ \theta=.499\). Denote the true smallest
four target-log angles on an arm in \(\mathcal Y\) by
\(u_1\le u_2\le u_3\le v\) with \(a<u_i,v\le z_0\), let
\(\sigma=u_1+u_2+u_3+v\), and put \(T=1-\sigma\).
For formulae on \(H/\log n\), actual own-log angles give the
same limiting cap with uniform \(O_J(1/L)\) errors on these
fixed-floor compact ranks. All hard support cuts continue to use
target-log angles.

At rank 5 the remaining genuine prime is a giant, since
\(T\ge .680\). The four-tag difference equals the average of
\(F^{(4)}\) over the centered expression
\(u_1 X_1+u_2 X_2+u_3 X_3+v X_4\) with \(X_i\)
uniform on \([0,1]\), times \(\prod_{i\le4}u_i\)
(where \(u_4=v\)). These are auxiliary integrals for the exact
one-integer polynomial difference. Its negative magnitude
after dividing by the four tag angles is
\[
              10080T^2+3360\sum_{i\le4}u_i^2-1056.
\]
The upper \(10080T^2\) suffices here since
\(3360\cdot4(.080)^2<1056\).
At rank 6 the same normalized magnitude is
\(40320 u_5u_6\le10080 T^2\).
At rank 7 use the [exact rank-seven density formula](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/Proof.md#2-the-whole-rough-negative-arm-comparison).
If \(b_7\) is the symmetric box-convolution density, then
\(|b_7''(0)|\le 2/(u_5u_6u_7)\) and \(b_7(0)\le1/u_7\le3/T\).
Thus at rank 7 the normalized magnitude is at most
\(2112+(80640\cdot3/T)u_5u_6u_7\le2112+8960T^2\).
For all three ranks we have the common pointwise upper
\[
                |Y(n)|\le L\left(2112+8960T^2+o(1)\right)
                                     \prod_{i\le4}u_i.               \tag{1}
\]
There is no completed-rank factor or assumption of independent
opposing factors in (1).

For a genuine nonnegative finite-rank compact positive-floor
product source \(Z\) on the opposite arm, use \(m(Z)\) for its
ordinary mass, namely \(\mathfrak S(N)\) times its unopposed
weighted count divided by \(M\) in the relevant interval, in
the large-\(N\) limit. It has the same normalization as in the
[explicit tiny proof](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/ExplicitTinyStop.md).
We prove
\[
                    \langle Z,|Y|\rangle/M
                         \le .1212\,m(Z)+o(1),                    \tag{2}
\]
also with the arms exchanged and the appropriate interval reflected.

In fixed angle boxes, open the product \(h\) of the four actual
least primes. The quotient is rough above the true fourth
tag \(N^v\), even when it contains additional primes at most
\(N^{.080}\). Upper-sift the quotient at the lower box floor
\(N^{v_-}\) with support exponent \(D=\theta-\sigma_+\), where
\(\sigma_+\) is the box upper tag sum. The actual tested modulus
is \(h e\le N^\theta\), far below the distribution ceiling
for the genuine \(Z\) prime products. The precise
weighted below-root transfer is the same as in
[the one-by-two proof, §2](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/OneByTwoTagCredit.md):
open the literal squarefree sieve divisors, keep the actual
unit residue \(N\bmod he\) and actual reduced source mask, and
retain the target factors in \(\mathfrak S(N)\).
Named primes or true source primes which occur in the local
model are polynomially large and of bounded number; deleting
them from the reciprocal model costs a relative \(1+o(1)\).
In the positive upper bound omitting tests at the named
primes only enlarges the actual receiver. No lower or signed
weighted sieve estimate is asserted. Tag allocation and joined
modulus counts have fixed divisor multiplicity, and the fixed
angle boxes can preserve the order of the actual primes before
the upper enlargement. The opposite source can be approximated
by bounded step weights on its true compact prime products.
These operations also apply to the fixed-floor truncations of
the explicit tiny remainder \(|V|\); that proof's physical floor
limits then restore \(|V|\).

Throughout these boxes the linear upper parameter is \(D/v_->2\).
For \(D\ge4v_+\) the coefficient normalized by the true
\(\mathfrak S(N)/L\) is at most \(.575/v_-\);
for \(3v_+\le D<4v_+\) it is at most \(2/(3v_-)\);
otherwise use \(\max(2/(3v_-),2/D)\). These follow from the
linear upper function at \(s=3,4\), its monotonicity,
and its exact \(2e^{\gamma_E}/s\) branch below 3. The
four \(\prod u_i\) in (1) cancel the four **actual** logarithmic
reciprocal prime factors, so the remaining upper integral is
simply box volume times the pointwise cap in (1) and the above
conditional coefficient. The checker below bounds this
explicit upper by .1212. Unlike a constant receiver for rough
integers, (2) uses the small sign-amplitude on its four true
least factors.

## 2. Actual ordinary mass of the whole \(\mathcal Y\) side

Write \(m_0=m(|Y|)\). We have \(m_0<.0554\).
Fixing the four literal least angles, the **ordinary unopposed**
quotient angles are all \(\ge v\). There is no requirement that
the fifth or sixth factor be above \(.080\).
Let \(b_{k,v}(T)\) be the density at \(T\) of
\((1_{s\ge v}\,ds/s)^{*k}/k!\) for \(k\) genuine residual prime
factors. This is the true prime reciprocal harmonic and final
prime density, on separate fixed prime-product boxes. Sorting
the four least labels uses an ordered four-dimensional integral;
the unordered residual labels give precisely the indicated
factorial. Repeated primes and target omissions have power-small
one-arm cost on these polynomial floors.

The following functions of the four least angles bound the three
ordinary integrands after canceling their reciprocal factors:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|l}
  5 &\displaystyle
          10080T+(3360\cdot4v^2-1056)/T\\[2pt]
  6 &\displaystyle 20160(T-2v)\\[2pt]
  7 &\displaystyle 2112\, b_{3,v}(T)+20160(T-3v)^2/T .
 \end{array}                                                       \tag{3}
\]
For rank 5 the quotient density is exactly \(1/T\), and we used
\(\sum_{i\le4}u_i^2\le4v^2\). For the rank 6 factor \(40320u_5u_6\)
cancels both residual reciprocal denominators, leaving the
Lebesgue interval volume \((T-2v)/2!\). For rank 7 use the two
terms from the density bound in §1 separately: the first
keeps \(b_{3,v}(T)\); the second cancels all three reciprocals
and gives \((80640\cdot3/T)(T-3v)^2/(3!\,2!)\).
Here \(T>3v\) everywhere on the actual domain. These are upper
bounds for the **entire** physical ranks 5–7, including the
cases with more than four \(\le .080\) tags.

Here are reproducible outward details for both (2) and (3).
For each \(v\), write \(u_1,u_2,u_3\) as a sorted triple
of independent integration variables in \([a,v]\), and let
\(z\in[0,3]\) denote the sum of their affine rescalings to \([0,1]\).
The ordered triple volume is \((v-a)^3/6\); the CDF of \(z\) is
\(\sum_{j=0}^3(-1)^j\binom3j(z-j)_+^3/6\).
Then \(\sigma=v+3a+(v-a)z\). Use \(v\) mesh \(1/5000\)
and \(z\) mesh \(1/40\); the latter's box masses are exact rational
CDF differences. Enlarge \((v-a)^3\) to the upper end and use
the actual interval extremes for \(T,v\).
The first integrand is increasing in \(T\) at fixed \(v\)
since its coefficient of \(1/T\) is negative.
The second and the last polynomial terms are maximized using
upper \(T\) and lower \(v\).
For \(b_{3,v}(T)=b_{3,1}(T/v)/v\) use the positive recursion
\[
   b_{1,1}(t)=1_{t\ge1}/t,\qquad
      t b_{k,1}(t)=\int_{k-1}^{t-1}b_{k-1,1}(s)\,ds.
\]
The factorial is intrinsic: multiply the \(k\)-fold convolution
by its total angle, mark one of its \(k\) entries, and integrate
its angle against \(ds\). The checker evaluates this recursion
with upward fixed-integer rounding on \(1/200\) intervals,
then takes the supremum over the exact outward range of
\(T/v\). The checker uses only rational arithmetic and the
already certified linear coefficient at 4. It prints the three
mass uppers \(<.010631,.020959,.023723\), their sum
\(<.055312\), and the receiver upper \(<.121096\).
The proof uses the slightly widened rational constants
\(.0554,.1212\).

## 3. The full paired union, with signs and no missing opposite ranks

The exact same score satisfies \(S=H+Q+W-T_{\rm tiny}\),
where \(T_{\rm tiny}=IH\) is the original .003 tiny part.
Since \(Y\) is a true bounded-rank compact source, the actual
[signed \(H\) transfer](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/Proof.md)
gives
\[
             \langle Y,H\rangle/M=m(Y)-\langle Y,\Pi\rangle/M+o(1)
                                  \ \ge -m_0+o(1).
\]
Here \(Y\le0\), so the genuine prime cross may only help.
The two nonnegative corrections instead have adverse signs.
Apply (2) using **\(Q\) and \(W\) as sources** to bound their
total cost by \(.1212(m(Q)+m(W))\); the existing true ordinary
bounds are \(.01701,.16921\), respectively. Applying the
receiver in the opposite, more expensive direction is not
needed.

Keep the original \(T_{\rm tiny}=A+V+E-D\) at \(b=.003\).
The actual transfer of \(A\) is \(c_A m(Y)\), with
\(|c_A|<.228\), and the \(E\) cross is \(o(M)\), exactly
as in the [original tiny transfer](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/ExplicitTinyStop.md)
with the [.003 parameter choice](../sextic_true_tag_rebate/ExplicitTinyStopTradeoff.md).
Use (2) with \(|V|\) at its real compact floor limits,
where \(m(|V|)<.009042\).
Use the original true receiver for \(|D|\), at most
\(.007445m_0\), in its cheaper orientation.
Thus for either actual orientation the lower for the
one-arm restricted sum is
\[
   \langle Y,S\rangle/M \ge
   - (1+.228+.007445)m_0
   - .1212(.01701+.16921+.009042)+o(1).
\]
The reflected estimate uses the actual interval \(1-J\)
rather than assuming \(J\) symmetric. Every upper is a signed
calibration or a positive source–receiver bound at the literal
fixed target, not a chosen coupling of factor distributions.

Finally, since \(Y=1_{\mathcal Y}S\), the signed union is exactly
\(\langle Y,S\rangle+\langle S,Y\rangle-\langle Y,Y\rangle\).
The overlap is removed once using
\(0\le\langle Y,Y\rangle/M\le .1212m_0+o(1)\), by (2) on the
genuine source \(-Y\).
The [rational replay](check.py), run by \(\texttt{python3 -OB check.py}\),
gives the resulting strict upper debit
\[
  2[(1+.228+.007445)(.0554)+.1212(.01701+.16921+.009042)]
     +.1212(.0554)=.1909332948<.191.
\]
This charges the full original union, not just its gross adverse
pieces or a separately chosen favorable subset.
