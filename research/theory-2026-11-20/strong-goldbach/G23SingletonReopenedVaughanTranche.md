Written research note. Use the [long-singleton Vaughan reduction](G23LongSingletonVaughanTypeII.md) and the [moving-slope covariance](G23SingletonMovingSlopeCovariance.md) notation, with \(P=2^j\), \(L=\log P\), \(z=e^{L^{.24}}\), \(y=e^{L^\delta}\). This is an actual additional signed estimate on an open exponent chamber of the Vaughan convolution field, not a statement that genuine prime t factorizes into two integers.

The exact remaining singleton term has \(a=p d b\ell\in PJ\), \(k=d b\ell\in\mathcal K\), \(t=b\ell\le X_t\), genuine prime p, true short z-rough w-smooth d, \(b>V_0\), and weight \(\mu(b)\beta(\ell)\), with \(\beta(\ell)=\sum_{r\mid\ell,r>U_0}\Lambda(r)\). Open this mark *without changing its sign or primality*, writing \(\ell=r h\); thus r is a genuine shifted Vaughan von Mangoldt mark, including prime powers exactly, and h is an unrestricted integer. Both \(r>U_0\) and \(b>V_0\), so the new rows are not contained in either short original Vaughan summand.

**Paid tranche.** Put \(Q=p d b r\) and impose the literal gate \(Q\le D_*=P^{1/2}e^{-L^{.6}}\). Uniformly in the true k- and t-endpoints, the contribution of these actual terms
\[
 -\sum_{\substack{p,d,b,r,h;\ b>V_0,\ r>U_0\\
 Q\le D_*,\ Qh\in PJ,\ d b r h\in\mathcal K,\ b r h\le X_t}}
   \lambda(d)\mu(b)\Lambda(r)R_z(brh)
   \frac{\log(Qh)}L\, R_y(P-Qh)\lambda(P-Qh)

is \(O_A(PL^{-A})\) for each fixed \(A\). The exact complement of this removed convolution tranche is the same field with \(p d b r>D_*\); the estimate does not assert that this complement is paid.

## Actual shifted BV proof

Split \(R_z(brh)=R_z(b)R_z(r)R_z(h)\) exactly (overlap of factors does not affect the identity for roughness). Require b,r rough before expanding the remaining rough h and m by the established Bonferroni depths \(O_A(\log L)\). Work with the genuine target units, so b,r,h,d,p are odd and the reflected m is odd. Each retained true auxiliary test \(v\mid h,e\mid m\) has \(\log v,\log e=O_A(L^{.24}\log L)=o(L^{.6})\). They give the physical class \(m/e\equiv P e^{-1}\bmod Qv\) and an extra factor 2 for the exact unit condition. Nonempty classes are reduced: a common odd factor of \(e\) and \(Qv\) would divide P. Reflect only after intersecting the real conditions \(Qh\in PJ,d b r h\in\mathcal K,b r h\le X_t\); both resulting endpoints comparable to \(P/e\). The genuine modulus \(2Qv\) is strictly below the square-root Liouville BV level at \(P/e\), with arbitrary fixed logarithmic spare.

The gate \(Q\le D_*\) is a restriction on the actual tuple and can be enforced before summing. No relative primality of p,d,b,r among themselves is assumed; at worst \(\tau_4(Q)\) factorizations occur. As these four variables are all z-rough for large P, the auxiliary v is distinguished from Q by the prime range. In any case divisor-power multiplicity suffices. On fixing e, coefficients are bounded by a divisor power times L; maximum endpoint BV and partial summation for \(\log(Qh)/L\) apply under arbitrary-log losses, exactly as in the published short Vaughan audit. For the weighted error one may apply Cauchy–Schwarz with unweighted uncentered maximal error \(E_f(q)\ll (P/e)/q+1\), and \(\sum_{q\le D}\tau(q)^C/q\ll L^{O_C(1)}\). Summing over e costs a harmonic log power. The positive absolute rough-shell sums are factorially small by \(\sum_{\ell\le z}1/\ell=O(\log L)\), and their floor costs are \(D_*P^{o(1)}L^{O(1)}\) even over all actual factorizations, from \(\sum_{Q\le D_*}\tau_4(Q)\ll D_*L^3\). Thus the full main assertion follows with literal physical arithmetic.

If replacing the reflected rough Liouville by a true prime-reflection test, this same grouping by \(Qv\) allows ordinary prime BV and gives its usual CRT prime main, not zero. No genuine prime is replaced by an integer in that statement.

## Nonzero physical mass, and the precise limitation

There is a nonempty fixed positive-angle rectangle of genuinely supported summands well inside this gate. Choose d=1, true p,b,r primes in fixed positive-width exponent intervals, with p between \(P^\sigma\) and \(P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\), b,r with exponents just greater than \(\gamma\), and total of the three upper exponents less than \(1/2\). Such choices exist because the original parameters satisfy \(\sigma<1/2-\varepsilon\) and \(2\gamma+\eta<\varepsilon\); take sufficiently small fixed widths and margins. Allow the full k and t windows for this demonstration. For each such true tuple the h interval \(Qh\in PJ\) has length \(\asymp_J P/Q\). For all sieve primes h's two actual affine tests are \(R_z(h)\) and \(R_y(P-Qh)\), and Q is a unit at them. At odd primes \(\ell\le y\) the excluded roots \(0,PQ^{-1}\) are distinct; at 2 they coincide; for \(y<\ell\le z\) only zero is excluded. Consequently a direct CRT Bonferroni count on the genuine h interval gives \((1+o(1))|J|P\kappa_z V_y(P)/Q\), uniformly in the rectangle: the factorial tails are arbitrary-log small and floors \(P^{o(1)}\) are negligible on this true polynomial interval. The genuine logarithm weight is bounded below and \(|\mu(b)|=1,\Lambda(r)=\log r\) here. Mertens on the actual polynomial prime windows yields constant positive reciprocal masses for p and b, and \(\sum_r\Lambda(r)/r\asymp L\). Thus the absolute mass of these actual summands is \(\gg P L\kappa_z V_y(P)\), even though their tested signed total is arbitrarily log-small. These are not zero-support diagonals or a power-scale floor. A true prime-reflection absolute mass \(\gg P V_z(P)\) on the rectangle likewise follows from the just-mentioned prime BV main, with positive coefficients and the same h-rough factor \(V_z(P)\) on the reduced reflection classes.

This is compensation in a real **Vaughan convolution field**. Each such row has composite \(t=b r h\), as genuine Vaughan Type II summands must; their role in the exact singleton expansion is algebraic compensation with the other Vaughan pieces. Thus the signed estimate is not claimed for an independently identifiable subpopulation of genuine prime t. For the unpaid \(Q>D_*\) region the free h variable and real shifted modulus leave the square-root BV range; the previously isolated joint moving-slope estimate or new distribution input is still needed. No full singleton or Goldbach conclusion.

## Exact high-gate compression of the reopened Vaughan field

This is a reduction, not a new bound for the residual. Keep the original actual \(P=2^j\), prime \(P^\sigma<p\le P^{1/2-\varepsilon}\), z-rough w-smooth \(d\le P^\eta\), \(U_0=V_0=P^\gamma\), \(2\gamma+\eta<\varepsilon\), and true physical restrictions \(pdt\in PJ,\ dt\in\mathcal K,\ t\le X_t\). The displayed weight below is the strict reflected rough Liouville version (not the prime-reflection variant). Let \(L=\log P,\ D_*=P^{1/2}e^{-L^{.6}}\), \(z=e^{L^{.24}}\), and \(\lambda_y=R_y\lambda\). Write the actual weight, including roughness and original \(-\lambda(d)\), as
\[
 W_{p,d}(t)=-\lambda(d)\,1_{pdt\in PJ,\,dt\in\mathcal K,\,t\le X_t}
       R_z(t)\frac{\log(pdt)}L\,\lambda_y(P-pdt).
\]
Every indicator and coefficient in this expression depends only on the true p,d,t, not on a particular split of t.

In the reopened Vaughan field put \(s=b r\), \(t=s h\), and \(T_{p,d}=D_*/(p d)\). The actual high gate is \(s>T_{p,d}\). With \(\mu_{>V_0}\) and \(\Lambda_{>U_0}\) denoting pointwise truncations, the coefficient of s is
\[
 F(s)=(\mu_{>V_0}*\Lambda_{>U_0})(s).
\]
Strict exponent slack gives \(T_{p,d}\ge P^{\varepsilon-\eta}e^{-L^{.6}}>U_0V_0\) uniformly over true p,d. The identity \(\mu*\Lambda=-\mu\cdot\log\) follows by convolving \(\Lambda*1=\log\) with \(\mu\) and using \(\log(xy)=\log x+\log y\), or by differentiating the reciprocal zeta series in absolute convergence. Thus on this true high gate,
\[
 F(s)=-\mu(s)\log s-(\mu_{\le V_0}*\Lambda)(s)-(\mu*\Lambda_{\le U_0})(s).
\]
The omitted fourth term \(\mu_{\le V_0}*\Lambda_{\le U_0}\) has empty support here. This equality includes prime powers in \(\Lambda\), not just true primes.

**The two high boundary sums are paid for strict reflected Liouville, uniformly in all original physical endpoints.** For the first, extend its s sum to all divisors of true t: \((\mu_{\le V_0}*\Lambda)*1=\mu_{\le V_0}*\log\), exactly the already paid original short Type I. For the second the corresponding identity is \((\mu*\Lambda_{\le U_0})*1=\Lambda_{\le U_0}\), which is zero because true \(t\gg_J P^{1/2+\varepsilon-\eta}\). These extensions keep \(W_{p,d}(t)\) **unchanged**, including literal \(R_z(t)\), so they are true identities before any estimate.

In each extension subtract the complementary low s gate \(p d s\le D_*\). This low sum is paid by the same actual BV computation as the reopened low Vaughan tranche. Explicitly, both boundary coefficients at s have absolute size at most \(\sum_{r\mid s}\Lambda(r)=\log s\). On the true rough support \(R_z(s h)=R_z(s)R_z(h)\), so require s rough and expand only the genuinely varying h and reflected m roughness. The retained auxiliary tests \(v\mid h,e\mid m\) have log sizes \(O_A(L^{.24}\log L)=o(L^{.6})\); on odd units the true reduced class is \(m/e\equiv P e^{-1}\bmod p d s v\), with a factor 2 for oddness. For fixed p,d,s, intersect the true inequalities \(p d s h\in PJ,\ d s h\in\mathcal K,\ s h\le X_t\) **before** reflecting; both endpoints are of size \(P/e\) when nonempty. The modulus \(2p d s v\le2D_*\exp(o(L^{.6}))\) lies strictly under the arbitrary-log uncentered Liouville BV level at that endpoint. The grouping costs at most a fixed divisor power in the physical pds, with the extra v product distinguished by its small prime range. Bonferroni harmonic tails and floor costs \(D_*P^{o(1)}\) are as in the reopened low tranche. The logarithm in \(W\) is handled by physical partial summation. Thus both actual low boundary terms, and hence both high ones, are \(O_A(PL^{-A})\).

Consequently the exact high reopened Vaughan sum equals, up to arbitrary logarithmic saving,
\[
 \sum_{p,d}\sum_{s>D_*/(pd)}\sum_{h\ge1}
    [-\mu(s)\log s]\,W_{p,d}(s h).
\]
There is now only one long squarefree coefficient in \(s\), with unrestricted h subject to the actual tests; its cutoff forces \(h\ll_J P/D_*\). This is the genuine hyperbola split of the alternative identity \(\Lambda=(-\mu\cdot\log)*1\). It does not supply another Type I estimate: the free-h approach has modulus \(p d s>D_*\), while exchanging roles to integrate s leaves \(\mu(s)\) inside the same shifted sum \(\mu(s)\lambda(P-pdhs)\). In the strict prime-reflection variant the analogous Type I groupings estimate actual CRT prime mains rather than arbitrary-log zero; this note does not claim the signed boundary cancellation there.

Simply swapping b and r creates no sign gain: if both are true primes in both ranges, the two real weights are \(-\log r\) and \(-\log b\), with the same sign. Composite b with nonzero \(\mu(b)\) is squarefree and is not eligible as the swapped \(\Lambda\) mark. This isolates the coefficient which remains; it does not prove a further paid positive-angle high subregion or cancellation of the full singleton term.


**Direct short derivation.** For integer t, from \(\mu*1=\delta_1\) one has \(\sum_{s\mid t}\mu(s)\log t=0\) (also for \(t=1\)). Splitting \(\log t=\log s+\log(t/s)\) yields \((-\mu\cdot\log)*1=\mu*\log=\Lambda\). Apply this directly to the exact original \(\Lambda(t)W_{p,d}(t)\) sum, splitting at the same \(p d s=D_*\): the lower-s term has \(|\mu(s)\log s|\le L+O_J(1)\), so the same actual shifted BV argument just written pays it; the upper-s term is exactly the residual displayed above. Thus both routes agree modulo the actually paid short term, uniformly in endpoints. For the true prime-reflection counterpart that same Type I expansion instead supplies its genuine CRT prime main (and a small remainder), not the zero-main strict-Liouville estimate.
