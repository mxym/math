# Goldbach written research, November 24

These results concern scoped pieces of the binary Goldbach problem. They neither prove nor refute the conjecture.

## Factor-closed components

- [All six-label components](EACSixFullStrictPowerSaving.md): at most $X^{3/4-\epsilon+o(1)}$ component-bearing centers up to $X$ with [the explicit choice](EACSixRationalSaving.md) $\epsilon=1/401$. It strengthens the [earlier three-quarter count](EACSixThreeQuarter.md), with a [paper proof of the lone-cube step](EACSixLoneCubePaper.md) and a separate chart replay.
- [Both exponent presentations singular](EACSixTwoThirdsProduct.md): the two least labels obey $pq\le N^{2/3+o(1)}$. This yields the $X^{2/3+o(1)}$ count in corank one, not in corank two. It supersedes the [seven-tenths intermediate estimate](EACSixSevenTenthsProduct.md).
- [Corank two with a pure fourth-power row](EACSixCorankTwoPureFourthSaving.md): a strict but not explicit power saving applies in this subclass.
- [All rows semiprime](EACSixSemiprimeSquareRoot.md): at most $X^{1/2+o(1)}$ centers in this narrower class.

- [Seven labels and semiprime rows](EACSevenSemiprimeQuarter.md): exactly one bipartite cofactor component, giving a quarter-power least-label bound; arbitrary seven-label arity has the weaker cube-root restriction.
- [Eight-label semiprime quarter-power minimum](EACEightSemiprimeQuarter.md): true product gaps and an equal-factor-sum reflection extend the pointwise minimum/maximum restrictions to eight labels and give exponent-matrix nullity at most two; no claim on the size of a terminal component.

- [Growing seed closures](SmallRadicalGrowingSeedRank.md), with [Kummer sharpening](SmallRadicalGrowingSeedKummer.md): at a small-radical target, bounded simultaneous all-composite orbits must contain many distinct singular cyclic components. A [feedback-depth variant](SmallRadicalFeedbackDepthRank.md) replaces the total size bound by a retained-size times acyclic-depth budget, sharpened further using [actual branching](SmallRadicalEffectiveBranching.md). No assertion is made that those components are terminal or that targets without a pair exist.

- [One-side-prime cycles](SmallRadicalOneSidePrimeCycle.md): genuine cycles using a single extra prime for all cofactors are excluded at small radical. A [high-chain scale theorem](HighFactorChainScaleCap.md) bounds narrow or small-cofactor chains, with an exact cap above $N/7$ when $7\nmid N$.

- [Prime-opposite entries](PrimeOppositeHighEntryDichotomy.md): a true prime sieve near the square root supplies many genuine entry rows, but long high-factor paths require many labels with no low prime parent.

- [Small low-factor dictionary](LowDictionaryHighExcursionDichotomy.md): under the stated local parameters, if the dictionary does not expand, direct telescoping forces a very long high excursion from each seed individually.

- [Single actual prime entrance](RealSinglePrimeSeedAnchor.md): a prime of fixed polylogarithmic size can reach any chosen small seed set on its very first reflected factor row.

## Actual fixed-target sieve correlations

- [Prime versus positive-block bounds](CQActualMixedPrimePositiveBlock.md): uniform weighted mixed inequalities for genuine complementary arguments with one actual prime, not a lower bound for two primes.
- [Fixed-modulus coupling obstruction](CQFixedCRTNoPairObstruction.md): exact preservation of specified row projections in a relaxed model, while the companion genuine sieve cap excludes this particular model. The relaxation does not preserve the actual equation or all genuine mixed information.

- [No-pair moment dichotomy](CQNoPairPositivePrimeDichotomy.md): a genuine two-direction inequality rules out simultaneously small prime crosses and an ordinary-sized positive block at a hypothetical exception.
- [Rough complement parity](SmallRadicalRoughLiouville.md), with [Möbius extension](SmallRadicalRoughMobius.md): relative sign cancellation for genuine complements of primes at each small-radical target, not prime–prime positivity.
- [Arbitrary small-factor profiles](SmallRadicalSmoothProfileParity.md): relative rough-part parity cancellation persists for weights of the entire small-prime valuation pattern. A [signed-switch application](SmallRadicalSmoothDivisorChannel.md) handles its smooth-divisor channel on nontrivial profiles, not the rough term.
- [First tag cover](SmallRadicalFirstTagCover.md): a direct covering decomposition saves a fixed log power in the same relative weighted parity estimate, still not the entire rough kernel.
- [Translated tag coverage](SmallRadicalTranslatedTagTypeI.md): the uncovered mass and short-variable pieces are controlled; a [separate off-diagonal reduction](SmallRadicalTranslatedTagGamma.md) removes shared-factor and close-tag cases, but [some high-source chambers](SmallRadicalShiftedHighChambers.md) also cancel or are negligible, and [one bilateral low-rank chamber](SmallRadicalShiftedLowRankPairing.md) cancels by reflecting actual pairs. One [joint narrow window](SmallRadicalShiftedBilateralSignTable.md) is also negligible; the general same-parity and other large-variable terms remain.
- [Actual high-rank exterior removal](CQTrueExteriorHighRankRemoval.md): the existing positive reserve pays the even ranks 10 and above with the stated rough floor, and a specified part of rank 8, with all shared pair regions accounted for.
- [Independent positive shell](CQIndependentPositiveShellRankEight.md): a disjoint real positive sector pays a separate rank-eight adverse sector, without reusing the existing reserve.
- [Asymmetric positive strip](CQAsymmetricStripRankSixPlateau.md): using a disjoint two-threshold region pays the dominant-prime sector of rank six. The [balanced rank-six dyadic mean](CQBalancedRankSixDyadicObstruction.md) shows that the current reserve alone cannot uniformly cover that sector; it does not give the sign at a prescribed target.
- [A real favorable semiprime reservoir](SmallRadicalSemiprimeNearFullPositive.md): each small-radical target has many actual semiprime sums with a tagged near-full prime on the other side; the unmarked coefficient has the favorable sign. A [natural-scale strengthening](SmallRadicalShiftedNaturalScaleReservoir.md) gives a favorable contribution of order at least $P V_y(P)$ even with the actual translated selector; it does not bound the adverse remainder.
- [Deep tagged prime rebate](CQDeepTaggedPrimeRebate.md): a quantified same-target prime credit also applies to a disjoint deep band, with all pair overlaps kept separate from its linear and square moments.
- [Two-range parity cancellation](SmallRadicalShiftedNaturalScaleParityFiber.md): within a genuine tagged cofactor family, both signs have natural-scale mass but their net contribution is smaller than the required threshold. The [whole-cofactor version](SmallRadicalShiftedFullPrimeCofactor.md) removes the two-range truncation and allows any fixed cofactor exponent below one half, still with fixed base tag occupancy. The [first-base-cover extension](SmallRadicalNearFullFirstBaseCover.md) pays all admissible covered families. The genuinely uncovered part is smaller than the whole but not yet controlled at the required precision.
- [Forced reject on a real rank-six overlap](CQRankSixDeepPairedCapacity.md): real third-prime windows consume part of the enlarged joint capacity but are excluded from the deep detector, giving a tighter same-target bound.
- [Single-window adverse semiprime upper](SmallRadicalAdverseSemiprimeWindow.md): a modular hyperbola/Weil bound controls a specified negative (two semiprimes) domain, including balanced windows, without constraining both sides. A [rank-three threshold strip](SmallRadicalAdverseRankThreeThreshold.md) also bounds a genuine adverse part with an unrestricted central prime or rank-three complement.
- [True middle overlap](CQMiddleOverlapPrimeExclusion.md): a shared budget bounds both negative overlap and the mixed positive–negative terms on the same target. This controls a specified part of the signed score, not the whole exterior.

- [Actual first-step rough perfect-power exclusion](DyadicFirstStepRoughPerfectPowerExclusion.md): for powers of two and certain moving small-radical targets, deleting an explicitly growing dictionary of tiny primes from the genuine complement to any sufficiently small unit prime leaves a nontrivial factor that is not a perfect power. This excludes a whole multiplicity pattern, not the remaining composite complements. A separate valuation argument shows that discarding all primes up to $(\log N)^\sigma$ with $\sigma<1/2$ still leaves almost all of the complement's logarithmic size.

- [Exact smooth-divisor alternative](SmallRadicalSmoothDivisorAlternative.md): an equivalent shifted budget makes all genuine odd-complement rows favorable or zero and pays actual least-prime annuli. On the smaller symmetrically rough set the mixed-sign composite pairs cancel up to a negligible error; normalized threshold combinations cannot erase the common even/even core.

- [Reverse pricing and its obstruction](CQYOldNegativeReverse.md): a rank-resolved reverse receiver sharply improves a true overlap upper bound. An exact weighted dyadic mean then shows why that whole intermediate sector still cannot be paid in isolation from the current reserve.
- [Three actual first-step factor thresholds](DyadicTwoLargeFactorsAndSquareDivisors.md): Stark's bounds exclude tightly balanced factors near the square root at even dyadic exponents, and exclude large square divisors at small-radical targets. A separate planted-cofactor construction excludes two large factors with a fixed angular threshold at the cost of a much larger prime input.

- [Short complementary-divisor exchange](SmallRadicalHalfDivisorTradeoff.md): in a different exact representation the whole all-large semiprime–semiprime bill disappears up to the target error. A broad balanced three-factor–three-factor bill turns adverse instead, and a nonlinear repair requires a genuine long two-prime modulus estimate.

- [Fixed-angle exclusion on actual prime-opposite rows](PrimeOppositeFixedAngleExclusion.md): for every sufficiently large even target, a positive-size subfamily of starts at each fixed scale $N^\rho$, $1/3<\rho\le1/2$, has rough complements with at most one factor above $N^{1/2-\delta}$, counted with multiplicity. The bound is uniform in the target radical and does not say a prime complement exists.

- [Calibrated angular prime-axis obstruction](CQPrimeAxisAngularCountermodel.md): a relaxed companion law preserves the current prime-axis counts and score constraints, while varying the key mixed moment. Does not model actual integer pairs or satisfy the missing composite-side joint constraints.
- [Fixed-angle exclusion without imposing roughness](PrimeOppositeAllSourceFixedAngle.md): a positive proportion of all actual prime inputs in each stated source interval has at most one very large complement factor. A separate elementary count also excludes much smaller repeated factors on either surviving subfamily.

- [True short-prime mixed parity](SmallRadicalShortPrimeMixedParity.md): a mixed two-Liouville factor with a genuine short-prime marker cancels on the actually base-covered set, and the corresponding first coefficient can be paid there. The polynomial rank-two/rank-three core often misses this cover, and the high contribution remains.

- [Two/three-factor bilateral core](SmallRadicalG23CoreBilateral.md): a different exact coefficient suppresses the entire low-rank adverse high term up to the target error, including the new three-by-three sign introduced by the half-divisor switch. Its cost is the same genuine marked mixed parity statistic, still uncontrolled off the base cover.

- [Squarefree rough complements and a three-factor threshold](PrimeOppositeSquarefreeThirdFactor.md): the true rough prime subfamily can also be made squarefree, and if a complement has a factor below $N^\kappa$, $\kappa<1/4$, its other two cannot both sit within the stated fixed power window below their balancing scale. Three factors near the cube root remain outside the estimate.

- [Genuine marked major-arc sign](SmallRadicalG23CoreMajorSign.md): on a width-positive polynomial-factor subcore, the actual mixed statistic restricted to the major arcs has a positive main term too large to discard, even with the true shifted mark. This is not the full signed count; other ranks and minor arcs matter.

## Signed minor arcs and the central operator

- [Fourier-comparable obstruction](SignedMinorFourierObstruction.md): a single alternative weight supported on the primes retains the stated linear and averaged controls, but yields zero convolution on a sparse target sequence. This tests proof inputs, not the true prime weight.
- [Two prescribed small-radical families](PowerFamilyFourierObstruction.md): separate alternative weights omit every power of two or every twice-power of three, while retaining the same square-root Fourier comparisons.
- [Finite-grid approximation](CentralSpectrumQuantization.md), [pointwise localization](CentralPointwiseLocalization.md), and [both signs of the spectrum](CentralParitySpectrumTests.md): relationship between signed window discrepancies and the central operator. These do not bound the discrepancies at an arbitrary target.
- [Quantitative almost-all calibration](CentralQuantitativeAlmostAll.md): classical circle-method control for all moving summand windows outside a small exceptional set, not a bound on its individual members.
