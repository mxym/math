# Complete top prime-modulus alias cancellation using Dirichlet GRH

This note explicitly uses **Dirichlet GRH**, in the scope recorded by [the MIT formal verification](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/). It extends [the unconditional small-alias result](DirectGoldbachPrimeModulusSmallAliasCancellation.md) to the *entire alias range* of the prime-modulus top, including the actual target by a separate upper bound. It also reaches moduli with one large prime and a polylogarithmic cofactor, and those made from two sufficiently large primes. It does not cover the full set of composite moduli, or assert Goldbach positivity.

Let \(X=N\), \(L=\log X\), and \(Q=X^{1-\delta}\) with fixed \(0<\delta\le.01\), and use the smooth interior weights \(w,g\) and the exact constant-kernel delta top of the cited note. Sum its high-input sector over \(Q/L^C\le q\le Q\), before applying any further product mask. Its prime-modulus subset has total \(o(X)\) at every sufficiently large fixed even \(N\). The same holds for the subset of \(q=mr\) with prime \(r\) and \(m\le L^{C_1}\) for fixed \(C_1\), taking the input cutoff \(Q/L^{K_0}\) below \(r\). All assertions apply to the actual log-weighted true prime vectors.

## A power-accurate true ternary alias formula

On a fixed-ratio short prime band of size \(Q'\) with \(Q/L^{O(1)}\ll Q'\ll Q\), use a fixed smooth weight \(u\) in \(q/Q'\), and put \(Y'=X/Q'\). Define \(I(z)=\int w(t)g(1+z-t)\,dt\), a fixed smooth bounded function. In the notation of the previous note, uniformly for every \(1\le |k|\le C_2Y'\), one has
\[
 \sum_{q\ {\rm prime}}(\log q)u(q/Q')R(N+kq)
 =XQ'\,\mathfrak S_k(N)\int u(s)I(ks/Y')\,ds
     +O(XQ'X^{-.015}).
 \tag{1}
\]
There is no restriction on the odd part or radical of \(k\). Fix the short major denominator cutoff \(P=X^{.08}\), with radii \(P/(eQ')\) at reduced \(a/e\) for \(e\le P\). These arcs are disjoint. The classical Vaughan prime bound outside this set, using Dirichlet at scale \(Q'/P\), is \(O(Q'X^{-.04+o(1)})\), also for the smooth weight. Thus outside its preimage under \(-k\alpha\) the integral of the two true long prime polynomials times the short one is \(O(XQ'X^{-.04+o(1)})\) by ordinary Parseval. The preimage is a disjoint union with reduced denominators \(d\) satisfying \(e=d/(d,k)\le P\). Its actual radii are \(P/(|k|eQ')\).

**Long errors on the broader preimage, without a pointwise numerator loss.** At each of these \(d\), consider the true long-prime measure in a unit residue minus the continuous principal measure divided by \(\varphi(d)\). For a length \(h\asymp |k|Q'/P\), the sum over unit residues of the squared short-interval errors is \(O(XL^{O(1)})\): Dirichlet character Parseval divides by \(\varphi(d)\), and each character's two prefix errors are \(O(\sqrt X L^{O(1)})\) by GRH, including the true prime-power removal. Smooth weights follow by partial summation, retaining compact supports, so the windows occur in an interval of total length \(O(X)\). Nonunit long primes do not occur because the prime supports exceed \(d\). Discrete additive Parseval over all numerators and the interval-box form of Gallagher/Plancherel then give, summed in \(a\), \(O(d X^2 h^{-2}L^{O(1)})\) for the squared Fourier error on the radius \(P/(|k|Q')\). Explicitly the physical short-interval squares are integrated over \(O(X)\), multiplied by \(d\) from additive Parseval, and divided by \(h^2\) by the lower box multiplier. This also proves the bound with the actual smaller radii. The principal Fourier model at reduced \(a/d\) is \(\mu(d)V_v(\beta)/\varphi(d)\). The standard GRH prime prefix input and its short-frequency use can be compared with [Liu–Liu–Wang](https://www.numdam.org/article/JTNB_1999__11_1_133_0.pdf); here only the simpler per-character GRH prefix bound is needed.

Each actual \(d\) divides \(ke\) for some \(e\le P\), so there are at most \(PX^{o(1)}\) such denominators and \(\sum d\ll |k|P^2X^{o(1)}\). The total long squared error is therefore \(\ll (Y')^2P^4 X^{o(1)}\ll X^{.34+o(1)}\). The long principal squared norm summed over these arcs is \(O(XL^{O(1)})\), using \(\sum_{d\le X}1/\varphi(d)\ll L^{O(1)}\) and full-line Plancherel for the smooth profile. Multiplying the two long errors or one error and one model, with \(U\ll Q'\) for the true short leg, costs far less than the error in (1), by Cauchy–Schwarz.

**Short principal term and tails.** On any preimage arc the short leg has reduced denominator \(e=d/(d,k)\) and shifted frequency \(|\gamma|\le P/(eQ')\). In its Dirichlet character expansion every Gauss coefficient has magnitude at most \(\sqrt e\), including imprimitive characters. GRH and partial summation bound the short prime model error by \(O(\sqrt{eQ'}(1+P/e)L^{O(1)})\ll\sqrt{Q'}P L^{O(1)}\). No short nonunit prime occurs. Pairing with both long models makes this error smaller than that in (1). Summing the three principal factors over reduced numerators gives the exact arithmetic coefficient
\[
 \mu(d)^2\mu(e)c_d(N)/\bigl(\varphi(d)^2\varphi(e)\bigr).
\]
Its absolutely valued sum with extra factor \(\sqrt e\) is \(O((\log\log(3X))^{O(1)})\): squarefree primes outside \(k\) contribute a summable \(O(p^{-3/2})\), and those dividing \(k\) contribute \(O(1/p)\), whose Euler product has the asserted bound. The \(e>\sqrt P\) tail thus costs \(P^{-1/4} X^{o(1)}=X^{-.02+o(1)}\) relative to \(XQ'\), including any omitted \(e>P\). For \(e\le\sqrt P\) the actual radius times \(X\) is at least a constant times \(\sqrt P\), by \(|k|\le C_2Y'\), and rapid decay of the long profiles completes their real integrals to arbitrary power precision. Fourier inversion gives the exact archimedean expression in (1). Its Euler factors are precisely \(\mathfrak S_k(N)\) of the previous note. All losses fit the exponent \(.015>\delta\). The estimates are uniform under the fixed smooth norms or partial interval cutoffs on the short leg, using Stieltjes partial summation there.

## All signed aliases, including the target term

For prime \(q\) the input projection leaves precisely the residue vector centered on units about the true total. Use its lattice representation \(B_{w,q},B_{g,q}\) from the previous note. For all physical \(|k|\ll Y'\) the mixed/lattice net at \(N+kq\) is
\[
   -X I(kq/X)+O\big((\sqrt X+X/q)L^{O(1)}\big),
\]
uniformly. This uses the GRH true-prime prefix bound for the two smooth mixed weights (at their actual shifted argument), smooth lattice counts, and omission of \(O(X/q+1)\) nonunit positions for each mixed or lattice term. The normalization factor \(q\Theta_v/((q-1)X\int v)=1+O(X^{-1/2}L^{O(1)}+q^{-1})\) has the same harmless power slack. These errors remain \(o(X)\) after summing the full polynomial \(k\)-range and the prime moduli with their \(1/Q\) weight.

For \(k\ne0\), combine (1) with the lattice terms using the actual archimedean weight \(I(kq/X)\). Removing \(\log q\) is done by including \(L/\log q\) in the short weight and an external \(1/L\); its smooth norms are bounded and vary harmlessly on the short scale. The approximation of the lattice sum over prime \(q\) by the integral has the GRH error \(O(\sqrt{Q'}L^{O(1)})\) with the same bounded weight, since \(|k|Q'/X=O(1)\). After the \(1/Q\) normalization, summing the per-alias power-saving errors is still \(o(X)\), with the appropriate factor \(Q'/Q\) on each slice. For the main, [the Euler-product floor calculation](DirectGoldbachPrimeModulusSmallAliasCancellation.md) shows that partial sums of \(\mathfrak S_k(N)-1\), \(1\le k\le M\), are \(O((\log\log(3N))^{O(1)})\) **for all \(M\)**, and the same for negative aliases. Partial summation in \(k\), since \(I(kq/X)\) has bounded total variation across the support, gives \(o(X)\) for the full signed nonzero alias sum on the prime-modulus slice. For \(k=0\), the genuine \(R(N)\ll X\mathfrak S(N)\) by the elementary two-prime upper sieve and the lattice terms are \(O(X)\). This exact unchanged term carries only \(O(1/L)\) total prime-modulus weight, hence is also \(o(X)\). Fixed-ratio dyadic short scales sum geometrically in \(Q'/Q\); the constant-kernel weight \(C_{q/Q}\) and the sharp endpoints have bounded variation on each. This proves the full prime-modulus claim.

For \(q=mr\) with \(m\le L^{C_1}\), use the exact CRT projection to \(sr\), \(s\mid m\), of the previous note. The analog of (1) is applied to the actual coefficient \(ks\) with short scale \(r\asymp Q/m\) up to fixed log factors. Its entire physical range still satisfies \(|ks|\ll X/r\). The mixed/lattice reference becomes \(B_s(N)I(ksr/X)\) using GRH or Siegel–Walfisz at \(s\le L^{C_1}\); use GRH prefix errors to get power slack even after summing polynomially many \(k\). The same partial sums control \(\mathfrak S_{ks}(N)-B_s(N)\) with \(O(B_s(N)(\log\log(3N))^{O(1)})\), uniformly for all cutoffs. The extra weighted sum in \(m,s\) is \(\sum_{m\le L^{C_1}}\sigma(m)/m^2\ll\log L\), and \(B_s(N)\ll(\log\log(3N))^{O(1)}\). Together with the \(1/L\) from the count of \(r\) and the power savings already established, the total remains \(o(X)\), including the \(k=0\) piece by the same sieve upper. No unproved estimate for arbitrary composite \(q\) is imported.


## Complete aliases on moduli with two sufficiently large prime factors

The same choice of parameters also pays the whole high-input slice \(q=rs\), with distinct genuine primes \(r,s\ge X^{1/4}\), \(Q/L^C\le rs\le Q\). Both factors are below the input cutoff \(R_0=Q/L^{K_0}\) and their product is above it. Decompose into fixed-ratio factor boxes of sizes \(R,S\) with \(RS\asymp Q'\), count each \(q\) once, and use the same smooth or literal windows as in the [unconditional small-alias calculation](DirectGoldbachBalancedTwoPrimeAliasCancellation.md).

The power-accurate proof of (1) works with the short polynomial over actual products \(rs\). Indeed the elementary bilinear/geometric-sum bound in that note gives the same \(X^{-.04+o(1)}\) relative minor bound on the complement of \(e\le P=X^{.08}\) since both physical factors are much larger than \(P\). On the short majors, first fix the genuine \(r\) and use the GRH Gauss expansion for the \(s\)-polynomial at phase \(ra/e+r\gamma\). There are no nonunit \(r\)'s, since \(r>P\). Its error per \(r\) is \(O(\sqrt{eS}(1+P/e)L^{O(1)})\), using \(|\gamma|RS\ll P/e\), and uniform in any literal cut endpoint. The remaining outer prime \(r\)-sum paired with the continuous principal profile is approximated by the ordinary GRH prime PNT at scale \(R\), with weight variation \(O(1+P/e)\) for each fixed logarithmic window. Relative to \(RS\), the two substitutions cost \(O(P L^{O(1)}(R^{-1/2}+S^{-1/2}))=O(X^{-.045+o(1)})\). The principal factor remains \(\mu(e)/\varphi(e)\) and the short Archimedean measure is the pushforward of the continuous window by \(r,s\mapsto rs\). The order cut \(r<s\), when needed, gives a uniform moving endpoint; on a near-diagonal box its contribution to the integrated variation is bounded, and on other boxes it is inactive. The long-factor arcs and estimates are unchanged, as is the singular tail. Thus, with \(q=rs\), the short integral in (1) is now over continuous \(r,s\) and carries the literal weight \(I(krs/X)\). Removing the two prime logarithms costs two factors \(1/L\) with bounded normalized variations. Approximating the corresponding unweighted moduli count weighted by \(I(krs/X)\) uses GRH PNT in both variables and costs only relative \(X^{-1/8+o(1)}\). This has enough power slack to sum over the entire polynomial alias range.

To avoid multiplying a maximal CRT-centering error by all aliases, compare centered **total** congruence forms first. Let \(A_v^0(a;q)=\Theta_v(a;q)-\Theta_v/\varphi(q)\) on units, and \(E_v\) be the exact double-centered high-input vector from the small two-prime note. Their difference is the sum of its two one-factor centered marginals, lifted to \(q\) with denominators \(s-1,r-1\). GRH gives \(\sum_a|\Theta_v(a;r)-\Theta_v/\varphi(r)|^2\ll XL^{O(1)}\), and likewise at \(s\), by Dirichlet character Parseval and the individual square-root GRH prefix bounds. Thus
\[
 \|E_v-A_v^0\|_2\ll \sqrt X L^{O(1)}\min(r,s)^{-1/2}.
\]
Trivial prime occupancy at modulus \(q\) gives \(\|A_v^0\|_2^2\ll X(X/q+1)L^{O(1)}\). Reflection \(a\mapsto N-a\), restricted to units on both sides, has operator norm at most one. Hence the difference of the **whole** reflected congruence forms is
\[
 \ll X L^{O(1)}
   \left(\sqrt{(X/q+1)/\min(r,s)}+\min(r,s)^{-1}\right),
\]
which is power-small compared with \(X\) under the stated exponents. This comparison is made on the \(d=q\) term after the proper pushforwards of the actual high vectors have already vanished; it is not assumed to commute with selection of individual aliases. Summing with the original \(1/Q\) factor and bounded physical kernel leaves \(o(X)\).

The ordinary centered vector \(A_v^0\) is synthesized by the unit lattice with constant density \(q\Theta_v/(\varphi(q) X\int v)\), to power accuracy by Poisson. For every literal alias use the same mixed/lattice main \(-XI(kq/X)\); omission of positions divisible by \(r\) or \(s\) costs at most \(O((X/r+X/s+1)L^{O(1)})\) per alias, still power-small after \(O(X/Q')\) aliases. The other errors are GRH square-root PNT and the vanishing difference of the density from one. Therefore for \(k\ne0\) the full signed total is negligible by the same Euler-product partial-sum bound with its actual archimedean weight. The result has \(1/L^2\) per factor box instead of \(1/L\); there are \(O(L)\) aspect-ratio boxes per product scale and geometric product-scale summation, so the combined bound is still \(o(X)\). The \(k=0\) term again uses only the true upper sieve and the \(O(1/L)\) total weight of all these two-prime moduli. No claim is made about arbitrary \(q\), or about individual-alias invariance under the replaced projection.
