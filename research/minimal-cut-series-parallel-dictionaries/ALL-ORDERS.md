# All orders and the treewidth-two specialization

The standard block characterization says a graph has treewidth at most two if and only if every block is two-terminal series-parallel for a suitable choice of its terminals (isolated vertices/bridges excepted in the usual way). Equivalently these are the graphs excluding a K4 minor. See [Cosme-Llópez and Pous, 2017, introduction](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol083-mfcs2017/LIPIcs.MFCS.2017.76/LIPIcs.MFCS.2017.76.pdf). No fixed root choices for the whole graph are being imposed.

By the TTSP bound proved in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md), the rate in this larger class is also exactly η=3^(1/3), with a limit over **all n**, not just a subsequence. For a connected graph on n≥2, glue its blocks successively along cut vertices. For a vertex sum A ⋁_w B of two nontrivial connected graphs, c(A ⋁ B)≤c(A)+c(B)+1: a minimal disconnecting cut containing w must equal {w}. For S avoiding w, if both factors remain connected then so does the union. Thus some factor, say A, is disconnected after deletion of S∩A, and removing only S∩A disconnects the whole, since B attaches to A only at w. Minimality gives S⊂V(A), and no smaller subset could disconnect A because it would also disconnect the union. This also handles ties and trivial connected graphs separately as needed. Consequently c(G)≤sum_B c(B)+number_of_blocks−1. There are at most n−1 nontrivial blocks (sum(|B|−1)=n−1). Each has c(B)≤C(|B|+1)η^(|B|−2) by [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) with just the edge atom. Hence c(G)≤O(n²)η^n; disconnected graphs contribute at most the single empty cut. The standard melon with floor((n−2)/3) length-four paths in parallel, plus (n−2 mod 3) additional length-two paths, has n vertices and ≥3^floor((n−2)/3) clean/global cuts. This achieves the reverse liminf. The case of extremely small n is irrelevant.

Likewise in the arbitrary finite-menu series/parallel theorem with the bare-edge seed available, the formula there is a full limit for orderwise maxima: choose a positive-interior seed B with h_B=ρ^q and q=p_B (include the three-interior path as a candidate). For any n≥q+2 write n−2=rq+j, 0≤j<q; take r copies in parallel and pad with j two-edge paths, each with exactly one clean cut. The graph has exactly n vertices and at least ρ^(rq) clean, hence also global, cuts. Because j is bounded, taking nth roots yields the required lower limit. The upper bound in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) has the same limiting base. This does not enlarge the theorem on arbitrary fixed treewidth beyond width two.

The 2025 [Gomes, Nogueira, dos Santos paper](https://www.rairo-ro.org/articles/ro/pdf/2025/01/ro230078.pdf) analyzes the structure/reconfiguration of marked vertex separators in 2-connected SP graphs, which already have exponential examples; distinguish this from a sharp exponential base for **global** inclusion-minimal cuts. Seymour's extremal benchmark and lower witness predate this argument. As [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) explains, the planar, edge-only TTSP and treewidth-two rates are already implicit in prior results.
