# Source and priority comparison

1. V. Livanos and R. Mehta, [*Minimization is Harder in the Prophet World*, SODA 2024](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977912.17), [formal proceedings, full PDF](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10511091). Section 5, first question on p. 451, compares one threshold with unrestricted stage-dependent thresholds and asks about an intermediate budget of at most $k$. Their one-threshold rule still accepts the final draw if necessary. For an Entire cumulative hazard with leading term $a x^d$, the lower quantile is asymptotic to $(u/a)^{1/d}$. The power-logarithmic order with one cutoff, and the matching order on a power-hazard example, are prior. The present result answers the stated budget question for regularly varying lower quantiles: it gives the fixed-$k$ asymptotic and, at positive index, the growing-budget complexity. It does not promise a universal guarantee across all finite-mean laws. Compare the [earlier preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07988v3).
2. Livanos and Mehta, [*Minimization I.I.D. Prophet Inequality via Extreme Value Theory: A Unified Approach*, EC 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3736252.3742682), [deposited ACM proceedings PDF, pp. 1157–1179](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10659245); [authors' full version](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.19851v1). The formal Theorem 1.5 is on p. 1165 and restated on p. 1173: its upper bound is for each fixed distribution in $D_\gamma$, $\gamma<0$, of order $(\log n)^{-\gamma}$. The paragraph preceding Theorem 1.5 on p. 1165 and Remark 4.1 on p. 1174 assert the constant-competitive single-threshold conclusion for $D_0$. Definition 1.2 uses a nondegenerate affine minimum limit; our two examples verify that condition directly. The passage to Eq. (19) in §4.1 of the full version uses regular variation at a multiplier chosen to grow with $n$; this is where retaining the ratio of quantiles matters. The ACM record describes a polylogarithmic guarantee in the abstract: our $D_{-r}$ example still has logarithmic exponent $r+o(1)$, and specifically contradicts the sharper displayed big-O. The unrestricted optimum (Theorem 1.3), the one-threshold worst-case lower bound and the multi-unit theorem are not the targets of these counterexamples. The known unrestricted limit is used as a benchmark, with a direct verification included here.
3. The one-threshold expected-cost decomposition already appears in those sources. Karamata's theorem, compact uniform convergence and the Potter bounds used here are standard facts from Bingham, Goldie and Teugels, [*Regular Variation*](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511721434), §§1.2, 1.5. No new regular-variation or general extreme-value theorem is claimed.
4. **Adjacent resources, not this budget.** Y. Azar, I. Biran and A. Fiat, [*Multi Choice Min Prophet*, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05085), [ESA 2026 formal paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ESA.2026.55), allow several selections and charge the least selected value; here exactly one cost is paid. [*The Competition Complexity of Prophet Inequalities*](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.2024.0684) studies extra independent copies in reward maximization, including block-threshold policies; the present model adds no samples. [*Optimal single-threshold stopping rules and sharp prophet inequalities*](https://doi.org/10.1017/jpr.2026.10117) also compares rewards to a maximum. Full-information secretary [expected-rank thresholds](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-applied-probability/article/abs/secretary-problem-minimizing-the-expected-rank-with-iid-random-variables/ACD24C14604B33115B97292FF291C3D9) concern rank, not expected cardinal cost.
5. **Earlier owner material.** [#237](../fixed-law-min-prophet-counterexample/) gives fixed bounded laws with divergent *unrestricted* ratio, and distinguishes a law outside EVT from the EC model. [#239](../min-prophet-fixed-law-criterion/) gives the nonrecursive criterion for boundedness of that unrestricted ratio and a zero-atom classification. Neither studies the complexity of a threshold budget. The two examples in this work lie *inside* the stated EVT domains; it is the restricted rule that fails.
