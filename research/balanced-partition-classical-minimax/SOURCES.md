# Primary sources and scope

* Bartosz Regula and Hao-Chung Cheng, [Sharp one-shot bounds and optimal exponents of universal randomness extraction, arXiv:2611.33270v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33270v1), 24 November 2026. §II.2 specifies the public-seed distance; §IV.5, Lemma 16 and Eqs. (145)–(146), uses random balanced bins, proves collision-two-universality and bounds a specified source by hashing separately on its known uniform-layer classes. The balanced-hashing construction and its usefulness on flat inputs are prior art. The result here is instead a finite source-oblivious minimax identity for unknown relabelings, with the exact value and a classical conditional guessing-probability constraint. The paper derives a source-specific i.i.d. type-class exponent in (147) and explicitly notes difficulties beyond the no-side-information case. This finite theorem neither extends their source-specific exponent to general side information nor answers the paper's question about weakest collision assumptions for their quantum converse or cover fixed 2* or 4* families.
* Jaikumar Radhakrishnan and Amnon Ta-Shma, [Bounds for Dispersers, Extractors, and Depth-Two Superconcentrators](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~amnon/Papers/RT.siam00.pdf), SIAM J. Discrete Math. 13(1), 2000, §2.1–2.2, Theorem 1.9 and Appendix B. Random subsets, hypergeometric estimates and the flat-source reduction are classical tools in extractor lower bounds. Their theorem controls seed size and entropy loss asymptotically, with constant-factor slack in error; it is not this exact unlimited-seed minimax computation.
* Yevgeniy Dodis, Rafail Ostrovsky, Leonid Reyzin, and Adam Smith, [Fuzzy Extractors: How to Generate Strong Keys from Biometrics and Other Noisy Data](https://cs-web.bu.edu/fac/reyzin/papers/fuzzy.pdf), SIAM J. Comput. 38(1), 2008, §2.4–2.5, Definition 2 and Lemma 2.4. The average conditional min-entropy and average-case strong extractor model are standard; their generalized leftover hash lemma gives an upper bound, not the finite optimal guessing-budget curve. Unlike their efficient definition we do not constrain the seed length or impose an algorithmic design goal.
* Marco Tomamichel, Christian Schaffner, Adam Smith, Renato Renner, [Leftover Hashing Against Quantum Side Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2436), IEEE TIT 57(8), 2011, DOI [10.1109/TIT.2011.2158473](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2011.2158473). Eqs. (15)–(16) are a standard source for including the public seed; their universal-hashing bound has a different scope from the exact classical minimax result. No claim about quantum advantage is made here.
* This repository has separate notes on [full-list versus selected-key guarantees](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/8b07ee5d6d29575c8d1f27cb76d1c0d7f078f5d9/research/public-seed-list-privacy-amplification) and on [exact three-collision assumptions](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/8b07ee5d6d29575c8d1f27cb76d1c0d7f078f5d9/research/three-collision-privacy-amplification). The former is a different output-model analysis; the latter optimizes in a restricted collision-order model. Neither finite hypergeometric minimax over all maps nor the conditional classical guessing-probability curve is asserted there.

A bounded literature search through 27 November 2026 for exact hypergeometric extractor error, minimax balanced hashing, and conditional flat-source error found the above near precedents. The elementary preimage-count balancing and flat-layer decomposition are not claimed new in isolation, and no priority claim is made.
