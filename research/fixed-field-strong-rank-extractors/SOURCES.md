# Primary sources and bounded comparison

- Zeyu Guo, Roshan Raj, Chong Shangguan and Zihan Zhang, [*Explicit Rank Extractors and Subspace Designs via Function Fields, with Applications to Strong Blocking Sets*, arXiv:2604.13431v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13431v2). Section 1 gives the indexed strong `sum of deficiencies` definition with equal output and test dimensions. Section 7 item 5 asks only for a weak fixed-field extractor with success at least `2^{-o(r)}`. Appendix A already proves strong **nonexplicit** existence by independent uniform matrices and a union bound. Section 7 item 1 seeks a much smaller aggregate budget at variable codimension: the present linear-ambient, exponential-rank lists with aggregate loss proportional to their own length do not solve it. Section 2 already notes full-row-rank filtering. Section 6.2 uses small bias and fiber indicators for blocking sets, a different guarantee.
- The [pinned weak package](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/d0ba6f9a1d42bf94b915506f320cefe5e6490729/research/fixed-field-weak-rank-extractors) supplies the very same Moore representation, labelled samplers and parameter choices of Theorem A. The additional result is a bound on the actual expected deficiency, proved via a dimension majorant rather than by inferring it from the probability of failure.
- Gil Cohen, Dean Doron and Noam Goldgraber, [*Algebraic-Geometric Parvaresh–Vardy Subspace Designs and Rank Condensers*, arXiv:2609.32834v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32834v2#S4.SS3). Corollary 4.7 controls aggregate loss with a bound proportional to the test dimension at fixed field. Corollary 4.9 gives exact output dimension with lossless success, requiring the field to exceed rank divided by tolerated failure. The comparison here addresses the regime of fixed field and growing rank, not their polynomial-size bounds in the applicable regimes.
- Rohan Goyal, Venkatesan Guruswami and Jun-Ting Hsieh, [*Explicit Constant-Alphabet Subspace Design Codes*, arXiv:2604.15218](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15218), Theorem 1.1, already controls aggregate intersection but over coordinate alphabets of substantially larger dimension than the test rank. Venkatesan Guruswami, Chaoping Xing and Chen Yuan, [*Subspace Designs Based on Algebraic Function Fields*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.05992), Theorems 1.2 and 4.2, give related effective strong designs, with a codimension gap or bounds that do not yield the constant average loss claimed here at fixed field and exact output dimension. Michael Forbes and Venkatesan Guruswami, [*Dimension Expanders via Rank Condensers*, ECCC TR14-162](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/162/), Proposition 8.5, use extension-to-base reduction with an increase in output dimension.
- Akhil Jalan and Dana Moshkovitz, [*Near-Optimal Cayley Expanders for Abelian Groups*, arXiv:2105.01149v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.01149v1), Theorem 1.1, character diagonalization and §3.4: fixed-alphabet near-optimal small bias. Proposition 4.5 uses the single linear zero-test consequence in a matrix-checking application. Victor Shoup, [*New Algorithms for Finding Irreducible Polynomials over Finite Fields*](https://www.shoup.net/papers/detirred.pdf), Math. Comp. 54 (1990), 435–447, supplies deterministic polynomial time for fixed characteristic. No improved small-bias or irreducibility algorithm is claimed.
- Noga Alon, Oded Goldreich, Johan Håstad and René Peralta, [*Simple Constructions of Almost k-wise Independent Random Variables*](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.3240030308), Construction 3 ([author copy](https://johanhastad.se/almostindep.pdf)), gives the binary inner-product/power construction; the direct-entry fallback is its prime-field analogue with the same polynomial-root proof.
- Jason Fulman and Larry Goldstein, [*Stein's Method and the Rank Distribution of Random Matrices over Finite Fields*, arXiv:1211.0504](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.0504), analyze the **uniform** matrix nullity law and its limiting distribution. Philippe Delsarte, [*Bilinear Forms Over a Finite Field, with Applications to Coding Theory*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(78)90015-8), and Maximilien Gadouleau and Zhiyuan Yan, [*MacWilliams Identity for Codes with the Rank Metric*](https://mrgadouleau.webspace.durham.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/187/2021/04/GY_EURASIP2008-macwilliams.pdf), §4.1, treat rank distributions and Gaussian-binomial moments. These are antecedents for the moment language, not claims of new moment identities.

Bounded public search covered fixed-field strong lossless extraction, exact output dimension, strong subspace designs, small bias and linear tests, and nullity/binomial moment comparisons. It did not locate an identical stated pair of uniform-statistic approximations for low-dimensional restrictions of one short fixed-field family. This is not a priority claim. The tools (Gaussian binomial identities, Newton interpolation, additive characters, Moore nonsingularity) are standard; the contribution is the concrete parameter combination, including the sharp distinctions between weak, strong and two-sided claims. No outside inputs are needed by the finite checks.
