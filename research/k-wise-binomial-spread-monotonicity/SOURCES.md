# Sources and bounded priority check

**Original question.** [Berend, Ernst, Kontorovich and Kumar, *Exact expressions for the maximal probability that all k-wise independent bits are 1*, 26 July 2024, arXiv:2407.18688v1, §5.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.18688v1#S5.SS5); [open journal version, J. Theor. Probab. 39, 58, published 29 May 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10959-026-01487-4). In both versions §5 assumes even $2\le k\le n-1$ and counts only bases whose feasibility interval has positive length. Immediately before Conjecture 5.12 the maximum is over those bases, with the distinguished site excluded. Tables 4–7 give empirical checks in restricted ranges. This note proves that very maximum is nondecreasing at every step in $n$. It is not monotonicity of a basis at fixed $p$, or of the LP objective, or over arbitrary moment sequences. The optional trivial $n=k$ endpoint is outside the §5 domain.

**Prior tools vs. result.** Paired dual supports and the moment LP are classical: [Prékopa (1988)](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.36.1.145), [Peled–Yadin–Yehudayoff, §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.0059), and BEKK. For interval moment data on the integer lattice, [Infusino–Kuna–Lebowitz–Speer, §§6.1–6.2](https://cmsr.rutgers.edu/images/people/lebowitz_joel/publications/599.pdf) give Gaussian-node comparisons and fixed-moment support selectors. Such selectors do not by themselves compare the maximal spread across two distinct binomial grids and all positive-length phases. [Hermann, arXiv:2609.25595v1, Theorem 1, Lemma 2 and Corollary 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25595v1) gives support reflection, uniqueness of the extremal count law and local exceptional-point reflection for fixed $n,k$, as well as the binomial reweighting identity. This is symmetry in $p$, not the comparison between $n$ and $n+1$. We claim neither a new LP reduction nor a new definition of spread.

[The repository phase theorem, #220](../k-wise-binomial-optimal-bases/PROOF.md), provides the feasibility and closed-phase coverage used in the proof. Its [four-wise classification](../k-wise-binomial-optimal-bases/FOUR.md) already proves 5.12 for $k=4$ by the stronger exact formula (5.13); $k=2$ is immediate. Its [spread asymptotic](../k-wise-binomial-optimal-bases/SPREAD.md) contains a bounded error and explicitly separates the fixed-order leading asymptotic from the one-step assertion. [#223](../k-wise-binomial-exact-basis-moves/MOVES.md) identifies elementary changes in $p$ for the same grid, including simultaneous changes; it does not compare maximum spread across grid sizes and is not an input here. The additional scope of the present proof is finite one-step monotonicity for all even $k\ge6$.

No later version or formal publication establishing this all-even-$k$ assertion was located. This is a bounded search: absence from an index does not establish priority.
