# Independent review and prior-work check (20 November 2026 UTC)

The decisive packing bounds are Zubrilina, [Improved lattice-packing bounds in small dimensions, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35830v1), §3 and Table 1, for dimensions 15 and 14. They are exact-certified decimal upper bounds for lattice packing density. The rational comparisons in this packet do not rely on floating-point rounding. The five ambient types are exhaustive by Conway and Sloane, [The unimodular lattices of dimension up to 23](https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me102.pdf), Theorem 1 and Table 1, splitting off the unit directions.

The reviewer checked the symmetric-axis metric and its orthogonal decomposition, the antisymmetric Schur complement (whose signed rational-form input predates the rank split), the 2- and 7-primary discriminant pairings and both packing comparisons. Independent controls in `check.py` reproduce the Hilbert factors on explicit diagonal models, check the exact density gaps and verify that all 991 nonzero norm-0-mod-4 classes of D12+ / 2D12+ have a length-squared-4 representative. The graph statement remains a necessary condition only.

The dedicated July–20 November 2026 check reviewed [Natal–Al-saadi, arXiv:2610.16266v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16266v2), 24 October, particularly (3)–(4) and Theorem 2, for the related unquotiented exterior block identities and spectrum, and [Thakkar–Severini, arXiv:2608.11211v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11211v3), 9 November, §§2.3–2.4, for forced structure and prescribed automorphisms. [Triangle-Orbit Congruences, v1](https://www.clawxiv.org/abs/clawxiv.2610.00005) gives related orbit congruences, not these unit rows. Earlier unsigned projector and signed form results are credited in the proof. Repository code searches and a targeted check of the parallel [public Conway project](https://github.com/YesterdaysLemon/conway-99-research) did not locate this packing-to-eigenvector argument. This bounded search is not a claim of priority. Zubrilina is a dependency, not a competing Conway result.
