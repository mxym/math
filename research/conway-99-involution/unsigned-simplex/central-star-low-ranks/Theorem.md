# Proof and replay design

## Input and orientation

Let the unique fixed vertex be o. Its fourteen neighbors occur in seven switched adjacent pairs (axes). Each exterior orbit of the involution has two axis parents for a positive representative and the involuted parents for its negative mate; there are two orbit labels on each of the 21 underlying axis pairs. Put B equal to the 7 by 42 unsigned endpoint incidence matrix and Z to its signed version: the columns on ij, i<j, are e_i+e_j and e_i−e_j. For distinct exterior orbit labels put T=0,1,2 for the number of links from one representative to the other orbit and L=0,±1,0 respectively, the sign in the single-link case recording like/opposite representatives. Both matrices are symmetric and have diagonal 0. Use the positive semidefinite signed/unsigned Gram matrices F=12I−4L−ZᵀZ and H=6I−2T−BᵀB+J. The signed mod-7 quotient has rank at most 13; in rank 13 the nondegenerate quotient has square determinant character. These are the prior signed prerequisites. The simplex theorem gives H_xy=(p_x,p_y) where p_y is a signed five-circuit in Z^15 in its prescribed simplex quotient class; each class has exactly six possible supports. Distinct labels on the same underlying ij may use equal supports.

Fix one central axis and orient each of its 12 incident outer labels toward its positive member. Their edges at the positive central member and at its negative lift give the ordered pair of perfect matchings P,N on the 12 labels: P records like links, N opposite links. Indeed the positive members adjacent to the positive central member must induce a perfect matching (its 12 nonroot/nonaxis-mate neighbors come in pairs); each positive representative has exactly one negative anchor neighbor since it has two common neighbors with the nonadjacent negative central member, one already the positive central member. The involution forces the second matching. The common endpoint involution M swaps the two labels of any of the six peripheral axes. The 12-blocks are
  H_A=5I−M−2P−2N, F_A=11I+M−4P+4N−J.
In particular F_A has zero row sum; rank≤11. We enumerate all ordered P,N up to the 2^6·6! label symmetries preserving M, via the exact catalogue with independent Burnside counts. The counts by rank 7,8,9,10,11 are respectively 3,5,97,637,2838. Physical star consists of o, its 14 neighbors, and the two vertices of each of the 12 orbit labels (39 in total).

What about the numbering of the Hamming cosets? The intrinsic 28 norm-2 classes are the edges of K8. Omitting one index gives seven used axes; the column classes are the complements of the 21 corresponding K7 edge classes. Coordinates in F₂^4 label the 15 positions. Recompute all 128 elements of C^⊥/C, 28 six-support sets and orbit maps for all 20,160 invertible binary 4 by 4 matrices: their induced K8 permutations are **all of A8**, and the ordered omitted/central-pair stabilizer induces precisely A6 on the remaining six axes (slabels-check.py). Thus we can fix the omitted and central axes, and must test both parities of the remaining numbering. Peripheral sign flips merely relabel the two twists at each central underlying edge, with the remote single-link signs reoriented accordingly; all such attachments and signs are tested. No graph automorphism other than the hypothesized involution is used. All subsets of signs of each five-support are generated, and new ambient coordinates at their first use are set positive by the independent simultaneous signs of the standard 15-space.

## A star and an extra orbit

For each parity enumerate all 12-word assignments realizing H_A, using the actual integer ±1 dot on the intersection of the two word supports. The explicit second counter sstar-independent.cpp uses independent scalar products/ordering and counts the bare stars (without exterior restrictions). For each outside label y enumerate all pairs of two distinct positive and two distinct negative anchors and the two prescribed parents; the two anchor 2-subsets cannot be identical since y and its mate may have at most two common neighbors. Both 2+2 counts hold since y is adjacent to neither central member, and the two common neighbors it needs with each central member must be in its 12 incident exterior neighbors. Construct the resulting physical 41-vertex graph, or equivalently enforce within it that each pair has at most 2−[adjacent] common neighbors. If anchor i already has a double (P_i=N_i), y cannot spend a second one there: the two vertices on i share exactly two common neighbors globally, already used by that double. Record t_i=a_i+b_i and l_i=a_i−b_i, with a_i,b_i the two direction mask bits.

The outside word must have dot u_i=1−|z_i·z_y|−2t_i with each anchor, where those two different underlying edges intersect in at most one endpoint. Its signed anchor column is f_i=−z_i·z_y−4l_i. Necessarily u,f belong to the rational ranges of their respective blocks and their projected norm costs are at most 5 and 10. The unsigned block is the weighted Laplacian of the graph on M,P,N (components its kernel); the signed block has kernel exactly constants, since 11I+M−4P+4N is a positive matrix in the quadratic-form sense (its smallest eigenvalue is ≥2) and removing J kills its constant. Add the all-ones block separately on each kernel component, invert by fraction-free elimination, check zero component sums and the Schur diagonal. All arithmetic is integral except modular inversion at 7. Early prefix pruning at six/nine words demands an outside word and a domain supporting each prescribed prefix; it never demands compatibility among independently relaxed labels.

At rank≤9 the unary test leaves 94 pairs of chart and parity: 2 at rank 7 (#288), 6 at rank 8 (#306,#620,#2264), and 86 at rank 9. Both orientations are separately checked. The other nine of the 105 underlying charts rejected as physical bases are counted once, not twice.

## The common residual space

The signed row sum relation on A lets us use the first 11 as rational generators of all 12 anchor vectors. Modulo 7, let r=rank(F_A) and choose r independent columns; their principal block Q is nonsingular: write F_A as the Gram on its nondegenerate rank-r column quotient. The pivot columns form a basis there. In the actual signed quotient the pivots therefore span a nondegenerate r-space. Work in a nondegenerate 13-space with square determinant: if the actual quotient has dimension less than 13, it can be enlarged by an orthogonal nonsingular summand of the requisite dimension and determinant. This is not a claim that the actual rank always equals 13. Complement V has dimension 13−r and determinant character equal to that of Q.

Write h_y[j]=f_y[j]−F_A[j,I] Q^−1 f_y[I] for the 11−r nonpivot anchors j among the first 11. These are the pairings with their residual vectors, which are mutually orthogonal null vectors, since F_A had rank r. For a **single fixed global realization** all h_y are in the image of the one common map from V defined by these residual vectors. Its rank is at most the dimension of a totally isotropic subspace. For the rank-seven survivor the local pivot determinant is 1, so dim V=6 is nonsplit (−1 is nonsquare in F7), Witt index 2. At rank eight, dim V=5 implies Witt index 2. At rank nine, dim V=4: nonsquare determinant implies Witt index 1; square determinant gives Witt index 2. These elementary finite-field classifications follow by splitting hyperbolic planes until a ≤2-dimensional anisotropic part remains.

Enumerate the single subspace W containing all h_y: all 2-spaces of F7^4 (2850) or F7^3 (57) when r=7,8; if r=9 and the residual anchor image has dimension ≤1, every line in F7² (8). Smaller images, including zero, are contained. W is chosen ONCE, not once per outside label. Discard a W if any of the 30 labels lacks any choice. At r=7 this already kills both orientations. For each remaining W choose one candidate for each of five distinct outside labels by smallest-domain backtracking; require every still-unselected label admit a candidate with all pair constraints relative to the selected labels. For opposite directions try every sign allowed by the unsigned dot and the actual 43-vertex cap (base plus the 2+2 members of a pair of outside labels). Define the residual Gram on all anchor radicals and selected extras by the blocks 0,H,Hᵀ,N, where H contains their h columns and N_xy=−4L_xy−z_x·z_y−f_x[I]ᵀ Q^−1 f_y[I] off-diagonal (diagonal 10−projection cost). Its rank is ≤13−r, and equality implies determinant character of V on a nonsingular principal pivot minor. Smaller ranks are not rejected on character grounds. All signs toward previously selected labels are enumerated coherently on the selected clique; independent future-support tests weaken requirements.

For square r=9 only, there is another case: the two residual anchor vectors may be independent. They form a basis of a maximal isotropic plane. Complete them to a hyperbolic basis: choose dual vectors, then subtract their half Gram times those anchor vectors to make the dual plane isotropic (2 is invertible). An exterior residual vector then has coordinates (u,h) with u,h in F7², 2u·h=10−f[I]ᵀQ^−1 f[I]. Enumerate every u for each known h and norm. The unseen signed link between two residuals prescribes u·h′+u′·h plus the pivot correction; test this and the same physical cap. At the first branch only, the shear u↦u+sJh with J the alternating 2 by 2 matrix fixes all anchor residuals, all cross products and h. It is transitive on the seven u of a nonzero h and fixed norm. If h=0 retain **all** u. Enumerate up to eight selected outside labels with independent continuing support in the remainder. A full graph necessarily passes this relaxation. Split enumeration does not assume a positive-definite binary residual plane or an ambient rank-11 bound.

## Result

The complete uncapped records, including overrides for every capped run, have no surviving necessary clique on any tested chart/parity/mode. The 64 nonsplit rank-nine chart-parities use the common line test; the 22 split chart-parities require **both** the ≤1 line and the independent-pair hyperbolic test. Rank 7 and 8 are eliminated by their own plane tests, not by any rank-9 argument. Together with the full unary catalogue this proves the conditional rank 10/11 assertion at any central axis. It does not settle the full order-two branch. Only reported runtimes are floating point.
