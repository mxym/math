# Scope and reproducibility

The finite conclusion covers every central label choice and both orientations. Among 3,580 local charts, 105 have candidate rank at most nine; 9 of these fail the physical-base tests. The remaining filter leaves 94 chart-parities: 8 of rank at most eight, 64 nonsplit rank-nine, and 22 split rank-nine. Every split case requires both the small-image and independent isotropic-pair tests. The two costly orientations of chart 333 were exhausted in 42 disjoint uncapped blocks over 20,848 embeddings. The range and coverage audit compares the terminal counts with a separately written bare-star counter. Any pilot with a cap must have its complete uncapped replacement; none of the caps proves a negative result. A fresh extraction passed all bundled checksums and `bash replay.sh quick`, including group-action, coverage and signed controls. Only the full replay reruns every terminal.

The earlier [global rank result](../../seven/README.md) concerns the entire 42-label Gram (11 or 13), while the [unsigned simplex reduction](../README.md) finds a lattice model, not this signed principal rank bound. We checked the current repository and recent sources through 21 November 2026, including [Thakkar–Severini v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11211v3), [Natal–Al-saadi v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16266v2) and [triangle-orbit congruences v1](https://www.clawxiv.org/abs/clawxiv.2610.00005). The separate [opposite-star research map](https://www.proofatlas.ai/collaboration/conways-99-graph-conjecture/) reports a different local block in the unrestricted residual approach, not this 12-label principal block of the involution. [Zubrilina v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35830v1) is an upstream input to the unsigned lattice theorem. None of these comparisons is a priority claim.
