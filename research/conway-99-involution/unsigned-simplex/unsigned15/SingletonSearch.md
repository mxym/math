# Singleton-cut root: exact coupled signed and unsigned extension tests

This finite test is conditional on a putative (99,14,1,2) graph with a one-fixed-point involution. The unsigned and signed exterior character coordinates are **different** forms. We use H=6I−2T−BᵀB+J with positive rational form I₁₅, and F=12I−4L−ZᵀZ with positive rational form of square determinant, rank 15 over Q and rank ≤13 mod 7. The earlier [signed seven-adic theorem](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/main/research/conway-99-involution/seven) obtains those properties of F prior to division into its rank-11/13 modular alternatives. No part below assumes global modular rank exactly 13.

**Hypothesis tested.** There is y∈L₀, y²=2, such that x_j=(p_j,y) is odd exactly on all 12 labels A incident to a fixed axis. RootsBridge.md proves that x_A has six +1 and six −1, and there are exactly two +2 and two −2 labels outside A. Only those 16 labels support x. The full 42 coordinates satisfy Bx=0, Tx=−4x, and 14y=∑x_jp_j. Choose the cut's singleton as axis 0 before fixing the chart. There are two labeled matchings P,N on A with T_A=P+N and L_A=P−N; the mate involution i↦i xor 1 pairs labels on the same K₇ edge. The 3,580 canonical matching charts are complete up to permutations of peripheral axes and changing their signs, actual choices of representatives in this model. Inside a fixed chart the colored stabilizer preserves all three matchings. Its elements are exactly the permutations generated by trying the possible images of one unmapped vertex and propagating through all three fixed neighbors (symstar.hpp). Outside labels relabel accordingly (changing signed representatives if needed); B and the unsigned row x have no additional sign. Thus one may normalize x|A up to the colored stabilizer and overall negation. auditroot.cpp independently enumerates the whole signed-permutation group of order 46,080 for each chart, derives the 39-point feasibility, computes canonical sign orbits by minimum image, and verifies all range and Schur cases from direct Laplacian cofactors.

**Root signs and simultaneous matching.** In a star sign word g, let s_i=±1 and a_i be the number of neighbors of i of its own sign in T_A, with multiplicity. E_same=(∑a_i)/2∈{0,2}, and a heavy vertex of either sign has at most min(1,2−E_same) own-sign weighted neighbors on A. This follows from RootsBridge.md; it uses the unique double and the equality in the four-heavy root budget. The star Gram H_A=5I−Dmate−2P−2N is the Laplacian of a nonnegative multigraph, with nullspace spanned by component indicators. Let J_comp be the direct sum of all-ones component blocks, and I/D=(H_A+J_comp)⁻¹. On each component-sum-zero column this is the inverse used for orthogonal projection. First require g perpendicular to the radical and a=2D−gᵀIg≥0. At a=0, the following Cauchy test is still used without any division.

For each of the 30 remaining labels d, enumerate every possible positive-member link pattern to the 12 anchor pairs and the two prescribed peripheral axis vertices. Each attachment has two same and two opposite links, encoded by two 2-subsets (duplicates between the sets mean a double), as in the earlier [signed local chart construction](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/main/research/conway-99-involution/seven). Require the ≤2−adjacency physical common-neighbor caps and the signed and unsigned one-column real PSD requirements (with the appropriate per-form nullspace condition). Keep **every signed lift**, even where several have the same unsigned T-column m_d. The unsigned star cross Gram column f_d has entries 1−B_i·B_d−2T_id. If d is to be heavy of sign t=±1, Cauchy after projecting the root and p_d off the star gives
  (2tD−gᵀ I f_d)²≤(2D−gᵀIg)(5D−f_dᵀI f_d).
Include equality, and require the same-sign star-contact bound above.

Choose two distinct positive heavy labels u₁,u₂ and two distinct negative labels u₃,u₄ (all four distinct). Each attachment's key comprises its 12 nonnegative unsigned T entries on A followed by the 6 peripheral incidences B_{a,d}. The sum of the positive keys minus the sum of the negative keys is prescribed by
- its star entry at i: −g_i(1+a_i), from T x=−4x on A;
- its peripheral entry at axis j: −(g_{2j}+g_{2j+1})/2, with the pairs indexed from zero, from Bx=0.
The implementation matches pairs by a base-8 linear encoding of this key, not by any unverified isomorphism. Every true equality is found; also the per-digit differences are <8 in magnitude, so encoding cannot alias an unequal key. Between the two positives and between the two negatives first try all T entries p,n=0,1,2 and try all three T values b between u₁,u₃. The other three cross entries are then fixed by the four equations that a heavy's opposite-sign heavy load minus its same-sign heavy load is 2 plus its own-sign star contact count. Keep precisely the possible values 0..2 and enforce the last equation. At most one double per row is allowed. On these 16 labels impose the necessary T² caps ∑_{k here}T_ik T_kj + T_ij + (B_i,B_j)≤4 for i≠j, which follow from T²+T=12I+4J−BᵀB.

**Rational residual and divisibility.** Remove one vertex in each star component to get a basis of its lattice (the sum of the whole component is zero); let its principal Gram determinant be d_A. For four heavies put S_ij=D H_ij−f_iᵀI f_j, so that S is D times the real Gram after projection off A. It must be PSD; every principal minor, including the four-by-four determinant by exact integer arithmetic, is tested. In unsigned star rank 11 and det S>0, d_A det S/D⁴ is the squared covolume of the 15 independent original vectors. They belong to L₀ of determinant 256. Moreover
  7y=∑_{i∈A,g_i=+1}p_i+∑_{heavy j}t_jp_j.
Adjoining y to their lattice has index exactly 7: eliminate the omitted anchor by its unique component relation; the coefficient of each independent heavy is still ±1. Hence the original determinant must be (16·7)² times an integer square, as tested. In lower star rank or when det S=0 no such full-dimension test is imposed.

**Exact physical lifting and signed Gram.** For each of the four heavies loop over all signed attachments inducing its selected unsigned anchor column. Between two heavies T=0 or 2 forces L=0, while T=1 has either signed orientation. Reconstruct both actual members of every exterior pair in the 39+8-point graph and impose the common-neighbor caps literally. Project the signed F columns off the 11-dimensional signed star Gram by the analogous inverse (F_A+J)⁻¹=I_s/D_s; its nullspace is the all-ones line. Require PSD of the four residual columns and rank≤13 of the signed principal block modulo 7. If full positive dimension 15, the determinant H_full of the 11+4 original signed columns is required to be divisible by 1024·49 with square quotient. This is independent of any K census: H_full is an integer square by the unconditional square determinant of the signed positive form. Since rank₂ F≤6, v₂(H_full)≥9 and hence ≥10 by evenness. Since rank₇ F≤13, v₇(H_full)≥2. These facts prove the divisibility, including at global rank11. The program equivalently uses (d_{F_A}/1024) times the scaled four-column residual determinant, divided by 49. Singular full-dimension determinants are not rejected just for being zero.

**One further label, consistently in both forms.** Fix a passed signed/unsigned quartet lift. For any other label e, loop over its allowed star attachments and T_{eh}∈{0,1,2} on the four heavies. Require x_e=0: ∑_A T_ei g_i+2∑_h T_eh t_h=0. The appropriate T² cap, including use of previously spent common-neighbor capacity, and at most one double per row remain necessary. The real residual Gram on the four heavies in either sector is PSD; choose an independent principal subset (real rank from exact principal minors), and for primes 97,257,1031 skip the prime if any used rational projection denominator, selected minor or leading determinant vanishes modulo it. Otherwise an outside column must pair with the omitted dependent heavy vectors according to their true common-coordinate range equations, checked over F_p. If the current rational span has dimension 15 the new residual squared norm must vanish; at dimension 14 the augmented 15-vector determinant must be square modulo p, zero allowed. Indeed both sector positive forms have square determinant. In notation S for the D-scaled heavy Gram, c for D times the outside/selected-heavy inner products and ν=D||e_perp||² after the star projection, the tested signed/unsigned quantities are
  c_dep=S_dep,J S_J⁻¹c_J;   v=ν−c_Jᵀ S_J⁻¹ c_J;
  full dimension: v=0;   codimension one: d_A det(S_J)v/D^{|J|+1} is square.
An empty heavy basis is valid. The code checks only necessary conditions and never rejects for an inaccessible prime. Signed candidate generation also takes all remaining heavy-link signs compatible with the selected unsigned t. Then reconstruct its two actual new vertices and test all 49-vertex caps. The compact check on the physical 47-point base uses the new positive member's mask M, its involute M′, and their intersection. A saturated old pair cannot occur in either mask, a pair with slack one cannot occur in both, |M∩M′|≤2, and every old vertex v has |N(v)∩M|≤2−[v∈M]. Invariance under the involution supplies the check for M′.

Finally let r be the **local mod-7 rank of the signed principal matrix of the 16 selected labels**, not the unsigned real rank. Adding one signed row f and its diagonal n increases rank by two if f is outside its column space; if f is in range, it increases rank by one exactly when n−f_Jᵀ F_J⁻¹ f_J≠0, using a nonsingular principal basis J. Thus if r=13 enforce range and zero Schur; if r=12 enforce range only; if r≤11 this test is vacuous. A genuine graph has rank≤13 irrespective of the global 11/13 branch. Every one of the 26 outside labels must have some jointly feasible candidate for the **same** chosen signed/unsigned quartet. They are permitted to be independent choices, so a first-layer survivor is not a graph.

**Second layer where required.** The source hpair.cpp recomputes any first-layer-open chart from the beginning, including every sign-word orbit, all heavy attachments and their signed lifts. It retains all feasible paired (signed, unsigned) extensions for each outside label instead of returning the first. For a pair of extra labels y,z, try each allowed common T_yz=0,1,2 and signed link L_yz=0 or ±1 consistent with that T, requiring both projected Gram equations on the same choice. Over each accessible comparison prime, after the actual star and heavy span a codimension-zero residual requires cross product c=0; codimension one requires c²=n_y n_z. The identical necessary residual-rank check applies at 7 to the original signed block when its local rank is 13 or 12, respectively, using data already forced into its column space. Lower local ranks do not trigger this mod-7 pair test. Also build all 51 actual vertices and impose every physical common-neighbor cap. A real graph must give a simultaneous pairwise-compatible choice on the eight smallest outside domains; DFS tests this clique exhaustively with specific signed representatives. Dropping the other labels and allowing individual pair links to vary can only introduce false positives. This second-stage statement does not pretend that each individual first layer is already closed.

**Range and controls.** All adjacency/sign-mask positions used are below 51 in 64-bit words. Each selected 12-anchor Gram basis has determinant ≤5¹¹ (unsigned) or ≤10¹¹ (signed) by Hadamard, with actual D_s≤382118472 across all charts from earlier algebraic checks. The signed/unsigned residual scalar entries are projections of norm-bounded candidates or tentative off-diagonal inner products, so their magnitudes are ≤(10+6)D_s in the signed sector or ≤(5+5)D in the unsigned sector. The D are computed with exact rational inverse reduction and can independently be bounded in the 3,580-chart input. Triple residual determinants are expanded in signed 128-bit arithmetic; fourfold products use GMP. Lexicographic and group normalization never use floating-point arithmetic; only the reported CPU seconds are in floating point. fixturescheck.py independently reconstructs the literal 47-vertex graphs and both Gram matrices for 58 local positive controls (including real signed/unsigned full and deficient ranks); it does not assert that these locally permitted fixtures extend.
