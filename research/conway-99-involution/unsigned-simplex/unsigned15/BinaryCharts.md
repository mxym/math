# Exact binary-glue atlas for potential unsigned completions (a necessary filter)

Apply the seven-row theorem and let C=2L₀*/2V. Put X=C⊥/C. L₊ spans L₀ modulo 2 because the index is odd. Therefore p_e generate C⊥ in V/2V and X has dimension 7, with its nondegenerate bilinear dot and norm mod 4 (norm descends because C has norms 0 mod4 and is orthogonal to C⊥). The column map F₂⁴²→X has Gram H≡BᵀB+J. In F₂⁷ the 21 binary v_ij=1+e_i+e_j with both twists repeated have the very same Gram: their dot is 1 plus the parity of the common endpoints. They span F₂⁷, as their differences span even-weight words and each v has odd weight; alternatively the Gram has rank 7 by the seven-row theorem. The common bilinear-Gram radical is precisely the kernel of both maps, hence an isometry sends the classes of p_e to v_ij. It preserves norm mod 4 because both have norm 1 mod4 and the polar terms are in both spaces twice the same F₂ pairing. This does *not* identify a coordinate support in the original 15-dimensional space.

Choose any orthonormal norm-1 (mod4) basis of X: exhaustive search in the scripts produces one, and the graph identification guarantees one exists. Characteristic class 1 maps intrinsically. In this seven-basis put h_ij=e_i+e_j (i,j≤7) and h_i8=1+e_i. These are the 28 norm-2 mod4 vectors. Their dot is 1 precisely at intersecting distinct K8 edges. Every change of basis of the required kind acts through automorphisms of this triangular graph T(8). Its eight seven-cliques are exactly the stars (a pairwise intersecting family of K8 edges without common point lies in a triangle). Thus up to relabeling the seven axes, covering every choice of the omitted K8 vertex covers every possible actual assignment of the 21 classes 1+h_ij. The singleton-cut root class is then incident to the omitted vertex, its parity support being the six pairs of K₇ containing the other endpoint. No sufficiency of an eight-choice atlas is assumed.

**Standard ambient V=I₁₅.** C is a doubly-even dimension-four binary code. A generator matrix is determined up to coordinate permutations by the multiplicity of each of its 16 possible columns; basis changes act by GL₄(2). Two separate elementary class enumerations give 20 equivalence classes: (1) select four independent columns, take them as the standard basis and recurse over all multisets of the remaining 11 columns, checking all 16 word weights and reducing via all 20160 invertible changes of basis (code15.cpp); (2) extend each dimension-d doubly even code by a new word, splitting every multiplicity c into t, c−t under the previous d rows, test weight, dot orthogonality and rank, and quotient by GL_(d+1) (checkcodes.py). Counts at dimensions 1..4 from the second implementation: 3,7,15,20. The final nonsystematic orbit union has 10064 labelled multiplicity vectors in both implementations.

For each of the 20×8 geometries, enumerate all weight-five supports in C⊥ of each required class 1+h_ij. Every column p_e is signed ±1 on one such support, since it has norm 5 and all unit pairings are 0 or ±1. If a support contains an entire weight-four codeword, all sign choices on it divided by two are norm-one in L₀*, and a compatible sign choice would have dot 2 with p_e, impossible. For each of 15 coordinates i, summing actual indicator occurrences must give 14 by tightness. Select two supports per class (same allowed), one per twist. Empty domains or a single coordinate forced too frequently/too rarely rule out 125 geometries. For the next 11, dualcert.tsv stores integral multipliers a_{ij} and b_i with a_{ij}+∑_{i∈m}b_i≤0 on every permissible support of class ij (here i on the sum denotes a standard unit-coordinate, not an axis). But 2∑a+14∑b>0, contradicting the sum over 42 actual columns. All signs checked by elementary integer arithmetic in checkcodes.py. Consequently only code IDs 17,18,19 (zero-based code15.cpp), each with 8 geometries, pass this particular standard filter. '24 remain' refers only to passing necessary binary conditions, not to a realization. ID 19 is the simplex code; no weight-4 word, and all 15 nonzero generator columns occur once.

**Mixed V=I₇⊥E₈.** For a mixed case not reducible to the standard ambient, projection C→F₂⁷ is injective, since a nonzero singular pure E₈ class would contribute an eighth orthogonal dual unit by its norm-four representative. Its image D is an even binary four-space. C is the graph of a map f:D→E₈/2E₈ satisfying Q(f(x))=wt(x)/2 mod2; its kernel R lies in the singular radical of this binary quadratic form on D. For a fixed (D,R) the induced quotient map is an isometry of subspaces to its image, and Witt extension in the nonsingular quadratic eight-space identifies all such embeddings (no nonzero ambient bilinear radical). The action lifts to E₈ because its Weyl group induces O⁺₈(2); see R. A. Wilson's [E₈ notes](https://webspace.maths.qmul.ac.uk/r.a.wilson/talks_files/E8.pdf), on the root reflections reducing to orthogonal transvections and the 135 nonzero singular classes having representatives of norm 4. code7e8.py enumerates all 651 four-spaces D in the even six-dimensional hyperplane of F₂⁷; its S7 orbit partition has seven classes. R up to the exact stabilizer of D gives 11 possibilities, each explicitly embedded in the hyperbolic eight-space. As an extra check, wittcheck.py successively fixes each previously chosen image vector and computes the orbit of the next one under the root transvections fixing that prefix; it verifies exact equality with the whole set of admissible images at each prefix. roots7e8.py constructs the 240 integral roots from simple reflections, checking the hyperbolic coordinate model; wittcheck.py verifies each nonzero singular class is a sum of two orthogonal root classes and so has a norm-four integral representative.

The unit support of a column p has weight 1,3 or 5, with core norm 4,2 or 0. Its E₈ residue must respectively be nonzero singular, nonsingular or zero. For any weight-four glue word with zero E₈ part all signs give dual units, hence its support overlap with p is ≤2. For a weight-two glue word with E₈ root r, arbitrary two signs and the sign of r are dual units after division by two, implying intersection size plus |(p_E,r)|≤2. The script conservatively lower bounds the latter by its parity, except in the root/root equal-residue case where it equals 2. Empty support domains or the 14-occurrence individual unit-axis inequalities reject 32/88 geometries. The 56 surviving geometries have mixed IDs 3,5,6,7,8,9,10. These filters deliberately ignore signed coherence, full E₈ norms and moment equations.

**Consequence for root search.** Enumerate all L₀ norm-two lines in V: in I₁₅ they are e_i±e_j among roots orthogonal mod 2 to C; in I₇⊥E₈ also the 120 E₈ root lines, identifiable with the nonsingular classes. For each retained model map these lines to norm-two classes of X and sum multiplicities at each vertex of T(8). The eight totals are identical within each retained model. Standard IDs 17,18,19 give respectively 4,2,0 at every vertex. Mixed IDs 3,5,6,7,8,9,10 give respectively 4,3,5,2,1,3,7 at every vertex. This is rootedges.py. Hence (subject to the finite quantifiers above) one need only exclude the **singleton** cut-root of RootsBridge.md to force a standard simplex-code ambient. This binary step alone does not exclude a singleton-cut root; see SingletonSearch.md and the finite replay for the subsequent test. The simplex case is not known extendible or impossible. No involution or Conway graph case is fully resolved by this note.
