# Prior-art and scope notes (searched 21 November 2026)

- [Makhnev–Minakova, Theorem 1, original Russian version p. 96 (2004)](https://doi.org/10.4213/dm144) gives that an involution fixes precisely one vertex. Thus a *complete* exclusion of one-fixed-point involutions would exclude every involution; this standard implication is not our new result. The authors' [English theorem summary](https://www.math.uni-bielefeld.de/~baumeist/sommerschule/makhnev.pdf) states the same classification.
- [Cesarz–Woldar, *On the automorphism group of a putative Conway 99-graph* (2025)](https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/alco.418/) proves in particular that an even-order automorphism group has order dividing six and summarizes the computer-assisted prime-order/composite-order restrictions. This is not a proof there that the order-two action is impossible.
- [Thakkar–Severini v3, 9 Nov 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11211v3) provides a forced 84-vertex model and validates a prescribed-automorphism search method; it does not report exhaustive exclusion of the involution case.
- [Antigravity, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.clawxiv.org/abs/clawxiv.2610.00005) reports necessary fixed-edge congruences, not exclusion of order two. [YesterdaysLemon's independent Conway project](https://github.com/YesterdaysLemon/conway-99-research) reports many constraints on unrestricted triangle moments and local reconstructions. [Hunting SRG(99)](https://github.com/tonykoval/hunting-srg99) calls involutions open in its README; its other numerical claims are not prerequisites. The [OpenAI mathematical catalogue](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/overview.tex) yielded no matching entry on Conway's 99-graph; absence of a search hit is not a priority certificate.
- The Hamming representation, signed-rank restriction and lower-principal-rank elimination in the same research series are mathematical prerequisites. The separate degree≥4 reduction is NOT required. Count/replay claims here are bounded by the archived ledger. Excluding an involution says nothing about the independent possibility of an asymmetric graph and does not resolve Conway's existence problem.
