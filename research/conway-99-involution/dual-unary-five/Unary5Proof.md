**Scope.** In the signed rank-13 reduction for a hypothetical Conway 99-vertex strongly regular graph with a one-fixed-point involution, the norm of the shortest nonzero vector of the rank-15 rational dual, measured by the integral form Δ=7·( , ), cannot be five. This is a restriction on a possible involution, not a resolution of its existence or of the graph problem. In particular, no second dual vector or independence modulo seven is assumed.

A vector in K* lifts to u∈V: primitiveness gives surjectivity Hom(V,Z)→Hom(K,Z), and unimodularity identifies V with its integral dual. Write u′ for the perpendicular projection. For z_e=e_i±e_j, i<j, the rank-15 frame w_e obeys (w_e,w_f)=F_ef where F=12I−4L−ZᵀZ, and Σw_e⊗w_e=28I. This packet regenerates all 7,476 such rows up to negation. The first axis may be chosen on the two-vertex side of the cut before fixing a star chart. The rank-15 integral K has determinant 49 and epsilon_p(K)=−1 at 2 and 7, +1 at all other finite p; for the latter use the splitting of rank-17 unimodular V by the two norm-7 markers as in the preceding packets. ## Two-star row bound
For any exterior label y, the **total unsigned adjacency multiplicity** to star j is 4−2|Z_{jy}|, by the axis/outer unsigned degree equations. Let A be star i, C its two intersections with star j, L signed and T_unsigned unsigned. Contributions T_unsigned(y,z) for z∈C and L(y,z) for z∈A are read from the anchor graph or the candidate physical outer column. Therefore
 n_y=4−2|Z_{jy}|−Σ_{z∈C} T_unsigned(y,z),
 b_y=−4q_y−Σ_{z∈A} L(y,z)q_z
are respectively the unsigned load and signed q-load on the ten unique nonzero labels in star j outside A. Every q coefficient there is ±1 and L≡T_unsigned mod2, |L|≤T_unsigned. Hence |b_y|≤n_y and n_y≡b_y mod2. This uses no knowledge of the other inner adjacency. The parity alone may already follow from the first star's degree; it is not claimed independent. The bound applies also to shared y. On A, L=P−N and T_unsigned=P+N in the root's fixed orientation.

## Star Gram and normalized successive minors
The star vectors span an 11-dimensional space W; d_F is the determinant of the first 11, a basis of its rational space. The parity relation in the previously constructed signed K gives (wᵢ+wᵢ′)/2 and (wᵢ−wᵢ′)/2 in K for each pair of opposite-twist labels on the same two axes. Adding the six integral mate half-sums/half-differences gives the integral rank-11 star lattice S of determinant d_s=d_F/1024 (the single relation is the sum of the six half-sums). Explicitly, writing wⱼ⁺=xⱼ+yⱼ and wⱼ⁻=xⱼ−yⱼ, the only relation is ∑xⱼ=0, primitive because it has unit coefficients. In the quotient by the original star generators, each xⱼ≡yⱼ and 2xⱼ=0, with this unique additional six-way relation. The index is 2⁵ and the determinant divisor is 2¹⁰. The inverse on the all-one-free subspace is I/D=(F_A+J)^−1. Write q_0=q_A, h=I q_0 and a=ED−28 q_0^T I q_0. If a<0 nothing extends. If a=0 then u'/s lies in W; consequently an exterior candidate with star scalar-product column f must satisfy Dq_y=h^T f. One can first check whether any of the thirty required labels has no candidate after this exact check; this is a complete rejection, independent of ternary arithmetic. If none empty, it is NOT an exclusion by this step.

If a>0, the integral 14-dimensional lattice B=S+Zc+Zd+Zu has determinant
 T=49d_s s²·a/(28D)=7s² d_s a/(4D)>0.
For each outer candidate x set v_x=Dq_x−h^T f_x and n_x^0=10D−f_x^TI f_x. For the actual projected residual r_x after W, c,d,u, the following are the T-scaled Gram:
 N_x=T||r_x||²=k(a n_x^0−28v_x²),

       = C_{xy,0}−4lT,
 where k=7s²d_s/(4D²), l∈{−1,0,1}.
Each is an integral (possibly bordered, not necessarily principal) Gram determinant using a single integral B basis; Cauchy gives 0≤N_x≤10T. For every required label choose one exterior candidate. A pivot x with N_x>0 increases B to the integral rank-15 lattice B+Z w_x of determinant N_x. If the search chooses a pivot label with a candidate N_x=0, that candidate is never rejected by a positive-pivot test: return unresolved conservatively. In the real 2-plane left by a positive pivot, the N_x-scaled Gram entries are
 M_y=(N_x N_y−C_xy²)/T,  H_yz=(N_x C_yz−C_xy C_xz)/T.
These are integral bordered Gram determinants. Again M_y≥0. The three unprescribed residual pair inner products are H_yz,0−4lN_x. A genuine pair must satisfy (M_y M_z−H_yz,l²)/N_x is a nonnegative integer square. For example take a Z-basis in unimodular integral V: the full 17 by 17 Gram determinant of B,w_x,w_y,w_z equals exactly this quotient and is its integer coordinate determinant squared, **also when dependent**. This direct argument proves exact division for true choices, and the numerator's division for arbitrary tentative pair inner products also follows by the integral bordered-minor identity. The implementation verifies each exact quotient by multiplication; there is no rounding acceptance rule.

## Optional local filters (in the positive-pivot code)
The binary rational complement inside K of W, u'/s and w_x has determinant squareclass N_x. The two successive diagonal pivots over the W form are A=a/(28D), B=N_x/T. At each finite p the augmented 13-space epsilon is h=epsilon(W)·(d_F,A)_p·(d_F A,B)_p. Its orthogonal complement has epsilon eta=epsilon(K) h (N_x,N_x)_p because the two factors' determinant classes coincide. A represented nonzero vector of squared length M_y/N_x diagonalizes the binary form as <M_y/N_x, N_x/(M_y/N_x)> up to squareclass; hence
 (M_y,−N_x)_p = eta (N_x,−N_x)_p.
Zero M_y is accepted by these local tests. At p>17 not dividing N_x, the integral B+w_x Gram is unimodular over Z_p and has unimodular rank-two orthogonal complement in V_p. Consequently if −N_x is a nonsquare unit, all its represented nonzero scaled norms have even p-adic valuation. Testing only the indicated subset of such primes is conservative.

## Machine arithmetic bounds
The stored 3,580 forms have D≤382118472 and d_s≤20250000 (recomputed directly from the input). Here T≤35d_s and N_x≤10T. Since the signed star f norm projects to at most 10 and the anchor-functional squared norm is ≤5/7, Cauchy gives |Dqₓ−hᵀfₓ|≤4D. The explicit fixed-width quotient routine recovers an integer candidate via a modular inverse modulo 2⁶⁴, but accepts only after exact multiplication in signed 128-bit arithmetic. All squares are selected by a fast mod-64 mask/floating initial estimate **followed by exact integer square comparisons**. The only floating value otherwise used is the reported CPU time. ## Frozen exact outcome
All 3,580 matching layouts are accounted for, 272 failing even the fixed 39-vertex graph cap. Among admissible chart/functional alignments surviving the elementary two-star inequalities, an independent implementation counts 298,107 negative anchor Schur values, 94 zero values, and 74,204 strictly positive values (from 19,369 positive chart-local row signatures). The search performed 44,897,445,910 single-pivot pair-domain checks and 3,224,896,176 residual pair checks; those are internal counts, not candidate completions.
