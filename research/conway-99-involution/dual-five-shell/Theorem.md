# At most one unoriented shortest dual direction in the rank-13 Conway involution lattice

**Statement.** Suppose a putative srg(99,14,1,2) has an involution with a unique fixed point, in the remaining branch where the signed exterior Gram has rank 13 modulo 7. In the associated rank-15 integral complement K, the shell of its dual at scaled squared norm Δ=7||u'||²=5 has at most two vectors, necessarily opposite. This excludes 46 of the previous 283 candidate isometry classes of K: the 28 earlier cases and 18 further, disjoint cases. At most 237 remain after this pair of tests. This does not resolve the involution branch or the whole graph problem.

The [preceding orthogonal/discriminant-independent theorem and replay](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/c1d2312d2d6e8af3d28fc70002cdf1891dc5e62c/research/conway-99-involution/dual-short-directions) supplies the setup, including the 283-class census and the full case Δ(u,v)=0. We recall what changes in the remaining case.

Let u',v' be two distinct unoriented shortest directions. The scaled pairing m=7(u',v') is an integer. Since every nonzero projected vector has scaled norm ≥5, their sum and difference have 10±2m≥5, so |m|≤2. From the unimodular rank-17 realization as the common perpendicular of the orthogonal norm-seven markers c,d, the binary Gram satisfies M≡−RRᵗ (mod 7) for the 2×2 matrix R of pairings with c,d of integral lifts. Consequently det M is a square or zero mod 7. If |m|=1 then det M=24≡3 is a nonsquare. If m=0 it is 25≠0 mod 7 and the preceding packet excludes it. The remaining case is |m|=2, with det M=21, discriminant-dependent at 7 but nonsingular over Q. No mod-7 independence assumption for this case is made. Among 283 explicit real-independent witnesses, check_witnesses.py verifies 28 with M diagonals 5,5 and m=0 and a further 18 with the same diagonals and |m|=2; the others are not excluded by these witness tests.

**Local signed rows.** Each Δ5 row g=(u',w_e) has 20 entries ±1, on the two labels of every edge of a 2-versus-5 axis cut, and Zg=0. The full 42-label rows are independently regenerated from the 7,476 sign-normalized possibilities. Mark a central axis belonging to the first row's 2-set. Use the same 3,580 local star matching layouts P,N under signed peripheral permutations as in the preceding theorem. Reverse each direction independently to make its first nonzero entry positive; consequently BOTH m=+2 and m=−2 must be searched. The exact frame identity is g·h=4m.

In the numerical-free formulas of the preceding proof, the sole mathematical change is replacing the zero off-diagonal entry in M by m. Thus the local Schur off-diagonal is b=mD−7g_AᵗI h_A. In each chart, all full rows with the prescribed dot product are included. A positive local 2×2 Schur determinant again leaves a rational binary residual. The same integral-square full Gram necessary condition, local Hilbert tests, 43-vertex common-neighbor caps and multipartite clique test apply. The signed-±2 positive ledgers report no remaining alignment. A negative/indefinite local Gram is impossible. The 272 impossible base charts are recorded, not counted as nondegenerate cases.

**Degenerate charts need not be individually eliminated.** For a singular positive-semidefinite 2×2 Gram, impose both kernel equations on every exterior cross-pair, and the signed pair Cauchy bound in the higher-dimensional residual, exactly as in the preceding proof. To retain a *conservative* set here, we additionally test a clique only on the five smallest nonempty domains and pairwise existence across all 30 labels. We do NOT claim the resulting survivors are full cliques or solutions. Retain their entire two signed 42-entry rows and chart IDs. The two lists have 438 and 414 entries, respectively, across 14 chart IDs in union. The per-chart number of entries equals the corresponding fourth outcome in the bounded singular ledger.

**Why no surviving marked pair can be global.** For a norm-five direction the small side of its cut has exactly two members. In a graph one may mark *either* member as the central axis; for equal-norm directions one may also repeat this with the other direction first. Any true full row pair would therefore be represented locally with both marked-axis positions of its first small cut, and (after transposition) both positions of its second small cut. This uses freedom to choose coordinates, not any additional graph automorphism.

Compare the rows under the actual signed permutations of seven axes. For clarity, fixing m=+2 globally loses no cases: if g·h=−8 reverse h; thereafter the joint sign reversal (g,h)↦(−g,−h) is already realized by reversing all seven axes. Each permutation and sign choice reorients every D7 root representative to make its first nonzero coordinate positive, multiplying both row entries by the same root sign. Sort the four membership classes of the ordered cuts: in both, first only, second only, neither. In these template labels the first 2-set is necessarily {0,1}. For each unmarked canonical pair key keep the union of every image of the actual central label over every canonicalizing isometry; this retains equivalent marked positions rather than choosing one arbitrarily. There are 23 unmarked ordered pair keys, only three of which can realize BOTH positions 0 and 1. In each of these three cases the key obtained by exchanging g and h is not one of those three. No global ordered pair remains possible. This does not require all 852 individual entries to be locally impossible; it proves that not all necessary marked-axis views are simultaneously available.

**Independent action checks.** pairglobal.py uses the directed coordinates k_ij=(g⁺+g⁻)/2, k_ji=(g⁺−g⁻)/2 and similarly for h. A sign switch at axis i negates its entire directed row; it therefore takes the joint lexicographically smaller signed row independently at each i for each admissible permutation. directorbit.cpp instead explicitly iterates all 128 sign masks under every cut-preserving relabeling and computes the signed root image and representative sign on each of the 42 D7 roots. The two independent encodings yield the same 852/14/23/3/0 accounting. Permutations interchanging u and v are used only in the final necessity comparison, never as an unjustified local chart gauge.
