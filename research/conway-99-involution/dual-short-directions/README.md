# Conway 99 involution: no mod-7-independent pair of shortest dual directions

In the signed rank-13 involution case, two dual projections of scaled squared norm five cannot be independent in the mod-7 discriminant group. This excludes 28 of the 283 candidate rank-15 complement classes in the attached census, leaving **at most 255 by this criterion**. The remaining rank-13 cases, the whole involution branch, and the existence of the Conway graph are not settled. The two directions considered here are orthogonal; nonorthogonal real-independent pairs with dependent mod-7 markers are not tested by this theorem.

The [proof](Proposition.md) normalizes the two cut-supported rows, considers all 3,580 local layouts, and uses the exact rational residual plane, Hilbert-symbol constraints and common-neighbor caps. The complete original search rejects all 91,967,546 nonsingular positive alignments and a separate general-PSD fallback rejects all 62,132 singular alignments. The two positive-search shards are disjoint parts of one implementation, not two full independent programs.
