# An exclusion by rational residual planes in the rank-13 Conway involution search

**Theorem.** Suppose an srg(99,14,1,2) has an involution with a unique fixed point, and the signed exterior Gram F has rank 13 over F7. Its associated K* cannot contain two vectors of Δ-norm 5 with independent residues in the two-dimensional 7-discriminant plane. In particular, 28 of the previous 283 possible rank-15 complement classes K are excluded by this test. This leaves at most 255 after this sieve; it does not show that any of the rest are realizable. No unconditional graph nonexistence or full involution exclusion follows. The exact census source, proofs and checksums are under prerequisite-census/. The 3,580 matching layouts are regenerated by catalog.cpp as part of replay. Pertinent previous sources: the [Conway involution research and signed orbit setup](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/main/research/conway-99-involution), and the [Chenevier lattice table](https://gaetan.chenevier.perso.math.cnrs.fr/unimodular_lattices/index_uni.html) for the underlying no-unit classification. These are finite inputs; no other agent-produced result or numerical optimizer is required for the (5,5,0) finite exclusion.

**Set-up.** In the surviving signed rank-13 case there is a rank-15 integral positive lattice K of determinant 49, the primitive common perpendicular of orthogonal norm-seven c,d in an integral unimodular rank-17 V. The 42 vectors w_e of norm 10 form a 28-tight frame of its real span. Index them by z_e=e_i+e_j,e_i-e_j for i<j, in lex pair order. Their Gram is F=12I−4L−Z^t Z, where L is signed adjacency between exterior twin orbits. Thus each off-diagonal l_xy is −1,0,1. Modulo 7 the Gram F has rank 13; the two nonunit 7-elementary divisors belong to the full metric K, not an assumption about every local star. Since (V,c,d) is a primitive gluing, every functional on K is the perpendicular projection of u∈V. Write u' for this projection. Its integral bilinear metric scaled by 7 is Δ(u,v)=7(u,v)−(u,c)(v,c)−(u,d)(v,d). The minimum Δ on nonzero projections is at least 5, by the earlier signed-star cut lemma. In particular g_e=(u,w_e) is an integer row with Zg=0, g·h=4Δ(u,v). The two coordinates on each support have equal parity and the odd pairs make a cut in K7. If Δ(u,u)=5 its cut must have 2 versus 5 axes and it is ±1 on each of the twenty cut labels, zero elsewhere.

**Explicit witness census.** The 283 lines in witnesses.jsonl fix u,v in models of all 283 K, NOT new claims that all these K embed. check_witnesses.py uses Python integer arithmetic and checks the stored coordinates against the root-form, glue generators, actual marked pair and the selected key in K_classes.tsv. The matrix R with rows ((u,c),(u,d)), ((v,c),(v,d)) is independent mod 7 in every case. The δ-Gram M satisfies M≡−RR^t modulo 7, so its determinant is a nonzero square mod 7. Its diagonal entries are at most 9, its determinant ≤72. Counts in the format (M11,M22,|M12|): (5,5,0):28; (5,6,0):45; (5,6,1):32; (5,8,1):32; (6,6,0):37; (6,6,2):14; (6,8,2):40; (6,8,3):1; (6,9,1):2; (8,8,0):47; (8,8,2):4; (8,9,0):1. These are counts of the *selected witnesses*, not a classification of possible choices. For two discriminant-independent Δ=5 vectors, |M12|≤2 by the Δ-minimum applied to their sum and difference, and only zero gives 25−M12² a nonzero square modulo 7.

The witnesses span a primitive sublattice of K*. Indeed in the integral Δ metric any 2-plane lattice of minimum at least 5 has determinant ≥75/4: take a shortest vector x and a reduced independent basis partner y with |(x,y)|≤x²/2. Then y²≥x² and det≥3(x²)^2/4. A saturation index at least two would have determinant at most 72/4=18. Thus pairing K into Z² against these two functionals is onto; equivalently extend the primitive pair to a basis of K* and dualize. In particular N=K∩u'^⊥∩v'^⊥ is integral of rank 13 and determinant det(M). To see this, choose a basis of K extending a basis of N by two integer lifts of the standard pair. Their Gram after orthogonal projection to span(u',v') is (M/7)^{-1}, giving 49=det K=det N·49/det M.

**Anchor and residual.** Select axis 0 in the size-2 side of g's cut for the (5,5,0) test. This choice is possible for any graph under investigation and imposed on no other star. Permuting axes and switching individual axis signs are representative choices, not graph automorphisms. The exterior D7 label orientations are restored to z_e after switching. On the 12 anchors A=(0,j,±) two matching involutions P,N are formed by same-twin and opposite-twin adjacency. The established 3580-orbit list fixes P,N up to permutations of the 6 pairs and interchanging their labels, both induced by permitted peripheral-axis changes; no simultaneous orbit normalization of g,h is imposed. Either functional may be reversed in the (5,5,0) case, so its first nonzero entry may be set positive. cutcheck.cpp enumerates all 2^19 sign choices per cut directly, retaining Zg=0, and matches all 7476 stored rows from the independent directed-flow generator. configs tests all resulting full row pairs with g_A of squared norm ten (distinguished central vertex) and g·h=0, after PSD screening. No assumption about g and h cut intersection.

Write F_A=11I+D_mate−4P+4N−J, a PSD matrix of rank 11 with kernel ones. Taking I/D=(F_A+J)^{-1} (D>0) thus computes F_A^+ on any sum-zero column. Put d=det F_A[0:11,0:11]>0. The two projected functionals after removing the anchor span have Gram K0/(7D), K0=DM−7[g_A,h_A]^t I[g_A,h_A]=[[a,b],[b,c]], t=ac−b². An actual assignment requires PSD. If t>0, their joint span B with the star has rank 13, determinant class d·t. Its complement is rational binary with square-class determinant d·t since det K=49. If t=0, keep it for the separate fallback, never invert it.

Every physical candidate X for one of the 30 remaining exterior twin pairs attaches its positive member to exactly two +anchors, two −anchors and its two appropriate axis parents. The first fact follows by counting common neighbors of this vertex with the two center-axis parents (nonneighbors with μ=2); their other base neighbors cannot contribute. Enumerate all two-subsets on each side; equal subsets would give both twins four common positive/negative anchors, impossible. RForm and caps.hpp do the established signed and unsigned one-column PSD and graph checks. Cross star column f_x=−4(same−opposite)−Z_A^t z_x, sum zero. With ν_x=10D−f_x^tI f_x and p_x=(Dg_x−g_A^tI f_x,Dh_x−h_A^tI f_x), define
 n_x=ν_x t−7p_x^t adj(K0)p_x,
 C_xy=(D(−4l_xy−z_x·z_y)−f_x^tI f_y)t−7p_x^t adj(K0)p_y.
These are exactly D t times the squared residual norm and residual inner product, so n_x≥0. For each pair of distinct labels an actual configuration requires d·t·(n_x n_y−C_xy²) to be an integer square, zero allowed. Indeed the determinant of the 15-vector Gram divided by it is the square 1/(7D²t)^2, and every full-dimensional rational Gram in K has square determinant. Singular Gram has determinant zero. Testing square residues can only reject nonsquares; exact survivors are checked using arbitrary-precision integer square testing.

**Unary rational form filter.** At each odd prime h(V)=+1 by a diagonal unit basis; at the real place h=+1, so at 2 also h=+1 by reciprocity. The orthogonal factor <7,7> has h=−1 at 2,7 and +1 elsewhere; determinants of both factors are squares, so h(K)=h(<7,7>). Let h(B) be computed by rational diagonalization, using positive leading minors of F_A[0:11,0:11] and the two extra diagonal entries a/(7D), t/(7Da). The rational residual binary plane R has determinant d t modulo squares, and h(R)=h(K)h(B)(d t,d t)_p. If −d t is a local square it must have h(R)=1; moreover any nonzero represented rational s=n_x/(D t) must satisfy (s,−d t)_p=h(R), by diagonalizing from that vector. Zero kept. We use this only at 2,3,5,7,11,13,17, with no sufficiency claim. controlh.py and controlh.cpp explicitly embed the desired synthetic frame in diag(7,7,1^13), including irrational-free Hasse, square and zero determinant controls.

**Caps and global candidate compatibility.** For each signed l_xy, consider all unsigned adjacency totals |l_xy| or 2 (l_xy=0), and reconstruct the 43-vertex shell (1 fixed point,14 axis vertices,24 anchor members,4 members of two new twin pairs), requiring explicitly every pair to have at most 2 minus their adjacency many common neighbors. A double cannot be chosen for x or y if already used towards an anchor, by twin common-neighbor cap. The ledger word `ok` counts individual signed pair checks that passed these conditions, not solutions. The fourth count after `totals` records the number of full row alignments still having a multipartite clique; these are distinct counters. Each label domain contains all necessary candidates. A true 99-vertex graph gives a 30-clique in the multipartite candidate compatibility graph, one choice per label. Rejection after partial pair tests or an exhaustive clique test on some or all labels is sound. Compatibility can overcount, as it need not determine a global plane orientation or a common graph completion. A positive pass is not existence.

**Singular cases.** For K0 PSD of rank <2, p_x must be in its range. Algebraically require a p_{x,2}=b p_{x,1}, c p_{x,1}=b p_{x,2}, and p_x=0 if a=c=0. If a>0 use the single residual direction: replace t by a, n_x by aν_x−7p_{x,1}² and C_xy by a(D(−4l−z_x·z_y)−f_x^t I f_y)−7 p_{x,1}p_{y,1}; if a=0<c use the second coordinate instead. If a=c=0 use ν_x and D times the ordinary post-star cross product. In each case only nonnegativity and C_xy²≤n_x n_y are required, along with the same 43-point graph tests and 30-way clique. No binary square or Hasse filter is applied to these higher-dimensional residuals. Of all 3580 charts, 272 fail the base graph; precisely 40 have PSD-degenerate full first/second cut-row assignments (62,132 assignments). Of these, 57,040 assignments already fail unary range/norm constraints; the other 5092 fail pair or clique constraints in the independent fallback run. Cross-check the 40 per-chart totals against the rank-census ledger in the archive. No count here concerns δ-pairs other than (5,5,0).

### Full result and accounting

The two parity shards partition the *same* exhaustive original search. They are not claimed to be independent implementations. Their joined positive ledger covers all 3,580 layouts, 272 already impossible in the base physical shell. Of 3,308 admissible charts, all 91,967,546 full row-pair assignments having positive augmented Schur form were rejected, and every one of the 62,132 with a singular nonnegative form was rejected by the separate higher-dimensional fallback. Negative Schur forms, or negative diagonal entries, cannot occur by positive semidefiniteness. The input-independent full row census and the final ledgers are reconciled explicitly by checkledger.py. A nonzero value of the separate `ok` counter just counts accepted individual pair checks; only the fourth `totals` counter records open cliques, and it is zero throughout.
