# Sources and attribution

* Claude Carlet and Darrion Thornburgh, [Determining those Boolean functions whose restrictions to affine spaces are plateaued](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/386), IACR ePrint 2026/386. We checked the [12 May 2026 copy](https://opencryptology.org/2026/386/versions/20260512T112848Z.pdf) and the [14 November 2026 copy](https://opencryptology.org/2026/386/versions/20261114T034025Z.pdf), accessible from the version index of Open Cryptology. Both conclusions claim six distinct classes for \(n\ge5\). In the 14 November copy see the abstract, §2 definitions and degree bound, §4 beginning and Remark 2/Theorem 2 as appropriate for the Walsh slice formula and complement-support construction, and the concluding paragraphs posing classification of the third and fifth classes. The two equalities at \(n=5\) correct its strictness threshold without changing its universal hyperplane restriction classification. We use the authors' known Walsh mechanisms rather than claim priority for them.
* Meltem Sönmez Turan and René Peralta, [The Multiplicative Complexity of Boolean Functions on Four and Five Variables](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=916439), particularly Table 4, informed our choice of small examples. Their classifications are not an input to the supplied proof or code; completeness here comes from the alternating-form normal forms plus enumeration of all quadratic additions.
* Anne Canteaut, Claude Carlet, Pascale Charpin and Caroline Fontaine, [On cryptographic properties of the cosets of (R(1,m))](https://who.paris.inria.fr/Pascale.Charpin/CCCF-i3e-01.pdf), IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory (2001), Example V.6/Remark V.3: the five-variable quartic with optimal nonlinearity but mixed 4/8 moduli was already known. It serves as a verification illustration, not a new Boolean function.
* The finite search covered the exact article title, both authors, partially-bent restriction, plateaued extension, 5 and 6 variables, and searches for corrections through 27 November 2026. It found literature on the known spectral tools and small representatives, not a correction stating the exact threshold or these precise small-dimensional extension classes. No general priority claim is made.
