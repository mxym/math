# Source and scope comparison

**Questions.** Thomas L. Draper and Feras A. Saad, [*Space–Entropy Lower Bounds for Random Sampling*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14503), [July 16, 2026, v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14503v1). Definitions 4 and 6 allow a known law on the natural numbers, a time-homogeneous per-round prefix map with a fixed initial state, and unrestricted work within the round. Exactness is required for each whole output word, not from every individual hidden state. Section 9, p. 17, poses the two questions separately. Their §§4–5 give the state-information potential, cycle bounds and the general lower bound (Theorem 14). Theorem 1(1.2) gives the sharp necessary coefficient one on a specified infinite Bernoulli family; Theorem 2 gives a different lower estimate for almost every fixed finite-support target. We do not reattribute any of these results or conclude that the sharp universal coefficient holds for each individual distribution.

**Earlier and adjacent results.**

1. D. E. Knuth and A. C. Yao, *The Complexity of Nonuniform Random Number Generation*, in *Algorithms and Complexity: New Directions and Recent Results* (Academic Press, 1976), pp. 357–428; [Knuth's university bibliography](https://profiles.stanford.edu/donald-knuth). Their discrete distribution generating trees and the classical dyadic prefix construction underlie our initialization and the easy direction of the zero-loss theorem. A fixed stateless decoder is not a necessity proof for arbitrary finite persistent memory. Han and Hoshi's [interval method](https://algo.inria.fr/seminars/sem94-95/hoshi.pdf) is a prior technique for exact simulation via nested intervals. It is used here for an escape interval and its conditional correction.
2. D. Kozen and M. Soloviev, [*Coalgebraic Tools for Randomness-Conserving Protocols*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.02735), [journal version](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/am/pii/S2352220821000973), study stream reductions, entropy efficiency and finite-state capacity (see in particular §§2.5, 4). Their finite-state transducers account for computation between input symbols. The counting obstacle to representing arbitrary real probabilities with such transducers is not a restriction on a per-round map supplied with the exact target law and unconstrained within a round. [Shao and Wang's MichelangeRoll](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00915) concerns repeated sampling from a fixed rational distribution with near-optimal entropy.
3. T. L. Draper and F. A. Saad, [*Efficient Online Random Sampling via Randomness Recycling*, SODA 2026](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9781611978971.89), [author-hosted formal PDF](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~fsaad/assets/papers/2026-DraperEtAl-SODA.pdf), Theorem 1.5 and Conjecture 1.6. The logarithmic-space upper order for bounded-denominator rational laws is prior, as is the resolution of that original lower-bound conjecture by their July paper. Neither is claimed here. Draper, Harris and Saad, [*Online Random Sampling with Real Probabilities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13828), give logarithmic persistent space also for real countably supported inputs, with additional algorithmic CDF-oracle complexity guarantees (Theorem 1.2, Algorithm 2 and (10)). Their two-integer reservoir and the coefficient-two comparison cited by the July lower-bound paper are predecessors. Our cardinality-independent leading coefficient one is for the abstract exact-map model; no oracle-efficiency improvement is asserted.
4. J. Duda, [*Asymmetric numeral systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.0271), and [*Asymmetric numeral systems: entropy coding combining speed of Huffman coding with compression rate of arithmetic coding*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.2540). Finite intervals of states, binary renormalization and a logarithmic-scale state profile are prior ANS ideas. [*The Compression Optimality of Asymmetric Numeral Systems*](https://doi.org/10.3390/e25040672) studies the compression process, including optimal dyadic cases. Compression of a prescribed source and running an uncorrected decoder on fair bits are not guarantees of the exact joint independence needed here. The ticket packing below the desired per-pair masses and the normalized residual correction establish that independence. No invention of ANS, interval decoding, Kraft codes or the entropy lower bound is claimed.
5. The July [ICALP 2026 paper on local product samplers](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.109) concerns parallel locality and approximation, not this exact sequential persistent-state model.
