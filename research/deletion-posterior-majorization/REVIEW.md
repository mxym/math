The channel conditioned on survival length is uniform over masks and independent of the prior. The factor normalizing K_y(x) is the same binomial(n,m) q^(n-m) for every observation because every mask specifies exactly m entries and leaves the rest free. Intermediate cylinder families need not be generated by one word; keeping the masks fixed is enough. For a chosen coordinate and fixed remainder, collect indicators into a common term C and symbol-specific totals D_b. Aligning the coordinate changes only that fiber, to one entry C+sum D_b and the rest C. These vectors have the same mass and the new sum of the r largest is at least the old for every r. Combining fibers by their old top-r index counts proves global majorization; this argument does not assume identical sizes of nonzero fibers. Compressing all coordinates yields exactly the constant observed word.

For any adjacent distinct symbols in y choose the two masks that keep positions (k,k+1) or (k+1,k+2) in the three-slot block, sharing the rest. Outside the midpoint both cylinders are jointly satisfiable, so align that midpoint first. The fiber with these positive competing requirements increases its sum of squares strictly; no other step can decrease it. Hence only constant words attain equality up to permutation (away from the stated endpoints). The same two masks cannot both match any host, so the maximum multiplicity for a nonconstant y is strictly smaller than binomial(n,m), yielding strict min-entropy. For support size, greedy matching reads each independent symbol against a target predictable from the prior letters; its retention success probabilities are identically 1/q conditionally on each past. Continue after the last target and the success count is exactly binomial; support size is therefore y-independent. Strict Schur concavity applies to all the stated finite positive Rényi orders.
