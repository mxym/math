# The type-A plus two type-B twelve-wing multiset is impossible at rank 36

Let A and B be the tree-7350 and tree-7140 types. Assume A+B+B as the three components near a triangle at mod-seven Seidel rank 36. In the [two-component current result](triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md), the whole A evaluation plane is disjoint from each B line, and the two B lines are distinct. Their joint span is totally isotropic. Split off a nonsingular 30-row wing basis (one constant, centered ranks 9,10,10), leaving a nondegenerate six-space. Its isotropic span therefore has dimension **exactly three**: at least three from A plus the first B, and at most three by nondegeneracy. Consequently there is a unique relation up to scalar
\[
 \phi(e_A)+k h(e_B)+l h(e_{B'})=0
\]
at every actual word, where \(\phi\) is nonzero in the A plane and \(k,l\ne0\). If one B coefficient vanished, a B line would belong to A; if \(\phi=0\), the two B lines would coincide. Normalize \(\phi\) to a representative of one of the eight projective A lines, fixing the relation's scale.

In the standard A template an independent pair is \(a=(1,4,3,6;6,4,3,1;0,0,0,0)\), \(b=(1,4,3,6;0,0,0,0;6,3,4,1)\). Take \(\phi=t a+b\) for \(t\in\mathbf F_7\) or \(\phi=a\), and take \(\psi=a\) in the first case, \(\psi=b\) in the latter. The basis is checked in the literal template. Fix A's physical wings. For each B, its template relabelings independently negate its current and can exchange its two outer wings preserving current if desired, as verified in the [BBB test](three-b12-zero-section.md). It suffices to choose its central physical wing and unsigned coefficient \(1,2,3\), hence nine options. The two B-components can be exchanged. All \(8\binom{10}{2}=360\) charts are retained, with no guessed graph symmetry.

Consider the far words with \(\phi(e_A)=0\), using every distinct such positive-multiplicity A tag at least once (there are 10,12 or14, depending on the line). In the four-space \(u_\phi^\perp/\langle u_\phi\rangle\), the independent images of \(u_\psi\) and the first B current span a totally isotropic two-plane. Indeed a linear relation with \(u_\phi\) would put a B vector in the A plane or make \(\phi,\psi\) proportional. Thus this quotient is a direct sum of two hyperbolic planes. Pairings to these two currents prescribe \(a_z=(-\psi(e_A),-h(e_B))\). With the 30-mark projected Gram \(g_{zz'}\), choose all \(d_z\in\mathbf F_7^2\) with \(2a_z^t d_z=-g_{zz}\), permitting all 49 if both sides vanish identically. The adjacency of two lifted words is forced to equal \(4-g_{zz'}-a_z^td_{z'}-d_z^ta_{z'}\). Words must take two contacts per physical wing, satisfy the relation above and the usual elementary near/adjacency capacities.

At the first search node only, the global coordinate change \(d_z\mapsto d_z+s(-a_{z,2},a_{z,1})\) fixes every pair product. Thus when \(a_1\ne0\) set the first chosen word's \(d_2=0\), when \(a_1=0,a_2\ne0\) set \(d_1=0\), and when \(a=0\) allow every \(d\). The [exhaustive transversal-clique checker](abb_orthogonal_quotient.py) rejects all 360 charts (at most 10,403 nodes in any chart, about 90 seconds total). This is one new unordered multiset exclusion.

Together with the original [66 cuts](triangle-wing-aa-direct.md), the [BBB cut](three-b12-zero-section.md), [five new BBU cuts](bbu-hyperbolic-quotient.md), [66 BUU cuts](buu-line-quotient.md), and [30 new AUU cuts](auu-plane-quotient.md), this eliminates exactly all \(169=\binom{15}{3}-\binom{13}{3}\) 12+12+12 multisets containing a seven-singular twelve type. The 286 pure-unit multisets are not excluded here. The arithmetic is scoped to the 13 previously retained types, and does not exclude the whole rank-36 case.
