# Rank 36 excludes the pair of centered dimension-three 18-wing components

Conditional theorem. In the triangle-wing notation of [the eighteen-current catalogue](triangle-wing-eighteen-currents.md), a rank-36 triangle cannot have two 18-site components both of centered Laplacian nullity three over F7. This is one particular abstract pair, not all 18+18 or an exclusion of rank 36.

Use the normal form of the already checked 18-template catalogue. Its unique centered-nullity-three type D has six ordered sites per wing, with internal mates (1,0,3,2,5,4), (2,4,0,5,1,3), (1,0,5,4,3,2). The first–second and second–third ties are identities; the third-to-first return map is (3,5,4,1,2,0). D realizes every wing permutation by an automorphism, verified by looping over the wing ordering and first-wing permutation, then forcing every other site's image from the two relevant matchings. Thus no relative wing orientation is assumed. In addition the wingwise permutation (1,0,4,5,2,3) induces minus identity on the centered currents and does not permute wings.

Fix common wing labels and an ordered basis for ker(3I−A_D) intersect 1-perp in F7^18. Every exterior site meets each D in a triple (the constant-current component identity) and each wing in two points. For a triple e write v(e) for its three basis evaluations (the sums over that triple). The preceding anchored parity proof guarantees each 3D evaluation map to the sixty exterior rows is injective. The same paper's rank36 dimension bound then forces the two evaluation images to coincide. Hence a single M in GL_3(7) relates the evaluations in EVERY exterior neighborhood: its left triple e and right triple f satisfy v(e)=M v(f). Up to the minus automorphism it suffices to let the first nonzero entry in M's first row be 1,2,3; this gives 171·336·294=16,892,064 maps. No identification by general graph automorphisms is made.

For two distinct sites in the same component the exact number of exterior common neighbors is m_ij=2−[adjacent]−|N_D(i) intersect N_D(j)|−[same wing]. A triple is eligible if all its pairs have positive m, it has at most two sites of any wing, and [i in e]+|N_D(i) intersect e|≤2 for every near i: the last follows from the common-neighbor quota for exterior vertex and near site i. There are 612 eligible triples in either copy. A fixed actual triple is used at most once in a component (otherwise two exterior sites have three common neighbors). For sites i and j in different components the required number of exterior common neighbors is 2−[same wing]. Each potential six-tuple must also sum to the vector (2,2,2) on the three wings.

For every M, check successively: each left pair lies in at least m_ij distinct left triples admitting a matching right triple; symmetrically for each right pair; every cross pair admits at least its required number of complete six-tuples. Respectively 896500, 103831, 27568 maps survive. For each survivor take a column per allowable complete six-tuple, with one entry for each of its six internal pairs and nine cross pairs. Target is the vector of their required counts. Any actual exterior would give a nonnegative integral sum of columns equal to the target; reducing this identity mod two preserves a necessary condition even if the target's even coordinates cancel. In every one of the 27568 cases, binary elimination proves the target is outside the span. Hence none can be realized.

Replay: the [stand-alone Python script](wing_eighteen_three_three.py) (NumPy only; no external data, LP solver, SAT solver, randomness, or floating arithmetic in the test) reconstructs the template, current basis, sign/wing automorphisms, eligible triples, and proves each count. Run with OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 wing_eighteen_three_three.py. This is both verification and full regeneration, about two minutes on one CPU; the existing entire 18-template census need not be rerun to replay this final comparison. The first scan pools the 343 candidates for the last row of M as integer bit sets: exclude the determinant-zero plane; each mandatory left pair needs at least m distinct eligible triples. The remaining checks enumerate actual candidates and use independent matrix encoding for binary elimination. No near-complete or full 99-vertex graphs are enumerated.
