# A clean square off a fully gridded axis spans three residual dimensions

Fix a root o, an axis e whose triangle T_e has six prism components, and distinct other axes a,b such that their four outside sign-pair sites C_ab complete a grid with o and the four signed parents on a,b. Take the mod-seven residuals r_x of the four vertices of C_ab perpendicular to the wing span of T_e as in the [fully gridded-triangle word construction](saturated-prism-isotropic.md). **They span exactly three dimensions: their sum is zero and there is no other linear relation.** This is rank-free. At rank 36 the residual space has dimension five, so these residuals cannot be pairwise orthogonal: a nondegenerate 5-space cannot contain a 3-dimensional totally isotropic subspace. Neither a saturated axis nor any whole rank is excluded.

**Four-word chart.** Number the six prism components by 0..5, with a,b first. Their six symbols are 2u+s with wing u∈{0,1,2} and prism-line bit s=0,1; use wing zero for o's wing. Each exterior site takes one symbol per component and exactly two per wing. The four vertices of C_ab have words X_i, i=0,1,2,3 with initial symbols (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1); their square adjacency toggles one bit. In each other component j they take the four different symbols 2,3,4,5, in some permutation. Indeed the cell C_ej is an actual square; each C_ab site has exactly one neighbor in C_ej by the one-neighbor-outside-grid rule for the grid on e,j, and conversely every C_ej site has exactly one in C_ab. The wing-zero points of prism j are its signed first-layer parents, disjoint from the prescribed a,b parents. The other four symbols are precisely its C_ej sites. The six prism line names can be switched independently: use this to put an even symbol in the first row of each of the last four columns. Permuting those four whole prism components permits ordering their columns. Thus one enumerates multisets of four from 12 permutations; retain rowwise wing multiplicity two to get 75 charts. These are names for actual inputs, not assumed graph automorphisms.

The nine marks of the grid on a,b sum to zero (its indicator g satisfies Ag=3g+1); the other five grid marks already lie in the wing/triangle span of T_e. Hence Σ_i r_(X_i)=0. Modulo this relation each hypothetical extra relation has a representative λ∈F7^4 with last entry zero and first nonzero entry one. It must annihilate the 4×4 residual Gram on X, using the four-word formula
60H_xy=43−18n−4k−6h−60A_xy, diagonal 91, where n counts exact matches, k shared wings, h different wings with equal bit. For 34 charts there are no candidate relations, for 36 there is one, and for 5 all 57 projective candidates survive this Gram test. A Gram kernel alone does not give a true relation in an isotropic space; the next test distinguishes them.

**Exact exterior separation.** For each would-be true λ, enumerate every possible other word z, paired with its adjacency vector to the four X_i. No z outside the four can use both wing-zero positions 0 and 1: each pair of signed a,b parents has just one other common neighbor besides o. If z uses one of these positions in wing zero, it has no neighbor in C_ab; if it uses neither, it has exactly one, by the grid on a,b. For each allowed choice, enforce the common-neighbor test n(z,X_i)+A(z,X_i)≤2 and orthogonality Σ_i λ_i H_(z,X_i)=0 mod seven.

An actual graph must supply 56 such columns. Each records (i) its 15 symbol pairs in distinct components and (ii), if adjacent to X_i, its six selected symbols in the corresponding X_i neighbor block. The pair targets are 2−[same wing] minus the four known word contributions. For prism symbol v in component j, the full far-neighbor count at X_i equals 2−[v=(X_i)_j]−[v adjacent (X_i)_j in that prism]: common neighbors between v and X_i are its near contacts plus the far ones. Subtract the contributions of the two known square neighbors X_i already has. The resulting 540+144 target entries have sum 984. Any integral y with target·y <56 times the least candidate-column product rules out that λ (as does an empty column set). The [exact checker](saturated_prism_clean_square.py) regenerates strict integer certificates for all 36+5·57=321 cases. All sparse matrices, proposed witnesses and final checks are integral; no optimizer or floating-point comparison is used. The fixed-point residual routine is a deterministic search for the witness; acceptance requires the strict integer inequality, with a verified size bound.

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 saturated_prism_clean_square.py` (NumPy and SciPy sparse, approximately 2.3 seconds). This exhausts the 75 four-word diagrams and additional linear relations, not 60-site exterior configurations.
