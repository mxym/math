# Five-adic circulation marks at an arbitrary saturated triangle

Call a triangle T saturated when all twelve possible transversal prism partners occur, so its 36 wing sites are partitioned into twelve transversal triangles. This does not require every prism to lie in a grid, or any symmetry. See [the existing spread argument](covered-spread.md) and [the wing marking construction](triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md). Contract the twelve transversal triangles, retaining the eighteen within-wing mate edges, to form a loopless cubic multigraph K on twelve vertices, with c connected components. Its edges are properly colored by the three physical wings. A wing site is a dart of K, its mate is the reversed dart, and its other two neighbors are the other darts with its tail. This description permits parallel edges.

**Universal mod-five section.** Use the rank-44 minus-four marks, with Gram `3I−A+J/9`. Their lattice has determinant `7^(44−r)/9` and is unimodular at five at every Seidel rank. Write L=3I−D for the ordinary 36-site wing Laplacian. For a mod-five harmonic coefficient function h on darts let s_i be its sum at tail i. Then the Laplace equation on e from i to j reads `4h_e−h_reverse=s_i`, or `h_e+h_reverse=−s_i`. Hence s is constant on each component. Subtract that component constant divided by three from every dart value to make all tail sums zero. What remains is a mod-five antisymmetric divergence-free flow. Conversely every such flow and every component constant is harmonic. The flow space has dimension 18−12+c=6+c. Since 36 is nonzero modulo five, adding J/9 restores exactly one of the constant directions. The wing Gram has rank **31−2c**, and a maximal nonsingular wing block has nondegenerate orthogonal complement of dimension **13+2c=2(6+c)+1**.

Component constants of total weighted sum zero map to the actual zero mark, including when an individual component size is divisible by five. Indeed a component of size s contributes s/9 to each wing evaluation, −2s/9 to a triangle-root evaluation, and −s/18 to a far evaluation, using s/6 far contacts; these identities hold at five. The actual radical image is therefore generated exactly by the 6+c flows. It is totally isotropic. For a true far site its pairing with a flow is minus the sum of the flow on its contact word. A flow is invisible precisely when every far word evaluates to zero. If no flow is invisible, splitting off 6+c hyperbolic planes leaves a single nonsingular line over F5. This is a **dichotomy**, not an assertion of independence.

A nonzero invisible mod-five flow can be lifted to a genuine integral circulation h on K: for example fix a spanning forest and use the integer fundamental cycles to lift its chord coordinates. Extend h by zero away from the wing. It has zero total and zero sum in each physical wing (darts paired by reversal), and Dh=−2h over the integers. If B is the far-versus-wing contact incidence, then `(3I−A)h` is zero on T, equals 5h on the wing, and equals −Bh outside. Thus invisibility gives a nonzero integral minus-four graph field `f=(3I−A)h/5`, with `||f||²=7||h||²/5`. For arbitrary integral circulations, visible or not, the SRG identity gives `||Bh||²=10||h||²`, including orthogonality of evaluations of circulations from different components. In particular the kernel is totally isotropic also for the dart-flow metric reduced modulo five, by applying the norm identity to sums and polarizing (the metric itself can be degenerate).

**Independent prism flows.** The current spaces of *any three distinct prism components* inject jointly. A prism contracts to three parallel differently colored edges; a nonzero flow has values ±a, ±b, ±c on its six sites with a+b+c=0. Its alphabet has at least three and at most four elements of F5: at least two distinct nonzero magnitudes or a zero as well, since three equal-magnitude signed nonzero values cannot sum to zero. If a zero occurs the alphabet has exactly three; otherwise it misses zero. Each far word selects one site per prism, and each pair of sites from different prisms occurs (twice or once according to whether their physical wings differ or agree). Hence two nontrivial prism alphabets add to all of F5, since any two subsets of size at least three have intersecting opposite translates. This cannot be canceled by a third prism alphabet of size at most four. The same positive pair coverage rules out dependencies on one or two prisms.

**Independent flat twelve-component flows.** Suppose W contains two flat twelve-components F, each the [allowed identity-return template](twelve-sites.md) with the three different mate matchings. Each contracts to K4, so its circulation dimension is three. The actual images of their flows are independent as a six-dimensional space over F5. This holds with **no assumption on the third twelve sites or Seidel rank**. A single nonzero flow is visible: a far word chooses two sites, each allowed pair occurring with its specified multiplicity, and all 124 nonzero flows have nonconstant evaluations (also directly checked by the code). If two flows give the same actual mark, their far evaluations agree everywhere. Enumerate the 124 nonzero coefficient labels on each component, antisymmetric under mate reversal with all four tail sums zero. The multiplicity of a tag e=(a,b) is `m(e)=2−D_ab−(D²)_ab−[a,b in the same wing]`. There are 48 positive tags of total multiplicity 60, and the histogram of each flow's tag sum is therefore fixed. Only 4,808 pairs of flow labels have the same histogram.

For two specified labels h,k let n(e,f) count far sites with those contacts in the two components. Its marginals are m(e),m(f). For each cross pair of sites a,b, summing over e containing a and f containing b gives exactly `2−[a,b in the same wing]`. Disallow columns with unequal flow sums modulo five, or more than two contacts in a physical wing. These are necessary constraints on nonnegative integer occupancies; a fortiori they must hold over the nonnegative reals. A K4 vertex permutation permutes the three physical edge colors; relabel the two components independently subject to the *same* color permutation, exchange equal components, and scale both flow labels by one common nonzero scalar. These legal coordinate changes reduce the 4,808 ordered pairs to 21 representatives. No global graph automorphism or independence of the 60 choices is assumed. For each representative the [checker](saturated_five_currents.py) decodes a signed integer row u, verifies u·rhs<0 and u·column≥0 on every allowed column. These 21 strict contradictions check 7,512 columns in total using Python's standard library, without any solver or numerical tolerances. The two-flow result demonstrates that a dependency cannot be postulated merely to avoid the one-line alternative; it does not by itself exclude a saturated triangle.
