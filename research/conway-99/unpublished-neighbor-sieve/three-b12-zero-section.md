# Three B12 wing components are impossible at rank 36

Let B denote the twelve-site triangle-wing type of tree number 7140 in [triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md](triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md). There is **no** triangle with all three components of type B in a graph of mod-seven Seidel rank 36. This removes one further unordered multiset from the [66 previously ruled out](triangle-wing-aa-direct.md); it does not exclude all decompositions into twelve-site components.

**Dependency of the three actual currents.** Use \(M=3I-A+J/9\) modulo seven, with diagonal zero and off-diagonal entries \(4-A_{ij}\). Each B component has a visible one-dimensional harmonic radical generated by the centered modulo-seven vector \(h=(2,1,5,6;0,0,0,0;5,6,2,1)\). Its associated mark \(u_c\) pairs with a far vertex of pair tag \(e_c=\{i,j\}\) by \(-h(e_c)=-(h_i+h_j)\); every far vertex takes two contacts from each component and two per physical wing. By the cited two-component incidence/parity test any two B currents are linearly independent in the actual marking space. The full wing Gram has rank 31: it has one nondegenerate constant direction and three centered quotients of rank ten each. Remove a nonsingular 31-mark principal basis \(I\). Its nondegenerate five-dimensional orthogonal complement \(V\) contains the three current marks, which are mutually orthogonal and isotropic. Thus they span exactly two dimensions. The relation has three nonzero coefficients, and forces
\[
 k_1h(e_1)+k_2h(e_2)+k_3h(e_3)=0
\]
on every word. The centers are real graph sites; no group action on the unknown graph has been assumed.

**Fourteen charts.** The B template has an elementary relabeling negating its current while preserving the three individual wings, and a relabeling interchanging the two outer wings (with either current sign). The verifier checks these actions directly on the literal cubic B graph. Consequently each B component needs only its middle physical wing \(w_i\in\{0,1,2\}\) and its unsigned coefficient \(k_i\in\{1,2,3\}\) (residues up to sign). Permuting the three components and physical wing names, and multiplying the entire relation by a common nonzero scalar, gives 14 canonical triples of pairs \((w_i,k_i)\). For each, enumerate all three B pair tags of positive mandatory multiplicity, taking exactly two contacts in every physical wing and satisfying the above relation.

**A three-dimensional quotient only on ten mandatory vertices.** Keep the words for which \(h(e_1)=0\). The ten possible first-component tags are exactly the ten distinct multiplicity-one tags of the [B12 zero-word inventory](b12-flat24-zero-words.md). Each tag must occur once. The Gram of the 31 basis rows has nonsquare determinant (the three centered B quotient signs are negative and the constant sign is positive; also checked directly). The full rank-36 graph marking form has nonsquare determinant as in the cited wing discriminant theorem, so \(V\) has square determinant. Its totally isotropic plane spanned by \(u_1,u_2\) splits off two hyperbolic planes (joint determinant square), leaving a line of square type.

Every word in our ten classes has residual in \(u_1^\perp\). Its pairings inside this set are obtained in \(u_1^\perp/\langle u_1\rangle\): one hyperbolic plane containing the nonzero image of \(u_2\), plus a line of norm one up to square. For a word \(z\) put \(p_z=(4\mathbf1-w_z)|_I,\ g_{zz'}=p_z^tG_{II}^{-1}p_{z'}\). Its quotient coordinates can be written \((a,d,t)\), with \(a=-h(e_2)\) prescribed, and \(2ad+t^2=-g_{zz}\). **Enumerate all \(d,t\in\mathbf F_7\) solving this equation, including \(a=0\).** For two lifted words, the forced adjacency is
\[
 4-g_{zz'}-ad'-da'-tt'\pmod7.
\]
It must be 0 or 1. One may impose \(t=0\) on the **first** selected word if it has \(a\ne0\): the common change \(t\mapsto t+sa,\ d\mapsto d-st-s^2a/2\) preserves all pairings and can normalize that one visible \(t\). No normalization is made on a later word when the first \(a=0\).

**Literal compatibility checks.** Two words cannot share more than \(2-A_{zz'}\) near sites, or a pair of near sites having remaining capacity one. For distinct near sites \(i,j\) this capacity is \(2-D_{ij}-(D^2)_{ij}-[\text{same wing}]\). For adjacent words \(z,z'\), every contact \(i\) of \(z'\) must have \(2-w_z(i)-(Dw_z)_i>0\), since otherwise the near-plus-far common-neighbor quota of \(i,z\) has already been filled. Impose the converse check too. A true assignment must yield a clique transversal on the ten tag classes in their lifts. Exact smallest-class-first bitset intersection enumerates all choices. No chart has a transversal. In canonical order, the largest tree has 25,059 nodes and all 14 trees are regenerated in a few seconds. The [finite proof](three_b12_zero_section.py) uses no linear/integer programming or near/far graph isomorphism assumption.
