# A union of eight contraction tails carries at most one invisible five-current

At any saturated triangle, let K₀ be a union of whole components of the three-colored contraction with **eight** tails (24 wing sites, 12 mate edges). The kernel of the far evaluation map on its mod-five circulation space has dimension at most one. Other components, Seidel rank and symmetries are unrestricted. In particular this includes a single connected 24-site wing component. The proof does not assert that a single invisible line occurs.

Suppose two independent invisible currents span E. As in the [nine-edge result](saturated-nine-edge-five-flow.md), any nonzero projective current has at most two zero coordinates on the 12 edges. Count zero incidences between the six projective points of E and the 12 edge evaluations. Any nonzero edge functional vanishes once, a zero functional six times. Thus there are at least twelve zeros and at most twelve; equality forces every edge functional nonzero and every projective current to have exactly ten nonzero coordinates. Each of the six possible projective edge-functional directions occurs twice. At any one tail the three outgoing functionals sum to zero: if two were proportional then all three would have the same direction, impossible. Thus any two form a basis of E*. Fix the first two outgoing colors at the first tail as the two dual basis vectors, uniquely gauging a basis h,g of E.

On ten nonzero edges a current has multiplicities (5,5) or (10,0) up to scaling. In the one-magnitude case every active tail has exactly two active edges: no three equal-magnitude nonzero residues can sum to zero mod five. This would need at least ten distinct tails, impossible within these eight. Consequently h,g and the four other projective combinations have precisely five edges of each magnitude.

**Exact terminal test.** The [checker](eight_tail_five_code.py) reuses basic arithmetic from [the connected twelve-tail checker](connected_saturated_five_rank.py). To cover disconnected K₀ as well, it enumerates the first two alternating matchings by partitions of 4 and all 7!! third perfect matchings. It canonically relabels each connected component by a colored breadth-first traversal, sorts the component codes and minimizes over global color permutations. This gives respectively 19, 9, 2 and 1 types with one through four components. The program enumerates all anchored projective flows h,g from the literal incidence kernel, requiring balanced magnitude (5,5) on every projective combination and h·g=0 mod5. These are necessary restrictions; the latter follows from the exact flow norm identity and polarization. The resulting numbers of candidate bases are 40, 480, 1,152 and 31,104.

Each far word has exactly four contacts in K₀ by the [wing component balance](triangle-complement.md). It uses distinct tails and has at most two letters per physical wing. Form a column for each such four-dart subset on which h and g both vanish. The column has a one on its six unordered pairs. For each pair xy the true total over all far words must be M(x,y)=2−D_xy−(D²)_xy−[x,y in the same physical wing]; here D is the wing adjacency on the closed K₀ part, so no near common neighbor was omitted. Disallow sets containing a zero-capacity pair. If h,g were simultaneously invisible, the integral vector M of multiplicities would lie in the column span over the integers, even if repetitions were permitted; so M reduced mod two would lie in the binary column span. Exact XOR pivot elimination rejects every one of the candidate bases. No solver, graph extension, assumption of a unique word per partial tag, or numerical tolerance is used. A disconnected union can have further individual-component quotas, which are not needed.
