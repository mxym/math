# Two singular 12-site wing components of type A have disjoint current images

In the notation of [the twelve-current theorem](triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md), if two distinct twelve-components of a triangle's wing graph have type A (seven-divisible tree count 7350, evaluated centered harmonic radical dimension two), their images on the sixty far sites have **zero** intersection, without any rank assumption. In particular at Seidel rank 36 **no triangle has two A components**, including when its remaining twelve wing sites consist of two disjoint prisms. This local exclusion does not rule out rank 36 or resolve the graph.

Suppose two nonzero currents have identical evaluations. Normalize a common scalar, and allow every relative labeling of the three wings. With the literal type A described in the preceding note and its 12 wing-respecting-or-permuting automorphisms, two independent coordinate changes within the two copies and an exchange of the copies give 23 orbits of named correspondence data. These operations only identify isomorphic abstract tests; they are never assumed to be symmetries of a candidate graph. Sixteen fail the pair-value histogram, two fail the earlier binary cross-site equations, and one fails a cross-cell capacity. Four remain. Write D for the set of possible pair pairs (e,f) with matching current value and at most two contacts in each true wing. Their cardinalities in the checker order are 366,336,328,324.

Let n_j in {0,1}, j in D, indicate actual occupied four-site contact sets. If R_j lists the two pair marginal rows and four cross-site rows belonging to j, the system is sum_(j:r in R_j)n_j=b_r. The multiplicities and right-hand sides are fixed entirely by the two small components, as proved in the preceding note. The first three finite charts admit **no** integral table. In the fourth there is exactly one table, but it has a site that cannot receive its eight required exterior neighbors.

For the 366-column test the checker stores a binary branching tree with 152 internal variable choices and 153 leaves. Each leaf is an integer vector y. After subtracting the columns previously fixed to one and suppressing all fixed columns, the checker verifies the strict inequality
 y dot b_res > sum_(j unset) max(0, sum_(r in R_j) y_r).
This is a literal integer contradiction independent of any numerical solver or original search process. Both possible integer values occur at each internal node. The accompanying zlib/base64 data file is only the compressed list of integer branch choices and coordinates; it encodes no executable input.

The other three charts are replayed by direct standard-library binary branching. Demand-zero rows delete every remaining column in the row, full rows force all, and elementary reduction modulo two forces unsupported choices by singleton rows. Branching on a binary entry covers both possibilities. Exact respective node counts are 1033, 1389 and 673, with zero, zero and one full 60-site contact table; no artificial node/time cutoff applies.

For the final table, take x of pair labels (e,f). For i in the first component the number of x's eight far neighbors also contacting i must equal 2−[i in e]−|N_W(i) intersect e|, and similarly in the second component. This follows from mu/lambda and from knowing every neighbor in the near part: no root of T meets x, and the contacts in its other wing components have no edges into these two closed components. The demands sum to 32. A neighbor y of x cannot share more than one of the four known contacts of x, by lambda=1. Any two of x's far neighbors have, in addition to x, at most one common known contact by the universal common-neighbor cap two. The checker selects candidate neighbors in the actual 60-site table, removing pairs incompatible with this latter condition and requiring the 24 site demands. No valid row for at least one x excludes the only remaining table. A reconstruction of the exterior graph is not assumed.

The rank accounting now gives 66/455 ruled-out multisets of three previously retained connected twelve-site templates, comprising 36 AUU, 11 ABU, 6 BBU, all 11 AAU, one AAB and one AAA (U signs as before). If A,A accompany two six-site components, both six-site graphs are prisms, hence seven-unit. The wing Gram has rank 37−4−4=29 and the two A current images span four, forcing graph rank at least 29+8=37. For A,A,A the ambient determinant further excludes the only numerical border rank 36 as in the preceding note.
