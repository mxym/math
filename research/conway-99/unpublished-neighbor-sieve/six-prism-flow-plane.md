# At most one invisible five-flow direction on six prism components

At any triangle whose wing graph is six prisms, the invisible mod-five flow space has dimension **at most one**, so at least eleven of the twelve circulation marks are independent. This strengthens the [five-component bound](five-prism-flow-code.md) and does not assume any Seidel rank. It leaves an actual one-dimensional kernel, or no kernel, open.

**Individual directions.** The five-component result implies every nonzero invisible relation uses all six prism components. Generate six multisets of nonzero triples h_i=(a_i,b_i,c_i), a_i+b_i+c_i=0 over F5; independently negate the triples, permute prism names, permute the three physical wing names simultaneously, and scale globally. This gives 1,138 labeled-type orbits. The circulation norm condition excludes 896, and another 23 miss a required pair of signed site labels. Apply the same 540 original site-pair equalities as in the five-component proof. For each of 169 more orbits an explicit integer row u is nonnegative on every allowed word column and has u·b<0. Write V for the remaining set of **50 necessary orbit types**. Membership in V does not establish any actual direction, graph, or even an integral contact design.

**Simultaneous directions.** Suppose the invisible space contains a plane. By the five-component bound, projection onto each component is injective on this plane and hence an isomorphism to that component's F5² flow space. After naming one nonzero generator h by some representative in V, choose the other generator k by fixing its projection at component zero to the lexicographically first nonparallel triple there: this is possible by that isomorphism. At each other component k_j can be any of the twenty nonparallel triples. Importantly the *same* six signed-site choices at every far vertex have zero sum in both directions. In particular, for t=0,1,2,3,4 each k+th must have its full unordered six local triples, folded up to sign, in V after a permitted global color permutation and scalar.

The checker uses partial multiset containment in these oriented six-block types for each of the five sums before branching further. Count local signed triples as digits in base eight (each digit is at most six). A valid whole multiset contributes all of its submultisets; this is just a pruning device and excludes no actual plane. From the fifty possible h, exactly 6,276 ordered full candidate partners remain after these necessary tests, including gauge duplicates. For each such pair retain only six-site words annihilated by **both** generators. Of these cases, 1,972 miss a required pair row, 4,223 more have right-hand side outside their simultaneous F2 column span, and 81 remain after these tests.

**Last two cases.** The 81 yield two label-change types, checked with exact 2-by-2 F5 algebra. Under a common wing permutation, form M_i with columns h_i,k_i on the first two resulting wing coordinates. Each M_i is invertible. A common change of the plane basis can make any chosen M_i the identity by right multiplication. Fold independent component signs, sort the six matrices, and take a canonical minimum over choices of i and wing names. Equal keys give only actual legal relabelings and a common basis change, not an unverified graph automorphism. The two representatives produce 220 and 306 simultaneous words. In each hand the script tries to cover all 540 site-pair multiplicities exactly, using **distinct words, no two agreeing on three wing sites**, since two far sites share at most two common neighbors. Choose a still-positive row with fewest surviving columns, try them in order, and remove any choice conflicting with already selected words or saturating a zero-capacity pair row. Skipping a preceding candidate in later sibling branches is legal: the current branch exhausts every completion whose first chosen item in this row is that candidate, also when its multiplicity is two. No hard stopping limit is used. The trees terminate with contradictions in 4 and 22 calls. This proves the asserted rank bound.

The [Python/NumPy replay](six_prism_flow_plane.py) decodes only 169 signed integer rows; every remaining coverage, basis, parity and nonnegative-integral test is regenerated. No optimization solver, inferred group symmetry of the Conway graph or numerical tolerance occurs.

After splitting off the hyperbolic planes of the visible currents, the remaining nondegenerate space over the five-element field has dimension one (if all twelve are visible) or three (if the invisible kernel has dimension one).
