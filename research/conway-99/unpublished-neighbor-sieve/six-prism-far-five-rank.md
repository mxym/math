# The five-rank of the sixty-site adjacency for six prism wings

Let a triangle have six prism wing components. Write B for its 60-by-36 far/wing incidence and H for the actual adjacency on the sixty exterior sites. Denote the dimension of the kernel of the twelve prism currents evaluated on the words by e. Then
\[
  \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf F_5}(H-I)=47-2e\in\{45,47\}.
\]
In particular this is a restriction on the actual far graph without any mod-seven Seidel-rank or symmetry condition. Moreover the words alone determine a canonical vector space with a form, on which the bilinear form of H-3I has rank exactly 1+2e, hence at most three. This does not exclude six prisms or either value of e.

**Real spectrum and integral word lattices.** Use [the triangle-complement theorem](triangle-complement.md) and [the saturated circulation construction](saturated-five-currents.md) for the contact quotas and current markings. Label the letters in component i by physical wing w and triangle sign s=±1. Each of its six letters occurs ten times; for i≠j, each pair of specified signed letters occurs once if the wings coincide and twice otherwise. Each actual word chooses one letter in every prism, two in every wing. For the cubic wing adjacency D the two SRG block identities are
\[
 HB=2J-B(I+D),qquad H^2+H-12I=2J-BB^{t}.
\]
The subspace of real word functions generated by the columns of B splits orthogonally as follows:
* The constant, with H-eigenvalue 8, and the six signs s_i, with H-eigenvalue −2. Their Gram is 60 times the 7-by-7 identity.
* Ten even wing contrasts x↦∑_i a_(i,w_i), with all six row sums and all three column sums of a zero. Their H-eigenvalue is −1, and their pairing is 24∑a_(i,w)b_(i,w). Indeed each w in i occurs 20 times; a given pair of wings in distinct components occurs 4 or 8 times, according as they agree or differ. Both types of zero sum now give 20+4 on the centered tensor.
* Twelve odd contrasts x↦∑_i s_i a_(i,w_i), ∑_w a_(i,w)=0 for each i separately. These are the mod-five currents before evaluation; their H-eigenvalue is +1. Their Gram is 20 times the ordinary dot product on six copies of the integral zero-sum plane A2. The signed contributions from distinct components cancel.

These spaces span the columns: decompose on signs and on wing means, and remove the redundant even row and column means using the true word quotas. The orthogonal complement has dimension 31; there the second identity gives H^2+H−12I=0. The trace of H is zero, so its multiplicities at 3 and −4 are 18 and 13. In particular the three groups of real eigenvalues reducing respectively to 1,3,4 modulo five have dimensions 25,25,10. This rederives the required spectrum directly from the contact block, without assuming a result about an arbitrary 8-regular graph.

**Integral five-rank.** Work over Z_5 in its standard unimodular dot lattice of rank 60. Its orthogonal split into the three primary summands above is integral and unimodular on each: their monic spectral factors are coprime modulo five, so Bezout/CRT yields integral self-adjoint idempotents. Call the primary summand of residue one N, of rank 25. Inside N the integral images E of the twelve odd contrasts have Gram 20·A2 on each block, so E*/E is elementary of order 5^12 locally (3 is a unit). Let E' be their primitive closure in N. The inclusion E⊆E'⊆E* gives 5E'⊆E. Multiplication by 5 identifies E'/E with ker(E/5E→N/5N): indeed that kernel is (E∩5N)/5E=5E'/5E. Its dimension is precisely e by the definition on actual far words. The elementary group E'^*/E' therefore has order 5^(12−2e), by index/discriminant; it is elementary because 5E'^*⊆5E*⊆E⊆E'. For the rational orthogonal projection P onto the +1 space (the span of E), primitivity of E' and unimodularity of N imply P(N)=E'^*: any integral functional on E' extends to N. On N, H+4I=5P since the only other eigenvalue is −4. Its reduction has rank 12−2e: inside N/5N the image is 5E'^*/5E' by E'∩5N=5E'. As H−I≡H+4I mod 5, and H−I is invertible on the other 35 dimensions, this proves rank 47−2e. Finally [six-prism-flow-plane.md](six-prism-flow-plane.md) proves e≤1.

**Canonical balanced-word quotient.** All subsequent spaces are over F5. The constant and the six signs remain linearly independent. To prove this, take coefficients in {0,±1,±2} for a hypothetical nontrivial linear relation, with a unit entries and b entries of magnitude 2. The squared integer evaluation on the sixty words sums to 60(a+4b), and each evaluation is divisible by five. Hence a≡b mod5. Up to rescaling by 2 and reducing the coefficients again, (a,b) is (1,1),(2,2),(3,3),(5,0) or (6,1). In the first case the odd evaluation has absolute value at most 3. In the second and last cases its parity is even and its absolute value is <10, forcing every evaluation zero over the integers. In the two remaining cases its absolute value is always 5 (odd, nonzero, <10), incompatible with mean square 15 or 5 respectively. This proves the assertion. The ten even contrasts are nondegenerate modulo five (6 and 3 are units, and 24 is a unit). The three sets belong to distinct primary summands: signs/constant in residue 3, centered odd in residue 1, and the even contrasts in residue 4.

Set C=col(B), K=ker(B^t)=C^perp, R=C∩K. In the residue-one primary summand C consists of the 12−e independent mutually orthogonal isotropic odd images. In the residue-three summand C is the 7-dimensional totally isotropic sign/constant space; in residue four it is everything. Therefore dim C=29−e, dim R=19−e, and K/R has its two nondegenerate orthogonal primary pieces of sizes 1+2e and 11. The second SRG identity shows that H satisfies (H−I)(H−3I)=0 on K; here J vanishes since 1∈C. Thus on K/R the actual H acts as scalar 1 on the first piece and as 3 on the second. In particular the restriction of x^t(H−3I)y to K has rank 1+2e. The underlying spaces C,K,R and their incidences are fixed by the words, even though their final eigenspaces need not be. Two completions with the same actual words induce operators differing in rank at most 2+4e≤6 on K/R, not necessarily on the whole sixty-dimensional space.
