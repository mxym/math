# No orthogonal Fano pair at Seidel rank 36

**Theorem.** If the Conway graph exists and its Seidel matrix has rank 36 modulo seven, no two actual norm-14 Fano directions are orthogonal. This includes displaced Fanos: no seven-integrality assumption is made on either normalized norm-two root. The proof uses only actual site marks in a 54-site configuration. It is not an exclusion of rank 36.

**Geometric input.** Start with the [unique 24-site signed core of an orthogonal pair](two-fano-core.md). Its four five-site exclusive sets are F+,F−,G+,G−. Write Z_F for the 15 vertices missing the support of F. Each seven-site sign together with Z_F is a Hoffman 22-coclique. Consequently Z_F is independent, and each vertex outside the signed support and Z_F has one neighbor of each sign and exactly three neighbors in Z_F. The two fifteen-sets are disjoint and avoid both signed supports, by the [orthogonal endpoint counts](../four-root-exclusion/README.md). Thus each Z_F site sees a pair of exclusive G signs in the core; conversely each G-exclusive site sees three Z_F. Interchanging F,G gives the symmetric facts, including the 3-regular incidence between the two fifteen-sets.

**First 39 sites.** The multiplicities of the labeled +,− contact pairs of the Z_F sites are a 5-by-5 nonnegative integer matrix with row and column sums three. Each entry is at most 2−A_ij−|N_core(i)∩N_core(j)|. These conditions give 4,987 tables. We quotient by the isomorphisms of this fixed 24-site labeled-core graph preserving F,G separately as unoriented lines. They induce four distinct cell permutations and give 1,357 representatives. The checker generates these permutations by an incidence-preserving backtrack; it does not assume any automorphism extends to a 99-graph. Label the 15 vertices in the order of the cells, repeating a cell as often as recorded. Once this is done the entire induced 39-site graph is fixed.

Its ordinary mark Gram A=3I−Adj+4J over F7 must embed in the [36-dimensional nondegenerate, nonsquare-type space](triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md). This eliminates ranks greater than 36 and rank-36 square type. The remaining 711 tables have:
- rank 36: 416;
- rank 35: 269;
- rank 34: 26, all with nonsquare nondegenerate determinant.

Choose a nonsingular principal index set I of size r=rank(A), put R=A_II^{-1}, and set C equal to its orthogonal complement in the true ambient space. For rank 36, C is zero; for rank 35 it is a nonsingular line; for rank 34 it is a *square-determinant plane*, since both the ambient and the 34-space have nonsquare determinant. This plane is anisotropic over F7, because −1 is nonsquare. In all cases C has no nonzero isotropic vector. Subtracting the projections onto I from each of the existing 39 marks gives vectors of norm zero in C (the Schur complement of A_II is zero). They are therefore actually zero, **even for ranks 34 and 35**. This justifies imposing every dependent-row identity below. Up to change of basis the complement has diagonal D empty; a one-entry representative 1 or 3 of its determinant class; or (1,1).

**The other 15 sites.** Each Z_G site has precisely two contacts among the 24 core sites, one on each exclusive F sign, and exactly three of the labeled first fifteen. Try all 23 positive-capacity sign-pairs and all C(15,3) triples. For the resulting incidence vector v, demand v^t Adj_39≤2·1^t−v^t componentwise. This is the true common-neighbor bound with each old site. Its required 39 modular pairings are the row w=4·1^t−v^t. Retain rows only when w_I R A_{I,*}=w. Enumerate *all* vectors y in the at-most-two-dimensional complement with y^t D y=−w_I R w_I^t mod7, the exact norm-zero condition. Each candidate is now a pair (v,y), including all multiplicities of possible residual orientations.

Two chosen candidates must have modular pairing w_I R w'_I^t+y^t D y'=4 and obey v·v'≤2, since Z_G is independent. The same candidate cannot be reused (its self-pairing is zero). Each of the ten exclusive F sites and each of the 15 Z_F sites must occur in exactly three chosen v's. These 25 column constraints, together with pair compatibility, are all the terminal backtrack uses. An exact search in each of the 711 charts never reaches 15 vertices. At a node take any column still needing u>0 hits. If fewer than u candidates still cover it, reject. Otherwise, every solution has a first remaining candidate that hits that column, so branch on this candidate, deleting its earlier alternatives, incompatibilities, and any now-full column. The verifier reports 1,626,328 total recursive calls, maximum 25,182 in a chart. No whole graph is enumerated or guessed.

**Replay.** Run [rank36_no_orthogonal_fanos.py](rank36_no_orthogonal_fanos.py) next to [two_fano_core.py](two_fano_core.py). Python 3 and NumPy suffice. All modular matrix entries, lifts, products, and pruning rules are integral; the short matrix dimensions bound all native 16-bit dot products by 2,000 before reduction. The proof uses labels only for enumeration, not global symmetry. The first 39-site conditions, pairwise 54-site bounds, and incidence quotas are *necessary*, not asserted sufficient in general.

**Consequences.** Distinct Fano directions have absolute dot at most four, since agreement on any three sites of the same sign determines the Fano line. Seven-integrality of the normalized pairing therefore forces exact orthogonality. In particular, each integral maximal seven-neighbor H contains at most one actual Fano line, so its norm-two Fano population is 0 or 2. If one is original, it also pairs seven-integrally with every graph-dual Fano: there can be no other Fano direction at all, so total raw N14=2. By the [two-edge-cut emission and 63-triangle count](arbitrary-wing-thin-cuts.md), at most one original direction can be emitted: no triangle has two distinct thin cuts, at least 168 of the 231 triangles are thin-cut-free, and at least 42 vertices lie on seven such clean triangles. No assertion is made that a Fano must exist.
