# Uniform signed norm-18 classes with neutral 24 and cost 21 allowed

Fix a maximal determinant-49 extension H. Suppose its selected signed-unit support is contained in `{018,024,121}`. On its norm-four circle, each of the eight **signed** classes contains exactly the same integer n of eligible oriented norm-18 fields, with **23≤n≤58**. Let r be its folded positive-charge (1,21) mass, M the number of eligible Hoffman cocliques, f the number of eligible antipodal Fano lines, and S its extra unit (1,23) mass beyond the ordinary graph marks and the eligible Fano translates; then r is an integer and **r+M is odd**. These are necessary conditions, not an exclusion.

Throughout G means literal H-eligible and W denotes G divided by eight on a nonisotropic species, unchanged on an isotropic one. For the fixed-H site/Fricke and site rows see the [terminal-transfer audit](cost21-terminal-transfer.md): the C columns represent H, not the smaller original-zero sector. Thus f=W014/2, M=W222 and S=W123−99−7W014. The [exact checker](terminal_018_024_121.py) verifies, using nonnegative actual site rows and the zero-class triangle mark,
```
G444 ≤ 600,      177 ≤ N18 ≤ 943/2,
6W018 = 291−17W121+32f+5M+2S,
G444 = 216−44W121+48f+32M+4S.
```
Its raw species exclusions are 016,020,125,226,228,230,337. In particular raw 125 vanishes because N16 and unit 125 both vanish. Raw 026 and 127 do not vanish: per projective residue they are 8 times 121 and 9 times 018 respectively via the site-preserving transfers in the [residue proof](rank42-u121-residues.md). Their partners' signs are legitimate in the projective equations as the graph-site shifts have zero seven-class.

For each terminal norm circle take the four opposite-pair half-sums in lexicographic residue order, minus the common mean W. For neutral fields (negation) and (1,21) fields (edge-reflection u↔d_p+d_q−u), each half-sum is the true population in **each** of the two individual signed classes. In other positive charges it may be a half integer. The [checker](terminal_018_024_121.py) regenerates 36 projective variables with four free deviations from the norm-four auxiliary quadratic and each other allowed shifted lattice, exactly as in the earlier calculation, this time retaining raw N24 and removing raw 125.

Write the four 018 deviations as (x,y,−y,−x). Both x+y and x−y are integers (differences of signed counts). Label the four 121 half-sums U_i and 444 half-sums V_i starting at zero. The exact rows yield
```
55(U1−U0)=48(x+y),       55(U3−U2)=48(x−y),
55(V1−V0)=−516(x+y),     55(V3−V2)=−516(x−y).
```
Since the U_i are integers and gcd(48,55)=1, both x±y are multiples of 55. A nonzero such multiple gives a V_i difference of magnitude at least 516. Nonnegative actual half-sums would then require G444=2ΣV_i≥1032, contradicting 600. Therefore x=y=0, W018=n is an integer, and the shell bounds give the stated range.

With x=y=0 the same exact rows give the deviations of 024,121,444 as t(1,1,−1,−1), 5t(1,1,−1,−1), and −(45t/2)(1,1,−1,−1) respectively. In particular 2r is an integer (the sum of two 121 counts with opposite deviations); 17r is an integer by the displayed scalar relation and integrality of n,f,M,S. Hence r is an integer. The same relation modulo two gives r+M odd. Also 2t is an integer by neutral 024 negation and 5t by r and each individual 121 count being integers: t is an integer. G444≥180|t| consequently forces **|t|≤3**, so each signed 121 class differs from its folded mean by at most 15, and each signed 024 class by at most three. No equality of the eight 121 classes has been assumed.

If the support restriction holds over the whole graph of rank R, averaging only the proven per-H 23≤W018≤58 yields 23p≤N18(graph)≤58p, p=7^((44−R)/2)+1, without graph-class equidistribution in the larger ambient discriminant group. For any R below 42 this actual total is also divisible by 14. Indeed apply the [all-radius tangent congruence](rank40-two-type-tangent-deck.md) to the norm-four neutral residues in each codimension-one pencil of maximal extensions as in the [terminal transfer](cost21-terminal-transfer.md). Count just one of the two opposite signed classes, whose populations agree by negation. Each pencil gives Σ n_H ≡ 0 modulo seven. Summing over pencils, each maximal H occurs a number congruent to one (its number of hyperplanes over F7), so the sum over all H is also zero modulo seven. The number of maximal subspaces divided by p is a product of factors 7^j+1: induct on Witt index by counting isotropic-line flags, using (7^w−1)(7^(w+1)+1)/6 lines at index w. Thus this ratio is prime to seven. Since Σ n_H=(#H/p)N18, seven divides the graph-wide count, and antipodes give divisibility by 14.

At R=38 the independent [rooted triangle-free bound](norm18-triple-remaining-bridges.md) also applies, since N16=N20=0, leaving same-oriented **7924≤N18≤12320**. No nonexistence or graph realization follows from that interval.

On rank 38, the [six-bit edge parity bound](cost21-edge-parity.md) gives a sharper graph-scale interval `10080≤N18≤12320` for the same oriented norm-18 count and rules out all original-zero-class Hoffman cocliques. Displaced cocliques and the support itself remain open.
