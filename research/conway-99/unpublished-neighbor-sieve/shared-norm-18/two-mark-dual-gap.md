# Full dual gap and the two rank-six mirrors

Assume exactly the hypotheses and notation of [the simultaneous residual](simultaneous-hoffman-33-residual.md). Let T be the marked 33-set, C the unique 22-coclique, Δ=|C∩T|, ℓ=3Δ−22 and D=154·22−ℓ². The following stronger assertion concerns **all** of W*, not just its seven-primary extension.

Every nonzero z in W* of squared norm at most 23/10 is the projection of an actual neutral norm-18 field t/√7 whose η=t·(-1_T) is ±9. Its squared norm is 108/53 for Δ=5, or 153/76 for Δ=12. In particular min(W*)>2. Moreover the antipodal pairs through 23/10 are in bijection with other true 33-sets T' with |T∩T'|=24: orient η=9 so T contains the nine negative sites and none of the nine positives of t; the signed difference -1_T−t is an all-negative G(3,33) field. Conversely the oriented difference of the two indicators is such a t. The fields project injectively by the preceding note.

**Complete finite argument.** Because W is primitive in U, restriction of integral linear functionals gives pr_W(U*)=W*. Lift z to x in U*. Write x=b°/√7 in its folded graph dictionary. Alter x by integer multiples of the two graph fields -m_T/7=w°/√7 and -m_C/3=c°/√7; the altered lifts need not remain in U*, but are actual charged graph fields in the enlarged graph dictionary. The W projection is unchanged, and coefficients of the new plane component in the real basis (m_T/7,m_C/3) may be taken in [-1/2,1/2]. Flip the entire field if necessary so folded charge 0≤a≤4. Then

    (t−11a²/9)/7=||x||² ≤ 23/10+(22/7+22/9+2|ℓ|/21)/4.

In particular t≤48 for either determinant, so the checker's t≤50 loop covers the range. Define p=b·(-1_C), s=b·(-1_T). They are integers of absolute values bounded by √(22t),√(33t). Because C has trivial seven class, p≡4a mod7. Because the terminal discriminant form is binary anisotropic of square determinant, with T class of norm 1 (discriminant pairing scaled to the squared graph norm), t−2a²−(s−6a)² is a quadratic residue including zero modulo seven. If α=11a/3−s and β=22a/21−3p/7, the two centered coefficients are 7(22α−ℓβ)/D and 3(154β−ℓα)/D, and

    ||z||²=(t−11a²/9)/7−(22α²−2ℓαβ+154β²)/D.

These exhaust the candidates by integer arithmetic. All graph signatures of all translates i c+j w, folded by adding a uniform integer, must remain legal. The checker only *rejects* a candidate when an actual translate with |i|,|j|≤3 is forbidden; no converse completeness about this radius is needed. The tests use the two-type raw shell absences, ordinary universal charge floors/parity, no neutral norm14 on this face, and the one actual (2,22) field (-1_C). No individual N18/33-set angle profiles beyond the displayed residue constraints, chosen 33-set, or root-set assumptions enter the enumeration. The only candidates left are (a,t,p,s)=(0,0,0,0) and (0,18,0,±9). The latter have the indicated squared norms. **Applications to positive rank-six complements.** A mirror H is a positive even lattice of rank six, determinant D and discriminant form opposite to W. It glues to W to produce an even unimodular positive rank-48 lattice; every root is pure in H since W* has minimum greater than 2.

At Δ5 start from the standard simple-root basis of D4 and pair two extra marks with different outside simple roots, one pairing of +1 for each. Give those marks Gram [[20,5],[5,46]]. Their Schur complement after the D4 root span is [[19,9/2],[9/2,45]], determinant 3339/4. This large Schur form proves H has exactly the 24 roots of D4. The dual of the second extra-coordinate functional has square 76/3339 and generates the cyclic discriminant; P's generator has square 22/3339. The unit ratio 76/22 is square mod3,53 and nonsquare mod7; this matches -W including the 3² component by odd-prime lifting. There is no 2-primary component.

At Δ12 start with A4⊕A1, attach one mark of norm320 at a simple end of A4 by a +1 pairing. The Schur norm is 1596/5 and det=3192. There are exactly the 22 roots of A4⊕A1. On the 3/7/19 components the dual mark norm is 10/3192, versus 22/3192 for P, with ratio square/nonsquare/square. At 2, A1 gives order two with quadratic value 1/4; the other line has coefficient 4·399/5 with unit 3 mod8, giving order four with quadratic value 3/8. The plane P diagonalized at 2 has coefficients 2·77, 4·57/11, with the same quadratic values. Thus H is a mirror. These collars exhibit two different fresh exact E48 root counts without presupposing any graph automorphism or admitting mixed roots. They do not exclude a tuple by themselves.
