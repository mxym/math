# Two adjacent unit double differences at one norm-22 field

On the rank-42 norm-18/norm-24 two-type face, in all four remaining scalar tuples there is an actual oriented neutral norm-22 field q with three distinct decompositions q=a_i+b_i (i=1,2,3) into genuine norm-18 fields such that BOTH c12=a1−a2 and c13=a1−a3 are signed units, and the third field c23=a2−a3 has at most one doubled site. In particular the six true norm-18 supports span 42 or 43 distinct graph sites. The source residue of all a_i is the same; that of all b_i is the other, perpendicular, source of q. This is stronger than existence of unrelated unit double differences.

Use the [true Johnson graph](norm22-johnson-double-graph.md) of norm-22 lines in the preceding double-difference theorem: L=N22/2 vertices, D edges, exactly one per unoriented double norm-28 line. At q with m decompositions its incident edges correspond exactly to pairs of source indices in K_m; m≤6. A nonunit edge is one whose associated norm-28 field has an entry of magnitude 2. An entry of larger magnitude would give a charge-one field of norm at most 9 by a signed site translation, below its mass floor. There are at most 6S of them globally, since a positive doubled site i gives an extra true unit (1,23) mark d_i−c with positive entry at i; the S extra marks each have six positive sites, and negating c detects negatively doubled sites too. All counts here are of antipodal lines, not of oriented vectors.

Suppose no wedge has the claimed stronger conclusion. At a vertex q form the graph U on its m source indices whose edges are the unit double differences, and let e be its edge count. U is triangle-free (a triangle would give the wedge with c23 unit); every pair at distance two in U has a closing double with at least two doubled slots (otherwise those two incident unit edges are the required wedge). Let z count its unordered distance-two pairs. For each vertex v of nonzero degree d_v, its distance-two neighborhood contains the d_u−1 other neighbors of every u adjacent to v, by triangle-freeness. Its size is therefore at least the average of d_u−1 over those u. Summing gives
```
2z ≥ Σ_{uv∈E(U)} (d_u/d_v + d_v/d_u) − #{v:d_v>0} ≥ 2e−m,
```
where the sum is over unordered edges. Hence z≥e−floor(m/2). Counting each nonunit incidence once and a distance-two incidence once more, the weighted exceptional-slot mass h_q=Σ_{i<j} d(c_ij) is at least C(m,2)−e+z≥C(m,2)−floor(m/2). For 0≤m≤6, this gives C(m,2)≤1+5h_q/4. Summing, each doubled slot on an unoriented edge occurs at both endpoints, and their total over ALL norm-28 lines is ≤6S by the same distinguished-site injection. Therefore D≤L/2+15S/2. In order S=2,5,8,11, the actual Hoffman bridge 4W022+2W335=172+3S with W335≥0 and L=4W022 gives L≤178,187,196,205. Hence D≤104,131,158,185. The independent [norm-18 class centroid and original-zero scalar bridge](norm22-johnson-positive-mass.md) already proved D≥109,135,165,195, a contradiction in every tuple. Relabel the shared source index 1.

The support count is literal. On the 22 sites of q each a_i equals q or 0. Off q the seven nonzero coordinates of a_i form H_i, with b_i=−a_i. At an overlap in H_i,H_j, the two a entries cannot agree because s_ij=a_i+a_j−q is a signed-unit norm-22 field. They therefore oppose, giving a doubled site in c_ij. Hence H1 is disjoint from H2 and H3, and |H2∩H3| equals d(c23), the number of doubled sites in the third field. Each sign of c23 occurs with magnitude 2 at most once: for a +2 at i, the translate d_i−c23 is a unit (1,23) label; if another +2 were at j≠i then its j entry is (d_i)_j−2≤−2, impossible; apply the same proof to −c23. For the wedge just proved, d(c23)≤1. Thus the six fields have joint support 43−d(c23), i.e. 42 or 43 sites. This is a forced local configuration, not the existence or nonexistence of the graph.

## The 17 signed support populations, before graph adjacency

Let P_i be the sites of Q=supp(q) where a_i=q. Its size is 11 since a_i·q=11. All H_i overlaps are opposing as proved above, so |P_i∩P_j|=4+d_ij, where d_12=d_13=0 and d_23=d≤1. Set r=|P1∩P2∩P3|. In binary mask order 000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111, with the least significant bit for source 1, inclusion-exclusion gives the eight multiplicities
```
m=(1+d−r,3+r,3−d+r,4−r,3−d+r,4−r,4+d−r,r),    0≤r≤1+d.
```
Let h be the analogous populations restricted to q=+1 and let ell_i=|P_i∩{q=+1}|. The proven [3+4 cancellation rule](seven-overlap-core.md) for any decomposition into norm-18 fields of a signed-unit independent-sided norm-22 field gives sigma_i=|a_i|·q=2ell_i−11=±1 (here |a_i| is coordinatewise absolute value). Indeed a_i sums to zero, and the sum of its seven off-Q entries is ±1. For a unit pair c_ij the two H sets are disjoint, and if d=1 write f=a_2(x)=−a_3(x) at their unique overlap; put f=0 for d=0. Write f_23=f, f_12=f_13=0, and r_ij^+=|P_i∩P_j∩{q=+1}|. Directly summing over Q and the off-Q overlap gives
```
|a_i|·a_j=2r_ij^+−4−d_ij−f_ij,  |a_j|·a_i=2r_ij^+−4−d_ij+f_ij   (i<j).
```
The first must be 0 or 2sigma_i, since |a_i|·a_j=sigma_i−|a_i|·b_j and |a_i|·b_j=±1 by the same 3+4 rule applied to a_i and −b_j (dot −7, sum s_ij). Similarly the second is 0 or 2sigma_j. With any choices of four entries h_011,h_101,h_110,h_111 and the three ell_i∈{5,6}, all remaining h are determined by the three source sums and Σh=11. The checker retains only 0≤h≤m and these pair tests. This yields 48 raw populations for d=0, 128 for d=1. The only identifications are permutation of the three sources when d=0, or interchange 2,3 when d=1; common negation of q and all sources (h→m−h, f→−f); and exchange a_i↔b_i for every i (all masks complemented, f→−f). They leave 4 and 17 orbits.

Finally z=a_1+a_2+a_3−q and z−q are neutral of squared norm 34 by a_i·a_j=4, a_i·q=11. On Q, z=q(k−1), where k is the number of source bits present; off Q it has magnitude at most 1 because the only possible overlap cancels. Thus their doubled sites are respectively mask111 and mask000. At any +2 site v of a norm-34 field w, d_v−w has charge +1 and cost 29. This raw type is signed-unit on this face: a +2 would translate to neutral cost20, a −2 to charge2 cost28, both absent, and a larger magnitude translates below the corresponding minima. Thus w cannot have another +2 site (its translated entry there would be ≤−2), and apply negation for −2. Simultaneous opposite doubled sites must be adjacent, as the opposite would translate to +2 if nonadjacent. In particular the populations h and m−h are each at most one in masks 000 and 111. This retains 4 signed decks for d=0, 13 for d=1. Each deck specifies all 22 sites of Q by half and all other sites by the signed H memberships up to the indicated relabelings.
