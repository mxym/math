# Two adjacent unit double differences at one norm-22 field

On the rank-42 norm-18/norm-24 two-type face, in all four remaining scalar tuples there is an actual oriented neutral norm-22 field q with three distinct decompositions q=a_i+b_i (i=1,2,3) into genuine norm-18 fields such that BOTH c12=a1−a2 and c13=a1−a3 are signed units. The source residue of all a_i is the same; that of all b_i is the other, perpendicular, source of q. This is stronger than existence of unrelated unit double differences.

Use the [true Johnson graph](norm22-johnson-double-graph.md) of norm-22 lines in the preceding double-difference theorem: L=N22/2 vertices, D edges, exactly one per unoriented double norm-28 line. At q with m decompositions its incident edges correspond exactly to pairs of source indices in K_m; m≤6. A nonunit edge is one whose associated norm-28 field has an entry of magnitude 2. An entry of larger magnitude would give a charge-one field of norm at most 9 by a signed site translation, below its mass floor. There are at most 6S of them globally, since a positive doubled site i gives an extra true unit (1,23) mark d_i−c with positive entry at i; the S extra marks each have six positive sites, and negating c detects negatively doubled sites too. All counts here are of antipodal lines, not of oriented vectors.

If the conclusion failed, at every q the unit edges would be a matching in K_m. Write h_q for the number of its incident nonunit edges. Then h_q≥C(m,2)−floor(m/2), and C(m,2)≤1+5h_q/4, as is checked at the seven integers 0≤m≤6. Summing gives D≤L/2+5D_non/4≤L/2+15S/2. In order S=2,5,8,11, the actual Hoffman bridge 4W022+2W335=172+3S with W335≥0 and L=4W022 gives L≤178,187,196,205. Hence D≤104,131,158,185. The independent [norm-18 class centroid and original-zero scalar bridge](norm22-johnson-positive-mass.md) already proved D≥109,135,165,195, a contradiction in every tuple. Relabel the shared source index 1.

The support count is literal. On the 22 sites of q each a_i equals q or 0. Off q the seven nonzero coordinates of a_i form H_i, with b_i=−a_i. At an overlap in H_i,H_j, the two a entries cannot agree because s_ij=a_i+a_j−q is a signed-unit norm-22 field. They therefore oppose, giving a doubled site in c_ij. Hence H1 is disjoint from H2 and H3, and |H2∩H3| equals d(c23), the number of doubled sites in the third field. Each sign of c23 occurs with magnitude 2 at most once: for a +2 at i, the translate d_i−c23 is a unit (1,23) label; if another +2 were at j≠i then its j entry is (d_i)_j−2≤−2, impossible; apply the same proof to −c23. Thus the six fields have joint support 43−d(c23), i.e. 41,42, or 43 sites. This is a forced local configuration, not the existence or nonexistence of the graph.
