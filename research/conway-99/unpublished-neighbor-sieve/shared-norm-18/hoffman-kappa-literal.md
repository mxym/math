# Marked coclique heights: a two-preimage bound and exact affine slot identities

Assume only neutral norm 18 and 24 among the selected unit shells, with no Fano/N14, as in the published two-type reductions. Fix one maximal determinant-49 extension H and an actual eligible Hoffman 22-coclique C; let c=-1_C be its affine charge-two field. All fields counted here are eligible in the same H. The plain counts below are oriented at charge zero and count positive charge otherwise, before any division by eight. Let m_k count eligible N18 fields with k positive and k negative sites in C. These equal sign counts and the range 0≤k≤3 follow from [the original residue balance](two-type-residue-balance.md): t·c≡0 modulo 7, a nonzero ±7 would give a forbidden (2,26) translate, and k=4 would force ten exterior zero heights for a norm18 marking, whose maximum is nine. The standard translation d_i=(3I−A)e_i has charge1, cost23, t·d_i=7t_i for neutral t, and d_i·c=−3 on C, 4 off C.

## The maximal-intersection projection is unit and at most two-to-one

Fix an eligible norm-18 t with k=3. Write X,Y for its three positive and three negative vertices in C, and A_+,A_- for its six positive and six negative vertices off C. The support is 4-regular bipartite, with no edges within a sign. Since C is independent, every x∈X sends its four negative contacts to A_-. The three four-subsets of A_- have mutual intersections at most 2. Their size-two complements are therefore disjoint, giving intersection exactly 2 for each pair of four-sets and giving each y∈A_- exactly two X contacts. The symmetric assertion holds for Y,A_+. Consequently every pair in X has both its common-neighbor slots occupied in A_-, and every pair in Y likewise in A_+. Any zero site q outside C sees at most one vertex of X and at most one of Y.

Put z=t|C extended by zero to the graph and w=3t−(3I−A)z. Then w vanishes on C, is a genuine neutral −4 field, and has norm 36. For completeness, (3I−A) is 7 times the −4 projector on the neutral hyperplane. Independently of local counts,
`||w||²=9·18−21||z||²=36.`
In the residual-lattice dictionary w comes from the projection of t and belongs in the dual of every maximal ternary extension of the rank-22 complement: in the notation of the [residual-theta construction](hoffman-residual-theta.md) the projected x is (1/7)∑_{b∉C}t_b m_b, hence locally in the primal at 3 and compatible at 7. No averaging over 3 is needed for these images.

The local graph contacts give more: w is signed-unit. On A_+ it is 3−2=1 and on A_- it is −3+2=−1. A zero outside site has the signed difference of its two contacts of size at most one, and hence w∈{0,±1} there. Thus w has 18 positive, 18 negative and 41 zero sites on the 77-site outside block set. The exact (contacts in X, contacts in Y) profile on w-positive sites consists of (0,2) at the six actual A_+ and (1,0) at the remaining twelve. For w-negative sites interchange signs, using (2,0) on A_− and (0,1) elsewhere. Every w-zero block has contacts (0,0) or (1,1), with exactly 18 of the latter: no graph common neighbor of a cross pair X×Y can lie in A_± (same signs there are independent), so each such nonadjacent pair has two w-zero witnesses, and each active w-zero block represents one pair.

Consider two distinct preimages t,u with the same w. As in the [ternary-kernel note](hoffman-ternary-rank.md), (z_t−z_u)|C is a nonzero word in ker(Dᵗ mod3), whose minimum Hamming weight is 12. Their six-site C supports must be disjoint; if their difference vanished mod3 then the signed-unit restrictions would coincide literally and w equality would force t=u. Also 3(t−u)=(3I−A)(z_t−z_u), whose squared norm is 21·12=252. Thus ||t−u||²=28 and t·u=4. Their nonzero outside entries are subset markings of the same w signs. Write m,m' for the sizes of their common actual positive and negative sets off C; m+m'=4.

Count common-neighbor witnesses of pairs in the union of their twelve distinct C sites. A block on w-positive sites has combined contact count four on the m intersections of the two A_+'s, three on 12−2m mixed choices, and two on 6+m other sites. The total number of C-pair witnesses on w-positive sites is 6m+3(12−2m)+(6+m)=42+m. Negatives similarly contribute 42+m'. If the two 18-site active sets among the 41 w-zero sites intersect in j, zeros contribute 6j+(36−2j)=36+4j. The C design assigns exactly 2choose(12,2)=132 witnesses, giving j=2. Three preimages would therefore yield three 18-subsets of 41 sites with pairwise intersections two, hence union size at least 54−6=48, impossible. The map is at most two-to-one. In particular m3 is at most twice the unit norm-36 population in each chosen ternary residual, improving the previously known factor three without an additional hypothesis on the design kernel dimension.

An optional lower translation is s=w−t, an actual eligible signed-unit neutral vector of norm30 (t and w agree on the outside support of t, and w vanishes on C). Indeed t·w=12 and ||s||²=30. This map is injective: z=−s|C and t=(s+(3I−A)z)/2. It makes no assertion about the whole norm30 shell.

## Exact affine profiles recover the complete κ distribution

A positive-charge profile below specifies charge, cost, and angle δ=b·c. Translations by c and any d_i preserve the terminal seven-class (the original class of c is in H, and the ordinary site mark has class zero), while negation reverses it. The target profiles and N18 are anisotropic at seven, so all use the same division-by-eight convention when expressed in W or K counts.

1. **(2,40), angle 22.** Exactly b=t+c for every eligible N18 t. Subtracting c gives neutral squared norm 40+22−44=18 and angle zero. On C the entries are k zeros, k copies of −2 and 22−2k copies of −1, so k is recovered exactly. In particular the raw C-square sum over this bucket is 22∑m_k+2∑k m_k.

2. **(3,43), angle 19.** Exactly b=t+c+d_i where i ranges over the k negative sites of t on C. Indeed b−c has charge1, cost 43+22−38=27 and angle −3. On this two-type face, every raw (1,27) field is an N18 translate t+d_i at a negative site, with unique +2 at i. Its dot with c is −3 precisely when i∈C since t·c=0 and d_i·c is −3/4. Thus there are k distinct outputs per t, and distinct input pairs cannot collide. On C each has one +1 (the switched site) and k−1 doubles −2, with C-square 19+2k. Total raw count is ∑k m_k and C-square sum is 19∑k m_k+2∑k²m_k.

3. **(4,50), angle 16, at least one positive site on C.** Exactly b=t+c+d_i+d_j for an unordered pair of the k negative sites of t on C. Each output has the two +1 coordinates i,j on C, k−2 doubles −2, and C-square 16+2k. To prove the converse, a positive coefficient b_i≥2 on C gives after subtracting d_i a charge-three vector of cost 50+23−2(7b_i+8)≤29, impossible; hence every such positive is +1. Subtract at any one of them to get (3,43), angle 19, from the preceding case. That field has exactly one +1 on C, at a *different* site j, since subtraction replaces b_i=1 by −2 and leaves the other C values unchanged. The reconstructed t has both i,j negative: at i, with i≠j, the formula b' =t+c+d_j has b'_i=t_i−1=−2. Thus the two possible choices of inverse site yield the same t and the same unordered pair, and are not counted twice. The precise multiplicity is choose(k,2) per original t, not twice this value.

4. **(4,50), angle 9, at least three doubles −2 on C.** Exactly one b per k=3 t, and in that b there are precisely three doubles, no other exceptional entries and no positive entries. Indeed cost minus negative mass is 50−44=6, consumed exactly by three −2 sites. Forward, with Y the three negative C sites of t,
   `b=−t−c−(∑_{i∈Y}d_i)−1.`
   Adding the three site marks to t+c has charge5 and cost61; folding by +1 then negating gives charge4 and cost50. Its angle is 9. On C it has −2 on Y, −1 on X (the three positive sites of t), and 0 elsewhere, hence C-square15. Off C it has zero at the original nonzero outside sites of t: a positive there meets precisely two Y and a negative meets none. A zero outside sees at most one Y, so the resulting entries are 0 or −1. Conversely for any such (4,50) b choose any i among its three doubles. Map b to b' = −b−d_i−1 (whose charge is again4 and norm50; use b·d_i=−14+8=−6 and fold the charge-five translate). Its angle is 16 and its other two doubles become the two +1 coordinates on C, with i remaining −2. Case 3 now reconstructs t with k=3 and the actual marked pair, and the forward whole-triple formula recovers b. Inverses with different i yield the same t (it is already recovered by subtracting the three read doubles along the whole-triple formula). Thus this bucket is exactly m3, while the k3 contribution to Case 3 is exactly 3m3.

All assertions concern aggregate literal field counts and actual angular slots, not a projected or freely realized harmonic witness. No rank42 tuple exclusion is inferred without further inequalities.


## No norm-18 field can avoid the actual compatible coclique

For any eligible t=1_P−1_N of norm18 put s=1+3·1_N−A1_P. This is the canonical [norm-36 height switch](height-three.md), injective on oriented t. To see the norm directly, the two signs of t are independent and have four cross neighbors at each site, so s=1 on P, zero on N, and 1−h on exterior common-sign height h. There are 81 exterior sites with height sum 90 and total pair contacts ∑choose(h,2)=18, giving ∑h²=126. Hence s²=9+81−180+126=36. Also s=1+D_P−3t, where D_P=∑_{p∈P}d_p has charge9; adding 1 subtracts nine from the charge, so s is a genuine neutral field. It preserves eligibility (ordinary site marks and the constant fold have no seven-component; t's class is multiplied by −3).

If t meets C in k vertices of either sign, each vertex in P outside C has four contacts to C, each vertex of P∩C has none, and the k negative t sites on C each use four such positive contacts. Thus the sum of heights over C outside t's support is 4(9−k)−4k=36−8k. The restriction of s to C therefore has sum k+(22−2k)−(36−8k)=−14+7k, and
`s·c=14−7k.`
If k=0 then s−c has charge −2 and norm 36+22−2·14=30. Its negative is a forbidden raw (2,30) field. There is no distinction between raw and unit in that small shell, by the published short-shell classification. This proves m0=0. In the rank-22 residual theta of the actual Hoffman coclique, every candidate in the D9 shell would be an H-eligible neutral norm-18 field vanishing on C. Hence **D9=0 in every maximal ternary extension of the rank-22 residual**, independently of the choice of ternary subgroup. The canonical map also yields the oriented angle inequalities m_k≤8 K(0,36,14−7k), using the normalized positive-residue notation where appropriate.

A further local consequence is that for k=3, the unique potential height-three exterior site cannot lie in C. If x∈C were such a site, b=s+d_x is the unit charge-one norm31 field from the public height-three switch; its angle is s·c−3=−10. Then b+c has charge3 and norm 31+22−20=33, and the minimal mass inequality forces it to have no positive entries. But x is in the independent C and sees three of t's positives outside C, so among the six original positives of t that miss x there are three outside C (and the three on C). These all retain value +1 in b=s+d_x, hence still in b+c, contradiction.
