# A terminal-lattice transfer excludes the cost-21 trio at every rank

Among the twelve selected signed-unit types the support cannot be contained in `{018,121,125}` at any of the four remaining seven-ranks. ## A codimension-one pencil finishes all three lower ranks

At seven-rank r_graph<42 choose a totally isotropic subspace H0 of codimension one in a maximal isotropic subspace. V=H0-perp/H0 is four-dimensional of minus type. Its fifty isotropic lines h correspond bijectively to the maximal H containing H0 via inverse image. For each opposite pair v of norm 5 in V, let x_v count all positive-charge 121 fields whose original classes lie in H0-perp and project to that pair (both signs of the seven-residue; do not negate the graph field). Norm 5 is 21−11/9 mod 7 in the graph-length convention. A projected pair in h-perp/h lifts to a tangent line of seven opposite pairs in V. The sum of those x's is the literal number of H-eligible fields in the given terminal opposite pair. No multiplicity or transitivity of original classes has been posited.

There are 175 projective points and four tangent lines for each h. If their four sums are a common value L_h for each h, the [finite tangent-deck linear algebra](rank40-two-type-tangent-deck.md) gives sum_h L_h = 0 mod 7 also for radius 5. Independently replay in this directory:

```sh
python -c 'from rank40_two_type_tangent_deck import conditions; assert conditions(5,7)==[[1]*50]'
```

An independent direct elimination at radius five has 124 pivots among the count columns and total rank 125. The code enumerates all 175 opposite pairs in diag(1,1,1,3), all fifty isotropic lines, and the 200 equalities on the 175 x's and 50 sums; the indicated row is left on eliminating the x's modulo 7. Consequently not all terminals through H0 can have the nine unit absences: otherwise every L_h=20 by the local filtration and 50·20=1000≡6 mod 7. In particular the graph-wide unit support cannot be contained in the named trio at r_graph=36,38,40. At r_graph=42 the [already proved 1092 versus 1112 contradiction](cost21-final-plane.md) closes the terminal case. No claim about a larger support is made.
