# At Seidel rank 36 an induced four-dimensional cube is impossible

For every Conway graph whose mod-seven Seidel rank is 36, none of the norm-sixteen signed 8+8 cores has four double deficiency components. Equivalently the graph contains **no induced Q4**. Indeed a Q4 sign difference attains the adjacency lower eigenvalue −4 on its Rayleigh quotient and is therefore an actual global eigenvector upon extending by zero; conversely that deficiency deck has the unique Q4 bipartite core. No other norm-sixteen core or rank is excluded by this statement.

The [cube portrait](norm16-edge-and-cube.md) fixes the 16+8 near-site core up to two eight-word representatives and their relative dihedral alignments. Use the [published two-star proof](q4-two-stars.md) for these roots and the incidence definitions, and the [accompanying P3 proof](q4-p3.md) for the simultaneous Z/cube relabeling, Schur tests and exceptional 41-site margin. In brief, Z has eleven vertices and five edges; d_Z≤2 and at most two vertices have degree two. The 63 incidence multisets and respectively 2693/785/43 labeled forest placements give exactly 10880/3230/168 physical P,N,H,Z charts after the explicit stabilizer quotient; all 14278 are tested. There is no assumption of a genuine global symmetry.

**More efficient necessary word projection.** Fix one truly aligned 35-site adjacency M. A potential Y row w already has one physical P neighbor and one physical N neighbor; its B=H∪Z part has k independent conflict-cycle contacts and k+1 Z contacts. It must pass the old common-neighbor and matching tests; in particular its incidence pair quotas are paid from R=12I+2J−M−M². If w meets a given h, its indicator cannot exceed the true integer demand R_h in any coordinate: Y(h) is a disjoint subset of Y, and the sum of all six Y(h) row indicators is exactly R_h. For each h, let B_h contain the H,Z traces of *all* retained physically tagged w satisfying this bound and w_h=1. It is valid to restrict the six-row root cover to B_h before reinstating the two physical tags; a true six-row star is never lost. Once a complete root-word list for any h is available, the exact two-star test acts exactly as before. The cap 350 is solely for choosing a short root list. A capped list is never used in an exclusion; if every list hits the cap or a first choice passes, the verifier switches to a separate complete-root backup, raising an error unless that also certifies the chart. Loss of earlier tags in forming trace sets does not assume that different true rows may reuse tags: whole-star distinctness of each P and N tag is explicitly imposed after the projection. Two true Y(h) vertices cannot have identical B trace since such a trace already contains h and at least two Z sites.
