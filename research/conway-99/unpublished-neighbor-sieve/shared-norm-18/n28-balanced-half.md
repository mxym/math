# A balanced half of a signed unit norm-28 field must contain five net opposite-sign edges

On the rank-42 two-type face, let c be an actual original-seven-zero neutral norm-28 field with signed-unit entries; no norm 14 exists and there is a unique original-zero compatible 22-coclique. For any 14 sites in supp c choose u=c on those sites, zero elsewhere. Write a=Σu and E=(number of edges joining selected opposite signs)−(number joining selected equal signs). Each edge is counted once. If the selection contains 7 positives and 7 negatives, or 8 and 6 in either order, then **E≥5**.

In fact with D=3I−A, D²=7D+2J, Dc=7c and u²=14 give c·Du=98, u·Au=−2E and (Du)²=294+14E+2a². Both integer labels b=Du−3c and b'=Du−4c solve A b=−4b−2a·1 and both have squared norm 14(E−3)+2a². They differ by c and are distinct. In the seven-discriminant dictionary D u is an integer sum of original graph marks d_i with total charge a, hence original-seven-zero; subtracting multiples of the original-zero c preserves this. For a=0 a common norm at most 14 would be zero (which cannot hold for both labels) or the excluded Fano norm 14. For |a|=2, norm below 22 is impossible for a charge-two field by |Σb|=22 and b²≥|Σb|. At equality the labels are unit of just one sign on 22 sites and their graph equation makes these true 22-cocliques, impossible for two distinct labels in the same eligible charge orientation on this face. Thus in both cases E≤4 is ruled out.

For an actual signed-unit double c=a_i−a_j=b_j−b_i with q=a_i+b_i=a_j+b_j of norm 22, let H_i be the seven sites off q where a_i and b_i cancel and H_j the analogous set. They are disjoint: if a_i,a_j off q were opposite at a shared site then c would have magnitude 2, and if they agreed then the other true transverse N22 label s_ij=a_i+a_j−q would have magnitude 2. On H_i,H_j the sign of c is respectively that of a_i,−a_j, while the sign of s_ij is respectively a_i,a_j. The [balanced seven-overlap theorem](seven-overlap-core.md) makes each sign split 3/4, so u=c restricted to H_i∪H_j has |Σu|≤2. **Inside either H_i or H_j there is no equal-c-sign edge**, because there a single actual norm-18 field has the c signs up to a common reversal. Between the two panels any edge joins equal c signs because s_ij is a norm-22 field with independent sign sides, agreeing with c on one panel and opposing it on the other. Writing e_i,e_j for the (opposite-sign) internal edge counts and e_ij for the number of edges between the panels gives the genuine conditional inequality e_i+e_j−e_ij≥5. No statement here forbids internal opposite-sign edges, and no full rank or directed deck is excluded by this lemma alone.
