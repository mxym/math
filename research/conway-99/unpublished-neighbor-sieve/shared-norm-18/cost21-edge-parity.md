# At most two reflected cost-21 pairs per edge, before choosing a discriminant class

Assume the global selected types 016,020,026,125 are absent. In particular raw G125=0 (its only nonunit source is N16); raw neutral N26 need **not** vanish. There are at most four actual positive-charge (1,21) fields per graph edge, at most **2,772** in all. This bound is independent of rank, and requires neither N24 absence nor lack of Fanos.

Each true u has a unique positive edge e=pq, and its partner is u'=d_p+d_q−u, by the [edge reflection](cost21-hoffman-edge.md). Put z=u−(d_p+d_q)/2, so reflection negates z and z²=12. Both fields are positive at p,q and negative at the common cap s, so z is zero on this triangle. The other 12 neighbors of p are its p-wing and split into six adjacent mate pairs; likewise at q. Exactly six p-wing sites are negative (p has seven negative neighbors, including s) and they are independent, hence there is one in each mate pair. Each x in the p-wing has exactly one neighbor in the q-wing: its two common neighbors with q include p, and the other cannot be s since p,x already have a common neighbor in their mate triangle. By symmetry this is a perfect matching between wings. On each matching edge its two ends cannot both be negative; since twelve of the 24 sites are negative, precisely one end per matching edge is negative. At a wing site z is +1/2 or −1/2. Thus six choices of signs at p-mate pairs determine both wings up to the fixed site/mate indexing, with opposite signs on each matching edge. Outside the triangle and both wings, z has three +1 and three −1 slots (the extra positives of u and u'), described more fully in [the six-cycle theorem](cost21-cycle-edge-cap.md).

Take two **distinct reflection pairs** on e. Orient their z,z' so d=z·z'≥0. Let k be the Hamming distance of their six p-mate choices. Their whole-wing scalar product is 6−2k: a changed p-mate flips four wing slots in total. Moreover z+z' and z−z' are genuine nonzero integral neutral graph fields (u+v−d_p−d_q and u−v), of squared norms 24±2d. The integral raw u·v and (d_p+d_q)²/4=9 show d is integral. Neutral minimum 14 gives d≤5, and absence of 16,20 leaves only 0,1,3,5. All neutral fields of squared norm ≤24 are signed unit by the site shift. In particular on the far sites every common nonzero of z,z' has **the same sign** there: an opposite overlap would double in z−z'. Let h count these overlaps. Thus d=6−2k+h.

* If d=0 or 3 then z+z' is unit as well. For 24 this is the unconditional site shift, and for 30 a double, oriented positive, would yield a charge −1 field of cost at most 25 on subtracting its graph column. More explicitly a +2 gives exactly cost 25 (absent raw G125), and ≥3 gives cost ≤11 below the charge-one minimum. Thus h=0, forcing d=0 and k=3.
* If d=1, z+z' has norm 26 and is nonunit by the absent unit 026. A norm-26 field has at most one double of each sign: a +2 at v gives the unit (1,21) field d_v−(z+z'), precluding a second +2 elsewhere (and a +3 or larger is excluded by the same cost shift). Negate for negative doubles. Doubles in z+z' are precisely its same-sign far overlaps. So 1≤h≤2. Since d=1, h is odd, hence h=1 and k=3.
* If d=5, z−z' is a norm-14 Fano, and outside its support at p it has k positive and k negative neighbors (two opposite entries per flipped mate). Every Fano exterior site sees at most one point from either sign, since every pair within a sign uses up both common-neighbor slots in its opposite shore; hence k≤1. As h≥0, k=0 would give d≥6. Thus k=1. No availability of the Fano's Hoffman endpoints was required.

Every such k is odd. Reversing either z changes k to 6−k, also odd, so this is a property of distinct reflection pairs regardless of orientation. Choosing one six-bit word per pair, any two have opposite word-weight parity. At most two pairs can occur. There are 693 graph edges and two fields per pair. With the unique positive edge recovering the edge from the field, this gives the global count.

**Rank-38 consequence on `{018,024,121}`.** Write N for the graph-wide oriented norm-18 count, and U for the whole positive-charge cost-21 count. The [fixed-terminal identity](terminal-018-024-121.md) has nonnegative f,M,S and yields 6n+17r≥291 in every H. Averaging with the true incidence factor 344 gives 6N+17U≥100104. The [rank-free rooted triangle-free bound](norm18-triple-remaining-bridges.md) applies because N16=N20=0, so N≤12320 and 17U≥26184>17·1540. In particular **U≥1542** by edge reflection. If an *original zero seven-class* Hoffman coclique C exists, every graph U121 field is coeligible with C in some terminal H and all such fields' positive edges avoid C: use the [true Hoffman-angle theorem](cost21-hoffman-edge.md) and the absence of raw G337. (A nonisotropic field's original perpendicular space contains a maximal isotropic H of the ambient minus space.) At most 693−22·14=385 edges avoid C, giving U≤4·385=1540. Contradiction. Thus **M_original=0** on this rank-38 face; no assertion excludes displaced cocliques.

There is also a sharper unconditional numerical interval *on this face*. From the same two terminal identities, Q=G444=12n−366−10r−16f+22M≥0. This is still a nonempty interval; no rank or support closure is claimed.
