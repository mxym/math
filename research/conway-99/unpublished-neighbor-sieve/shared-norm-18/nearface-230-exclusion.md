# The selected support {neutral 18, neutral 24, (2,30)} is impossible at every rank

No hypothetical Conway graph has all selected unit types outside {018,024,230} absent. Indeed the stated absences among the actual fields in even one maximal determinant-49 extension H are impossible. There is no equality-of-classes or automorphism assumption. This removes one more common exact-three support (sixteen down to fifteen), not an entire Seidel rank. A [companion extension](nearface-230-337.md) also allows the additional unit shell `(3,37)`.

Write n=N18(H)/8, s=4n, Z=W028, L=4W022, V=8W024, M=W222, S=W123−99−7W014, u=W230, v=W024. Z counts oriented neutral norm28 vectors of zero residual class, L counts unoriented norm22 lines, and V counts all oriented eligible neutral norm24 fields. The [earlier fixed-H scalar certificate](nearface_230_scalar.py) implies F=W014=0. All (1,29) fields here are unit: translating a +2 or −2 site gives neutral norm20 or a charge-two field of cost28, respectively, both absent (the latter is always unit). Larger entries give costs below the corresponding minima. All (2,30) fields are unit: a +2 would give charge one at cost17, while a −2 gives charge three at cost33 with a positive entry at the pivot, whereas cost33 consists of thirty-three entries −1. Larger entries are impossible by the same shift. Write A=8W129 and B=8u for their true positive-charge counts, with respectively nine and four positive sites per field. Also (1,25) is absent since N16 and U125 are, so every neutral norm30 is signed-unit. Norm22 and norm24 are always signed-unit. These use [the true-unit substitutions](../short-shell-moments.md).

## Source pairs and unsigned overlap counts

Take one orientation of each of the four projective norm18 residue directions, with class sizes n_i summing to s. Number them so directions 1 and 2 are perpendicular, as are 3 and 4. Put p=n1+n2, r=n3+n4, P=n1n2+n3n4 and Δ=p−r. Within a signed direction distinct products are 4 or −3; perpendicular products are 0 or ±7; the four across-block products after a fixed sign choice per block are 1 or −6. This is the [usual anisotropic-plane angular classification](unconditional-cross-shell-centroids.md) with norm16 and norm20 absent. Let e count within-class product-4 pairs, E7 and E6 the unordered exceptional projective pairs of absolute product 7 and 6. Let D count the zero-class norm28 lines having two representations by within-class differences. The [blocked-edge bound](nearface-four-exclusion.md) was proved under just N16=N20=U125=0; in particular it allows U230:
```
2D ≤ 3L+(75/2)S,   e≤Z/2+D,   E6≤V/2,   E7≤(3/2)L+D.
```
For the last inequality, the norm22 centers have multiplicities m(q), E7=Σm and 2D=Σ C(m,2); use 2m≤3+C(m,2) pointwise. These are sums over fixed representatives of antipodal lines.

An ordinary across-block absolute-one pair has, after choosing signs, a norm34 resultant z=t+w with t·w=−1. The number of shared support sites is one plus twice the number of doubled coordinates of z. Each such norm34 line is represented at most once: for a fixed orientation of z both source classes have norm four and scalar product 17 mod seven with its nonzero class, so they are the two points of a line-circle intersection in the residue plane. They are distinct and nonopposite since the class norm is 34 mod seven, neither zero nor 4·18 mod seven. For a second representation t′+w′ in that same ordering, distinct t,t′ have t·t′≤4, so t′·w=17−t′·t≥13, impossible for these distinct nonantipodal source classes. Likewise each norm36 line arising from an absolute-zero pair is uniquely represented: use 18 in place of 17, and class norm 36 mod seven. Each absolute-zero pair supplies two different such lines t+w and t−w (their classes are not antipodes). No signed duplication is introduced: replacing a resultant by its negative reverses both source signs. For any norm34 field there is at most one positive double and at most one negative double: two same-sign doubles, oriented positively, give z−d_i−d_j of charge −2 and norm ≤28 at a nonedge, 14 less at an edge. For the lines under discussion the entries are exactly +2 and the two sites are nonadjacent because both sources are positive there; the norm is then exactly 28, a forbidden raw type. Thus an ordinary pair with t·w=−1 has at most three opposing overlaps.

**The sparse inverse for doubled norm34 sites.** Given a doubled site i of a represented norm34 line, orient z so the site is +2. Then b=d_i−z has type (1,29), b_i=1. Conversely a field b and a positive site i determine the oriented z. Call i a represented pivot if this z is such a resultant, and let k be the number of represented pivots of b. The graph induced by the nine positives of b is a matching. In fact if three positives span h≥2 edges, b−d_i−d_j−d_k has charge −2 and norm 29+69+12−14h−54=56−14h. For h=2 this is the forbidden raw (2,28); for h=3 it is below the charge-two minimum.

Consider two nonadjacent represented pivots i,j of this same b. Their z_i,z_j have the same nonzero signed residue, so order the two signed source classes consistently and write z_i=t_i+w_i, z_j=t_j+w_j. At i both its sources are +1; the two sources of z_j sum to −1 there, so exactly one is −1 and the other is zero. The reverse holds at j. These sites alone give FOUR opposing overlaps between the two pairs. No source t_i=t_j (or w_i=w_j) is possible: t_i·b=7−17=−10, so b+t_i is raw (1,27); it cannot have positive doubles at two pivots. Here every such field is the standard d_h+v from an N18 field with v_h=−1, hence has a unique positive double.

Let α=t_i·t_j and β=w_i·w_j, each in {4,−3}, and γ=t_i·w_j, γ′=w_i·t_j, each in {−1,6}. Their sum z_i·z_j=29−9−9+2=13. If α=β=4, one cross is 6 (no opposing sites, by norm24 unitarity), the other −1 (at most three opposing sites). If exactly one within pair has dot −3, it has just three opposing sites (norm30 is unit) and both crosses are 6. The remaining product pattern is impossible. In all cases some dot-four within pair must oppose **at one of i,j**.

Suppose this occurs at i for the first source class, interchanging names or pivots temporarily if necessary. Then c_i=t_i−t_j has a +2 at i, so g=d_i−c_i has type (1,23) with g_i=1. It is not an ordinary d_h since none has a +1, and F=0 excludes the Fano translates; it is one of the S extra signed-unit fields. The identity w_j−w_i=d_j−g forces g_j=1 since the left side has value at most 2 at j. Thus c_j=d_j−g is doubled at j in the other within-class pair. This works in both directions; for any fixed ordering i<j, call the class doubled at i the first class. The two differences are then c_i=t_i−t_j and c_j=w_j−w_i.

The number of all such nonedge pivot pairs across all b is at most 240S. Indeed an extra g gives at most C(6,2)=15 unordered pairs of positive sites. For a fixed oriented c_i, the [norm28 representation rule](unconditional-cross-shell-centroids.md) allows at most two projective directions, and each gives at most one representation in a chosen signed class. Counting both possible signed classes gives at most four ORDERED differences t_i−t_j=c_i; likewise for w_j−w_i=c_j. Thus at most 16 choices per site pair, each determining b=d_i−t_i−w_i. If more than one assignment were possible for a given original b and pair, this bound would only overcount. As the positive graph is a matching, the k pivots give at least C(k,2)−floor(k/2) nonedges; and k≤2+(C(k,2)−floor(k/2))/2. Therefore the total doubled-site incidence over the represented norm34 lines is at most 2A+120S. We may loosen this to 3A+120S.

For the perpendicular blocks, an absolute-zero pair with support overlap 2j has j agreements and j oppositions. For each of z=t+w and t−w, the j doubles split between signs. Across both lines the total number of same-sign pairs of doubles is at least 2(j−2), using C(x,2)≥x−1 for each of the four sign buckets. Orient each such pair of doubles positively. Its two sites i,j are nonadjacent because their source signs agree in each source. The vector d_i+d_j−z has charge two, norm 50+36−56=30, and +1 on both pivots. Thus all these pairs over all uniquely represented lines are at most 6B, using two of the four positive sites. The total perpendicular support overlap is therefore at most 4P+3E7+6B; every exceptional absolute-seven pair has overlap exactly seven by norm22 unitarity. The across-block total is at most pr+5E6+2(3A+120S); exceptional absolute-six pairs have overlap six.

## Two opposite pressure inequalities

Within a signed class a dot−3 pair has support overlap three. A dot4 pair has overlap four plus twice the number of doubled sites of its norm28 difference. The sum of these latter doubled-site counts over the within-class pairs is at most 12S: every unoriented such line has at most two representations across the projective directions, and the norm28 doubled sites over all lines are at most 6S by the (1,23) pivot inverse. Consider the sum of all s unsigned 0/1 support indicators of size 18. Its squared norm is at least (18s)²/99, and by expanding the diagonal plus twice the preceding overlap sums it is at most
```
15s+3Σn_i²+8P+2pr +2e+6E7+12B+10E6+12A+528S.
```
Here 528=48+480. The class-size expression is at most (9/4)s²+(5/4)Δ² (the omitted terms are minus half the squared differences inside the two perpendicular pairs). Insert the D bounds to get
```
(45/44)s² ≤ 15s+Z+21L+678S+12B+5V+12A+(5/4)Δ².             (U)
```
The [signed centroid proof](unconditional-cross-shell-centroids.md) simultaneously gives 3Σn_i²+√2 pr≤84n+7Z+21L+(525/2)S+(7√2/2)V, using e≤Z/2+D and E6≤V/2. Its left side is at least (12+4√2)n²+(3−√2)Δ²/4; again no class uniformity is required.

For the final arithmetic the [short exact checker](nearface_230_exclusion.py) verifies in the native fixed-H harmonic row span
```
6n=291+2S+5M,                             V=8v, B=8u,
Z=4182+(134/3)S+(380/3)M−4v+8u,            L=606+(22/3)S+(64/3)M−2v+4u,
A=8(234−4S−19M−6u),                       v=222+(13/6)S+(17/3)M+u+W335.
```
Use 1.414<√2<1.415. After inserting the scalar rows in the signed inequality and (U), respectively, both right-side slacks decrease with v (coefficients −70+28·1.415 and −6). Thus replace v by its displayed lower bound (W335≥0). With the terms in Δ² moved aside, the signed slack has u coefficient 5481/50, the unsigned slack −394. Form 394 times the former plus (5481/50) times the latter. Its variance allowance is nonpositive because 394(3−1.415)>5(5481/50). Consequently the following polynomial must be nonpositive:
```
75197425 M² + 60157940 MS + 4416628635 M
 + 12031588 S² − 547446717 S + 7399613502.
```
But the last quadratic is strictly positive for all real S (negative discriminant, positive leading coefficient), and the three M terms are nonnegative for M,S≥0. This excludes M=0,1,2,3 and indeed does not use the previously proved upper M≤3. The scope is precisely the indicated fixed-H support face, not a decision on existence of the graph.

The degree-two rational identity is checked on the simplex points of total degree at most two; these determine the coefficients by ordinary finite differences.
