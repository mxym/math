# An order-two tensor trade for five literal populations

In one fixed terminal seven-plane of any hypothetical graph, suppose the full eligible populations of neutral costs 20, 24, 26 and charge-three cost 37 vanish. Then the number of eligible positive-charge cost-35 fields is either **zero or at least 16**. No rank, transitivity or chosen Hoffman coclique is needed. The hypotheses are full-field absences, not just selected-unit absences.

**The tensor source.** Use the untwisted rank-48 extension of the terminal determinant-49 lattice by the two binary blocks `((2,1),(1,4))`, in the usual seven-gluing coordinates (e.g. [the class dictionary](rank42-u121-residues.md)). Identify each graph residue with its matching point of the auxiliary plane, whose bilinear form is 4 times the dot product. Changing the anti-isometry by R=((2,2),(−2,2)) in F7 preserves the auxiliary form. Apply the norm-four quadratic harmonic identity (second coefficient plus 48 times first is zero) with coefficients (1,−1,1,−1) at the four rotations 1,R,R²,R³. Pairing opposite isometries means that each projective class is counted by the half-sum of its two signed populations. This introduces the SAME factor 1/2 for all populations when replaced by literal projective sums. Pure root contributions at the first coefficient are invariant under the rotation and cancel: an eligible first-shell point of nonzero graph projection is necessarily neutral cost 14 in the zero class (the untwisted occurring charges are 0 and ±3).

For a scalar test polynomial which is a trace-free quadratic in the graph 44-space, the coefficient depends only on the number of auxiliary projections. The centered field is b°=b+(a/9)1 at charge a; graph scaling is uniform. Write Δ_s T for the sum of the centered tensors b°⊗b° over the two axis directions minus the sum over the two diagonal directions in the projective residue circle of species s, counting literal fields. Axis means at least one zero coordinate in this model; the nonaxis points on these circles have equal squares of their coordinates. The tensor
```
Δ_020 T − Δ_024 T − Δ_026 T − 2 Δ_335 T − 2 Δ_337 T
```
is scalar on the 44-dimensional graph eigenspace. This statement before the vanishings is only about its **traceless part**.

The [standalone verifier](g335_projective_trade.py) expands all these coefficients directly. In a norm-four projection with pairing coordinates g=(a,b,h,j) (local letters), let D=4a²−2ab+2b²+4h²−2hj+2j². The graph species have cost 28−D for neutral charge and 39−D at charge three, with multiplicities 1 and 2 respectively. The residue is (a+5b,h+5j). The bound |g_i|≤4 follows by Cauchy against each auxiliary basis vector (norm ≤4). The rotation sum gives −4 times the displayed coefficient, with zero on all other classes. On the four-absence face, the same alternating sum on the *pure auxiliary* trace-free quadratic 2b²−4a² has coefficient −40χ on 335 alone, χ=+1 on axes and −1 on diagonals. Hence both sides have the same **number** k of fields. They also have equal tensor trace since all centered fields in 335 have the same squared norm (24). The scalar difference therefore vanishes, so their full second-moment tensors are exactly equal.

**Literal coordinates and mod seven.** Since the charge is three, Σ b=−33 and Σ b_i²=35. The only possible shape is one +1, 34 entries −1 and all others zero: the other arithmetic shape, one −2 and 31 entries −1, is impossible because (A b)_x=2 at its negative double while b≤0. At each site write P_x,N_x for the axis count minus the diagonal count of positive and negative occurrences respectively. Equal second moments there give 5P_x+N_x=0, and the actual field sum with signs χ is 6P. Consequently P is an integer neutral −4-eigenfield. In particular whenever 2k≤12 one has P=0: if k<5 then |N_x|≤k already proves this; otherwise |P_x|≤1 and it is supported on at most 2k≤12 positive pivots, contradicting the graph-field norm minimum 14 if nonzero. Both groups of k fields thus have the identical positive-pivot multiset, with at most k distinct sites, and their full uncentered field sums and uncentered second moments agree.

At 7 the class map is linear on the centered local lattice (3 and 9 are units). It can be applied to either or both factors of an equality between integral tensors in that lattice: the lattice is free, so equality in its ambient rational tensor space suffices. The product convention is b·c ≡ 2a_b a_c −4β_b·β_c (mod 7). At charge three, cost 35, the model circle has projective representatives (1,0),(0,1),(2,2),(2,−2). Applying the class map to both factors yields congruent counts on all four directions, since the two off-axis counts must agree by the off-diagonal coordinate, then the diagonal coordinates give the same count on each axis. For k≤6 there are fewer than seven fields per side, so each projective direction has the *same* count k/2. In particular k is even.

* k=2: equality of the first and second moments of two vectors determines the unordered pair, since it determines the difference up to sign. Distinct residue directions on the two sides make this impossible.
* k=4: on each direction the sum of signs is in {−2,0,2}. Equality of the uncentered sums says a=2(c+d), b=2(c−d) modulo 7 for the two axis and diagonal imbalances a,b,c,d. Only the all-zero choice works. Thus both signs occur once in each direction. Outside the at most four pivot sites all entries are 0 or −1. Apply the class map to one tensor factor and the site coordinate to the other. The four within-direction signed differences in the negative masks belong to {−1,0,1} and satisfy the same two congruences; again only all-zero works. Opposite signs on any one direction give two distinct fields agreeing outside the ≤4 pivots. Their difference has squared norm at most 4+2·3=10, allowing two exceptional absolute doubles, a contradiction.
* k=6: each direction has size 3. The two sign-sum congruences give eight choices for the four numbers of plus signs. For each, label the six fields of each side accordingly. At any site outside the ≤6 shared pivots, the two negative masks L,R have equal size (first moment) and equal residue sum (one tensor factor reduced modulo seven). Up to simultaneous complements and omission of the constant row, only 13 possible 6+6 binary masks remain. The rows from any two such sites must satisfy |L∩L′|=|R∩R′| by the second moment. They must separate all twelve field columns: two coincident on all nonpivot sites would differ on ≤6 sites with at most two absolute doubles, squared norm at most 12.

The verifier tests all mutually compatible subsets of the 13 masks. Exactly 12 separating families remain for each sign choice; all have five rows and, together with the all-ones row, form a 6×6 invertible matrix L on the first side. Each resulting O=L⁻¹R describes the linear correspondence at **every** site, including pivots: for pivot rows this follows by pairings with the five binary rows and the all-ones row via equality of first and second moments, and O is orthogonal since LLᵗ=RRᵗ. Using a complemented row is valid because both groups sum equally and both have size six. In particular the raw Gram on the second group equals OᵗGO if G is the Gram on the first, and the cross Gram equals GO.

Each off-diagonal dot d for distinct labels is an integer from −2 (each label has a unique positive entry) through 28 (the difference has cost at least 14). Remove 22,23,25 because the difference would have absent cost 26,24,20; remove 0 because b+c+1 has charge −3 and cost 37+2d. Apply also d≡4−4β_b·β_c (mod 7). The true diagonal is 35. The verifier enumerates each possible row of G, checking the cross row by exact multiplication by O, then glues symmetric rows and checks OᵗGO. All 96 small charts are empty (48 at 62 recursion nodes, 48 at 136); no floating-point bounds or graph completion tests are used. This excludes k=6. The final k=7 case has a separate short argument below.

**Seven vs seven is impossible as well.** At k=7 the two axis counts, congruent modulo 7, sum to 7; hence they are 7 and 0. Likewise the seven diagonal-side fields all occupy a single projective line. The two occupied lines are independent in the 7-plane. Within either group let ε_i be its signed residues in its one occupied direction, let p count the plus signs, and t=Σ ε_i b_i. Reduce one tensor factor and evaluate the other at site x. Independence of the two lines forces the signed sum of centered entries to be zero modulo 7 *on each side separately*. Thus 3t_x+2p−7≡0 (mod 7) for every x. If p is 0 or 7, outside the group's at most seven positive-pivot sites all seven fields agree there (the number negative must be 0 or 7), and two distinct fields would differ in squared norm at most 7+6=13. Hence 0<p<7.

Outside the pivot sites t_x ranges in [−p,7−p]. The only residue repeated in this interval is −p, and that equals −(2p−7)/3 modulo 7 only when p=0 modulo 7. Consequently t_x is constant there. The integer vector z=(3t+(2p−7)1)/7 is neutral since each Σb_i=−33. Also |z_x|≤4 by writing its numerator as Σ ε_i(3b_i(x)+1). Its constant value outside at most seven sites is necessarily zero, since at least 92 constant nonzero entries could not be cancelled by 7 entries of size ≤4. Thus 3 divides 2p−7, and p=2 or 5. Reverse all residue signs in this group so p=2. Its exterior value is now t_x=1; at every site |t_x|≤7 and t_x≡1 modulo 7, so z≤0. Neutrality gives t=1 everywhere.

For two different fields in that group the raw inner product d is in {7,14,21,28} when their residue signs agree, or {1,8,15} otherwise, by the preceding congruence and cost exclusions. Taking the dot of t=1 with either plus field shows the sum of its five opposite-sign dots is 68 plus its dot with the other plus field. The left side is at most 75 and the extra dot is at least 7. Hence the extra dot is 7 and all five are 15. The same t identity at each of the other five fields forces its four same-sign dots to sum to 28, hence all equal 7.

After centering, each of the seven vectors ε_i b_i° has norm 24 and every mutual dot is −4. These seven vectors sum to zero and form a tight frame with bound 28 on a six-dimensional space, by their Gram 28I−4J. Their tensor sum agrees with that of the other seven vectors, so both six-spaces coincide. In their 7×7 cross matrix each row sums to zero and its squares sum to 28·24=672. All entries are integers (centering subtracts 11 before signing) and congruent to one m modulo 7 where m=1 or −1, since the occupied axis and diagonal line representatives have dot ±2 and the residual metric contributes −4. For a row m+7q_i the row-sum identity gives Σq_i=−m, hence 49 divides 672+7m². But 96+m²=97 is not divisible by 7, a contradiction. This excludes k=7 and finishes the gap theorem.
