# A common four-way hit below rank 42

At mod-seven Seidel rank 38 or 40, at least one of the following actual selected unit populations is positive: neutral norm 20, neutral norm 24, charge one cost 25, or charge one cost 27. In particular rank 40 cannot have support contained in the other eight selected types, including the prior exact-three candidate `{026,228,230}`. This is not an exclusion of rank 40. Rank 36 already satisfies a stronger three-way hit `{020,024,125}` in the [joint seed criteria](rank36-joint-seed-hits.md); no such conclusion at rank 42 follows from this proof.

[The two certificates](ranks38-40-four-way-hit.json) use positive combinations of rows from the 205-variable [original-site chart](original_site_populations.py). Under the four indicated absences, the negative of each absent normalized unit population is zero. In addition to the chart's own positivity, compatibility, lifted marks and norm bounds, the only supplementary nonnegative forms are the raw `W(4,44)` population, the literal original zero-sector `C(0,14)`, and the averaged unit surplus `S=W(1,23)-99-7W(0,14)` from the [marked coset classification](../../marked-coset-shells/README.md). The chart term `N` at rank 38 is the global norm-14 packing cap; `w14` at rank 40 is the cap of four for its normalized mean, not the literal global count. Here `W` denotes the normalized eligible population averaged over maximal extensions; the support absences are graph-wide, so imply the vanishing of the associated nonnegative eligible masses.

A hypothetical vanishing of all four types would give a positive constant plus a positive integral combination of nonnegative terms equal to zero; the constants are 109076768640 at rank 38 and 425097792 at rank 40. Run `python3 ranks38_40_four_way_hit.py` alongside the usual native chart and its dependencies. The verifier uses exact arithmetic and no LP.
