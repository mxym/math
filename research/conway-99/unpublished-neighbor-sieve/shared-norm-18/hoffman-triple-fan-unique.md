# A prescribed signed Hoffman triple has at most one eligible three-hit completion

Take a compatible actual Hoffman coclique C of size 22 and work in one fixed maximal seven-extension H on the [norm-18/norm-24-only face](two-type-true-hoffman.md) (in particular at original rank 42). The needed hypotheses are that eligible nonzero integral neutral norms below 18 are absent (including the established Fano exclusion) and the resulting two-dimensional anisotropic seven-discriminant module has square Gram determinant. Fix a triple X in C. In an eligible unit norm-18 field with positive triple X and negative triple Y in C, the six off-C negatives consist of **all** the two common neighbors of each of the three pairs of X. Indeed each of the three X vertices has four opposite neighbors, all off C; if h of the three see an off-C negative, its contributions to the six common-neighbor slots are C(h,2). With six off-C negatives and twelve incidences, convexity forces six h=2 and all slots used. The same holds on the other side. In particular those six distinct sites, denoted B_X, depend on X alone. Only triples with the required six distinct witnesses can occur. Put a_X=1_X−1_{B_X}, of squared norm 9, and similarly a_Y. The full field is exactly t=a_X−a_Y, with disjoint supports of its two fans; in particular the actual graph and X,Y determine every support sign.

Suppose a second completion with the same positive triple X gave a different negative triple Z and field t'=a_X−a_Z. Since both are compatible with the **same H**, their difference a_Z−a_Y is an eligible neutral field. Its squared norm is 18−2|supp(a_Z)∩supp(a_Y)|: common sites of two fans always agree in sign (positive on C, negative off C). It is nonzero since Y≠Z, so the short norm gap forces disjointness. Thus the three fans are pairwise disjoint and t·t'=9. Their 2×2 Gram determinant is 18²−9²=243≡5 mod 7, a nonsquare. Such a pair cannot occur in the residual two-dimensional plane: dependent classes would give zero determinant and independent classes a square determinant. Hence there is at most one eligible completion for fixed X. In particular at original rank 42, at most one of the candidate 12-cycle or two-6-cycle completions on the six fixed opposite witnesses can occur for any given eligible positive triple (and the graph, not just the triple incidence, is held fixed). No isomorphism or symmetry assumption is used.
