# Rank 36 forbids the two-degree-two zero forest of a four-cube: exact finite test

For an induced four-cube, use the [cube portrait theorem](norm16-edge-and-cube.md). Its 16 vertices split into cube parities P,N; H is the independent eight-set of height-two vertices; Z has 11 height-zero vertices and five edges; there are 64 other vertices Y, each adjacent to exactly one site of P and one of N. Number H cyclically so consecutive vertices share a pair of antipodal signed sites, nonconsecutive ones share none. By the [height identities](../n16-zero-star-forest.md), the H-neighborhood B_z of any zero site is an independent subset of this cycle, of size 2+d_Z(z), and each h has four Z neighbors. Hence d_Z≤2. There can be at most two degree-two vertices: a size-four independent subset is an alternating parity of C8, and two identical such rows would have four common neighbors. If there are two, **their H neighborhoods are complementary**. There are then respectively 2,6,3 Z vertices of degrees 2,1,0.

**Initial finite family.** The 16-site cube and H contacts are exactly the two dependency-file representatives (4,10). Each chart has its eight H contact masks written explicitly in cycle order, with the cube indexed by its even, then odd binary four-tuples in increasing order. Reordering H by the rotations of this cycle gives either two or four alignments: the stabilizer induced by signed coordinate permutations and cube translations is precisely h→±h+r with r even (first cube portrait), or r divisible by four (second). These stabilizer actions are relabeling, not assumed graph automorphisms. Equivalently one may try all sixteen dihedral alignments.

The Python procedure incidence() generates all multisets of Z-to-H words in descending size, with every h occurring four times and each pair of h's co-occurring at most twice, reducing by all sixteen dihedral permutations only *before* cube attachment. Words of size at least three cannot repeat. For a=0,1,2 degree-two vertices it gives 11,33,19 orbits. forests() then enumerates every graph on the eleven currently labeled Z sites with degrees |B_z|−2. Those for which some pair already has too many common neighbors, or a component fails the established path shape, are discarded. There are 2693,785,43 placements respectively before further symmetry; the theorem's scope is exactly the last 43. Each is tested in both portraits and all 2+4 alignments, giving 258 stated candidates, with repetitions harmless.

**True Y words.** For adjacency M on the 35 sites P,N,H,Z, R=12I+2J−M−M² is the residual matrix of common-neighbor incidences in Y. A Y-neighborhood word has one p, one n, k H sites and k+1 Z sites by the height equations. Its neighbors in H avoid every conflicting pair; at most four can occur, since H is a conflict C8. Their pairwise old Y residuals R_ij must be positive, and its neighbors in the 35-old-site graph induce a matching (possibly with isolated sites). In particular W·M≤2−W coordinatewise, and a chosen p,n must have positive R_pn. These are all necessary filters.

For a fixed h, its four cube neighbors form two already-mated cube edges, and its four Z-neighbors and six Y-neighbors comprise the remainder of its matching neighborhood. Each of the six Y neighbors has a different p-tag, omitting the two P neighbors of h, and a different n-tag likewise: for any site p the two antipodal negative neighbors of h include exactly one cube neighbor of p, so R_hp=1−[p~h]. To list these six rows before assigning p,n, hsets covers the demand R[h,H∪Z] exactly using B=H∪Z parts of actual words. Two such distinct words cannot have the same B trace (it contains h and at least two Z's), nor share three old sites. Their pair incidences cannot exceed R. Those with no neighbor in N(h)∩Z must match amongst themselves, covering each exactly once; only pairs which share h and no other B site can be mates. The h chosen to minimize the bounded word list is a deterministic shortcut: only a complete root list can discharge a case, and the driver explicitly reports an unresolved case if the backup cutoff is hit.

**Rank and second star.** For each first-star portrait, trytags assigns distinct p and n labels from the respective six, without selecting any graph automorphism. The Gram used is G=3I−M+4J over F7. If the graph has rank 36 its ambient quotient is nondegenerate of dimension 36 with nonsquare determinant (see the [rank-36 determinant calculation](../triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md)). On a pivot principal submatrix G_I of rank r the h-star's six new projected pairings are known: new-old g=4−W and new-new off-diagonal 4−[mate], diagonal zero. The Schur residual U must have rank at most L=36−r. If its rank is L, the determinant class must be 3/det(G_I). The incremental principal-minor filters are only necessary tests and the full residual rank/type is checked for each returned six-row extension. Old singular rows ignored at this step only weaken the test.

Write U41 for the 41-site adjacency including these six. Each of h's two conflict neighbors h' already shares exactly two cube sites with h, therefore none of the six Y-neighbors is shared. In particular h' still needs six exterior Y rows, one for each prescribed p. Its residual demands are the actual row of 12I+2J−U41−U41². For every Y word on the original 35 sites meeting h', try all 64 choices of adjacency to the first six Y (discard forbidden zero quotas and violations of W U41≤2−W). If the 41-site Gram already has rank 36, require new-old consistency on every singular row and exact zero residual norm; if it has rank 35, consistency is still necessary since a one-dimensional nondegenerate complement admits no nonzero isotropic radical, and its residual norm must be zero or in the square class 3/det. If rank≤34, conservatively retain the first-star extension. For each demanded coordinate j, the sum over six p-tags of the maximum attainable indicator of j must reach the actual demand. A failed demand is a rigorous exclusion; successful screening is not a graph completion. Testing h±1 separately is legitimate. No PSD rounding, LP, or optimization status is used in the certificate.

**Replaying the exact stated tranche.** With NumPy and Python3, `python3 q4-two-stars.py 3478 3521 2 012345`. `catalog()` enumerates every family and asserts the 2693/785/43 counts. All initial charts for a=2 are exactly indices 3478 through 3520; the other a values remain open unless separately checked. The verified replay examined all 258 core diagrams and 4,274 first-star lifts. It returned an empty open list. Neither of the other zero-degree profiles, nor rank 36 itself, is excluded by this test.
