# Short integer combinations sharpen the unrestricted cross-shell bound

Fix any maximal determinant-49 extension H and retain the notation of [the unrestricted centroid inequality](unconditional-cross-shell-centroids.md) and its [parallelogram dictionary](norm18-parallelogram-dictionary.md). All following fields, sums and class counts are genuine H-eligible ones; no equality among residue-class populations is assumed. Let n=N18(H)/8, Z be the number of oriented neutral norm-28 fields in the zero residual class, and V be the number of oriented eligible norm-24 fields. Then
```
(12+4√2)n² ≤ 84n + 7R Z + (7√2/2) K V
```
with R=10 unconditionally, R=4 if norm16 is absent, R=6 if norm20 is absent, R=2 if both are absent. For the **net** E6−E8 term one may always take K=45/13, or K=40/13 if selected unit N26 is absent, or K=1 if norm20 is absent. Without subtracting E8 the unconditional multiplicity K is 5, reduced to 4 if selected unit N26 is absent. These bounds also admit the previously justified Jensen average; they do not alone lower the currently certified unconditional scalar cap N18(H)≤2712.

1. At an oriented zero-residue norm-28 field z, the centered source vectors h_i=t_i−z/2 in a single signed source direction have Gram 14I−3J. Four sources would have integral centered sum of norm 8. Thus at most three occur in each direction. In two nonperpendicular directions every cross h_i·h_j has one common sign and absolute value 1. If each had three sources, their centered triple sums have norm 15 and mutual product ±9; choose the sign so that their difference or sum has norm 12. This vector is integral because the two half-integral multiples of z combine integrally. Both norms 8 and 12 violate the neutral minimum 14. The four directions form two perpendicular pairs, with every across-pair direction nonperpendicular. Thus at most 3+3+2+2=10 sources occur; if no group has three, there are at most eight. When norm16 is absent no direction can repeat; when norm20 is absent only one perpendicular pair can be occupied, as in the prior centroid proof. This gives the four stated R values.

2. At an oriented norm-24 resultant q write consistent representations q=t_i+u_i in the two determined signed residue classes. The centered vectors h_i=t_i−q/2 have Gram 14I−2J. Any six would give an integral centered sum of norm 12. Therefore μ24(q)≤5. At exactly five, each four-subset centered sum is an integral norm-24 field and is signed-unit (a doubled site would translate to charge one at cost ≤19<21). At each of the 24 support sites of q, a t_i coordinate either selects q(x) or is zero. Every four-subset selects that site one, two or three times, so exactly two or three of all five select it. In fact exactly twelve sites have each weight, as each t_i selects twelve sites. Off q each pair t_i+t_j−q is a signed-unit norm-20 field, hence two t_i cannot have the same nonzero sign. Consequently y=Σ t_i−2q and y'=q−y are both signed-unit. Their squared norms are 5(14−2·5)+24/4=26 by orthogonality of all h_i to q. Absence of the marked *unit* norm-26 type consequently precludes multiplicity five even if raw nonunit norm-26 fields are present. Also the ten norm-28 differences t_i−t_j have exactly eight doubled sites counted with incidence multiplicity: summing within-q pair overlaps gives 12 C(2,2)+12 C(3,2)=48, while the sum of their raw dot products is 10·4=40. Any agreement occurs on q, any opposing overlap occurs off q and gives a doubled difference there.

3. For a norm-20 resultant the two fixed signed source directions likewise give centered Gram 14I−J: t_i·q=10, and t_i·t_j=4 for i≠j because any other same-class option would give t_i·u_j=13 or 20, beyond the [unconditional angular cap](../short-shell-moments.md#bounding-the-norm-18-alternative). Hence μ20≤14. The exact parallelogram identity B=Σ_{q24} C(μ24,2)=Σ_{q20} C(μ20,2) is over antipodal resultant **lines**, each with a fixed representative. Because E8=Σμ20, we have E8≥2B/13. Thus
```
E6−E8 ≤ Σ_{q24}(μ24−(2/13) C(μ24,2)).
```
The summand is bounded by 45/13 at m=5 or by 40/13 at m=4. There are V/2 lines in this sum, and the norm20-absent case already has μ24≤1 and E8=0. The signed Hadamard proof of the original mass bound uses only E6−E8, which yields exactly the claimed coefficient improvement.

As a further universal fiber check, at a norm-22 center the centered Gram 14I−(3/2)J admits at most eight sources: the sum of any nine centered vectors plus q/2 would be integral of squared norm 9(14−27/2)+22/4=10. No analogous claim about the actual number of norm-22 labels follows from this bound alone.
