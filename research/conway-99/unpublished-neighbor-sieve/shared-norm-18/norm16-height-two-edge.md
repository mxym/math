# A norm-16 field has at most one edge among its height-two sites

Let t=1_P−1_N be an actual neutral norm-16 field. The [norm-16 deficit argument](exclusion.md) gives independent eight-sets P,N with four opposite neighbors at every signed site; the exterior per-sign height h(x)=|N(x)∩P|=|N(x)∩N| has distribution 11,64,8 at heights 0,1,2. Write Z,H for the zero- and two-height sets.

At a site v∈H, (A²1_P)(v)=16−2=14. Its two negative neighbors contribute eight to that sum, its two positive neighbors zero. The remaining ten exterior neighbors have height sum six; because only heights 0,1,2 occur, v therefore sees exactly 4+deg_H(v) sites of Z. If H spans e edges there are 32+2e incidences with Z.

Each positive sign site sees exactly two H (and so does each negative sign site). For instance at p∈P the two-walk sum is 12+16=28, of which its four negative neighbors furnish sixteen. The ten exterior neighbors all have height at least one, with total twelve; precisely two have height two. Consequently the sixteen sign sites already consume sixteen pair-common-neighbor slots on H. The total slots across all unordered pairs in H is 2·binom(8,2)−e=56−e, leaving at most 40−e for contributions by Z. But at e≥2 the eleven sites of Z have in total at least 36 H-neighbors and must therefore consume at least 8·binom(3,2)+3·binom(4,2)=42 pair slots. This contradiction proves **e≤1** without other shell hypotheses.

In particular the [midpoint obstruction](u226-pair-midpoint.md) for two c=0 cost-26 fields on the same positive pair is unconditional. Its only remaining case would have |U∩U′|=2 and t=b−b′ of norm16. All four square corners lie outside its support and have height two: the two differing U sites of either sign meet p, likewise V at q, and each common zero z,w sees the two differing isolated-negative witnesses in each of the two U sets via its injective partial bijection. The height-two graph would contain the entire induced square, whereas it spans at most one edge. Thus every positive pair supports at most one c=0 U226, regardless of N16, U127, or Seidel rank. The other U226 subtypes are not excluded by this statement.
