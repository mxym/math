# Binary Hoffman design code, grids, and the dyadic residual

Fix a 22-site Hoffman coclique C in an SRG(99,14,1,2). Let D be its 22-by-77 binary incidence matrix to the other sites, viewed over the required ring. Its columns are distinct four-subsets: a repeated column would give four common neighbors. They form a 2-(22,4,2) design; in particular DD^T=12I+2J. Let K=ker_{F2}(D^T), k=dim K, and B_i=count of weight-i words in K^perp. Then
```
2^k (B4+7 B2+28) = 17920,       G <= B2,       B4 >= 77,
```
where G is the number of literal 3-by-3 rook grids in the graph. Thus k≤7, with G≤5 if k=7, G≤25 if k=6, and G≤65 if k=5. These are conditional relations among true graph invariants, not a graph exclusion.

For a support S of a binary kernel word write w=|S| and n_j for the number of columns meeting S in j points. Only j=0,2,4 can occur. Count incidences and pair incidences to get 2n2+4n4=14w and n2+6n4=w(w-1), whence n4=w(w-8)/4, n2=w(22-w)/2. The all-one word is in K, so the same reasoning applies to w and 22-w. The possible weights are 0,8,10,12,14,22, all even.

Let K_i(w) be the length-22 binary Krawtchouk polynomial, the coefficient of t^i in (1+t)^(22-w)(1-t)^w. Character orthogonality gives 2^k B_i=Σ_{z∈K}K_i(wt z). At weights 8,10,12,14 one has K4(w)+7K2(w)+28=0; its value at 0 and 22 is 8960. This proves the identity, and the 77 block indicators lie in K^perp.

Every nine-site graph grid meets C in a pair e, by orthogonality of the two centered indicators in distinct restricted eigenspaces. If Q denotes its seven other sites, D·1_Q=1+3·1_e: each C point off the grid has one grid neighbor and each site of e four. Reduction mod two makes e orthogonal to every z∈K, since K is even. The pair uniquely determines the grid ([grid uniqueness](../../complementary-rank56/tail-budget.md)): it determines the two common neighbors, hence a square shared by any two such grids, which must coincide. In particular the grid pairs form a graph on C contained in the disjoint union of cliques on equal coordinate columns of any generator of K. It is NOT asserted that B2=0; differing rows of D do not imply this.

## Quarter checks and exact two-valuation

The following relates this code to the [positive rank-33 residual](hoffman-plus-det2.md). Define K4={c∈(Z/4)^22:D^Tc=0}, E the image of K4 reduced into K; both E and K contain the constants, and put j=dim E-1. Equivalently z∈E iff its literal 0/1 lift satisfies D^T z/2∈im(D^T mod 2), since changing the lift to z+2y adds D^Ty. Every actual grid pair {p,q} also satisfies **c_p+c_q=Σ_i c_i modulo 4 for every c∈K4**, by the same exact D·1_Q identity. Thus the quarter checks may separate pairs not separated over two.

Over Z2 let M be the original rank-54 even unimodular graph-mark lattice on the +3 side (since 2 does not divide the determinant of M there); the Gram 4I+A-2J/11 has even diagonal. Let C0 be the rank-21 sublattice generated by the 22 coclique marks v_i; their only rational relation is Σ_C v_i=0. Let C' be its primitive saturation, and S its orthogonal complement in M. The 21-by-21 coclique Gram has determinant 4^21/22 and inverse (I+J)/4, so v2(det C0)=41 and the exponent of C'/C0 divides four. A half-mark sum Σz_i v_i/2 is in M iff D^Tz=0 mod 2, by pairings with all graph marks. The full 22-coefficient choice identifies vectors differing by a constant, so (C'/C0)[2] has dimension k-1. For a quarter-mark sum Σc_i v_i/4, the tests on C first impose Σc_i even (each marking gives c_i-(Σc_i)/22). The pairing tests on the exterior then reduce to D^T c≡0 mod4, since 2Σc_i/11 is a multiple of four. Conversely D^Tc≡0 mod4 implies c mod2 is in K and hence has even sum, so suffices. Doubling these classes gives exactly E modulo constants. Therefore
```
[C':C0] = 2^(k-1+j).
```
In fact E⊆K∩K^perp: pairing a quarter-mark lift of z with every half-mark lift of s∈K gives (c·s-(Σc)(Σs)/22)/2 integral, whose correction numerator is even, hence z·s=0 modulo two.

The projected mark lattice at two is L_+=2 S*. Indeed restriction M→Hom(S,Z2) is surjective by primitivity of S and unimodularity of M, and under the form it is realized by orthogonal projection; the graph marks span M. The primitive orthogonal sublattices C' and S have equal determinant valuation (standard gluing, or the preceding restriction argument gives [M:C'+S]=det C'=det S). Consequently
```
v2(det L_+) = 66-v2(det S) = 23+2k+2j,
                 25 <= v2(det L_+) <= 49.
```
Combined with the seven-part from the rank-33 construction, the full determinant is 2^(23+2k+2j)·7^(54-r), where r is the mod-seven Seidel rank.

## A literal grid as a residual coset

For a proposed pair e of C put h=D^T1_e. All grids through e correspond exactly to integral +3 eigenfields t vanishing on C with
```
t_B≡-h mod4,        sum t_b^2=80.
```
To see this, set g_C=1_e and g_B=(t_B+h)/4. Since Dt_B=0, DD^T1_e=12·1_e+4·1, and A_BD^T=2J-D^T, one gets Ag=3g+1. The sum of g is 9. Since t_B⊥h and h²=32, its squared norm is 2+(t_B²+32)/16. Thus 80 makes g a binary nine-set and hence a rook grid by the [complementary charge-one classification](../../complementary-rank56/README.md). This is an equivalence, not a presumption that a coset is nonempty. In particular there is no such t with norm<80, and norm112 is also absent by the charge-one norm-11 exclusion.
