# The full 22-site Hoffman translation fiber

Fix an actual Hoffman coclique C in an SRG(99,14,1,2). Write D for the 22 by 77 incidence matrix to its complement, A for adjacency, H for a compatible maximal seven-extension (at rank 42 the extension is the graph lattice itself), and K=ker(D transpose mod 3). A graph label is an integer field b of charge a satisfying A b=−4b−2a·1, with sum −11a, and c=−1_C. Usual uniform folding b→b+1 changes charge by −9, and presents each physical label at −4≤a≤4. Required seven-class compatibility is unaffected by the folds. Put d_i=(3I−A)e_i.

**Coordinates.** Let x=b restricted to C, δ=b·c=−sum x, and m=(3a+δ)/7. The last quantity is integral for any label in the compatible H chart, since the centered c mark has zero seven-class. Also define
```
z_C=0,  z_B=3b_B+Dᵀx+m·1,
r=x+m·1 (mod 3),   n=||z||².
```
Then A z=−4z and D z_B=0; z mod 3=Dᵀr on B, sum r=0 mod 3, and r is perpendicular to K. For example, D b_B=−4x−2a·1; all ternary kernel words have coordinate sum zero, so orthogonality to x is automatic. The squared norm identity is
```
9||b||² = n+21(||x||²−m²)+18a².
```
It follows by expanding the squared norm using DDᵀ=12I+2J and D b_B=−4x−2a·1. Equivalently, if y=b°/√7 is the 44-dimensional centered physical vector, its projection off the span of the C marks has norm n/63.

The pair (z,r) is invariant under adding any d_i, i∈C, adding c, and uniform fold. In the three respective operations the changes (a,x,m) are (1,3e_i,0), (2,−1,4), and (−9,1,−7). These are precisely the orbits: fix integral m,x with x≡r−m (mod3) and sum x=3a−7m. First translate by −2m·c and fold −m times to normalize m to zero; the C coordinates then change by +m at every site. All integer choices in one residue r are attained by adding individual d_i's. For fixed m,x the outside label is forced as b_B=(z_B−Dᵀx−m·1)/3, so no overcount. This is an identity for a labeled graph; no permutation of C is claimed to preserve adjacency.

**The three part and the converse.** Let L0 be the 22-dimensional even residual generated by m_j=√3 P u_j, with the prescribed seven-extension but with NO enlargement at three (see [the Hoffman residual construction](hoffman-residual-theta.md)). The label z of the fiber lies in the seven-partial dual of L0, i.e. locally in L0 at 3 and dual elsewhere. Its residual representation satisfies ||z||²=21||z*||². The inclusion at three follows from the lattice map (1/7)Σ_{j∉C} b_j m_j, using Σm_j=0 for the charge offset. Conversely if z* is in this partial dual, its true graph label z vanishes on C. At three its pairing array modulo three belongs to im Q, Q=9I−3A_B+J−DᵀD. As Q=Dᵀ(J−I)D over F3, its residue is Dᵀr for some r with sum r=0, r⊥K. (Any integral Z_3 lift may be reduced to coefficients in F3.) At all other primes the fiber is unobstructed: take any lift x of r, set a=(sum x)/3, and b=(z+(3I−A)x)/3, extending x by zero off C. This is integral, has the desired charge, and is H-compatible at seven: 3 is a unit at seven; the C marks have zero seven-class and z is H-compatible. All choices r+K occur for this same z by the formula. Thus if B(q)=θ_{√7 L0^{*7}}, then the number of pairs (z,r) of squared norm n is **3^dim(K) [q^(n/6)] B**. This does not identify B with the entire graph population.

For further arithmetic: F=θ_{L0}, F* = θ_{√21 L0*}; let A3=θ_{√3 L0^{*3}}. Seven is anisotropic, hence [q^j]A3=[q^(7j)]F* and [q^j]F=[q^(7j)]B. Full Fricke transforms interchange F,F* and A3,B with determinants respectively 49·3^(2k+1) and 49·3^(21−2k), k=dim K. No unsigned partial Fricke phase is needed.

**Counting without coordinates.** Define h(n,p,q) as the number of actual oriented pairs (z,r) as above with r having p plus and q minus coordinates. Thus h(n,p,q)=h(n,q,p); when p=q, h counts both orientations except for the fixed pair (z,r)=(0,0). Let T_j(X,Y)=Σ_{l≡j mod3} X^l Y^(l²). For a,t,δ in the raw graph chart put m=(3a+δ)/7. The raw number is
```
Σ_{n,p,q} h(n,p,q) [ X^(−δ) Y^((9t−n+21m²−18a²)/21) ]
        T_{−m}^{22−p−q} T_{1−m}^p T_{−1−m}^q .
```
Terms with a nonintegral exponent count zero. Published W normalization divides this **raw sum** by 8 exactly for 9t−11a² nonzero modulo seven in rank42. The orientation of r must be kept in the formula before identifying antipodes.

For example, the z=0 population counts the K words; a pure positive weight 15 is absent on the unique-coclique face, and a balanced weight 18 cannot occur in any simple 2-(22,4,2) design: its 9 positive/9 negative points require 12+12 pure triple blocks, 36 double-pair blocks by the positive-pair slots, and 18 mixed single-pair blocks, total 78>77. Thus its only other types are (6,6),(6,9),(9,6).

**True transport on the two-type face.** Suppose the only selected types are N18,N24. Let m_k count oriented true N18 fields with k sites of either sign in C; k∈{1,2,3}, as in the two-type Hoffman cut. The positive and negative sides are independent and opposite degrees four. Consequently
```
m_k=h(162−42k, k,k).
```
Indeed x is its unique standard lift of r on C (the only ±1,0 lift and all N18 are unit), and the norm identity gives the stated n. For charge1 norm27 with δ=4 every label since the unit type is absent equals t+d_i for N18 t and a unique negative i outside C. The unique +2 identifies i. Write j for the number of positive C sites adjacent to i. No negative site of t is adjacent to negative i. The C coordinates of b=t+d_i are unit or zero, of sum −4 with exactly s=k−j positives in C. Thus r has 18−2s plus and s minus coordinates, n=162−42s. Conversely each such r gives exactly one unit-or-zero C restriction of that shape, hence exactly one field of this type and angle per (z,r). Equivalently this follows by substituting in the coefficient formula. Write B_s=h(162−42s,18−2s,s) for 0≤s≤3. There are 9−k outside negative anchors at t, and they have Σ j=4k (every positive C site has four opposite neighbors). If k=1 this gives four each at j=0,1. If k=2 write H_t=# j=2, so # j=0 is H_t−1 and H_t≤2 by the two common-neighbor slots of the positive C pair. If k=3 there are six anchors with total 12; their pair-slot count Σ binom(j,2)≥6 equals its upper bound 2·binom(3,2)=6, so every j=2. Hence
```
B3=0;   B0−B2=4m1+m2;
B0+B1+B2=8m1+7m2+6m3;   0≤B2≤m2.
```
These identities refer to actual raw graph labels, not average formal counts.

**Compulsory marks.** The 77 ordinary outside d_i give distinct (z,r) with z=Qe_i and r=1−1_{block i}, so h(126,18,0)≥77. The 385 edges between outside vertices split into 154 capped at C and 231 not capped at C. The capped count is 22·7 since N(c) is a matching at each C site. For an exterior edge ij take d_i+d_j, with z=Qe_i+Qe_j. Capped and uncapped cases yield respectively h(126,6,15)≥154 and h(168,8,14)≥231. For injectivity, diagonal Q=6 and off-diagonal lies in [−3,1]. Two disjoint pairs had equal sums only if their coefficient difference v had vᵀQv=0, but this is at least 24+2(−6−4)=4. A shared endpoint is easier. Qe_i+Qe_j cannot equal any +Qe_k by the same estimate; equality with −Qe_k would force all three pair entries −3 and thus an actual triangle each of whose edges is already capped in C, impossible. Even if two pair-sums were negatives, their signed residue types distinguish the two in the oriented pair fibers. No statement about an abstract lattice automorphism is needed.

This decomposition and these population restrictions do not settle rank 42 or exclude the remaining two-type case without an additional obstruction.
