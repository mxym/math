# The C4-free 33-set switch graph and the marked cycle lattice

Work on the rank-42 two-type face, with unique true Hoffman C. We use the published 33-set cycle/pair switches and the [simultaneous two-mark residual W and its dual gap](two-mark-dual-gap.md). Write w_T=-1_T (raw), Δ=|T∩C|, ℓ=3Δ−22, and D=154·22−ℓ².

1. **The 33-set graph with edges |T∩R|=24 is bipartite and C4-free.** Orient the edge from T by t=w_T−w_R, so t∈N18 and t·w_T=9. Both t and w_T have seven-discriminant classes, with squared centered raw residues 4 and 1 and mutual residue 2. Anisotropy gives [t]=2[w_T], so [w_R]=−[w_T]. Also Δ is preserved since every N18 is orthogonal to C. Thus each component lies on a fixed Δ and a single pair of opposite residues. For distinct neighbors R,S of T, t=w_T−w_R and u=w_T−w_S have no sign conflict, as both negatives lie in T and both positives off T. They occupy the same signed residue; their possible mutual dots 4 or −3 therefore force 4. Then c=w_S−w_R=t−u is a genuine original-zero N28. For a fixed c and fixed antipodal source line there is at most one unordered decomposition into two N18's of opposite residues; this is the per-line part of the published two-decomposition bound ([Johnson geometry](norm22-johnson-double-graph.md)). Directly, if t−u=t'−u'=c, with t,t' in the same signed class, and t≠t', then t·u'=t·t'−14. The options −3,4 for t·t' make this ≤−10, violating the global absolute bound 7, unless u'=−t, which has the opposite signed class and is impossible here. The orientation [t]=−2[w_R] fixes the order, so this unique pair fixes the center T. This is an actual codegree bound, not an inferred bound from a diagram. If a fixed residue pair and Δ has part sizes L,R and e switches, it follows for R>0 that e(e−R)≤R L(L−1), and symmetrically for L>0; this is the usual count of ordinary length-two paths. The number of antipodal directions of W_T* in its shell through 23/10 equals the true switch degree of T by the prior dual-gap theorem; no positive lower is asserted.

2. **Every nonconstant integer weighting of the T-cycles has projected norm >23/10.** Suppose the cycles of T have sizes k_i and j_i of their sites in C. For an integral coefficient f_i on the whole ith cycle, x=sum_{v∈T} f_{cycle(v)}u_v projects into W*: every W field has charge zero and every u_v pairs integrally with it. Subtracting any uniform cycle coefficient changes x by a multiple of m_T and does not change the projection. A zero projection would mean that (3I−A)f, up to a global constant, lies in the indicator span of T and C. On T its restriction equals f itself, so f=α+β1_C on T. Every C site has both T-neighbors outside C on the same cycle, forcing β=0 and f constant. Nor can the projection coincide with a short switch direction. Orient the corresponding true norm18 signed-unit field so t|T=−1_N, where N⊂T is its independent nine-site negative support. On this face 1≤|C∩N|≤3 ([actual Hoffman κ bound](hoffman-kappa-literal.md), including the canonical norm36 exclusion of κ=0). If the projections agreed, restriction to T would now give f+1_N=α+β1_C. Take x∈N∩C. A T-neighbor belongs to neither independent set and has the same cycle value, so β=1. Take y∈N\C. A T-neighbor misses N and comparing at y would force either β=−1 or 1=0. By the full two-mark dual gap these exhaust all unwanted short vectors.

The squared norm of the projected weighting is explicitly

    sum k_i f_i² − (sum k_i f_i)²/33
         − (1386/D)(sum (j_i−Δ k_i/33) f_i)².

Indeed the Gram of the T sites is 3I−A_T+J/9, and each cycle constant is a 2-eigenvector of A_T. Project first off m_T, then off m_C−(ℓ/154)m_T; the residual pairing with the latter is 3 sum (j_i−Δk_i/33)f_i. For a proper union with total k and C count j, the formula simplifies to ρ=k(33−k)/33−(1386/D)(j−Δk/33)²>23/10. In particular the total size k of C-free cycle components is at most **22** when Δ=5, and at most **8** when Δ=12: for nonempty such a union ρ=k−19k²/477 or k−5k²/57 respectively. In the latter case k=3 is also excluded, as was already known by the triangle-flip proof. Since no 33-set cycle can have length 4, at most one C-free cycle can occur at Δ=12 (of length 5 through 8).

The optional tiny checker enumerates unordered (length,number in C) decks only, not graph embeddings and not the arrangement of C around a cycle. Starting with whole-set size33, C overlap Δ, all lengths≥3 except4, C counts j≤⌊k/2⌋, and the previously proved ban on a C-free triangle at Δ12, it finds 7138→7089 formal decks for Δ5 and 2503→2494 for Δ12 after all integer weight inequalities. Completeness of the weight test: put h=1−(1386/D) sum (j_i−Δk_i/33)²/k_i. Weighted Cauchy bounds the displayed squared norm below by h times sum k_i(f_i−bar f)². If range r≥1 occurs then this latter variance is at least (3/2)r² by the two extreme cycles of sizes ≥3. On every deck passing the Boolean-union tests the checker verifies (27/2)h>23/10, so r≥3 is automatic. Exact 0/1/2 weights suffice for r=2 on the few decks with 6h≤23/10. These counts reflect only this particular necessary test, not realizability and not a rank closure.
