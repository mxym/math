# The two extra (1,23) marks on the smallest no-unit-triple alternative

Assume S=2 on the rank-42 N18/N24 two-type face and no source triangle all of whose double differences are signed units. Then both extra signed-unit charge-one cost-23 fields have independent six-site positive sides. Their 17-site negative sides each have exactly one internal edge. Moreover either the two fields have ordinary graph dot product 2 or the exact branch L=178, D=113, G(3,35)=0 below is necessary. No graph construction or nonexistence claim follows from these alternatives.

A general incompatibility at a source center q is responsible. Write q=a_i+b_i in common source classes as in the norm-22 Johnson [graph theorem](norm22-johnson-double-graph.md). Suppose c_ij=a_i−a_j and c_kl=a_k−a_l are nonunit on disjoint index pairs. Choose a doubled site p of the first and r of the second, and independently orient the two fields c,c' so c_p=c'_r=2. Every such site is outside Q=supp(q). There a_i and a_j are opposite, and ALL other a indices vanish, because a pair agreeing nontrivially would make its s_ab=a_a+a_b−q nonunit, contrary to true norm-22 signed unitarity. Hence p differs from r and c_r=c'_p=0. Choose one positive index from {i,j} at p and one from {k,l} at r; their s-label has +1 at both, and its positive half is independent. Thus p,r are nonadjacent. Also c·c'=0 because all cross products between distinct a indices equal 4. The two (1,23) marks have
```
(d_p−c)·(d_r−c') = d_p·d_r − 7c_r −7c'_p + c·c' = 2.
```
Here d_i=(3I−A)e_i, d_p·d_r=2−7A_pr for distinct sites, and d_p·c'=7c'_p. Both marks are extra unit labels (their distinguished site is +1), and distinct since their norms are 23.

If the two available extra marks have dot different from 2, then at each q the family of nonunit pairs on its m source indices is pairwise intersecting. Any such family is contained in a star or a three-edge triangle: two intersecting edges determine a common center, and a third missing it must join their opposite endpoints. Since its complement has no triangle, m≤4. In case m=4 the nonunit family must be the triangle: if it were contained in a star the other three vertices would give a unit triangle. Therefore C(m,2)≤1+2h_q, h_q the number of incident nonunit lines. Equality holds only for (m,h_q)=(2,0),(3,1). It follows that D≤L/2+2D_non.

If an extra mark u has a positive edge ij, the [standard switch](../edge-switches.md) v=d_i+d_j−u gives the other extra mark: v retains +1 at both endpoints, excludes the ordinary d_k, and v≠u since d_i+d_j has an odd entry at a site meeting just one endpoint of the edge. It has u·v=7(u_i+u_j)+4−23=−5 (thus the preceding intersecting bound applies), and \(ij\) is the unique positive edge of each by the cap below. All nonunit norm-28 lines have at most 6S=12 doubled slots in total. The four positive-site inverses at the two endpoints of these two marks already account for two distinct lines with TWO doubled slots: d_i−u=−(d_j−v) and d_j−u=−(d_i−v), with values +2 at the distinguished endpoint and −2 at the other. They differ up to sign since d_i≠d_j and v≠u. Thus even the entire norm-28 shell contains at most 10 nonunit lines. The intersecting bound gives D≤178/2+2·10=109, contrary to the [centroid lower bound D≥113](nonzero-centroids-two-sided-wedge.md). Therefore there is no positive edge in either mark; double-counting positive-to-negative edges using Au=−4u−2·1 gives e_negative=e_positive+1, hence exactly one negative edge per mark.

In the dot-not-2 case, now D≤178/2+2·12=113, attaining the centroid lower bound, so equality everywhere. This gives L=178, D=113, D_non=12. Every nonunit edge is singly doubled. All 24 of their endpoints are distinct μ=3 centers with exactly one nonunit neighbor, and the remaining 154 centers have μ=2 and none; hence these edges form a matching. The [norm-24 bridge](norm22-johnson-positive-mass.md) gives G(3,35)=4(178−L)=0. The entire 1735-line original-zero N28 shell must be represented: the four class centroids force at least 1848 close pairs, which have at most 113 duplicated lines, and N28=−1386+90·50+2·178=3470. Thus all class-centroid norms have their minimum value 18 and every single/duplicated N28 line is actually represented. This is a highly constrained alternative, not a contradiction.
For completeness, the same-side edge cap used above follows directly from the eigen-equation. If the six-site positive side has \(e\) edges, the 17-site negative side has \(e+1\), and a negative vertex with negative-side degree \(h\) has \(2+h\) positive neighbors. Negative vertices therefore use at least \(17+4(e+1)\) of the \(2\binom{6}{2}-e=30-e\) common-neighbor slots of positive pairs. Thus \(5e\le9\), so \(e\le1\).
