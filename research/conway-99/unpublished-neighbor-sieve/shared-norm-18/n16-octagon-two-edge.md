# An exactly certified second rank-free octagon exclusion

On the neutral norm-16 eight-cycle deficiency deck, exclude the first of the four height-two conflict octagons in the [five-shore catalogue](n16-five-shore-cores.md). Together with the [separate 24-site characteristic-polynomial exclusion of the second](n16-octagon-interlacing.md), this implies **every height-two word in any surviving noncube conflict octagon sees exactly one crossing edge among its four signed-site neighbors**. The two other overlays are not excluded here. No Seidel rank or absence of a short field is assumed.

Use positive-site numbering 0,…,7 and negative columns (53,77,83,106,154,166,169,212). The target H in cyclic order has masks (4355,4737,8896,9312,17456,49176,34828,2310). Its conflict octagon makes H independent. A finite check generates real relabelings of this fixed signed 16+8 graph: all eight rotations are obtained preserving the two shores, and all eight reflections exchanging them. Nothing requires that such a relabeling extend to an automorphism of the full graph. The eleven zero-height vertices Z therefore have the same unsigned 63 incidence multisets used for the cube, and only **2433 distinct** full H–Z / Z–Z placements under D16 and equal-Z-word permutations. We check this quotient explicitly, rather than reusing the cube's relative-coset count.

For completeness let h=A1_P be the number of positive old contacts. It is 0 on P and Z, 4 on N, 2 on H and 1 on the 64 exterior sites Y. From A²1_P=12·1_P+16·1−A1_P, a Y point seeing r H points sees r+1 Z points as well as one P and one N; an H sees six Y and four Z; a Z has internal Z degree |neighbors in H|−2. Consecutive H already share two signed contacts, so a Z-word is independent on C8, with size 2,3 or 4. There are only two independent 4-sets; no words of size at least 3 repeat. At most two Z-sites have degree 2, all others have degree at most 1, so Z forms paths of order at most four. Pair quotas and these degrees give exactly 3521 labeled-Z forest placements after fixing the incidence multisets, before the final simultaneous quotient. The cited cube proof supplies these same general incidences, not the cube's additional distinct signed-tag or root-mate shortcuts.

**Spectral witnesses.** On every induced adjacency B, the two integer matrices 27I−9B+J and 44I+11B−2J are positive semidefinite, being positive multiples of the two restricted-eigenspace projectors before compression. For an old 35-core, first check an integer quadratic-form certificate if it is already non-PSD. A prospective Y-word contains one literal P and one N and as above r H and r+1 Z; the verifier retains all possibilities meeting the old pair and neighborhood-matching quotas. Test each signed word prospectively against both projectors of its induced 36-site extension. For the old matrix K and its column b, diagonal d (28 or 42), an integer v and scale s=256 exclude this word exactly when vᵀKv−2s vᵀb+s²d<0. A real linear solve only proposes v: every exclusion is an integer witness. No real eigenvalue sign or floating feasibility outcome is used to reject without an exact test. In the code all proposed entries are bounded by 100000; the signed-64-bit expression is bounded in absolute value by 41²·44·10¹⁰+2·256·41·11·10⁵+42·256²<10¹⁵. Let R=12I+2J−B−B², exactly the Gram sum of all true Y neighbor-word indicators. Two distinct Y cannot use an identical word since its weight is at least three. In each group of words tagged by p,n at most (and exactly in the real graph) R_pn members occur. Reject if the group is too small or if, entrywise, the sum over groups of min(R_pn, W_pnᵀW_pn) fails to dominate R. Those elementary tests leave 79 alignments. At each H root the six distinct Y words must sum to its full 35-site demand R_h (including multiplicities two on some signed tags). The actual 14-element neighborhood consists of eight known old neighbors and these six new neighbors; it induces a perfect matching. Enumerate six-row choices by exact-column multicover and all matchings of the still-unpaired Y among themselves. Enforce the pair quotas, then reject a completed lift only with an integer non-PSD witness on the resulting actual 41 sites. If at least 1000 lifts have been generated the corresponding h is **not** used to exclude anything; every final exclusion uses a fully enumerated root.

The native run classifies all 2433 physical charts: 1985 initial spectral or row-screen rejections, 33 signed-tag deficits, 336 pair deficits, 49 empty exact rooted covers, and 30 charts whose remaining rooted lifts all fail the exact pair or spectral tests.
