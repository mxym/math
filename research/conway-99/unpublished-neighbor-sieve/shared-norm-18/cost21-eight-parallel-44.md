# The eight equitable 44-sets have the same overlap with each actual Hoffman coclique

Work only at the last [rank-42 cost-21 aggregate](cost21-four-coclique-walk.md), with S=0, M=27, G335=0 and exactly eight actual positive-charge fields b=−1_X of type (4,44). For any fixed actual Hoffman coclique C, either **all eight X meet C in 9 points, or all eight meet it in 16 points**. The choice may depend on C; neither the existence of a high C nor its absence follows from this argument. In particular all centered differences 1_X−1_Y are perpendicular to the whole Hoffman span, which here is 16-dimensional.

Use literal raw K(a,t;d), the number of signed fields with b·c_C=d, c_C=−1_C, positive charge not averaged with its negative. On charge zero both signs are counted separately (unless b=0). The only possible angles on (4,44) are 9 and 16: literal intersection size in [0,22] and seven-residue give 2,9,16; angle 2 would yield by adding c_C and folding by a constant a charge-three field of cost 37, absent. The three degree-two rows in the [companion checker](cost21_eight_parallel_44.py) prove the identity
```
8K(0,28;7)−8K(1,23;−3)+24K(1,23;11)−32K(2,22;1)+3K(4,44;16)
 +8K(1,25;11)+4K(1,27;−3)+3K(3,35;12)=4072.
```
These are the actual raw K, not one-eighth of them. The verifier uses the same rank-48 marked auxiliary translation as the published single-C angular proof: for the single shift (−1,1,−1,1) and the three auxiliary directions 0,e1,e2 added to the same actual centered C direction, the harmonic norm-four sum is minus 48 times the root sum. It writes the exact identity as a rational linear combination of the three zero rows and the 13 actual folded shell totals W from the previous aggregate, and checks it coefficient by coefficient on every orbit. Nonisotropic raw coordinates are multiplied by eight in assembling the coefficient target; zero-sector coordinates have multiplier one. Orbit identifications use sign and translations by the actual c_C with charge folding, all of which preserve the residual normalization.

Specifically in this palette N18 and N22 are orthogonal to C (their forbidden double-charge return shells are raw 226 and 230, respectively; on N22, a larger angle would also force a subminimum cost). All raw 121 have angle 4 by the positive-edge reflection. The nonunit 026 shell comes entirely from the signed cost-21 site translates (angles 0,±7), and 127 entirely from cost-18 site translates (angles −3,4). Thus no other removal of a potentially nonempty fixed-C histogram is used. The checker also verifies all these constants against the exact original-site model for the sole aggregate and for every remaining parameter y. The 13 ordered W in the checker are 1,59/2,91/2,2854,10,99,79,531/2,27,269,33,0,1. Half-integers in these normalized populations are harmless.

Here K(1,23;−3)=22 and K(1,23;11)=0 because all (1,23) fields are the ordinary site marks. Also K(3,35;12)=0. Finally each raw (1,27) field is uniquely t+d_i for t a neutral norm-18 field and t_i=−1. Since t·c_C=0 it has angle −3 precisely for i∈C. Thus K(1,27;−3) is even: t and −t contribute the same number of sites in C by t·c_C=0. Reducing the identity modulo eight leaves 3K(4,44;16)=0 mod 8. With exactly eight fields this proves the dichotomy.
