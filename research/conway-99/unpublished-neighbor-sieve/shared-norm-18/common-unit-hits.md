# Thirty-two rank-independent support constraints

At any of the four remaining Seidel ranks, 32 explicit subsets of the twelve selected unit types must **each** meet the actual support. This applies equally to graphs with four, five, or more types. In particular only nineteen of the 220 support triples escape this test. It does not assert that any of them occur. The [six-family identity](common-six-family.md) independently removes one more, giving a combined upper bound of eighteen.

Use the same 24 folded coordinates and nine homogeneous rational equations as [the common moment system](higher-moments.md), regenerated by `rank36_three_types.short_equations()`. Despite the importing file's name, neither the nine equations nor the keys depend on the rank: they are the projection onto the indicated short coordinates of the harmonics of a fixed determinant-49 extension, averaged over the choices of that extension. All twelve designated coordinates are **unit** counts, not raw counts. For these twelve (all nonisotropic) the folded count W is the graph-wide count divided by p=7^((44−r)/2)+1. Other coordinates follow the original short-shell convention, in particular W000=1. No rank-specific Fourier or automorphism input is present.

The inequalities used are: coordinate nonnegativity; W123≥99+7W014 (ordinary graph marks and Fano-site translates); W014≤4, the compatible oriented Fano bound in each determinant-49 extension; [the norm-16 switch](exclusion.md), U127≥N16; [the degree-ten site inequality](site-degree-ten-global.md) and [the four-family Sidon inequality](coclique-sidon-global.md), both in their actual graph-wide forms divided by p. The norm-16 implication of the same switch is also applied separately to candidate positive supports.

The accompanying checker reconstructs the nine exact moment rows. For each listed hit set H, it adds positive integer multiples of the listed graph inequalities and of −W_k≥0 for selected unit coordinates k∈H, valid if those unit types all vanish. The result is a **negative integer multiple of W000** modulo the nine homogeneous equations. This directly verifies impossibility, without a numerical solver and without first restricting the number of true positive types. All coverage counts after verification require only the 4096 subsets of a twelve-element set.
