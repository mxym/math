# A seven-type family at rank 36 fails the sharpened star cap

Assume the five selected unit types **016, U026, U121, U230, U337** vanish. No restrictions are put on 018,020,024,125,127,226,228. In particular the unit U127 may be present and N20 is unrestricted. At rank 36 the [native exact chart](original-site-populations.md) forces **N18≥55,350**, contradicting the [independently tightened star bound](norm18-eleven-site-packing.md) **N18≤54,476** under the sole hypothesis N16=0. This excludes the entire stated seven-type support family, not rank 36. In particular the previous four-type candidate {018,020,125,127} is removed.

The [checker](norm18_rank36_star_sharp.py) gives an exact identity whose specialization is
```
19304874·(N18−54476) ≥ 16856484174.
```
It uses p=2402 and counts both signs of the actual neutral field. Its positive remainder uses six nonnegative raw W shell counts, S=W123−99−7W014, actual G014, original C014, one compatible-minus-original site slot, the ordinary-edge mark C236 at −2, and two nonnegative original angular slots. The five vanished terms are the unit coordinates V, not raw substitutes. No Fano-absence premise is needed. No upper bound on N18 is used in deriving the identity; the upper and lower results are only compared at the end.

The accumulated [earlier necessary tests](norm18-rank36-dot8-star.md) now leave four size-four supports: `{018,020,024,026}`, `{018,020,026,125}`, `{016,018,024,127}` and `{016,018,125,127}`. No conclusion about their realizability or about all larger supports is drawn here.
