# Seven cancelling sites of two norm-18 fields

Suppose two oriented neutral norm-18 fields a,b of a hypothetical (99,14,1,2) graph have inner product −7. Their sum q has squared norm 22. Suppose q is signed-unit and each of its two sign classes is independent. Then of the seven sites where a and b cancel, **three have one sign orientation and four the other**. In particular the 5+2 cancellation is impossible. There is no automorphism or Seidel-rank assumption in this conditional assertion.

On the rank-42 norm-18/norm-24-only face the hypotheses hold for every decomposition: norm-22 is signed-unit by the usual short affine gap, and a same-positive edge uv in q would give d_u+d_v−q of charge 2 and cost 36+22−28=30, a forbidden raw type; also apply this to −q. Thus the first of the three formerly surviving directed mass decks for a unit double norm-28 line is excluded (it has a five/two pair on one of its four 7-overlaps). The other two decks do not have this imbalance and are not excluded here. Neither this face nor rank 42 is ruled out.

## Why there are only two preliminary imbalances

The signed unit fields a,b,−q sum to zero. Every nonzero site has one positive and one negative component, so label it by their ordered colors i→j, taking colors 0,1,2 in the order a,b,−q. Every color has as many tails as heads (9,9,11 each sign); unordered pair multiplicities are 7 on 01 and 11 each on 12,20. Let A,B,C denote 0→1,1→2,2→0 and use primes for their reverses. There are exactly 29 marked vertices and 70 unmarked. The three differences in antipodal panel size are equal. Negate all signs so this common difference δ is positive; it is odd. Every marked vertex has exactly four opposite-sign neighbors in each field in which it is nonzero. No two equal nonzero signs of any field can be adjacent. Consequently a vertex of reverse degree r has exactly 4−r neighbors in each other panel of its own handedness and no other marked neighbors. The two groups of 3 panels need not be isomorphic.

Let A have size m≥4, with 7−m reverse sites of A-neighbor counts r_i≤4. The nine heads of 0 and nine tails of 1 are two opposite-side groups for A, overlapping in exactly A'. Each carries 4m contacts. For any two vertices of A, there are only two common neighbors. Therefore the sum of binomial pair counts on these two groups, subtracting the contributions counted twice on A', is at most 2C(m,2). For m≥6, the individual 9-site group minimum is given by balancing 4m contacts, and at most one reverse site can have been counted twice. At m=6 this gives 2·21−6>30; at m=7 there are no reverse sites and 2·30>42. Thus δ=1 or 3. For m=5 there are two reverse sites, sizes r_1,r_2≤4, with r_1+r_2≤7. The analogous bound
```
C(r_1,2)+C(r_2,2)+2 min{Σ C(h,2): seven nonnegative h sum to 20−r_1−r_2} ≤ 20
```
forces r_1=r_2=3 and equality. The other fourteen targets (B and C) each see two of the five A points, and there is no other common neighbor of any two A points.

## Complete case generator for δ=3

The sizes are (5,7,7)/(2,4,4). The A-neighbor triples of the two A' points meet in h=1 or 2; normalize to (012,034) or (012,013). For each pair e in A let b_e,c_e be its multiplicities as the neighbor pair of B and C sites. Their sum is exactly 2 minus the number of reverse triples containing e. The 4−r_A degrees also prescribe the sum over incident pairs separately for B and for C. Enumerating these ten small intervals, quotienting by permutations preserving the unordered pair of triples and the swap of B,C, leaves four charts for each h. Swapping B and C here is the allowed label change swapping colors 0,1 and simultaneously reversing all signs; it does not abbreviate nonisomorphic graphs under an assumed automorphism.

Every B (and C) site now sees two A sites, two of its own four reverse sites, and two sites in the other large clockwise panel. Each A' sees one B' and one C'. Label each four-set so that the two A' contacts are (0,0) or (0,1), retaining both. Enumerate the seven 2-subsets on each four-set with the resulting reverse degrees, respecting all already determined pair quotas. Sort only B sites having the same A-neighbor pair and quotient the free reverse sites by permutations fixing the ordered anchors. Independently do this for C. Between B' and C' there are two edges, determined by a matching of their two copies of anchor contacts; try both pairings and discard double edges. In the 7-by-7 B,C block, try all two-element neighbors for each C, requiring every B-degree two. At any stage reject an adjacent pair already exceeding one common neighbor or a nonadjacent pair exceeding two; checking an edge addition also checks that the now adjacent endpoints have at most one common neighbor. These upper-bound checks cannot discard an actual graph. No positive case is approximated by a count of subgraphs.

The exact raw chart totals (h=1 then h=2) are (424,1956,274,588;0,106,18,874), hence 4,240 enumerated completions before exterior checks. Canonicalization by equitable color refinement and exact individualization only permutes actual vertices, with optional simultaneous B,C and B',C' exchange. It leaves (106,279,151,320;0,43,7,245) = 1,151 per-chart colored representatives counted with possible isomorphisms across different charts. The certificate fixes the SHA-256 of the concatenation of all exact canonical adjacency masks in lexicographic order for **each** chart; equal masks are actual isomorphisms, not compared spectra.

## Why the certified terminal tests are necessary

For a core adjacency matrix F, write L for its 29-by-70 exterior incidence. From the SRG identity,
```
R = LLᵀ = 12I+2J−F−F².
```
An integer vector with vᵀRv<0 directly rules out a core. Otherwise enumerate each possible exterior contact subset w of the 29 sites; include the empty set. Each pair in w must have R_ij≥1. For every marked vertex i, |w∩N_F(i)|+[i∈w]≤2: if i is adjacent to the exterior site, only one shared neighbor is allowed. The signed marginals in w must balance at each of the three colors. At a zero site of a signed unit field of side size n, each of h neighbors of the opposite side has four neighbors in the positive set P. Thus 4h≤(A²1_P)_x=2n−h and h≤3,3,4 for the three colors. Clique recursion enforces these bounds and balance, preserving every possible contact subset. No choice of mutual exterior adjacency is assumed.

Writing x_w for the nonnegative integer population of this word, the 70-site count, every diagonal R_ii and every pair R_ij constrain x exactly. A required positive right side with no column eliminates its core immediately. So does an integer linear combination with nonnegative coefficients on all x and negative right side. For the remaining cores include the following necessary inequalities: a triple of distinct core sites can appear in at most one exterior word counted with multiplicity (otherwise two exterior vertices would have three common neighbors). Any nonempty word has size at least two by flow balance, a size-two word occurs at most twice, and a larger word at most once. The empty word need only be bounded by 70.

In each norm-18 field there is at most one zero site of height three; in the independent-sided norm-22 field at most one zero site has height four. For completeness, with sides of size n and all inner degrees four across the signs, the exterior height counts H_j satisfy ΣH_j=99−2n, ΣjH_j=10n, ΣC(j,2)H_j=2C(n,2)−6n. For n=9 these give H_0+H_3=9. At h=3 the 14−6=8 external neighbors have total height 18−5·3=3, so at least five have height zero; two such sites would need at least eight different zeros, whereas H_0≤7. For n=11 the identities give H_0+H_3+3H_4=11. At h=4 the six external neighbors have total height 22−5·4=2 and at least four are zero; two such sites would need at least six zeros whereas H_0≤5. The total-height claim follows by subtracting the 4h contributions of the negative neighbors from (A²1_P)_x=2n−h. After subtracting any maximal-height sites already in the core, these give the last three checked inequalities.

Each terminal certificate tree splits one **integer** multiplicity into the exhaustive cases x_j≤s and x_j≥s+1. If b contains the right sides of all equality and upper-bound rows and M their columns, a leaf provides integer y, nonnegative on upper-bound rows, for which
```
y·b < Σ_j (Mᵀy)_j (lo_j if (Mᵀy)_j≥0 else hi_j).
```
The right side is the minimum over the current box, contradicting every feasible assignment. All quantities in the verification, including quadratic forms and leaf comparisons, are arbitrary-width integers. Optimization was used only to discover the stored certificates; neither an LP solver nor a trust assumption about solver statuses is required.

**Replay:** put `seven-overlap-core.py`, `seven-overlap-certificates-a.json` and `seven-overlap-certificates-b.json` together and run `python3 seven-overlap-core.py` under Python 3.10 or later. Optional chart arguments such as `10 11` select individual chart keys. The pure standard-library run enumerates every case afresh and checks all leaves, not a sample.
