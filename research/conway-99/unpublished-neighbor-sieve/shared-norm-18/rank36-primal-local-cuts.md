# Two rank-36 sparse faces excluded by the primal and maximal-extension theta coefficients

For Seidel rank 36, neither of the following can contain all present types among the twelve selected unit types:
* **A:** neutral norms 20, 24 and unit norm 26;
* **B:** neutral norm 24, unit norm 26 and unit (2,30).

These are two separate exact contradictions; neither requires the global Sidon theorem for arbitrary cocliques. Graph symmetry is not assumed. Use [the code](rank36_primal_sidon.py) with argument A or B.

## Two distinct scalar theta pairs

Let L denote the rank-44 lattice generated by the original graph marks as in the [rank-44 Fricke construction](../../maximal-marked-lattice/README.md), with determinant 7^8 at Seidel rank 36. Use X_j for the theta coefficient at q^j of sqrt(7)L* and Y_j for that of L. Use Ŷ_2 for the **mean** norm-four count of the determinant-49 maximal extensions U_H; it is not Y_2 in general. W_at is the mean of the raw folded labels in the [short-shell convention](../short-shell-moments.md), dividing by eight only when the residual quadratic value is nonzero. Put p=2402.

The Fourier series of L* contains precisely the allowed graph charges 0,+3,−3. For j>0 this gives X_j=N_(2j)+2G(3,2j+11), since the squared evaluation norm for opposite charge three is 2j+11. X_0=1 and X_1=...=X_6=0 by the global short minima. X_7=N14. At any 8≤j≤13 the seven-norm is nonzero, so averaging the marked extensions gives
```
X8=pW016, X9=pW018, X10=pW020,
X11=p(W022+2W333), X12=p(W024+2W335), X13=p(W026+2W337).
```
These are **raw** W026 etc.; a zero unit component does not itself imply raw zero. The script explicitly reverses the four substitutions made by the [full harmonic rows](higher_moments.py) and then imposes the chosen unit zeros. X14 is a separate variable, not identified with either the neutral N28 or Y2.

For an isotropic nonzero seven-class the inclusion probability in H is 1/p, while the original zero class lies in every H. The anisotropic residual plane of U_H has no nonzero vector of zero quadratic value. Applied to norms two and four, these facts give
```
X7 +(p−1)Y1 = p W014,
X14+(p−1)Y2 = p Ŷ_2.
```
Here a norm-two root has only the neutral marking (charge-three minimum excludes it). The determinant-49 [scalar identity](rank36-coclique-tangent.md), averaged over H, reads
```
Ŷ_2=1000−8W014+200W016+88W018+64W020+24W022+48W333+8W024+16W335.
```
For the original L, Y_0=1 and all Y_j are computed from the first 15 X coefficients by the [15-dimensional Fourier basis](../../maximal-marked-lattice/verify.py). Its second column is the minus-Fricke transform; the determinant multiplier for q=8 is 7^(11−8/2)=7^7, not the determinant-49 factor 7^10. Neither the special fixed numerical controls in that program nor the assertion X_(7j)=Y_j for the original lattice is used.

## Seven nonnegative quantities, two exact rows

Let M=W222 be the mean compatible-coclique count, S=W123−99−7W014 the mean unit (1,23) count. The [coclique variance and disjoint shifts](rank36-coclique-tangent.md) and the [negative-edge injection of the S fields](coclique-sidon-cut.md) give the following linear lower slack at any integer t. Indeed use m²≥2tm−t² in the actual inequality within each H before averaging:
```
H_t = 9[Ŷ_2+2W014−462−41M−2S]−5(2tM−t²) >= 0.
```
Six other nonnegative quantities in order are W333,W335,W444,Y1,990−N14,Y3−41058. The last two use the [Fano packing bound](../../short-support-packing/README.md) and the [primal marked norm-six lower bound](../../rank-34-short-support/README.md#combining-the-bounds); both apply to the **original graph**, independently of the sparse face. In particular 990 caps all oriented norm-14 graph eigenvectors, not solely the roots in a single extension.

For A the coefficients on those six slacks and H_64 are respectively
```
633601962, 477991995, 532498179, 972321651, 12497841, 240443, 1258648.
```
Their linear combination is **−9388413395**. For B the coefficients on the same six and H_48 are
```
120332994, 84940725, 68424573, 88455927, 2944977, 55566, 175346.
```
Their combination is **−53385405**. These equalities are checked by appending each claimed homogeneous target row (constant represented by W000=1) to the full averaged higher-moment rows, the three primal/extension equations above and the unit-zero rows: the exact rational rank remains 48 in both cases. The positivity of every coefficient proves both contradictions.
