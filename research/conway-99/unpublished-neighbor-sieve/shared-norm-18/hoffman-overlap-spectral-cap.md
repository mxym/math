# The Hoffman overlap graph removes the two largest rank-42 cost-21 populations

On the rank-42 unit-support face contained in {018,121,125}, the [eight equal signed cost-21 counts](rank42-u121-residues.md) have common value **9≤r≤13**, improving 9≤r≤15. The remaining five values are not excluded by this proof.

## Orthogonal spans

Here every Hoffman coclique is original. Let C be their full family of size M, and S_C the real span of their centered indicator vectors, a subspace of the 44-dimensional (−4)-space. Every neutral norm-18 field t is perpendicular to S_C: its dot with c_D=−1_D is divisible by 7; absolute value 7 would give raw G226 and a larger value a charge-two field below cost 22. Consequently t is perpendicular to every Fano, since each Fano is a signed difference of two actual Hoffman endpoints.

The norm-four terminal residue circle of t has four antipodal directions. Pick signed representatives β1,β2,β3,β4, which in an orthogonal equal-norm basis are represented by (1,0),(0,1),(2,2),(2,−2), with pairings multiplied by 4 in F7. By the preceding residue theorem the corresponding signed populations are n_A,n_A,n_B,n_B, each at least 18, and N18=4(n_A+n_B). Distinct fields with a common signed residue have dot −3 or 4: the residue is 4 mod 7, −10 would give N16, 11 a Fano difference (impossible by perpendicularity), and larger magnitudes violate the minimum or are identical. Between the two pairs of directions, however, the entire off-diagonal 2×2 array of fiberwise dots is constantly [[1,1],[1,−1]]: the residues are ±1; ±6 or ±8 give forbidden N24 or N20, and larger possibilities violate the minimum. No balance of individual rows beyond these true residue facts is needed.

Within either signed fiber of size n, let A record off-diagonal dot 4. Its Gram is 7(3I+A)−3J. With H=I−J/n, the dimension of its difference span is the rank of B=H(3I+A)H. For x the average degree, tr B=3(n−1)−x. Expansion and Cauchy on the squared degrees give tr B²≤9(n−1)+(n−6)x−x². Thus rank B≥(tr B)²/tr B²≥24(n−1)/(n+24): multiplying through, the numerator of this last inequality is n[5x−3(n−1)]². In particular the ceiling d(n) is a genuine difference-span lower bound.

Let L_A be the sum of the difference spans formed **within each** of the first two fibers; define L_B likewise for the last two. Their dimensions are at least d(n_A),d(n_B). L_A is orthogonal to every member of both B fibers, and vice versa. In particular L_A⊥L_B. Projecting one representative of each of the four signed fibers orthogonally off L_A⊕L_B leaves the cross 2×2 Hadamard matrix unchanged (projection of each A-vector to this summed space belongs to L_A and every B-vector projects into L_B). Its rank two proves that at least two additional dimensions survive, beyond those difference spaces. All these vectors are in S_C-perp inside the same 44-space.

The [exact original-site verifier](hoffman_overlap_spectral_cap.py) gives N18≥181. As the total is divisible by four, n_A+n_B≥46. The smaller fiber has at least 18 members. If the smaller has size 18, the larger has size at least 28, giving d(18)+d(28)=23. If the smaller has size 19 through 22, the larger has size at least 24, giving d(19)+d(24)=23 by monotonicity. Otherwise both have size at least 23 and the sum is at least 24. With the two extra dimensions, the norm-18 fields span dimension at least 25, hence dim S_C≤19.

## A sparse graph of defects from eight-overlap

Let e1,e15 be the numbers of unordered pairs of distinct Hoffman cocliques with overlaps 1,15. Every other pair meets in eight. Then e15≤W014/2 by the uniqueness of the endpoint pair of an oriented Fano. Also e1≤W446. Indeed a one-overlap pair maps to the actual (4,46) sum c_C+c_D, whose unique double point p recovers the common point. If c_E+c_F were a second decomposition, then E contains p and lies in C∪D, so |E∩C|+|E∩D|=23. For a genuinely different decomposition these two numbers must be 8 and 15. After interchanging the summands, assume |E∩C|=15: then c_C−c_E=c_F−c_D is an oriented Fano with two distinct ordered endpoint pairs, impossible. Both W446 and W014 here are actual populations without an eightfold division.

On the M cocliques form a signed adjacency A=A1−A15, with e=e1+e15 edges. Their centered Gram is 7(2I−A+(4/9)J). Its nullspace is a subspace on which the Rayleigh quotient of A is at least 2. A simple signed graph with e edges has at most floor(e/3) eigenvalues ≥2: deleting a vertex of degree at least three costs at least three edges and decreases the count by at most one by interlacing. In the remaining maximum-degree-two graph paths have none and each cycle has at most one, at a cost of at least three edges (switch edge signs on a path, or use the signed-cycle spectrum). Thus M−dim S_C≤e/3 and
```
2(3M−W446)−W014 ≤ 6 dim S_C ≤ 114.
```
But if r≥14 the exact verifier gives 2(3M−W446)−W014≥131, a contradiction. Its two rows are positive integer combinations in the original rank-42 205-coordinate site system after setting the nine forbidden unit types to zero; only the inequality with constant 131 uses r≥14. The computation verifies exact arithmetic, not feasibility tolerances.
