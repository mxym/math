# A large compatible Hoffman family forbids two integral Fano roots

For a fixed integral graph-lattice extension U at seven, let p count its antipodal pairs of roots, represented by oriented neutral norm-14 Fano fields modulo simultaneous sign. Let M count its compatible true 22-cocliques, meaning those whose marked representatives lie in U; the same argument applies to a subfamily of them. In particular M is at least the number of original zero-class cocliques.

**Theorem.** If p=4 then M≤8; if p=3 then M≤9; if p=2 then M≤54. Thus M≥55 forces p≤1. This statement does not assume the Fano endpoints themselves are compatible with U. The old [integral root argument](../../marked-coset-shells/README.md) gives p≤4 used in the projection formulas below. The [later three-Fano exclusion](../README.md#three-orthogonal-fano-configurations-cannot-coexist) already gives p≤2 for the graph. Thus the new obstruction concerns the still-relevant case p=2; the p=3,4 formulas are included only as projection identities and not claimed as new exclusions.

Write a centered indicator as c°=1_C−2·1/9 in the 44-dimensional negative eigenspace. For one Fano let E,F be its two endpoint cocliques (meeting in fifteen), let s=e°+f°, and let h=e°−f° be the signed Fano field. For a U-compatible coclique C the scalar h·1_C is divisible by 7: divide by seven to get the lattice pairing of the root with the marked representative of C, up to the integral root evaluation at the chosen base site. In detail, if u_0 is the base mark and y_C=(Σ_{i∈C}u_i)/7, then the root pairs integrally with y_C−u_0 in U and its pairing with u_0 is an integer. Unless C is an endpoint, the possible gap between the two intersection sizes is <7 by the [Fano endpoint table](../../marked-coset-shells/README.md); the only equal-size option is (1,1). Thus the two endpoint overlaps are both one, even if E,F themselves lie outside the chosen marked coset.

Distinct integral Fano roots up to sign are orthogonal in the real root space by the [integral-overlattice root argument](../../maximal-marked-lattice/README.md), and all four cross-endpoint overlaps are one by the [endpoint table and cross-root argument](../../marked-coset-shells/README.md). For the p endpoint pairs let s_i,h_i be their sums/differences. The h_i are perpendicular to all s_j and to the other h_j, with squared norm 14. The Gram of the s_i has diagonal 490/9 and off-diagonal −140/9, hence is positive definite for p≤4. These 2p directions are independent. Every other compatible C has h_i·c°=0 and s_i·c°=−70/9. Projecting orthogonally away from the 2p-dimensional span therefore subtracts exactly 4900p/[9(630−140p)] from every entry of the other-coclique Gram. Its original diagonal is 154/9, and the off-diagonals are 28/9 or −35/9 (a fifteen-overlap within the remaining compatible family would supply another integral Fano root).

For p=4 the subtraction 280/9 exceeds the diagonal, so none remains. For p=3 it is 70/9; the projected diagonal is 84/9, off-diagonals at most −42/9, so summing all the vectors gives at most three others. Add at most 2p compatible endpoints in each case.

For p=2 write m for the number of other compatible cocliques. Their projected Gram is 7(2I−A), where A is the simple adjacency matrix of their overlap-one graph. Thus every connected component has spectral radius at most 2; its nullity in 2I−A is at most one by Perron simplicity. In a singular component take primitive positive integer coefficients l_i of the null vector and write L=Σ l_i. Indeed these coefficients annihilate the projected vectors. The projection adds (s_1+s_2)/5 to each original centered vector, giving the pointwise integer relation
```
5 Σ_i l_i 1_Ci + L Σ_four endpoints 1_E = 2L · 1.
```
Since 4·22<99 some site lies in no endpoint, proving 5 divides L. Components of size one or two have spectral radius <2. A connected three- or four-vertex simple graph of spectral radius exactly two is a cycle C3 or C4: a tree on these orders is a path or the three-leaf star, each of radius <2; any proper connected enlargement of such a cycle has radius >2 by Perron comparison. For either cycle primitive l_i=1 and L=3 or 4, impossible. Thus each singular component has at least five sites and the projected rank is at least 4m/5. Its ambient dimension is at most 44−4=40, so m≤50 and M≤54. An affine C5 component, if present, would satisfy the displayed 2-cover identity with all coefficients one.

## Actual rank-40 and rank-42 consequences

The [native four-row verifier](hoffman_fano_large_family.py) gives the following **original zero-class** lower bounds, not a claim about any averaged coclique count.
* Rank 40: if `018,127,228,230,337` vanish then 323490552·C222 ≥24969389676, hence C222≥78.
* Rank 42: if `016,018,228,230,337` vanish then 41888·C222 ≥3255840, hence C222≥78.

Every maximal extension contains the original class, so every one has at most one root pair on the respective face. There are no two graph Fano lines orthogonal **in the seven-primary discriminant form** on this face: such isotropic classes span a totally isotropic subspace, extend to a maximal one and would give two different integral root pairs there. Conversely distinct Fano roots that actually coexist in an integral extension are also orthogonal as real vectors by the earlier integral-root theorem. At rank 40 the result implies the graph-wide oriented count N14 ≤100−49·C014: the nonsplit four-space has 50 isotropic lines, a nonzero isotropic original class enters exactly one such maximal extension whereas an original-zero root enters all 50. In particular a root in the original lattice then forces N14=2. At rank 42 the original discriminant plane is anisotropic, so N14≤2 directly. Neither statement excludes the respective rank or forces even a single root.

Separately if `018,121,127,228,230,337` vanish, the **graph-wide** raw positive-charge G(2,32) is at most 1575 at rank 40 or 252 at rank 42. The two statements have their separately specified supports; both apply to `{020,024,026}`. On that narrower face, the [Fano-angle transfers](original-site-fano-four.md) also rule out all nonzero ordinary dot products between distinct Fano lines: at angles 1, 2 and 4 one can use respectively `U125=G121=0`, `U127=N18=0`, and `G230=0`. Thus every two graph Fano lines on the narrower face would be orthogonal, and the new obstruction gives the global `N14≤2` at rank 40 as well as at rank 42.

Besides the 205-variable [original-site chart](original_site_populations.py), both sets of certificates use the nonnegative raw W444 and the class-preserving [disjoint triangle/coclique/extra-mark transfer](rank38-three-hit-sets.md), W339≥231+22W222+(W123−99−7W014). At rank 42 the original anisotropic plane has no nonzero isotropic class, so the selected isotropic compatibility slack is zero in either direction, including for the indicated site-coordinate slot. All certificate constants are exact integers; neither averaged population is rounded as a graph count. Run `python3 hoffman_fano_large_family.py` alongside the native chart.
