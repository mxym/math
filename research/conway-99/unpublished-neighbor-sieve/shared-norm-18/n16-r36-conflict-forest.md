# Rank 36: the height-two conflict graph of every norm-16 marking is a linear forest

The [five-shore classification](n16-five-shore-cores.md) shows that the intersection-two graph of the eight height-two words is a linear forest or a conflict octagon. An octagon occurs only on the four-double deficiency cube ([excluded at rank 36](q4-excluded-r36.md)) or on four actual cyclic alignments of the C8 deficiency deck. The first two noncube portraits are already excluded rank-freely, respectively by the [complete two-edge-portrait](n16-octagon-two-edge.md) and [24-site interlacing](n16-octagon-interlacing.md) proofs. Here we exclude the **last two** at rank 36. We do not exclude the other 239 overlays of this C8 deficiency deck, or any possible rank.

**The added rank-36 two-star step.** For a fully enumerated root h, append its six Y neighbors and their genuine neighborhood matching, giving an induced 41-site adjacency U. Check its literal common-neighbor quotas, and its two ordinary real-eigenspace compressed Gram matrices by verified integer negative witnesses. If needed, look at either adjacent h' on the *conflict* octagon. The two H sites have already used their two common neighbors in the old signed sites; no Y(h') lies among the six Y(h). Each prospective Y(h') appends some six independently enumerated 0/1 contacts to a previously valid Y-word on the 35 old sites. Its entries are individually bounded by the true row R_(h') of 12I+2J−U−U², and its known adjacent-neighbor sum at every site must be at most two minus its adjacency there.

Let G=3I−U+4J over F7. An ambient nonsingular 36-space with the Conway Seidel form has nonsquare determinant. Rank above 36, or full rank 36 with square determinant, is impossible. For rank lower than 35 this particular filter does nothing. At rank 35 or 36 keep a nonsingular principal pivot matrix P on a set I of size rank(G); the symmetric Gram has such principal pivots (independent columns yield a nonsingular principal block). The orthogonal complement of P in the ambient 36-space is zero or a nondegenerate line. Thus any zero-norm residual old vector vanishes in that complement, and every new candidate pairing column g=4−w must agree with its projected column G_(:,I)P⁻¹g_I on all 41 indices. The Schur norm −g_IᵀP⁻¹g_I at residual dimension zero must vanish; at dimension one its product with det(P) must be zero or nonsquare. These are finite-field necessities only. At each physical P tag p an actual second star can use at most R_(h',p) of the retained candidate words; the sum over p of that count times the coordinatewise group maximum must dominate R_(h') on every old index. This is an upper bound, not an assumption of distinct signed tags. If no feasible first lift pays this necessary quota at both candidate second roots, the chart fails.
