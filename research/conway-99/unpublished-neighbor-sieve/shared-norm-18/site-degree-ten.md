# Using actual graph sites in the rank-48 gluing: a degree-ten certificate

It is impossible for the only present types among the twelve selected unit types to be neutral norm 24, unit (1,27), and unit (3,37). In particular this eliminates one of the original ten rank-36 three-type alternatives. The argument uses a determinant-49 maximal extension, but no maximum graph-wide counts particular to rank 36, so the same conditional exclusion holds at any remaining rank. It says nothing by itself about the unrestricted graph.

## What is counted

Fix one maximal totally isotropic H and the rank-44, determinant-49 extension as in the [short shell moments](../short-shell-moments.md). Keep actual graph labels b satisfying Ab=−4b−2a, sum b=−11a, sum b²=t, and whose seven-component is in H-perpendicular. For a graph site p and value i count those with b_p=i. Average over the 99 choices of p and divide by eight at nonzero residual quadratic value, or one at zero value, exactly as for the original W(a,t) gluing population. Denote this number K(a,t,i). Thus sum_i K=W(a,t) in **raw** (pre unit-subtraction) variables. The division by eight is an averaging over the eight residual class identifications, not an assumption that their graph counts are equal. The averaging over p likewise assumes no vertex transitivity. Every K is nonnegative. Negating b identifies (a,t,i) with (−a,t,−i).

At the chosen p let d=3e_p−Ae_p. Translation by nd preserves the seven-component since the corrected vector added is nu_p and 3u_p is in the original lattice. Before folding, the parameters become
```
A=a+n, T=t+23n²+2n(7i+2a), I=i+3n.
```
To return to −4≤a≤4 put j=floor((A+4)/9). The normalized signature is (A−9j,T−22Aj+99j²,I+j) by adding j times the all-ones vector to the raw label; this does not change the corrected vector. Each such switch is reversible, so may equate the corresponding site counts, including after negation. The code groups positions with a common translated/signed representative z and writes their common value X(z). A finite range of switches suffices because each identification made is an actual bijection; missing further identifications would only weaken the test.

The allowed index filters are conservative. Always t≥11|a|, t≡a modulo two, the nonzero neutral minimum is 14, charges ±1 cannot have t≤19 and ±2 cannot have t=24, by the [affine dictionary](../affine-witness.md). On site p, its 14 neighbors and its 84 nonneighbors the coordinate sums are i, −4i−2a and 3i−9a. The three Cauchy bounds give 84i²+6(4i+2a)²+(3i−9a)²≤84t. Any failure after a switch also excludes the original slot. The checker enumerates every integer −sqrt(t)≤i≤sqrt(t) subject only to these necessary tests; it is harmless if unremoved dummy slots could never arise.

The raw population and its first two site moments satisfy sum_i (9i+a)K=0 and sum_i (99i²−t)K=0. The [upstream higher-moment rows](higher_moments.py) use unit rather than raw counts for four short transfers. To use them the code first undoes their recorded substitutions; it then imposes the zero *unit* populations on the face, not zeros on unchecked raw counts. In this particular face every source of those four transfers vanishes. The script includes all projected types occurring at shells 2,4,6 in **each** of the four shifted/unshifted gluings, whether or not present in the short nine-row projection.

## Graph-site harmonic rows

Use exactly the even unimodular 48-dimensional gluings, projected numerators w, auxiliary Gram matrix B and scale s=1 or 3 of [the higher-degree identity](higher-moments.md). For any actual site p and any fixed integer coefficient vector h in the auxiliary four-space take the real harmonic axis v=9u_p+3h. Its squared length is 252+9 hᵗBh, since u_p²=28/9. The inner product of a norm-2n gluing vector with this axis is
```
9i+a + (3/s) w·h.
```
The graph and auxiliary parts are orthogonal; in the graph part the corrected evaluation is i+a/9. Average over all seven-primary anti-isometries as in the upstream gluing and over p. At each nonzero residual value a fixed auxiliary w pairs with each class with probability 1/8, independent of the chosen site p. Negative charges give the reflected tuple (−b,−w), whose contribution is identical for the even degrees considered. Thus for a≥0 each enumerated numerator contributes its zonal harmonic at the displayed angle with multiplicity K(a,t,i) times 2 if a>0 and 1 otherwise.

The spherical harmonic has terms c_j(v,z)^(d−2j)(v²z²)^j; c_0=1 and c_(j+1)/c_j=−(d−2j)(d−2j−1)/[2(j+1)(48+2d−2j−4)]. A weighted theta series is a cusp form of weight 24+d because the lattice is even unimodular. Write Qn for its coefficient at q^n. At weight 26 the one-dimensional space generated by Delta E14 gives Q2=−48Q1, Q3=−195804Q1. For d=4,6,8,10 the two-dimensional cusp spaces give Q3=c1 Q1+c2 Q2 with (c1,c2)=(151740,192),(−99180,−552),(50220,432),(−22140,−312) respectively. The first three are the upstream rows; for d=10 one may expand Delta E4^4 E6 and Delta² E4 E6 through q³. Three fixed auxiliary directions h=(0,0,0,0),(1,2,3,1),(1,−1,1,−2), not all directions or any graph symmetry orbit, suffice.

## Exact sign certificate

There are two integral lower bounds on the averaged, folded site counts. K(1,23,−1)≥14: each of the 99 ordinary d_j gives −1 at its 14 neighbors. K(2,36,−2)≥7: for each of 693 graph edges xy, d_x+d_y has entries +2 at x,y, has −2 at their unique common neighbor, and hence is distinct from the mark of every other edge. Its squared evaluation norm is 36. Both marked families have trivial seven-component, so there is no divisor of eight in these two inequalities.

The normalized nonnegative expression
```
5940 X(−4,44, 2) +1650 X(−4,46,−1) +2970 X(−4,48, 2)
 +946 X(−1,23,−1) +220 X(−1,29,−1) +1260 X(0,22,−1)
 +627 [K(1,23,−1)−14] +165 [K(2,36,−2)−7]
```
is **−70** by the exact row span. The representative X(−4,44,2) can even be zero by the integer absolute-value bound, but this is not needed. The checker [site_degree_ten.py](site_degree_ten.py) constructs the raw rows, the actual marked site harmonic averages and every signature switch, using only integer/rational arithmetic. The 60 signed-charge types (using the nonnegative charge orientation) produce 172 site orbit columns. Including the constant in the affine rows gives rank 165, unchanged on appending the displayed identity. Its impossible sign proves the assertion without any linear-programming feasibility decision.

The [unconditional lift and explicit rational witness](site-degree-ten-global.md) preserve the nine absence rows as variables. The direct dot-product replay does not need a row reduction.
