# At maximum binary Hoffman nullity, every signature is distinct and there is no rook grid

**Subsequent strengthening.** [Binary nullity seven is impossible](hoffman-binary-nullity-six.md) in an actual Hoffman design. The argument below first restricts that hypothetical case; its signature conclusion is used in the later proof.

Use the [binary Hoffman design code and its dyadic lift](hoffman-binary-grid-deck.md). If k=dim ker(D^T mod 2)=7, then B2=0, the graph has no nine-site rook grid, and j=0 (so v2(det L+)=37). Without fixing k, v2(det L+)≤45. This is a conditional result: it does not prove either that k=7 or that a grid must occur.

Write p=B2. The binary code identity gives B4=112−7p, and the design has 77 distinct actual blocks, so p≤5. The p candidate pairs are the pairs of equal coordinate signatures, meaning columns of any generator matrix of K. Join two disjoint candidate pairs e,f in a mate graph when e∪f is an actual block. Every pair e is in exactly two blocks by the design. In either one, the complementary pair f has equal signatures because a block belongs to K^perp. The two complements are disjoint: if not, two distinct design columns would share three C sites, impossible by the SRG common-neighbor cap. Thus this mate graph is simple and 2-regular.

Suppose p>0. A class of size four would already give six pairs. If a class of size three exists, it consumes three pairs, leaving only two others. Its three internal pairs cannot have each other as mates (they overlap), so every one of the three must have both remaining pairs as mates; this contradicts degree two. Consequently all nonsingleton classes are doublets, mutually disjoint. The 2-regular graph then has p≥3 (indeed it is a p-cycle). We now rule this out.

Each of the 2p doublet endpoints occurs in fourteen actual blocks, just two of them with its mate. Thus there are 24p incidences using a doublet singly, counted with multiplicity, so at least 6p actual blocks use some doublet singly. On any dual tetrad the independent swaps of doublets preserve membership in K^perp (a swap changes its indicator by a weight-two word of K^perp). An orbit using d≥1 doublets singly is a d-dimensional binary cube. Two vertices of that cube adjacent by a swap share three points, so they cannot both be actual blocks. Pairing opposite sides by any one coordinate gives an injection of the actual blocks of this orbit into its omitted tetrads. In total at least 6p of the B4 dual tetrads are omitted. However the number omitted is exactly 35−7p. This gives 13p≤35, contrary to p≥3. Therefore p=0. The known grid-pair injection proves G=0. As a count of the remaining code words, complement symmetry gives A8=A14=x and A10=A12=y with x+y=63. The weight-two Krawtchouk identity with B2=0 gives 462+14x−18y=0. Thus the complete weight enumerator by Hamming weight is 1+21t⁸+42t¹⁰+42t¹²+21t¹⁴+t²².

Finally let c∈(Z/4)^22 be a quarter lift with D^Tc=0, and z its reduction mod two. For integer representatives 0,1,2,3 put σ=Σc and w=wt(z). From 16 dividing ||D^Tc||²=12||c||²+2σ², one gets σ≡w mod4 (both are even, and 6w+σ²=0 mod8). Thus if n_a are the four multiplicities, n2+n3 is even. Set x_i+iy_i=i^(c_i), U=Σ_i i^(c_i). Every block has phase product 1, so the sum of its four phases has real or imaginary part zero. Indeed if the number of odd labels is two, the phase product forces one of the even or odd phase pairs to cancel; the other parity counts are immediate. Therefore 0=(D^Tx)·(D^Ty)=2(Σx)(Σy), since x·y=0 and DD^T=12I+2J. The even integers Re U, Im U sum to 22−2(n2+n3)≡2 modulo four, so exactly one of them is 2 modulo four.

This also holds for c+2s for every s∈K. The real and imaginary parts remain separately unchanged modulo four: wt(s) is even and z∈K^perp by the half-mark pairing in the binary code note, so the flipped signs in each parity sector are even in number. Hence the same one of these two sums vanishes for every s. That sector's signed coefficients must cancel inside each signature class, by orthogonality of the functions s↦(-1)^{s_i} on K. In particular all signature classes of odd multiplicity occur in the other parity sector. At k=7 every signature is distinct, so z is constant. Thus the reduction of the quarter kernel has just the constants, j=0, and v2=23+2·7=37. For k≤6 the previously proved j≤k−1 gives v2≤45.
