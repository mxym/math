# No two-edge cuts in a saturated contraction

At any saturated triangle T of a hypothetical Conway graph, the [cubic contraction](saturated-five-currents.md) K has **no edge cut of size two**, independently of all graph ranks. In particular every connected component of K either consists of two tails joined by all three parallel edges (a prism wing), or is a simple, 3-edge-connected cubic graph. This rules out 465 of the 580 connected, properly three-colored contraction types with twelve tails as a single literal component, leaving 115 types before other graph constraints. The numbers are isomorphism classes of colored local contractions, not graph counts.

**Cut marking.** For any set S of k contraction tails, take a coefficient vector a equal to k/2 on the three roots T, one on the three wing sites at each selected tail, and zero elsewhere. Write G=3I−A+J/9 and f=Ga. The coefficient sum is 9k/2; directly f vanishes on T. At a wing dart i→j the two other darts of its tail are its near neighbors, together with its mate and its physical root. Thus f(i→j)=1_S(i)−1_S(j). At a far site x, f(x)=k/2−the number of its contacts at selected tails. Since G²=7G and its image is the neutral minus-four eigenspace,
    f·f=7 a·f=7 |δ_K S|.
Each crossing edge contributes once to a·f, through its dart whose tail lies in S. The vector is integral when k is even.

In each physical color the mate edges form a perfect matching on the tails. Its number of edges crossing S therefore has the same parity as k. If the total cut has size two, k is even and the two edges have the same color. This gives a nonzero integral neutral graph field of squared norm fourteen. The [norm-fourteen Fano characterization](../short-support-packing/README.md) says its seven positives and seven negatives are disjoint independent sets, with each positive incident to four negatives and with the two common neighbors of any two positives already in the negative set. (Equivalently the signed bipartite field is the complement of Fano incidence; the assertions also hold after negation.) But the physical root of the common cut color is a zero site and sees both distinct positively oriented boundary darts. This violates the common-neighbor cap of two. The same perfect-matching parity already rules out a single-edge cut. Two tails carrying exactly two parallel edges would have just two edges leaving their pair, so they are impossible; three parallel edges form a whole component.

**Optional catalogue count.** Starting from the independently verified [connected contraction enumeration](connected-saturated-five-rank.md), the [short tally](saturated_two_cuts.py) tests all proper tail subsets up to complement for the size of their cut. On 2,4,6,8,10,12 tails respectively the total catalog sizes are 1,2,5,19,81,580; the cut-two exclusions are 0,1,3,13,64,465. No catalogue is needed for the theorem.
