# The short rectangles give the entire original-class kernel over the integers

Continue the [short rectangle spanning theorem](rooted-rectangle-fields.md) at any vertex o. Write V_Z for the integral kernel of the unsigned incidence D on the 84 labels, E for all integral minus-four fields vanishing on N[o], and J for the additive lattice generated by the conflict-free rectangle fields at o. If r is the mod-seven Seidel rank, then

    J=(3I-A)V_Z,             7E is contained in J,
    E/J ≅ (Z/7Z)^(44-r).

Moreover J consists exactly of the fields of *original* seven-class zero inside E. Thus every original seven-class has an integral representative zero on N[o], with any two representatives of the same class differing by an integral sum of norm-56/70/84 rectangle fields. This is independent of any choice of dyadic extension. The elementary divisors and the discriminant rank were already available from the rooted lattice; the additional point here is the explicit integral short generating set for the full kernel.

## Integral rectangle generation

The light/heavy comparison argument of the spanning theorem works over any field, as it uses only equalities and telescoping. In every characteristic it says that all functions annihilating conflict-free rectangles are g_ij=a_i+a_j+c. In odd characteristic absorb c/2 into each a_i. The usual 14-dimensional potential map is injective, since the connected label graph contains a triangle on which zero pair-sums force all three potentials zero. In characteristic two the potential image is its cut space of dimension 13: constants are the only kernel. Also the edge function one is not a cut, since its sum on a triangle is one. Hence the annihilator still has dimension 14. (One must not instead claim that these rectangles span the entire mod-two incidence kernel, which has dimension 71.)

The integral kernel V_Z is primitive of rank 70, because the integer incidence image is torsion-free of rank 14. In particular V_Z mod p embeds as a 70-dimensional subspace of the ambient 84 coordinates for every prime p. The integer span of the conflict-free rectangles lies in V_Z, and its reduction has rank 70 for every p by the annihilator calculation. Its index in V_Z is therefore one. Applying 3I-A proves the first equality. For f in E we have f in V_Z and (3I-A)f=7f, proving 7E⊂J.

## Identification at seven

Use the [rooted frame](rooted-rank32.md), with m_b of Gram Q and evaluation x↦t. By the frame identity the full E corresponds to L*, with inverse x=(sum t_b m_b)/56, where L is the unsaturated span of the marks m_b. For t=(3I-A)z, z in V_Z, the inverse is x=(sum z_b m_b)/8. At seven these projected combinations give the entirety of L over Z_7: DD^t=11I-M+J, with eigenvalues 12,10,24 (M is the original mate permutation); the determinant is a seven-unit. The projection onto ker D along im D^t is thus integral over Z_7, and the m-combination map kills im D^t. The last fact follows from their residual construction (every evaluation field vanishes on N and so lies in ker D), equivalently QD^t=0. Multiplication by 1/8 has no effect on the local span.

The quotient is therefore the seven-primary discriminant of L, the elementary group of dimension 44-r from the rooted frame. The 14 closed-root neighbor marks split off a unimodular factor at seven in the global graph lattice, and the residual similarity by eight is a seven-adic unit square, so this is precisely the original graph seven-class group under the rooted identification. There is no other kernel obstruction, since all elementary divisors of E/J divide seven. Equivalently one can compute the mod-seven rank of (3I-A) on V via Q/8; Q has rank r-14 and im D^t is killed, giving 30-(r-14)=44-r nontrivial elementary divisors.
