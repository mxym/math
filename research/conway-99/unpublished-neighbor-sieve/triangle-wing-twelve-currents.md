# Disconnected triangle wings: twelve-site harmonic currents obstruct rank 36

Fix a triangle T, its 36-site cubic wing graph W and sixty far sites X, in the notation of [triangle-complement.md](triangle-complement.md) (including the earlier Lou–Murin wing decomposition). Each connected component of 6k meets all three wings in 2k sites each; each x in X has precisely k contacts in the component, and two contacts overall in each wing. Use [twelve-sites.md](twelve-sites.md) and its existing checker for the 15 unordered connected twelve-site components satisfying the elementary pair quotas; two of these were already excluded there. No graph symmetry is assumed when minimizing names of abstract wing templates.

**Theorem.** On the mod-seven Seidel rank-36 branch, for a split of W into components of sizes 12 and 24, if the larger tree number is a seven-unit, the surviving twelve-site component of tree number 7350 cannot occur. The twelve-site component of tree number 7140 can occur in this situation only if the larger tree number is a nonsquare mod 7. For a split into three connected twelve-site components, 58 out of the 455 unordered component multisets on the previously retained 13 connected types cannot occur.

Write A,B for the types of tree numbers 7350,7140, in that order, and U+ or U− for a seven-unit tree number that is square or nonsquare modulo 7. Precisely the following 58 multisets are excluded by the stated dimension and discriminant test (not a claim that every other multiset is possible):
* A,U,U with equal U signs: 36.
* A,B,U: 11.
* B,B,U+: 6.
* A,A,U−: 5.

Also, in the A,A,A case the three distinct current planes below must be coplanar in the projective sense: their vector-space span has dimension exactly three.

## Rank accounting and graph inputs

On W the ordinary graph marking Gram is G=L_W+J/9. Over F7, each component size in the two splits above is a unit, so centered and constant coefficients separate. There is only one nonzero constant direction, represented by the all-one vector of W and of norm 36²/9, a unit square. In each component take the centered Laplacian block in the basis e_i−e_last. For a component with tree number a unit, its determinant has the square class of the tree number by the matrix-tree theorem (the basis multiplies the tree cofactor by the square of the component size). For a singular block, let its sign be the quadratic character of the determinant of its nondegenerate quotient. The checker enumerates the old 15 templates, omits only the two already excluded, and obtains six U+, five U−, one A with centered radical dimension two and sign +, and one B with radical dimension one and sign −. With the previous notation for the three local mate matchings and cross return permutation, one choice of representatives is A=(M0,M1,M0,(0,2,1,3)) and B=(M0,M1,M0,(0,3,2,1)). Thus both A and B are genuine survivors of the prior pair-quota chart.

Let ell be the rank of G mod 7. For a centered radical current h on a twelve-component (padded by zeros elsewhere), its pairing with the graph mark of a far vertex x is −(h_i+h_j), where e={i,j} are the two contacts of x in that component. Every such pair occurs with multiplicity m_e=2−[i~j]−|N_W(i)∩N_W(j)|−[same wing], exactly as in twelve-sites.md. The virtual kernel consisting of unbalanced component constants is not counted as a current. All nonzero centered harmonic currents on these twelve-components are visible on X. Indeed the matrix with rows 1_e for m_e>0 has full column rank 12 over F7, checked independently of an exterior assignment. (Equivalently each connected component of its pair-support graph contains an odd cycle.)

If the evaluations on X span dimension s, the whole graph marking Gram has rank at least ell+2s over F7. In detail, split off the ell-dimensional nondegenerate part of W, then select s radical currents with independent pairings and choose s exterior columns dual to these pairings. Their span has Gram congruent to a hyperbolic 2s-block, with determinant (−1)^s up to square. This only needs each pair of distinct currents within the selected span to pair to zero, automatic since they originate in the W radical. At rank 36 the full nondegenerate marking space has nonsquare determinant, by the seven-local unit/discriminant calculation in [triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md](triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md): after removal of 7^8 the total determinant is a square and the dimension-eight seven-discriminant has minus type. Consequently equality ell+2s=36 forces the product of the W nondegenerate sign with (−1)^s to be negative. Here (−1) is also the nonsquare character of minus one in F7.

## Comparing currents of two twelve-components, without knowing the whole graph

Their sixty pair evaluations share the same far vertices; they cannot be freely permuted. If two currents evaluate identically, assign an exterior vertex its two pairs e,f. Their values h(e)=h(i)+h(j) and k(f) must agree mod 7. The histogram including multiplicities must agree. For any orientation of the named wings (all six relative permutations checked), remove combinations using more than two sites in a wing. Let n_ef be the number of exterior vertices assigned these pairs. Each n_ef is 0 or 1 since two distinct sites cannot have four common neighbors. The e and f marginals are their prescribed pair multiplicities. For any i in the first component and j in the second,
 sum_(e contains i, f contains j) n_ef = 2−[i and j in corresponding wing].
Indeed i,j are nonadjacent and no W-site meets both; in the same wing their triangle root is one of their two common neighbors; otherwise neither common neighbor lies in T. These are exact *integer* equations, in particular equations modulo two. The parity obstruction uses the little column matrix with exactly six ones per possible (e,f): its two marginal equations and four cross-site equations. Binary Gaussian elimination directly checks membership of the required RHS in its column space. A row with fewer possible entries than its required integer total already rejects.

For A,B and B,B choose h in every projective current line of the first component (one scalar normalization) and every nonzero k on the second, checking each relative wing permutation. A,B has 252 inconsistent histograms plus 36 parity contradictions; B,B gives 24 plus 12. Thus these pairs of evaluated spaces intersect trivially in every actual graph. For A,A, equality of the two entire planes would prescribe an invertible 2-by-2 basis comparison on their vector-valued pair labels. Of 2016 GL(2,7) maps times six relative wing permutations, 12024 fail histograms and all other 72 have a cross-cell capacity shortfall. Thus two A current planes are distinct (we do not assert that they meet trivially).

## Arithmetic

For 12+24 with unit larger tree, ell=35−d where d is the smaller centered radical dimension; visibility gives s=d. At d=2 the lower rank is 37. At d=1 equality in dimension 36, the sign of B is −, and the hyperbolic plane contributes −, requiring a nonsquare sign from the larger tree.

For 12+12+12, ell=34−2a−b where a,b count A,B. A current has dimension 2, B current 1; A,B and B,B have trivial intersection and A,A are not equal. Using these lower bounds on s in the rank and determinant test gives the stated table by 455 elementary multiset checks. With three A planes, rank forces s≤4 and nonsquare determinant excludes s=4; pairwise distinct planes give s≥3. The [checker](triangle_wing_twelve_currents.py) uses only the existing twelve_sites.py and Python's standard library. No topology of the far graph or global branch exclusion is inferred.

[The full type-A intersection test](triangle-wing-aa-direct.md) further shows two A current images are disjoint, raising the excluded rank-36 count to 66 of 455 in this particular split.

**All seven-singular types in the three-twelve split are excluded.** The [A plus two unit components test](auu-plane-quotient.md) and [A plus two B components test](abb-orthogonal-quotient.md) complete the zero-section exclusions of this split. At rank 36 any actual 12+12+12 triangle must have three seven-unit tree numbers: 169 of the 455 abstract multisets are excluded by the singular-type result, leaving the 286 pure-unit multisets for other arguments. This does not assert that any of those are realizable or rule out other triangle-wing splits.

**First seven-unit family.** The [far-cap boundary test](hhu-cap-boundary.md) also excludes all eleven pure-unit multisets containing at least two copies of the hexagonal-prism type. Thus at least 180 of 455 multisets in this specific rank-36 split are excluded, leaving 275 not ruled out by these tests.
