# Rank 36 has no original Fano root

In a hypothetical Conway graph of Seidel rank 36 over F₇, no norm-14 field f has its normalized root r=f/√7 in the original marked lattice at seven. Thus the original norm-two coefficient Y₁ is zero. By the [two-edge-cut emission](arbitrary-wing-thin-cuts.md), every one of the 231 triangle wings is free of such a thin cut; each connected component of its cubic wing is 3-edge-connected, since bridges are also impossible. This does not rule out displaced Fanos or the graph.

**Two sites to pin.** Use the [rank-three residue-space test](fano-six-contact-determinant.md), including its 32 labeled six-contact masks. Label the seven positive indices and seven negative indices by the nonzero binary three-vectors. The cross edge (p,n) means p·n=1 over F₂. Fix p=(1,0,0), and let q=(0,1,0), a different positive site. Index P,N,Z by 0..6,7..13,14..28, and p's ten external contact neighbors Yp by 29..38. Each H-site outside P∪N∪Z has one positive label, one negative label, and a triple of its fifteen Z-neighbors. There is one H-site on an adjacent signed pair, two on a nonadjacent one.

The 15 Z-point triples on Yp are equivalently the 15 edges of a *simple cubic graph* D on its ten sites, since each z is seen exactly twice there and each pair already shares p. First order the four caps of Yp by their negative labels, then the three double fibers in order by negative label. Their two copies can be numbered so that the three actual mate edges between these fibers are (4,7),(6,9),(8,5) in 0..9 coordinates. This is possible because the mate and duplicate matchings form a single alternating six-cycle. The resulting 32 six-site masks have respectively 4,5,6,9 internal D-edges with multiplicities 12,18,1,1. For direct verification of the upstream filter, the checker computes the scaled residual Gram in the order (sum of all six, three oriented double differences). Its matrix is H−Lᵀ D₆ L, where the rows of L at a double copy are (1, ±eᵢ), H₁₁=4, H₁ⱼ=0, Hᵢᵢ=5 for i>1 and the other off-diagonals are 3. It has rank three and square determinant type exactly in the 32 cases. The sum of the four caps in the upstream formula and minus the sum of all six doubles agree in the corresponding quotient; after scaling, the altered fourth diagonal and signs give this displayed convention. Only the 32 licensed masks are required below.

**3,692 cubic diagrams.** If the mask has m edges there are m−3 edges among the four caps, by cubicity. Choose them and fill all edges between the six doubles and four caps to the exact degree deficits. Label Z by the now-specified edges of D. Only isomorphisms of this finite rooted diagram are used: the 24 binary linear transformations fixing p act contragrediently on negative labels. On double copies each induces the unique swap pattern preserving the three mate edges. First quotient masks, then full D diagrams by the mask stabilizer. The exact diagram counts are 1,344 / 2,211 / 136 / 1, respectively. These changes of coordinates on the hypothetical graph need not be graph automorphisms: the second site q exists with its own ten H-contacts in every resulting labeling.

**The spanning core.** Form the true 39-site adjacency A₀ and append the original root to the ordinary mark Gram G=3I−A₀+4J: its evaluations are +1 on P, −1 on N and zero elsewhere, and its norm is 2. In all 3,692 cases the 40-by-40 Gram has rank 36 over F₇. A basis indexed by nonsingular principal indices I therefore spans the entire mod-seven mark space. This conclusion uses original membership of the root; it must not be inferred for a displaced root.

**The only further test is the ten external contacts of q.** A candidate v has fixed signs q,n, an arbitrary three-subset of Z, and its unknown adjacency set in Yp. It is nonadjacent to p. If n∼p the common-neighbor quota requires one member of Yp; otherwise two. In the latter case these two are nonadjacent, with exactly one common neighbor (p) in the known 39 sites before v is added; there are 24 allowed pairs. Writing C for the resulting 39 binary contact entries, discard rows with C A₀ + C > 2 in any entry. Its required pairings w are 4−C on the 39 sites and zero on the Fano root. For T=G_{II}^{−1} require w_I T G_{I,*}=w and w_I T w_Iᵀ=0. There is no hidden residual orientation. Two rows cannot represent different sites with identical pairings because their mutual pairing would be zero rather than 3 or 4.

For every candidate pair, the forced mark pairing w_I T w′_Iᵀ must be 3 for an edge or 4 for a nonedge. The common neighbors already in the core must number ≤1 or ≤2 accordingly. In N(q), a singleton-contact cap at an adjacent n is already mated to n, so it cannot be adjacent to another Yq contact. Nor can two contacts of the same double label be adjacent (they share both q and n). A true set of ten candidates must contain exactly 2−A(q,n) contacts per negative label n, and exactly two contacts at every Z point, since all common neighbors of q and z belong to Yq.

The checker chooses a needed column with least number of candidate subsets and branches over the earliest chosen candidate in that column. It removes incompatible rows and any columns whose quota was filled. This enumerates all still-possible ten-site sets, without trying to complete a 99-vertex graph. Every one of the 3,692 inputs is rejected at the fixed q above, in 1,043,033 total search nodes, at most 613 on any chart. The defensive search cutoff of 100,000 nodes does not count as exclusion and is never reached. Typical replay is about three minutes with Python, NumPy and SymPy. No floating-point arithmetic, random choices or external solver are used.

Run `python rank36_no_original_fano.py` with Python, NumPy and SymPy.
