# The actual outer adjacency five-rank for every saturated triangle

In a hypothetical Conway graph let T be a saturated triangle (all twelve transversal triangles), S the 39 coordinates in T and its wings, X the 60 exterior coordinates, and H their adjacency. Let n_i be the even sizes in tails of the connected components of its colored cubic contraction, c their number and e the kernel dimension of its far mod-five current evaluation. Put z=#{i:5 divides n_i}, equal to one exactly for partition (10,2) and zero otherwise. Then the **actual** exterior adjacency satisfies
  rank_F5(H−I) = 53−c−2e−z.
This recovers the [six-prism rank](six-prism-far-five-rank.md) when c=6. Combined with the [universal bound on e](universal-saturated-five-rank.md) it gives a restricted interval in a single parity for every component shape. For connected K the possible ranks are 46,48,50,52; no existence assertion about any value is made.

Work throughout over F5 in the 99-coordinate nondegenerate dot space. Since 7, 9 and 99 are units at five, the rational symmetric projectors stay integral here; their ranks are unchanged (their images are direct summands over the 5-local integers). P=(3I−A+J/9)/7 projects to the rank-44 minus-four eigenspace, and Q=I−P to its full orthogonal complement, **including the constant eigenspace**. Write P_* and Q_* for their images. The far matrix satisfies H−I=2Q_XX+J_X/9 by reduction. Since H has row sum eight and 60=0 mod5, Q_XX 1=1. Hence Q_XX and H−I have equal rank: the latter is 2(I+J_X/18)Q_XX, and J_X²=0.

Set M=S∩Q_*, U=P(S), with the coordinate subspace S understood. The three triangle marks lie in the wing mark span: the three relations l_w=4e_(t_w)+Σ_(v∈T,v≠t_w)e_v+1_(physical wing w) are killed by P, and their root-coordinate coefficient matrix is 3I+J, invertible. By [the saturated-wing calculation](saturated-five-currents.md), on the 36 wing sites the mod-five Gram has rank 31−2c and actual radical-image dimension 6+c−e. Thus dim U=37−c−e and dim M=2+c+e. More explicitly M is the internal direct sum of:
* three relations l_w;
* wing functions constant a_i on each component, with Σ n_i a_i=0 (dimension c−1, since Σn_i=12≠0);
* the e invisible circulations, represented by paired opposite dart values.
All are killed by P by the same current theorem, and independent (look first at triangle roots; then use reversal even/odd). Their dimensions exhaust M. These three subspaces are mutually orthogonal: the component-weight constraint handles l_w against constants, and the paired darts cancel in both constant and physical-wing sums. The first block has Gram (3I+J)²+12I=21I+9J, nonsingular at five. The second has Gram induced by 3Σ n_i a_i b_i on the indicated hyperplane. The constant direction of the full component coefficient space is nonsingular because Σn_i=12; its orthogonal complement is precisely that hyperplane. Thus its radical consists of the z coordinates with weight zero modulo five. The last block is totally isotropic by the flow norm identity. It follows that dim rad M=e+z.

For completeness, the following exact linear-algebra step requires no semisimplicity assumption about the induced far adjacency. The map y↦Qy gives a surjection ker(Q_XX)→rad M: y belongs to X and Qy has only S coordinates, so Qy∈M; since P y projected onto S is −Qy, this belongs to M⊥ inside S. Conversely the projection of P_* onto S is exactly M⊥, by taking orthogonal complements in the coordinate subspace. For any m∈rad M choose p∈P_* projecting to −m in S. Then y=p+m∈X and Qy=m. The kernel is P_*∩X, of dimension 44−dim U (equivalently, it is the orthogonal complement of U inside the nondegenerate P_*). Hence nullity(Q_XX)=44−(37−c−e)+e+z, giving the stated rank.
