# Rank 36: the dimension-three 18-component has no 18-component partner

Fix a triangle of a hypothetical Conway graph, and its 36-site cubic wings and sixty exterior sites. Use the named-wing parametrization and centered F7 Laplacian current of [triangle-wing-eighteen-currents.md](triangle-wing-eighteen-currents.md). The unique unlabelled 18-component type D3 of centered nullity 3 cannot occur in an 18+18 split at rank 36.

The earlier dimension/determinant theorem leaves only another D3 or the unique nullity-two positive-determinant type D2: an 18+18 split has near rank 35−d−e and rank at least that plus twice the dimension of the joint exterior current evaluations. Each individual current evaluation map is injective. For d=3 the only partners allowed are d=3 and d=2 of positive sign, and in the latter case the entire 2-space of exterior evaluations is contained in the 3-space. The (D3,D3) case was excluded separately by [binary elimination](wing-eighteen-three-three.md). Here we exclude (D3,D2).

Relative wing labels may be fixed: the D3 template realizes every wing permutation. Its three within-wing mate permutations are (1,0,3,2,5,4), (2,4,0,5,1,3), (1,0,5,4,3,2); alternate successive cross matchings are identities from wing zero to one and from one to two, and the last is (3,5,4,1,2,0) from two to zero. D2 uses the same mates, with last permutation (4,3,1,0,2,5). For fixed bases, let v3(f),v2(e) denote the current sum vectors on one triple in each component. Containment now says v2(e)=M v3(f) simultaneously for all 60 far sites with an unknown 2-by-3 rank-two matrix M mod seven. This involves neither arbitrary row permutations nor a graph automorphism assumption.

For a pair of distinct near sites i,j, its exterior common-neighbor quota is
m_ij=2−[adjacent]−|N_W(i)∩N_W(j)|−[same wing].
Form one row per m_ij>0, with target m=(m_ij). Each far site has three contacts in each component and two in each wing. Its triples have all internal pair quotas positive, at least two distinct wing labels, and each satisfies [i in e]+|N_D(i)∩e|≤2 for every site i of its component. These last inequalities count known common sites of i and that far vertex. There are 578 allowable left D2 triples and 612 right D3 triples. For a fixed M take a column b per allowable triple pair with complementary wing counts and v2(e)=M v3(f), containing precisely the 15 near pairs in its six contacts. An actual exterior would give m=Σ_x b_x, with exactly sixty terms. Thus any integer y satisfying

    m·y < 60 min_b b·y

rejects M, even if repeated columns or arbitrary nonnegative *real* masses of sum 60 are allowed.

There are (7³−1)(7³−7)=114,912 possible matrices. Enumerate each component's wing-aware automorphisms: choose a permutation of wings and of the six sites of wing zero, then use its two perfect cross matchings to force every remaining image, checking edges. Each automorphism induces its matrix U (or V) on v2 (or v3) by exact F7 elimination. Simultaneous pairs with the same wing permutation act by M↦U M V^−1; there are 12 actions. Choosing least base-seven matrix code in the orbit gives exactly 9,726 representatives (verified by direct comparison with all 114,912 maps, without assuming free action).

The [stand-alone integer verifier](wing_eighteen_three_two.py) constructs a strict separator for every representative. The search only proposes integral y using a deterministic residual iteration with sparse integer matrix products, and plays no mathematical role unless the strict displayed inequality is then checked. The target is positive with total 900, and each candidate column has 15 ones. The verifier bounds all separator coordinates strictly in absolute value by 2^42 before evaluating its integer dot products, so the comparisons are exact in signed 64-bit arithmetic. No LP status, matrix conditioning, convergence, tolerance or assumption about unseen vectors enters this proof. Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 wing_eighteen_three_two.py` (NumPy plus SciPy sparse integer multiplication, on the order of a minute per core). No external data, optimization solver or full-graph generation is used.

There is a second, direct exclusion: D3 cannot occur with three six-site components at rank 36. Every six-component is the triangular prism (the other simple connected cubic possibility K3,3 violates the common-neighbor bound). Its tree number is 75, e.g. from Laplacian spectrum 0,2,3,3,5,5; the determinant character of its centered block is therefore negative over F7 (75≡5). The centered dimensions for the four components total 36−4=32 before removing the three-dimensional radical of D3; the global constant block contributes one nondegenerate square direction, so ell=30. The three visible D3 currents force three hyperbolic planes, exhausting rank 36. The residual determinant character of D3 is positive, each of the three prisms is negative, and each of the three hyperbolic planes is negative: the product is positive. This contradicts the nonsquare ambient determinant at rank 36.

Remaining scope: in an 18+12+6 split with D3, a seven-unit 12-component gives ell=31 and rank at least 37. For the singular 12-components A (d=2) and B (d=1), the twelve-current image must be contained in the three-current image; neither is claimed excluded by this note. This does not exclude every 18+18 configuration, rank 36, or the unrestricted graph.
