# A short integral field vanishes on every closed triangle neighborhood

At mod-seven Seidel rank 36, every triangle T of a hypothetical SRG(99,14,1,2) admits a nonzero integral −4 eigenvector of ordinary squared norm at most **76** that vanishes on T and on all 36 of its wing vertices. If the cubic wing graph is disconnected the bound is at most **70**. No rank or graph is excluded here. The calculation is independent of the [rank-36 tree square-class criterion](triangle-wing-tree-rank36.md); it also applies when the tree number is divisible by seven.

## Exact determinant, including the integral index

Let W be the wing graph, with components of orders s_j=6k_j, j=1,…,c, and tree numbers τ_j. Each component meets each wing in 2k_j sites. Write L_W for the graph Laplacian and n=36. The marks u_v of the [rank-44 lattice](../maximal-marked-lattice/README.md) have Gram matrix 3I−A+J/9 and frame constant 7. The lattice M generated by **all marks** has determinant 7^(44−r)/9: the [known even lattice](../marked-coset-shells/README.md) generated by their differences and 3u_0 has determinant 7^(44−r) and index three in M (u_0 has nonintegral norm 28/9, so does not belong to it).

The W marks have Gram L_W+J/9. Their only real coefficient relations are the component constants of total sum zero. Their integral span E has determinant

$$\det E=\frac{\gcd(s_1,\dots,s_c)^2}{9}\prod_j\tau_j.$$

Indeed delete one mark from each component except the last. The resulting Gram determinant is s_c²·∏τ_j/9 by a rank-one update of the block Laplacian minors: the adjugate of the unreduced last block is τ_c J and each other reduced block has determinant τ_j. The deleted marks generate an extension of index s_c/gcd(s_j), because each deleted mark is congruent modulo retained marks to (s_j/s_c) times the sum of marks in the last component, a primitive vector in the retained basis.

Let H be the span over the integers of the W and T marks together. The −4 equation at each triangle site expresses the vector of wing sums as −(3I+J) times the vector of triangle marks. A combination with coefficients z_1,z_2,z_3 at T is thus represented on W by the three constants p_i=−z_i/3+(Σz)/18 on the three wings. It lies in E iff we can add a component constant at each component, weighted sum zero, to make all coefficients integral. Every component meets all three wings. We first need z_i=u+3v_i with u common modulo three, a condition of index nine. Then p_i≡(Σv_i−u)/6 mod 1, and the further condition is that gcd(s_j) divide 6(Σv_i−u): it is exactly solvability of an integer linear combination of the s_j with that right hand side. It has index g=gcd(k_j), since the residue Σv_i−u modulo g is well-defined (g divides six) and surjective. Consequently [H:E]=9g and

$$\det H=\frac4{81}\prod_j\tau_j.$$

Set I=[M∩span_R(H):H], a positive integer; **I need not be one**. The evaluation map from M* gives precisely all integral −4 fields by the frame identity, with ordinary field inner products seven times the lattice inner products. Vanishing on the closed T neighborhood is perpendicularity to H. In the dual of M the lattice perpendicular to a primitive section H' has determinant det(H')/det M (extend a basis of H' to M and apply the complementary Gram-minor identity). Its rank is d=7+c. Therefore the actual rank-d Euclidean field lattice F_T has determinant

$$\boxed{\det F_T=\frac4{9I^2}\,7^{r-37+c}\prod_j\tau_j.}$$

## Short vector using only uniform cubic tree bounds

For any connected cubic graph on n vertices, the directed nonbacktracking edge matrix B has spectral radius three minus one. For 0<u<1/2,
$0<\det(I-uB)\le1$ by the logarithmic expansion with nonnegative traces. The Bass identity is
$$\det(I-uB)=(1-u^2)^{n/2}\det((1+2u^2)I-u A).$$
For a direct proof, take the two vertex-to-oriented-edge incidence matrices O,H at origins and heads, and let R reverse directed edges. Then B=H^t O−R, O H^t=A, O R H^t=3I; factor I+uR and use det(I−PQ)=det(I−QP). For nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues λ_2,…,λ_n set h=(1−u)(1−2u). The determinant on the right is h·∏(h+uλ_i). Its nonzero eigenvalue sum is 3n and ∏λ_i=nτ. The map x↦log(1+h/(ux)) is convex for x>0. Jensen gives the exact rational bound
$$\tau\le\big[n(1-u^2)^{n/2}u^{n-1}h(1+(n-1)h/(3nu))^{n-1}\big]^{-1}.$$
Use u=9/20 throughout. A six-vertex component is the prism (the cubic alternative K₃,₃ violates the common-neighbor cap), with tree number 75.

The Hermite constant γ_8=2 (equivalently the lattice packing bound in eight dimensions, see [Viazovska](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04246)) and Mordell's inequality give γ_d≤2^((d−1)/7) for d≥8. Indeed a shortest primitive dual vector of a determinant-one lattice cuts out a rank-(d−1) primitive section of determinant its squared norm; optimize the Hermite bounds on the section and on the dual to obtain γ_d≤γ_(d−1)^((d−1)/(d−2)). The squared norm m of a shortest nonzero field satisfies m^(7d)≤2^(d(d−1))(det F_T)^7. Its coordinate sum is zero, hence m is an even integer. The exact [fraction checker](triangle_wing_short_field.py) evaluates this inequality for the eleven partitions of six. At rank 36 the squared-norm caps are respectively

## What happens at the first possible norm

The [short-field classifications](../short-support-packing/README.md) give independent positive and negative sides of size d₀=7,8,9 at norm 14,16,18, with four opposite-sign neighbors per site. A site off the support sees the same number h of each sign. The total exterior incidence of pairs on one side is 2·binom(d₀,2)−6d₀; on W there are 6d₀ single-site incidences because each support site is far from T. Thus the wing contribution alone is at least 6d₀−36, using binom(h,2)≥h−1. Norms 14 and 16 are impossible (0<6 and 8<12). At 18, equality forces every wing site to have h=1 or 2, and every other exterior site h≤1. Each wing has exactly six of each height. Of the 42 far nonsupport sites, six have height zero.

In this last case let P,N be the two signed sides. The [canonical norm-36 field](shared-norm-18/height-three.md) is s(t)=1+3·1_N−A1_P. Write D_T=(3I−A)1_T=1_T−1_W. The field s(t)−D_T is the indicator of the 18 height-one wing sites, the six far zero-height sites, and P. It satisfies A b=6·1−4b: it is an actual 33-set inducing a 2-factor and with six neighbors from every other vertex. Replacing t by −t gives a second such 33-set, with N instead of P. They meet in exactly 24 sites. In particular if graph fields of charge three and cost 33 are absent, then even norm 18 is impossible in every F_T.
