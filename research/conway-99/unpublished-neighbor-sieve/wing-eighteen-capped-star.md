# Rank-36 dimension-three 18-wing: the final single-star test

**Theorem.** No triangle of a rank-36 Conway graph has an 18-site wing component of centered mod-seven Laplacian nullity three. [Previous component/split reductions](wing-eighteen-twelve-six.md) leave precisely the 18(D3)+12(B)+6 profile and 39 comparison charts. The integer single-star test below rejects all 39. The theorem says neither that such a component is unavoidable nor that rank 36 or the unrestricted graph is impossible.

**The local target.** Label the 18-, 12-, and prism components by 0..17, 18..29, and 30..35, exactly as in the previous 18+12+6 checker. Each eligible exterior vertex has a literal six-site contact tuple X of shape 3+2+1 and the signed equality v2=M v3 from the earlier current-containment proof (v2 here is scalar as B has one current). The set of possible six-tuples is reconstructed completely using the same wing and pair rules; the exterior incidence variables themselves are no longer solved. Pick a 12-component vertex i whose two cross-wing neighbors j,k are nonadjacent. Its fourteen neighbors consist of the triangle root, three wing vertices, and ten far vertices. The root and its same-wing mate are paired in the matching induced on this neighborhood. The edges i-j and i-k require their unique mutual neighbors, two different far caps a,b: their B labels are the singleton pairs (i,j),(i,k), respectively. They cannot be the same far vertex, as it meets B in only two sites. In the induced matching, a is mated to j and b to k. Consequently a,b are nonadjacent to each other and to the remaining eight far neighbors of i; those eight must induce four disjoint edges, with no isolated site. Each near site v distinct from i occurs among these ten tuples precisely m_iv times, the previous exact pair quota. All of this is necessary in *every* actual graph.

**Hyperbolic coordinates.** Over F7 take the actual marking Gram 3I−A+J/9, which has diagonal 0, adjacent entry 3 and nonadjacent entry 4. Its ambient quotient has dimension 36. The 36 wing vertices have Gram of rank 30; the verifier chooses any independent set of thirty columns, indexed I, whose principal minor Q is nondegenerate (basis of its nondegenerate quotient; also checked by inversion). For a candidate x, set g_x=(4−1_(v in X)) indexed by v in I and write P_xy=g_x^T Q^−1 g_y. Splitting off this near nondegenerate space leaves six dimensions. The three formal D3 centered currents represent a totally isotropic three-dimensional space inside that complement: their far evaluations are independent by the earlier anchored-current theorem. With negatives of the currents used as first isotropic basis, a far tuple x has known three-vector s_x equal to its sum of the three current coordinates on the D3 triple (a simultaneous basis change below is harmless). Since characteristic is odd a complementary isotropic basis dual to these exists: start with any dual basis, subtract half its Gram in the isotropic coordinates. Consequently the residual pairings are s_x*t_y+s_y*t_x for as-yet unknown t_x in F7^3. Every actual far point has norm zero, i.e. 2s_x*t_x=−P_xx. Adding a common skew-linear function of s_x to all t_x preserves all pairings.

**Calibrate two fixed caps.** Every cap has nonzero s, since its inherited B edge label has nonzero current sum: the six possible cross edges without a near cap have sums 1,1,2,5,6,6 modulo 7. Loop over each allowable pair of cap six-tuples; discard if their known common-neighbor count exceeds two or if any remaining m_iv is negative. Swapping the prism's two triangle lines simultaneously preserves the entire template, so the first cap's prism contact can be required to be even (one site per named wing). For the first chart at i=19 there are 94×100=9400 initial cap assignments before this twofold reduction. There is no assumption about the automorphisms of a completed graph.

If s_a,s_b are independent, complete them to a basis of signature rows so they become (1,0,0),(0,1,0) in the new coordinates. Write d_a=−P_aa/2,d_b=−P_bb/2,r=4−P_ab. The simultaneous skew gauge lets one put t_a=(d_a,0,0), t_b=(r,d_b,0); these are the diagonal and fixed nonedge conditions. For each remaining candidate y, the two nonedges to a and b fix t_y0 and t_y1. Its norm fixes t_y2 uniquely if s_y2≠0, otherwise allows all seven values if the equation is consistent and none otherwise. All allowed lifts are retained.

If dependent, in a chosen signature basis s_a=(1,0,0), s_b=(lambda,0,0) with nonzero lambda. Set t_a=(−P_aa/2,0,0) by the skew gauge. The nonedge fixes t_b0 and its diagonal must hold; enumerate all 49 remaining (t_b1,t_b2). For y, the first nonedge fixes t_y0, the second accepts or rejects that candidate, and the norm is a single linear equation in the remaining t_y1,t_y2. Enumerate all solutions: seven if at least one of those two coefficients is nonzero, 49 or zero otherwise. Thus even proportional cap signatures are covered without assuming an isomorphism, generic rank, or a generic orbit.

**Complete only the eight remaining neighbors.** Each allowed lifted candidate y has five non-anchor near contacts and complete hyperbolic representation relative to the caps. Choose eight with the exact remaining m_iv frequencies. Full pairing modulo seven distinguishes adjacency 3 from nonadjacency 4: discard every other value for selected pairs; require overlap of their whole near-contact tuples ≤2 for a nonedge and exactly one (i) for an edge. Disallow a second mate for any chosen vertex and require no unmatched vertex when all quotas are filled. The enumerator picks an unmet site quota with the fewest compatible candidates, and branches on the first selected one in its ordered list. Earlier candidates from that list are then excluded; this covers every set, also for multiplicity two. Different lifts of the same six-tuple can never be simultaneously chosen, since they share six near sites. Eight is forced by 40 required residual contacts, at five per column. Cuts based on deficient candidate counts all only remove impossible states. A per-branch node guard is explicitly inconclusive if exceeded and makes the checker fail rather than purport to exclude that case.

Replay by Python with NumPy only, no solver or floating arithmetic in the mathematical check: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 wing_eighteen_capped_star.py`, or specify one or several zero-based chart indices after the filename. The 39 inputs exactly match the previously frozen and publicly replayable pair-incidence output. Each chart chooses one admissible actual anchor i minimizing the number of cap assignments after the cheap overlap bound; a single failed necessary star is enough for that chart. No adjacency or configuration outside these ten far neighbors is searched.

**Coverage.** All 39 charts failed, with 159,374 cap assignments after normalizing the prism-line swap; 18,605 shared at least three near sites and the remaining 140,769 were checked. Their chosen zero-based anchors 19,23,27 occur in 17,14,8 charts. The complete original exact run took 433.61 seconds on one CPU and had no inconclusive node-limit return. A separate audit checked both hyperbolic gauges and local mate requirements. An independent complete replay of the published verifier passed in about 402 seconds on one CPU and reproduced the exact assignment and anchor counts. The 39 input vectors matched the prior verifier’s actual output exactly. The final verifier additionally rejects any already-negative pair quota up front; this only discards impossible cap assignments. It performs the exact mathematical tests anew and does not trust stored status or a timeout.
