# Four prism components have eight independent five-flow marks

In the [saturated five-flow construction](saturated-five-currents.md), the images of the two-dimensional flow spaces of any **four** distinct prism components are independent over F5. More generally the four-component claim only needs the selected prism components to exist in the cubic wing, since all calculations take place on their contacts. No rank or restriction on the remaining twelve vertices is assumed. With six prism components this gives block distance ≥5 for the invisible code in six two-dimensional blocks, hence dimension at most four (project to any two blocks).

A nonzero prism flow has signed values ±a,±b,±c with a+b+c=0, assigned to its three physical wings. Each far word has exactly one site in the prism. For two prisms and a specified choice of one site in each, the number of far words containing this choice is exactly one for sites in the same physical wing and two otherwise. This fixes the entire histogram H_ab of the sum of any two local flow values. It is symmetric under negation because all six sign pairs occur under simultaneous sign reversal with the same multiplicities. As already shown for up to three prisms, pair coverage and the Cauchy–Davenport size-three threshold rule out shorter relations.

For a hypothetical relation of support four with labels a,b,c,d, the histograms at all three cuts into pairs must agree: H_ab=H_cd, H_ac=H_bd, H_ad=H_bc. Generate four multisets of nonzero triples (entries ordered by wing), allowing independent sign reversals of the prism, exchanging the four prisms, a common permutation of the three wings, and a common nonzero scalar. There are 142 such orbits. The elementary histogram loop leaves five. The necessary flow norm congruence Σ h_i²≡0 mod5 leaves just
`(0,1,−1), (1,1,−2), (1,1,−2), (1,1,−2)`.
Here for a mod-five invisible current the real norm identity `||B h||²=10||h||²` on integral lifts forces 5| ||h||²; the factor two for paired darts can be dropped modulo five. This identity continues to hold for flows supported on prism components even if the other components are not saturated.

In the final orbit take the specific third-wing site with value −1 from the first prism and the site with value +2 in the same wing from the second. Exactly one far word contains both. Its remaining prism contacts cannot lie in that already-filled wing, because each far word has exactly two contacts per physical wing. Each of the last two values is therefore ±1, so the total 1±1±1 cannot be zero modulo five. This contradiction finishes the four-prism case. The [standard-library enumeration](four_prism_five_flows.py) checks the five and the final orbit without optimization, graph search, or assumed global automorphisms.
