# A B12 wing cannot have a flat 24-site companion at rank 36

Fix the 12+24 decomposition of the 36 wing sites of a triangle at mod-seven Seidel rank 36. Suppose the 12-component is type B of tree number 7140 and the 24-component has seven-unit tree number. Then its cross-return permutation on the eight triples of cross-matching labels is **not identity**: in particular, it does not contain eight transversal triangles. Equivalently, the connected component is not the triangle expansion of a cubic, properly three-edge-coloured eight-vertex multigraph. No restriction on its three mate matchings and no graph-level automorphism is assumed. A singular larger tree, other 12-components, or fewer than eight transversal triangles are not ruled out by this theorem.

Use [triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md](triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md) for the B12 representative and that the larger tree must be nonsquare mod seven. Use the [one-visible-current contact theorem](one-current-two-components.md) for the nondegenerate principal 34-row wing Gram and a split residual plane. Here are the needed facts independently of the 46-word reconstruction: in \(M=3I-A+J/9\) mod 7, the wing Gram is \(G=3I-D+4J\). Fix a rank-34 principal basis \(I\) of this Gram. The centered B12 current is
\[
 (2,1,5,6;0,0,0,0;5,6,2,1).
\]
The ten pairs of B12 sites with current sum zero and positive prescribed contact multiplicity are the isolated edges \(02,8\!-\!10\) and the edges of cycles \((1,3,11,9)\) and \((4,5,6,7)\); every one has multiplicity exactly one. These facts follow immediately by counting the two sites' shared neighbors in the triangle and wings. Since other contact pair types necessarily have nonzero current, the current represents a nonzero isotropic vector in a residual nondegenerate plane. The residual of each of these **ten actual zero-current words is proportional to that vector,** so all their residual mutual products including squares vanish. If \(w\) is a prospective full contact indicator, with two contacts in each wing and two in B12 (so four in the 24-component), put \(p_w=(4\mathbf1-w)|_I\) and \(g(w,v)=p_w^tG_{II}^{-1}p_v\) over \(\mathbf F_7\). Therefore \(g(w,w)=0\), and the adjacency of two different mandatory zero words is determined by their contacts alone: \(a=4-g(w,v)\) must be 0 or 1. The 50 remaining words never enter this test.

**Exact bounded inventory.** When the cross-return permutation is identity, number the eight transversal triangles \(0,\dots,7\). Each wing's internal mate edges form a perfect matching on these eight indices. First normalize the first matching to \(01,23,45,67\), then enumerate all \(105^2\) ordered choices of the other two, identifying only simultaneous relabelings by the size-384 stabilizer of the first matching. Do not quotient permutations of the three wings: their orientation relative to the named B12 component is retained. This leaves 86 representatives (including disconnected ones). The matrix-tree cofactor mod seven has counts \(47,3,6,14,0,4,12\) at residues \(0,1,2,3,4,5,6\); the 30 representatives in residues 3,5,6 are connected and are the only cases to test.

**Finite ten-part contradiction.** Enumerate, for each of the ten B12 pairs \(e\), every four-subset of the 24 sites completing \(e\) to two contacts per wing. A near pair \(i,j\) in such a word needs positive capacity
\(2-D_{ij}-(D^2)_{ij}-[\text{same wing}]\).
Each near site \(i\) must also satisfy \(w_i+(Dw)_i\le2\) by its common-neighbor quota with the far site. Keep words passing these tests and the zero square above. Two words of different classes may coexist only if:
* \(a=4-g(w,v)\) is 0 or 1, \(|w\cap v|\le2-a\), and no one wing supplies two common contacts (a same-wing near pair already shares its triangle parent).
* They share no near pair of capacity one.
* If \(a=1\), the remaining near-site quota \(2-w_i-(Dw)_i\) of \(w\) must be positive at every contact \(i\) of \(v\), and conversely. The second far site would otherwise be an uncompensated common neighbor.

A transversal clique with one member of each of the ten classes is necessary. The checker generates all the data exactly, repeatedly branches over the smallest remaining class, and intersects other classes with the selected word's allowed partners. All 30 search trees are empty of transversals, the largest visits 173 nodes. There is no random selection, floating point, arbitrary solver, or scan of possible full exterior graphs. Full replay (Python 3 + NumPy) takes about eight seconds: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 b12_flat24_zero_words.py`. The independently reproduced total is 1,251 DFS nodes.

**Further exclusion.** The [cross-return transposition proof](b12-six-transversal.md) also rules out exactly six transversal triangles. Thus this component has at most five in any remaining case.
