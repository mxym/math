# Exact contact counts for two Conway wing cuts

The [forced 12+12+12 split](thin-cut-six-middle.md) gives two disjoint outer shores and a twelve-site middle when two thin cuts occur at one triangle. The following additional counts are exact but do not rule out that remaining configuration.

The [canonical 24-site signed core](two-fano-core.md) of two orthogonal Fanos gives all 75 exterior vertices a fixed contact-degree census into the core. There are 30 with just one contacted Fano (the two disjoint 15-site support-free sets), all with two distinct signed neighbors. Among the 45 contacting both, exactly two see two shared signed sites: these are the distinct unique outside caps of the two complementary-paired signed core edges 03 and 12. Each of the four shared signed sites has seven neighbors in the core and therefore seven outside. Thus the 28 total outside contact incidences to shared signed sites give two outside vertices with two such contacts, 24 with one, and 19 of the doubly contacted sites with none. Consequently the exterior core-neighbor degree multiplicities are exactly 32,24,19 at degrees 2,3,4.

A balanced k-per-wing positive shore U of a single cut contains exactly k/2−1 sites in Z_f, all in the two transmitting physical wings. Indeed, there are two signed positive sites in U, with four distinct neighbors still in U, necessarily zero-valued and contacted. The five far positives each have k/2−1 neighbors in U, all of them remaining zero-valued sites, disjoint by the same-shore common-neighbor constraint. Every other U site is support-free: there are no positive sites outside U in D, and none on T. The nontransmitting root of T itself belongs to Z_f and Z_f is independent (every such root has fourteen contacted neighbors by (A²1_{P_f})_z=14), so no U site in its physical wing can be support-free.

When both disjoint shores have size 12, U is completely g-zero. Summing U contacts over the 14 support sites of g gives 20 incidences: the far contact formula 2−f_x sums formally to 28 on that support, from which the four selected W sites remove 8, with one extra removed exactly when the common negative site is among them. In that last case the selected common negative endpoint has one actual U contact through the f-cut, restoring the missing incidence. Thus U has two sites in Z_g, as ten of its twelve sites see one of each g sign. Symmetrically V has two sites in Z_f. The entire wing has exactly four sites in each Z_f and Z_g: among its 32 zero-valued sites for a fixed field, 24 see a far positive (five far positives with six wing neighbors, minus their six incidences to the two negative wing endpoints), and four others see a wing positive (both positive endpoints contribute two distinct zero wing neighbors each). In the size-12 middle case there is consequently exactly one site of Z_f and one of Z_g in the middle. They are distinct, but this alone does not exclude the middle configuration.
