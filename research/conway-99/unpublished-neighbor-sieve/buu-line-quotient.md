# A B12 cannot accompany two unit twelve-components at rank 36

At mod-seven Seidel rank 36 **no triangle has twelve-components B+U+U'**, where B is the 7140-tree type and U,U' are any two among the eleven previously surviving seven-unit twelve-types. This excludes all \(11\cdot12/2=66\) unordered multisets of this shape, including repeated types. These are disjoint from the old AUU/ABU/BBU/AAU/AAB/AAA cuts and the subsequent BBB and BBU cuts. This is not a result about the unit-only 12+12+12 split.

Use the [catalogued 12-types and discriminants](triangle-wing-twelve-currents.md). Each component has the three internal mate matchings and three cross perfect matchings as in [the literal inventory](twelve_sites.py). In \(M=3I-A+4J\bmod7\), the 36 wing marks have a rank-33 Gram: one constant direction, one centered rank-10 B quotient and two centered rank-11 unit blocks. Choose a nonsingular principal 33-row Gram \(G_I\). Its orthogonal complement \(V\) in the full marking space has dimension three. The actual B current \(u\ne0\) is isotropic in \(V\) and evaluates by minus the sum of \(h\) over the two B contacts, for \(h=(2,1,5,6;0,0,0,0;5,6,2,1)\). Splitting its hyperbolic plane leaves a nondegenerate line. The quadratic character of that line is **the character of \(\det G_I\)**: full rank-36 determinant and the hyperbolic plane are both nonsquare, hence cancel in the quotient.

There are ten mandatory far vertices whose pair in B has \(h\)-sum zero, each with a different prescribed B pair tag of multiplicity one. Their residual products are exactly those of the nonsingular line \(u^\perp/\langle u\rangle\). Write \(\epsilon=1\) for a square line and \(\epsilon=3\) for a nonsquare line. For a candidate contact word \(z\), let \(p_z=(4\mathbf1-w_z)|_I\), \(g_{zz'}=p_z^tG_I^{-1}p_{z'}\). Enumerate all \(t\in\mathbf F_7\) with \(\epsilon t^2=-g_{zz}\). Its adjacency to another lifted candidate is forced to be \(4-g_{zz'}-\epsilon tt'\in\{0,1\}\). One actual lifted word is necessary in each of the ten classes simultaneously. Impose the same literal near-pair and common-neighbor capacities as in the [three-B zero-section proof](three-b12-zero-section.md): no shared near pair with capacity one, at most \(2-A\) shared near sites, and for adjacent words the other word's contact sites must all have positive remaining common-neighbor capacity with the first word. A ten-partite clique is necessary; no assumption about the fifty other vertices is made.

**Exact coverage.** Keep the physical wings distinguished relative to B. Generate the [eleven wing-unordered unit templates](twelve_sites.py), then all six physical assignments of each. In a fixed physical orientation every wing-preserving relabeling is determined by the permutation of the four first-wing sites: the next two site orders are read from the consecutive cross matchings. Minimizing the adjacency code over these 24 orders leaves **25** distinct ordered unit templates. It suffices to take all \(25\cdot26/2=325\) unordered pairs of these ordered templates since the two unit components can be exchanged. Repeated components and all relative orientations are covered without assuming their relabelings extend to graph automorphisms. For each such pair the verifier regenerates every allowed six-contact word, retaining two sites per component and two per wing. All 325 lifted ten-class clique trees collapse; the largest visits 62,777 nodes. The [standalone replay](buu_line_quotient.py) uses Python and NumPy only and takes about 2.5 minutes.
