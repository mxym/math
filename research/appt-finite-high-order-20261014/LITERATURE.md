# Literature boundary for the finite-power continuation

Checked on 14 October 2026 before the manuscript was integrated. Links below
identify primary sources and versions. The reading scope is stated explicitly;
this is not a certificate that no other author has obtained the same result.

| Primary source | Actual reading and role |
| --- | --- |
| [Tran 2609.18568v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18568v1), Spectral Optimization for Absolutely PPT States: Purity, Entropy, and Volume Decay | Read the introduction and the full Lemma 2.1/Theorem 2.2 proofs in Section 2, and the finite von Neumann entropy statements and comparisons in Section 3. Its vertex list, purity optimum and comparisons are credited inherited results. The displayed α=2 case here reproduces that result. The new manuscript's continuous real-power comparison is written out independently. A text search for Rényi does not establish priority. |
| [Johnston 1309.2006, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/1309.2006) | Verified the actual theorem's full qubit–qudit range and its stated three-way equivalence. Read the main argument and supporting discussion; its complete external proof and every earlier lemma were not re-audited as a new formal certificate. Used as a published ordinary mathematical input only to pass from actual qubit APPT to ASEP. No general-dimensional equivalence is inferred. |
| [Vidal–Tarrach quant-ph/9806094](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9806094), Robustness of entanglement | Read Section 4.B equation (41) and Appendix B.1 through the explicit separable phase mixture. The white-noise pole is a known consequence of their pure-state robustness. The finite independent cubic-phase version in POLE_SEPARABILITY supplies a self-contained endpoint check, not a new threshold claim. The rest of this external paper was not completely re-reviewed. |
| [Song–Chen 2409.14347v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.14347v1) and [published record](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/7pnp-qgrq) | Read the actual arXiv v1 qubit extreme-point statements (Theorems 9–10 and Corollary 11), and the published abstract/metadata. The arXiv and published versions need not have identical corollary numbering or scope. The current published full PDF was inaccessible through the browsing tool, so it is not claimed read. This paper is background, not a required new proof input. |

Focused primary-source searches included APPT/absolute-separability with
Rényi, power extrema, qubit–qudit entropy and Song–Chen extreme points.
Search results with no matching primary theorem are not treated as proofs.
A secondary index mislabeled arXiv 2609.18570 with the APPT optimization
title; the actual primary paper has a different subject and was excluded.
The correct optimization identifier used here is 2609.18568.

The known extremal eigenvalue cap, physical rank-two condition, singular
spectrum and pole are not newly counted discoveries. This continuation
proves the stated real-power envelope, actual equality classifications and
finite high-order guarantees. It does not claim the general finite APPT
minimum, a sharp transition constant, another solved major conjecture,
verified historical priority, complete Lean, or external peer review.
