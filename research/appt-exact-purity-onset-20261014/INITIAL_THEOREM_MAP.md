# Whole target: the minimal eventual exact purity onset

14 October 2026. Proposed ordinary manuscript; not independently reviewed yet.
This map is saved after exploratory scalar/algebra calculations and before the
new full manuscript. It is not represented as predating those calculations.

Let P_max(m,n) be purity maximized over actual complex density matrices that
remain PPT after EVERY global unitary, 3<=m<=n. Put t=2/(m-1), D=mn, and
P_G(m,n)=max_(integer 0<=k<=D) Tr[((I+t Pi_k)/(D+t k))^2]. Define
N_2(m)=min{integer N>=m: EVERY integer n>=N has P_max(m,n)=P_G(m,n)}.

**Q0, whole minimal onset.** There exists M such that, for EVERY integer
m>=M, N_2(m)=ceil((m^2+5m+7)/4). Establish all larger n and an actual
all-unitary APPT state beating P_G at n=N_2(m)-1; a trial family alone is
insufficient. This includes the linear and constant onset terms and
integer locking. No claim for every small m or an effective M.

**Q1, all optimizing matrices.** For those m and all n>=N_2(m), every
optimizing matrix is (I+t Pi)/(D+t k), k=ceil((m-1)n/2), with Pi any
rank-k orthogonal projection. Give the exact parity-dependent purity.

**Q2, complete critical upper.** Use the reviewed full physical C0-C4/E0-E4
inputs to localize every possibly profitable actual spectrum, exclude the
empty critical branch by a relative bound, and retain ALL omitted anomaly
mass in an exact finite witness. Prove strict cost domination of every
remaining head before the scalar upper reduction; no finite-head ansatz.

**Q3, actual lower and integer rank.** Derive a rational boundary-PSD
one-head family, optimize its ACTUAL trace with integer endpoint rank, and
control the rounding loss. The continuous body maximum is an upper bound,
not a claimed physical state. Preserve the explicit small-m counterexample
to extending Q0 to all dimensions.

Credited inputs remain immutable. This is a new purity-only ordinary result
in the APPT direction, not the complete entropy-axis problem, an ASEP result,
a newly solved major conjecture, a Lean theorem, or an expanded old audit.
Publication/promotion requires a separate complete independent review.
