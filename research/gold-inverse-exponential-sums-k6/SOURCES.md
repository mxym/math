# Primary-source map

1. Aina Johansen, Tor Helleseth and Alexander Kholosha, [Further Results on m-Sequences With Five-Valued Cross Correlation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2009.2032854), *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 55(12), 5792–5802 (2009). Original attribution for the Gold-inverse sum conjecture; see the explicit restatement and broader gcd formulation in the next source.
2. Omran Ahmadi, Gary McGuire and Antonio Rojas-León, [Decomposing Jacobians of curves over finite fields in the absence of algebraic structure](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2015.04.014), *Journal of Number Theory* 156, 414–431 (2015); [arXiv:1409.8510](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.8510) and the [author-hosted manuscript](https://personal.us.es/arojas/files/Jacobian-Decomp.pdf). Sections 4–5 give the same Artin–Schreier family, its genus, Conjecture 12 on the prime-factor \(L\)-polynomial form, and Conjecture 17 with its more general gcd formulation immediately after. Corollary 20 lists the prior Magma computations for \(k\le5\). The three comparison numerators \(L_1,L_2,L_3\) are independently regenerated here, not claimed new. The displayed point formula after Conjecture 17 in the accessible author manuscript §5 has a minus sign; the actual formula is \(2^n+1+G_n^{(k)}\), as the two-to-one trace count and their own \(L_1\) show. The typo does not change equality of two point counts.
3. Xiaogang Liu, Michael Harrison and Yuan Luo, [A note on the five valued conjectures of Johansen and Helleseth and zeta functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.5674v3), especially the \(k=3\) calculations. These precede the higher-index comparison here.
4. Claude Carlet and Darrion Thornburgh, [Resolving a conjecture on quadratic APN functions and a new quadratic (n,n)-function associated to crooked functions](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23888v2), 7 September 2026. Conjecture 5.19 is the gcd-dependence assertion in this note. Proposition 5.20 and Corollary 5.21 give congruences, not the new exact \(k=6\) equality.
5. Henning Stichtenoth, *Algebraic Function Fields and Codes*, 2nd ed., Springer, 2009, Proposition 3.7.8 for the standard Artin–Schreier ramification formula used in the genus calculation. For the curve zeta numerator and its standard normalization, see also the setup in Ahmadi–McGuire–Rojas-León, §1.

**Prior-art boundary.** On 27 November 2026 we checked the original publication record, the explicit full gcd conjecture as restated in the author manuscripts, and the published/author-hosted forms of the related work, and searched the author names with “k=6”, “Gold”, “inverse”, “\(L\)-polynomial”, “gcd”, “1409.8510”, “2608.23888” and distinctive numerator coefficients. No primary source with the \(k=6\) numerator or the full \(k=6\) gcd assertion was located. This is not an absolute priority claim. Two related nearby papers, [Divisibility of L-Polynomials for a Family of Curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.04189) and [On the existence of a morphism between certain Artin-Schreier curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15717), concern different curve families and are not prior evidence for this \(k=6\) case.
