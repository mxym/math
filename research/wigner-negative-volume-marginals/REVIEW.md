# Adversarial review: the imaginary part / marginal negative volumes

**Scope:** Independent internal AI review found no error in the $m=64$ argument. This is not external peer review or priority certification. The review does not rely on numerical quadrature.

**Original question.** [Cerf–Hertz–Van Herstraeten, §IV.1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.19296v2) use argument in $(-\pi,\pi]$, explicitly choosing $+\pi$ on negative values. Thus $\Im h_c(W)=\pi\int(-W)_+$ with $0\log0=0$; no minus sign appears. Immediately following (41) they distinguish the *proved* product-state identity from their unproved numerical observation for an arbitrary two-mode joint Wigner function. No purity or entanglement assumption there. This review concerns the latter, not a counterexample to Property 4. Choosing the other boundary value would reverse the entropy sign; the integrated negative volume is unambiguous.

**Oscillator and measure conventions.** Independently sum number-state Wigner symbols using Mehler's formula. For real $0<z<1$ the Hermite-wavefunction Mehler kernel evaluated at $q-y,q+y$ has prefactor $[\pi(1-z^2)]^{-1/2}$ and exponent $-[(1-z)/(1+z)]q^2-[(1+z)/(1-z)]y^2$. Its Gaussian Fourier integral in the Wigner convention $\pi^{-1}\int e^{2ipy}(\cdot)dy$ is
$$\sum_{j\ge0}z^j W_j(q,p)=\frac{1}{\pi(1+z)}\exp[-t(1-z)/(1+z)],\qquad t=q^2+p^2.$$
Equivalently it is $e^{-t}/\pi$ times the Laguerre generating function at parameter $-z$, argument $2t$, giving exactly $(-1)^j L_j(2t)$. The vacuum and parity-at-origin signs agree. The radial measure after integrating one angle is $2\pi r\,dr=\pi dt$. For tensor products the two quadrature pairs yield $\pi^2dt\,du$, not one angle for four variables; cancellation of the two vacuum prefactors leaves $e^{-t-u}dt\,du$. Linearity and partial trace are compatible with these product measures. Finally, bounded polynomial times Gaussian symbols have absolutely integrable $W\log|W|$, by domination with a multiple of $\sqrt{|W|}$.

**State positivity.** Whether the formula's two tensor terms are positive separately is irrelevant. In the joint number basis it is diagonal. The minimum diagonal entry of $D$ on the stated support is at least $2^{-64}-a>2a$, and $|X_{ii}|\le1$, so every displayed mass is strictly positive, with other masses zero. Traces and both marginals follow from $\mathrm{Tr}\,X=0$; division by $(1-a)^2$ is required. As an independent finite check I formed a common integer denominator and 4,225 exact integer numerators, and checked every entry, trace, both marginals and nonproductness. A three-term integer recurrence for $j!(-1)^jL_j(2t)$ checked all 65 unit radial integrals and all 65 coefficients of the binomial symbol without importing the manuscript's implementation. See [independent.py](independent.py).

**Signed integration.** Let $\mu=e^{-t}dt$, $I=1_{A<a}$, $K=1_{B<a}$. Since $\int G(t,u)d\mu(u)=(A-a)(1-a)$, each marginal negative volume multiplied by $(1-a)^2$ equals $-\iint I G$ or $-\iint K G$. Subtracting them leaves $J=G_-+(I+K)G$. Consequently the contributions are $G_-$ on $++$, $G_+$ on each mixed rectangle, and $G_-+2G$ on $--$, which is $G$ once $G<0$ there. Sets $A=a$ or $B=a$ are $\mu^2$-null. This rules out both a sign mistake and a double count at a boundary.

1. On $--$, $G\le a^2-4a^2(1-a)^2<-2a^2$. The probability of each coordinate interval $[0,r)$ is at least $r-r^2/2\ge r/2$, so both give a contribution $\le-a^2r^2/2$.
2. On $++$, if only one of $A,B$ exceeds 1 the correction is nonnegative. If both exceed 1, subtraction of $4a^2(A-1)(B-1)$ cannot overcome the first term. Otherwise $G_-\le4a^2$ and $G_- >0$ forces $(x^m-1)(y^m-1)<4$ with $x=t/r,y=u/r\ge1$. The scaled Lebesgue area is $\le2I_m^2$ by the reciprocal-square-root majorant. Independently of the manuscript's inverse-trig integral, set $x=e^{s/m}$:
$$mI_m=\int_0^\infty e^{s/m}(e^s-1)^{-1/2}ds.$$
The range $0<s<1$ costs at most $\int_0^1 2s^{-1/2}\,ds=4$. For $s\ge1$ the integrand is at most $2e^{-\alpha s}$, $\alpha=1/2-1/64$, whose integral is $\le2/[\alpha(1+\alpha)]<3$. Thus $I_{64}<7/64$ and this quadrant contributes at most $(392/64^2)a^2r^2$, strictly smaller than the permitted $a^2r^2/8$. The discarded Gaussian factors are $\le1$ on nonnegative integrands, so the upper bound includes the full unbounded range. The manuscript's original $I_m\le2\pi/m$ is independently valid as well.
3. On $A<a\le B\le1$ both terms in $G$ are nonpositive. For $A<a$ and $B>1$, $G>0$ implies $(a-A)(B-a)<4a^2(1-A)(B-1)\le4a^2(B-a)$. Thus $t>r(1-4a)^{1/m}$; the width is at most $4ar$, and $G_+\le4a^2B$. Bounding $e^{-t}\le1$ and integrating $B$ over the full $\mu$-range ($\int B\,d\mu=1$) gives $16a^3r$. Interchange gives exactly one more such rectangle. Both together are $<a^2r^2/8$, since $a/r<1/256$.
4. Combining and dividing by the strictly positive $(1-a)^2$ gives the negative gap asserted. No fixed observational significance or resolution is claimed: the rigorous deficit witness is small.

**Distinctions and repository check.** This is continuous two-mode signed Wigner *imaginary*-entropy/negative-volume failure. This is not a real-part entropy result. The [Wigner-positive vacuum-entropy work by He, Zenodo 22941099](https://zenodo.org/records/22941099) concerns the real Shannon entropy of nonnegative Wigner densities. I inspected its available PDF text; there the Wigner negative volume is zero. [Tschaffon–Freyberger (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjd/s10053-025-01086-8) compare the global negative volume to the product-of-marginals negative volume; the latter exceeds the sum of marginal negative volumes by $2N_1N_2$, so a below-product example alone need not refute the numerical suggestion. [Kenfack–Życzkowski](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406015) treat conventional Wigner negative volume, not this comparison. The repository scan's two ECQC parity hits concern finite-dimensional complementary measurements, not this phase-space disparity. Since the author's checked main, increments #89 (sparse RRRB) and #90 (trine maximizers) through 865c2cb143830985afbcc08466d5eea4e71f78a3 have been inspected; none is the present claim. No conclusion of priority.
