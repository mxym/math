# Sources, related work and scope (November 2026)

* Exact source: Nicolas J. Cerf, Anaelle Hertz, Zacharie Van Herstraeten, [published 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40509-024-00325-8), [arXiv:2310.19296v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.19296v2), §4.2 after Property 4, Eq. (41). They describe a numerical suggestion for “arbitrary two-mode Wigner functions”; no restriction there to pure states. Their proved statement for product inputs is not affected. Appendix A separately shows (in part numerically) failure of real signed one-mode entropy versus the sum of its position and momentum entropies; that problem and the adjacent real-part two-mode suggestion are not used here.
* Foundational: [Kenfack–Życzkowski](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406015) use integrated Wigner negativity for nonclassicality. [Albarelli et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05763) develop resource monotones, including log Wigner 1-norm, under Gaussian protocols. Tensor-product additivity of the log norm is multiplicativity of the Wigner 1-norm, not the general marginal inequality. Monotonicity under partial trace only gives the maximum of the marginal negative volumes, not the sum.
* Important nearby prior: [M. E. N. Tschaffon and M. Freyberger](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjd/s10053-025-01086-8), EPJD (2025), Eqs. (14)–(15) compare global negative volume to that of the **product** of the marginals. The subtracted quantity exceeds the sum by 2N₁N₂; even a negative value of their difference, hypothetically, would not on its own imply the present strict inequality. Their examples compare correlation negativity with CHSH; no example below the marginal sum was located there. [Taghiabadi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01635) find separable examples with positive marginals and negative global Wigner functions, the opposite direction. [Pizzimenti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00880) examine the complex-valued Wigner non-Gaussianity functional; negative volume as the imaginary part is not new.
* [He](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13312), [Liu–Qiao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23277), and [Van Herstraeten–Cerf–Chabaud](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24670) address the **other** Wigner entropy conjecture, the vacuum lower bound for Wigner-*positive* (hence zero-negative-volume) states. This note makes no new claim about it.

* Explicit external archive audit: Zixuan He, [Zenodo 22941099](https://zenodo.org/records/22941099), dated 24 September 2026, is an expanded version of arXiv:2609.13312v1. Retrieved record metadata and its complete single 33-page PDF, 832,950 bytes, verified deposited MD5 e6a97901d15285a9377a84fc5d1870d9 (SHA-256 297cdef3cc6c06ae1d6bc19f46d7073b8cf863556f5a02765550b9886b810a1f). Its §1, Eq. (2), treats ordinary real Shannon entropy of a nonnegative phase-space probability density against the vacuum; Theorems 1–2 certify positivity and sub-vacuum entropy for coherent two-Fock-level examples. Its many-mode Eq. (16) defines the deficit *from vacuum*, and Eqs. (17)–(18) amplify it using tensor powers of Wigner-positive states. Eq. (19) concerns loss and nonnegative inputs, still with the same real entropy objective. The intermediate negative tail in §3 is repaired to restore positivity. For nonnegative W, its two reduced Wigner functions are nonnegative by integration, hence N(W)=N(W₁)=N(W₂)=0: these counterexamples cannot violate the present marginal-sum inequality. Independently, all product states satisfy the exact CHVH product law. This archive establishes relevant prior work, but not a counterexample to the arbitrary-correlated-state negative-volume proposal.
