# Sources and exact scope

- Robin Kothari, Matt Kovacs-Deak, Daochen Wang and Rain Zimin Yang, [*Rational degree is polynomially related to degree*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08727), [arXiv:2601.08727v3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.08727v3), 28 September 2026. Section 5.1, Theorem 10 / Conjecture 1 / Equations (60)–(63) discuss the reduced product certificate, not bounds on the h_i by themselves. The theorem treats two forms under product-zero and no-common-zero. Their Fact 7 / Equations (66)–(71) give the separator lower bound for two affine forms if the product-zero condition is dropped. This paper's unrestricted conjecture has already been refuted by Rajagopalan–Saurabh; we investigate the exact affine-form threshold.
- Sudharshan Rajagopalan and Nitin Saurabh, [*On Non-Deterministic Representation of Boolean Functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11966), [arXiv:2611.11966v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11966v1), 9 November 2026, FSTTCS 2026 preprint. Section 4 and §4.1: Theorem 6 gives three quadratics with unbounded degree; Theorem 7 excludes counterexamples for three affine forms; Proposition 26 gives five affine forms with degree≥n and explicitly refers to the three-form case rather than treating four. Their Proposition 25 imports Fact 7 above. We credit their zero-density argument (using the product of remaining forms) and their use of Littlewood–Offord in the three-form finite-variable bound. Our additions are the four-form case via an arbitrary minimal cover plus remaining-fiber assignment, its 80-degree explicit bound, and matching degree-n upper certificates for the already published examples.
- Paul Erdős, [*On a lemma of Littlewood and Offord*](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1945-04.pdf), Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 51 (1945), 898–902. For exact equality constraints on affine real forms, the needed central-binomial antichain bound follows by changing coefficient signs and counting maximal chains, as in Section 2; no probabilistic asymptotic or field-specific estimate is needed.

A bounded duplicate search (2026-11-27) used both paper titles and arXiv IDs with “effective hypercube Nullstellensatz”, “four affine”, “four linear”, and “Proposition 26”; no matching four-form completion was found in the primary works examined. This is not a priority claim. The original three-quadratic and five-linear families and the separator lower bound are not new. The upper bound 80 is not claimed sharp. The results are for real polynomials and products reduced on the Boolean cube, not for ordinary polynomial ideal membership off the cube.
