# Source and novelty-scope check

Checked during this work (October 8, 2026, author-local date). The repository worktree is named with the October 9 UTC date. Searches were repeated using two search engines, including exact combinations of ECQC, stabilizer, full-rank equality, same-basis mutually unbiased measurements, and Holevo equality.

## Primary sources actually used

- **Hasan Iqbal**, *On the CQC conjecture: a sufficient condition and an extension*, arXiv:2509.08286v2, Conjecture 3.1 / equation (6), and Section 5. Publisher: Quantum Information Processing 25, 250 (2026), DOI `10.1007/s11128-026-05258-2`. The identical-local-basis convention, the ECQC deletion of one setting, and the separately posed pure-state question were checked in the source, rather than inferred from its title. [Version](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.08286v2).
- **Jinbo Wang, Qihang Wang, Kun Chen**, *When Complementary Measurements Count the Same Classical Bit Twice: Counterexamples to CQC, ECQC, and Complementarity-Based Certification*, arXiv:2608.03828v2. This already refutes unrestricted ECQC with mixed classical–classical states; that disproof is not new in the present work. [Version](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03828v2).
- **Yongxian Zhang**, *The pure-state ECQC conjecture: counterexamples and a prime-dimensional classification*, `mxym/math`, frozen commit `2d67375906d729862d4eb085186a5fd5670ef390`, directory `research/ecqc-pure-state-counterexamples`. Its existing all-odd-prime pure counterexamples and sharp `p=3,5` ratios were treated as prior results, not reannounced as new. It explicitly leaves larger-prime sharp ratios and equality states unresolved.
- **Mary Beth Ruskai**, *Inequalities for Quantum Entropy: A Review with Conditions for Equality*, arXiv:quant-ph/0205064v2, Sections 7.2–7.3; J. Math. Phys. 43, 4358–4375 (2002), DOI `10.1063/1.1497701`, erratum 46, 019901 (2005). The needed standard implication is that attainment of accessible-information Holevo equality requires commuting ensemble density operators. The arXiv abstract states this explicitly; the full section and support discussion were checked. The paper does not invoke more delicate equality formulas for unrelated monotonicity inequalities. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0205064).
- **David Fattal, Toby S. Cubitt, Yoshihisa Yamamoto, Sergey Bravyi, Isaac L. Chuang**, *Entanglement in the stabilizer formalism*, arXiv:quant-ph/0406168. Cited as background for standard stabilizer methods. The exact projector, graph, and Born formulas needed here are rederived in the manuscript. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406168).
- **James Schneeloch, Curtis J. Broadbent, John C. Howell**, *Uncertainty relation for mutual information*, Phys. Rev. A 90, 062119 (2014), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.90.062119`. The original two-basis CQC statement and its pure-state result are not contradicted by adding more than two measurement terms.

## What the search supports

The checked versions do not contain the present full-rank equality classification, the exact all-pure-stabilizer ECQC formula, or the general flat-spectrum/rank-gap theorem. Our claims of contribution are restricted to these explicit mathematical statements and their comparison with the checked sources. The underlying facts about Weyl operators, finite-field projective lines, MUB decompositions, and Holevo equality are established tools, not claimed as new discoveries.

## What the search cannot establish

A finite search cannot prove that no other public proof exists, establish historical priority, or certify that all relevant recent papers were indexed. Acronym-only ECQC searches also returned unrelated material and were not treated as evidence about quantum information. No "world first" or broad originality guarantee is made. The manuscript can be corrected in a new version if a more direct prior result is located; frozen versions are retained.
