# Scope and source notes

1. N. Fernando and B. Kousik, *A further study of polynomial \(g_{n,q}\) over finite fields*, [arXiv:2606.01037v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01037v1), 31 May 2026, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01037). Equation (1.1) defines \(g_{n,q}\) by the translation sum. **Question 6.2**, [PDF p. 17](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.01037v1#page=17), fixes \(q=4,e>2,n=1+q^{e-2}+q^{e-1}+2q^e\) and asks exactly which \(e\) give a permutation. The displayed polynomial equals \(S_{e-1}S_{e-2}+S_e^2\) using the formal sum identity verified in the proof. Their Table 1 already records the positive instance \((n,e)=(149,3)\).
2. N. Fernando and X. Hou, *From \(r\)-linearized polynomial equations to \(r^m\)-linearized polynomial equations*, Finite Fields and Their Applications 37 (2016), 14–27, [DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2015.08.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2015.08.003), [arXiv:1503.03162v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03162), Table 1. The exact positive instance \((149,3;4)\) was already in this table (PDF p. 10), with no cited explanation in that row. We do not claim the positive finite instance as new. The extension exclusion and its trace-sieve proof classify the full domain in Question 6.2.
3. A. Weil, [*On Some Exponential Sums*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.34.5.204), Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 34 (1948), 204–207. We use the standard complete additive Weil bound \(\lvert\sum_{x\in K}\psi(P(x))\rvert\le(\deg P-1)\sqrt{|K|}\) when \(\psi\) is nontrivial and the degree is prime to the characteristic. In the application the degree is 9 or 13 in characteristic two. Equivalently one may apply the Hasse–Weil estimate to the Artin–Schreier curve \(Y^2+Y=P(X)\), whose odd degree gives genus \((\deg P-1)/2\) and one point at infinity over \(K\).

On 2026-11-27, the original version and recent follow-up search were checked by title, exact question number, DOI/arXiv ID, \(g_{149,4}\), the trace polynomial and “permutation.” No matching complete classification was located in that bounded search. The proof uses no external code or data. This package treats Question 6.2 only, not the paper's other questions or multivariate conjecture.
