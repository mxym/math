# Sources and scope

1. Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang, [*Maximal input entropy for binary two-deletion and two-insertion channels*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01064v1) (2026), [version history and ancillary materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01064). Lemma 2.1 gives the mask posterior. Their Theorem 2.2 settles the distinct radius-two extremal problems; §4 already groups masks for insertion. Their Theorem 5.1 gives every alternating-host histogram at arbitrary insertion radius. Corollary 5.2 specializes it to the maximum *candidate value* at radius three. Proposition 6.1 certifies the three-insertion maximizers through received length 18, including all exceptions below length seven. The target here is only the **second** assertion of their Conjecture 6.2: a global upper bound and equality characterization for every received length at least seven. Their Lean companion is not a formalization of this new comparison.
2. Shubhransh Singhvi, Omer Sabary, Daniella Bar-Lev and Eitan Yaakobi, [*Conditional Entropies of k-Deletion/Insertion Channels*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3581849), [open manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.10026v2). Lemma 3 gives the insertion posterior via embedding numbers; Theorem 7 characterizes the one-insertion maximizers; §VII-A poses the radius-two optimization. The [Ma–Zhang linked dossier](https://www.trureturing.org/research/singhvi-sabary-barlev-yaakobi-2024-deletion-channel-max-input-entropy/) addresses those two-error questions. A maximum for two insertions or the complete histogram for one particular host does not make the comparison across all hosts at three insertions.
3. Atashpendar et al., [*From Clustering Supersequences to Entropy Minimizing Subsequences for Single and Double Deletions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00703), and their [fixed-observation hidden-word-statistics analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11609), consider the posterior on **supersequences**. Conditional Shannon uncertainty under that different channel, in particular the three-deletion half of Ma–Zhang's conjecture, is not resolved here. This note concerns neither capacity maximization nor the number of distinct subsequences without their multiplicities.
4. Repository antecedents: the [constant-output majorization note](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/c07483ed7729e5efaa0a97e89c08661a91c3a56e/research/deletion-posterior-majorization) treats deletion posteriors. The subsequent [fixed-radius no-least note](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/ef804354fe8b6059ee0a8d403aa7642c49520018/research/deletion-posterior-no-least) compares *supersequence* posteriors under majorization and Rényi entropies; it does not determine a three-insertion Shannon maximizer. The [single-deletion incidence symmetries project](https://github.com/mxym/math/tree/c07483ed7729e5efaa0a97e89c08661a91c3a56e/research/single-deletion-incidence-symmetries) concerns permutations preserving incidence, not output-wise entropy.

Primary literature and linked ancillary project pages were searched for subsequent proofs of the named three-insertion assertion, for extremal output entropy under three exact erasures, and for alternate formulations involving subsequence embedding multiplicity. The [original arXiv submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01064) displayed v1. No previous all-length comparison for the stated insertion problem was identified in the sources checked. Search limitations preclude any global novelty or priority conclusion.
