# Scope and sources

- The precise finite question is [Marckert and Rahmani, *Around Sylvester’s question in the plane*](https://doi.org/10.1112/mtk.12110), Mathematika 67 (2021), pp. 860–884, §1.1, Conjecture 1. Its inequalities compare all planar convex sampling bodies with a triangle and a disk for every sample size n≥6. Theorem 1 settles n=5 with the two equality classes; the classical n=4 extremizers are due to Blaschke. The 2015 [Marckert preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03658) and the 2021 paper introduce the parallel-comb conditioning used here. This manuscript supplies an upper proof at n=6 only.
- [Marckert, *The probability that n random points in a disk are in convex position*](https://doi.org/10.1214/16-BJPS315), Brazilian J. Probab. Stat. 31 (2017), 320–337; [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.3512), eq. (7). The six-point disk probability is already evaluated there. The later [author-hosted manuscript](https://www.labri.fr/perso/marckert/Conv_Points_Disk.pdf) writes its complement as 305/(24π²) − 473473/(11520π⁴); this is identical to the expression in the theorem. The new comparison is with all convex bodies, not a new evaluation.
- The [six-point triangle minimum](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/194) is a prior, complementary result of this repository. Its polynomial evaluator is reused unchanged and credited. The upper proof adds a sign-orthant reduction and binary-matrix certificate; it is not a consequence of the lower proof.
- [Morin, regular polygons (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1017/apr.2024.63), introduction, states the planar extremal conjecture; [general polygons (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11706) derives large-n asymptotics. [Marckert and Morin (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08456) treat a fixed-floor event involving the floor; their [bi-pointed case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26330) studies conditional hulls/large-sample limits. Neither is the fixed-six uniform-in-body extremum. In particular, the d+2 model at d=2 has four points.
- [Campi–Colesanti–Gronchi (1999)](https://www.openstarts.units.it/items/ac389773-c662-480b-986a-f7e12e2a9494) study moments of the volume of random hulls; [Paouris–Pivovarov (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2012.03.019) extend random-polytope isoperimetric comparisons. A hull-volume law and the event that all sampled points are hull vertices are distinct. No previously published general six-point upper proof was located in the targeted search; that does not guarantee priority.

[Valtr (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01271274) evaluated the triangle probability for all sample sizes; this gives 91/900 at six. The unrestricted minimization over sampling bodies was the separate result of [#194, pinned manuscript](https://github.com/mxym/math/blob/59c823212883e1b26bfda23c34627a1d718d0c0b/research/sylvester-six-point-triangle-minimum/LOWER.md). The [2026 refinement via combinatorial types](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-026-00820-2) treats samples of size d+2, not an unrestricted planar six-point comparison.

No previous unrestricted six-point upper comparison was located. This is not a priority determination.
