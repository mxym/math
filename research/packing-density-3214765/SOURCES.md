# Sources and scope

* P. A. Hästö, [*The packing density of other layered permutations*](https://doi.org/10.37236/1673), EJC 9(2) (2002), R1, [full text](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v9i2r1/pdf/). Theorem 1.3 gives four-layer attainability for every $[k,1,k]$ with $k\ge3$; Lemma 4.1 gives the endpoint-to-neighbor inequalities. The proof of Theorem 4.3 writes out the four-layer polynomial, bounds endpoint masses and gives quantitative packing bounds. Immediately before Proposition 4.4, Hästö says symmetry at $k=3$ was not determined and suggests it might hold. Proposition 4.4 already excludes symmetry for $k\ge4$. Our perturbation addresses the omitted case in the four-layer optimization. The polynomial objective and the reduction to it are prior; the exact optimum is the conclusion here.
* M. H. Albert, M. D. Atkinson, C. C. Handley, D. A. Holton and W. Stromquist, [*On packing densities of permutations*](https://doi.org/10.37236/1622), EJC 9 (2002), R5: Stromquist’s layered replacement theorem and the Price-polynomial framework. In §4 they proposed the four symmetric masses for **$[2,1,2]$**, a different pattern. [Sliačan and Stromquist](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/4263) later gave close bounds there, and [this repository’s #207](https://github.com/mxym/math/pull/207) proves their match. Neither the $[2,1,2]$ symmetry nor its packing density transfers to $[3,1,3]$.
* A. Kabela, D. Král’, J. A. Noel and T. Pierron, [*Density maximizers of layered permutations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01723), EJC 29(3) (2022), P3.56, Theorems 4–5. These establish layered boundedness in the symmetric-endpoint setting of interest here. The formerly claimed assertion for general $[a,1,b]$ is not valid without restrictions. Qualitative boundedness, and Hästö’s stronger four-layer existence, are not contributions of this note.
* [D. Warren, PhD thesis](https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/UF/E0/00/98/40/00001/warren_d.pdf), Theorem 3.2.5, restates Hästö’s four-layer result and bounds; Example 4.2.7 mentions 3214765 as an example with an isolated point. This is not the same as evaluating its density exactly. [Bastos and Coregliano](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07312) consider other layered families, including ones with initial strings of singleton layers.
