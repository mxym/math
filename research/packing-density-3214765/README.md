# The asymmetric optimum for the seven-point permutation 3214765

The packing density is approximately **0.12451626237**. [The exact value](THREE.md) is specified by the unique admissible root of a degree-ten polynomial in the squared imbalance between the outer layers, followed by a cubic and a cube root. The two optimal **four-layer** vectors are reverses of each other; neither is symmetric.

Hästö proved in 2002 that four layers suffice for this pattern $[3,1,3]$ and expressly left symmetry at three unresolved. His nonsymmetry theorem starts at $k=4$. Here a short perturbation already rules out equal outer layers at $k=3$; a separate exact elimination gives the density and the unique four-layer masses up to reversal. We do not claim that all maximizing permutons, or all finite optimal permutations, must have this structure.
