# Source comparison and scope

**What is already known.** Ekbatani, Niazadeh, Nuti and Vondrák (ENNV), [STOC 2024](https://doi.org/10.1145/3618260.3649786), [open conference manuscript](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/12758/files/Prophet-Inequalities-with-Cancellation-Costs.pdf) and [first long version §§1.1,4](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00527v1#S1.SS1), introduced prophet inequalities with linear buyback and investigated hard instances for several horizons. They identified the adjacent-offer indifference property and constructed explicit examples. The expanded [EC 2026 version](https://ec26.sigecom.org/program/main-technical-program/), [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00527), identifies the unrestricted optimum for each positive fee and constructs an exactly tight finite **knot family**. These fundamental results, including exact attainability and the recurrence for the offer amplitudes, are ENNV's, not results of this manuscript.

Specifically, ENNV's [v3 Theorem 2.9(iii)](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00527v3#S2.SS3) bounds the number of compositions of their increasing function required to fall below the first normalized knot `c=f/(1+f)`, by `1+1/f`. This is a bound on iterations, not a sharp length formula; their [§3.3 and Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00527v3#S3.SS3) then construct the finite extremizing family by inverse iteration and adjoining the endpoint 1. In their Lemma 3.2 and Eq. (12), active values are determined up to common scale, and the online optimum and expected maximum have exact finite sums. We use those identities rather than an assumption about a guessed policy. Their introductory observation that the required examples become longer at low fees, their explicit exact small fixed-horizon regimes, and their qualitative construction are not credited to the present analysis.

**What the new asymptotics count.** The number and locations here are those of distinct, strictly increasing probability knots in an exactly extremizing ENNV-indifferent instance; the arcsine limit gives equal mass per knot, not probability mass per arriving offer. In particular it covers the ENNV construction without redundant terminal padding, but it does not classify arbitrary worst-case probability laws. The [preceding small-fee coefficient](../prophet-buyback-three-arrivals/SMALL-FEE.md) is a quantitative input to the proof. Our [preceding uniform horizon estimate](../prophet-buyback-horizon-transition/PROOF.md) and [near-attainment criterion](../prophet-buyback-horizon-transition/HORIZON-COST.md) instead quantify when the *loss* is close to the unrestricted one, without requiring exact equality. None of these earlier estimates imply the present exact-instance length or the distribution of its knots.
