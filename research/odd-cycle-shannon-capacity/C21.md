# An explicit size-132636042 independent set in the eighth power of the 21-cycle

**Research draft.** The construction below certifies a lower bound; it makes no claim about optimality or worldwide priority. Write $d_m(a)=\min(a\bmod m,(-a)\bmod m)$. Two words in the strong power $C_m^{\boxtimes h}$ are confusable (equal or adjacent) exactly when every coordinate difference has circular distance at most 1. An independent set of size $N$ gives $\Theta(C_m)\ge N^{1/h}$.

**Claim.** $\alpha(C_{21}^{\boxtimes8})\ge682\cdot21^4=132636042$; therefore $\Theta(C_{21})\ge132636042^{1/8}>10.359354480853559$.

**Outer orbit.** For $t=0,\ldots,681$, take $s(t)=(t,5t,26t,130t)\bmod682$. Every two distinct words differ in a coordinate with circular distance at least 130. Indeed, by reflection we need only check differences $k=1,\ldots,341$, and $k\ge130$ is settled by the first coordinate. With $1\le k\le129$, the condition $d_{682}(5k)<130$ leaves the two intervals $[1,25]\cup[111,129]$. Adding $d_{682}(26k)<130$ leaves exactly
$$
1,2,3,4,22,23,24,25,127,128,129.
$$
In this order the values of $d_{682}(130k)$ are $130,260,292,162,132,262,290,160,142,272,280$, all at least 130.

**Compression.** On integer lifts put $f(x)=\lfloor21x/682\rfloor$, so $f(x+682)=f(x)+21$. If $d_{682}(x-y)\ge130$, choose lifts $x<y<x+682$. Both $y-x$ and $x+682-y$ are at least 130. Since $\lfloor a+b\rfloor-\lfloor a\rfloor\ge\lfloor b\rfloor$, each forward image gap is at least $\lfloor21\cdot130/682\rfloor=4$. Thus $z(t)=f(s(t))\bmod21$, coordinatewise, gives 682 distinct four-words over $\mathbb Z_{21}$, every two separated by circular distance at least 4 in some coordinate.

**Inner fibers.** For $z\in\mathbb Z_{21}$ let $F_z=\{(u,2u+z):u\in\mathbb Z_{21}\}$. Each fiber is independent in $C_{21}^{\boxtimes2}$: if distinct first coordinates differ by $\pm1$, the second coordinates differ by $\pm2$. Otherwise their first coordinates separate them. For confusable pairs $(u,2u+z),(v,2v+z')$, both coordinate differences have representatives in $\{-1,0,1\}$. Subtracting twice the first difference from the second yields $z-z'$ modulo 21; hence $d_{21}(z-z')\le3$. A label gap at least 4 therefore suffices for separation of the entire fibers; a full equivalence is not required.

**Combine.** Replace each outer word by the product of its four size-21 fibers, in eight scalar coordinates. Distinct words in one product are separated in some differing fiber. Distinct products are entirely separated in the outer distinguishing coordinate's fiber. This proves the cardinality, since each product has $21^4$ words and the 682 products are disjoint. The actual code is specified by these formulas; no unstated numerical or structural input is required.

**Sources and scope.** The [July BPZ preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29681) gives a lower result, whereas their [updated Lean source](https://github.com/spectra-research/shannon-capacity-lean/blob/aa21eeb12b75b0413d3fa9fb4208b5d0bf2c4d65/ShannonBounds/CosetC21.lean) gives a size-11466 fourth-power set, i.e. $10.347918137743866\ldots$. Taking its square gives 131469156 eighth-power words; the explicit improvement over that certificate is 1166886 words. Its 26 cosets of a rank-two group already use the same inner-fiber idea up to coordinate ordering. [Polak and Schrijver](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07438) used cyclic orbits in fractional graphs for zero-error capacity; [de Boer, Buys and Zuiddam](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16763v2) discuss floor cohomomorphisms in Lemma 3.2 and cyclic subgroup constructions in Section 6. Their Theorem 6.5, attributing the general two-factor result to Badalyan and Markosyan, gives the exact two-factor packing number 26 for two copies of the fraction graph $E_{21/4}$; our four-factor size 682 exceeds its direct square $26^2=676$. These methods are not new. We did not locate an earlier published C21 rate matching the above during targeted searches, but general symbolic packing bounds or unindexed constructions could imply it. The quantitative historical comparison is confined to the named certificate.

**Verification.** Run `python3 -B -O verify_c21.py`. It uses only standard-library integer arithmetic and needs no solver, network, or input files. Besides checking each of the 681 orbit differences, the program enumerates all 232221 unordered floored pairs and all 97020 unordered inner vertex pairs without materializing the full code; it proves a strict decimal bracket by exact integer powers.

An independent implementation, `audit_c21_cells.py`, instead places all discrete side-four cubes in the label torus and checks for overlapping cells. For the inner problem it places the side-two squares of each fiber and verifies all cross-fiber intersection types. See [the review](REVIEW.md).
