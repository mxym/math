# Internal review: independence, source checks, and limits

Two AI-assisted passes derived and checked the constructions. This document records which claims were tested and which still rely on prior work. It is not outside peer review or a machine-checked proof.

## 21-cycle: checking the certificate two ways

1. **Orbit method.** `verify_c21.py` checks all 681 nonzero residues, the short hand-checkable arithmetic in the proof, all 232,221 unordered pairs of floored labels, and all 97,020 distinct unordered pairs of inner vertices. The strong-product confusability test includes equality in each individual coordinate. The wrap at zero matters: floor compression bounds both directed circular gaps because $21\cdot130=2730\ge4\cdot682=2728$. Translation-invariance of rounded differences is neither true in general nor needed.
2. **Disjoint-cell method.** `audit_c21_cells.py` does not use the orbit-distance tests. For each four-word anchor it marks all $4^4$ integer cells of a discrete side-four cube on the label torus. All $682\cdot4^4=174592$ cells are distinct, out of $21^4=194481$. Two such cubes overlap if and only if all four circular coordinate distances are at most three. A gap of exactly four is allowed. For the inner construction it places the 21 side-two squares of each fiber; these occupy 84 distinct cells. The square supports of two fibers overlap exactly for offsets $0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3$. This is the full cross-fiber conflict test, not a sample.
3. Both methods prove the strict bracket $10.359354480853559<N^{1/8}<10.359354480853560$ with integer powers. Testing all pairs of 132 million final words is unnecessary: separate arguments cover conflicts within and across products.

**Historical comparison.** The [July Buys–Polak–Zuiddam preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29681v1) reports a 21-cycle rate of $10.342455853338\ldots$. The stronger size 11,466 in the fourth power is from [their later public Lean source](https://github.com/spectra-research/shannon-capacity-lean/blob/aa21eeb12b75b0413d3fa9fb4208b5d0bf2c4d65/ShannonBounds/CosetC21.lean), blob `f1fb3fe7b07cc1348574916ff15999a10b15e981`. We checked this source but did not rerun the upstream Lean project. The new explicit eighth-power count is compared with $11466^2=131469156$, not with an upper bound.

**Other comparisons checked.** In [de Boer–Buys–Zuiddam, §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16763v2#S6), Theorem 6.5 credits the exact general two-factor formula to Badalyan and Markosyan (Hales for two odd cycles). It gives 26 for two copies of the fraction graph at ratio 21/4. Squaring gives 676, not the 682 in this note. Theorem 6.4 gives 714 in four factors as an *upper*, not lower, bound. Theorem 6.9 concerns three factors with ratios between two and three. Polak's recursive examples quoted in Remark 6.15(2), at dimension four and parameters five or six, give respectively a size of 469 or require a ratio larger than 21/4. Floor maps and cyclic subgroup searches are known tools. [Baumert et al., Theorem 1 and Corollaries 1–2](https://www-math.mit.edu/~rstan/pubs/pubfiles/17.pdf) also give averaged widening inequalities: direct asymptotic transfer from BPZ’s quoted 19-cycle rate $9.357200030000796\ldots$ gives about $10.3422$, below the displayed rate. These are selected comparisons, not a proof that no previous construction implies the new count.

## 7-cycle: arithmetic versus upstream graph geometry

- Independently compared [Tandon’s exact ledger](https://github.com/tandonravi/C7-Shannon-Capacity-Heterogeneous-Recursion/blob/c753408492dde92a239708986d16c15dbf6c3235/data/paper_claims.json) (Git blob `4cf3296acb328a0427b47bd4f1905bf6c7477c8c`) and [his §4.2 and Appendix B.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30273v1#A2.SS4) with the local vectors. The input dimensions 30 and 55 and the ordering $B,N,A,D,O,H,V$ agree. The dimension-125 intermediate and 500th-power terminal are regression checks. We did **not** reconstruct Tandon’s five underlying neighborhood computations from primitive cycle vertices. The 7-cycle result depends on the cited constructions, unlike the 21-cycle certificate.
- Compared the substituted word lists, terminal and letter separations with [BPZ’s original Lean definitions](https://github.com/spectra-research/shannon-capacity-lean/tree/aa21eeb12b75b0413d3fa9fb4208b5d0bf2c4d65/ShannonBounds), then directly checked admissibility. In the $B,N$ lists, $AHN$ is a common *word*. It specifies an actual common box since the corresponding coordinate children are identical across both lists. We do not use or require equality with the full intersection of the two output families. The $N$-lane separates its points from the other five lanes. Independence within $B$ and within $N$ isolates these points from the remaining members of either lane.
- Matched the seven entries of $W$ and the unpeeled integer with [Stavriianov, Theorem 1 and Appendix A](https://zenodo.org/records/22728595/files/c7-recursion-tree.pdf?download=1); his accompanying software is [a separate archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22727743). The unflipped instance is his construction. Alternating two relabelings gives **the same integers**, including *after* peeling. The only improvement claimed over the named certificate is the isolated-box extraction and ensuing asymptotic count.
- The macro-coordinate estimate includes all residual-position patterns. Two different patterns differ in a coordinate occupied by an isolated symbol on one side and a residual one on the other; hence including a binomial factor is justified. The check of the decimal endpoints uses strictly positive integer differences before taking fourth powers.
- An exploratory 11-cycle calculation is not included as a result. Its rate $5.295495077846038\ldots$ is already below [Matthew Protti’s public R10 value, $5.295526013632343\ldots$](https://github.com/matthewprotti/c11-shannon-capacity-lower-bound/blob/dfaef37e60e55c55b1744d9badd1f26c5364c7d5/shannon_checked_release/source/ShannonBounds/C11R10D213.lean).

Targeted searches of the cited sources and searches by the numerical rates and equivalent packing question did not locate a matching previous 21-cycle or peeled 7-cycle certificate. They are not a comprehensive literature audit. Neither exact capacity is determined.
