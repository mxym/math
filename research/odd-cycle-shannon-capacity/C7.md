# Extracting an isolated box in a construction for the 7-cycle

Use unlogged Shannon capacity: for confusability graph $G$,
$\Theta(G)=\sup_h\alpha(G^{\boxtimes h})^{1/h}$. In a strong power two vertices are confusable if their coordinates are equal or adjacent in every position. Call two sets *separated* if no point of one is confusable with any point of the other. This includes disjointness.

**Inputs (not new).** [Ravi Tandon, §4.2 and Appendix B.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30273v1#A2.SS4) constructs actual seven-family realizations $P=\widetilde{\mathbf n}_6$ in $C_7^{\boxtimes30}$ and $Q=\widetilde{\mathbf n}_{11}$ in $C_7^{\boxtimes55}$. The notation here names the families and, when counting, their cardinality vectors. In each realization the lanes $B,N,A,D,O,H,V$ are individually independent. The pairs $BO,BH,BV,NA,ND,NO,NH,NV,AD,AH,DV$ are separated. No separation between $B$ and $N$ is required. The vectors in (119), (142), including their coordinate order, agree with [Tandon’s ledger](https://github.com/tandonravi/C7-Shannon-Capacity-Heterogeneous-Recursion/blob/c753408492dde92a239708986d16c15dbf6c3235/data/paper_claims.json). This note relies on Tandon’s realizations; checking an integer vector is not a construction of those sets.

The substitution $S_{3b}$ and terminal $K_{4a}$ use [Buys–Polak–Zuiddam’s admissible word lists](https://github.com/spectra-research/shannon-capacity-lean/tree/aa21eeb12b75b0413d3fa9fb4208b5d0bf2c4d65/ShannonBounds); the exact lists used here are in [c7_data.json](c7_data.json). A word describes the Cartesian product of the corresponding lanes, one from each child. Two distinct words in the same list differ in some separated coordinate, as do two words from lists whose output letters must be separated. These properties are checked directly. Thus the unions of boxes within each output lane are disjoint, independent and have the required cross-separations. Each cardinality is a sum over words of the product of the child weights.

**What is already known.** Take $W=S_{3b}(P,Q,Q)$ in dimension $30+55+55=140$. Applying $K_{4a}$ to four copies of $W$ gives precisely the 288-digit integer of [Oleksii Stavriianov, Theorem 1 and Appendix A](https://zenodo.org/records/22728595/files/c7-recursion-tree.pdf?download=1), with 560th root $3.258834362237710794\ldots$. Both that integer and the seven components of $W$ are independently compared with Appendix A. Although the search also tried an alternating relabeling $(A\ D)(H\ V)$ in positions two and four, it gives the same integer before and after extraction in this instance. We use the unflipped presentation throughout.

**The shared box.** The word $AHN$ is in both output lists for $B$ and $N$ under $S_{3b}$. With the very same children above, the concrete product
$$
U=P_A\times Q_H\times Q_N
$$
is contained in $W_B\cap W_N$. Its cardinality is
$$
c=5631349727733969284989622195220571011357410651413363748231448212184000.
$$
We do not assert that $U$ is the full intersection. Each point of $U$ is separated from every *other* point in $W_B\cup W_N$ by independence of the lane containing both points, and from every point in the other five lanes by the $N$-lane separations. Thus deleting $U$ from the union subtracts exactly $c$ from each of the $B,N$ weights, changes no other weight, and preserves admissibility. Let $w'$ be the resulting vector and $M=K_{4a}(w',w',w',w')$, the size of a code composed entirely of residual symbols in four macro-coordinates; its exact integer is printed by `verify_c7.py`.

**Counting across macro-coordinates.** In $t$ macro-coordinates designate $j$ of them as residual. Use free choices in $U$ elsewhere; on the designated positions in increasing order use $\lfloor j/4\rfloor$ blocks of the residual code followed by a fixed residual symbol on any unused positions. Such a symbol exists since $M>0$. Different patterns are separated since each residual symbol is outside and separated from $U$. Within each pattern, distinct choices in $U$ separate, as do distinct residual codewords. There are at least
$$
\sum_{j=0}^t {t\choose j}c^{t-j}M^{\lfloor j/4\rfloor}
\ \ge\ M^{-1}(c+M^{1/4})^t
$$
independent words in $C_7^{\boxtimes140t}$. Taking $140t$-th roots and passing to the limit gives
$$
\Theta(C_7)\ge(c+M^{1/4})^{1/140}>3.258834499096250 .
$$
This is an asymptotic lower bound, **not** a claimed code of this rate in dimension 560. The excluded upper endpoint $3.258834499096251$ bounds the *displayed construction’s rate*, not the capacity. The exact arithmetic, including these comparisons, is in [verify_c7.py](verify_c7.py). The independent internal check did not reconstruct all upstream neighborhood counts from the original cycle vertices; see [review](REVIEW.md).
