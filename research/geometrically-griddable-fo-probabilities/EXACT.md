# The exact first-order probability field at exponential growth

Let a fixed infinite hereditary permutation class be contained in a fixed geometric grid class, and let its growth rate be $r>1$. For the uniform permutation of each size, the limits of the probabilities of individual first-order sentences in the two orders form **exactly** $[0,1]\cap\mathbb Q(r)$. The polynomial case is not covered by this assertion. CONVERGENCE and DENSITY prove existence and the field upper inclusion; we give the converse. Sentences can depend on the class and on the individual target. We do not assert a uniform bound on their length. All limits are along all sizes.

## 1. Interpreting the entire finite path

Use the fixed partial multiplication matrix, canonical cuts and cell labels from DENSITY. On the canonical geometric gridding the strip precedence arcs are acyclic. Its reachability poset is first-order definable: on a shortest directed path any two nonconsecutive vertices in a common column would have a forward arc (a backward arc contradicts acyclicity), shortcutting the path. Thus each column contributes at most two vertices and there is a fixed bound on path length. This also covers the empty and one-point cases.

We explain explicitly how to define the order of the *entire* lexicographically least linear extension, not only bounded prefixes. In a current phase $H$ (initially all points), use the restriction of this already definable strict precedence poset and the remaining ordered alphabet. Let $a$ be the greatest remaining label. Its occurrences are a chain $h_1<\cdots<h_s$. For each point $x$ of the phase let $I(x)$ be its set of strict predecessors among these $a$-points of this phase. The possible sets are initial segments of that chain. Process the points in order of increasing $I$ under inclusion; within each equal-$I$ block put the points of label other than $a$ first, then its unique $a$-point if it exists. Recursively order the former group by the same rule with $a$ removed. This is the greedy least extension: after the first $i$ top-label points have been processed, any outstanding predecessor of any still-unprocessed non-$a$ point having precisely these $i$ top-label predecessors is itself non-$a$ of this block (all earlier blocks are past). Thus so long as such a point remains, some such point is available, and none requiring a later top-label point is available. Next comes $h_{i+1}$ if it exists, whose remaining predecessors have all just been exhausted. Within each subphase the available-point rule is that of its induced poset. Induct on the size of the remaining alphabet, allowing an absent greatest label. This constructs an FO comparison: inclusion/equality of $I(x),I(y)$ uses a universal point variable restricted to $H$; the next subphase is the non-$a$ part of the same equal-$I$ block, definable relative to either representative. The recursion depth is only the fixed alphabet size, including when some labels are absent. Points in one cell are totally ordered, so this agrees with the canonical word of SHAPES.

Each edge label of the accepting path in SHAPES can now also be read by FO at its word position. Indeed the two subword types there record which of the finitely many subwords up to its fixed bound occur strictly before/after the relevant cut. Membership is given by a bounded list of increasing letter positions. The normal-form scan's blocked label $a$ is recorded iff the scanned prefix contains a letter $b>a$ independent of $a$ with every letter following that witness in the prefix still independent of $a$, exactly its fixed update rule. The types and scanner on both sides of a position, together with its own letter, identify its transition; cuts at the beginning/end are included. There are no extra choices of distinct parallel transitions with the same source, target and label in that presentation. Thus each FO expression about positions of the accepting path with unary edge predicates translates into a TOTO expression on the permutation; this is an inverse interpretation for the specific canonical path. In particular one can name next position, first occurrence after a specified position, fixed substrings, entries/exits of particular components, and first hitting of a named state after a specified boundary. Boundaries in a finite path can be coded by the position just after them, with a separate terminal sentinel and with the first position representing the initial boundary. All finite case distinctions are harmless.

## 2. Accessing one common infinite experiment

Use the unambiguous primitive-component presentation of SHAPES, with finitely many productive initial states and accepting routes. Call a component critical if its spectral radius equals $r$. With probability tending to one the path has a dominant route; conditional on any such route the length $j$ spent in its first critical component tends to infinity in probability. This follows from the route/Dirichlet counting in SHAPES (the corresponding limiting simplex coordinate is positive almost surely; when it is the sole critical component its limiting proportion is one). Fix the route and entry and exit states; conditional on component length, its internal segment is uniform over walks of that length with these endpoints. For any fixed horizon the beginning of this segment converges to the Parry chain started at its entry state: each internal edge from $x$ to $y$ has probability $u_y/(r u_x)$ for a positive right Perron vector $u$. Each length-$j$ walk from entry to exit has the same probability. For a fixed initial $h$-edge segment, the residual conditioning factor is the ratio of the $(j-h)$-step endpoint probability from the segment end to the $j$-step endpoint probability from the entry, which tends to one by irreducibility and primitivity. There are finitely many routes, entry and exit choices. An experiment from the entry that stops almost surely after a finite random time in each infinite chain therefore has the same limiting output law on these finite paths (first truncate at a fixed horizon, then let it tend to infinity; both truncated success and noncompletion are finite-prefix events). In a finite path ignore all later components: start at the *first* critical component; if absent or if required steps reach beyond its internal walk, prescribe rejection. The chance of failure to complete tends to zero. For definiteness a productive path starting in the chosen component enters it at time zero. We only use experiments that can be specified by FO, not arbitrary stopping times.

## 3. A uniform finite die, without modular counting

In each critical component $C$ choose $v$ with two distinct outgoing internal edges: otherwise its irreducible adjacency matrix has row sums one, contrary to $r>1$. Extend these edges by first return to $v$ inside $C$ to obtain two distinct fixed first-return edge strings $A,B$. From entry, wait for the first visit to $v$, almost surely finite; below, all experiments start at $v$. For a given integer $q\ge2$ consider the $q$ markers
$$W_j=A A B\,B^j A B^{q+1-j},\qquad 0\le j<q.$$
They are distinct, each the concatenation of three $A$'s and $q+2$ $B$'s, hence have equal length in edges, regardless of the lengths of $A,B$. In the alphabet of return excursions they start with $AA$, have no other $AA$, and end with $B$. Thus no proper nonempty suffix of any marker is a prefix of another: a one-letter suffix is $B$; a longer proper suffix cannot begin $AA$. This rules out proper overlaps of actual *edge* occurrences too: a proposed second start must visit $v$, hence must be on an excursion boundary, and the intersection ends at $v$, so unique decomposition at visits to $v$ gives a suffix-prefix equality in return letters. At a common start at most one type is possible.

Take the earliest marker starting at or after the current boundary. Any particular marker occurs eventually almost surely in the finite irreducible Parry chain (for example, uniformly positive probability to reach $v$ and then traverse that fixed word within a fixed horizon from any state). To see that its type is uniform, fix a finite horizon $H$, and consider the paths with some marker completely present by $H$. Replace the earliest complete block $W_j$ with $W_k$. Its endpoints, edge length, and all other edge positions are unchanged. A newly earlier start that ends before or at the left boundary is unchanged; any other earlier start would now overlap the installed block, which is impossible by the preceding argument. Equal lengths ensure 'earliest complete' has the same order as earliest start. The reverse replacement is its inverse. Each such length-$H$ path keeps its terminal state and thus its probability (Perron edge probabilities telescope). This proves exact equal masses for all types by $H$, and then without the horizon as $H\to\infty$. For $q=1$ the claim is trivial. Checking which of finitely many fixed edge substrings starts first after a named boundary is FO, even though the waiting time has no fixed bound: existentially choose its start and forbid any such start in the open earlier interval (including the initial boundary if distinct). Nothing needs to count intervening visits.

## 4. One coin that separates the algebraic conjugates

Let $r_0$ be the largest absolute value of any nonidentity conjugate of $r$, or zero for rational $r$. Then $r_0<r$ because any critical primitive integer matrix has $r$ as its simple uniquely dominant eigenvalue, and its characteristic polynomial contains the minimal polynomial of $r$ as a factor. All conjugates of $r$ are nonzero. For the chosen vertices in this finite set of components, $(M_C^L)_{vv}/r^L$ tends to a strictly positive constant. Choose a fixed $s$ smaller than $1/2$ and half the smallest such constant, then choose $L$ sufficiently large that
$$m=\lfloor sr^L\rfloor>\max(1,r_0^L),\qquad m<(M_C^L)_{vv}\quad\text{for every }C.$$
In each component select exactly $m$ distinct closed walks of length $L$ at $v$. The next $L$ steps form one of these with probability $p=m/r^L$, independent of which component was selected. Record the resulting bit, then wait for the first visit to $v$ at or after the endpoint. This is an almost surely finite stopping time; if the endpoint is $v$, do not wait further. The strong Markov property therefore supplies as many independent $p$-bits as any *fixed* finite experiment requests, followed if desired by an independent uniform die as in §3. Every such finite number of trials is expressible in FO: name their successive starting boundaries and endpoints $L$ positions later, compare each intervening fixed edge word with the finite menu, require each restart state $v$ and forbid a $v$ in the half-open interval from the previous endpoint to the restart. No variable-length counting, parity test or modular arithmetic on positions is used.

Write $K=\mathbb Q(r)$. In fact $\mathbb Q(p)=K$: if $K/\mathbb Q(r^L)$ were nontrivial, separability would yield a distinct embedding into $\mathbb C$ fixing $r^L$, hence a conjugate of $r$ of modulus $r$, impossible. Every other conjugate of $p$ has modulus greater than one by the choice of $m$. Hence $p$ is the only root of its rational minimal polynomial $h$ in $[0,1]$, and it is simple.

## 5. Positive rational Bernstein representation and acceptance

Take any target $\alpha\in K\cap(0,1)$. Choose $f_0\in\mathbb Q[t]$ with $f_0(p)=\alpha$. On $[0,1]$ the function $(\alpha-f_0(t))/h(t)$ extends continuously across $p$ (the sole root there, simple). By uniform polynomial approximation and coefficient approximation by rationals, take $b\in\mathbb Q[t]$ approximating this continuous function well enough that $f=f_0+hb$ lies strictly between zero and one everywhere on $[0,1]$. Indeed its distance to $\alpha$ is at most the approximation error times $\|h\|_\infty$. We still have $f(p)=\alpha$.

Writing $f(t)=\sum_j a_jt^j$, its degree-elevated Bernstein coefficients at degree $N\ge\deg f$ are
$$c_{N,k}=\sum_j a_j (k)_{\underline j}/(N)_{\underline j}\quad(0\le k\le N),$$
by taking binomial factorial moments, with the empty product one. Uniformly in $k$, the ratio differs from $(k/N)^j$ by at most $\binom j2/N$ (couple sample draws with and without replacement, separating on a repeated label). Thus $c_{N,k}$ approaches $f(k/N)$ uniformly as $N$ grows. By the positive compact margin some finite $N$ makes all these rational coefficients strictly between zero and one. Write $c_{N,k}=d_k/q$ with one positive common denominator. Simulate $N$ $p$-bits in the selected component, then one independent uniform $q$-die. Choose acceptance when its type belongs to a fixed $d_k$-element subset if these $N$ bits contain $k$ successes. The finite disjunction over the $2^N$ outcomes makes this FO (the sentence may be long). In every critical component its probability is
$$\sum_{k=0}^N\binom Nk p^k(1-p)^{N-k} d_k/q=f(p)=\alpha.$$
By §§1–2 the corresponding TOTO sentence has limiting probability $\alpha$ in the original class. Constants handle zero and one. The reverse inclusion from DENSITY completes the equality. In particular rational exponential growth gives the rational interval, not all real numbers. No conclusion here applies to stronger logics.
