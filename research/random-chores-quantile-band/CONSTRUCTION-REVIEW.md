# Independent review of the linear fixed-law positive band

**PASS.** I read frozen [the construction](CONSTRUCTION.md), in its pre-formatting version, SHA-256 90af67fcbd345427e0b8dcca2d01fd46a46c6860c929a08e6e68a2744f1acebb independently and checked the linear-deficit construction. Its precise conclusion is correct: for each fixed nonatomic law on [0,1] whose CDF obeys a x≤F(x)≤b x near zero, and any sequence 1≤k=2n−m≤δ(b/a)n, complete exact EF with k singletons and n−k doubletons holds whp. The proof supplies one positive, exceptionally small, sufficient δ; it is not an optimal or sharp constant. Only under the original global density hypothesis do the published results cited here automatically settle the entire region m≥2n. This is internal AI review.

1. The new concentration step is valid: all capped optima avoid every capped edge on the simultaneous expansion event for every permutation, since one can replace the corresponding alternating cycle at strictly smaller cost than the cap. Merely bounding the largest **uncapped** optimum edge would not in itself justify this conclusion, but the actual proof gives the stronger pathwise statement. Bounded differences for N independent rows have per-row constant M_N, and N M_N²→0. A completely independent justification of the needed total-cost conclusion comes from [Wästlund (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1214/ECP.v14-1475): E C_N→π²/6 and Var C_N=O(1/N) for the exponential square optimum. Consequently, each of the fixed d+1 squares has its optimum ≤2 whp. The less quantitative bound 3 in the draft suffices. Reserving floor(n/2) columns costs ≤2 in total whp because their independent exponential minima have total mean ≤1 and variance O(1/n). Their maxima are O(log n/n) by exponential tails. No concentration of chosen entries individually across different rows of a common assignment is presumed.
2. The omission sets O_h have size k and are fixed/disjoint, possible since dk≤n/2. All d arrays are generated on the **whole** n×(n−k) set before restricting the optimum to each square subarray. This point ensures that d times the minimum of the latent costs in each actual cell remains Exp(1), including cells omitted in one layer. Distinct cells and the A array remain independent. An optimizer in each eligible square is a uniform bijection; these bijections are independent across h and may be dependent on true B costs. The max-over-layers numerator for the row is bounded in total by 5+3d², since max≤sum and each latent optimum has total ≤3. Quantiles need not be strictly invertible: F(x)≥ax ensures Q(u)≤u/a for u≤ax₀, and the deterministic layer bound ensures all required u are in range for n large. Choice of T_i therefore remains valid even for distribution functions with plateaus.
3. On conditioning on reserve winners and their minima (but not losing entries), the unreserved assignment data and all B layers, the T_i are fixed. Earlier removed rows in a reserve column are fresh; eligible losers have independent truncated laws. After fixing the history of winning rows these constraints factor across different reserve columns. Thus the indicators of safe columns are conditionally independent even when their success probabilities vary. Uniformly in the winning column, its conditional safe probability is ≥Π(1−F(T_j))≥e^{−2bΣT_j}≥p; all T_j are eventually ≤x₀ and bT_j≤1/2. With r≈n/2 there are at least pn/5 safe columns whp. Safe owners may depend on B. Nevertheless the graph on these owners where two rows meet a candidate B column has maximum degree ≤d(d−1). Greedy packing supplies ≥pn/[5(d²+1)] separated owners: selecting exactly k with pairwise disjoint B neighborhoods is valid pathwise. There is no false conditional independence of the selected S and the B-candidate graph.
4. The low-cost true matrix is iid Bernoulli after thresholding; each bad candidate yields a four-cycle and two bad pairs at a common endpoint would produce distinct cycles meeting there. Counting two-cycle unions gives o(1), so bad **distinct** pairs are a matching. This was already the central conflict-removal technique of [Manurangsi–Suksompong, §3.4 Proposition 3.9/Figure 1](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1279125) in the divisible goods setting. The new multiplicity claim handles all copies: for a fixed vertex, more than one excess incidence beyond unique neighbors means either a triple, or two disjoint equality pairs among the colors. For rows each draw is uniform on N labels; for columns the draws are independent, each uniform with atom size at most 1/N on different supports. Each prescribed event has probability ≤N^{-2}; d is fixed so union over all O(n) vertices is o(1). A column loses at most two distinct rows (one for conflict, one for S) and therefore at most 3 original incidences. A surviving row starts with ≥d−1, loses at most 2 to a single bad neighbor, and also retains ≥d−3. Duplicate-pair contacts cannot cause a hidden fourth loss.
5. In the two-sided small-set event, let t distinct vertices in a fixed eligible domain be required to hit a specified s-set. Their probability is ≤(s)_t/(N)_t; it is zero if a required source is omitted, and for reverse demands possible destinations are W intersected with the layer's domain. The weaker (s/N)^t bound applies; multiplication uses only independence **between layers**. The number of choices of demands is binom(d,3)^s. Counting U,W by (en/s)^{2s} gives precisely the stated expression. With d=24, δ≤1/48, even the relaxed n/N≤12/11 and s/N≤6/11 give 8(12/11)²binom(24,3)(6/11)^19=129164765370544816128/672749994932560009201<1/2. For s≤√n the geometric base vanishes uniformly; all larger s≤n/2 contribute o(1). No Hall estimate is conditioned on the final S. In a deficient set U of size s>n/2 in the surviving N×N graph with k≥1, choose N−s+1 opposite nonneighbors, at most floor(n/2); their own neighbors lie in the N−s outside U. The two orientations exactly cover this case, without a parity exception. The resulting doubletons cannot be envied since any envious nonowner supplies the deleted bad-edge witness. Singletons were safe for *all* nonowners, including other singletons. The constructed complete partition is of the original real chores.
