195 (2022), 89–206, [DOI 10.4007/annals.2022.195.1.2](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2022.195.1.2).
The all-k theorem here uses it explicitly; it does not claim that imported
geometric result as this project's contribution. Our text and computation
are supplied independently, with attribution to this prior result.

`LITERATURE_STATUS.md` gives the additional source comparisons and their
limits. The OpenAI repository snapshot checked was
`fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb`; its unrestricted propeller
theorem is adjacent literature, not a premise of this proof.

replay infrastructure. `results/lean.json` records compiler, pinned
dependency provenance, all four proof/check source hashes and all four
logs. Its Gaussian-endpoint-formalized field is deliberately false.
`results/exact.json` records finite diagnostic controls, not a certificate
for the written theorem. The two `review/*_FINAL.md` documents review the
frozen manuscript hash; they are internal model reviews.
