## Mathematical reconstruction

1. Hermitian eigenvalues are taken from Mathlib's canonically ordered `eigenvalues_0`, not from an unproved externally supplied ordering. Two injective finite label maps extend to a permutation, realized by the already proved physical global-unitary bridge.
2. For the rank-two corner the four labels are least, next-to-least, largest, and third-to-least. The genuine PSD principal witness yields both the determinant and all-ones inequalities. Trace one and antitonicity imply the 3/(D+2) upper bound and strictly positive normalization pivots.
3. For the shifted star, a finite injection fixes the center, leaf diagonals, positive star slots and negative star slots. The leftover leaf-pair values are oriented by an involution which fixes every diagonal and the entire distinguished star. A second finite extension supplies one global permutation. This avoids assuming compatibility of individually chosen pair labels.
4. The recovered wrapper used the valid smaller pivot at bottom rank 2m-1. That alone was not the exact statement of the paper. The new argument permits every sorted pivot between the first-head and leaf-diagonal bounds. It proves that the paper pivot at bottom rank m(m+1)/2 is admissible, using exact natural-number arithmetic including division by two. Both nonnegativity and the quadratic budget are derived internally.
5. With a_i=lambda_i/b-1 and beta_i=1-lambda_(D+1-i)/b, the physical inequality normalizes to sum(a_i+beta_(i+1))^2<=4(1-beta_1). The scalar logarithmic theorem then bounds sum[f(a_i)+a_i beta_i]+sum[f(-beta_i)] by two, including zero least eigenvalues. No Taylor approximation replaces the real logarithm.
6. As a separate spectral check, determinant zero is converted to an actual eigenvalue zero via the determinant product formula. PSD and ordering place a zero at the least eigenvalue; the physical rank-two determinant makes every other eigenvalue equal. Trace one fixes the common value. This proves only the necessary singular-spectrum statement, not its converse construction.
