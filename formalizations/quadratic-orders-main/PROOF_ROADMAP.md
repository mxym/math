# Source roadmap

## 1. Actual ideal coefficients and analytic supply

`Entry002.IdealNormCoefficient`, `IdealNormDivisibility` and `IdealFactorLogarithm` use actual finite ideal-norm fibers, multiplication/quotient equivalences and normalized factorization. The convolution identity feeds `PrimeIdealLogDerivative` and `IdealNormAnalytic`, relating the genuine coefficient series to mathlib's Dedekind zeta.

`PrimeIdealEulerLogIdentity`, `PrimeIdealEulerLogRealPole` and `PrimeIdealEulerLogRemainder` establish the real Euler-log identity, pole behavior and bounded error terms. `PrimeIdealSplitDirichlet` and `PrimeIdealSplitDirichletCutoff` yield positive upper Dirichlet supply of actual completely splitting rational primes after any finite conductor cutoff. No natural PNT is inserted.

## 2. From Dirichlet supply to suitable windows

`WeakSupplyDirichletCutoff`, `WeakSupplyDyadicChebyshev`, `WeakSupplyDyadicSeries` and `WeakSupplyDirichletGoodBins` turn prime-specific positive upper Dirichlet supply into positive logarithmic weight for fixed-density good dyadic bins. The window log certificate, independent-grid weighted count and averaging modules select cofinally many suitable scales, not every sufficiently large scale.

## 3. Geometric finite sieve

The 15 `WeakCore*` modules reproduce the geometry, entropy and common-law charge from A1–A4 and the proved weaker supply. `WeakCoreGoodBinFiniteSieve` and `WeakFiniteSieveConsequences` establish the finite Q²-component/walk bounds. Floors, windows, bands, the actual finite prime pool and one common law are preserved.

## 4. All-conductor arithmetic bridge and original Main

The five owned external bridges are:

- `ArithmeticSupplyRayBridge`
- `ArithmeticSupplyRayConductor`
- `ArithmeticSupplyWeakFromDirichlet`
- `ArithmeticSupplyWeakMainReduction`
- `ArithmeticSupplyWeakMain`

They use actual ray-class-field generators and normal closure, together with the new cutoff Dirichlet supply. Their separate nonofficial foundation comprises exactly 995 pinned-and-patched ClassFieldTheory, ValuedFieldTheory, GaloisCohomology and ProCGroups modules. The last bridge combines the closed weak principal supply with the closed weak finite-sieve theorem to inhabit the literal original `MainTarget`.

## 5. Validation layers

Source/pin identity, fresh compilation, declaration ownership, stored-type/body dependency traversal, stock-axiom identity, genuinely empty trust-zero kernel replay, mathematical source interpretation, licensing and publication status are separate checks. Passing one is not a substitute for another.
