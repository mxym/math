## What the supply proof actually needs

For every quadratic field and every positive conductor, the proof needs a finite-modulus ray class extension with reciprocity, a finite normal closure over the rationals, a positive natural splitting-prime density, and a rational prime-counting asymptotic. It then needs actual congruent-to-one prime generators in the conductor order, prime-order residue rings, two distinct principal kernels, and intersection equal to the rational-prime ideal. Ordinary ideal-class finiteness or a density predicate alone does not supply these conclusions.
