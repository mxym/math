## 4. Norm, conjugation, kernels and paired pO are genuine

- Degree-one norm is Mathlib ideal norm `Ideal.absNorm P = p`; a principal generator gives the integer determinant norm via `Ideal.absNorm_span_singleton` (`Entry002/ArithmeticPrimeIdealCounting.lean:20–33`).
- For all positive conductors, the order is finite free over ℤ (`Entry002/Orders.lean:35–40`), has full rational rank (58–67), and localizes to K (81–114). This justifies equality of the conductor-order determinant norm and maximal-order norm (`Entry002/ArithmeticSupplyElementary.lean:129–134`), with no Dedekind assumption on the nonmaximal order.
- The quotient-cardinality proof is the determinant/index identity on the actual principal ideal in an arbitrary finite free integral order (`Entry002/ArithmeticSupplyElementary.lean:26–46`). A norm-p generator then produces a surjective unital `O →+* ZMod p` with exact divisibility kernel (`51–70`), and a maximal principal ideal (`74–83`).
- Conjugation is an actual restriction of the nonidentity rational automorphism to `ℤ+f𝓞_K` (`Entry002/ArithmeticConductorConjugation.lean:14–40`). Such a nonidentity automorphism is proved to exist for every quadratic number field (`138–151`).
- The paired ideals are genuinely distinct: e=f=1 gives a decomposition stabilizer of cardinality one, so the nonidentity automorphism moves P (`Entry002/ArithmeticConductorConjugation.lean:72–92`); the conjugate generator ideal is its actual translate (`96–103`), and distinctness reflects back to the conductor order (`Entry002/ArithmeticSupplyElementary.lean:138–146`).
- The product of conjugates is the actual integer norm, so it is associated to p in the conductor order itself (`Entry002/ArithmeticSupplyElementary.lean:256–292`). The two distinct norm-p maximal ideals are comaximal; the proof derives `(a|x ∧ b|x) ↔ x∈pO` by coprime divisibility (`88–101`). No pair-intersection hypothesis remains.
- `Entry002/ArithmeticWeakPrincipalSupplyAssembly.lean:40–84` builds the signed residue data with surjectivity, actual conjugate generators, unital ring maps and that exact intersection. The endpoint obtains and supplies every required witness (`audit/ArithmeticSupplyWeakFromDirichlet.lean:57–82`).
