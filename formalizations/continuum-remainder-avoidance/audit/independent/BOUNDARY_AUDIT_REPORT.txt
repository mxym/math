   The proof uses the genuine cached mathlib Besicovitch density theorem, not a new assumption. At a density point y of E, centered density >3/4 forces occupancy of the right shell (y+r/2,y+r]; a missing shell would constrain the intersection to an interval of length 3r/2 inside a ball of length 2r. Its outside-image set is empty.
   This countercontrol applies in particular to positive-measure closed nowhere-dense periodic E. Thus swapping the target's family and E quantifiers cannot be a harmless stronger interpretation.
