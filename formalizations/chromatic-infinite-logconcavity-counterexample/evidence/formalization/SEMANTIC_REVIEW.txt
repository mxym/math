1. Actual graph and actual proper colorings
ChromaticC17.C17 abbreviates mathlib SimpleGraph.cycleGraph 17 on Fin 17.
The inherited graph relation is the undirected modular successor relation.
Coloring is mathlib's graph homomorphism into completeGraph, so adjacent vertices
must have unequal colors. No local replacement of Coloring or its cardinality is
used. The final explicit counterexample includes actual graph connectedness.

coloringEquivLoops constructs a bijection between these genuine colorings and
closed length-17 walks in the complete color graph. Forward traversal is in the
fixed vertex order 0,1,...,16,0. The inverse reads walk vertices. Both inverse laws
are proved, including the final wraparound edge. Thus the bijection does not count
rotations, graph isomorphism classes, or colorings modulo permutation of colors.

2. Full chromatic-polynomial bridge
coloring_card_eq_trace uses the bijection, finite sigma cardinality, and mathlib's
adjacency-matrix-power walk-count theorem. The complete-graph matrix is J-I. The
new transferCoeff induction uses J*J=q*J and establishes its trace power formula.
It is valid for every q : Nat, including zero and one; it does not divide by q.

cyclePolynomial = (X-1)^17 - (X-1) is an actual Polynomial Int. The theorem
cyclePolynomial_isChromatic proves, for every natural q, that its evaluation is
the cardinality of C17.Coloring (Fin q). IsChromaticPolynomial is this all-q law.
IsChromaticPolynomial.unique proves that it determines the polynomial uniquely,
using infinitely many integer evaluation points. This supplies the standard
counting definition even though pinned mathlib has no chromatic-polynomial API.

3. Actual coefficients and the iteration convention
absCoefficients P k is abs (Polynomial.coeff P k), indexed by ascending degree.
cyclePolynomial_coeff proves every coefficient, using the binomial coefficient
formula, not a separate unconnected integer list. Its degree>17 zero tail is
proved. The first six absolute coefficients are 0,16,136,680,2380,6188.

logConcavityOp a k = a(k)^2 - previous(k)*a(k+1), where previous(0)=0 and
previous(k)=a(k-1) for positive k. logConcavityIterate applies exactly this operator
repeatedly. Its zero-tail lemmas prove that all iterates retain the finite right
endpoint. InfinitelyLogConcave requires every positive iterate at every natural
index to be nonnegative. This agrees with the standard endpoint-retaining,
zero-extension convention in the primary sources. The coefficient index zero,
and zero coefficients in general, are retained.

4. Exact negative certificate and quantified conclusion
third_iterate_value proves L^3(absCoefficients cyclePolynomial)(2) equals
-28272276537344, with ordinary kernel-checked arithmetic proof construction.
cyclePolynomial_not_infinitelyLogConcave derives the failure of the full infinite
property. every_C17_chromatic_polynomial_fails applies uniqueness to every
polynomial meeting the genuine C17 coloring-count law.

ChromaticInfiniteLogConcavityConjecture quantifies over all n, all SimpleGraph
(Fin n), and every Polynomial Int meeting that law. The final theorem supplies
n=17, the actual cycle, and its proven counting polynomial to refute it. Every
finite unlabeled graph can be represented by such labels; a single labeled
counterexample already refutes the universal conjecture. No graph-size bound,
extra positivity premise, or unproved polynomial formula is assumed.
