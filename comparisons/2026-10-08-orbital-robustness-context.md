## Exact dependencies and classical structures

1. **Bristiel–Caputo (2024)**, *Entropy inequalities for random walks and permutations* (DOI 10.1214/22-AIHP1267, Corollary 1.14), supplies the sharp **uniform** permutation-permanent inequality imported into our general perturbation Theorem 1. This is not presented as our result.
2. **OpenAI/math (September 26, 2026)**, *A strict four-row permanent inequality and permutation moments*, Section 2, motivates the robust perturbation problem, proves a below-2 four-row exponent and a nonexplicit marginal-preserving perturbation neighborhood. Our manuscript treats all arities quantitatively and separately develops exact group-action atom/TV theory.
3. The **Johnson association scheme** on k-subsets, with orbital relations indexed by |A∩B|, is classical. See for example the introduction of Shekarriz et al., *Distinguishing threshold of graphs*, Journal of Graph Theory (2023), https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jgt.22923 . Our paper does not claim to invent Johnson orbitals or group conjugation averaging.
4. Finite **linear-programming strong duality**, **Burnside's orbit counting**, **AM–GM**, **Gibbs entropy duality**, **Chebyshev polynomials**, and the **Markov polynomial endpoint inequality** are all established mathematical tools. The proposed original scope, subject to broader literature investigation, lies in the **particular sharp atom-TV distributional constants and constructions**, the explicit all-degree pair-action formula, the triple-action sharp first-order asymptotic, and the finite exact certificates.
