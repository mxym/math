# Focused comparison for 005 v5

**Date:** 7 October 2026.

Version 5 classifies a recursively defined two-parameter family built from the projection-volume product/join calculus:
\[
K_{j+1}=(K_j^t)^{*t},
\qquad
K_0=T_p.
\]
The theorem is internal to this recursive geometry: it proves that the unique largest asymptotic root rate over all integers \(t\ge2,\ p\ge1\) occurs at \((t,p)=(2,5)\).

## Upstream and classical background

OpenAI family 088, *A product counterexample to the simplex maximum for projection-body volume*, supplies the attributed Cartesian-product identity and simplex values used as the starting point of entry 005. It does not contain the later join invariant, the spectral parameter, or the balanced homogeneous recursion classification.

N. S. Brannen, *Volumes of projection bodies*, Mathematika 43 (1996), 255--264, DOI 10.1112/S002557930001175X, studies sharp bounds for the projection-body functional on subclasses and formulates the historical simplex-maximum conjecture.

C. Saroglou, *Volumes of projection bodies of some classes of convex bodies*, Mathematika 57 (2011), 329--353, DOI 10.1112/S0025579311001860, treats sharp three-dimensional extrema for zonoids, cones and double cones and investigates equality cases.

These papers are relevant to the ambient extremal problem but do not, in the material located in this focused search, state the product/join spectral recursion or the infinite-arity classification proved in version 5.
