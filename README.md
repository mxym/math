# The complete ternary projective Reed–Muller weight hierarchies on PG(4,3)

San-José's [Conjecture 6.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08203v2) proposes a generalized Hamming-weight formula for all orders of projective Reed–Muller codes. We verify it for every positive order and every generalized weight on the 121 points of \(\operatorname{PG}(4,3)\).

Combining the 2024 recursive lower bound with the mean constraint on a least-intersecting hyperplane leaves only two gaps against the 2026 upper bound at the intermediate orders \(d=3,4,5,6\): \(76\) versus \(77\) at \((d,r)=(3,11)\), and \(44\) versus \(45\) at \((6,31)\). Treating the recurrence as a restriction on *every* hyperplane gives \(d_{11}=77\) by the first incidence moment and \(d_{31}=45\) by the second. The degree-3 value also follows by duality once that least-intersection comparison verifies the degree-5 hierarchy. The degree-6 value is the only remaining entry that requires the second incidence moment. The author's linked [public tables, release v1.0.0](https://github.com/RodrigoSanJose/ghw-tables/blob/ab10b60819c81517022ff8e96ebd6f0dd6614681/docs/data/q3_m4.json) still list 12 intervals at these parameters; the least-intersection and all-hyperplanes steps together close all 12. The result is a complete fixed-field, fixed-projective-dimension case, not a general proof of Conjecture 6.9.

The same argument also rederives a previously computed \(\operatorname{PG}(3,3)\) base entry without enumerating subcodes.
