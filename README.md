# The sharp star Steiner ratio in Euclidean dimensions at least three

For finite Euclidean point sets, the worst-case ratio of the shortest input-centered star to the shortest star with arbitrary center is the mean unit-sphere chord in every dimension d ≥ 3. In particular the value in three dimensions is 4/3, as conjectured by Fekete and Meijer. A separate appendix gives the strict upper bound 1.2865 in the plane, not a resolution of the planar 4/π conjecture. The main argument is analytic and needs no computation. The archive contains the proofs, source comparisons, exact checks for the planar bound, and two reviews internal to the AI-assisted workflow; it is not externally peer reviewed.
