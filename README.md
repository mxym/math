# The Planar Flat-Floor Sylvester Conjecture: Triangle Minimum and Square Counterexamples

**Yongxian Zhang (ORCID 0009-0000-3864-3536).** AI-assisted working paper, internally checked but not externally peer reviewed.

The lower and upper clauses of [Marckert and Morin's Conjecture 1.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.08456v2) have different answers. For every n≥3, the minimum over planar convex bodies with a marked segment on a supporting line is attained exactly by triangles with their entire bottom side marked. For every n≥3, an explicitly parameterized pentagon beats the square, even with the floor the entire bottom edge and no overhang. This is the fixed-floor problem, not the ordinary all-random Sylvester problem, and the actual maximizing body is not identified.
