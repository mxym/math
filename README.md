# The EHZ volume inequality for parallelotopes

The Ekeland–Hofer–Zehnder volume inequality is proved for all full-dimensional affine images of cubes in even-dimensional standard symplectic space. Equality is classified, including strictness in dimensions six and above. No claim is made for arbitrary centrally symmetric bodies or other capacities.

Author: Yongxian Zhang. AI-assisted proof development and internal verification; no claim of external peer review or priority.
