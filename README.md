# Two exact results for periodic translation-invariant quantum max-flow

Yongxian Zhang · November 2026 · Version 1.0.0

This working paper gives matching lower bounds for Gesmundo, Landsberg and Walter's binary even-cycle result and equal-dimension four-cycle result. It is an AI-assisted, internally checked research note, not an external peer-review claim. Both proofs are elementary tensor calculations; they arose from the Euclidean tensor geometry research program but do not invoke its pricing theorems.
