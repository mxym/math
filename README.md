# Exact randomness support on forests, cycles and unicyclic graphs

Three companion results in the [Marszalik model](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2395). Forests, simple cycles of length at least 4, and connected simple unicyclic graphs other than the bare triangle are covered; see each paper for its precise statement. Individual perfect privacy and exact correctness are required. This classifies dealer-support cardinality, not Shannon entropy, coalitions, approximate privacy, or unrestricted graphs.

Run `bash replay.sh` separately inside each subdirectory. The three manifests fingerprint the pinned files.
